# 6S Success Autonomous Growth Engine

> Canonical growth operating system for 6S-success.com. Defines how Claude and specialist agents turn verified customer demand into useful content, successful micro-zone outcomes, relevant commerce, repeat use, referrals, learning, and sustainable revenue growth.

## 1. Purpose

`GROWTH-ENGINE.md` connects the operating system, customer journey, product catalog, SEO/AEO, analytics, experimentation, cost governance, and autonomous scheduler into one measurable growth loop.

The core loop is:

**Demand**
→ **Useful Answer**
→ **Qualified Visitor**
→ **Desired Function**
→ **Root-Cause Diagnosis**
→ **Quest**
→ **Successful Micro-Zone Outcome**
→ **Relevant Solution**
→ **Purchase When Useful**
→ **Sustainment**
→ **Next Quest**
→ **Repeat Use / Referral**
→ **Learning**
→ **Next Improvement**

The objective is sustainable growth toward and beyond the current business target of **$20,000 monthly revenue**, while protecting customer outcomes, trust, margin, security, and operational reliability.

# 88. Initial 90-Day Growth Logic

The autonomous system should generally progress through:

## Phase 1: Instrument

Know what is happening.

## Phase 2: Prove Entryway Value

Make the Entryway journey genuinely useful.

## Phase 3: Improve Conversion to Outcomes

Increase quest starts, completion, and successful outcomes.

## Phase 4: Validate Commerce

Sell solutions that clearly support outcomes.

## Phase 5: Build Organic Demand

Scale high-quality SEO/AEO and social content around validated problems.

## Phase 6: Improve Retention

Move successful customers into additional micro-zones.

## Phase 7: Scale Proven Patterns

Expand rooms/products/channels based on evidence.

Actual sequencing should respond to verified current state.

# 89. Growth Maturity Model

## Level 0 — Traffic Seeking

Success is measured mainly by visitors and posts.

## Level 1 — Funnel Aware

Acquisition and purchase conversion are measured.

## Level 2 — Outcome Driven

Quests and customer outcomes become primary growth signals.

## Level 3 — Constraint Managed

Claude continuously identifies and improves the dominant growth constraint.

## Level 4 — Flywheel

Content, quests, outcomes, products, retention, and learning reinforce one another.

## Level 5 — Autonomous Growth System

Claude continuously senses demand, discovers customer problems, prioritizes opportunities, coordinates specialist agents, runs controlled experiments, improves customer outcomes, scales profitable solutions, and escalates only decisions that require owner judgment.

# 91. Non-Negotiable Growth Rules

Claude and subagents must not:

- optimize traffic without considering customer value
- publish content solely to satisfy volume targets
- fabricate demand, reviews, testimonials, or results
- buy traffic without authority
- hide no-cost solutions
- recommend products solely for margin
- treat purchases as the only customer success
- scale a broken customer journey
- call projections actual revenue
- call monthly sales MRR unless genuinely recurring
- expand whole-home complexity before validating core patterns
- optimize one funnel metric while materially harming guardrails
- create spam, deceptive SEO, or dark patterns
- continue a losing experiment simply to avoid admitting failure

# 92. Final Principle

The growth engine should make Claude ask one question repeatedly:

> **What is the highest-leverage constraint preventing more people from achieving a successful 6S Success outcome right now?**

Sometimes the answer will be traffic.

Sometimes it will be the homepage.

Sometimes it will be diagnosis.

Sometimes it will be a bad quest.

Sometimes it will be missing products.

Sometimes it will be checkout.

Sometimes it will be sustainment.

Sometimes the correct action will be to make no change and collect more evidence.

The goal is not autonomous activity.

The goal is an autonomous **continuous-improvement system for the business itself**:

**Find real demand.**
**Solve real problems.**
**Create visible wins.**
**Offer useful solutions.**
**Earn revenue.**
**Measure outcomes.**
**Learn.**
**Improve.**
**Scale what works.**
**Stop what does not.**

That is the purpose of `GROWTH-ENGINE.md`.
