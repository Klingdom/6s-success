THE PRIMARY BATHROOM

SEVEN ZONES. START WITH THE COUNTER.

●○○○○

The primary bathroom is the smallest room you use the most, and it carries three problems no other room has all at once: standing moisture, open chemistry, and dates that quietly pass while you sleep. This card is the map and the order.

How it works

Start here. PBZ-001 Vanity Counter. Counter first, shower second. Those two are what you stand in front of every morning, and finishing them makes the under-sink cabinet worth opening. Leave the medicine cabinet for a session where you are willing to read small print on the back of a box.

The seven zones, in order

  1. PBZ-001 Vanity Counter
  2. PBZ-002 Vanity Drawers
  3. PBZ-003 Under-Sink Cabinet
  4. PBZ-004 Medicine Cabinet or Wall Storage
  5. PBZ-005 Shower or Tub
  6. PBZ-006 Toilet Area
  7. PBZ-007 Linen and Towel Storage

How to play

  1. Deal the seven ZONE cards face up. Pick the one that is annoying you today, or take the one this card says to start at.
  2. Lay out that zone's three FRICTION cards. Keep the ones that are true in your bathroom. Put the rest back.
  3. Turn a friction card over and pick the answer that is true. It names a ROOT CAUSE.
  4. The root cause card names the ACTION cards that fix that cause. Pick the one that matches the time you actually have: fifteen or thirty minutes.
  5. Do it standing up, with the card in your hand.
  6. Read the victory condition out loud. If it is not true yet, you are not finished, and that is the whole scoring system.
  7. Fill in the zone's STANDARD card with a pen and put it where the zone is.
  8. Draw an EVENT card when an ordinary hard day happens, and see whether the standard held.

Players. 1 to 4. With more than one, split the chain: one clears the counter and drawers, another works the cabinet and the shower.

Safety first. Do PBA-016 The Open-Chemistry Check before any rebuild. It takes thirty minutes and covers what makes this room different from every other: chemicals, water and electricity, and dates that pass while you sleep.

How it works

Done looks like: One tray you can lift with one hand, holding soap, the toothbrush holder, and no more than four daily products. Bare stone on both sides of the basin, a mirror with no splatter, and no cord crossing the sink.

Six things to check

Burn or fire A hot iron set down on a towel or a cosmetic bag to cool. Give it a metal or ceramic rest it always returns to, in the same spot every time.

The tool that lives out. The straightener or the dryer sits on the counter because the outlet is right there and you use it three or four times a week. CLEAR THE COUNTER TO THE DAILY FOUR

15 MINUTES. 1.

●●○○○

Clear the counter down to bare stone and put back only the tray with the soap, the toothbrush holder and the four products you actually touched today.

How it works

A counter with no boundary on what belongs is why it fills back up within two days, and 'I might use it' is not the same test as 'I used it today.'

You need

  • a cloth
  • nothing else beyond fifteen minutes

Steps

  1. Take everything off the counter, wipe the bare stone and the mirror, then put back only the soap, the toothbrush holder and the four products you touched today.
  2. Coil or unplug any cord that crosses the basin.
  3. Move any glass bottle back off the counter edge to the row against the mirror.
  4. If this counter is shared, give each person their own tray rather than one merged pile.

Victory. Bare stone on both sides of the basin, and one tray you can lift with one hand.

●●●○○

To store cleaning supplies and backstock around the plumbing without ever hiding a leak.

How it works

Six things to check

The pipe you cannot see. You have packed this cabinet wall to wall because it is the only storage in the room, and the P-trap is now buried behind a tower of paper towels.
