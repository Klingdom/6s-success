The Entryway Deck

Forty six cards that take one entryway through all six passes, five micro zones at a time. Each card names one job, two things to do, and what to look for when it is done. Cards are colour-coded by S, and Safety is the fourth S.

Start with card 13, the Landing Spot. It is about twenty minutes and it is the square foot of your house that everybody touches first.

To print: press Ctrl and P, or Cmd and P. You get true 2.5 by 3.5 inch cards, nine to a letter page. Card stock holds up better than paper, but paper works.
