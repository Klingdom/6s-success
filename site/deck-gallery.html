The Entryway deck

Every card, front and back

72 of the deck's 89 cards are drawn and shown here, front and back. The other 17 are written in full and waiting on their artwork. Tap any card to turn it over.

The deck is one room, its 12 micro zones and everything that holds them. The method behind it covers 114 micro zones across twenty rooms and the print and play PDF is free.

Download the printable deck Twenty sheets, nine cards to a sheet, fronts and backs, 25 MB PDF. Print double sided at 100 percent, flip on the long edge, then cut on the marks. Free, and no email address needed.

What one card is

A card is one micro zone, one pass, and one finish line: trading card size, two and a half by three and a half inches, printed on both sides.

The front is what to do

The zone or the problem, five numbered points on the picture that show you where they are, the objective, a thirty second win, and the action to take in your own home.

The back is why, and what next

Why the zone matters, the symptoms that say it has slipped, the best practices, a seven day challenge, and the card to pick up afterwards.

Counted whole, the deck is 89 cards including the single Room card that names the room. The catalogue and the deck page quote 88, which is the same deck with that one divider left out. Every count on this page is the whole-deck one.
