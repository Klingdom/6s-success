One method, six moves

The six S's, in the order that works

Each S is one clear move. Do them in order and the home shifts from chaos to calm, one step at a time. Safety is the fourth S, the difference that makes this method a home method.

03

05

06
Sustain
Keep it on the rhythm of the day, for good.
