Room

Home Office

This is the room where physical clutter and unmade decisions about paper live in the same drawer. Almost nothing here is heavy or dirty, which is exactly why it sits untouched for years: every item asks for a judgment rather than a lift. Start with the desk surface, because you need one finished workspace to sort everything else onto, and give the file drawer a morning of its own.

The 6 micro zones, in working order

A micro zone is one session, not a whole day. Finish one before you start the next.

  1. The Primary Desk (45-75 min)
    One clear surface for the work you are actually doing right now.
  2. The Desk Drawers and Pedestal (30-45 min)
    Hold the small tools you reach for without looking, and the live files you touch weekly.
  3. The File Storage (60-90 min)
    Keep the documents you are genuinely required to hold, and put your hand on any one of them without opening a second drawer.
  4. The Bookshelf and Reference Zone (45-75 min)
    Hold the reference you genuinely reach for, at the height you reach for it from.
  5. The Printer and Scanning Station (30-45 min)
    Turn paper into files and files into paper, without hunting for either.
  6. The Supply Cabinet (45-75 min)
    Hold the consumables this office genuinely burns through in a year, and no more.

For this room

  • Where to start. Clear the desk surface completely before you open a single drawer. Every other zone in this room gets emptied onto that surface at some point, so it has to be the first thing finished and the last thing you let refill.
  • Cleaning note. Everything in here is either electronics or paper, so a wet cloth is the risk. Power devices down first, spray screen cleaner onto the cloth and never onto a screen or the scanner glass, and use a dry cloth for toner dust, because water turns loose toner into ink.
  • The trap. This is the room with a door that closes, so it quietly becomes the household's holding pen: the broken lamp, the unopened box from the last move, the bag of things with nowhere else to go. Anything that is not office work has to walk out today, or it becomes permanent furniture.
Before you start. This is guidance for organising and cleaning a home. It is not instruction for electrical, gas, structural, or any other licensed work. Do not lift or move anything unsafe, do not mix cleaning products, and follow the label on anything you use. If a job needs a professional, that is the right answer. See the full safety notice.
