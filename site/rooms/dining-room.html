Dining Room

The dining room has one job, and it is the only room in the house that can quietly lose that job without anyone noticing. The table becomes a desk, the sideboard becomes a filing cabinet, and dinner moves to the couch. Start with the table, because a room that can seat everyone tonight is a room worth defending tomorrow.

The 5 micro zones, in working order

A micro zone is one session, not a whole day. Finish one before you start the next.

  1. The Dining Table (30-45 min)
    Seat everyone for a shared meal without having to move anything first.
  2. The Sideboard Surface (15-30 min)
    Hold serving dishes during a meal and a small, deliberate display between them.
  3. The Sideboard Storage (45-75 min)
    Hold the things that only come out when the table is being set.
  4. The China or Display Cabinet (45-75 min)
    Keep the good tableware safe, visible, and genuinely usable.
  5. The Beverage or Coffee Station (30-45 min)
    Make a drink and hand it over without crossing into the kitchen.

For this room

  • Where to start. Start with the Dining Table. It is the quickest zone in the room and the only one whose result shows up at dinner the same evening, which buys you the goodwill to open the sideboard drawers tomorrow.
  • Cleaning note. Work top down and begin with the light fixture hanging over the table, since dust falls from it straight onto the surface you eat from. Chair rungs and legs next, tabletop last, and condition bare wood a few times a year so spills bead instead of soaking in.
  • The trap. Clearing the table into the sideboard drawers is not clearing. The sideboard quietly absorbs everything the table sheds, which is why its drawers and shelves take far longer to work through than the wood sitting on top of them.
Before you start. This is guidance for organising and cleaning a home. It is not instruction for electrical, gas, structural, or any other licensed work. Do not lift or move anything unsafe, do not mix cleaning products, and follow the label on anything you use. If a job needs a professional, that is the right answer. See the full safety notice.
