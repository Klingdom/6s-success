Room

Primary Bedroom

This is the only room in the house whose job is to help you stop. Every object in it either supports sleep or competes with it, and the ones that compete arrive quietly: the charger, the chair that collects worn jeans, the box of shoes that did not fit. Start at your own nightstand, because it is small and you will see the difference from the pillow that same night, and save the closet for a morning when you are fresh.

The 6 micro zones, in working order

A micro zone is one session, not a whole day. Finish one before you start the next.

  1. The Bed and Bedding Zone (30-45 min)
    The bed and the linens on it have one job, which is to get you through the night without waking up.
  2. Your Own Nightstand (15-30 min)
    This is your side, and it holds only what you reach for between getting into bed and falling asleep.
  3. The Other Sleeper's Nightstand (15-30 min)
    This is the other sleeper's side, and it is the one zone in this room that is not yours to decide about.
  4. The Dresser Top (15-30 min)
    This is where your pockets land at the end of the day and where tomorrow's watch and rings wait for you.
  5. The Dresser Drawers (60-90 min)
    These drawers hold the folded clothes you wear this season, split into categories you can find without digging.
  6. The Primary Closet (60-90 min)
    The closet holds the clothes you actually wear this season, visible and reachable in one look.
