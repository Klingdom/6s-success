Entryway micro zone

The Bench or Console

Somewhere to sit while you lace a boot, and somewhere to set a bag down for thirty seconds.

One session: 30-45 min. most of it in Sort, because the drawer holds far more than its size suggests

What done looks like

Three items or fewer on the top, a seat with nothing on it at all, and a drawer where every divided section has one named category and you can see the base of the drawer between things.

The six passes, in order

Work them in this order. Sorting after you have arranged things means arranging things you were about to remove.

1. Sort

Decide what stays. Everything else leaves the zone now.

Tip the drawer out onto the bench. Dead batteries, pens that do not write, single earbuds, sunscreen from three summers ago and takeaway menus all go now. Anything you would not want while wearing your outdoor shoes belongs in a different room.

2. Straighten

Give what stays a home, placed where the hand actually reaches.

Divide the drawer into named sections and hold to them: dog lead and waste bags, sun cream and insect spray, a torch, one pen. Bags and the vacuum go underneath the bench, never on the seat.

3. Shine

Clean it, and use the cleaning to inspect what you cannot see when it is full.

Take the drawer fully out and tip the sand and crumbs out of its corners. Wipe the seat itself, which collects the same grit as the floor because people sit on it in outdoor clothes.

4. Safety

Make the zone safe for everybody who uses it, including the shortest person.

Fix the console to the wall if it is taller than it is deep, because children climb open drawer fronts like a ladder. Move the box cutter and the scissors out of the shallow drawer that opens at toddler head height.

5. Standardize

Write down the best way you know today, so it survives you forgetting.

Write the three permitted top items on a card and tape it inside the drawer, so the cap survives everyone who did not read this book.

6. Sustain

Attach the reset to something that already happens, so it keeps itself.

When you clear the dinner table, carry the plates past the bench and take the top back down to three on the way through.

What the drawer is for

This drawer becomes the second junk drawer within a month of being emptied. It happens every time, because it is the nearest place to set down something you do not want to carry any further. The decision being avoided is not what to throw away. It is admitting the drawer needs a job at all. The rule: this drawer serves the door and nothing else. One test question per item, would you need this while wearing your shoes, either on the way out or in the first ten seconds back in. Dog lead, yes. Sun cream, yes. Spare car charger, yes. The screwdriver, the birthday candles and the case for a phone you no longer own all fail, and they fail even though they genuinely have nowhere better to live. Find them somewhere better today, in another room, with a name on it. If you do not, they will be back in this drawer by the weekend and so will four of their friends.

Check these before you start

  • Fall, cut, or crush A narrow console with drawers is a climbing frame to a three year old, and it comes over forwards with the drawers out. Strap it to the wall and keep the heaviest contents in the bottom drawer.
  • Poison, choke, or strangle Loose button batteries, coins and small magnets roll into the back corners of this drawer and it opens at exactly the height a toddler can reach. Batteries go in a lidded tin or out of the room entirely.

Cleaning it properly

Take the drawer fully out and tip the sand and crumbs from its corners, then wipe the seat, which collects the same grit as the floor because people sit on it in outdoor clothes. Work top down, back to front.

Inspect as you clean. Cleaning is the only time you see the zone empty, so use it.

  • A console that rocks or tips toward you when you lean on the top, the sign it is top-heavy and not yet held to the wall. Flag it.
  • A drawer runner screw backing out so the drawer drops or drags when it is pulled open. Note it.
  • A hairline split opening in the seat, or a splinter lifting on a wooden bench. Flag it before someone sits on it.
  • A wobble in a leg joint or a cracked or seized caster. Note it.
  • A dark water ring or swelling on the top where a wet umbrella or bag keeps landing. Flag it.

The standard that keeps it fixed

Three items on the top, an empty seat, and every drawer section holding one named category.

Reset trigger. You already open this drawer to put the dog lead back after a walk. While it is open, glance at the top and take it back to three.

Before you start. This is guidance for organising and cleaning a home. It is not instruction for electrical, gas, structural, or any other licensed work. Do not lift or move anything unsafe, do not mix cleaning products, and follow the label on anything you use. If a job needs a professional, that is the right answer. See the full safety notice.
