How to clean anything in your house, surface by surface

Every surface in the house, with the method for cleaning it: 749 of them, grouped by the room and the micro zone they belong to. Each one links straight to its own method, which names the product, the order of work and the reason for it.

Two rules run through all of it. Work down, not across, because whatever comes off a high surface lands on the one below. And sort before you shine, because cleaning around something that should not be there is cleaning you will do again next week.
