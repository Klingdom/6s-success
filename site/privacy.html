Privacy

Last updated 19 August 2026

Draft pending professional review. These terms were written to describe accurately what this site actually does today. They have not been reviewed by a lawyer, and they are not legal advice. If anything here conflicts with your rights under the law where you live, the law wins.

This page describes what happens to information when you use 6s-success.com. It is short because this site collects very little.

What we count

We count visits, so we can tell which pages are useful and which are not. This is done with self-hosted software running on our own server, not a third-party analytics service, so your visit is never handed to another company.

What is recorded for a page view: the page address, the site that referred you, and coarse details your browser announces anyway, such as country, browser and whether you are on a phone or a computer. No cookies are set, and no identifier is stored on your device that would let us recognise you on a later visit. We cannot build a profile of an individual and we do not try to.

What we never do

There is no Google Analytics, no Meta pixel, no advertising network, no session recording, and no behavioural profiling. Nothing here follows you to other websites. We do not sell, rent, or share personal information.

Third-party requests

The site makes no requests to third-party servers. Fonts, styles, scripts, images and the visit counter are all served from this domain, so browsing here does not reveal your visit or your IP address to any other company.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies. There is no cookie banner because there is nothing to consent to.

Your shopping cart

If you add something to the cart, that list is saved in your own browser using localStorage, under the key sixs_cart_v2. It never leaves your device and is never transmitted to us. Clearing your browser storage deletes it permanently, and we have no copy.

Forms

The contact and newsletter forms on this site are not yet connected. Anything typed into them is discarded by your own browser and is never sent or stored anywhere. This is stated on the contact page too. Until that changes, the only way to reach us is by email.

Email

If you email support@6s-success.com, we receive your address and whatever you write, held in our email provider's systems and used only to reply to you.

Server logs

The web server may keep ordinary technical logs, which can include IP address, the page requested, and the time. These are standard for any web server, are used for security and troubleshooting, and are not combined with anything else or used to build a profile of you.

Children

This site is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this site starts collecting anything, whether an email list, an account system, or payments, this page will be updated before that goes live, and the date at the top will change.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@6s-success.com.
