How it works

A reset in four steps

Every engagement follows the same arc, whether it is one video call or a full day on site. You always leave with a plan you can keep.

1

Walkthrough

We tour the space, in person or on camera, and name what is fighting you. You leave the first session with priorities, not homework.

2

Reset with you

We run Sort, Straighten, and Shine on the chosen area side by side, so the method sticks in your hands, not just on paper.

3

Standardize

We add the safety fixes, then set the visual controls and labels that hold the new order in place. A home for everything, marked so it is obvious.

4

Keeping plan

You get a written standard and a simple rhythm to sustain it, so the reset does not quietly undo itself two weeks later.
