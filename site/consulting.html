Virtual Home Consult

One hour on video · $250

Available anywhere with a phone signal. You carry the camera round the room, and we work out together what that space is supposed to do, what is stopping it, and which micro zone to fix first.

What happens

Before
We email you to arrange a time and ask three things: which room, who else uses it, and what a good day in there would look like.
The hour
A live walkthrough. The room first, then zone by zone in the one that matters most. We name the desired function, the friction, and the root cause underneath it, in that order, because a bin bought before the root cause is known is usually the wrong bin.
After
A written plan by email within two working days: the zones in the order to work them, the standard each one should hold when it is done, and a product list of what would genuinely help.

What happens

Before
We confirm your address and a date before anything is booked, and agree which area we are doing. If we cannot reach you, nothing is charged.
The day
Walkthrough, then Sort, Straighten and Shine on the chosen area side by side, so the method ends up in your hands and not just on paper. Then the safety pass: anchors, alarms, clear paths, anything sharp or heavy in the wrong place.

Choosing

Which one you actually need

Most people need the cheaper one, and it is worth saying so plainly, because the expensive one is only the better answer in a narrow set of cases.

Start with the $250 hour if

You are willing to do the work but do not know where to start, or you have started three times and it keeps coming back. Knowing the order and the root cause is usually the whole problem, and an hour is enough to find both.

Book the $1,200 day if

The area has beaten you more than once, there is more of it than one person can face alone, or a date is doing the deciding: a move, a downsize, a new baby, a parent moving in. And you are in the Treasure Valley.

Book neither, yet, if

You have not tried the free version. The Home Quest takes one micro zone in about fifteen minutes and costs nothing, no account and no email. If that works in your house you may not need us at all, and we would rather you found that out first.
