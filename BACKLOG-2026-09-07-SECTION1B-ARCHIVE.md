**Why this is recorded rather than acted on.** 37 queries out of 2,827 is about 1.3% of measured demand, and the cost is not one article. The 20 rooms are the product's own definition: `content/manual/source/content.json` drives 114 zone pages, 20 room pages, 20 decks, the 50-chapter book, the Micro Zone Manual and the app corpus. Adding a room means authoring its zones, its diagnosis layer, its deck and its chapter, then re-deriving everything downstream. That is a product-scope decision and an owner one (`CLAUDE.md` section 53), not something to start because a keyword table said 37.

**The honest counter-argument, for whoever revisits this.** A basement and an attic are where the rest of the house's overflow ends up, which is the thesis of the Garage chapter already written ("what every other room evicts"), so the method transfers and the authoring would not start from nothing. If arrivals ever reach a level where 1.3% of demand is worth two rooms of work, this is the first place to look. Today arrivals are 7 to 14 a week, so it is not.
