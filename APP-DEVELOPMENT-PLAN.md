## 2. The honest gap

The app does one thing well: draw a card, do the pass, mark it done, advance
through the six S passes, finish a zone, start the next. That is the entire
product loop and it works.

It does not yet have: accounts, household or family play, photos, progress sync
with the web Quest, notifications, purchases, or a store presence. Two of those
(accounts and purchases) are the gate on roughly half the revenue plan.

## 3. Principles for this build

1. **Offline first is not negotiable.** The terrain is a garage, a basement, a
   closet. The app must work with airplane mode on. This is why there are zero
   network calls in the source today and why any sync feature must be additive.
2. **The corpus is derived, never copied.** The website is the single source of
   content truth. `build_mobile_corpus.py` regenerates and records the source
   hash so drift is detectable rather than discovered.
3. **Ship the loop, not the feature list.** Every addition must survive the
   question: does this help somebody finish a real zone in their real home?
4. **Accessibility is part of done,** not a later pass. Contrast, target size,
   Dynamic Type and screen reader announcement are checked before a phase closes.
5. **No dark patterns.** No streak anxiety, no fake scarcity, no engagement
   metrics that do not correspond to a room actually improving.

## 4. Phased plan

### Phase 0: Prove it on a device (blocked on Phil, 20 minutes)

Everything else is speculation until this is done.

- Run the 16 checks in `mobile/quest-app/ON-DEVICE-TEST.md` (12 core, plus 4
  more if there is time).
- Scan the Expo QR, confirm the loop, persistence, offline, screen reader,
  Dynamic Type, and the web import.
- **Exit criterion:** 16 of 16 pass, or a written list of what did not.

**Why it is first:** every claim in section 1 that says "from source" becomes
either confirmed or a defect list. A failure here is worth more than a pass.

### Phase 1: Make it releasable (2 to 3 cycles, unblocked except the accounts)

- App icon, splash, and store screenshots generated from the real UI.
- `app.json` completed: bundle identifiers, versioning, permissions, privacy
  strings.
- EAS build configuration; produce a signed internal build for both platforms.
- Crash and error reporting that respects the privacy stance (no household
  content leaves the device).
- Privacy policy and data disclosure written to match what the app truly does,
  which today is: stores progress on your device, sends nothing anywhere.
- **Gate:** Apple Developer Program ($99/yr) and Google Play ($25 once). Only
  Phil can create these. Everything up to them will be ready.

### Phase 2: Progress that survives a phone (2 to 3 cycles)

- Export and import already work one way. Make it round trip and obvious.
- Local backup file, restore, and a visible "last backed up" state.
- **Deliberately no account yet.** Sync without accounts, via file, is honest
  and shippable and does not block the store.

### Phase 3: Accounts and household play (gate: Phil's call)

This is the largest single unlock and it is a decision, not a task. It enables
household play, cross-device sync, and 6S Plus. It also introduces the first
customer data we would hold, so it changes our privacy and security posture.

- Recommendation: passwordless email link, minimal profile, household as a
  shared code rather than a social graph.
- Prepare: schema, threat model, data retention policy, deletion path.
- **Do not build until Phil decides**, because the wrong choice here is
  expensive to reverse.

### Phase 4: Purchases (gate: store accounts, follows Phase 1 and 3)

- Store in-app purchase for the digital products already sold on the web.
- Restore purchases, receipt validation, and the entitlement model.
- Note: Apple and Google take 15 to 30 percent. The web checkout does not.
  Pricing must account for this rather than absorbing it silently.

### Phase 5: The camera loop (2 to 4 cycles)

Before and after photography is the single most motivating feature in this
category, and it is also the highest privacy risk.

- On-device only by default. Photos never leave the phone unless the person
  explicitly shares them.
- Paired before and after per zone, with the six-S signature.
- **This is the feature most likely to make the app worth reopening.**

### Phase 6: Notifications, carefully

- Only for sustain: a zone's standard is due for a check.
- Never streaks, never guilt, never "you have not opened the app".
- Opt in, with a plain explanation of what will be sent.

## 5. Sequencing rationale

Phase 0 first because it converts assumptions into facts. Phase 1 next because a
buildable, listable app is the precondition for every revenue path. Phase 2
before accounts because it delivers most of the value of sync at none of the
risk. Phase 3 is deliberately a decision point, not a sprint. Phases 4 to 6 are
ordered by revenue and retention impact against risk.

## 7. Definition of done for the app

The app is done for v1 when a person can, entirely offline: open it, draw a
card, follow it, finish a zone, see their progress the next day, and repeat
without confusion. Everything after that is improvement, not completion.
