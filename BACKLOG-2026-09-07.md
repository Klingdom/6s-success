## 7. What I would tell you if you only read one line

The site is technically finished and commercially unproven. Six independent
reviews this week found no significant technical debt and eleven separate places
where **the source was corrected and the shipped artifact was never re-derived
from it**. That is now the dominant defect class, it is what the gates are being
rebuilt around, and it is more dangerous than any missing feature, because every
one of those defects passed a check that reported green.
