## 1. Amazon Associates

**Exists, unchanged name, in-house (not on any network).** Apply at https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/.

**THE 180-DAY RULE — this is the critical finding.**
From Amazon's own help page https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/G8TW5AE9XL2VX9VM:

> the Associates team reviews applications "once you've driven qualified sales (we require at least three within the first 180 days)" — "personal orders do not qualify."

And from https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/G7MJTPEP9NC3YKMG:

> "Once you've applied, you have 180 days to refer a sale through one of your Associates links."
> "Your application can be withdrawn if there were not enough qualifying sales for 180 days after your application was submitted."
> "We're unable to reinstate your account or Associates ID after it's been rejected."

At 3–4 visitors/day the site gets roughly 550–720 sessions in 180 days. Getting 3 *purchases* out of that requires a click-through-to-purchase rate that a brand-new site with no commercial intent traffic essentially never hits. **Applying today most likely burns the account.** Reapplication is possible but starts from scratch and the old Associates ID is gone.

**Site eligibility (same help page):** "robust original content" with "at least 10 posts"; website content "must be recent (generally within the last 60 days)"; social pages must be "established, with a substantive number of organic followers/likes (in most cases, at least 500)". 6s-success.com clears the content bar easily; the risk is entirely the sales bar.

**Attribution** (from https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies): a Session ends when "24 hours elapse from that click", an order is placed, or a different Special Link is clicked. Cart items purchased "no later than 89 days after their initial click-through" still qualify.

**Commission rates** (https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/GRXPHT8U84RAYDXZ, Associates Program Standard Commission Income Statement):
- Physical Books, Kitchen, Automotive — **4.50%**
- Home Improvement, Lawn & Garden, Pets Products — **3.00%**
- Furniture, Home — **3.00%**
- Luxury Beauty — 10.00%
- 0%: Gift Cards, Wireless Service Plans, Alcoholic Beverages, and others.

**PDF/ebook restriction — hard prohibition.** From the Program Policies:

> "You will not engage in any Promotional Activities in any offline manner, including by using any of our or our affiliates' trademarks or logos (including any Amazon Mark), any Program Content, or any Special Link in connection with any printed material, ebook, mailing, or any oral solicitation."

Email is the one carve-out: "You may include Special Links in emails, SMS and direct messaging from your social media Sites; provided, that such communications are solicited (i.e., opted into by the receiving customer)."

**This kills the primary use case.** The 6S book (EPUB/KDP/print) and the downloadable PDFs cannot carry Amazon links at all. Amazon links can only live on the website.

**Required disclosure** (Operating Agreement §5, https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/agreement):

> "As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases."

Also required when displaying Amazon prices: a date/time stamp adjacent to the price, plus the verbatim disclaimers "CERTAIN CONTENT THAT APPEARS [IN THIS APPLICATION or ON THIS SITE, as applicable] COMES FROM AMAZON. THIS CONTENT IS PROVIDED 'AS IS' AND IS SUBJECT TO CHANGE OR REMOVAL AT ANY TIME." and "Product prices and availability are accurate as of the date/time indicated and are subject to change…"

**Payout** (verified: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/GP8Z3AZ27ZFHTEUL): direct deposit and gift certificate — "If we owe you less than **$10**, your commissions will be rolled into the next month's total." Check — minimum **$100**, and Associates may set a higher personal threshold. A check processing fee exists (Amazon links a "Check Fee" topic) but the amount was not visible on that page; the commonly cited $15 is `[SECONDARY]`.

**Verdict: LIKELY REJECTED (auto-closed at 180 days).** Do not apply until the site can plausibly produce three purchases inside six months. Rough gate: sustained 100+ visitors/day on buying-intent pages, or an email list actively promoted to.

## 2. Lowe's — Lowe's Creator Network

**Exists and is new.** Lowe's launched the "Lowe's Creator Network" on 2025-06-10 (verified: corporate.lowes.com press release, "Lowe's Taps Creator Economy to Launch First Home Improvement Creator Network, With MrBeast Among the First to Join"). The press release states the network is "free to join", has "a multi-tiered system built to support creators at every stage of their journey", had "over 17,000 creators enrolled during its beta", and directs applicants to **Lowes.com/Creator**.

- **Landing page:** https://www.lowes.com/l/creator and https://www.lowes.com/l/creator/joinlowescreator
- **Application:** https://connectwith.lowes.com/connect (the "Lowe's Creator Community Portal")

**Network — inferred, not stated.** Lowes.com blocks automated fetching (403), so the program page could not be read directly. However live Lowe's creator storefront URLs indexed by search carry `irclickid=…&irgwc=1&cm_mmc=aff-…` query parameters. `irclickid` and `irgwc` are **Impact Radius** tracking parameters. That is strong evidence the Creator Network tracks on **Impact**. Separately, the older Lowe's affiliate program has historically run on **CJ Affiliate**, and Lowe's is also reachable through **Sovrn Commerce** (verified: https://www.sovrn.com/lowes-affiliate-program-sovrn-commerce/ lists Lowe's as an available merchant).

**Rates / cookie / payout — `[SECONDARY]` only.** Multiple affiliate directories state "up to 20%" commission, a **30-day** attribution window, and $50 minimum payout by PayPal or direct deposit. These match each other but I could not read them off a Lowe's page. **Do not quote these to anyone as fact.**

**Eligibility:** described as open to "US-based content creators, influencers and bloggers with an online following" `[SECONDARY]`. No numeric follower or traffic minimum found anywhere.

**PDF/ebook policy:** `[UNVERIFIED]`. Nothing found either way. One directory states links may be shared "across social channels and web blogs", which implies web placement is the expected channel.

**Verdict: UNCERTAIN — but the best risk/reward of the ten.** A newly launched network that enrolled 17,000 creators in beta is in acquisition mode, not filtering mode. The stated eligibility is qualitative ("an online following"), not numeric. 6s-success.com is squarely on-topic for home improvement. Downside of rejection is low because no bar-on-reapplication rule is published.

## 3. Target — Target Partners

**Exists. Called "Target Partners" (the Target Affiliate Program).** Home page verified: https://partners.target.com/

**Network: Impact.** Verified from the Target Partners home page itself: "Quick sign-up, opportunities for commission & easy tracking links through **Impact**".

**Commission:** the home page states "**Earn up to 8%**" on qualifying purchases. Target does **not** publish a per-category rate table publicly — the terms say rates are "published on affiliate.target.com" and direct you to "your Insertion Order/Contract for your specific rates and exclusions." The FAQ (https://partners.target.com/faqs) instead publishes an EPC:

> "Target's E.P.C is approximately $3-$4. This means that the average payout for every 100 clicks is $3-$4."

Note that is materially lower than the $12–13/100 clicks some third-party blogs quote. **Trust the $3–4 figure — it is on Target's own FAQ.**

**Cookie:** verified on the FAQ — "The cookie length is **7 days**. You get credit for any order within 7 days of the original click placed by the customer," using "last-click attribution methodology."

**Eligibility:** verified on the FAQ — eligible applicants are "any website owner, blogger or creator network that runs a **family-friendly website** and has **mainly US-based viewership**." International applicants are accepted "as long as Impact Radius supports your country of residence and your content is directed toward the U.S. market." **No traffic or audience minimum is published.**

**Payout:** the T&Cs (https://partners.target.com/termsandconditions) state "All Affiliates must enroll in the Target Vendor's payment program which is operated by an independent third-party payment processor," paid monthly. **No threshold is stated** on any public page.

**Email / PDF restriction:** the T&Cs state "You may only include Licensed Materials in emails and newsletters that are approved in advance by us" and prohibit use "in direct mail or email without first submitting a sample of such use to us and obtaining our prior written approval." **PDFs, ebooks and downloadable documents are not addressed either way** — that silence is not permission; ask the program manager (targetpartners@target.com) before putting Target links in the book.

**Required disclosure — Target specifies exact wording:**

> "[INSERT YOUR NAME] is a participant in the Target Affiliate Marketing Program, an affiliate advertising and marketing program that pays commissions to affiliates, like me, that advertise and link to [INSERT APPLICABLE TARGET DOMAIN NAME] if you buy products from Target through my link."

For space-constrained social posts, Target accepts the hashtags **#PaidLink** and **#TargetPartner** at the beginning of the post.

**Verdict: UNCERTAIN, leaning ACCEPT.** Target's published criteria are qualitative — family-friendly, US audience, real website. 6s-success.com meets all three. Impact-run programs generally approve content sites without traffic gates. The realistic outcome is approval with negligible revenue (7-day cookie, ~$3–4 per 100 clicks means ~10 clicks/month at current traffic ≈ $0.35/month). Apply for the account and the Impact relationship, not for the money.

## 4. Walmart — two separate programs

Walmart runs **two distinct programs**, and it matters which one you apply to.

### 4a. Walmart Creator — https://creator.walmart.com/

**This one has a hard follower gate and 6s-success.com probably fails it.**
From Walmart Creator's own terms (https://creator.walmart.com/terms), verbatim requirements:

> "(1) be of majority age in the jurisdiction you reside, which is at least 18 years or older in most states; (2) reside in the United States"
> "have a **minimum of 1,000 followers across your connected social media accounts**"

Also: must maintain an active Walmart.com account, cannot be a Walmart employee. Payment is facilitated by "the Impact Radius Platform" and "you may only use a U.S. Bank account for Commission payout purposes" — a foreign account triggers termination and forfeiture of all earnings.

Commission rates and the exact attribution window are **behind the creator login and not published**. Third-party sources claim "up to 18%" and a 14-day window; both are `[SECONDARY]` and Walmart reserves the right to change rates.

Link restrictions: the terms prohibit posting qualifying links via "unsolicited electronic communications (spam/email)", shortened/obscured links, unauthorized ad networks, and trademark keyword bidding. **PDFs and ebooks are not mentioned.**

**Verdict: LIKELY REJECTED** unless there are 1,000+ followers across connected social accounts. This is a stated, numeric, checkable requirement — not a judgement call. Do not apply until it is met.

### 4b. Walmart.com Affiliate Program (classic) — https://affiliates.walmart.com/

**Network: Impact Radius, verified on Walmart's own FAQ** (https://affiliates.walmart.com/faqs):

> "Walmart.com uses Impact Radius as our Affiliate Service Provider."

**Commission:** the site states "up to 4%". No public per-category table.

**Eligibility (verbatim from the FAQ):**

> "We review each application carefully and determine eligibility at our sole discretion. We reserve the right to reject or remove sites at any time, for any reason."

No traffic minimum is published. The landing page claims "The fastest approval process. Sign up and get started within a day."

**Payment (from the affiliate agreement, https://affiliates.walmart.com/terms):**

> "Approximately ninety (90) days following the end of each calendar month, you will receive a check for the Commissions earned on Qualifying Products that were shipped during that month"

No minimum payout threshold is stated. Note the ~90-day lag.

**Cookie window:** the agreement refers only to "the relevant cookie window … ('Referral Window')" without a number. **`[UNVERIFIED]`.**

**Email/PDF:** Section 3(b) "Prohibitions Regarding Electronic Communications" bars generating or using any Electronic Communication containing Walmart trademarks or "any of the Qualifying Links or URLS provided to you" without "express written permission." **That language is broad enough to cover a distributed PDF or ebook — treat Walmart links in the book as prohibited unless you get written permission.**

**Required disclosure — Walmart specifies wording:**

> "You agree that when you display Qualifying Links on the Affiliate Website there will be a clear and conspicuous disclosure that it is an advertisement placed next to the Qualifying Links so the disclosure is unavoidable."

and for endorsements, they must be "clearly and conspicuously identified and labeled as **'#WalmartPartner'** or **'paid link'**."

**Verdict for 4b: UNCERTAIN, leaning ACCEPT.** Approval is discretionary and unstated, but the classic program historically approves content sites quickly and has no published gate. The 90-day payment lag and 4% cap make it low-value near term.
