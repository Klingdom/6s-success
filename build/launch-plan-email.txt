THE SITE CANNOT TAKE MONEY RIGHT NOW.

All six payment links on the live site are deactivated in Stripe. Anyone
who has clicked buy in the last few days reached a dead link. I confirmed
it three ways: the live pages, the Stripe API's active flag, and a second
independent check.

The cause is that production is serving a build from before the links were
regenerated. The repository's links are all active. So one Redeploy in
Hostinger Docker Manager fixes it, and that is the single highest value
action available to this business today.

Why nothing caught it: every check that existed was true. The pages return
200. The dead payment links return 200 as well, because a deactivated
Stripe link serves the same JavaScript as a working one and only says "no
longer active" once your browser runs it. Our own checks looked at the
repository, where the links are correct, which is exactly what made it
invisible. There is now a check that asks Stripe whether the links the
LIVE site serves are active, and it runs on every preflight.

WHAT I FIXED TODAY, BEFORE ANY OF THIS SHIPS

Eight specialists audited the site. Several things would have been wrong
the moment you deployed:

  - Terms said six things are for sale and "everything else is not for
    sale yet". 155 products are for sale. Deploying would have published
    terms denying the catalogue exists.
  - Every surface promised delivery "within the hour". Measured across
    the last 40 fulfilment runs: median 85 minutes, longest 12.4 hours,
    and 40% of intervals over an hour. The promise now matches reality.
  - The privacy page's only mention of payments was a promise to update
    it before payments went live. They went live weeks ago.
  - The Quest's only button did nothing at all for anyone running an ad
    blocker, and the finish screen carrying the $19 offer never rendered.
    Two unguarded analytics calls.
  - Nothing validated the nginx config before shipping. A syntax error
    there does not slow the site down, it stops the container starting,
    which would have taken production down on the very deploy meant to
    bring it back.
