GOOGLE BUSINESS PROFILE: WHAT IS READY, AND THE STEP THAT IS YOURS

Short version. I drafted everything a Google Business Profile listing needs,
but I cannot create or verify the listing itself. It goes under your name and
needs a phone number that does not exist anywhere in the written record for
this business yet, so completing it is your step, not mine. This is the same
category of action as backlog 3.8's directory listings, which were researched
and deliberately not executed for the same reason: creating an account under
the business's identity on a third-party platform is a visible, externally
facing action taken on your behalf.

THE GAP THAT BLOCKS IT

No business phone number exists in this repository, on the live site, in
STRIPE.md, or in any operating document. Google Business Profile requires
one for a service-area business (it is how a listing gets verified and how a
customer calls). Until a number exists, this cannot go live, whatever else
is ready.

Verified before writing this: grepped the whole site and every top-level
markdown file for a phone number. contact.html has no phone field, only
email and a message form. STRIPE.md documents that each Stripe payment link
collects a customer's phone number at checkout, but that is not the same
thing as the business having one to publish.

WHAT TO DO WITH THIS FILE

1. Get a phone number. A Google Voice number tied to your own Google account
   works for verification and costs nothing; a real forwarding line works
   too if you would rather calls ring somewhere specific.
2. Go to google.com/business, sign in with the account you want to own this
   listing (not a shared or throwaway one, since ownership transfers are a
   hassle later), and start a new profile.
3. Paste the fields below. Everything here is drawn from what the site
   already says publicly, nothing invented for this listing.
4. Verify however Google offers for a service-area business without a public
   storefront: usually a phone call or text to the number from step 1, since
   there is no retail address for a postcard.
5. Once it is live, send me the listing URL (or just say "GBP is live") and
   the next operator cycle links it from consulting.html and records it done
   in BACKLOG-2026-H2.md, backlog item 3B.2.

Note: this does not by itself authorize the local demand test in 3B.1, which
is a separate, explicit budget-and-stop-date decision. A live listing is
infrastructure either way and costs nothing to have; running paid local
acquisition against it still needs your sign-off on 3B.1 first.
