REFERRAL PARTNER OUTREACH: WHAT IS READY, AND THE STEPS THAT ARE YOURS

Short version. This drafts backlog item 3B.3: message templates for the
three partner categories the backlog names (real estate agents, senior
move managers, professional organizers), plus a log to record real
contacts and responses. I cannot make the contacts myself. The backlog's
own owner column already says why: "Phil makes contact, operator drafts."
A cold outreach message to a named business or licensed professional is a
visible, externally facing action taken under your name, the same category
of action already declined to do alone for the Google Business Profile
listing (3B.2) and the directory submissions (3.8).

TWO THINGS TO DECIDE BEFORE SENDING ANYTHING

1. Compensation. Nothing below offers a referral fee, a discount, or any
   other consideration for a referral, on purpose. Paying real estate
   agents for client referrals can run into state real estate licensing
   rules even though this is not a settlement service, and offering senior
   move managers or professional organizers a kickback changes the
   relationship from "trusted colleague" to "paid lead source" in a way
   that can read badly to their own clients. If you want to offer
   something, decide what and check whether it needs disclosure, then the
   templates below are easy to add a line to. Until then they ask for
   nothing but a conversation.

2. Where to send them once they say yes. Right now that is
   consulting.html and a request-a-quote form, both live. The Google
   Business Profile listing (3B.2) is not live yet; once it is, add its
   link to these templates so a partner has somewhere with reviews and
   verified contact details to point their own clients at, not just a
   single page on a new domain.

WHY THESE THREE CATEGORIES

Read straight from what consulting.html already says about who the
service is for, not invented for this outreach:

- Senior move managers. Direct overlap with "Moves and downsizing," the
  service's own second named use case. A move manager's whole job is
  deciding what earns the truck; a reset day that runs Sort first is the
  same job with different tools. This is the strongest fit of the three.
- Real estate agents. Overlaps "Moves and downsizing" (a listing is a
  move) and "Overwhelmed homes" (pre-listing declutter is the same
  problem consulting.html already describes). An agent whose seller is
  stuck on where to start is exactly who "Book a consult" is for.
- Professional organizers. The trickiest of the three, because they can
  read this as a competitor rather than a referral partner. The honest
  position: 6S Success is a book, cards, and a consulting arm built on a
  Lean Six Sigma method, not a home organizing business competing for the
  same clients on price or availability. Some organizers will want
  nothing to do with that positioning regardless. Send this one carefully,
  and only to organizers whose own public material already signals they
  are open to method or training partnerships, not a blanket list.

WHAT THE MESSAGE DOES NOT CLAIM

No customer count, no star rating, no "trusted by," no before/after
result. The one sale this business has taken was a personal referral from
Phil, not a stranger, so there is no customer outcome to cite yet and the
templates do not invent one. What they do cite is checkable: the method's
origin (Philip Kling, twenty years in Lean Six Sigma, Master Black Belt,
the same background site/about.html already states publicly) and the
service itself (In-Home Reset Day and Virtual Home Consult, both live,
both bookable). If a partner asks "who have you done this for," the honest
answer today is "we are just getting started taking outside clients," not
a dodge and not a fabricated case study.

SERVICE AREA, SO THE MESSAGE NEVER OVERSTATES REACH

In-Home Reset Day: Boise, Meridian, Eagle, Nampa, Kuna, Star, and Garden
City, Idaho, copied verbatim from consulting.html so the message can never

HOW TO USE THESE

1. Find 20 to 30 real local contacts (senior move managers and organizers
   often list on NASMM and NAPO's own directories; agents through local
   brokerages or Zillow agent profiles). Real people and businesses only,
   never a purchased or scraped list.
2. Personalize [Name] and, where it fits naturally, one line referencing
   their actual specialty or service area. A form letter with no
   personalization reads as spam and gets treated as spam.
3. Send from your own email or LinkedIn, not a shared or automated
   address, since this is relationship building, not a campaign.
4. Log every contact and response in
   build/referral-partner-outreach-log.csv, whatever the outcome. A
   partnership that produces zero conversations after 20 to 30 real
   contacts is itself the answer this backlog item asks for.

This does not authorize or require the paid local demand test in 3B.1,
which is a separate budget-and-stop-date decision. Referral outreach costs
only time and produces its own signal independent of any ad spend.
