AFFILIATE PROGRAMMES: WHAT I DID, WHAT I FOUND, AND THE FIVE MINUTES THAT ARE YOURS

Short version. I could not open the accounts, and that is not a limitation I
can work around: those accounts carry your legal identity, tax details and

primary sourced, the link machinery is built and gated, and the legal pages

Two of your top ten would actively cost you something if you applied today,
and one of them is the one ranked first.


DO NOT APPLY TO AMAZON YET. THIS IS THE IMPORTANT ONE.

From Amazon's own help page, not a blog: they require at least three
qualifying sales within the first 180 days, the application is withdrawn
otherwise, and "We're unable to reinstate your account or Associates ID after
it's been rejected."

At three to four visitors a day, 180 days is roughly 550 to 720 sessions in
total. The expected number of Amazon orders across that window is about 0.25,
which puts the probability of reaching three at roughly one quarter of one
percent. Applying now does not risk a rejection you can retry. It most likely
burns the Associates ID permanently.

Amazon also expressly prohibits affiliate links in printed material, ebooks
and mailings, so the book cannot carry them under any circumstances.

Apply when the site is at about thirteen visitors a day, which is four times
current traffic.


DO NOT APPLY TO WAYFAIR AT ALL, FOR NOW.

Wayfair's Creator terms forbid links on websites, in email, or in any offline
medium. You would be applying to a programme you are not permitted to use.


WALMART CREATOR AND HOME DEPOT CREATOR WILL REJECT YOU TODAY.

Walmart Creator has a hard minimum of 1,000 followers across connected social
accounts. Home Depot Creator is social-first intake. Home Depot's rate is the
best of the ten at 8 percent on everything with a 14 day cookie, so it is
worth returning to once there is a social account to connect.


THE CONTAINER STORE: HOLD DELIBERATELY, NOT BECAUSE IT IS WEAK.

Their own page says they work with "a limited number of high-quality sites"
and review each one. It is the best category match of all ten. That is exactly
why it should not be spent on a first impression made at three visitors a day.

WHAT THE PROGRAMME IS ACTUALLY WORTH, HONESTLY

Ninety days, at current traffic: about forty cents. Most likely zero.

That is not a reason to skip it. It is a reason to spend an hour on it rather
than a week, and to protect the accounts that have a probation clause. It
compounds with any traffic that arrives later, and the work is done once.

One finding changed the plan. Every one of the 115 zone pages already carries
the nineteen dollar Print Pack, which nets about $17.90 at roughly 97 percent
margin. An affiliate conversion nets about $1.58. So putting retailer links on
the zone pages, which is the obvious move because they are the highest intent
pages on the site, would lose roughly sixteen dollars every time it moved a
click. The links go on one new kit page instead.


WHAT IS ALREADY BUILT

The 123 product catalogue is in the repository, tiered, with eight universal
products that between them are called for by 109 to 114 of the 114 micro
zones. Those eight are the first release.

The link layer refuses to emit a link for a programme that is not approved. It
returns nothing rather than a plain retailer URL, because an untracked link
earns nothing and looks identical to a working one.

The compliance gate has a contract behind it rather than a preference. It
reads inside EPUB and PDF containers, which matters more than it sounds: a
plain text read of your book finds zero web links and the decompressed entries
hold 313, so the first version of this check looked at the flagship product
and reported it clean. It now scans all 310 documents a customer can actually
receive, and it runs immediately before the fulfilment job sends anything.

The terms page is rewritten: which pages carry links, that we are not the
seller, that the retailer handles returns and warranties and our refund terms
do not extend to them, that shown prices are indications rather than offers,
and that a commission never decides a recommendation.

I also found and fixed my own error. The disclosure printed "We are not paid
by any retailer to feature a product" on any page without an Amazon link,
which is nine programmes out of ten, directly beneath a sentence saying we may
earn a commission. A false denial is worse than no disclosure.
