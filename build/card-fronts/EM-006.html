Objective

Keep coats off the floor, easy to grab, and ready to wear. Reduce clutter and moisture while protecting your space.

Quick win

(30 SEC) Hang up any coats on the floor or draped over furniture.

Real world action

Look at yesterday's coats. Are they easy to grab today? Adjust hook height or spacing if needed.

The 6S lesson

A place for every coat. A coat that can breathe. A system that supports tomorrow.

Pro tip

Hooks at two heights work best: adults upper, kids lower. Leave space between coats for airflow.
