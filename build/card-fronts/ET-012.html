Objective

Create a dedicated location where electronics are charged, organized, and ready.

Quick win

(30 SEC) Gather cables and place your most-used devices on one charging station today.

Real world action

Install your charging station near the Landing Zone. Plug devices in as part of your arrival routine so batteries are full when you leave.

The 6S lesson

Set in Order applies to electronics just as it does to physical items. Every device has a logical home where it is stored and ready.

Pro tip

Use short cables and integrated management to reduce clutter. Label ports or spots if multiple people share the station.
