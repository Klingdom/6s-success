How to use this book

This book is in two halves, and they are meant to be read in different ways.

Chapters 1 to 30 teach the method. They are written to be read in order, once, from the beginning. Each of the six S's builds on the one before it, and the worked examples run continuously through them. Read this half like a book.

Chapters 31 to 50 apply the method, one room at a time. They are written to be used, not read through. Each chapter takes one room, breaks it into its micro zones, and gives step-by-step instructions for each zone with the exact inputs you need. Find your room, and work the zones in the order given. Read this half like a manual.

You do not need to finish the first half before you start the second, but the room chapters assume you know what the six S's mean, so read at least Chapter 2 first.

The companion volumes. The 6S Home Micro Zone SOP Field Manual is the field reference for all 114 micro zones and is designed to be carried to the shelf you are working on. The workbook is where you record your own audit scores, photographs, and decisions. Neither is required to use this book.

A note on the six S's. The second S is Straighten. Some industrial versions of this method call it "Set in Order." This book uses Straighten throughout. The fourth S is Safety, which is the addition that makes this six S's rather than five.
