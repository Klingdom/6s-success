The opportunity

People want an organized, clean home. They cannot tell what to buy or where to start.

Home organization is a multi-billion-dollar market, yet the customer journey is broken into disconnected pieces: a store sells bins with no method, a cleaner arrives with no system, an app nags with no products, and a book inspires with nothing to buy. The result is a full cart, a half-finished closet, and a home that slides back within a month.

◱

The blank page

"Where do I even start?" Overwhelm is the number-one reason resets never begin, and the number-one reason they are abandoned.

⛃

The wrong cart

Shoppers over-buy duplicates and miss essentials because no one maps products to the job. Money spent, problem unsolved.

↺

The slide-back

Without a standard and a rhythm, the room drifts back. The work has to be redone, so people stop trying.

The system

6S, broken down to the smallest unit of progress: the micro-zone.

The unit that makes it buyable

A micro-zone is small enough to finish in one sitting, and specific enough to sell as a kit.

"Reset the entryway" is a project. "Reset the launch pad" is a micro-zone: a defined surface, a 15-minute reset, and a short, exact list of inputs. We have already mapped over 100 of them across 20 rooms, each with steps and a bill of materials.

The moat

Hundreds of kits. One lean catalog.

Here is the operational insight that makes this a real business and not a craft project: the products overlap. Of our 123 fully-specified product types, 88 (72%) apply to every room, anchored by 33 core products that appear in nearly every kit. So a micro-zone kit is mostly shared core plus a few zone-specific add-ons.

Market & timing

A large, fragmented market ready to be assembled.

Home organization products, cleaning supplies, professional organizing, and home cleaning services are each multi-billion-dollar markets, and no one owns the seam between method, product, and service. That seam is our category.

  • Decluttering is culture. A decade of shows, books, and creators created demand with no complete product behind it.
  • Subscription commerce is normal. Customers expect refills and guidance to arrive on a rhythm.
  • Aging-in-place & downsizing. A demographic wave needs guided, done-for-you resets at scale.
  • AI guidance is here. A phone can now coach a reset step by step, which finally closes the "how" gap.
