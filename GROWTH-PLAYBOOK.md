> The tactical companion to `GROWTH-ENGINE.md` (the operating loop) and `ROADMAP-2026-2029.md` (the strategy and arithmetic). This file answers a narrower question: given the current backlog item, what does an operator actually do. Required by `CLAUDE.md` section 56. Written 2026-08-24 from what has actually worked so far, not from theory.

## 1. Purpose and scope

`GROWTH-ENGINE.md` defines the concept of the growth loop. `ROADMAP-2026-2029.md` defines the arithmetic and the horizons. Neither tells an operator, mid-cycle, how to write the next article or judge whether a traffic tactic is worth the session. This file does.

If a tactic here conflicts with the roadmap's three load-bearing facts (the digital catalogue cannot reach the revenue target alone, no stranger has ever converted, Nova has no list to borrow), the roadmap wins and this file should be corrected.

## 2. What is actually known about what works

**Search is the only durable, unbought channel**, and it takes 12 to 18 months to compound on a new domain with no inbound links. Nothing here should be read as a way to skip that timeline.

**One sale exists, and it was a referral**, not a market signal. Do not treat it as evidence any tactic below converts a stranger. Epic 1 (measurement) has to close before any tactic's effect on conversion is more than a guess.

**Articles grounded in the site's own zone data hold up.** The pattern used for the last several published articles: pick a micro-zone with rich, unused root-cause content already sitting in `content/`; write from that zone's own passes, `the_call`, and `watch_for` fields rather than inventing new claims; keep the FAQ schema matched word for word to the visible answers; verify at both desktop and mobile widths before shipping. This produces specific, defensible content rather than generic filler, and it has passed every gate run against it so far.

**Internal link depth is already inside budget.** All 114 zone pages and 20 room pages sit at 2 clicks from home, confirmed 2026-08-24. New pages should be wired into the existing nav and sitemap by hand at publish time, not left orphaned for a later sweep.

## 4. Directories and citations: a judgment call, recorded so it is not repeatedly reopened

Backlog item 3.8 asks for "directory and citation listings, only legitimate ones." Researched 2026-08-24 and deliberately not executed this cycle, for three reasons:

1. **No verified physical location or service area exists in the written record for this business today.** Local business directories (the kind that carry the most real SEO weight) expect a real address and phone number; fabricating one, or listing an address that is not genuinely how a customer reaches the business, is exactly the kind of manufactured authority signal `CLAUDE.md` section 11 forbids.
2. **Generic "submit to 300 directories" lists skew toward exactly the low-quality, thin-content link schemes the same section warns against.** A quick survey of what is currently promoted as "free business directories 2026" turned up mostly volume-over-quality submission mills and, for the ebook-specific searches, free/pirated-ebook aggregators, which is the wrong audience for a paid product entirely.
3. **Actual submission means creating accounts under the business's identity on third-party platforms.** That is a visible, externally-facing action taken on Phil's behalf, and it is worth his awareness before it happens, even though it is not RED-tier irreversible.

If a specific, clearly-legitimate, niche-relevant directory is identified later (a professional-organizing association, a genuine curated resource list with an editor who reviews submissions rather than accepting everything), evaluate it on its own merits against these three concerns rather than treating this note as a blanket rejection.
