## 1. The arithmetic this plan is built on

The $20,000 month was stated for weeks and never divided by a price. Divided,
against the live catalogue:

| Product | Price | Orders/mo | Visitors/mo | Phil's hours |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Home Edition eBook | $18 | 1,111 | 259k to 778k | none |
| Whole House Print Pack | $19 | 1,053 | 246k to 737k | none |
| Micro Zone Manual | $29 | 690 | 161k to 483k | none |
| Complete Digital Bundle | $49 | 408 | 95k to 286k | none |
| Virtual Home Consult | $250 | 80 | 19k to 56k | 160 |
| In-Home Reset Day | $1,200 | 17 | 3.9k to 12k | 150 |

Visitor figures assume a 1 to 3 percent visit-to-checkout rate, which is
assumed, not measured, multiplied by the observed 1-in-7 checkout rate, which is
measured on a sample of seven and is therefore a coincidence with a percentage
sign. Reproduce with `python ops/revenue_model.py`.

**Three conclusions, and every decision below follows from them.**

**One. The digital catalogue cannot carry this goal.** A quarter of a million
visits a month is a mid-sized media property. A well executed niche content site
worked hard for three years plausibly reaches 30,000 to 100,000 a month. That is
a real business and it is roughly $3,000 to $8,000 a month at these prices. It
is not $20,000 and pretending otherwise wastes the three years.

**Two. Services reach the number and cost the goal.** Seventeen in-home days is
$20,400 and 150 of Phil's 160 available hours. That is not a business reaching a
target, it is a person taking a job. It only becomes a business if somebody
other than Phil delivers it.

**Three. The gap between them is where the strategy has to live**, and nothing
buildable from today's assets fills it. The $49 bundle already contains every
digital asset that exists, so a $99 tier would be the same files with a bigger
number on them.

## 3. The three horizons

### Horizon 1, now to August 2027: prove a stranger converts

The only question worth answering in year one is whether anybody who was not
told about this site by Phil will pay for anything on it. Everything else is
premature.

**Honest revenue target: $500 to $3,000 a month by month twelve.** Not $20,000.
Search takes 12 to 18 months to compound on a new domain with no links, and the
site is nine days old. A plan that puts $20,000 in year one is a plan that will
be abandoned in month four, and abandoning a compounding asset in month four is
the single most expensive mistake available here.

What has to happen:
- The funnel converts a stranger at all. Until EXP-001 answers this, nothing
  downstream is interpretable.
- Search traffic becomes measurable. Impressions before clicks, clicks before
  conversions, and 30 days of data before any conclusion.
- The email list exists and grows. Currently zero, and the signup is withdrawn
  because the shared Listmonk sends another company's branding.
- Consulting delivers on Phil's network, which is the only proven channel.

**Kill criterion.** If by August 2027 search has produced fewer than 500 visits
a month and no stranger has bought anything, the content thesis is wrong and
horizon two should not be attempted as written.

### Horizon 2, 2027 to 2028: make one bet, not three

By month twelve there will be enough evidence to choose. The three candidate
bets, in the order I would currently rank them:

**Bet A, delivered services with somebody other than Phil.** The In-Home Reset
Day already works and already has a price. It reaches the number. Its only
defect is that it consumes the founder, and that is a hiring problem rather than
a product problem. The Boise warehouse and showroom concept already sketched is
the physical version of this bet.
*Requires:* real demand, which one referral does not evidence. Test with paid
local acquisition before hiring anybody.

**Bet B, the professional and workplace market.** Nova already does workplace 6S.
A licensed workshop kit at $500 to $2,000 needs tens of customers rather than
thousands, and the delivery is a file. The one buyer so far was a Lean
consultant, which is weak evidence and the only preference anybody has expressed.
*Requires:* a sales motion that does not exist, and content for office zones
that has not been written.

**Bet C, volume content and digital.** Keep compounding the search asset and
accept it as a $3,000 to $8,000 a month business that funds the others.
*Requires:* nothing new. It is already running.

**The rule: one bet, chosen on evidence, in Q1 2028.** Running all three is how a
solo operation with an AI produces three half-built things.

### Horizon 3, 2028 to 2029: compound the bet that worked

Whichever bet was chosen, this horizon is about repetition rather than
invention: more of the thing that worked, priced better, delivered by more than
one person. $20,000 a month is reachable here and is not reachable before it.

## 3b. Decision gates

The point of a gate is that the business can be seen approaching a decision
rather than being surprised by one. Each names the evidence, the date, and what
it flips.

| Gate | Evidence | By when | Flips to |
|---|---|---|---|
| **G1** | Fewer than 500 organic visits a month AND no stranger has bought anything | Aug 2027 | Digital becomes a background asset, not a target bearing route |
| **G2** | A capped local demand test produces 3 or more paid non-referral bookings, or 1 or fewer | ~Nov 2026 | 3 or more: plan hired delivery for Q1 2027. 1 or fewer: services closed pending new evidence, and do not hire |
| **G3** | 20 to 50 direct contacts to workplace buyers produce zero qualified conversations | before any workplace content is written | Do not build the professional edition ahead of this |
| **G4** | Whichever of G1 to G3 has resolved | Q1 2028 | The formal choose-one-bet decision |
| **G5** | Neither G1 nor G2 has produced a viable path | Aug 2027 | **Re-baseline the target down** to the digital ceiling of $3,000 to $8,000 a month rather than keep restating $20,000 without evidence it is reachable |

**G5 exists on purpose.** If both the traffic route and the demand route fail
their tests, $20,000 a month is not a credible target for this business inside
this roadmap on these assets, and the honest move is to say so and re-baseline.
An honest ceiling is worth more than an optimistic plan.

**On hiring:** engaging a technician is a material financial commitment and sits
in the RED band in CLAUDE.md. It requires Phil's explicit authorisation before
any commitment, not merely before payroll. It must never follow from a single
paid booking, which is the natural impulse the moment one arrives.

## 4. What this plan refuses to do

- **No new digital tier until there is new content.** The catalogue is not short
  of products, it is short of visitors.
- **No paid acquisition until the funnel converts organically.** Buying traffic
  into an unvalidated funnel converts money into noise.
- **No second illustrated deck until the free Entryway deck has produced
  evidence.** That is what it exists for.
- **No fabricated proof, ever.** No testimonials, no customer counts, no
  "companies are discovering". One sale, and it was a referral.
- **No thin pages for search.** The 176 that exist each carry real method.

## 5. How this document stays honest

Reviewed monthly against measured numbers, not against intentions. Every target
above is marked as measured or guessed. When a guess is replaced by a
measurement, the guess is struck through rather than deleted, so the record
shows what was believed and when it stopped being true.

The three conclusions in section 1 are the load-bearing claims. If any of them
turns out wrong, this roadmap is wrong and should be rewritten rather than
patched.
