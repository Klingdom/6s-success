# Retrospective, fifteenth cycle ending 2026-08-30

Two commits, and the end of the list. Everything the pre-traffic audits found
that does not need the deploy or a decision from Phil is now done.

---

## What went well

**The last three items were cleared and each was small.** Breadcrumb markup on
27 articles, an Organization logo, and a 404 page that belongs to the site
rather than to nginx. None of them individually justified a cycle; together
they were the tail of a list that has been shrinking for a week.

**The breadcrumb markup describes the page rather than an idea of it.** It is
built by reading the rendered breadcrumb out of each file, so it cannot claim a
trail the page does not show. If the visible crumb changes the markup follows,
and a page without one gets nothing. Every URL in all 27 was resolved against
the filesystem before shipping.

**I declined to add sameAs, and that is the more important half of that
change.** sameAs lists the profiles that prove an organisation is who it claims
to be. Nothing in this repository or on this site references a social profile,
so anything I put there would be a fabricated authority signal. The logo went
in because the file is real; the rest stayed out because it is not.

**Two audit findings on the new 404, taken separately rather than together.**
It gained the analytics tag, because a 404 nobody counts is a broken link
nobody finds. It stayed exempt from the canonical rule, because a not-found
page has no canonical address and self-referencing one would assert that
/404.html is the preferred version of whatever the visitor actually asked for.
The reason is written into the check, not just into this file.

## What did not go well

**Nothing failed this cycle, which is itself worth noticing.** Fifteen cycles
in, the work has become small and safe, and the reason is that the large and
risky things are all on the other side of a button I cannot press.

**The honest position is now reachable.** For a week I have been able to answer
"what next" with something real. That list is empty in the sense that matters:
what remains needs the deploy, or Phil's decision on the email provider and the
decks, or traffic data that does not exist yet. Continuing to find smaller
tasks would be activity rather than progress, which section 2 of CLAUDE.md
names directly.

## What to change next cycle

**Say the list is done rather than shrinking it further.** The next cycle
should verify the deploy, and if it has not happened, do the smallest honest
thing and say so plainly instead of manufacturing work to fill a cycle.

**Keep re-measuring the outage every cycle regardless.** It is the one number
that changes the entire picture, and it costs one command.
