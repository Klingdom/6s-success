# Entryway Deck — Hero Photo Regeneration Prompts

Paste-ready ChatGPT / DALL-E prompts that produce **clean hero photographs only**.
All card text, callout pins, difficulty stars, and info rows are added afterward by the
card template layer — so the image must contain **NO text of any kind**. This is the
fix for the garbled-baked-in-text problem: never generate body text, labels, signage,
QR codes, or brand logos into the hero photo.

**Shared HOUSE LOOK (already baked into every prompt below):**
> Photorealistic interior photograph, warm natural window light, eye-level ~40mm lens,
> shallow-ish depth, real modern home entryway, warm neutral palette, clean composition
> with empty calm areas reserved for later text overlay. NO text, NO lettering, NO
> signage, NO labels, NO QR codes, NO brand logos or product branding of any kind.
> Leave the lower-left and a header strip visually clean/simple for template text.

Reference cards for the deck's look: EM-001 Front Door, ET-001 Key Bowl, EP-012 Morning Rush
(warm window light, ~40mm eye level, real modern-home props, clean for Micro-Zone/Tool
cards or a believable realistic mess for Problem cards, neutral warm palette).
