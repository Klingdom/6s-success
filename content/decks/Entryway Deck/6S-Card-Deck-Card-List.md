## Card taxonomy
### System (4)
- **Deck Cover** — The room, its signature colour, and the deck count. The card that fronts the box.
- **How to Use · The Ways to Play** — The modes: full room reset, whole-home S-pass, one zone in one sitting, daily draw, family challenge, audit night, safety blitz.
- **The 6S Colour Legend** — The chaos-to-calm ramp and what each S means, in canon order: Sort, Straighten, Shine, Safety, Standardize, Sustain (Safety is the 4th).
- **The Room Kit** — The exact cleaning and organization products this room needs, grouped by the shared core plus room-specific add-ons, from the 123-product library.

### Room activity (7, not micro-zone based)
- **Primary Purpose** — The room's one job, in a sentence. What it is for, and what must always work. Read this first, every reset points back to it.
- **Family Values Sharing** — A household conversation card: what should this room feel like, who uses it, what everyone agrees to keep true here.
- **Before Photos** — How and what to photograph before you start: the honest starting point, the angles, the drawers to open. The record you will beat.
- **6S Audit** — Score the room against the six S's, a quick, kind check-in (not an inspection). Catch drift while it is small.
- **Safety Blitz** — A room-wide hazard hunt using the five safety questions (trip, burn or fire, poison-choke-strangle, fall-cut-crush, water and electricity). Find it, fix it now.
- **Room Signature · After** — Take the ideal-state photo of the finished room. That photo becomes the room's standard, the picture of what right looks like.
- **Keeping Rhythm** — The three cadences for this room: the daily touch, the weekly check-in, and the seasonal 6S event. Anchored to moments that already happen.

### Per micro-zone
- **Zone Card** — Names the micro-zone, its purpose, what a reset zone looks like, and the specific products it needs. The anchor for that zone's six S cards.
- **Sort** — Decide what stays. Touch each item once; keep what earns its place; route the rest out the Five Doors (Donate, Sell, Store, Recycle, Throw). Red-tag the maybes with a date.
- **Straighten** — Give everything a home where it is used. Group like with like, set the point-of-use spot, add the organizer, label the home so anyone can return it.
- **Shine** — Clean to inspect, with the exact inputs and the nine-rule method (top to bottom, dry before wet, mist the cloth, two cloths, match the cleaner, dwell time, clean-to-inspect-and-flag).
- **Safety** — Ask the five questions of this zone and fix the hazard on the spot: anchor, cover, move up high, unplug, or clear the path.
- **Standardize** — Write the one-line standard and take the zone's ideal-state photo, so the reset is repeatable and anyone can see what right looks like.
- **Sustain** — Bolt this zone's upkeep to a moment that already happens, the daily touch that keeps it from sliding back.
