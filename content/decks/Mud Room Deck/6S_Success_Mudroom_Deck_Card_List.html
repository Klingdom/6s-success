6S Success Mudroom Deck

Canonical 90-card production list for the Mudroom room deck. The architecture mirrors the Entryway pilot while the content is rebuilt around interception, six canonical zones, wet-weather flow, family ownership, grit control, drying, pet readiness, seasonal rotation, and utility safety.

Production rule: Each final production image must show the front and back of one true poker-sized card side-by-side. Audit and approve one card before generating the next. Illustration-first, minimal readable copy, no QR codes, and no infographic-style overcrowding.
Mudroom design principle: The room catches rather than stores. Fix the wall before the floor, stop grit in three layers, use visible daily storage, dry wet gear fully, and judge the room by whether the household can come through wet and leave nothing behind.
