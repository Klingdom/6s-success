6S Success

The company.

The operating system for a calmer home.

6S Success is a housekeeping brand that turns a proven industrial method — Lean 6S — into a complete system of books, tools, apps, games, and services that help families reset and keep any room.

Most home-organization brands sell one of two things: inspiration (books, influencers, TV) or objects (bins, baskets, closet systems). Almost none sell the thing that actually changes a home, a system that connects the two and makes it last.

6S Success is built the other way around. One method — Sort, Straighten, Shine, Safety, Standardize, Sustain — applied at the smallest meaningful unit of a home, the micro-zone, and delivered in every format a family actually uses: read it, play it, buy the kit, run the app, or hire us to do it with you. Every product teaches the same method, and every product sells the next. The result is not a book or a bin, but a brand a household can grow with, from a $25 card game to a whole-home transformation.

1 · The market & the brand

A method brand in a market of gurus and bins.

Home organization is large, growing, and deeply fragmented, split between decluttering media, professional organizers, and a vast storage-product aisle. What none of them reliably deliver is sustain: families declutter, then drift back, because inspiration fades and objects alone don’t build a habit.

6S Success competes on exactly that gap. It is not a guru and not a bin store, it is a method brand. Lean 6S is measurable, visual, and repeatable, the same rigor that runs factories, made warm and family-friendly, and it uniquely includes Safety as the fourth S, the professional discipline no consumer system carries.

The moat

A proprietary method & taxonomy. A documented system of 20 rooms and 114 micro-zones, finished and validated, not a set of tips.

A product-overlap core. 72% of the 123 catalog products apply to every room, so the catalog stays lean and every room naturally cross-sells the same core kit.

A multi-format ecosystem. Content, digital, physical, and services each feed the others, an owned flywheel rather than a single SKU.

Finished IP. A complete 50-chapter book, a companion manual, a working app prototype, a commerce site, and a reviewed card-game line already exist.

Positioning in one line: the calm, credible, systematic home brand, the one that gives a family a method and then every tool to run it.

4 · Growth & roadmap

Lead with the wedge, monetize with the ladder.

Go to market with the lowest-friction, most giftable products, the Home Edition book and the Home Quest Entryway game, backed by an SEO content hub at 6s-success.com. Convert readers and players into app subscribers and kit buyers, then graduate the most engaged into services and, eventually, into consultants who sell the brand for you.

Now
Launch the wedge
Ship the Home Edition book + Home Quest Entryway + the app free tier as the low-friction front door.
Next
Complete the core loop
Roll the 20-room kits, app Premium, and the Home Quest room line on the shared product core.
Then
Open the service network
Launch the Certified Consultant program so trained graduates deliver in-home resets and expand reach.
Later
License the method
B2B channels: realtors, senior living, corporate facilities, and international editions.

Channels

Direct-to-consumer (the site + marketplaces) for books, games, and kits; the app stores for digital; and B2B partnerships, realtors staging homes, senior-living move management, corporate facilities, and professional organizers, for licensing and wholesale. The family/gift angle carries the game; the professional-rigor angle carries B2B.

Already in hand: a finished 50-chapter book, the micro-zone manual, an app MVP, a deployable commerce site, a reviewed Home Quest prototype with a full fix pack and expansion concept, and the product taxonomy. The costly IP is built, the work ahead is packaging and go-to-market.

5 · Organization & the ask

A lean core with a network edge.

6S Success runs as a small owned core, IP & content, product & digital, and operations, with a scalable network edge of certified consultants who deliver services under the brand. That structure keeps fixed costs low while letting the service business grow without linear headcount.

Functions

Content & IP: the book, manual, method, and taxonomy, the brand’s durable asset.

Product & digital: kits, games, the app, and the storefront.

Services & certification: consulting delivery and the consultant-training program.

Operations: fulfillment, partnerships, and marketing.

Risks & mitigations

Breadth vs. focus: lead with one wedge (Entryway + book) rather than launching everything; the shared core makes each addition incremental.

Services don’t scale: the certification network turns delivery into a licensed, asset-light channel.

Commoditization: the documented method, Safety-as-4th-S, and the multi-format flywheel are hard to copy piecemeal.

The opportunity. The method, the content, and the products are built and reviewed. The next step is to package the ladder, launch the wedge, and stand up the recurring and service tiers, turning a finished body of IP into a multi-product housekeeping brand with revenue from a $25 game all the way to a certified-consultant network.
