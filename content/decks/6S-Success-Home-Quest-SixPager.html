1 · The problem & the insight

Households know they should reset. Almost none sustain it.

The entryway is a daily friction factory: the morning hunt for keys, the coat that never reaches a hook, the mud tracked across every floor, the “guests in an hour” scramble. Families feel the cost every day and still can’t hold a fix, because the advice they’re given is a chore list, and chore lists die on Tuesday.

The deeper failure is sustain. Organizing books and videos teach the what; they don’t manufacture the doing or the holding. Lean 6S works in factories precisely because it is visual, measured, and repeated on a cadence, but carried into a home it usually stays abstract, a poster nobody lives by.

The insight

A game supplies the three things a chore list cannot: a loop, a reason to repeat, and a way to keep score. And 6S already is a game loop, audit → fix → sustain → re-audit. Home Quest simply names that loop as the rules, binds the score to the real room through a before/after audit, and turns sustain into a streak you defend. The method gets done because doing it is how you play.

Why now, why us. The 6S Success system is already built, the book is complete, the kits are defined, the app has a working MVP. Home Quest is the one missing layer that is tactile, social, and giftable, and the layer most likely to pull a whole family in and convert them to the rest of the ecosystem.

3 · How it works

The core loop

The nine types are not a list, they are a chain, and the chain is exactly how a family reasons through a messy doorway: a Zone is afflicted by a Problem, solved by a Tool, held by a Habit, taught as a Skill, tested by an Event, helped by an Expert, built up by an Upgrade, and proven by a Win.

On your turn you face the most urgent Problem afflicting a Zone. You spend Momentum to equip the Tool that ends it, then you do the real reset and score the before/after delta of that card’s own symptom checklist. Solving Zones advances the six S-tracks and pays Momentum, which you reinvest in stronger Tools and Upgrades, and in Experts to blunt the Events that spike pressure. A Zone you stop maintaining decays back to its Problem side. You win the room when all six S-tracks are full and every Zone holds a verified seven-day streak, at which point the deck hands off to the next room.

4 · The game system

An economy that can’t be faked.

One currency, one target

Momentum is the only currency: earned by solving Problems and completing Zones for real, spent to equip Tools, buy Upgrades, and call Experts. It has a sink and a target, so it is an economy, not a score that only ever climbs. The six S-tracks are the win condition, each completed Zone advances one.

SortStraightenShineSafetyStandardizeSustain

The reality gate

You score the before/after delta of each card’s Common-Symptoms list, a quick zero-to-five audit before, the reset, then zero-to-five after. Points are the change in the actual room, never a self-ticked box, so Home Quest is impossible to win from the couch, and it finally cashes in the audit baseline the method always promised. Decay then forces upkeep: a neglected Zone flips back to a Problem, so gains have to be held.

Ways to play

Daily Draw. one card, today's two-minute task
Zone Run. reset one zone start to finish
Family Co-op. beat the room before the Events pile up
Campaign. fill the S-tracks, then the next room
Head to Head. race to Habit-Established on your zones

The collectible layer

The same set powers a trading-card format: the six S’s become types (energy), a shared Time-Effort token is the resource, Zones evolve Messy → Cleaned → Standardized → Sustained, rooms are gyms that earn badges on a House Passport, Experts are trainers, and Events are the opposition. It is de-fanged by design: no paid boosters, rarity is a difficulty label rather than a paywall, every card ships in the base box, and money can never fake the photo, so pay-to-win is structurally impossible.

5 · Product line, ecosystem & pricing

One room now, the whole house next.

The Entryway is the flagship base set (~79 cards across all nine types). Each of the 20 rooms in the system becomes its own boxed set on the identical nine-type grammar, 114 micro-zones in all, and the House Passport is the campaign spine that strings them into a season. A Family Edition cuts a leaner, sturdier, picture-first version with kid roles and the Safety hunt played as a scavenger game.

The ecosystem & the moat

Home Quest is not a standalone toy, it is the playable face of a system that already exists. Every Tool card is a real micro-zone kit you can buy; the deck feeds the app’s tracker and the book’s method and the consulting service. Because 72% of the product core is shared across all 20 rooms, the catalog stays lean and every room set cross-sells the same core kits, a durable moat and a built-in upsell path from a $25 deck to kits, app, and consulting.

6 · Traction, risks & the plan

From a reviewed prototype to a printed flagship.

What’s built: a photoreal Entryway prototype (~31 cards across three types), three independent expert reviews (6S canon, print design, game design), a consolidated improvement plan, a Fix Pack (corrected on-canon copy spec, a validated code and link table, and paste-ready regeneration prompts), and the full nine-type concept. The underlying system, the 50-chapter book, the manual, the app MVP, and the website, is complete and deployable.

What’s left (and it’s well-scoped)

Production: today’s cards are ~300 DPI flattened spreads with rasterized text; fixes go into the source template, then re-export front/back with bleed at higher resolution.

Content: author the six referenced-but-unbuilt card types (Habit, Skill, Upgrade, Win, Event, Expert), the Rules Card + glossary, and the handful of missing base cards.

Risks & mitigations

“It feels like homework.” Co-op default, short modes, defendable streaks, and real rewards make the doing the fun.

Art / print quality. Solved by the template fix + high-res re-export; the good families (Problems, Tools) prove the house look works.

Canon drift. A frozen copy spec + a QA gate (every code resolves, zero baked text, zero trademarks) before any render-to-print.
