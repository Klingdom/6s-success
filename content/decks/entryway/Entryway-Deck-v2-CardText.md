## Card 4
'border-color:#BC4B2A'> ROOM KIT &nbsp; What the entryway needs 4 / 46 Cleaners. Neutral-pH multi-surface; streak-free glass/mirror; a cleaner matched to your actual floor. Cloths & tools. A colour-coded microfiber set (glass · surfaces · floor/boot-tray · dry buffing); a compact vacuum with crevice, soft-brush and upholstery tools; a small soft detail brush; a stiff dry brush for the boot tray; a portable caddy. Organization. A landing tray, a key hook, a rail and hooks, a shelf, a hat/glove bin, a shoe rack, a boot tray, two mats, an umbrella stand, an off-season box. Types, not brands. Buy for the job. Never wash microfiber with fabric softener, it kills the grab. The two-cloth rule One cloth to clean, one to dry. A wet doorway surface streaks, and a wet floor is a fall waiting to happen. Colour-code Glass, surfaces, floor/boot-tray each get their own colour so grit never travels from a dirty surface to a clean one. Entryway · 6S Micro-Zone Deck
