ROOM KIT  What the entryway needs4 / 46

Cleaners. Neutral-pH multi-surface; streak-free glass/mirror; a cleaner matched to your actual floor.

Cloths & tools. A colour-coded microfiber set (glass · surfaces · floor/boot-tray · dry buffing); a compact vacuum with crevice, soft-brush and upholstery tools; a small soft detail brush; a stiff dry brush for the boot tray; a portable caddy.

Organization. A landing tray, a key hook, a rail and hooks, a shelf, a hat/glove bin, a shoe rack, a boot tray, two mats, an umbrella stand, an off-season box.

Types, not brands. Buy for the job. Never wash microfiber with fabric softener, it kills the grab.

The two-cloth rule
One cloth to clean, one to dry. A wet doorway surface streaks, and a wet floor is a fall waiting to happen.
Colour-code
Glass, surfaces, floor/boot-tray each get their own colour so grit never travels from a dirty surface to a clean one.

SAFETY BLITZ  Two-minute hazard hunt · five questions9 / 46

Ask each of the five once, and fix on the spot:

  • Water & electricity · dock and power strip out of the drip line below wet umbrellas and sleeves.
  • Burn / fire · never chain a dock, a lamp and a heater to one strip; keep the door clear as an exit.
  • Poison / choke / strangle · scarves and straps off low hooks; button batteries, coins, magnets in a lidded tin.
  • Fall / cut / crush · anchor a tippy console or top-heavy rack; heavy things low.
  • Fall · the stride of floor inside the door clear, the mat flat and gripped.

Everything your cleaning flagged, this is where you act on it.

Shine  Landing Spot · Shine15 / 46
  1. Clear & unplug. Everything off, tray out, folder out, unplug the dock before any damp cloth.
  2. Dust high & dry first. Dry buff cloth along the mirror/frame top and the console back-edge seam.
  3. Mirror / glass. Mist glass cleaner onto the cloth (never the glass), wipe one direction, buff dry in the crossing direction.
  4. Wall & switch plate. Barely-damp surface cloth, lift the finger marks round the switch and the door-side scuff, dry behind you.
  5. Console top. Mist multi-surface on the cloth, back edge to front, let sticky rings soften, buff dry.
  6. Wash the tray. Wipe the grey film ring, wash the tray in and out, detail-brush the corners, dry fully before keys return.
  7. Dock & cable, dry only. Unplugged, wipe body and cradle dry, detail-brush the port and cable, coil loosely.
  8. Floor last. Vacuum grit under and around the legs, then mop the sealed floor and let it dry.
Inspect & flag as you go (do not stop to fix)
  • A brown water ring under the hooks (dripping sleeves)
  • A cracked, frayed or hardened cable, or a scorched or warm plug
  • A power strip that is warm or yellowed at a socket
  • A console leg or wall bracket that rocks
  • Green or white crust on the dock contacts (corrosion)

Shine  Coats & Outerwear · Shine22 / 46
  1. Take the bin down & empty it. Shake it out; set contents on a clean surface, not the floor.
  2. Dust the shelf, then wipe. Dry cloth first, then barely-damp surface cloth, reach the back edge and the front lip's grey line.
