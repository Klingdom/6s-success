# Entryway Deck — Frozen Art Style Bible (paste into every image prompt)

*The single source of visual consistency for all 46 cards. Do not change it between cards.*

## The three setup answers (locked)
1. **Kids age range:** 5–10 (early readers) with caregivers alongside.
2. **Style:** friendly flat-vector, **cartoon-meets-clean-infographic** — warm and characterful, never babyish, and readable at a small trading-card size.
3. **Colours / brand:** the 6S palette below.

## Format
- **Trading-card size, portrait, aspect 5:7 (2.5 x 3.5 in).** The illustration is the hero and fills the card; keep a calm lower third for a one-line big idea and a clean top corner for the S-type badge. Almost no text inside the art (1–3 word labels/callouts only); the words live in the card layout and the storyboard.
- Print-safe: bold, even line weight (~4px feel), high contrast, no tiny detail that dies at card size.

## Palette
Cream ground #F7F2E9 · warm near-black line #2B2622 · terracotta #BC4B2A · honey #DDA63A · slate blue #3C5A6B · soft green #6E8B5B · soft oak #E7C58B for wood. Warm daylight. Scandinavian-modern home.

## The six-S type colours (the badge in the top corner)
Sort #CB4B36 · Straighten #BC4B2A · Shine #D98A2B · **Safety #DDA63A (the 4th S)** · Standardize #6E8B5B · Sustain #4E7A57.

## Consistent character system (same on every card)
- **RILEY** — a cheerful kid about 8: warm brown skin, curly dark hair, a mustard striped tee, teal shorts, orange sneakers. Curious, capable, doing the task.
- **A GROWN-UP HELPER** — a caregiver (vary gender/appearance across the deck for inclusivity), simple and warm, helping alongside, never doing it *for* Riley.
- Optional recurring **small friendly dog** for warmth.
- Show kids of different abilities and backgrounds across the deck. Real, kind faces; natural poses; never posed stock smiles, never scary.

## The colour legend (identical every card)
- **Green glow / green check** = done, great, the good way.
- **Red mark / red warning triangle** = watch out / not safe / don't do this.
- **Honey highlight ring or spotlight** = look here, this is the focus of the card.
- **Terracotta arrows** = the order to do things (1 → 2 → 3).
- Callouts are small rounded label chips; keep them to 1–3 words.

## Hard rules (prevent the defects)
- If the six S's appear, they are exactly, in order: Sort, Straighten, Shine, **Safety (4th)**, Standardize, Sustain. Never a five-step list, never Safety dropped or moved, never "Set in Order."
- No QR codes. No brand names, logos, or real product packaging. No garbled or misspelled text. No em dashes.
- No frightening imagery. Practical for a normal home. Inclusive and age-appropriate.
- Same characters, same line weight, same legend, cream ground, warm daylight — every single card, so the deck reads as one hand.
