Always read and follow the manufacturer's instructions for every product, tool, and appliance you use. Never mix cleaning products. Unplug tools, or remove their batteries, before cleaning them. Keep chemicals, tools, batteries, and medicines away from children and pets. Test any method on a small hidden area first.

This material does not teach structural, electrical, plumbing, gas, roofing, or carpentry work. Where a job needs one of those trades, stop and call a qualified professional.
