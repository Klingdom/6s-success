6S Success: Home Quest

LinkedIn Card-a-Day Campaign for the 90-card Entryway prototype deck. Each post is intentionally brief, card-specific, conversational, and designed to introduce the bigger whole-home Home Quest idea without sounding like an advertisement every day.

Improved Launch Post

I have finished the first full prototype of the 6S Success: Home Quest Entryway Deck, and the feedback loop has already started. The next version expands the idea into more than 100 household micro zones, with quick quests for cleaning, organizing, safety, standards, and habits. Here is the Entryway Room Card, front and back. I will share one prototype card each day through August. If you want to beta test the digital or physical deck, message me.

#6S #HomeOrganization #HomeQuest
Posting strategy: Lead with the card image and the problem or idea behind it. Keep the beta-test invitation strong on the first post, then use it selectively every 5–10 posts so the series feels useful rather than repetitive.

Recommended cadence

Mix card families rather than publishing all 12 micro zones, then all 12 problems, then all 12 tools. A stronger sequence alternates useful ideas, relatable problems, practical tools, habits, skills, upgrades, wins, and real-world events. That keeps the feed varied while slowly teaching the full Home Quest system.
