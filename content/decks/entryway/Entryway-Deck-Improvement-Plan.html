The eight moves (deck-wide)
1 · Turn the Glance Test into pass/fail with 3 tiny metrics Black Belt
The deck is almost all qualitative. Adopt and repeat three counted states: seconds to launch (<60, zero hunting), items on any surface (≤3), empty hooks (=2). Put them on Purpose (5), capture as before/after numbers (7, 10), watch the first daily (11), score them in the audit (8).
2 · Thread one-in-one-out through the whole deck Organizer
The single rule that keeps a finished entryway finished. State it in Family Values (6) and echo it on every zone's Sustain card. A new coat, bag, or pair means an old one leaves that day.
3 · Add the salt fix (the biggest cleaning gap) Housekeeper
Road-salt / white haze is alkaline mineral crust, the neutral cleaner the deck uses everywhere cannot touch it. Add a mild white-vinegar solution (~1:4, dwell 2–3 min, wipe & rinse), never a dry scrub, on the boot-tray, mat, and salt-etched-floor cards (29, 43, 44). Reserve acid for salt only; neutral for routine.
4 · Build the systems the deck only implies Organizer
"Give paper a verdict" with no slot becomes a pile. Add a mail sorter with named slots (Action / File / Shred-Recycle) and an outbound station by the door (returns, mail, donation bag, reusable bags, keys) so things that must leave physically block the exit. This is the deck's biggest structural gap.
5 · Poka-yoke over willpower Black Belt
Restore the taped mat outline + door-swing floor marking (it is in the source manuscript and was dropped, cards 42/45), the deck's cleanest visual poka-yoke. Add 5-Why prompts for the chronic failures (coat-on-the-chair 25, paper pile 13) to fix the layout cause, not the person. Prefer can't-happen fixes over redo-it fixes on Safety.
6 · Rescue the sparse cards & make Safety visible Graphic Artist
Give each card type a fixed side-panel unit (Sustain → a "3 clocks" chip stack; Straighten → a "a home for it" mini-diagram; Zone → a product-icon caddy row). Add per-loop-step glyph badges, a time/effort footer pill, and per-zone tokens (keys/coat/shoe/bench/door). Give Safety, the 4th S, a warm-alert colour + shield so it stops vanishing into the greys.
7 · Add the pro cleaning craft the method skips Housekeeper
Real dwell times (never scrub dry); fold-and-flip to a clean cloth face; damp-not-wet (wrung, no drip); melamine foam for scuffs; streak-free glass in indirect light with a dry-buff; odor control at the source (baking soda in bins/trays, invert the umbrella stand); mat cleaning by type; per-zone time estimates; and a chemical-safety line (ventilate, glove, spot-test, never mix).
8 · Use the real illustrations Graphic Artist
10 of the 15 Chapter-31 plates are usable (see table). The before/after and the 5-zone map match the deck's style; the photoreal per-zone standards and the Shine/inspection sequences fit with a photo-frame treatment. 5 are rejected, 2 bake a WRONG five-phase model that contradicts the six-S legend.

Game design & mechanics · the two game experts
Give it a live score, not post-hoc grading Game Theory + Designer
The three metrics become an always-on 10-point Calm Track, and the existing 6S Audit becomes the scorecard (before/after delta). Win the room only when the After photo matches the Standard, honest by construction. Win caps at 10 ("there is no 11") to kill clean-forever grinding.
Make triage a real decision Game Theory
Add a Time-Token economy: you can't do everything, so you choose the highest-value zones and steps. Scarcity is what turns a checklist into a game with meaningful choices.
Protect the skippable steps by design Both
Interlocks: no Shine, no photo; no Safety, no lock. A Hazard Bounty makes Safety (the 4th S) the jackpot step. Now the boring-but-critical steps are the rewarding ones, not the skipped ones.
De-fang the events, default to co-op Game Theory
"Life Happens" cards grant bonuses only, never punish real chores. The shared room is a collective-action problem, so default to co-op with secret zone assignments (one person can't carry), kid handicapping (kids own the low zones + the empty-hooks check), and a hazard-hunt scavenger mode for Safety.
Anti-guilt retention Game Theory
Streaks with a weekly grace day, loss-aversion via defendable "Kept" tokens, and the win cap. Keeps the daily loop motivating without shaming a missed night.
Reframe the 46 cards as a 5x6 board Designer
Identical frame, unique hero + zone spine + loop position, so the five near-identical S-runs stop feeling repetitive and become a tableau you clear cell-by-cell with real tasks.
Redesign the card as double-sided tarot Designer
FRONT = at-a-glance play face (corner index, one hero image, one imperative, a time/effort pill, and a 6-dot loop-rail showing your position in the zone's order). BACK = the deep-read method. Solves at-a-glance-vs-deep-read and the sparse/dense card problem at once.
Ship two new components + a line spine Designer
A Tracker Mat / tear-off pad (grid + photo pockets + audit + streak calendar = a refill SKU) and a token punchboard (red-tag, hazard-triangle, done-check, empty-hook gauge). Across the line, a House Passport wall tracker is the campaign/legacy spine and an expansion SKU.
Illustration = function Designer (extends the Artist)
Zone art shows the win-state target; Safety art is a hazard-hunt board; Standardize/Before/After are frames for the player's own photos. Photo for recognition, icon for speed, diagram for order. Both game reviewers independently confirmed plates 31-13/14 would break the six-S rules the game teaches.
Three print-ready modes Both
The Reset Run (co-op flagship, full room), The Daily Draw (5-minute streak / habit engine, draw-and-discard), and Family Race the Clock (handicapped, and audit-gated so rushing can't cheat the clean).

Two ways to build v3

v3a · the enriched reference deck (smaller lift): weave every expert tip into the card bodies, add the fixed side-panel units and the Safety colour/shield, restore the taped-mat poka-yoke, and place the 10 usable plates. Keeps the current print-and-play reference format.

v3b · the real game (the two game experts' direction): redesign as double-sided tarot cards, add the 10-point Calm Track + Time-Tokens + step interlocks + Hazard Bounty, ship the Tracker Mat and token punchboard, and print the three modes. This is the version you can sell as a game per room, with a House Passport line spine.
