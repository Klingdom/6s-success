# Graphic-Artist Review — Entryway Deck v2 (46 cards)

Reviewer role: information designer / premium instructional-deck art direction.
Scope: (A) visual-system critique + per-card notes, (B) assessment of Chapter-31 illustration plates for card use.

---

## PART A — VISUAL SYSTEM

### The system in one line
The bones are good: colour-by-S header, terracotta room border, two-column body, footer. It reads as a system. The two real problems are **(1) rhythm** — five card types repeat 5× each, so repetition is felt as monotony rather than as a reassuring pattern — and **(2) the sparse cards**, where the right-hand side panel is under-fed on the lighter, shorter cards.

### Top-priority fixes (in order)

1. **Fix the sparse cards with a repeatable side-panel unit, not more prose.**
   The empty cards are the **Straighten** cards (14, 21, 28, 35, 42) and especially the **Sustain** cards (18, 25, 32, 39, 46), plus 5, 6, 11. They currently carry one short callout in a big right column. Give each card type a *fixed* right-panel object so the empty space becomes structured, not blank:
   - **Sustain cards** → a small **"3 clocks" chip stack** (Daily / Weekly / Seasonal) with a dot-anchor icon ("bolt it to a moment that already happens"). This turns the sparsest card in the deck into the most scannable.
   - **Straighten cards** → a **"a home for it" mini-diagram**: an outline of the zone furniture with 2–3 labelled home-spots (tray, hook, folder). Reuse the label-tag glyph. Stops the identical "The label rule" callout from reading as filler on all five.
   - **Zone cards (12, 19, 26, 33, 40)** → the "Inputs · gather into your caddy" list is long; convert it to a **product-icon row** (bottle / cloth-swatch / vacuum / brush / caddy) so the kit reads in one glance and the column balances.

2. **Introduce a shared "card-type badge" strip** so a player identifies the loop step before reading. Sort / Straighten / Shine / Safety / Standardize / Sustain each get a tiny fixed glyph next to the header title (magnifier, house, sparkle, shield, camera, loop-arrow — exactly the vocabulary the Chapter-31 plates already use). This is the single highest-leverage upgrade for a deck that repeats a 7-step loop across five zones.

3. **Add a difficulty/time badge to every activity and zone card.** The deck already promises "Landing Spot ~20 min" on Card 1 — surface that everywhere: a small footer pill (⏱ 15 / 20 / 25 min · ●○○ effort). Players sorting the deck for "a 10-minute job tonight" need this, and it fills footer whitespace consistently.

4. **Give each zone a colour sub-accent AND an icon**, so the five zones are told apart at a glance the way the six S's are. The room border is uniformly terracotta (good — it says "Entryway"), but within a zone the seven cards currently look identical except for the header S-colour. Add a small **zone token** (keys / coat / shoe / bench / door — these exact glyphs exist in plate 31-02) in the header corner. Then Zone 1's seven cards all carry the keys token, etc. This is what lets the "grab one zone's cards" play work physically.

5. **"Reset looks like" deserves a picture, not a sentence.** Zone cards describe the target state in prose. Add a tiny 2-state **before→after thumbnail** (grey scribble → clean line-icon) in the zone card's side panel. Plate 31-01 proves how powerful the before/after read is; a miniaturised version belongs on every Zone and every Standardize card.

### Iconography & colour-coding legibility
- The **S-ramp red→green** and the **six-S colour set** are the deck's strongest visual asset — keep them, and make Card 3 (Legend) the master key that every other card's header colour refers back to. Add the tiny S-glyphs there so the legend teaches the badge system in fix #2.
- **Watch Safety's colour.** Safety is the 4th S and the book's differentiator; it must not read as "just another slate/grey card." Give Safety a distinct, slightly warmer alert tone (honey-amber or the deck's warning ochre) and the shield glyph, consistently on cards 9, 16, 23, 30, 37, 44. Right now Safety risks disappearing into the system/room greys.
- **Contrast check the light headers.** Straighten/Sustain/Standardize sit on the pale end; verify white header text hits AA on the lightest greens. If not, darken the header band or switch to ink text on the palest chips.

### Diagram-level notes (existing inline SVGs)
- **Shine top-to-bottom method diagram** — keep; it is the spine of the deck. Consider numbering it 1→n to match the nine-rule Shine order, and reuse the same numbered strip on every Shine card as a mini progress rail (dust→glass→hard→detail→floor).
- **Five-doors / five-safety-question row** — good; make the five safety icons the exact set reused on all Safety cards so the "five questions" become a recognisable motif.
- **5-zone map (SVG)** — you now have a far richer illustrated version in plate **31-02**; either upgrade the SVG to match that composition or use the plate directly (see Part B).
- **Six-S colour ramp** — perfect as the Card 3 backbone.
- **Before/after pair (SVG)** — thin compared to plate 31-01; consider swapping in the illustrated plate on the cover / Card 7 / Card 10.

### Per-card-type notes (covers all 46)
- **1 Cover** — hero illustration candidate; use plate 31-01 or 31-02 full-bleed behind the title. Strongest card to sell the deck.
- **2 How to use** — the six play-modes want six tiny mode-icons (grid / colour-swatch / single-card / calendar-draw / two-people / clipboard), not a paragraph.
- **3 Legend** — make this the master colour+glyph key (see above). "Chaos left → calm right" = the S-ramp; show it.
- **4 Room kit** — dense; break the cleaners/cloths/organization blocks into three labelled icon clusters.
- **5 Primary purpose** — sparse; add a one-line "leave in under a minute" stopwatch vignette in the side panel.
- **6 Family values** — sparse; a two-chip "agreements" card (weather-count / named-hook) with the empty-hook glyph.
- **7 Before photos** — put a real image here (plate 31-01 *before* half, or hero-entry). Add a camera-framing icon (stand-here dot).
- **8 Audit** — build the 0–1–2 score into a **6-cell mini scorecard grid** (one row per S) rather than a text list; this card can look like a real scoresheet.
- **9 Safety blitz** — five-question icon row; anchor the Safety glyph/colour here.
- **10 Signature/after** — image card; plate 31-01 *after* half, or a "same-spot" framing diagram.
- **11 Keeping rhythm** — the "three clocks" unit; prototype the Sustain side-panel here.
- **Zone cards 12/19/26/33/40** — add zone token + product-icon caddy row + "reset looks like" before/after thumb.
- **Sort cards 13/20/27/34/41** — the red-tag concept is strong; give it a literal **red date-tag glyph** as the recurring side-panel motif.
- **Straighten 14/21/28/35/42** — the sparsest-feeling repeat; replace the identical "label rule" callout with the per-zone "a home for it" mini-diagram (fix #1).
- **Shine 15/22/29/36/43** — text-dense (the nine-rule method); add the numbered top-to-bottom rail + a small "inspect & flag" callout box so the flagged-defects list is visually distinct from the cleaning steps.
- **Safety 16/23/30/37/44** — shield glyph, warm-alert colour, "From the Shine flags" as a bordered alert box with warning-triangle bullets (plate 31-10 is the model).
- **Standardize 17/24/31/38/45** — every one is "photograph it" — give them all a **framed-photo / target glyph** and a dashed "tape it inside the cupboard" placement cue.
- **Sustain 18/25/32/39/46** — the three-clocks chip stack (fix #1). Highest-impact rescue in the deck.

## PART B — IMAGE ASSESSMENT (Chapter-31 plates)

**Two visual families exist.** (1) *Warm hand-illustration* — plates **31-01** and **31-02** — matches the deck's cream/terracotta/illustrated aesthetic almost exactly; these are the best fits and can be used near-as-is. (2) *Photoreal AI room composites with callout chips* — 31-05/06/07/08/09/10 and the two hero photos — high quality and internally consistent, but photorealistic, so they clash with the deck's flat SVG diagrams unless framed in a consistent photo-inset treatment.

**Canon flags found (important):**
- Plates **31-13 and 31-14 bake a WRONG five-phase model** — *See Clearly / Remove the Extra / Shine Every Day / Keep It Safe / Reset Together* — this is NOT the deck's six S's (Sort/Straighten/Shine/**Safety**/Standardize/Sustain) and would directly contradict Card 3's legend. **Reject for the deck.**
- Plate **31-11 (Friction Meter)** uses the friction-meter device that this v2 deck explicitly **retired** in favour of a before/after signature. **Reject** (off-brand for v2).
- Plate **31-03** is an 8-step zone *reset flow* that folds Safety into "Inspection" — it does not carry Safety as its own S. Fine as an overview flow, but must **not** be labelled "the 6S method."
- No fake QR codes, no brand logos, no garbled text in any plate. Text is legible throughout.
