# Visual & Print-Readiness Review — Entryway Deck Card Images

**Reviewer:** Senior graphic artist / print & packaging designer
**Scope:** 26 card PNGs from `Entryway Deck/card-images/` (all three families EM / EP / ET, all four "-a" variant pairs, plus spot-checks)
**Deck format:** two-sided trading cards, target trim ~2.5 × 3.5 in, color-coded families (EM = Micro Zone green, EP = Problem red, ET = Tool/Improvement green)
**Cards examined:** EM-001(+a), EM-002, EM-003, EM-004 Landing, EM-004 Mail(+a), EM-005, EM-006, EM-008(+a), EM-009, EM-011, EM-012, EP-004, EP-005, EP-009(+a), EP-012, ET-001, ET-003, ET-005, ET-007, ET-009, ET-012

---

## Executive summary

The deck is **not yet one deck** — it is two production generations photographed in two different media, glued together. The **EP (red Problem) and ET (green Tool) families are excellent and consistent**: photoreal heroes, matched headers, correct color coding, clean type. The **EM (green Micro Zone) family is the problem child**: most EM cards are painterly *illustrations* (they clash with the photoreal EP/ET), and roughly a third of them use a second, off-brand back template that puts **red "Problem-card" styling on green Micro Zone cards**. On top of that there are hard defects: two cards have **garbled AI gibberish baked into the front** (EM-005, EM-006), two cards carry **real brand logos** (ET-007 DYMO, EP-005 Amazon), one card's whole back uses the **wrong card-code family** (ET-012), and several cards have **internal code collisions** with their filenames. None of this is fatal — the good families prove the house style works — but the EM family needs a regeneration pass before this prints.

The single most important structural note: **the "FRONT" / "BACK" words centered above many cards are proofing annotations sitting outside the trim. They must not reach print.**

---

## 1. Style consistency — does it read as one deck?

**No — there are two axes of drift, both concentrated in the EM family.**

### Axis A — medium (photo vs illustration). This is the #1 visual problem.
- **Photoreal (correct house look):** all EP (EP-004, EP-005, EP-009, EP-009-a, EP-012), all ET (ET-001/003/005/007/009/012), plus **EM-001 Front Door** and **EM-009 Umbrella Station**.
- **Painterly illustration (clashes):** EM-001-a, EM-002, EM-003, EM-004 Mail, EM-004 Mail-a, EM-004 Landing, EM-005, EM-006, EM-008, EM-008-a, EM-011, EM-012.

Roughly 24 cards are photographs and ~12 are storybook illustrations. Fanned out on a table the green Micro Zone cards look like a *different product* from the red/green photo cards. **Pick one medium deck-wide.** Because the majority (EP+ET ≈ 24 cards) and the two best EM cards are photoreal — and the book itself is described as AI-photoreal — **standardize on PHOTOREAL and regenerate the illustrated EM cards** to match. EM-001 (photoreal) and EM-009 (photoreal) are your in-family proof that the Micro Zone cards *can* be photos.

### Axis B — back template (two conflicting layouts).
- **Template A (on-brand, keep):** family-colored "COMMON SYMPTOMS" (green on EM/ET, red on EP), family-colored trophy, no FRONT/BACK proof labels, and a **two-line italic tagline in a beige footer box with a star emblem + bookmark**. Used by all EP, all ET, and EM-001, EM-004 Mail, EM-004 Landing, EM-008, EM-009.
- **Template B (off-brand, fix):** FRONT/BACK proof labels, **RED "COMMON SYMPTOMS" and RED warning triangle on GREEN Micro Zone cards**, **orange trophy / orange bulb**, and a single-line non-italic footer "BUILD THE HABIT. IMPROVE THE SYSTEM. ENJOY THE RESULTS." Used by EM-001-a, EM-002, EM-003, EM-004 Mail-a, EM-005, EM-006, EM-008-a, EM-011, EM-012.

Template B **breaks the color code**: red + orange are the Problem-card language, applied here to green Micro Zone cards. Standardize every EM card on **Template A** (green symptoms, green trophy, 2-line italic footer, no proof labels).

**Net:** EP and ET are camera-ready for style. EM needs a unify pass on both axes.

## 2. Legibility at true card size (~2.5 × 3.5 in)

The fronts and especially the **backs are over-stuffed**. At trim, the smallest type on the back is roughly 3–4 pt and will not survive. Ranked by what dies first:

1. **RELATED CARD PATH (back, bottom-right) — dies completely.** Nine tiny icons, each with a label *and* a "to EX-002" micro-code, in two rows. At card size this is an unreadable gray smear and it is also where almost every data error lives (see §4). **Cut to a maximum of 5 icons, drop the "to XX-000" codes**, or move the routing to a companion index/QR. This is the highest-value legibility cut in the deck.
2. **PROGRESS TRACKER (back, bottom-left) — mostly dies.** Five labeled rows of checkboxes competing with the card path. **Reduce to 3 checkboxes** (e.g., Assessed / Improved / Habit) or drop the labels and keep icons + boxes.
3. **Front third + fourth tiers.** Below the hero the front stacks: callout captions → OBJECTIVE/QUICK WIN/REAL-WORLD → GAME EFFECT/6S LESSON/PRO TIP → RESET/MAINTENANCE/FAMILY FRIENDLY → footer bar. That's five text bands under the photo; the GAME EFFECT/6S LESSON/PRO TIP body copy is ~4 pt. **Cut one band** — merge or drop PRO TIP on the front (it repeats on the back), and let the callout captions grow.
4. **Callout captions on 7-pin cards.** EM-011 Guest Welcome and EM-012 Departure carry **7 numbered captions**; the caption text is tiny and cramped. **Cap callouts at 5–6.**
5. **Stat sunburst badges (7X, 20 HOURS, 30%, 1 POUND, 100+).** The thin radial tick rings will moiré/break up at small size and in print. **Thicken the strokes or swap to a solid ring.** The numbers themselves are fine.

What survives fine: the top meta bar (code, family label, DIFFICULTY/FRICTION/PERMANENT BONUS stars), the big title, the WHY IT MATTERS paragraph, BEST PRACTICES bullets, and the footer tagline. Those are the load-bearing elements — protect them and give them the room freed by the cuts above.

## 3. Callout numbering on the front photos

Generally clear — solid numbered pins (green discs on EM/ET, red on EP) with a matched numbered caption list. Good. Issues:

- **EP-009-a Mud Trail — duplicate pin "3".** Two separate pins are both labeled **3** (one on the boots, one on the floor mat). Fix numbering before use, or prefer the base EP-009 (clean 1–5).
- **EM-012 Departure Checklist — appears to repeat "5".** The Outbox/Drop-Bin marker shows twice; verify against the 7-item caption list.
- **Pin/caption density:** on EM-011 and EM-012 the 7 pins crowd the scene and the captions shrink below comfortable reading size (see §2.4).
- **Pin placement is otherwise good** — pins sit on the object, not floating, and match caption order left-to-right/top-to-bottom on the photoreal cards.

## 4. AI-image artifacts (named)

**Critical — garbled baked-in text:**
- **EM-005 Shoe Zone (front).** The entire bottom-left block is nonsense AI text — "PHEE GUESST FRIENDLY," "Shoe Shoey," "CARGEN ACB EARNS," a scrambled checklist — **plus a fake/non-functional QR code.** This card cannot ship. Regenerate; the info row (RESET/MAINTENANCE/FAMILY FRIENDLY) must be set as real vector type, not generated into the image.
- **EM-006 Coat Storage (front).** Same failure: the bottom info row is garbled ("RESET E8ENFE," "Phep comaned," "Fleey Conoed," "Rat 6mooed," "Rably nay be equipped"). Regenerate.

**Brand / trademark risk (baked-in real logos):**
- **ET-007 Label Maker.** A photoreal **DYMO** unit with legible "DYMO" and "LabelManager 160" branding. Trademark exposure on a product you sell. Regenerate as a generic label maker, or blur/remove the wordmark.
- **EP-005 Package Pile.** The boxes carry **Amazon-style Prime tape and smile marks.** Same risk. Regenerate with plain/kraft parcels and neutral tape.

**Wrong data baked/placed on the card:**
- **ET-012 Charging Station (back).** The routing uses the **wrong code family**: NEXT CARD reads "**ID-001** Key Bowl" and the RELATED CARD PATH points "to **ID-001 / IH-001 / IS-001 / IU-001 / IW-001 / IE-001**" — I-series codes on an Entryway (E-series) card, and "Key Bowl" is really ET-001. Correct to E-series.
- **Internal code vs filename collisions** (will corrupt NEXT CARD / RELATED CARD navigation):
  - `EM-001-...-a.png` is internally coded **EN-002** (should be EM-001).
  - `EM-004-Entryway-Landing-Zone.png` is internally **EM-004** but titled *Landing Zone* — EM-004 is Mail Station; Landing Zone is EM-002. Duplicate/again-used code.
  - `EM-008-...-a.png` is internally coded **EM-009**, colliding with the real EM-009 Umbrella Station.

**Minor scene artifacts:** EM-005's illustrated shoe row has a couple of duplicated/melting shoe forms; EM-004 Mail (base) has key fobs with illegible micro-labels. Neither is shipping-critical but both improve on a photoreal regenerate.

**No malformed hands/limbs** were found (few figures in scene), and signage that *is* meant to be decorative (WELCOME HOME, RAIN OR SHINE, DROP PACK SET GO, whiteboards) reads cleanly on the photoreal cards — the garble is confined to the two cards above.

## 5. Color / brand / print

- **Family coding works** where it's applied correctly: EP red and ET green are consistent and instantly legible; DIFFICULTY vs FRICTION LEVEL vs PERMANENT BONUS in the meta bar is a nice, clear family tell. **The only failure is the Template-B EM cards borrowing red/orange** (see §1B) — fix and the system is coherent.
- **Contrast/accessibility:** dark forest-green and deep-red headers with white knockout type are well above AA. The green *title* type on white fronts is fine. Watch the **cream benefit band** on ET cards — the mid-gray body text on cream is the lowest-contrast text in the deck; nudge it darker.
- **Rich black / ink build:** ensure the dark green header and red footer are defined spot/near-solid builds, not 4-color muddy blacks, or small knockout type will fill in on press. The footer bars run to the card edge — **they need full bleed** (extend art 3 mm past trim); today the rounded white card border implies the color stops at the die line.
- **Thin strokes:** the sunburst stat rings and some line icons are hairline — below reliable min stroke for small offset/digital. **Thicken to ≥ 0.75 pt at final size.**
- **Trim / safe margin:** content (footer bars, corner house icons, the Template-B red triangle) sits close to the edge. Establish a **~3 mm bleed + ~3 mm safe margin** and pull the corner icons and footer text inside it. **Corner radius is inconsistent** — some fronts show squared green top corners (EM-001), others fully rounded white cards. Lock one die-cut radius.
- **Proof marks in the art:** the "FRONT"/"BACK" labels above Template-B cards, and any drop shadow implying a floating card, must be removed for a print file (they read as UI mockup, not a die-cut card).

## 6. Variant "-a" pairs — keep/cut

- **EM-001 Front Door → KEEP BASE.** Base is photoreal, Template A, correct green color coding, correct code. The **-a** is illustration, Template B (red symptoms), and mis-coded EN-002. Base wins on every axis.
- **EM-004 Mail Station → KEEP BASE (back), borrow -a composition.** Both are illustrations. Base uses the on-brand Template A (green symptoms, italic footer); -a is Template B (red symptoms). But **-a has the cleaner front scene** ("One-Touch Mail Rule," tidier hooks) vs base's fussy key-fob/tool clutter. Ideal: base's Template-A back + -a's cleaner layout, regenerated photoreal.
- **EM-008 Pet Station → KEEP BASE.** Base is Template A / correct code. **-a is mis-coded EM-009 and uses Template B (red).** The -a's cute dog adds warmth and appeal, so **fold a pet into the base regeneration** — but keep base's code and template.
- **EP-009 Mud Trail → KEEP BASE.** Both are photoreal and Template A (good). Base has **clean 1–5 numbering** and a stronger "mud tracked from the open door" narrative. **-a has a duplicate "3" pin** and a tote-bag hero that reads less like an entryway mud problem; its only edge is a more logical NEXT CARD (EP-010 Sports Gear vs base's ET-001 Key Bowl). Recommendation: **ship base**, and if you want -a's next-card link, fix the duplicate pin and re-point base's NEXT CARD instead.

**Pattern:** in every pair the base is the more correct card (photoreal and/or Template A and/or correct code); the "-a" files are the older Template-B generation. Retire the "-a" set once their few better ideas (cleaner Mail composition, the pet, EP-010 routing) are merged into the bases.

## 7. Front/back balance & the one thing to simplify

Both sides try to be a full page. The back is the worse offender — it carries eight sections. **The one thing to simplify deck-wide: collapse the back's bottom third (PROGRESS TRACKER + the 9-icon RELATED CARD PATH) into a single slim strip** — 3 tracker checkboxes on the left, up to 5 category icons with no sub-codes on the right. That one change (a) recovers the most illegible real estate, (b) removes the zone where nearly every data error hides, and (c) lets DID YOU KNOW / NEXT CARD breathe. On the front, drop PRO TIP (it repeats on the back) to give callout captions and the GAME EFFECT row usable size.

## DECK-WIDE: top 8 visual fixes, ranked by impact

1. **Unify the medium to photoreal.** Regenerate the ~12 illustrated EM cards as photographs to match EP/ET (and EM-001, EM-009). Biggest single lift to "one deck." *(Style)*
2. **Fix the two broken cards now.** EM-005 and EM-006 have garbled gibberish (and a fake QR) baked into the front — regenerate, and set all info-row text as real vector type, never generated. *(Blocker)*
3. **Kill Template B / restore color coding.** Every EM card gets green "COMMON SYMPTOMS," green trophy, the 2-line italic footer, and no FRONT/BACK proof labels. Removes red/orange from green cards. *(Brand)*
4. **Simplify the back's bottom third.** 3 checkboxes + ≤5 code-less category icons. Recovers legibility and eliminates the error-prone micro-codes. *(Legibility)*
5. **Correct all card codes.** ET-012's I-series back → E-series; fix internal codes on the "-a" files (EN-002, the duplicate EM-004, the EM-009 collision) so NEXT CARD / RELATED CARD navigation is trustworthy. *(Data integrity)*
6. **Remove real brand logos.** ET-007 DYMO and EP-005 Amazon → generic. *(Legal/print)*
7. **Set up print mechanics.** 3 mm bleed on the full-edge footer bars, 3 mm safe margin (pull corner icons in), one locked corner radius, defined green/red ink builds (no muddy rich black), thicken hairline strokes (stat rings, icons) to ≥0.75 pt at size. *(Print-readiness)*
8. **Trim front density + fix pin numbering.** Cap callouts at 5–6 (EM-011/EM-012), drop front PRO TIP, and fix duplicate pins (EP-009-a "3", EM-012 "5"). *(Legibility/QA)*

## House visual standard (the rule the deck should hold to)

- **One medium:** photoreal hero, natural window light, ~35–50 mm eye-level lens, warm neutral palette, real entryway props — no illustration, no logos, no baked-in body text.
- **One family color, applied by role:** Micro Zone = forest green, Problem = deep red, Tool = forest green; the meta bar (DIFFICULTY / FRICTION LEVEL / PERMANENT BONUS) is the family tell. Symptom header + trophy always match the card's own family color.
- **One back template (Template A):** family-colored COMMON SYMPTOMS, WHY IT MATTERS paragraph, ≤6 BEST PRACTICES bullets, HOME QUEST, DID YOU KNOW (solid-ring stat badge), NEXT CARD, and a single slim routing strip (3 checkboxes + ≤5 code-less icons). Two-line italic tagline in the beige footer with star + bookmark. No FRONT/BACK proof marks.
- **One legibility floor:** no live text below ~6 pt at 2.5 × 3.5 in; ≥5:1 contrast for body; ≤6 numbered callouts per hero; hairlines ≥0.75 pt.
- **One geometry:** locked corner radius, 3 mm bleed, 3 mm safe margin, full-bleed footer bars, defined ink builds.
- **QA gate before render-to-print:** every card's internal code must equal its filename; NEXT CARD and RELATED CARD targets must resolve within the deck; zero generated body text; zero real trademarks.
