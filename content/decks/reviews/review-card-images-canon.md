# Entryway Deck — Card-Image Content & Canon Review

**Reviewer role:** Lean Six Sigma Master Black Belt / 6S canon guardian
**Scope:** 35 PNG card images in `Entryway Deck\card-images\` (front + back read on each)
**Canon reference (non-negotiable):** the six S's are exactly, in order —
**Sort · Straighten · Shine · Safety (4th S) · Standardize · Sustain.**
The system uses **"Straighten,"** NOT the 5S term **"Set in Order."** Safety is the 4th S and the signature difference. The book **retired the friction meter** — any "Friction Level" is a decision point.

---

## HEADLINE FINDINGS (read this first)

1. **"Set in Order" (a 5S term the system rejects) appears in the 6S LESSON block of 22 of the 35 images.** This is the single biggest canon defect. It must read **"Straighten,"** and on most cards the lesson also names the *wrong* S for the card's actual theme.
2. **Card-code chaos:** the same code is reused for different cards (EM-004 = Landing Zone AND Mail Station ×2; EM-009 = Pet Station-a AND Umbrella Station), one whole card is coded **EN-002**, and multiple broken prefixes exist (**EN-001, ID-001, IH-/IS-/IU-/IW-/IE-, IW-001**), plus **"EVERTS"** typos and self-referential links.
3. **The retired friction meter is still live on all 9 Problem (EP) cards** as a "FRICTION LEVEL" star gauge.

8. **Image-generation garble** on the FRONT bottom-left of EM-005 and EM-006 (nonsense text + fake QR).

### B. Friction-meter decision (book retired it)

- The **"FRICTION LEVEL" star gauge is present on all 9 EP problem cards** (EP-004…EP-012, incl. both EP-009 variants). This is the retired meter.
- IMPACT blocks also lean on friction language ("High Daily Friction / Wastes X min/day").
- **Recommendation:** remove the "FRICTION LEVEL" gauge deck-wide. Replace with either a neutral **Severity/Impact** label or the book's current **before/after signature**. Keep the "wastes X min/day" only if reframed as plain impact, not "friction." (EM cards correctly use "DIFFICULTY," ET cards use "PERMANENT/TEMPORARY BONUS" — those are fine and can stay.)
