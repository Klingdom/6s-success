Entryway Deck · card-image review

Strong cards. Not yet one deck, one game, or fully on-canon.

Three expert reviewers (a 6S Black Belt, a print designer, and a game designer) each opened the 35 finished card images. They independently converged on the same conclusion: the art and information design are genuinely good, but a shared set of template defects — a canon slip, colliding card codes, and a decorative game layer — must be fixed before this prints or plays.

Canon

“Set in Order” (the rejected 5S term) is templated onto 22 cards; the retired friction meter is back on all 9 problem cards; codes collide.

Visual

Reads as two products: EP/ET are camera-ready photos, but ~12 Micro-Zone cards are painterly and off-brand; two cards have garbled text; two carry real logos.

Game

The economy is decorative — no rules, no win condition, scoring is self-ticked, and the Related-Card-Path is full of copy-paste code bugs.

The game layer — the loop it should be

On your turn, face the most urgent Problem afflicting a Zone (its Friction both rewards solving it and penalizes the household each round it festers). Spend Momentum to equip a Tool that fixes it, then do the real reset and score the before/after delta of the card’s own Common-Symptoms list — you can’t score without changing the room. Solved Zones advance the six S tracks and pay Momentum, which you reinvest in stronger Tools (each a defined Permanent Bonus that lowers a Zone’s upkeep) and in Experts to blunt Event cards. A Zone you stop maintaining decays. You win the Entryway when all six S tracks are full and every Zone holds a verified 7-day streak — then it hands off to the next room.

The house standard to hold the deck to

Canon

  • Six S’s only, in order; the word is Straighten, never “Set in Order”
  • Each 6S LESSON names the S the card is actually about
  • Safety is the 4th S — give it a visible beat in the deck
  • No invented stats; soften or source every number

Visual

  • One medium: photoreal, natural light, real props — no illustration, no logos, no baked-in body text
  • One back template (green symptoms on Micro-Zone cards)
  • No live text below ~6pt at 2.5×3.5in; ≤6 callouts
  • 3mm bleed + safe margin; one corner radius; defined ink builds

Game

  • A Rules Card + glossary; one canonical start (EM-001)
  • Score the before/after audit delta, not checkboxes
  • Momentum has a sink and a target; Zones can decay
  • Every card’s code = its filename; every link resolves in-box
