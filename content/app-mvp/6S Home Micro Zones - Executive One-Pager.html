The problem

People start and quit getting organized because the job feels too big, generic apps do not tell you how, and the AI that could coach you usually wants photos of your home uploaded to a server.

The product

Break the home into small micro zones and run each through the six-step 6S method in about ten minutes: Sort, Straighten, Shine, Safety, Standardize, Sustain. A before and after photo bracket every zone and the after becomes its standard.

Why it wins (three pillars)

It finishes. Micro zones plus a visible before-and-after make starting easy and completion satisfying.
It sticks. The after photo is the standard; a light sustain audit on your rhythm keeps it that way.
It respects your home. On supported iPhones the AI reads your photos on-device. Nothing is uploaded.

Business model

  • Free app: full method, all launch rooms, on-device AI.
  • 6S Plus $4.99/mo or $39.99/yr (7-day trial): cloud AI, analytics, family sharing, expanded rooms.
  • Products: affiliate now, 6S own-label later.
  • Ecosystem: book, Micro Zone Manual, video series.

The economics in one line

On-device AI is free to serve, so free users are contribution-positive (via affiliate), not a cost. ~$31/yr contribution per Plus subscriber; blended ~$1.60 per install. The constraint is acquisition cost, so the plan is organic-first (ASO + the book/video ecosystem).

Status & differentiation

A runnable MVP is built and verified (full core loop, three-tier AI architecture), and the Apple on-device AI module is complete and ready to build. No competitor combines an operational step method, per-zone photo proof, a built-in safety pass, private on-device AI, and a sustain loop. That bundle, plus the content ecosystem, is the moat.

The decision requested

Approve the September 1, 2026 iOS launch and the resourcing to close the six-week critical path. The runway is aggressive; the primary risk is the native iOS build, de-risked by shipping a native shell around the already-verified core plus the finished on-device AI module. Fallbacks if the date is at risk: a later date, or a smaller launch room set.
