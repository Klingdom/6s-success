Descriptionlimit 4000 ~1,650 / 4000
Getting organized shouldn't take a whole weekend of heroics. 6S Home Micro Zones breaks your home into small "micro zones", a single drawer, a landing surface, one shelf, and walks you through each one in about ten minutes. Finish one, then the next, until the whole home is done. Every zone runs through the proven six-step 6S method: - Sort: keep only what belongs - Straighten: give everything a home - Shine: clean the surface - Safety: find and fix hazards - Standardize: make the right place obvious - Sustain: keep it with a two-minute check on your rhythm Take a before photo and an after photo, and the app shows them side by side with a count of what you moved out of your home. The after photo becomes the zone's standard: what "done" looks like next time. PRIVATE AI COACHING THAT NEVER SEES YOUR PHOTOS Turn on optional AI and, on supported iPhones, it runs entirely on your device. It reads your before photo to score clutter and suggest a plan, flags safety hazards, and reviews your finished work, without a single photo ever leaving your phone. It even works in airplane mode. Prefer no AI? The full method has clear built-in guidance. MADE FOR REAL LIFE - Start small: the app points you to one quick win, not a giant to-do list - Works offline; no account required - Your photos and progress stay on your device - Friendly to busy parents and anyone who gets overwhelmed by clutter FREE, WITH AN OPTIONAL UPGRADE The full six-step method, all launch rooms, on-device AI, before-and-after, and the sustain audit are free. 6S Plus ($4.99/month or $39.99/year, with a 7-day free trial) adds cloud AI coaching, a deeper safety check, whole-home analytics, family sharing, and the expanded room library. No ads. We never sell your data. Part of 6S Success Home Edition: the book, the Micro Zone Manual, and the video series. Download free and reset your first zone today.
