1. Positioning & pricing (tuned)

Positioning statement For overwhelmed households who have tried and quit getting organized, 6S Micro Zones is the home-organization app that turns the whole house into finishable ten-minute wins and keeps them that way. Unlike cleaning-schedule apps that only tell you when, or method books that stay on the shelf, 6S puts a proven operational method in your hand zone by zone, with visible before-and-after proof and private on-device AI coaching.

Three pillars: it finishes (micro zones + before/after) · it sticks (standards + sustain) · it respects your home (local-first, on-device AI). Category: home organization & decluttering (lifestyle-productivity crossover).

Pricing rationale: anchored between habit apps (~$3 to $5 per month) and premium coaching apps (~$10+). The free tier is deliberately generous because on-device AI makes free users nearly costless to serve, so they function as top-of-funnel and an affiliate base rather than a cost center. Annual pricing lifts lifetime value and smooths churn.

The core economic insight Because on-device AI is free to serve, free users are contribution-positive (via affiliate), not a cost sink. So the constraint is not cost of goods, it is cost of acquisition. Blended contribution per install at these targets is roughly $1.60 (about $0.04×$31 from Plus, plus ~$0.40 from free). CAC must stay well below that, which is why the strategy is organic-first: App Store optimization plus the book, manual, and video ecosystem as owned acquisition channels.

4. Launch timeline: critical path to September 1

Roughly a six-week runway from late July. It is aggressive, and the biggest schedule risk is the native iOS build. The plan de-risks it by shipping a native iOS shell around the already-verified core plus the completed on-device AI module, rather than a from-scratch UI rebuild.

September 1 readiness gate (all must be green)

  • Native iOS app approved on the App Store, with privacy label, privacy policy, and camera/photo permission strings.
  • On-device AI module device-tested on Apple Intelligence hardware; graceful fallback verified on older iPhones.
  • Photos encrypted at rest; Cloud-tier consent flow live (already built in the beta).
  • Zero-retention proxy deployed with rate limiting, pointed at the low-cost model with prompt caching.
  • Launch room content complete and reviewed; in-app feedback + photo-free diagnostic export working.
  • Store listing assets and privacy-respecting crash/analytics-lite pipeline in place.
