The game

Everyone gets a wipe-clean house sheet of rooms drawn as micro-zone squares. Roll the custom six-S dice and mark or clear squares in S-order, racing a shared clock. Push your luck for bigger combos, or bank your progress before a clutter roll triggers "life happens."

How it plays
  • Roll the six-S dice; mark or clear zone squares following the S-order.
  • Push your luck for room combos, or bank before a clutter roll strikes.
  • Optional real-time mode: everyone resets at once against one sand timer.
  • Score the calm on your friction track when time is up.
