Six S: The Home Reset

Beat the clutter together, before the house wins.

The whole family teams up to reset a house full of clutter, walking a shared friction meter from chaos to calm, before life buries a room.
1–5 players Ages 8+ 30–45 min Weight Light–medium MSRP $39 Mechanisms: Co-op, action points, set collection, modular board
The game

A house is built from modular room tiles. Each round, an event deck (guests, mail, spills, new toys) drops clutter and pushes a physical friction dial back toward chaos. Players spend actions to run the six S's on micro-zone cards, and win by walking the shared needle to calm, together.

How it plays
  • Draw an event: clutter enters rooms, the needle drifts to chaos.
  • Run the six S's on a zone: Sort through the Five Doors, Straighten, Shine, fix with Safety, Standardize to lock it.
  • Standardized, sustained zones defend themselves; unprotected ones slide back.
  • Win as a team by reaching the goal before a room is overwhelmed.
