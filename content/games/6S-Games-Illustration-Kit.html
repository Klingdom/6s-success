The super prompt · the Style Bible (built into every figure below)

This is the single "super prompt." It already leads every figure prompt in this kit; you can also paste it alone and describe your own scene.

6S SUCCESS BOARD GAMES -- HOUSE ILLUSTRATION STYLE (paste-in, do not change)
A premium, warm, characterful illustration style for a family home-organization board-game line. In the spirit of modern hobby board-game box art (cozy, inviting, a touch playful) crossed with DK Eyewitness / Kinfolk warmth and clean, calm composition. Every image must look like it came from the same illustrator as the last.

VISUAL FAMILY. Hand-illustrated, warm flat color with soft shading (not photoreal, not clip-art, not childish cartoon). Cozy, lived-in Scandinavian-modern homes: oak wood, white walls, plants, natural textiles, soft daylight. Real, diverse families and households in natural poses, never staged stock smiles. Generous whitespace, one clear focal point, balanced composition.

PALETTE (use these, warm and muted). Cream #F7F2E9 and warm white #FBF7EF grounds; warm near-black #2B2622 for line; terracotta #BC4B2A as the primary accent; honey amber #DDA63A for highlights; slate blue #3C5A6B for calm and labels; soft green #6E8B5B for calm and success; deep slate #22323C for rich dark grounds. Use a small amount of red ONLY to mark clutter or chaos.

THE SIGNATURE MOTIF. The friction meter: a semicircular gauge that sweeps from a red CHAOS zone through amber to a green CALM zone, with a needle. And the six steps drawn as a chaos-to-calm color ramp (red to green). Use the gauge and/or the ramp as the recurring visual DNA of the whole line.

TEXT INSIDE THE IMAGE. Almost none. Leave clean, uncluttered space where a title or logo will be added later in layout. At most a few one-to-three-word labels, spelled correctly and crisply. Never bake sentences, rules, or paragraphs into the art.

HARD RULES (these prevent the defects that keep appearing).
- If the six steps appear, they are EXACTLY, in this order: Sort, Straighten, Shine, Safety, Standardize, Sustain. Safety is the FOURTH step. Never a five-step list, never Safety omitted or moved, never "Set in Order," never an invented extra S.
- NO QR codes anywhere.
- No brand names, logos, real product packaging, or recognizable book or box covers.
- No em dashes and no long text; when in doubt, remove the text.
- No clip-art, no googly-eyed mascots, no corporate stock look, no generic AI-slop sheen, no garbled lettering, no neon or video-game palette.

AVOID. Luxury mansions, sterile minimalism, PowerPoint or Canva graphics, busy layouts, tiny unreadable text, staged smiles.
How to use it (the drift-free workflow)
  1. Work ONE image per chat. Open a fresh ChatGPT conversation for every figure. Never generate two figures in one thread. Each prompt below already carries the full style, so the model needs no memory of the last image, this is what stops the drift.
  2. Generate GAME-01 first. Regenerate until it is right. This first approved image is your STYLE ANCHOR for the whole line.
  3. For every later figure, open a fresh chat, ATTACH the anchor image, then paste the figure's prompt (each one ends by telling the model to match the attached reference's style, palette, and warmth exactly).
  4. The six-S ramp (COMP-02) is the one figure a model loves to get wrong. Its prompt hard-locks the six names and order; if any label comes out misspelled, reworded, reordered, or with Safety missing or moved, reject and regenerate. When in doubt, set that one as clean vector text in the page layout instead of generated art.
  5. If a figure drifts, do not argue with it in the thread. Start a fresh chat, re-attach the anchor, paste the prompt again. Fresh context beats correction.
  6. Name each saved file by its figure code (game-01.png, box-01.png ...) so it drops straight into the matching document.
Before you use any image · the 30-second canon check
  • The six S's, if shown, read exactly Sort, Straighten, Shine, Safety, Standardize, Sustain, in order, Safety fourth. This is the single most common failure. Reject any image that drops Safety, reorders the steps, says Set in Order, or invents an extra S.
  • No QR codes. No brand names, logos, or real product packaging. No misspelled or garbled words.
  • No rules text, sentences, or numbers baked into the art that could be wrong. Keep title space clean for layout.
  • It matches the palette and the friction-meter motif, and looks like the same hand as the anchor.
