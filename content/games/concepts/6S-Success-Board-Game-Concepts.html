Route clutter through the Five Doors: donate, sell, store, recycle, throw. A card-routing / hand-management move.

Concept 01 · Flagship · Cooperative family game

Six S: The Home Reset

Beat the clutter together, before the house wins.

1–5 playersAges 8+30–45 minWeight light-mediumCo-op · familyTarget $39

The board & layouts

A house is built from modular room tiles in the middle of the table. Each room holds a strip of its real micro-zone cards. A physical friction-meter dial with a moving needle is the shared progress track: it starts in the red "chaos" arc and the whole table is trying to walk it into the green "calm" arc, to the GOAL, together.

Core loop

  • Life happens. Draw an event (guests, mail pile, a spill, new toys). Clutter cubes drop into rooms and the needle drifts back toward chaos.
  • Work the zones. On your turn, spend action points to run the six S's on micro-zone cards, in order: Sort the clutter through the Five Doors box, Straighten by placing a home token, Shine to flip the zone clean and reveal a hazard, fix it with Safety, then Standardize to lock it.
  • Hold the line. Sustained, standardized zones defend themselves; unprotected ones slide back when life happens again.
  • Win together by walking the shared needle to the goal before the clutter deck buries a room. Lose if any room is fully overwhelmed.

Why it wins & how it fits the brand

This is the teaching game and the flagship: cooperative, family-friendly, and it literally rehearses the method, "a little, often, together." Start with five rooms and unlock the other fifteen as expansions, giving a long tail and a legacy-lite campaign. Every box is a funnel to the real kits and the app, and it is the most natural retail and gift-market fit.

Concept 02 · Strategy · Competitive engine / kit-builder

Micro Zone

Reset more rooms, with fewer products, than your rivals.

2–4 playersAges 12+45–60 minWeight mediumCompetitiveTarget $55

The board & layouts

Each player runs a home-reset practice on a personal house board. A central market offers product cards (the 123-item catalog, drawn as the seven families) and micro-zone contracts. The friction gauge is the shared round track.

The heart: the overlap engine

The real business moat becomes the strategy. A lean shared core of common products (cloths, cleaners, bins, labels, safety) powers many zones; zone-specific add-ons finish a contract. The player who reuses core products across the most zones builds the most efficient engine. Complete a micro-zone, tuck it into your house, and it pays out calm points each round if kept Standardized and Sustained, or slides back and bleeds points if neglected.

The six S's as actions

  • Sort & Straighten: draft and place product cards into a zone kit.
  • Shine & Safety: flip the zone clean and secure its hazard to certify it.
  • Standardize: lock a completed zone so it keeps scoring.
  • Sustain: pay upkeep; skip it and entropy claims your best rooms.

Why it wins & how it fits the brand

This is the hobbyist and gift-for-the-organized title: replayable, strategic, and it quietly teaches the economics that make the real business work, that a smart kit reuses a shared core. It gives the brand credibility with the strategy-game audience and a premium price point, and it doubles as an internal training tool for consultants and retail partners.

Concept 03 · Fast & portable · Flip-and-write / real-time

The 15-Minute Reset

One short burst. How much calm can you bank before life resumes?

1–6 playersAges 8+15–20 minWeight very lightRoll/flip-and-write · real-time optionTarget $19

The board & layouts

Everyone gets a wipe-clean house sheet: rooms drawn as grids of micro-zone squares. Custom dice show the six S faces plus a clutter face. On your turn you flip or roll, then mark or clear zone squares, obeying the S-order (you must Sort before you Straighten a zone, Shine before Safety), racing a shared 15-minute clock.

Core loop & the twist

  • Push your luck. Keep resetting for a bigger combo, or bank it, because a clutter face triggers "life happens" and can undo an unfinished zone.
  • Combos. Finish a whole room, or run all six S's on one zone, for calm bonuses.
  • Real-time variant. Everyone resets at once against one sand timer, the closest thing to the actual daily reset.
  • Score the calm on your friction track when time is up. Fastest to a full, standardized home wins.

Fun components

Wipe-clean house sheetsSix-S custom diceDry-erase markersSand timerShared friction trackCombo cardsTiny box (impulse / travel)

Why it wins & how it fits the brand

Cheapest to make, fastest to teach, tiny to ship: the impulse buy, the checkout-counter and app-bundle product, and the lowest-risk way to validate the whole game line. It is the most direct translation of the book's fifteen-minute reset, and it pairs naturally with the phone app as a physical companion.
