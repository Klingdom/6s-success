The 15-Minute
Reset

One short burst. How much calm can you bank before life resumes?

1–6 playersAges 8+15–20 minRoll-and-write · push-your-luck
The idea, in one breath

A home slides into clutter a little every day. In one short reset you race the six S's, Sort, Straighten, Shine, Safety, Standardize, Sustain, across your rooms, filling each micro-zone in order. Push your luck for the numbers you need, but every re-roll ticks the clock toward "life happens." When time is up, the tidiest home wins.

What you need (print & play)
  • 1 House Sheet per player (page 3, print one each).
  • A pen or pencil each. Laminate or use a sleeve to play again.
  • 3 ordinary six-sided dice (shared). Read each die as an S, see the key.
  • Optional: a sand timer for the real-time mode, or fold the six-S die on page 4.

Setup (60 seconds)
  1. Give every player a House Sheet and a pen.
  2. Put the 3 dice in the middle.
  3. Pick the Rounds mode (12 rounds) or the Real-time mode (a 15-minute timer). New players: start with Rounds.
  4. Youngest player goes first, then clockwise.
The one golden rule

A micro-zone must be reset in order: Sort, then Straighten, then Shine, then Safety, then Standardize. You can only fill a zone's next open box, and only with the matching die. A 6 (Sustain) is wild and fills any next box. That is the whole puzzle.

The 15-Minute Reset

How to play

Your goal

Reset as many micro-zones as you can before time runs out. Each zone you finish adds Calm. The most Calm wins.

A round

The active player rolls all 3 dice once for the whole table (everyone uses the same roll, so no one waits).

Then every player, at the same time, applies the dice to their own sheet:

  • A die showing N fills box N of a micro-zone, but only if it is that zone's next open box (boxes before it already filled).
  • Spread your 3 dice across different zones, or advance one zone if the numbers line up.
  • A 6 / Sustain is wild: fill any zone's next box, or erase one Clutter mark, or shield a finished zone.
  • You never have to use a die. Fill a zone's 5th box and the zone is reset, circle its star.
Push your luck

Chasing the exact number you need? After the roll, the active player may re-roll any dice up to twice to fish for better faces. The whole table lives with the final roll.

But every re-roll ticks the clock. Each re-roll, every player marks one box of the shared Life Happens meter.

Life happens

When the Life Happens meter fills (6 marks), life interrupts:

  • Roll one die. That room number takes a Clutter mark on its next open box (scribble an X): it is blocked until a Sustain erases it.
  • Then clear the meter and play on.

So pushing for one more re-roll helps you, but nudges everyone toward chaos. That is the game.

When time is up

Rounds mode: after 12 rounds, stop. Real-time mode: when the 15-minute timer buzzes, pens down.

Scoring · count your Calm
  • Each reset micro-zone: +2
  • Each fully reset room (all its zones): +3
  • Whole home reset (every room): +6
  • Each leftover Clutter mark: −1

Circle your total on the Calm track. Highest Calm wins. Tie: fewest Clutter, then most finished rooms.

Three ways to play
  • Rounds (default): 12 shared rolls, turn by turn.
  • Real-time (15:00): each player rolls their own dice as fast as they can against one timer. The truest reset.
  • Family / age 6+: ignore the S-order, skip Life Happens, just reset the most zones.

Reference & make-a-die

Turn, in six words

Quick reminders
  • Fill a zone 1→5 only. Die must match the next open box.
  • 6 = Sustain = wild: any next box, or erase a Clutter, or shield a done zone.
  • Every re-roll = +1 to Life Happens (shared). At 6, a room gets Clutter.
  • Five boxes done = circle the star, that zone is reset.
  • Time up = score Calm. Most Calm wins.
Designer notes (for playtesters)

Tuning knobs if a game runs long or short: change rounds (10–14), Life-Happens cap (5–7), or dice (2 for a tighter squeeze, 4 for a looser one). Track which zones finish most, that guides which real kits to feature. Please note your player count, time, and final scores and send them in.

Make the six-S die (optional)

Any d6 works with the number key. To make the themed die, cut this cross out, fold on the lines, and glue the tabs, or just print it as a sticker set for a blank die.
