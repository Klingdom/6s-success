Six S:
The Home Reset

Beat the clutter together, before the house wins.

1–5 playersAges 8+30–45 minCooperative
The idea

Life keeps dropping clutter into your rooms. Working together, spend your actions to Sort the mess and run the six S's on each micro-zone, in order, to reset it. Every zone you reset walks a shared Friction Needle from Chaos toward the Goal. Reach the Goal to win, but if any room is buried in clutter, the house wins.

Components (print & play)
  • 1 House Board + Friction Meter (page 3).
  • Life Happens deck (pages 4–5, cut out).
  • Role cards and tokens (page 6): clutter cubes, reset markers, standard shields, the needle marker.
  • Tip: use coins or beads for clutter if you prefer not to cut chits.
How you win / lose
  • Win: move the Needle to the Goal (12).
  • Lose: any room fills its clutter track (4), or the Life Happens deck runs out first.
Setup
  1. Put the House Board and Friction Meter in the middle. Needle marker on 0 (Chaos).
  2. Shuffle the Life Happens deck. Difficulty = deck size: Easy 14 cards · Standard 18 · Hard 20.
  3. Each player takes (or is dealt) a Role card.
  4. Seed clutter: add 1 clutter to three rooms (roll a die for each).
  5. Youngest player is first. Advise each other freely, the active player decides.
The golden rule

A zone's S-icons must be marked in printed order, and you can only work a zone in a room with 2 or fewer clutter. So Sort first, then clean. Finish all icons = the zone is reset: shield it and move the Needle +1.

How to play

On your turn

Play goes around the table. Each turn has two steps.

1 · Life Happens
  • Draw the top Life Happens card and resolve it. Clutter cubes drop into the named rooms.
  • If a room would go past 4 clutter, it is Overwhelmed, the team loses immediately. Watch your weak rooms.
2 · Reset (3 actions)

Spend 3 actions in any mix (your Role may change this):

  • Sort (1 action): remove up to 2 clutter from one room (route it out the Five Doors).
  • Work a zone (1 action): mark the next S-icon on a zone whose room has ≤2 clutter. Order matters.
  • Finish a zone's last icon: it is reset, place a shield and move the Needle +1. Shielded zones are safe.
Reading a zone

Each zone shows a recipe of coloured S-icons, e.g. 1 3 5. Mark them left to right (put a reset marker on each). A hazard zone includes 4 Safety.

1 Sort · 2 Straighten · 3 Shine · 4 Safety · 5 Standardize.

Roles

Each player keeps one Role for the game (see page 6). They make the team more than the sum of its parts, the whole point of a cooperative reset.

Winning together
  • The Needle only moves forward, by resetting zones. Reach 12 (Goal) and the home is calm, you all win.
  • Talk it out: which room is about to overwhelm? Who should Sort, who should clean?
Make it easier / harder
  • Easier: smaller deck, start with less clutter, Goal 10.
  • Harder: bigger deck, Goal 14, only reset zones move the Needle (no Deep-Breath help).
  • Solo: play two Roles and take two turns per round.
