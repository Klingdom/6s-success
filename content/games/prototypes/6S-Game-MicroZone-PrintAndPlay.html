Micro Zone

Reset more rooms, with fewer products, than your rivals.

2–4 playersAges 12+45–60 minEngine / kit-builder
The idea

You are rival home-reset consultants. Draft product cards into your kit, then complete micro-zone contracts your kit can afford. The trick, and the whole strategy, is overlap: a handful of core products (★) are reusable equipment that satisfy many zones, while add-ons are used up. Build the leanest engine, certify the most valuable zones, and bank the most Calm.

Components
  • 1 Player Board each (page 3, print one per player).
  • Product deck (pages 4–5): core ★ + add-ons.
  • Zone Contract deck (page 6).
  • Tokens & score pad (page 7): Calm points, certification seals, round track.
How you win
  • Most Calm at game end. Certify zones for their value; lock them to keep it.
Setup
  1. Each player takes a Player Board and 2 core products of choice (starting kit).
  2. Shuffle the Product deck; lay a market row of 5 face up.
  3. Shuffle the Contract deck; lay 4 face up.
  4. Set the Round track to 1. Play 5 rounds (each player takes 3 turns per round).
Core vs add-on

Core ★ cards stay in your kit forever and can satisfy any number of zones (reusable equipment). Add-ons are spent, attached to the one zone they complete. Owning the right core early is the engine.

How to play

Your turn — one action
  • Shop: take 1 product from the market into your kit (core → equipment row; add-on → hand). Refill the market.
  • Sign: take 1 zone contract from the display into your queue. Refill.
  • Reset a zone: pick a contract you hold. If your kit owns the core icons it needs and you attach (discard) the add-on it needs, it is done: place a certification seal, put it in your house, and score its Calm now.
  • Lock (Standardize): discard any 1 card to place a Standard on one of your certified zones. Locked zones score a +2 end-game bonus and cannot slide.
The round track

Everyone takes 3 turns per round, 5 rounds. Then final scoring.

Reading a contract

A contract shows the core icons you must own and one add-on you must spend, plus its Calm value. Example: needs Cleaner Caddy + Degreaser, worth 4.

Scoring — count your Calm
  • Each certified zone: its printed value (scored when completed).
  • Each locked zone: +2 at game end.
  • Full room (any 3 zones of the same room): +3.
  • Efficiency: most zones certified from the fewest core cards: +3 to the leanest engine.

Highest Calm wins. Tie: most locked zones.

Playtest knobs
  • Length: 4–6 rounds. Market size 4–6. Starting core 1–3.
  • Note which zones/products get played most, it points to the real kits to feature.
