# 6S Source Data for Game Design
*Extracted from 6S Success: Home Edition (book, Micro Zone Manual, product appendix) as raw material for the board games.*

## The six S's, and how each maps to a mechanic
| S | Meaning | Gameable action |
|---|---------|-----------------|
| 1 Sort | Keep what earns its place | Route clutter through the Five Doors (card routing / hand management) |
| 2 Straighten | A home for everything | Place a "home" token; the zone resists clutter |
| 3 Shine | Clean to inspect | Flip a zone clean; cleaning reveals a hidden hazard |
| 4 Safety | Find and fix hazards (the 4th S) | Ask the five questions, remove the hazard |
| 5 Standardize | Write the best way | Lay a Standard Card / snap the ideal-state photo; locks the zone |
| 6 Sustain | Keep it on rhythm | Spend daily-time tokens; push the shared needle to the goal |

**The overlap moat (the strategy engine for "Micro Zone"):**
- 88 of 123 products (72%) apply to EVERY room -> a lean shared core.
- 33 "Core" level products anchor nearly every kit.
- A typical product recurs across ~7 zones (median); the most common covers 100+.
- Price band $4-$900, typical mid ~$22.

## Other gameable concepts from the book
- **The Five Doors** (Sort decision): Donate, Sell, Store, Recycle, Throw. -> routing box / card slots.
- **Red tags + holding area** (the "Maybe" system): tag an item, set a deadline, decide later. -> a timer/deadline mechanic.
- **The friction meter:** chaos -> calm gauge with a needle and a GOAL. -> the signature score/doom track.
- **Standard card + ideal-state photo:** locks a zone against sliding back. -> a "protect/certify" action.
- **The 15-minute reset:** short, shared, recurring. -> the whole basis of game #3.
- **Daily 6S times, audits, the recurring event (the loop):** -> Sustain / upkeep mechanics.
- **Before/after room signature:** -> end-of-game scoring / photo objective.
- **The antagonist:** clutter & entropy, "because life keeps arriving." -> the event/"life happens" deck.
