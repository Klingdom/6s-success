Privacy, safety & compliance 
 posture The sensitive asset is photos of the inside of people's homes. Design position: photos are private by default, encrypted in transit and at rest, never used for training, never shared without an explicit share action, and deleted with the account. AI analysis is user-initiated per photo set Ñ nothing is scanned passively. This posture is a marketing asset, not just compliance. Children. The app is operated by adults; kids' rooms are content, not users. No child accounts in v1.0 (avoids COPPA scope\ Guidance nudges users to avoid uploading photos with identifiable children. Chemical safety. Cleaning guidance includes incompatibility warnings (already in the substep engine Ñ e.g., chlorine/ammonia\oduct safety notes surface from the catalog; a general "follow product labels" disclaimer sits in Terms and the product sheet. Affiliate disclosure. FTC-compliant "we may earn a commission" at the sourcing surface and in Terms. 15. Engineering roadmap 1 Port & local-first core Ñ ~3 weeks.
