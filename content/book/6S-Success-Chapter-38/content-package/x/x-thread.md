Part Nine reaches the room with no daily user: the guest bedroom. Like the rooms before it, this chapter gives the exact inputs and steps for each zone. But this one you clean for something the others never asked of you.

(223 chars)

---

2/
"The guest bedroom is the one room in the house with no daily user, which is exactly why it fills up and why it goes stale, so here you clean for freshness and for a guest's first evening rather than for spills."

(215 chars)

---

3/
So you air the room as much as you wipe it, you draw a line between what belongs to a visitor and what the household stashed here, and you clean it so it works on the first evening.

(184 chars)

---

4/
"The guest bedroom is five small zones, not one big job: the guest bed and its linens, the guest nightstand, the guest dresser, the guest closet, and the guest welcome and work surface."

(189 chars)

---

5/
Start with the closet, where the overflow actually lives. You cannot judge how much room a guest has while half of it is hidden behind a closet door.

(152 chars)

---

6/
"Nobody sleeps here most nights, so the enemy in a guest room is not spills, it is settled dust and stale, shut-away air." Dust builds in a grey band along a headboard nobody sits up against.

(194 chars)

---

7/
So strip and hot-wash the set on the bed, shake and air the spare set before it goes back, and leave the empty drawers pulled open with the window cracked while you work.

(173 chars)

---

8/
And make the bed up the week the guest arrives, not the month before, because linen folded since spring carries the smell of the cupboard it sat in even though it is clean.

(175 chars)

---

9/
"A guest room fills up because nothing in it ever objects when you set a box down." So the two top drawers and the guest half of the rod belong to the visitor and are never available.

(186 chars)

---

10/
Name the room's second job out loud, the office or the sewing table or the exercise bike, and give it a footprint in tape that clears into one bin in a single trip, or it moves out for good.

(194 chars)

---

11/
"You clean a guest room so it works on a guest's first evening, not so it looks right in a photograph." Switch the lamp on to prove it lights before they are hunting for it.

(177 chars)

---

12/
Charge a real device from each of the two cables you leave out, because an untested cable is not hospitality, it is a trap with a bow on it. Set out the tissues and the wifi card.

(183 chars)

---

13/
"The person who sleeps here does not know this room, and they cross it in the dark on the first night to find the bathroom." So the path between the bed and the door stays clear.

(182 chars)

Do one zone this week, the closet where the overflow lives, and give the next visitor a room that welcomes them. Full steps for all five zones in Chapter 38.
