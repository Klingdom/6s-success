# X Short Posts: The Guest Bedroom

Chapter 38 of *6S Success: Home Edition*. Ten standalone posts, each at or under 280 characters counting its number line. Plain numbering.

---

1/
The guest room is the one room with no daily user, which is exactly why it fills up and goes stale. So you clean it for freshness and a guest's first evening, not for spills. Air it as much as you wipe it.

(208 chars)

---

2/
Nobody sleeps in the guest room most nights, so the enemy is settled dust and stale air. Strip and hot-wash the bed set, shake and air the spare set, and leave the empty drawers open with the window cracked.

(210 chars)

---

3/
Stop making the guest bed up early. Linen folded since spring smells of the cupboard even though it is clean. Wash the set and make it up the week a guest arrives, and open the window while you do it.

(203 chars)

---

4/
Your spare room fills up because nothing in it objects when you set a box down. Draw a line: the guest drawers and the guest half of the rod belong to the visitor and are never available.

(190 chars)

---

5/
Almost every guest room is also the office, the sewing room, or where the exercise bike ended up. Name the second job, give it a footprint in tape, and prove it clears into one bin in a single trip.

(201 chars)

---

6/
An untested cable is a trap with a bow on it. A guest will trust it, plug in, and wake to a phone that never charged. Keep two, one per connector your visitors carry, and prove each one by charging a real device.

(215 chars)

---

7/
Clean a guest room for the first evening, not the photo. Switch the lamp on to prove it lights, and set out the tissues, the coaster, and the wifi card, so a guest at 2am never has to knock on your door.

(206 chars)

---

8/
The guest linen got here because it stopped being good enough for your own bed. The rule: if you would not sleep on it yourself for a week, it is cleaning cloth, not guest linen. Two good sets beat six tired ones.

(216 chars)

---

9/
A guest crosses an unfamiliar room in the dark on the first night to find the bathroom. Keep the path between the bed and the door clear of suitcases, footstools, and extension leads.

(186 chars)

---

10/
A guest room is not ready because it looks tidy. It is ready because you aired the linen, drew a line against the overflow, and tested the lamp and the cables. Chapter 38. Next . Chapter 39, The Kids Bedroom.

(212 chars)
