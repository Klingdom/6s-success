# Chapter 38 Content Package: The Guest Bedroom

The EIGHTH room playbook of Part Nine (Chapters 31 to 50), built to the instruction-centric template. The chapter CORE is the detailed, step-by-step clean-and-shine instruction for each of the five guest-bedroom zones, with the exact inputs.

## The chapter in one line
The guest bedroom is the one room with no daily user, so you clean it for freshness and a guest's first evening, not for spills: air the room and the linen, draw a line against the overflow, and test the things a guest reaches for in the dark.

## The five zones
The guest bed and its linens, the guest nightstand, the guest dresser, the guest closet, and the guest welcome and work surface. Each carries an Inputs list, numbered Steps, and an inspect-and-flag list.

## The guest-bedroom Shine layer (nine rules from Chapter 31 plus)
- Air it, do not chase spills: strip and hot-wash the bed set, shake and air the spare, leave the empty drawers open; make the bed up the week a guest arrives.
- Draw the line: the guest drawers and the guest rod are never available; name the room's second job and give it a taped footprint, or it moves out.
- Clean for the first evening, not the photo: switch the lamp on, charge a device from each of two cables, set out tissues and the wifi card.
- The unfamiliar room in the dark: keep the bed-to-door path clear; tie up looped cords; strap the dresser; take your own medication out.
