# Chapter 38 Repurposing Map: The Guest Bedroom

## Spine (the how-to-clean value, in priority order)
1. The guest-room Shine method: air it not spills, draw the line, clean for the first evening, the room in the dark.
2. The zone-by-zone centerpiece: the exact inputs and numbered steps for the bed, the nightstand, the dresser, the closet, and the welcome desk.
3. The airing pass: hot-wash and air the linen, air the empty drawers, make the bed up the week a guest arrives.
4. The calls that matter: the demoted sheets, the two tested cables, the overflow you told yourself was free, the box from the move, the room's second job.

## Reuse cautions
- Types, not brands, everywhere. No brand names or invented prices.
- Never depict an untested cable offered as ready, or the guest drawers used as household storage.
- Every asset closes on the before-and-after signature (friction meter retired for Part 9).
- Stay in the guest bedroom: do not reuse primary-bedroom (Ch 37) or kids-bedroom (Ch 39) material.
