# Chapter 38 LinkedIn Carousel Outline: The Guest Bedroom

Eleven slides. Product types only.

- Slide 1 (hook): The one room with no daily user, which is exactly why it fills up.
- Slide 2: So clean it for freshness and a guest's first evening, not for spills.
- Slide 3 (air it): the enemy is settled dust and stale air. Air the room, the linen, the empty drawers.
- Slide 4 (five zones): bed, nightstand, dresser, closet, welcome desk. Start with the closet.
- Slide 5 (make it up late): make the bed up the week a guest arrives, not the month before.
- Slide 6 (draw the line): the guest drawers and the guest rod are never available.
- Slide 7 (second job): name it, tape a footprint, or it moves out for good.
- Slide 8 (first evening): switch the lamp on, charge a device from each cable, set out tissues and the wifi card.
- Slide 9 (untested cable): an untested cable is a trap with a bow on it. Keep two, tested.
- Slide 10 (the dark path): keep the bed-to-door path clear for a stranger who cannot picture the floor.
- Slide 11 (CTA): Do one zone this week, the closet. Chapter 38. Next: the kids bedroom.
