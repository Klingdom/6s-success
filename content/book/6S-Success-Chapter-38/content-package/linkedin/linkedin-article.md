# The Guest Bedroom: Fresh, Ready, and Guest-Proof

*A LinkedIn article from Chapter 38 of 6S Success: Home Edition.*

The guest bedroom is the only room in your house with no daily user, and that single fact explains everything about it. Nothing in here objects when you set a box down, so the room quietly absorbs whatever the rest of the house could not decide about, and then you excavate it under time pressure every time someone visits.

> The guest bedroom is the one room in the house with no daily user, which is exactly why it fills up and why it goes stale, so here you clean for freshness and for a guest's first evening rather than for spills. Nobody disturbs this room, so dust settles in bands and the linen and the drawers take on the shut-away smell of the cupboard they sat in, and you air the room as much as you wipe it. Nothing here objects when you set a box down, so you draw a line between what belongs to a visitor and what the household has stashed here. And you clean it so it works on the first evening, testing the things a guest reaches for in the dark rather than making it look good in a photo.

## Five zones, not one room

> The guest bedroom is five small zones, not one big job: the guest bed and its linens, the guest nightstand, the guest dresser, the guest closet, and the guest welcome and work surface. Do them one at a time and the room that sleeps nobody most of the year is ready for someone by evening, a zone at a time.

## Air it, do not chase spills

> Nobody sleeps here most nights, so the enemy in a guest room is not spills, it is settled dust and stale, shut-away air. Dust builds in a grey band along a headboard nobody sits up against, and linen folded since spring carries the smell of the cupboard it sat in even though it is clean.

## Draw a line against the overflow

> A guest room fills up because nothing in it ever objects when you set a box down, so it quietly absorbs whatever the rest of the house could not decide about, and then you excavate it every time someone visits. So you do not tidy this room, you draw a line through it: the two top drawers and the guest half of the rod belong to the visitor and are never available.

## Clean it for the first evening

> You clean a guest room so it works on a guest's first evening, not so it looks right in a photograph, and the difference is that you test the things a guest depends on in the dark. You switch the lamp on to prove the bulb lights before they arrive, not after they are hunting for it.
