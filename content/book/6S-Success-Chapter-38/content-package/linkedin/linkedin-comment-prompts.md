# Chapter 38 LinkedIn Comment Prompts: The Guest Bedroom

1. Be honest: what is your guest room actually storing right now that has nothing to do with a guest?
2. When did you last air the spare linen, rather than just keeping it folded on the shelf?
3. Do you test the guest lamp and charging cables before a visitor arrives, or find out when they do?
4. What is your guest room's second job, the office, the sewing table, the exercise bike?
5. How far in advance do you make the bed up, and does it smell of the cupboard by the time they arrive?
