# Fresh, Ready, and Guest-Proof

*A 6S Success: Home Edition newsletter, from Chapter 38, The Guest Bedroom.*

Hi friend,

We have cleaned the room you sleep in. This week, the room your guests sleep in, the one room in the house with no daily user, which is exactly why it fills up and goes stale.

**Nobody disturbs this room, so dust settles in bands and the linen and the drawers take on the shut-away smell of the cupboard they sat in, and you air the room as much as you wipe it. Nothing here objects when you set a box down, so you draw a line between what belongs to a visitor and what the household has stashed here.**

The room breaks into five zones, each with its exact inputs and numbered steps: the bed and its linens, the nightstand, the dresser, the closet, and the welcome and work surface.

## To try this week

Do the closet, where the overflow actually lives. Lift each labelled bin down and wipe its lid, run a damp cloth the full length of the rod so it stops marking dark shoulders, dust the still corners where moths and damp hide, vacuum the floor, and set a strip of tape on the rod marking where the guest section ends.

Two printables go with this one: The Guest Room Reset card and The Guest-Ready in an Hour checklist.
