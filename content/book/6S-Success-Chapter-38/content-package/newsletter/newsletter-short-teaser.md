# Chapter 38 Newsletter Short Teaser: The Guest Bedroom

A short digest blurb (about 120 words) with a read-the-chapter CTA.

**The Guest Bedroom: fresh, ready, and guest-proof.**

The guest room is the one room with no daily user, which is exactly why it fills up and goes stale. So you clean it for freshness and a guest's first evening, not for spills: air the room and the linen as much as you wipe.

Chapter 38 gives the exact inputs and numbered steps for each of the room's five zones, plus the habits that make it work: draw a line against the overflow so the room stops becoming a storage unit, and test the lamp and the cables a guest reaches for in the dark.
