# Chapter 38 Landing Page Intro: The Guest Bedroom

The guest bedroom is the only room in your house with no daily user, and that single fact explains everything about it. Nothing in here objects when you set a box down, so the room quietly absorbs whatever the rest of the house could not decide about, and then you excavate it under time pressure every time someone visits.

This chapter breaks the guest bedroom into five small zones and gives you, for each one, the exact inputs to gather and the numbered steps to clean and shine it: the bed and its linens, the nightstand, the dresser, the closet, and the welcome and work surface. It shows you why a guest room needs airing more than scrubbing (nobody disturbs it, so the enemy is settled dust and stale air), how to draw a line against the overflow so the room stops becoming a storage unit, and how to clean it so it works on a guest's first evening, testing the lamp and the cables a visitor reaches for in the dark.

Product types only, no brands. Start with the closet, where the overflow actually lives.
