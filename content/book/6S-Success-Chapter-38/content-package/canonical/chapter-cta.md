# Chapter 38 CTA and Companion Resources: The Guest Bedroom

## Primary call to action
Do one zone this week, the closet where the overflow actually lives, and give the next visitor a room that welcomes them without a scramble.

## Companion resources
- **The Guest Room Reset card** (printable and phone wallpaper: the five zones, the done-looks-like for each, and the air-it, draw-the-line, first-evening order of work).
- **The Guest Room Kit list** (printable: the cleaners, tools, and storage types the room needs, grouped by job, with the air-it-out, test-the-cables, and anti-tip notes that matter here).
- **The Guest-Ready in an Hour checklist** (the fast pass for the week a visitor is coming: strip and wash, air, test the lamp and cables, set out the welcome bin).

## Register and fences
- Warm, practical, expert, and hospitable without fuss. Product types only, never brand names. No hype, no invented statistics or prices.
- Friction meter retired for Part 9; close on the before-and-after room signature.
- Previous: Chapter 37, The Primary Bedroom. Next: Chapter 39, The Kids Bedroom.

## Words to avoid
- No brand names (product types only). Never offer an untested charging cable. Never treat the guest drawers as free storage.
