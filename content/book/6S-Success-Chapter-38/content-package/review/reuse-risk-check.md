# Chapter 38 Reuse and Risk Check: The Guest Bedroom

## Safety-claim review
- **The dark path**: framed correctly as a night-time fall hazard, made sharper by the fact that the sleeper does not know the room; the guidance (keep the bed-to-door path clear) is standard and safe. PASS.
- **Anti-tip dresser and closet rod**: strapping a dresser a visiting child might climb, and not overloading a rod, are correct, mainstream safety advice. PASS.
- **Looped blind and curtain cords**: correctly flagged as a strangulation risk for a visiting small child; the fix (tie them up out of reach) is standard child-safety guidance. PASS.
- **Medication and bedding bags**: taking your own medication out of the guest drawer and removing dry-cleaning film and sealed-bag plastic from a low drawer are correct and protective. PASS.
