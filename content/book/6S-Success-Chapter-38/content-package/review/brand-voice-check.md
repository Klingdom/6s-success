# Chapter 38 Brand Voice Check: The Guest Bedroom

## Voice target
Warm, practical, expert, and hospitable without fuss. The register of a knowledgeable friend who has actually got a guest room ready more than once, not a hotel manual.

## Scan results
- **Product types only, no brands**: PASS. A brand-name scan across the whole package (common household brands) returned zero hits. Everything is a type: compact vacuum with attachments, colour-coded microfiber cloth set, neutral pH multi-surface cleaner, streak-free glass and mirror cleaner, mattress protector, matching hanger set, labelled lidded bins, moisture absorber or humidity monitor, anti-tip anchor and hardware, folding luggage rack, floor boundary tape.
- **No hype, no invented numbers or prices**: PASS. No statistics, no fabricated study claims, no dollar figures.
- **Hospitality framed with warmth, not performance**: PASS. The tone is "a room that welcomes them without a scramble", not showroom perfection; the guest is treated as a person who will cross the room in the dark, not an audience for a photo.
- **Dash and punctuation house style**: PASS. Middot for label separators; no em/en dashes; no prose spaced-hyphen separators.
- **Inclusive, non-gendered**: PASS. "A guest", "a visitor", "a visiting family", "the person who sleeps here" throughout; no gendered assumptions.

## Consistency with the series
- The zone-by-zone instruction voice matches Chapters 31 to 37.
- The one-idea-to-keep close and the before-and-after signature match the Part 9 pattern.
- The two fences held everywhere: never offer an untested cable, never treat the guest drawers as free storage.

## Verdict
On voice. No changes needed.
