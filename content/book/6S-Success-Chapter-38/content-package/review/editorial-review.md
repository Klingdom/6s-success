# Chapter 38 Editorial Review: The Guest Bedroom

## Verdict
Ready for Phil's review as a draft. The chapter meets the instruction-centric standard: every one of the five zones carries an Inputs list, numbered Steps, and an inspect-and-flag paragraph, and the guest-bedroom Shine layer is distinct from the seven rooms before it.

## What works
- **The layer is genuinely its own.** Air-it-not-spills, draw-the-line, and clean-for-the-first-evening are specific to a room with no daily user, and they do not repeat the primary-bedroom (clean-for-sleep-and-air) layer even though the two rooms share furniture types. The pivot from "the room you sleep in" to "the room nobody sleeps in" is the chapter's spine, and it holds.
- **The Manual reset instructions are folded in faithfully.** The strip-vacuum-turn-air bed sequence, the two-tested-cables nightstand, the air-the-empty-drawers dresser, and the clean-the-rod closet are carried straight from the source shine_detail and elevated into numbered procedures without inventing steps.
- **The overflow material is the chapter's real subject and it is handled well.** Drawing a line against the household's storage, and naming and footprinting the room's second job, turn the most-ducked decisions in the house into clean instructions.
- **Safety is integrated and correctly re-aimed.** The unfamiliar-room-in-the-dark reframe reframes every hazard around a sleeper who does not know the floor, which is the right lens for this room, and the flags are resolved in the Safety pass per the Shine-finds-Safety-fixes rule.

## Watch items (minor, non-blocking)
- The chapter runs long (about 5,956 words) for a five-zone room, because the guest room carries more decision-making (the calls) than the pure-instruction rooms; the length cap is lifted for Part 9, so this is acceptable, and the Calls section is the easiest trim if Phil wants tighter.
- The welcome-and-work-surface zone doubles as the place to resolve the room's second job, so it carries both a cleaning procedure and the biggest call; the text keeps them separate (the zone steps clean the desk, the call names the second job), which reads cleanly.

## Consistency checks
- Nine Shine rules recapped, not re-taught: PASS.
- Friction meter retired; before-and-after signature closes: PASS.
- Product types only: PASS.
- Hand-off to Chapter 39 (the kids bedroom) present and light: PASS.

## Recommendation
Approve as draft. No structural edits required.
