# Chapter 38 Facebook Posts: The Guest Bedroom

Five short standalone posts.

## 1. Air it, do not chase spills
Nobody sleeps in the guest room most nights, so the enemy is not a spill, it is settled dust and stale air. Air it as much as you wipe it: strip and hot-wash the bed set, shake and air the spare set, and leave the empty drawers open with the window cracked. A guest notices the air of a room before anything else.

## 2. Make the bed up late
Linen folded since spring smells of the cupboard even though it is clean, and a guest notices the moment they turn the covers back. So do not make the bed up a month early. Wash the set and make it up the week a guest arrives, and open the window while you do it.

## 3. The guest drawers are not free storage
A guest room fills up because nothing in it objects when you set a box down. Draw a line: the two top drawers and the guest half of the rod belong to the visitor and are never available, not for a week and not for the box you mean to sort properly.

## 4. An untested cable is a trap with a bow on it
A guest will find the cable you left, trust it, plug in, and wake to a phone that never charged. That is worse than nothing. Keep exactly two, one of each connector your visitors carry, and prove each one tonight by charging a real device with it.

## 5. Clean it for the first evening
You clean a guest room so it works on a guest's first evening, not so it looks good in a photo. Switch the lamp on to prove it lights, set out the tissues and the wifi card, and keep the path from the bed to the door clear for a stranger crossing it in the dark. Chapter 38. Next: the kids bedroom.
