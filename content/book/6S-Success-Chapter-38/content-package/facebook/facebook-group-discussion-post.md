# Facebook Group Discussion Post: The Guest Bedroom

For a home or hosting group. A useful tip, an open question, a soft close.

---

A guest-room tip that saves the pre-visit scramble.

The guest room is the one room with no daily user, which is exactly why it fills up and goes stale. Nobody disturbs it, so the enemy is not a spill, it is settled dust and shut-away air. So air it as much as you wipe it: strip and hot-wash the set on the bed, shake and air the spare set, and leave the empty drawers open with the window cracked while you work. And make the bed up the week a guest arrives, not the month before, because folded linen smells of the cupboard even when it is clean.

Two more that change everything. First, draw a line: the guest drawers and the guest half of the rod are never available, not even for the box you mean to sort. Second, clean it for the first evening, not the photo: switch the lamp on to prove it lights, and charge a real device from each cable you leave out, because an untested cable is a trap with a bow on it.

Be honest: what is your guest room actually storing right now that has nothing to do with a guest?
