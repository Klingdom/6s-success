# Chapter 38 Image Generation Prompts: The Guest Bedroom

Warm, illustrated, flat-color editorial house style (paper #F7F2E9 ground, ink #2B2622 line, terracotta/honey/slate/green accents, Shine green #6E8B5B). No photorealism, no brand names or logos, no text baked into the art beyond the noted labels. Product types only.

## Prompt 1 (opener, two states)
A warm flat-illustration of one guest bedroom shown twice, side by side. Left, labelled "before": a bed buried under folded laundry, a dresser storing boxes and coats, a closet of sealed cartons, an exercise bike in the corner. Right, labelled "after": the bed made up with a second set folded on the shelf, the drawers empty and pulled open to air, the closet cleared with a taped line on the rod, a clear working desk, and a clear strip of floor.

## Prompt 2 (five zones plan)
A warm overhead floor plan of a guest bedroom, gently shaded, with five numbered and labelled zones: guest bed and linens, guest nightstand, guest dresser, guest closet, welcome and work surface. Caption strip at the foot: "one room, five small jobs".

## Prompt 3 (method and kit, two panels)
A two-panel warm infographic. Left "the method": an open window with the spare set shaken out and empty drawers pulled open, a taped line on a closet rod beside a crossed-out box in a guest drawer, a lamp switched on with a device charging from each of two cables, and a clear floor path with a tied-up blind cord and an anti-tip strap. Right "the kit": labelled type icons for a vacuum with attachments, a microfiber set, a neutral pH cleaner and a streak-free glass cleaner, laundry detergent, a mattress protector, matching hangers, labelled lidded bins, a moisture absorber, two tested cables, tissues and a wifi card, an anti-tip anchor, a luggage rack, and floor boundary tape.

## Prompt 4 (bed-zone step ribbon)
A warm step-ribbon titled "how to clean the guest bed and linens": dust the headboard band, vacuum behind the bed, strip and vacuum the mattress and turn it, hot-wash and air the sets, air the pillows, wipe the frame, vacuum under the bed. The air-the-linen and turn-the-mattress steps highlighted in the Shine green.

## Prompt 5 (before-and-after signature)
A warm matched pair of the guest bedroom, the made bed, cleared surfaces, and the floor path, shot from the doorway, before on the left and after on the right, with a small camera icon and the labels "the before, kept" and "the after, kept as the standard".
