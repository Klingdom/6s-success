# Chapter 38 Diagram Ideas: The Guest Bedroom

House palette (paper #F7F2E9, panel #FBF7EF, ink #2B2622, terracotta #BC4B2A, honey #DDA63A, slate #3C5A6B, green #6E8B5B for Shine). Warm, illustrated, not photoreal. Product types only, no brands or logos. Spark red reserved only for a true hazard (a warm scorched extension lead, a looped blind cord by the bed).

## Diagram 1 (opener, figure 1): two states
The guest bedroom before and after, side by side. Before: a bed buried under folded laundry, a dresser storing what nobody could decide about, a closet of sealed boxes, the exercise bike in the corner. After: the bed made up on a clean protector with a second set aired on the shelf, the drawers empty and pulled open to breathe, the closet cleared to a decision with a taped guest line on the rod, a clear working desk, and a clear path a stranger can cross in the dark.

## Diagram 2 (figure 2): the five zones plan
A warm overhead plan, five numbered zones marked and named: guest bed and linens, guest nightstand, guest dresser, guest closet, welcome and work surface. Footer: "one room, five small jobs".

## Diagram 3 (figure 3): the Shine method and kit
Two panels. Left, the method: air it (an open window, the spare set shaken out, empty drawers pulled open); draw the line (a taped line on the rod, a crossed-out box in a guest drawer); clean for the first evening (a lamp switched on, a device charging from each of two cables); the room in the dark (a clear floor path, a tied-up blind cord, an anti-tip strap). Right, the kit: a vacuum with attachments, a microfiber set, a neutral pH cleaner and a streak-free glass cleaner, laundry detergent, a mattress protector, matching hangers, labelled lidded bins, a moisture absorber, two tested cables, tissues and a wifi card, an anti-tip anchor, a luggage rack, and floor boundary tape. Footer: "air it, draw the line, clean for the first evening".

## Diagram 4 (no HTML twin): the guest-room Shine method
A standalone visual: air it, draw the line, clean for the first evening, the room in the dark. Names the next chapter, the kids bedroom.

## Diagram 5 (no HTML twin): how to clean the guest bed and linens
A step ribbon: dust the headboard top band, vacuum behind the bed, strip and vacuum the mattress and turn it, hot-wash and air the sets, air the pillows, wipe the frame, vacuum under the bed and air the suitcase. Highlight the air-the-linen and turn-the-mattress steps.

## Diagram 6 (before/after signature) and the photo brief
The Part 9 signature: a matched before-and-after pair of the made bed, cleared surfaces, and the clear path, from the doorway. **Best before-and-after photo brief:** stand in the doorway and frame the bed, the cleared surfaces, and the strip of floor a guest crosses in the dark together in one shot; same spot, angle, and light, before and after; do not tidy before the before (Ch 6); keep the after as the ideal-state standard (Ch 25), taped inside the closet door. The retired friction meter is not drawn.
