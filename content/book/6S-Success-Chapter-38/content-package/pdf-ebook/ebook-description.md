# Chapter 38 Ebook Description: The Guest Bedroom

The eighth room playbook of Part Nine. The guest bedroom is the one room in the house with no daily user, which is exactly why it fills up and goes stale, so the standard is not a showroom: it is a room a visitor can walk into and use tonight without you touching anything first.

This chapter gives the exact inputs and the numbered steps for each of the room's five zones, with three moves running through all of them: air it rather than chase spills (strip and hot-wash the bed set, shake and air the spare, leave the empty drawers open to breathe, and make the bed up the week a guest arrives, not the month before), draw a line against the overflow (the guest drawers and the guest rod are never available, and the room's second job gets a taped footprint or moves out), and clean it for the first evening (switch the lamp on to prove it lights, charge a real device from each of the two cables, and set out the tissues and the wifi card).

You will learn why a guest room needs airing more than scrubbing, how to keep a spare room from becoming a storage unit, and how to make a room work for a stranger crossing it in the dark. The chapter closes on a before-and-after room signature and the two photos to take.
