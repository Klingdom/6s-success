# Chapter 38 Back Cover Copy: The Guest Bedroom

## Back-cover text
The guest bedroom is the one room with no daily user, which is exactly why it fills up and goes stale. So tidying is not the point. You clean it for freshness and for a guest's first evening.

The Guest Bedroom breaks the room into five zones and gives you, for each, the exact inputs to gather and the numbered steps to follow. Air the room and the linen as much as you wipe. Draw a line against the overflow so the room stops becoming a storage unit. Test the lamp and the cables a guest reaches for in the dark. And keep the path a stranger crosses at night clear.

Do one zone this week, the closet, and give the next visitor a room that welcomes them without a scramble.
