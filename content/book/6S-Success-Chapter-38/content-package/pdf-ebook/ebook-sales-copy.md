# Chapter 38 Ebook Sales Copy: The Guest Bedroom

## Headline
The one room with no daily user, which is exactly why it fills up. Here is how to clean it so a guest can use it tonight, without a scramble.

## The promise
In the room your guests sleep in, tidying is not the point. Nobody disturbs it, so it goes stale, and nothing objects when you set a box down, so it fills up. This chapter gives you the exact inputs and the numbered steps for each of the room's five zones, plus the habits that make it work: air it rather than chase spills, draw a line against the overflow, and clean it so it works on a guest's first evening.

## What you get in this chapter
- The guest-room Shine method: air it rather than chase spills; draw a line against the overflow; clean it for the first evening; the unfamiliar room in the dark.
- The five per-zone clean procedures, each with its inputs and numbered steps: the guest bed and its linens, the guest nightstand, the guest dresser, the guest closet, and the guest welcome and work surface.
- The airing pass that makes a room feel cared for, and why you make the bed up the week a guest arrives, not the month before.
- The line that keeps a spare room from becoming a storage unit, and how to name and footprint the room's second job.
- The first-evening test, the lamp and the two cables, and the before-and-after room signature.

## The common mistake it fixes
> Making the bed up a month early so it smells of the cupboard by the time a guest turns the covers back, offering charging cables nobody has ever tested, and treating the room as free storage all year and then excavating it under time pressure the afternoon before someone arrives.
