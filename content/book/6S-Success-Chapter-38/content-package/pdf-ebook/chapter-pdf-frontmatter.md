# The Guest Bedroom

*The guest bedroom is the only room in the house with no daily user, which is exactly why it fills up and why it goes stale, so cleaning it is not about tidiness, it is about a room a visitor can walk into and use tonight without you touching anything first. This playbook points the whole loop at it one zone at a time, and gives you, for each of the five zones, the exact inputs to gather and the precise, step-by-step way to clean and shine it, so the next guest arrives to a room that welcomes them instead of a room you excavated that afternoon.*

## How to read this chapter

This is a PLAYBOOK, not a method chapter. It applies the six-S loop you already own to one room, decomposed into five micro-zones, and its centerpiece is the per-zone clean-and-shine instructions with the exact inputs. It recaps but does not re-teach the nine-rule Shine method from Chapter 31; the new material is the guest-room layer (air it rather than chase spills; draw a line against the overflow; clean it for the first evening; the unfamiliar room in the dark) and the numbered steps for each zone.

Products are named as TYPES, never brands. The exhaustive product list is a separate appendix.

The friction meter, which reached its goal at Chapter 30, is retired for Part 9; the chapter closes on a before-and-after room signature and the two photos to take.
