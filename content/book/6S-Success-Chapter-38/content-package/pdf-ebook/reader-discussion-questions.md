# Chapter 38 Reader Discussion Questions: The Guest Bedroom

1. What is your guest room actually storing right now that has nothing to do with a guest?
2. When did you last air the spare linen, rather than just keeping it folded on the shelf?
3. How far in advance do you make the bed up, and does it smell of the cupboard by the time a guest arrives?
4. Do you test the guest lamp and charging cables before a visitor comes, or find out when they do?
5. What is your guest room's second job, and could its footprint clear into one bin in a single trip?
6. How many sheet sets does your guest room hold, and how many would you happily sleep on yourself for a week?
7. Are there boxes in the guest closet that have not been opened since a previous address?
8. Is your guest dresser strapped to the wall, in a room a visiting child may be left to play in?
9. Is there a looped blind or curtain cord within a child's reach of the guest bed?
10. Which zone will you do first, and what inputs do you need to gather before you start?
