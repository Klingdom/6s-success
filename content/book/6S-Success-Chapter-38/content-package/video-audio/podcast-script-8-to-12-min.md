# Chapter 38 Podcast Script (8 to 12 min): The Guest Bedroom

Single host, warm and practical.

## Cold open
Here is why getting the guest room ready always seems to take a whole afternoon: you are not cleaning it, you are excavating it. Today we fix that, and clean a guest room the way it actually needs it, for freshness and for a guest's first evening.

## Part 1: The idea
Welcome back to the room-by-room playbooks. Today, the guest bedroom, the one room in the house with no daily user. That single fact explains everything. Nothing in here objects when you set a box down, so it quietly fills up, and nobody disturbs it, so it goes stale. So you clean it for three things: for freshness, because the enemy is settled dust and shut-away air, not spills; against the overflow, drawing a line between what belongs to a visitor and what the household stashed here; and for the first evening, testing the small things a guest reaches for in the dark.

## Part 2: The five zones
The room is five zones: the bed and its linens, the nightstand, the dresser, the closet, and the welcome and work surface. Start with the closet, because that is where the overflow actually lives, and you cannot judge how much room a guest has while half of it is behind a closet door.

## Part 3: Air it
Nobody sleeps here most nights, so you air the room as much as you wipe it. Strip and hot-wash the set on the bed, shake and air the spare set before it goes back, and leave the empty drawers pulled open with the window cracked while you work. And make the bed up the week a guest arrives, not the month before, because folded linen carries the smell of the cupboard even when it is clean.

## Part 4: Draw the line
The guest drawers and the guest half of the rod belong to the visitor and are never available, not for a week and not for the box you mean to sort. And name the room's second job out loud, the office or the sewing table or the exercise bike, and give it a footprint in tape that clears into one bin in a single trip, or it moves out for good.

## Part 5: Clean for the first evening
Switch the lamp on to prove it lights before a guest is hunting for it. Charge a real device from each of the two cables you leave out, because an untested cable a guest trusts is worse than none. And set out the tissues, the coaster, and the wifi card so a guest at two in the morning never has to knock on your door.

## Part 6: Safety and the calls
The person who sleeps here does not know this room and crosses it in the dark on the first night, so keep the path from the bed to the door clear, tie up looped cords, strap the dresser, and take your own medication out of the drawer. And the calls are mostly about the line: the demoted sheets, the two tested cables, the overflow you told yourself was free, the box from the move.

## Close
A guest room is not ready because it looks tidy. It is ready because you aired the linen and the drawers, drew the line against the overflow, and tested the lamp and the cables. Do one zone this week, the closet. Next time: the kids bedroom.
