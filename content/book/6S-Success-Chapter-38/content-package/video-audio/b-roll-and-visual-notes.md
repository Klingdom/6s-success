# Chapter 38 B-Roll and Visual Notes: The Guest Bedroom

Warm, natural light. Product types only, no brand labels on screen.

## Visual vocabulary
- The two-state opener: a stale, storage-filled before and a fresh, aired, guest-ready after.
- The recurring "air it" motif: an open window, the spare set shaken out, empty drawers pulled open to breathe.

## Core shot list (seven shots)
1. The bed: stripping it, working the upholstery tool across the mattress and into the seams, and turning it end to end.
2. Hot-washing the set and shaking out the spare set from its pillowcase, then airing it by an open window.
3. The dresser: leaving the empty top drawers pulled open with the window cracked while the room airs.
4. The nightstand: switching the lamp on to prove it lights, and charging a real device from each of the two cables.
5. The closet: running a damp cloth the full length of the rod, and setting a strip of tape to mark the guest section.
6. Naming the second job: an exercise bike or a sewing table lifted into a taped floor footprint.
7. The safety beat: a tied-up blind cord, an anti-tip strap into a stud, and a clear path swept from the bed to the door.

## On-screen text bank (use sparingly)
- Air it, do not chase spills.
- Make the bed up the week they arrive.
- The guest drawers are never available.
- Test the lamp and the two cables.
- Keep the dark path clear.

## Lower thirds (rotate)
- Zone 1 to 5, one per segment.
- Do one zone this week.

## Editing notes
- Close each cut on the before-and-after room signature from the doorway (made bed, cleared surfaces, the clear path).
- No presenter required; hands and surfaces only.
