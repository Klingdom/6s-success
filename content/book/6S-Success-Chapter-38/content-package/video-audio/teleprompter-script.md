# Chapter 38 Teleprompter Script: The Guest Bedroom

Short lines, one breath each. Warm and practical.

The guest room is the one room with no daily user.
And that is exactly why it fills up.
Nothing in it objects when you set a box down.
And nobody disturbs it, so it goes stale.
So you do not clean it for spills.
You clean it for freshness. And for the first evening.
Strip and hot-wash the set on the bed.
Shake and air the spare set before it goes back.
Leave the empty drawers open with the window cracked.
And make the bed up the week they arrive.
Not the month before.
Then draw a line against the overflow.
The guest drawers are never available.
Not for a week. Not for the box you mean to sort.
Name the room's second job.
Give it a footprint marked in tape.
Or it moves out for good.
Then clean it for the first evening.
Switch the lamp on to prove it lights.
Charge a real device from each cable.
An untested cable is a trap with a bow on it.
Set out the tissues and the wifi card.
Keep the path from the bed to the door clear.
A guest crosses it in the dark, on the first night.
Do one zone this week. The closet.
And give the next visitor a room that is ready.
