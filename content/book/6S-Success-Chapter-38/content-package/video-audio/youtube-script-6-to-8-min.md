# Chapter 38 YouTube Script (6 to 8 min): The Guest Bedroom

Alternating HOST and VISUAL. Product types only.

HOST: Getting the guest room ready always seems to take a whole afternoon, because you are not cleaning it, you are excavating it. Today we fix that.
VISUAL: Two-state opener, storage-filled before and fresh after. An open window, the spare set shaken out.

HOST: The guest room is the one room with no daily user. Nothing objects when you set a box down, so it fills up, and nobody disturbs it, so it goes stale. So you clean it for freshness, against the overflow, and for the first evening.
VISUAL: Empty drawers pulled open; a damp cloth down a closet rod.

HOST: Five zones. Start with the closet, where the overflow lives. Lift the bins down and wipe the lids, run a damp cloth the full length of the rod so it stops marking dark shoulders, and vacuum the still corners.
VISUAL: Bins lifted; the rod wiped; the nozzle in the corners.

HOST: The bed: strip it, vacuum the mattress and turn it, then hot-wash the set and shake and air the spare. Make the bed up the week a guest arrives, not the month before.
VISUAL: The upholstery tool on the mattress; the spare set aired by a window.

HOST: The dresser: air the empty drawers as much as you wipe them, and strap it to the wall. The nightstand: switch the lamp on to prove it lights, and charge a real device from each of the two cables.
VISUAL: Open drawers airing; an anti-tip strap; a device charging from each cable.

HOST: Now draw the line. The guest drawers and the guest rod are never available. And name the room's second job, the office or the exercise bike, and give it a footprint in tape.
VISUAL: A taped line on the rod; an exercise bike lifted into a taped floor square.

HOST: Safety is tuned to a stranger in the dark: keep the path from the bed to the door clear, tie up looped cords, and take your own medication out of the drawer. Do one zone this week. Next: the kids bedroom.
VISUAL: The before-and-after room signature from the doorway. End card.
