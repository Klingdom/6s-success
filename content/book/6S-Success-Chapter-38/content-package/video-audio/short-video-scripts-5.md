# Chapter 38 Short Video Scripts (5): The Guest Bedroom

Each 30 to 90 seconds. Product types only.

## 1. Air it, do not chase spills (40s)
HOOK: Your guest room does not need scrubbing. It needs airing.
SCRIPT: Nobody sleeps here most nights, so the enemy is settled dust and stale, shut-away air. Strip and hot-wash the set on the bed, shake and air the spare set before it goes back, and leave the empty drawers pulled open with the window cracked while you work.
ON-SCREEN: Air it, do not chase spills.
CTA: Chapter 38. Air the guest room this week.

## 2. Make the bed up late (30s)
HOOK: Stop making the guest bed up early.
SCRIPT: Linen folded since spring smells of the cupboard even though it is clean, and a guest notices the moment they turn the covers back. Wash the set and make it up the week a guest arrives, not the month before, and open the window while you do it.
CTA: Chapter 38, the guest bed.

## 3. The guest drawers are not free storage (35s)
HOOK: Your spare room is becoming a storage unit.
SCRIPT: It fills up because nothing in it objects when you set a box down. Draw a line: the two top drawers and the guest half of the rod belong to the visitor and are never available, not for a week and not for the box you mean to sort.
CTA: Chapter 38. Draw the line this week.

## 4. An untested cable is a trap with a bow on it (30s)
HOOK: Never leave a guest a cable you have not tested.
SCRIPT: A guest will find it, trust it, plug in, and wake to a phone that never charged. That is worse than nothing. Keep exactly two, one of each connector your visitors carry, and prove each one tonight by charging a real device.
CTA: Chapter 38. Test the cables.

## 5. Clean it for the first evening (40s)
HOOK: Clean a guest room for the first evening, not the photo.
SCRIPT: Switch the lamp on to prove it lights before they are hunting for it. Set out the tissues, the coaster, and the wifi card. And keep the path from the bed to the door clear, because a guest crosses an unfamiliar room in the dark on the first night.
CTA: Chapter 38. Next: the kids bedroom.
