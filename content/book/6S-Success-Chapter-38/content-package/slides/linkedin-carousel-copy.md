# Chapter 38 LinkedIn Carousel Copy: The Guest Bedroom

Twelve slides, on-slide copy. Shine green accent. Footer: AIR IT, DRAW THE LINE, CLEAN FOR THE FIRST EVENING. Product types only.

- Slide 1: The one room with no daily user, which is exactly why it fills up.
- Slide 2: So clean it for freshness and a guest's first evening, not for spills.
- Slide 3: The enemy is settled dust and stale air. Air the room, the linen, the empty drawers.
- Slide 4: Five zones: bed, nightstand, dresser, closet, welcome desk. Start with the closet.
- Slide 5: Make the bed up the week a guest arrives, not the month before.
- Slide 6: Draw a line: the guest drawers and the guest rod are never available.
- Slide 7: Name the room's second job. Tape a footprint, or it moves out for good.
- Slide 8: Clean it for the first evening. Switch the lamp on. Set out the wifi card.
- Slide 9: An untested cable is a trap with a bow on it. Keep two, tested.
- Slide 10: Keep the dark path clear for a stranger who cannot picture the floor.
- Slide 11: A room a visitor can walk into and use tonight without you lifting a finger.
- Slide 12 (CTA): Do one zone this week, the closet. Chapter 38. Next: the kids bedroom.
