# Chapter 38 Visual Storyboard: The Guest Bedroom

## Design system
House palette: paper #F7F2E9, panel #FBF7EF, ink #2B2622, terracotta #BC4B2A, honey #DDA63A, slate #3C5A6B, green #6E8B5B (Shine). Fraunces display, Newsreader body, Inter labels. Footer strip: AIR IT, DRAW THE LINE, CLEAN FOR THE FIRST EVENING.

## Recurring motif
The "air it" cue: an open window, the spare set shaken out, and empty drawers pulled open to breathe. It ties the room's lesson together and reappears on the method slide.

## Two fences
- Never depict an untested cable offered as ready; never depict the guest drawers used as household storage.
- Product types only, no brand names or real logos.

## Beats (twelve)
1. Title over the two-state opener.
2. The room with no daily user, filling up.
3. Air it, not spills: the open window, the aired linen.
4. The five zones overhead plan.
5. The definition: a room a visitor can use tonight.
6. The method and kit two-panel (figure 3).
7. Bed and linens: strip, air, make it up late.
8. Draw the line: the guest drawers and rod, never available.
9. The second job, given a taped footprint.
10. Clean for the first evening: the lamp tested, the two cables tested.
11. Safety: the dark path; the before-and-after room signature.
12. Close: one idea to keep, and the Chapter 39 hand-off.

## Carrying it across the set
Every asset closes on the before-and-after room signature from the doorway (made bed, cleared surfaces, the clear path). The friction meter is retired for Part 9. Register warm, hospitable, expert. Previous Ch 37, next Ch 39 The Kids Bedroom.
