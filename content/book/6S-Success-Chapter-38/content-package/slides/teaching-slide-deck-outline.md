# Chapter 38 Teaching Slide Deck Outline: The Guest Bedroom

Fourteen slides. House palette; Shine green #6E8B5B. Footer strip: AIR IT, DRAW THE LINE, CLEAN FOR THE FIRST EVENING. Product types only.

1. Title: The Guest Bedroom. Part Nine, Room-by-Room Playbooks. The room with no daily user.
2. The problem: nothing objects when you set a box down, so it fills up and goes stale.
3. What you will learn: the five outcomes.
4. The rule: clean for freshness and the first evening, not for spills; draw a line against the overflow.
5. Five zones, not one room: with the overhead plan figure. Start with the closet.
6. The definition: a room a visitor can use tonight without you touching anything first.
7. The method (its own slide): air it, draw the line, clean for the first evening, the room in the dark. Figure 3, method and kit.
8. Bed and linens: strip, vacuum, turn the mattress; hot-wash and air; make it up the week they arrive.
9. Nightstand: the one square foot a guest reaches for in the dark; two tested cables.
10. Dresser and closet: air the empty drawers; clean the rod; tape the guest line.
11. Draw the line: the guest drawers are never available; name the second job.
12. Clean for the first evening: test the lamp and the cables; set out the welcome bin.
13. Safety and the signature: the dark path; strap the dresser; the before-and-after room card.
14. Close and CTA: one idea to keep. Do one zone this week, the closet. Next: the kids bedroom.
