# SEO Title Options: Chapter 24, Checklists, Schedules, and Responsibilities

Five title options. Character counts are in parentheses. The canonical title is listed first.

1. Checklists, Schedules, and Responsibilities (43)
   Angle: canonical chapter title. Use as the H1 and primary on-page heading, with "a checklist," "a schedule," "a responsibility," "the three tools," and "run without you" early in the body.

2. A Recipe on a Card Does Not Cook Itself (39)
   Angle: leads with the hero line. Names the gap the chapter closes in a single image, a written standard is necessary but not enough, and is quotable and snippet-ready for the reader whose chore list is being ignored.

3. The Three Tools a Standard Needs (32)
   Angle: leads with the hero device. Says a standard runs when three things are true, a checklist for the what, a schedule for the when, and a name for the who, and answers a real search for why a written standard still does not get followed.

4. What, When, and Who (19)
   Angle: names the three plain questions the chapter answers. Short, memorable, and true to the calm register, it maps each need to its tool and reads as a promise the reader can act on in five minutes.

5. Make a Standard Actually Happen (31)
   Angle: opens with the reader's real search, why a good intention on a card never gets done, and answers it, give it a list, a time, and a name. Concrete and outcome-led, and echoes the primary keyword.
