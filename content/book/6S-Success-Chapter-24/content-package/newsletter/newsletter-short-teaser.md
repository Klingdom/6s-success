# Newsletter Short Teaser: Checklists, Schedules, and Responsibilities

For a roundup or digest. 90 to 140 words.

---

Last chapter you wrote a standard, the best way you know put on a card. Then, maybe, you watched it get ignored for a week. A standard is a recipe on a card, and a recipe on a card does not cook itself; knowing what good looks like has never once been the same as doing it. A written standard fails in exactly three places, and each has a tool. A checklist answers what, so nothing is forgotten. A schedule answers when, so the work happens on a rhythm instead of in a crisis. A responsibility answers who, so one chosen name owns it. Give a standard all three and it becomes a small machine that runs on a tired Tuesday. The needle holds, and the catch behind it, now three teeth, grips harder.
