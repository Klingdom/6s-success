# Chapter 3 SEO Title Options

Chapter: "The Six Steps That Transform Any Space"
Slug: the-six-steps-that-transform-any-space
Part One, Discovering 6S | 6S Success: Home Edition

Five options below. Character counts shown for the title text only (no site or brand suffix). Pick one as the page title and keep the others for A/B testing or social headers.

1. **The Six Steps That Transform Any Space** (38 chars)
   Angle: The canonical book title. Use this as the default H1 and page title. Warm and benefit-led, and it matches every other asset in the package.

2. **The Six Steps of 6S, in Order** (30 chars)
   Angle: Leads with the primary keyword and the search intent people actually type. Best for matching "what are the six steps of 6S in order."

3. **Sort, Straighten, Shine, Safety, Standardize, Sustain** (53 chars)
   Angle: Names all six S words up front. Strong for readers searching the steps by name, and good for featured-snippet matching.

4. **How to Do 6S: Walk the Whole Loop Once** (39 chars)
   Angle: How-to framing for practical searchers. Signals a demonstration, not a definition list.

5. **6S Step by Step: One Cabinet, One Full Lap** (43 chars)
   Angle: Pairs the keyword with the concrete under-sink story. Curiosity plus specificity, good for click-through.
