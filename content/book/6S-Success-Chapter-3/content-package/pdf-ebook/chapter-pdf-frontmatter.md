## How to Read This Chapter

Read this one straight through before you pick up a sponge. It is built as a single walk, not a reference list. We take one small space and move through all six steps in order, Sort, Straighten, Shine, Safety, Standardize, Sustain, so you can watch each step hand the next one something it could not have done alone. That chain of handoffs is the real lesson, more than the six words themselves.

You do not need the earlier chapters open to follow along, though this chapter assumes you already know what 6S is. Notice the order as you go. It is not bureaucracy. It is closer to physics, and the chapter shows you why.

When you reach the last section, "Your First Lap," stop reading and start doing. Set a fifteen-minute timer and point it at one drawer, one shelf, or the cabinet under your own sink. Do not aim for perfect. Aim for one full lap.
