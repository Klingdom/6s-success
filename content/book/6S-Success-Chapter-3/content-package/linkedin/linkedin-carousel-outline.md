# LinkedIn Carousel Outline: The Six Steps That Transform Any Space

Eleven slides. Square or portrait. Warm, plain. Slide 1 hooks, slide 11 is the soft CTA. Slides 4 through 9 walk the loop, one step each, and name the handoff.

---

## Slide 1 (Hook)
**Headline:** The cabinet under your sink went feral.
**Body:** Not filthy. Not overflowing. Just full of things with no job, no home, no reason. Here is how six steps fix it, in order.

---

## Slide 2 (The reframe)
**Headline:** It is a loop, not a menu.
**Body:** The six steps are not separate chores that happen to start with S. They are a chain. Each one sets up the next. You do not pick your favorite three.

---

## Slide 3 (Why the order)
**Headline:** The order is physics, not bureaucracy.
**Body:** You cannot give something a home until you know it is staying. You cannot see what a space hides until it is empty and clean.

---

## Slide 4 (Step 1)
**Headline:** Sort: make room.
**Body:** Take everything out onto a towel. Keep, relocate, or release. You are not organizing yet. You are deciding. Handoff: Sort hands Straighten an empty cabinet.

---

## Slide 5 (Step 2)
**Headline:** Straighten: give it a home.
**Body:** Put back only the keepers, daily things at the front. Tidy means lined up. Easy means you reach what you need first. Handoff: Straighten hands Shine a clear view.

---

## Slide 6 (Step 3)
**Headline:** Shine: clean until it talks.
**Body:** Shine is not about clean. It is about inspection. A slow wipe found a hidden leak the clutter hid for months. A plumber would have charged you. Your sponge found it for free.

---

## Slide 7 (Step 4)
**Headline:** Safety: now look low.
**Body:** Crouch to a toddler's height. The drain cleaner is at grabbing level behind a door that does not lock. Move it up. Add a one dollar latch. Handoff: the clean view made the danger obvious.

---

## Slide 8 (Step 5)
**Headline:** Standardize: make the good way the easy way.
**Body:** Take a photo of the finished cabinet and tape it inside the door. The standard stops being a memory and becomes a picture anyone can match.

---

## Slide 9 (Step 6)
**Headline:** Sustain: keep it alive.
**Body:** A ten-second reset when you close the door. A monthly glance at the leak. And then it loops back to Sort, because a home is alive and its contents keep changing.

---

## Slide 10 (The big idea)
**Headline:** Fix what will not stay fixed: find the skipped step.
**Body:** A drawer that will not stay neat was usually never truly sorted. The work compounds only when you do the steps in order.

---

## Slide 11 (Soft CTA)
**Headline:** Take one lap. Fifteen minutes.
**Body:** One drawer, one shelf, or the cabinet under your own sink. Aim for one full lap, not perfect. The whole walk-through is Chapter 3 of 6S Success: Home Edition, free in the online book.
