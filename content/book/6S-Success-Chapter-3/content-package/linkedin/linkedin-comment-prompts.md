# LinkedIn Comment Prompts: The Six Steps That Transform Any Space

Five prompts to start a genuine conversation. Drop one into the comments of any post above, or use as a standalone question.

---

1. Cleaning slowly once turned into an inspection for me and found a problem I never knew about. What has a slow cleaning ever revealed in your home, a leak, an expired jar, a long-lost remote?

2. Be honest: which of the six steps do you skip most? Mine is Sustain, the ten-second reset that I somehow never reset. Curious where everyone else drops off.

3. Think of a space that will not stay fixed no matter how many times you redo it. If the real cause is a skipped step, which step do you think you skipped?

4. Crouch down to a toddler's height in front of one low cabinet. What is sitting at grabbing level that you have walked past standing up for years? I would love to hear what you find.

5. What is the smallest space you could walk through all six steps in fifteen minutes today? Name it in the comments, and that is basically a promise to yourself to do it.
