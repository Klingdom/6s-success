# Chapter 3 Summary: The Six Steps That Transform Any Space

*6S Success: Home Edition - Part One, Discovering 6S*

## One-line summary

This chapter walks the entire 6S loop once, end to end, through one real space (the cabinet under a kitchen sink), so you can feel how each step hands the next one something it could not have done alone.

## Short summary (about 100 words)

Chapter 3 stops explaining and starts doing. It picks one feral little space, the cabinet under a kitchen sink, and walks all six steps through it in order: Sort, Straighten, Shine, Safety, Standardize, Sustain. Along the way it shows the real point of the method, which is not the six words but the handoffs between them. Sort hands Straighten an empty space. Shine hands Safety a clear view of the danger. The chapter even finds a hidden slow leak while wiping, and a drain cleaner sitting at a toddler's eye level. It ends by asking you to take your own fifteen-minute lap.

## Full summary (about 250 words)

Chapter 3 is the pivot of Part One. The first two chapters told you what 6S is and where it came from. This one demonstrates it, on a single small space, so you see the whole method work before the book slows down to master each step later.

The throughline is the cabinet under a kitchen sink, the kind that "went feral" one reasonable item at a time. The chapter introduces its hero motif, the 6S Loop: six nodes in a ring, connected by handoffs, with Safety in the middle rendered in slate, and Sustain looping back to Sort.

Then it walks the loop. Sort empties the cabinet onto a towel and decides keep, relocate, or release. Straighten gives the survivors homes by how often you use them. Shine wipes the empty cabinet slowly and, in doing so, finds a slow leak the clutter had hidden for months, because cleaning done slowly is really inspection. Safety has you crouch to a child's height and notice the drain cleaner at grabbing level, then move it up and latch the door. Standardize tapes a photo inside the door so the right arrangement is visible, not just remembered. Sustain is a ten-second reset and a monthly glance at the leak, and it loops back to Sort.

The core idea is that each step makes the next one possible, so 6S is an order, not a menu. The chapter closes by inviting you to take one fifteen-minute lap on a small space of your own, which earns the friction meter its first honest move.
