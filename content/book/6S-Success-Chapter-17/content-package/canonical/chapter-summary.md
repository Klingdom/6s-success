## Full summary (about 250 words)

Chapter 17 is the second chapter of Part Five and continues Shine, the third of the six S's. Chapter 16 reframed cleaning as inspection and did one first pass on the launch pad; this chapter turns that single pass into a plan that covers the whole home and shares the labor. It runs into two walls in order. First, you do not actually know everything that needs cleaning, because the targets that most need Shine are the ones you never see. Second, even a complete list, in most homes, quietly falls to one person.

The fix is one artifact, the Shine list. Stop thinking room by room and start thinking kind by kind: surfaces, equipment and fixtures, stored things, and point of use. Grouping by kind drags the overlooked targets into the open. The rule for targets is: clean the whole thing, not just the part that shows, because the surface that faces away is the one that fails. Then the harder half, the names. The rule for assignments is: a job that belongs to everyone belongs to no one, put a name on every target. Let people choose their targets rather than get handed them, match jobs to ability, and share or rotate the worst ones.

Because the chapter plans but cleans nothing new, the friction meter holds exactly where the first Shine step left it, with a dashed forward projection toward the goal. The plan is a loaded spring; the move comes once the targets are cleaned. It hands off to Chapter 18.
