# X Short Posts: Shine Targets and Assignments

Chapter 17 of *6S Success: Home Edition*. Ten standalone posts. Each is at or under 280 characters, counting its number line, and makes sense on its own. Soft CTA on a few, not all.

---

1/
Chapter 17 of 6S Success: Home Edition gives Shine a plan for the whole home. One zone is clean, but the rest of the house is full of targets no one has named, and the work lands on the same tired pair of hands. This chapter names them, and names who. Free online.

---

2/
The rule for what to clean:

"Clean the whole thing, not just the part that shows."

The dirt that shows wipes off in a second and tells you nothing. The vent, the coils, the underside, the back: those are the parts that quietly decide how long your things last.

(265 chars)

---

3/
The rule for who cleans:

"A job that belongs to everyone belongs to no one. Put a name on every target."

A target that is nobody's job flows to whoever notices first, the same person every time. A name on the line ends the default. Read the free chapter.

(259 chars)

---

4/
Stop cleaning room by room. Clean by kind of target instead.

Four kinds catch almost everything: surfaces, equipment and fixtures, stored things, and point of use, the handles and switches your hands hit a hundred times a day and almost never clean.

(253 chars)

---

5/
"The dirt that matters is rarely the dirt you can see. It is on the top of the cupboard, behind the machine, under the mat, inside the drawer, and on the handle every hand in the house touches. A target you never named is a surface no one ever reads."

(254 chars)

6/
You cannot share a feeling of obligation. You can share a list.

The invisible pile of things you feel you ought to get to gets cleaned by one person, resentfully, or not at all. Written down as a set of lines, it can finally be split. Read the free chapter.

(261 chars)

7/
Every unassigned target flows toward the person who notices first, the same person every time. Nobody chose this. The absence of names chose it.

"A home kept by everyone asks less of anyone, and it stops depending on a single person never getting tired."

(258 chars)

---

8/
"A job you chose is a job you will actually do. A job you were handed is a job you will resent. So let people pick their targets before anyone gets handed the leftovers, and share out the jobs nobody wants instead of leaving them all with one person."

(254 chars)

---

9/
"A Shine list is one shared inventory of everything in a space that needs regular cleaning and inspection, grouped by kind of target, with a person's name against each one, so nothing is forgotten and no job quietly lands on a single pair of hands."
