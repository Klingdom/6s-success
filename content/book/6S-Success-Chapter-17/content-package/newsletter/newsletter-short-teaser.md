# Newsletter Short Teaser: Clean the Whole Thing, Not Just the Part That Shows

For a roundup or digest. 90 to 140 words.

---

One zone is clean, but the rest of the home is still unnamed, and Shine that belongs to no one lands on the same tired pair of hands. This chapter turns that first pass into a plan for the whole home. Two walls stand in the way: you do not know everything that needs cleaning, and even if you did, it falls on one person. The Shine list answers both. Group targets by kind, surfaces, equipment and fixtures, stored things, point of use, so the hidden ones surface, then put a name on every line so the work is shared. Clean the whole thing, not just the part that shows. The friction meter holds: a plan is real work, but not yet cleaning.
