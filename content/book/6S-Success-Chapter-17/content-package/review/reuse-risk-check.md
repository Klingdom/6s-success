# Reuse Risk Check: Chapter 17 Content Package

When one chapter becomes fifty-plus assets, the risk is overexposure: the same line or image hitting the same follower until it feels canned. This check flags where that can happen.

## Same-day collision
The calendar avoids two assets sharing a hook on the same day. If your LinkedIn and Facebook audiences overlap heavily, push one back a day so "A job that belongs to everyone belongs to no one. Put a name on every target." does not land twice in one day.

## Cross-posting risk
- The LinkedIn carousel outline (`linkedin/linkedin-carousel-outline.md`) and the slides carousel copy (`slides/linkedin-carousel-copy.md`) cover the same targets-and-assignments content. Post only one to LinkedIn (use the copy-ready slides file; keep the outline as planning).
- The LinkedIn newsletter and the email newsletter share structure; do not send both to the same subscriber the same day.

## Duplicate-content / SEO risk
- Web landing intro, ebook description, and LinkedIn article overlap in phrasing. Set the chapter page as the SEO canonical and mark syndicated copies with a canonical link.

## Cross-chapter risk (specific to Chapter 17)
- Chapter 17 is the SECOND Shine chapter and a PLANNING chapter. Keep it distinct from Chapter 16 behind it: Chapter 16 reframed cleaning as inspection and did one first pass; Chapter 17 does not re-argue why we clean, it lists the targets and shares them. Assets must not re-run Chapter 16's reframe as if new.
- Do not let targets-and-assignments slide FORWARD into the neighboring chapters. Ch 17 assets must not teach how to clean, products, or tools (that is Chapter 18), and must not build a repeatable routine, reset, or schedule (Chapter 19). Nor may they build Standardize standards/checklists or Sustain maintenance systems (later S's, a critical fence). Chapter 17 decides WHAT and WHO, not HOW and not how-to-keep-it-going. Grep confirms "cleaning methods," "products," "routine," and "schedule" appear in reader-facing copy only inside named fences or forward teasers to Chapters 18 and 19.
- Do NOT re-open Sort or Straighten: the list cleans and checks stored things, it does not re-sort or re-arrange them. Assets must not drift back into deciding what to keep (Sort) or where it lives (Straighten).
- The friction-meter HOLD is specific to this chapter (a principled planning hold with a dashed forward-projection arrow, the needle unmoved from Chapter 16's first Shine step). Do not reuse Chapter 16's FIRST-Shine-STEP move framing or imply the needle advanced here, and keep the goal honestly ahead (Shine has three chapters left; Safety, Standardize, and Sustain still follow).

## Originality and source-fidelity risk
- The chapter is a draft authored in the book voice, grounded in the 6S source ("Principles of 6S: Shine Step," the Steps to Complete Shine and the Traditional Shine Target Categories), not supplied verbatim source. All derived assets are faithful to that draft; re-sync if the chapter is revised.
- Quote cards are verbatim: 10 cards plus 11 alternates (21 quoted lines), each verified byte-identical to a quoted line in `canonical/chapter-quotes.md`. Nothing borrowed from outside the book; the four target categories and the Toyota / original-method origin are referenced lightly, home-scaled, not overclaimed.

## Recommendations
1. Rotate CTA wording (highest-value, lowest-effort fix; several posts close on "Read the free chapter").
2. Pick one carousel version for LinkedIn.
3. Set the chapter page as the SEO canonical.
4. Keep Chapter 17 from blurring into its neighbors: distinct from Chapter 16 (a reframe plus one first pass) behind it, and distinct from Chapters 18 to 19 (methods, then the reset) and the later S's (Standardize, Sustain) ahead of it. Chapter 17 is a planning chapter: the list and the names.
5. Re-sync all assets if the authored chapter changes after author review.
