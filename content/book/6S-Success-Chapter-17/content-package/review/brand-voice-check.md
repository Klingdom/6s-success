# Brand Voice Check: Chapter 17 Content Package

The voice of *6S Success: Home Edition* is warm, plain-spoken, and quietly confident. Chapter 17 is the SECOND chapter of Part Five and continues Shine, the third of the six S's. Its job is to turn Chapter 16's single first pass into a whole-home plan and to share the labor, so the register must be fairness and relief, not exhortation: not "clean harder," but "name every target, and name who." Because this chapter touches a live nerve (who actually does the housework), the voice must stay firmly anti-shame (a home where the cleaning silently fell to one person is the common case, not a moral failing), keep the plan honest (naming and assigning is real work, and it is not yet cleaning), and hold the goal honestly ahead.

## Voice fingerprint (from the authored final HTML and manuscript)
- **Warm and personal.** Talks to "you," returns to the launch pad from Chapter 16, and pictures the ordinary invisible targets: the top of the wardrobe, the coils behind the fridge, the inside of the medicine cabinet, the underside of the bath mat, the handle of every door.
- **Concrete over abstract.** The idea is taught through the microwave vent thick with grease, the fridge coils that make it run hot and die early, the bathroom overflow hole where the smell comes from, the "surface that faces away."
- **Anti-shame.** "In most homes the cleaning is not shared so much as absorbed, by one person, invisibly, until it feels like their personality rather than a pile of unassigned jobs." The load is a default, not a decision: "Nobody chose this. The absence of names chose it."
- **Fairness, not control (the reframe distinct from earlier chapters).** "A name against every target is not about control. It is about fairness, and about a home that stays cared for whether or not one person is watching."
- **Honest about scope and the meter.** The needle HOLDS on purpose because nothing new was cleaned: "A planning chapter that nudged the needle would be a small lie." The dashed arrow keeps the promise honest: "The move is coming; it is simply honest to say it has not happened yet."

## Specific wins
- "A job that belongs to everyone belongs to no one. Put a name on every target." is the most portable line in the chapter and travels intact across channels (grep-confirmed in 31 package files).
- "Clean the whole thing, not just the part that shows." delivers the targets rule in nine words and lands as a shareable reveal (grep-confirmed in 39 files).
- "You cannot share a feeling of obligation. You can share a list." gives the hero device a clean, quotable form (grep-confirmed in 27 files).
- "Nobody chose this. The absence of names chose it." names the no-owner failure mode without blaming anyone.

## Voice risks to watch
- **Hero vocabulary is dense.** "target," "list," "clean," "name," "assign," "share" recur because they are the subject; expected, not a tic, but vary the surrounding language when excerpting, and watch "whole," "everyone," "kind," and "hands" as the payoff words most likely to stack.
- **CTA sameness.** Rotate the LinkedIn and X sign-offs; several close on "Read the free chapter" / "Read it free online."
- **Keep the register fairness, not guilt.** The chapter touches who-does-the-housework directly, so any asset must not tip from "the fix is a shared list" into "you should feel bad." The frozen framing keeps it anti-shame (a default to fix, not a character flaw) and keeps the goal honestly ahead (Shine has three chapters left; Safety, Standardize, and Sustain still follow).

## Verdict
Voice is consistent and intact across all eleven channels, and the fairness-and-relief, anti-shame, planning-is-real-but-not-yet-cleaning job of Chapter 17 is honored throughout. Approve the voice. The chapter itself still needs author sign-off, since it was authored in the book voice (grounded in the 6S source, "Principles of 6S: Shine Step," the Steps to Complete Shine and the Traditional Shine Target Categories) rather than supplied from a verbatim source file.
