2. Chapter 16, Shine: Cleaning as Inspection
   The setup. Before you could plan the cleaning for the whole home, one first pass had to prove the idea. Chapter 16 opened the third S by reframing cleaning as inspection, don't just clean it, read it, and cleaned the launch pad on purpose, catching an umbrella-stand base rusting in standing water and a key hook worked loose in the wall, both cheap to fix today and expensive to ignore. What it left behind was one clean zone in a home still full of targets that were both unread and unnamed. This chapter names them, and names who.

3. Start your Shine list (the chapter action)
   Walk one room and write down five things that need regular cleaning but that nobody has ever been assigned, the top of something, the inside of something, a handle everyone touches. You just started your Shine list.

4. The Shine List worksheet (free resource)
   A printable carrying the four target categories, surfaces, equipment and fixtures, stored things, and point of use, with room to list your targets and write an owner against each, so the whole home gets on one page.

5. The Shine Board (free resource)
   A printable or posted chart pairing areas or targets with named owners, put somewhere the whole household sees it, the shared, visible version of the list, so the work stops quietly landing on one person.
