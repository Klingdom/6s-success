## You are here blurb
You are at Chapter 17, the second chapter of Part Five, Shine, still inside the third S. Chapter 16 reframed cleaning as inspection, don't just clean it, read it, and did one first cleaning-and-inspection pass on the launch pad, which caught two small problems early, a rusting umbrella-stand base and a loose key hook. This chapter turns that single pass into a plan for the whole home and shares the labor. It runs into two walls in order: first, you do not actually know everything that needs cleaning, because the targets that most need Shine are the ones you never see; second, even a complete list, in most homes, quietly falls to one person. The fix is one artifact, the Shine list. Stop thinking room by room and start thinking kind by kind, the four target categories, surfaces, equipment and fixtures, stored things, and point of use, so the overlooked targets come into view. The rule for targets is: clean the whole thing, not just the part that shows, because the surface that faces away is the one that fails. Then the harder half, the names: a job that belongs to everyone belongs to no one, put a name on every target, and let people choose their targets before anyone gets handed the leftovers, matched to ability, with the worst jobs shared or rotated. Because this chapter plans but cleans nothing new, the friction meter holds exactly where the first Shine step left it, with a dashed forward projection toward the goal showing where it steps once the plan is carried out. This chapter does not re-argue why we clean (Chapter 16), does not teach how to clean, products, or tools (Chapter 18), and does not build the repeatable reset (Chapter 19), and it does not re-open Sort or Straighten. The goal is still ahead, because Shine has three chapters left to run and Safety, Standardize, and Sustain still follow.
