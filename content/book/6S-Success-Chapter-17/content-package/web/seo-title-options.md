# SEO Title Options: Chapter 17, Shine Targets and Assignments

Five title options. Character counts are in parentheses. The canonical title is listed first.

1. Shine Targets and Assignments (30)
   Angle: canonical chapter title. Use as the H1 and primary on-page heading, with "Shine list," "clean the whole thing, not just the part that shows," and "put a name on every target" early in the body.

2. Put a Name on Every Target (26)
   Angle: leads with the frozen assignments rule. Names the fairness idea in six words and is highly quotable.

3. Clean the Whole Thing, Not Just the Part That Shows (51)
   Angle: opens with the frozen targets rule, the invisible-target insight. Answers a real search for why the surface that faces away is the one that fails.

4. What to Clean, and Who Does It (30)
   Angle: matches the plain search intent for a whole-home cleaning plan and shared labor. Strong for readers who cleaned one zone and want the rest.

5. The Four Kinds of Cleaning Target (33)
   Angle: names the reference device readers arrive wanting, grouping targets by kind rather than by room. Concrete and outcome-led for search.
