# Chapter 17 LinkedIn Comment Prompts: Shine Targets and Assignments

Five prompts to start genuine discussion. Drop one in the comments of a post or article to invite replies. Anti-shame voice, fairness not lecture: a home where the cleaning fell to one person is the common case, not a failing, and the fix is a list with shared names, not trying harder.

---

1. Be honest with me here. In your home, is there one person the cleaning quietly falls to? It is rarely a decision; it is a default, because an unassigned job flows to whoever notices first and cannot let it sit, the same person every time. No blame in naming it. Naming it is where the fix starts.

2. Clean the whole thing, not just the part that shows. What is the target in your home that everyone cleans the front of and no one cleans the back of? The microwave vent, the fridge coils, the sink overflow, the top of the cupboards. The surface that faces away is usually the one that fails.

3. Point of use is the kind of target people forget most: the handles, the switches, the remotes, the spots your hands hit a hundred times a day and almost never clean. Look around one room right now. What is the high-touch thing you have never once thought to clean?

4. A job you chose is a job you will actually do; a job you were handed is a job you will resent. If you have ever tried to share the housework, what actually worked, letting people pick their own targets, or handing them out? And what happened to the jobs nobody wanted?

5. You cannot share a feeling of obligation. You can share a list. If you have ever moved the cleaning off one pair of hands and out into the whole household, how did you do it? What changed once the work was written down instead of carried in one head?
