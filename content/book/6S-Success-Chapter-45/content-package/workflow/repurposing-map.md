## Reuse cautions
- Types, not brands, everywhere. No invented prices.
- **Disposal is always hazardous waste or a local collection.** Never a drain, gutter, or the ground.
- **Never depict a car on a jack, anyone under a raised vehicle, or any chemical being decanted or mixed.**
- Never depict a ladder on a slope or a bin carried down with no free hand.
- Deliver the fire and poison material as maintenance, not alarm; state the oily-rag and antifreeze facts once each.
- Every asset closes on the before-and-after signature and keeps the pale floor as the element that keeps working.
- Stay in the garage: do not reuse home-office (Ch 44) or workshop (Ch 46) material.
