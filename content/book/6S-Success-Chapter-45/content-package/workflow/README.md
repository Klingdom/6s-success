## Fences for this chapter
- Product **types** only, never brand names.
- **Disposal is always household hazardous waste or a local collection.** Never a drain, a gutter, or the ground, in any asset.
- **No vehicle servicing, and nobody under a raised car.** The jack and axle stands appear in the hazard list only, never as a procedure.
- No chemical is ever shown or described being decanted or mixed.
- The safety material is delivered as maintenance, not alarm. The two surprising facts, oily rags and antifreeze, are stated plainly and once each.
- Friction meter retired for Part 9; every asset closes on the before-and-after signature, and keeps the pale floor as the element that keeps working after the photo.
