# Chapter 45 Landing Page Intro: The Garage

Every other room in this book fails from use: the kitchen from cooking, the laundry room from washing, the bathroom from water. The garage does not. It fails from arrival.

Things come in from every other room in the house, they are set down, and because the door stays shut most of the week nothing ever comes along to force a verdict on any of them. That is how a good chisel and a box nobody has opened since the move end up on the same shelf, treated exactly alike. So the sorting question here is not whether you still want a thing. It is whether it was ever actually stored, or only postponed.

This chapter breaks the garage into seven small zones and gives you, for each one, the exact inputs and the numbered steps to clean and shine it, from the bench dog holes to the roof deck above the overhead rack. The bench comes first because it is the only surface anything can be laid out on. The slab comes last, and it gets degreased properly once, because a pale floor turns every new drip into information.

Product types only, no brands. Start at the bench.
