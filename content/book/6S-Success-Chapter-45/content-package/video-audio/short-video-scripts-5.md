# Chapter 45 Short Video Scripts: The Garage

Five scripts, 30 to 60 seconds each.

## 1. It fails from arrival
**Hook:** Your garage does not fail from use. It fails from arrival.
**Body:** Every other room gets dirty because you use it. The garage takes what every other room evicts, and because the door stays shut most of the week nothing ever forces a verdict on any of it.
**Close:** That is why a good chisel and an unopened box sit on the same shelf.

## 2. Stored or postponed
**Hook:** Nothing in your garage is being stored.
**Body:** The chair with the wobbly leg. The charity sack by the side door. The printer you replaced. None of that is storage. It is a decision somebody put down and walked away from.
**Close:** No rail, hook, shelf, or bin waiting for it? Then it gets a verdict today.

## 3. Clean the floor last
**Hook:** Clean your garage floor last. Not first.
**Body:** Everything in the room wants to end up on the slab. Until the walls and racks have taken what belongs on them, there is no floor to clean, only a pile to work around.
**Close:** Bench first. Slab last.

## 4. What a pale floor buys you
**Hook:** Here is why you degrease a garage floor properly once.
**Body:** After that a sweep holds it. And a pale floor turns every new drip into information: you see the gearbox weeping the week it starts, not the month it fails.
**Close:** On a dark floor, all of that is invisible until it is expensive.

## 5. Oily rags do not need a flame
**Hook:** This one surprises people.
**Body:** Rags soaked with oil, stain, or solvent, balled up in an open bin, can heat on their own as the oil cures. No spark. No flame anywhere near them.
**Close:** Metal can. Lid on. Tonight.
