# Chapter 45 Teleprompter Script: The Garage

*Two minutes. Read at an even pace. Pause at each line break.*

Every other room in this book fails from use.

The garage fails from arrival.

It takes what every other room in the house evicts.

And because the door stays shut most of the week, nothing ever forces a verdict on any of it.

That is why your best chisel and a box you have never opened sit on the same shelf.

So the question here is not do I still want this.

It is: was this ever actually stored, or was it only postponed.

The chair with the wobbly leg. The sack by the side door.

Those are not storage. They are decisions somebody put down.

The rule: anything carried in with no rail, shelf, or bin waiting for it gets its verdict the same day.

Seven zones. Start at the bench, because until one surface is clear, everything you pick up goes back down somewhere else.

And finish on the floor.

Because until the walls and racks have taken what belongs on them, there is no floor to clean.

Degrease it properly once. After that, a sweep holds it.

And what you bought is a pale floor.

A pale floor turns every new drip into information.

You will see the gearbox weeping the week it starts. Not the month it fails.

Weight goes low. Bolts go into joists.

Oily rags go in a metal can with a lid.

And nothing in this room is ever decanted into an unlabelled bottle.
