# The Garage: Nothing in It Is Stored, It Is Postponed

*A LinkedIn article from Chapter 45 of 6S Success: Home Edition.*

Every other room in this book fails from use. The kitchen fails from cooking, the laundry room from washing, the bathroom from water. The garage does not fail from use at all. It fails from arrival.

> The garage takes what every other room in the house evicts, and because the door stays shut most of the week nothing ever forces a verdict on any of it. That is why your best chisel and a box you have not opened since you moved in can sit on the same shelf. So understand what is actually in here: the chair with the wobbly leg, the charity sack that has stood by the side door since spring, the printer you replaced. None of it is being stored. It is being postponed.

## Seven zones, bench first and slab last

> The garage is seven small zones, not one big job: the primary workbench, the hand tool wall and cabinets, the power tool and battery zone, the automotive care zone, the sports and recreation zone, the lawn and garden tool zone, and the bulk and overhead storage.

## Why the floor is cleaned last

> Everything in this room wants to end up on the slab, which is why the slab is cleaned last: only once the walls and the racks have taken what belongs on them is there a floor to clean rather than a pile to work around. Then degrease it properly, once. And here is what you actually bought with that afternoon: a pale floor turns every new drip into information.

## Weight and height

> Every other room's hazards were small. This room's are heavy, and they are all above somebody.

## And the chemistry

> This is the room where the household's genuinely dangerous chemistry lives, and none of it is dangerous by itself. It becomes dangerous by proximity.
