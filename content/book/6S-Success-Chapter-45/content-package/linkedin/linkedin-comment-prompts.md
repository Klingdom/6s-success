# Chapter 45 Comment Prompts: The Garage

1. What is in your garage right now that is being postponed rather than stored?
2. How long has the charity sack, or its equivalent, been standing by your side door?
3. How many hammers, spades, or adjustable wrenches do you actually own?
4. Can you see the colour of your garage floor?
5. Where do your oily rags currently live?
6. Is there a tool in your garage on a battery platform you can no longer buy packs for?
7. What is in the box overhead that you have never opened?
8. Do your overhead rack bolts land in joists, or did somebody hope?
