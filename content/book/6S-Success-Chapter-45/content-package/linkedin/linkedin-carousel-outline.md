# Chapter 45 LinkedIn Carousel Outline: The Garage

Twelve slides. Product types only.

- Slide 1 (hook): Your garage does not fail from use. It fails from arrival.
- Slide 2: It takes what every other room evicts, and the shut door means no verdict is ever forced.
- Slide 3: The question is not "do I want this". It is "was this stored, or only postponed".
- Slide 4: The one-day rule: no rail, hook, shelf, or bin waiting means a verdict today.
- Slide 5: Seven zones. Bench first, because it is the only surface you can lay anything out on.
- Slide 6: Slab last, because until the walls take what belongs on them there is no floor to clean.
- Slide 7: Degrease once. Then a sweep holds it.
- Slide 8: What you bought: a pale floor turns every new drip into information.
- Slide 9: Weight low, bolts into joists, nothing overhanging where a car door opens.
- Slide 10: Oily rags can heat on their own. Metal can, lid on.
- Slide 11: Antifreeze tastes sweet. Nothing is ever decanted unlabelled.
- Slide 12 (close): Do one zone this week. Make it the bench.
