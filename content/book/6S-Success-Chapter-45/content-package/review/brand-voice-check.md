# Chapter 45 Brand Voice Check: The Garage

## The register
Warm, practical, expert, and plain about danger without alarm. Second person. This chapter carries more genuine hazard than any other in the book, so the discipline is to state each danger once, at normal volume, with the reason attached, and then move on.

## Tone risks specific to this chapter, and how they were handled

**Fear.** This chapter contains a self-heating fire mechanism, a poison a child would drink willingly, and several hundred kilos of stored weight above people's heads. The temptation is to escalate. The controls used: each hazard is stated once in the frozen safety block, the two counterintuitive ones get exactly one extra sentence each in prose, and none is repeated in a headline position in any social asset. No asset opens on fear; every opener is the arrival diagnosis.

**The antifreeze sentence.** "A dog will drink a puddle of it, and a small child will drink from a decanted bottle" is the bluntest line in the book so far. It is kept because softening it defeats its only purpose, and it is followed immediately by the actionable rule rather than left hanging.

**Sentimental calls.** Three of the seven ask the reader to release things that carry identity: the half-finished project, the sport they stopped playing, the box that moved house with them. Each names the cost before giving the test, and the sports call offers a genuine exception with a date rather than pretending there is never a reason to keep. That is the pattern the book has used since the Sort chapters.

**The "you own three hammers" opening.** This is the closest the book comes to teasing the reader. It works because the very next sentence concedes the point, that each one held on its own still works, before reframing the test. Checked, and it reads as recognition rather than mockery.

## Fences observed
- Product types only, never brands.
- No disposal route other than hazardous waste or a local collection.
- No servicing, no vehicle lifting, and nobody under a raised car in any asset or b-roll note.
- Friction meter retired; closes on the before-and-after signature.
- Uses without re-teaching: Chapter 31 (the nine rules), Chapter 14 (visual controls), Chapter 21 (the hazard hunt), Chapter 22 (prevent and prepare), Chapter 24 (the dated bin and the map), Chapter 25 (the ideal-state photo).
