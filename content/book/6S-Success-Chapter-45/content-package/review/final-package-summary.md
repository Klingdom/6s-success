## Product appendix now 110

The Manual's Garage `products_used` lists required eight inputs the 102-type library did not carry. All eight were added and the appendix regenerated in Master and on the Desktop: `MPL-00104` light machine oil, `MPL-00105` concrete degreaser, `MPL-00106` oil-absorbent granules, `MPL-00107` electrical contact cleaner (Cleaning Supplies); `MPL-00108` wire brush and scraper set, `MPL-00109` push broom, `MPL-00110` extendable duster (Cleaning Tools); `MPL-00111` stable step ladder (Safety, distinct from the existing step stool because an overhead rack is not a step-stool reach). Garage mapped types rise from 28 to 36.

**A line was drawn and is recorded in the brief for Phil to move if he disagrees.** Five further items named in the Manual's `products_used` were deliberately NOT added and appear as ordinary nouns in the steps: a garden hose, baking soda, a mild gear disinfectant, a flat file, and compressed air. The rule applied: if it is a cleaning or protection input a reader must deliberately buy and will reuse across rooms, it becomes a catalogue type; if it is a household commodity or a one-job hand tool, it stays a noun. Without a line like this the appendix inflates on every tool-heavy room.
