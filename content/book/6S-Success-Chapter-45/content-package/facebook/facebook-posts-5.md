# Chapter 45 Facebook Posts: The Garage

Five standalone posts.

## 1
Your garage does not fail from use. It fails from arrival. Things come in from every other room, get set down, and because the door stays shut most of the week nothing ever forces a verdict on any of them.

## 2
The sorting question in a garage is not "do I still want this". It is "was this ever actually stored, or only postponed". The chair with the wobbly leg. The charity sack by the side door. Those are not storage. They are decisions somebody walked away from.

## 3
Clean the garage floor last. It sounds backwards, but everything in the room wants to end up on the slab, so until the walls and racks have taken what belongs on them there is no floor to clean, only a pile to work around.

## 4
Degrease the slab properly once and a sweep holds it after that. What you actually buy is a pale floor, and a pale floor turns every new drip into information: you see the gearbox weeping the week it starts, not the month it fails.

## 5
Oily rags do not need a flame. Rags soaked with oil, stain, or solvent, balled up in an open bin, can heat on their own as the oil cures. They belong in a metal can with a lid, not a plastic bucket under the bench.
