# Chapter 45 Facebook Group Discussion Post: The Garage

**Question for the group: what is in your garage right now that is being postponed rather than stored?**

There is a real difference, and it is the whole idea of this chapter. The garage does not fail from use like the kitchen or the bathroom. It fails from arrival: things come in from every other room, get set down, and because the door stays shut most of the week nothing ever forces a verdict on any of them.

The chair with the wobbly leg. The charity sack by the side door. The printer you replaced. None of it is stored. It is postponed.

Two more while we are here:

- **Can you see the colour of your garage floor?** Degrease it properly once and a sweep holds it, and what you get is a pale slab that shows every new drip. You spot the gearbox weeping the week it starts instead of the month it fails.
- **Where do your oily rags live?** If the answer is an open bin under the bench, move them to a metal can with a lid today. Oil-soaked rags can heat on their own with no flame anywhere near them.

Tell us which one you are settling this weekend.
