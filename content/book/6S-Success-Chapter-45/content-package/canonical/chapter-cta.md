# Chapter 45 CTA and Companion Resources: The Garage

## Primary call to action
Do one zone this week, and make it the workbench, because until one solid surface is clear, everything you pick up just goes back down somewhere else.

## Companion resources
- **The Garage Reset card** (printable: the seven zones, the done-looks-like for each, the bench-first and slab-last order of work).
- **The Garage Kit list** (printable: the cleaners, tools, storage, and safety types the room needs, grouped by job).
- **The Postponed or Stored card** (the one-day rule and the test: does this thing have a rail, hook, shelf, or bin already waiting for it).
- **The Garage Chemistry card** (what must never share a shelf: fuel and fertiliser, packs and fuel, rags and an open bin, and nothing decanted unlabelled).

## Register and fences
- Warm, practical, expert, and plain about danger without alarmism. Product types only, never brand names. No invented statistics or prices.
- Disposal is always named as household hazardous waste or a local collection, never a specific service, and never a drain, gutter, or the ground.
- No repair, servicing, or vehicle-lifting instructions. The chapter cleans, looks, and flags. Jacks and axle stands are named as a hazard, never as a procedure.
- Friction meter retired for Part 9; close on the before-and-after room signature.
- Previous: Chapter 44, The Home Office. Next: Chapter 46, The Workshop.

## Words to avoid
- No brand names. No instruction to work under a raised vehicle. No advice to mix, decant, or dispose of chemicals other than via hazardous waste.
