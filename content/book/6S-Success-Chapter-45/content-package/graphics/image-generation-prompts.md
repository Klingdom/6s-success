# Chapter 45 Image Generation Prompts: The Garage

House style: warm paper background, editorial illustration, serif display type, muted terracotta, honey, slate, and green accents. No logos, no brand names, no readable packaging or label text, no QR codes. No em dashes in any rendered text.

1. **Opener, two states.** A domestic garage drawn twice side by side. Left: a workbench buried under boxes and offcuts, bottles crowded on a shelf, bikes lying on a dark stained slab, a rake leaning in a corner. Right: the same garage with a bare bench holding a vise and a task light, hand tools hanging on painted outlines, bottles upright on a tray, bikes on wall hooks, and a pale swept floor.
2. **The seven zones.** A warm overhead floor plan of a garage with seven numbered areas marked and labelled in a serif face, and the parking bay shown clear.
3. **The pale floor.** Two concrete slabs side by side, one dark and oil-stained, one pale and clean with a single fresh drip visible. Calm and diagrammatic.
4. **Degreasing the slab.** A person working absorbent granules into an old stain with a stiff brush, kneeling, with a push broom leaning nearby. Warm and workmanlike.
5. **The tool wall.** A pegboard with hand tools hung on painted silhouettes, one silhouette empty, saw teeth sheathed.
6. **The proximity rule.** A simple editorial diagram of a shelf showing a fuel can and a bag of feed separated, with a metal rag can with its lid on beside them.
7. **The signature pair.** A matched before-and-after of the whole garage from the doorway, same framing, same light, with the floor clearly the changed element.
