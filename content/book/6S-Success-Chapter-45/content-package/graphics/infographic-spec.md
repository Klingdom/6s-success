# Chapter 45 Infographic Spec: The Garage

## Figure 2: the seven zones
- Canvas 1000 x 560, warm paper fill (taller than the six-zone rooms, since seven cells need three rows).
- Title in Fraunces, 22px: "The garage, drawn as its seven zones".
- Seven panels on a three-row grid, each 270 x 130, rounded 8, panel fill with a rule-coloured stroke.
- Each panel carries a green numbered disc and a serif zone name: 1 Workbench, 2 Hand tool wall, 3 Power tools, 4 Automotive care, 5 Sports and rec, 6 Lawn and garden, 7 Overhead storage.
- Footer in terracotta: seven small jobs, then the slab, which is cleaned last of all.

## Figure 3: the method and the kit
- Canvas 1000 x 480, two panels of 460 x 410.
- Left panel headed "The method": four rules, each a coloured lead line with grey gloss lines beneath. Lead lines: "Postponed, not stored" (terracotta), "Get it off the floor, then read it" (green), "Weight and height are the hazard" (slate), "This room holds the real chemistry" (terracotta).
- Right panel headed "The kit": nine lines of product types grouped by job, leading with the concrete degreaser and absorbent granules.
- Footer in terracotta: give it a verdict, get it off the floor, put the weight low, keep the chemistry apart.

## Figure 4: the signature

- The after panel carries an extra honey-outlined label reading "a floor pale enough to read", and the caption beneath reads "the after, and a working instrument".
