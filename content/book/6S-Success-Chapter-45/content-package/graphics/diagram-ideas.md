# Chapter 45 Diagram Ideas: The Garage

1. **Stored against postponed** (hero): two columns of the same objects. Left column labelled "has a rail, hook, shelf, or bin waiting", right column labelled "has nowhere to go, so it is a decision put down". Caption: only one of these is storage.
2. **The order of work**: the seven zones drawn as a sequence with the bench at the head and the slab detached at the end, with an arrow from every zone pointing down onto the floor. Caption: there is no floor to clean until the walls have taken what belongs on them.
3. **The pale floor as an instrument**: two slabs side by side, one dark and stained, one pale with a single fresh drip clearly visible on it. Caption: the same leak, on two floors.
4. **The proximity map**: a shelf elevation showing what must never share a shelf, fuel with fertiliser, packs with fuel, rags in an open bin, with each pairing crossed and its safe placement shown beside it.
5. **Load and height**: a rack elevation with heavy bins on the bottom rail and light bulky items on the top, plus a bolt detail showing a joist against plasterboard.
6. **The drawn outline**: a pegboard section with tools hung on painted silhouettes and one blank outline standing out. Caption: an empty outline is a question you answer before you close the door.
7. **The dated bin end**: a close crop of an overhead bin end with a large label readable from below and a month and year written under it.
