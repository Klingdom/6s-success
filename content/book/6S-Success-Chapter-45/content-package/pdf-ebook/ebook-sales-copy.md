# Chapter 45 Ebook Sales Copy: The Garage

**Your garage does not fail from use. It fails from arrival.**

Chapter 45 of 6S Success: Home Edition takes the six-S loop into the largest, heaviest, and most dangerous room in the house, and starts by naming what is actually wrong with it: nothing in there is being stored. It is being postponed.

Inside:

- The seven zones of a garage, with the exact inputs and the numbered steps for each, from the bench dog holes to the roof deck above the overhead rack.
- The one-day rule that keeps a garage clear once you have cleared it.
- Why the floor is cleaned last, how to degrease a slab properly once, and what a pale floor then tells you about your car and your house.
- The load rules for a room where the hazards are heavy and stored above somebody's head.
- The proximity rules for the household's genuinely dangerous chemistry, including the two facts most people do not know about oily rags and antifreeze.
- Seven hard calls, one per zone: the project that has been there since spring, the third hammer, the tool whose batteries no longer exist, the fluids for the car you no longer own, the gear from the sport you stopped playing, the half-used chemicals, and the box you have never opened.

Product types, never brands. Seven zones, four new rules, and a floor that starts working for you.
