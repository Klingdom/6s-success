# Chapter 45 Reader Discussion Questions: The Garage

1. Walk your garage and name three things being postponed rather than stored. Who put each one there?
2. Which room in your house sends the most things to the garage, and what would stop that at the source?
3. Can you see the colour of your garage floor? If not, what would a pale slab have told you this year?
4. How many duplicates of one tool do you own, and which one does your hand actually go to?
5. Where do your oily rags live right now, and where should they live by tonight?
6. Is anything in your garage decanted into a container that does not carry its own label?
7. Do you know whether your overhead rack bolts land in joists?
8. Which of the seven calls in this chapter is the one you have been putting off longest?
