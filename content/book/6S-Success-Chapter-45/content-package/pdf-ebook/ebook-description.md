# Chapter 45 Ebook Description: The Garage

The fifteenth room playbook of Part Nine, and the longest room in the book at seven zones.

Every other room in 6S Success fails from use. The garage fails from arrival. It takes what every other room in the house evicts, and because the door stays shut most of the week nothing ever forces a verdict on any of it, which is why a good chisel and a box nobody has opened since the move end up on the same shelf.

This chapter breaks the room into seven zones and gives, for each, the inputs to gather and the exact order of steps to clean and shine it. The order matters at both ends: the bench first because it is the only surface anything can be laid out on, and the slab last because until the walls and racks have taken what belongs on them there is no floor to clean, only a pile to work around.

On top of the nine rules of Shine it adds four of its own, and one of them pays a dividend no other room offers. Degrease the slab properly once and you own a pale floor, and a pale floor is not a cosmetic result. It is an instrument that reports on your car and your house the week something starts going wrong rather than the month it fails.

It is also the heaviest room in the house and the one holding the household's real chemistry, so the safety pass here is most of the point rather than a tidy-up at the end.
