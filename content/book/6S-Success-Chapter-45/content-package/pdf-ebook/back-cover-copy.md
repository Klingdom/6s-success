# Chapter 45 Back Cover Copy: The Garage

*Nothing in your garage is being stored. It is being postponed.*

The garage takes what every other room in the house evicts, and because the door stays shut most of the week nothing ever forces a verdict on any of it. That is how your best chisel and a box you have not opened since you moved in end up on the same shelf.

Seven zones. Four rules on top of the nine. The one-day rule that keeps it clear. And the reason you clean the floor last, degrease it properly once, and end up with a slab that tells you what your car is doing before your car does.

**6S Success: Home Edition**, Chapter Forty-Five.
