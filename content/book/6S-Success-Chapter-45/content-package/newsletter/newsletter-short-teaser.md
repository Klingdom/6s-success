# Chapter 45 Newsletter Short Teaser: The Garage

The garage takes what every other room in the house evicts, and because the door stays shut most of the week nothing ever forces a verdict on any of it. That is why your best chisel and a box you have not opened since you moved in can sit on the same shelf.

Chapter 45 breaks it into seven zones with the exact inputs and numbered steps for each, and adds four rules to the nine rules of Shine: postponed not stored, get it off the floor then read the floor, weight and height are the hazard, and this room holds the real chemistry.

Start at the bench. Leave the slab until last.
