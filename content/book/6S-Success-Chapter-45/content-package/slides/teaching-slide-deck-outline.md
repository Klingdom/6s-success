# Chapter 45 Teaching Slide Deck Outline: The Garage

Eighteen slides, about 30 minutes. The longest room in the book.

1. Title: The Garage. Part Nine, room-by-room playbooks.
2. The claim: this room fails from arrival, not from use.
3. Why that matters: nothing ever forces a verdict behind a shut door.
4. The chisel and the unopened box on the same shelf.
5. Stored or postponed: the sorting question that is unique to this room.
6. The one-day rule, and why it is the thing that keeps a garage clear.
7. Seven zones, and why the order matters at both ends.
8. The bench first: the only surface you can lay anything out on.
9. The slab last: there is no floor until the walls have taken what belongs on them.
10. Degrease once. Then the sweep holds it.
11. What a pale floor actually buys: the slab as an instrument.
12. Weight and height: heavy low, bolts into joists, nothing over the car door.
13. The real chemistry: dangerous by proximity, not by itself.
14. Two facts people do not know: oily rags, and antifreeze.
15. The centerpiece: zone by zone, inputs and numbered steps (walk one zone live).
16. The seven hard calls, one per zone.
17. Safety: the five questions in the heaviest room in the house.
18. The signature, and the floor that keeps working after the photo.
