# Chapter 45 LinkedIn Carousel Copy: The Garage

**Slide 1**
Your garage does not fail from use.
It fails from arrival.

**Slide 2**
It takes what every other room evicts.
The shut door means no verdict is ever forced.

**Slide 3**
Your best chisel and a box you never opened,
on the same shelf, treated exactly alike.

**Slide 4**
The question is not "do I want this".
It is "was this stored, or only postponed".

**Slide 5**
No rail, hook, shelf, or bin waiting for it?
Then it gets its verdict today.

**Slide 6**
Seven zones. Bench first.
It is the only surface you can lay anything out on.

**Slide 7**
Slab last. Until the walls take what belongs on them,
there is no floor to clean, only a pile to work around.

**Slide 8**
Degrease once. A sweep holds it after that.

**Slide 9**
What you bought: a pale floor
turns every new drip into information.

**Slide 10**
Weight low. Bolts into joists.
Nothing overhanging where a car door opens.

**Slide 11**
Oily rags can heat on their own.
Metal can. Lid on.

**Slide 12**
Do one zone this week.
Make it the bench.
