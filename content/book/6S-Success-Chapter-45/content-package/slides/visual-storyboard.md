# Chapter 45 Visual Storyboard: The Garage

Four figures, matching the chapter's SVGs. Warm paper, terracotta, honey, slate, green. Types, not brands.

## Figure 1: the opener, two states
A full-width pair. Left, the room as it runs: a bench buried under other zones' overflow, fluids for a car long sold, bikes lying on the floor where they roll under the car, and a slab so dark a fresh drip would never show. Right, the same room: a bare bench with the vise and the task light, tools on drawn outlines, dated bottles upright on a tray, bikes on wall hooks, and concrete you can see the colour of.

## Figure 2: the seven zones
A warm overhead plan, seven numbered cells on a three-row grid: workbench, hand tool wall, power tools, automotive care, sports and rec, lawn and garden, overhead storage. Footer line: seven small jobs, then the slab, which is cleaned last of all.

## Figure 3: the method and the kit
Two panels. Left, the four rules of the garage layer with a short gloss under each. Right, the kit as product types grouped by job, leading with the concrete degreaser and absorbent granules because the slab pass is what makes this room different.

## Figure 4: the before-and-after signature
A matched pair from the same doorway with the camera icon between them. The after panel carries a small honey-outlined label reading "a floor pale enough to read", because in this room the floor is the part of the picture that keeps working after the photo is taken.
