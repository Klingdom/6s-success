## Important notice: please read before you begin

**This book is a guide to organizing and cleaning a home. It is not a safety manual, a medical
reference, a veterinary reference, or a substitute for a qualified professional.**

The author and publisher have made a genuine effort to describe safe, ordinary household practice.
Even so, homes differ, materials differ, and people differ. You are the only person who can see
your home, and you remain responsible for judging what is safe to do in it.

**By using this book you accept the following.**

**Do the work at your own risk.** Every task described here is undertaken at your own risk. The
author and publisher accept no liability for any loss, injury, damage, or expense arising from
following, or failing to follow, anything in this book, to the fullest extent permitted by law.

**Know your limits, and stop at them.** This book does not teach structural, electrical, plumbing,
gas, roofing, or carpentry work, and it never asks you to attempt any of them. Where a task
approaches one of those trades, the instruction is always to stop and call a qualified
professional. Follow that instruction. If a job requires a licensed trade in your area, use one.

**Read the label, every time.** Cleaning products are chemicals. Always read and follow the
manufacturer's instructions, warnings, and dilution directions for every product you use, and the
manufacturer's instructions for every tool and appliance. Where this book names a type of product,
the manufacturer's own label always takes precedence over anything written here.

**Never mix cleaning products.** Mixing products, in particular anything containing bleach with
anything containing ammonia or acid, can release dangerous gases. Use one product at a time, and
ventilate the room.

**Test first.** Test any cleaning method on a small, hidden area before you use it on a whole
surface. Finishes, stone, fabrics, and older materials vary widely.

**Protect children and pets.** Keep all cleaning products, tools, batteries, and medicines out of
reach and, where possible, locked. Never leave a task, a chemical, or a tool unattended where a
child or an animal can reach it.

**Power down before you clean.** Unplug corded tools and appliances, or remove the battery from
cordless ones, before cleaning them.

**Ladders and height.** Use a stable step ladder on a level surface. Do not stand on furniture,
boxes, or the top step. Where possible, have another adult present.

**In an emergency, stop and call for help.** If someone is injured, swallows a household product,
or is exposed to fumes, contact your local emergency number or your regional poison control
service immediately. If you smell gas, leave the area, do not operate switches or flames, and call
your gas supplier or emergency services from outside.

**Medicines and hazardous waste.** Dispose of medicines and hazardous household waste according to
the guidance of your local authority, pharmacy take-back scheme, or national regulator. Local
rules differ, and they take precedence over any general suggestion in this book.

**Nothing here is medical, veterinary, or professional advice.** Where this book touches on infant
sleep, allergies, medicines, pets, or anything affecting health, it is describing general household
organization only. Consult a qualified professional for advice about your own circumstances.

**Products and links.** This book names categories and types of product, not brands. Any resource

## How to use this book

This book is in two halves, and they are meant to be read in different ways.

**Chapters 1 to 30 teach the method.** They are written to be read in order, once, from the
beginning. Each of the six S's builds on the one before it, and the worked examples run
continuously through them. Read this half like a book.

**Chapters 31 to 50 apply the method, one room at a time.** They are written to be used, not read
through. Each chapter takes one room, breaks it into its micro zones, and gives step-by-step
instructions for each zone with the exact inputs you need. Find your room, and work the zones in
the order given. Read this half like a manual.

You do not need to finish the first half before you start the second, but the room chapters assume
you know what the six S's mean, so read at least Chapter 2 first.

**The companion volumes.** *The 6S Home Micro Zone SOP Field Manual* is the field reference for all
114 micro zones and is designed to be carried to the shelf you are working on. The workbook is
where you record your own audit scores, photographs, and decisions. Neither is required to use this
book.

**A note on the six S's.** The second S is **Straighten**. Some industrial versions of this method
call it "Set in Order." This book uses Straighten throughout. The fourth S is **Safety**, which is
the addition that makes this six S's rather than five.
