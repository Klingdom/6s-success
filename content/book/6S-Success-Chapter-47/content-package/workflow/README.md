## Fences for this chapter
- Product **types** only, never brand names.
- **Pet treatments are a storage-height rule only.** No veterinary advice, no dosing, no product identification anywhere in the package.
- Never advise mixing cleaners or decanting into an unlabelled container.
- **Never depict anyone on a wet hard floor in socks**, even to illustrate the hazard.
- In the sorting sequence, coats are **handed to their owners**, never lifted away by one person. That is the chapter's argument and it must not be contradicted visually.
- This is the most populated film in Part 9: show more than one person wherever possible.
