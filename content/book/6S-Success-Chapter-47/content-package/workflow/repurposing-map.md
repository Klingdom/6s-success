## Reuse cautions
- Types, not brands, everywhere. No invented prices.
- **No veterinary advice.** Pet treatments appear only as a storage-height rule.
- **Never depict anyone on a wet hard floor in socks**, even as a hazard demonstration.
- **Never depict a coat being lifted away by one person during sorting.** It is handed to its owner; that is the chapter's argument.
- Never depict decanting, or two cleaners in one closed caddy.
- Every asset closes on the before-and-after signature and keeps the floor strip as the thing being judged.
- Stay in the mudroom: do not reuse workshop (Ch 46) or hall closet (Ch 48) material, and keep the entryway (Ch 31) distinct.
