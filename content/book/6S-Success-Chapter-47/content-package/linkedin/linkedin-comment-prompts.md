# Chapter 47 Comment Prompts: The Mudroom

1. What is on your mudroom or entry floor right now, and which full hook put it there?
2. Does every person in your house have a hook they can reach without asking?
3. How many layers stop grit at your door: none, one, or three?
4. When did you last shake out the mat or rinse the boot tray?
5. Is your boot tray where boots actually come off, or two steps further in?
6. Is there a lidded box in your entry you have not opened this season?
7. Which coat has hung by your door untouched since March?
8. Where does your pet's food bin live, and where does the bowl actually sit?
