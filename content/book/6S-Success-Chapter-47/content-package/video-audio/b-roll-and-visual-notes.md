# Chapter 47 B-Roll and Visual Notes: The Mudroom

## Must-get shots
- The floor beneath a full hook rail, tight, with bags and boots on it. This is the opening image and the whole diagnosis.
- Coats coming off a rail and being handed person to person, not lifted away by one person.
- Hooks being set at three different heights on three vertical strips, with a child reaching their own without help.
- A damp cloth going along the wall at shoulder height, and the grey smear visibly lifting.
- The crevice tool running the narrow floor strip under the hooks, and the felted band of dust along the skirting.
- The three threshold layers, shot in sequence: bristle mat outside, absorbent mat inside, lipped tray.
- The outside mat shaken out, and the tray scrubbed and tipped, on the same day.
- A boot tray being placed at the spot where boots actually come off, with a hand marking its footprint in floor tape.
- The lead, collar and bag roll clipped together as one kit and hung as a single unit.
- A broom clipped to the wall head-up with its bristles clear of the floor.

## Do not shoot
- Any readable brand name, logo, or label text. Types only.
- Anyone walking on a wet hard floor in socks, at any point, even to illustrate the hazard.
- Any pet treatment product close enough to read, and no dosing or application whatsoever.
- Any cleaner being decanted or two cleaners together in one caddy.
- A person lifting a coat away from someone else in the sorting sequence; the whole point is that it is handed over.

## Tone
Domestic and warm, and noticeably more populated than the other room films, because this is the one chapter done with the household present. Show more than one person in frame wherever possible.
