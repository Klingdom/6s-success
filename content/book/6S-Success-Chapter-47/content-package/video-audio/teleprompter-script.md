# Chapter 47 Teleprompter Script: The Mudroom

*Two minutes. Read at an even pace. Pause at each line break.*

The mudroom is where the weather stops.

Wet boots. Muddy paws. Dripping umbrellas.

And the coat wall that grows a layer every October.

It is the first room in this book whose job is not to store things, but to catch them.

Which means it is judged by what gets past it. Not by what it holds.

A mudroom holding a great deal, neatly, has usually failed.

Now go and look at your floor.

Almost every item on it is there for one reason. The wall above it ran out of room.

A bag on the ground is a hook that was full.

So the floor is a symptom. The wall is the cause.

Hooks first. Floor last.

And do the hooks with everyone standing there.

Because a coat removed by somebody else is a coat that comes back.

Give each person a strip of wall at their own reach.

A child who can reach their own hook stops leaving their coat on the floor.

Then stop the grit at the door, in three layers.

Scrape outside. Soak inside. A lipped tray for the drips.

And maintain them, because a loaded mat is a supply of grit at your door.

One last thing. Do not buy anything with a lid for this room.

A lid hides the drift.
