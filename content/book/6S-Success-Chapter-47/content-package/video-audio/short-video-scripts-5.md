# Chapter 47 Short Video Scripts: The Mudroom

Five scripts, 30 to 60 seconds each.

## 1. The floor is a symptom
**Hook:** Everything on your mudroom floor is there for one reason.
**Body:** The wall above it ran out of room. A bag on the ground is a hook that was full. A boot in the walking line is a rack with no space left.
**Close:** So the floor is a symptom. The wall is the cause. Hooks first.

## 2. Clean the floor last
**Hook:** Stop cleaning your entry floor first.
**Body:** It is what everyone does, because the floor is what looks bad. But nothing about why those things landed there has changed, so you will be doing it again within a week.
**Close:** Fix the wall, and the floor stops filling up on its own.

## 3. Three layers at the door
**Hook:** Grit is what wears an entryway out.
**Body:** And it does the damage underfoot, because grit ground into a floor sands the finish off it. So stop it at the threshold in three layers: a bristle mat outside to scrape, an absorbent mat inside to soak, and a lipped tray for the drips.
**Close:** And maintain them. A loaded mat is just a supply of grit at your door.

## 4. Do not buy anything with a lid
**Hook:** This is the one room where a lid makes it worse.
**Body:** A mudroom is one door from the street, so it becomes the holding bay for the whole house. The reflex is to contain it. But a lid lets a pile keep growing while it stops being visible.
**Close:** Open bins. Bare hooks. Keep the drift embarrassing.

## 5. Hand it over
**Hook:** Do the coat wall with everyone standing there.
**Body:** Not out of politeness. A coat is the most personal thing in that room, and a coat removed by somebody else is a coat that comes straight back.
**Close:** Hand each one to its owner. The decision sticks.
