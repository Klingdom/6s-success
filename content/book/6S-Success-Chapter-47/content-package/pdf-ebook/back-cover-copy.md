# Chapter 47 Back Cover Copy: The Mudroom

*The mudroom is where the weather stops.*

It is the first room in this book whose job is not to store things but to catch them, which means it is judged by what gets past it rather than by what it holds.

Look at what is on the floor. Almost every item is there for one reason: the wall above it ran out of room. So the floor is a symptom, the wall is the cause, and the hooks come first.

Six zones. Three layers at the threshold. And the one room in the house where you should not buy anything with a lid.

**6S Success: Home Edition**, Chapter Forty-Seven.
