# Chapter 47 Reader Discussion Questions: The Mudroom

1. Walk to your entry and name three things on the floor. Which full hook or rack put each one there?
2. Can every person in your household reach their own hook without asking an adult?
3. How many layers currently stop grit at your door, and when were they last maintained?
4. Is your boot tray where boots actually come off, or where it happens to fit?
5. What lidded container is sitting in your entry right now, and when did you last open it?
6. Which coat by your door has not been worn since the last change of season?
7. If you have a pet, where does the food bin live and where does the bowl actually sit?
8. Could your household come through your entry wet, in the dark, all at once, and leave nothing behind?
