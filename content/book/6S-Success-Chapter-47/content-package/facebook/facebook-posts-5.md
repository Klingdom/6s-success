# Chapter 47 Facebook Posts: The Mudroom

Five standalone posts.

## 1
Almost everything on your mudroom floor is there for one reason: the wall above it ran out of room. A bag on the ground is a hook that was full. The floor is a symptom. The wall is the cause.

## 2
Do the hook wall with everyone standing there. A coat is the most personal thing in that room, and a coat removed by somebody else is a coat that comes straight back. Hand each one to its owner and the decision sticks.

## 3
Beat grit at the door in three layers: a stiff bristle mat outside to scrape, an absorbent mat inside to soak, and a lipped tray for anything still dripping. Those three do more than any amount of wiping further in.

## 4
A mat already full of grit has stopped being a mat. It has become a supply of grit sitting at your door. Shake it out and rinse the tray on the same day.

## 5
Do not buy storage with a lid for your entryway. A lid hides the drift, and this is the one room in the house where the accumulation needs to stay visible.
