# Chapter 47 Facebook Group Discussion Post: The Mudroom

**Question for the group: what is on your entry floor right now, and which full hook put it there?**

That is the whole idea of this chapter. Almost everything lying on a mudroom floor is there for one reason: the wall above it ran out of room. A bag on the ground is a hook that was full. A boot in the walking line is a rack with no space left.

So the floor is a symptom and the wall is the cause, which is why you fix the hooks first and clean the floor last.

Two more while we are here:

- **Can everyone in your house reach their own hook?** A child who can reach theirs stops leaving their coat on the floor. It does more than any amount of asking.
- **Is your boot tray where boots actually come off?** Not near the door: at the spot where people stop and take them off, which for most of us is a step or two further in than we think.

Tell us what is on your floor.
