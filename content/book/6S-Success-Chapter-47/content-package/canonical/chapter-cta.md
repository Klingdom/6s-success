# Chapter 47 CTA and Companion Resources: The Mudroom

## Primary call to action
Do one zone this week, and make it the hook wall, with the household standing there, because almost everything on that floor is only there because the wall above it ran out of room.

## Companion resources
- **The Mudroom Reset card** (printable: the six zones, the done-looks-like for each, and the hooks-first, floor-last order of work).
- **The Three Layers card** (the threshold system on one page: scrape outside, soak inside, drip on a lipped tray, and the same-day maintenance that makes it work).
- **The Named Column card** (one strip of wall per person at their own reach, with the limit to say out loud: one coat, one bag, one hat).
- **The Mudroom Kit list** (printable: threshold control first, then hooks and holding, then cleaning, grouped by job).

## Register and fences
- Warm, practical, and household-facing, because this is the one room chapter done with other people standing there.
- Product types only, never brand names. No invented statistics or prices.
- Pet treatments are named as a storage-height rule only; no veterinary or dosing advice of any kind.
- Never advise mixing cleaners, and never suggest decanting into an unlabelled container.
- Friction meter retired for Part 9; close on the before-and-after room signature.

## Words to avoid
- No brand names. No veterinary advice. No instruction that puts a person on a wet floor in socks, and no fixing advice for a hook rail beyond finding a stud or a proper anchor.
