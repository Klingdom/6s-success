# Chapter 47 Teaching Slide Deck Outline: The Mudroom

Fifteen slides, about 22 minutes.

1. Title: The Mudroom. Part Nine, room-by-room playbooks.
2. The rooms get small again, and the job changes.
3. The mudroom is where the weather stops.
4. The first room whose job is to catch rather than store.
5. Judged by what gets past it, not by what it holds.
6. Look at the floor: every item is a hook that was full.
7. The floor is a symptom. The wall is the cause.
8. So: hooks first, floor last.
9. Do the hooks with the household there, and why that matters.
10. A named column at each person's own reach.
11. The three-layer threshold: scrape, soak, drip.
12. Maintenance: a loaded mat is a supply of grit at your door.
13. Do not buy anything with a lid, and why this room is the exception.
14. The centerpiece: zone by zone, inputs and numbered steps (walk one zone live).
15. The signature: judged on the floor strip, and on coming through wet in February.
