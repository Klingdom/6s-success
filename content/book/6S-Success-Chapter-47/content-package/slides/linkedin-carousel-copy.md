# Chapter 47 LinkedIn Carousel Copy: The Mudroom

**Slide 1**
The mudroom is where
the weather stops.

**Slide 2**
The first room in this book whose job
is to catch things, not store them.

**Slide 3**
So it is judged by what gets past it.
Not by what it holds.

**Slide 4**
Look at your mudroom floor.
Almost all of it is a hook that was full.

**Slide 5**
The floor is a symptom.
The wall is the cause.

**Slide 6**
Hooks first. Floor last.

**Slide 7**
Beat grit at the threshold.
Three layers.

**Slide 8**
Scrape outside. Soak inside.
Drip on a lipped tray.

**Slide 9**
A loaded mat is not a mat.
It is a supply of grit at your door.

**Slide 10**
Do not buy anything with a lid.
A lid hides the drift.

**Slide 11**
Do the hook wall this week.
With everyone standing there.
