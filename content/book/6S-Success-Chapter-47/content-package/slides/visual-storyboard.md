# Chapter 47 Visual Storyboard: The Mudroom

Four figures, matching the chapter's SVGs. Warm paper, terracotta, honey, slate, green. Types, not brands.

## Figure 1: the opener, two states
A full-width pair. Left, the room as it runs: one rail carrying every coat in the house four deep, bags on the floor beneath it in the walking line, boots two steps past an unused tray, a lidded box in the corner, a broom stood on its bristles. Right, the same room: one named column per person at their own reach, bare floor along the whole run, the wet tray exactly where boots come off, open bins, and the broom clipped head-up.

## Figure 2: the six zones
A warm overhead plan, six numbered cells in two rows of three: family hooks, bench, shoes and boots, pet station, seasonal gear, cleaning corner. Footer line: the wall decides what happens to the floor, so hooks first and floor last.

## Figure 3: the method and the kit
Two panels. Left, the four rules of the mudroom layer with a gloss under each. Right, the kit as product types, leading with the entry mat set and the boot tray, because the threshold is this room's real work.

## Figure 4: the before-and-after signature
A matched pair from the same doorway with the camera icon between them. The after panel carries a small honey-outlined label reading "come through wet, leave nothing", and the caption beneath reads "the after, judged on the floor strip". Draw the strip of floor beneath the hook run clearly in both panels, since that is where the change actually shows.
