# Chapter 47 Diagram Ideas: The Mudroom

1. **The floor is a symptom** (hero): a hook rail drawn full, with dotted lines running from each overflowing hook down to a corresponding item on the floor below. Caption: every bag on the ground is a hook that was full.
2. **The three layers**: a cross-section of a threshold showing mat one outside, mat two inside, and the lipped tray, with grit and water visibly reducing at each stage.
3. **A loaded mat**: two mats side by side, one clean and one visibly full, with the second labelled "a supply of grit at your door".
4. **Named columns**: a wall elevation with vertical strips per person, hook heights stepping down to a child's reach, coat on the upper hook and bag on the lower.
5. **Lid against no lid**: two identical bins, one open with a visible pile, one lidded with the same pile hidden inside and a dotted outline showing how far it has grown. Caption: a lid hides the drift.
6. **Where the tray goes**: a floor plan with the door, a wrong tray position near the threshold, and the right one where boots actually come off, with the invisible film drawn as a faint band leading towards the kitchen.
7. **The floor strip**: a before and after of just the narrow band of floor beneath the hook run, which is where the change actually shows.
