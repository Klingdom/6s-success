# Chapter 47 Infographic Spec: The Mudroom

## Figure 2: the six zones
- Canvas 1000 x 440, warm paper fill.
- Title in Fraunces, 22px: "The mudroom, drawn as its six zones".
- Six panels, two rows of three, each 270 x 130, rounded 8, panel fill with a rule-coloured stroke.
- Each panel carries a green numbered disc and a serif zone name: 1 Family hooks, 2 Bench, 3 Shoes and boots, 4 Pet station, 5 Seasonal gear, 6 Cleaning corner.
- Footer in terracotta: the wall decides what happens to the floor, so hooks first and floor last.

## Figure 3: the method and the kit
- Canvas 1000 x 480, two panels of 460 x 410.
- Left panel headed "The method": four rules with a gloss under each. Lead lines: "This room catches, it does not store" (terracotta), "Grit stops in three layers" (green), "The floor is a symptom" (slate), "Lids hide the drift" (terracotta).
- Right panel headed "The kit": nine lines of product types, leading with the entry mat set and the boot tray, because the threshold is the room's real work.
- Footer in terracotta: stop the grit at the door, fix the wall before the floor, and buy nothing with a lid.

## Figure 4: the signature
- Canvas 1000 x 430, matched before and after panels with a slate camera disc between them.
- The after panel carries an honey-outlined label reading "come through wet, leave nothing", and the caption beneath reads "the after, judged on the floor strip".
- Draw the narrow band of floor beneath the hook run clearly in both panels; it is where the change actually shows.
