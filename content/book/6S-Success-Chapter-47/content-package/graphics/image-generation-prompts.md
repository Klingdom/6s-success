# Chapter 47 Image Generation Prompts: The Mudroom

House style: warm paper background, editorial illustration, serif display type, muted terracotta, honey, slate, and green accents. No logos, no brand names, no readable label text, no QR codes. No em dashes in any rendered text.

1. **Opener, two states.** A domestic mudroom drawn twice side by side. Left: one rail carrying coats four deep, bags on the floor beneath, boots scattered past a tray, a lidded box in the corner, a broom leaning on its bristles. Right: the same room with individual hook columns at different heights, bare floor along the run, a lipped tray with boots on it, open bins, and a broom clipped head-up on the wall.
2. **The six zones.** A warm overhead floor plan of a small mudroom with six numbered areas marked and labelled in a serif face.
3. **The three-layer threshold.** A clean cross-section illustration of a doorway showing a bristle mat outside, an absorbent mat inside, and a lipped tray, with the amount of grit and water visibly reducing at each stage.
4. **Named columns.** A wall elevation with four vertical hook columns at four different heights, one clearly at a child's reach, each with a coat on the upper hook and a bag on the lower.
5. **The hook wall, done together.** A family standing at a coat wall, an adult handing a coat to a child rather than lifting it away. Warm and ordinary, not staged.
6. **Where the tray goes.** An overhead floor plan of a doorway with a tray in the wrong place near the threshold crossed out, and the right position marked where boots come off.
7. **The signature pair.** A matched before-and-after of the whole mudroom from the doorway, with the strip of floor beneath the hooks clearly visible in both.
