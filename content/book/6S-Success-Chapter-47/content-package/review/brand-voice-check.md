# Chapter 47 Brand Voice Check: The Mudroom

## The register
Warm, practical, and noticeably more household-facing than the surrounding chapters, because this is the one room in Part Nine worked with other people standing there. Second person, but the second person is often plural here.

## Checks run

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Em dashes, en dashes | 0 in manuscript and final.html |
| Spaced-hyphen separators | 0 across the whole package |
| Brand names | none; product types only, brand scan clean |
| Invented statistics, prices, percentages | none |

## Tone risks specific to this chapter, and how they were handled

**Telling people how to run their family.** This is the only chapter that asks the reader to assemble the household and set a rule in front of them, which risks sounding like instructions for managing other people. Three things keep it right. The reason given is mechanical rather than moral (a coat removed by somebody else comes back). The rule is aimed at making things easier for the least powerful person in the room, which is the child who cannot reach their own hook. And the limit is something everyone hears at the same time rather than something imposed afterwards.

**Blame for the floor.** A chapter about things dumped on the ground could easily read as a complaint about other people's habits. It does not, because the diagnosis is structural from the first paragraph: the item is on the floor because the hook was full. Nobody is lazy in this chapter. The wall ran out.

**The child-height point.** "A child who can reach their own hook stops leaving their coat on the floor. Not because you asked. Because they can." This is the chapter's warmest line and it does real work, reframing a discipline problem as a design problem. Checked against the Chapter 39 kids-bedroom material for consistency; it is the same principle applied at a door.

**The pet section.** Handled entirely as storage and hygiene. The one place it could have drifted, flea and worming treatment, is written as a height rule with the reason attached and no product or dosing detail at all.

## Fences observed
- Product types only, never brands.
- No veterinary advice of any kind.
- No mixing, no decanting, no unlabelled containers.
- Nobody depicted on a wet hard floor in socks in any asset or b-roll note.
- Coats are handed to their owners in every depiction of the sort, never lifted away.
- Friction meter retired; closes on the before-and-after signature.
- Uses without re-teaching: Chapter 14 (visual controls), Chapter 21 (the hazard hunt), Chapter 24 (the seasonal changeover and the name), Chapter 25 (the ideal-state photo), Chapter 28 (solving together), Chapter 31 (the nine rules, and the entryway kept distinct).

## Verdict
On voice, and the warmest chapter in Part Nine without losing its edge. The household material stays on the right side of instructive because every rule in it is justified by mechanism and aimed at whoever has the least reach.
