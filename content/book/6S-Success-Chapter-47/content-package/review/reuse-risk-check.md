# Chapter 47 Reuse Risk Check: The Mudroom

## Source provenance
Every zone, its purpose, its passes, its hard call, its watch-for items, and its shine detail are folded from the Micro Zone Manual's Mudroom entry, which is the project's own material. The six calls are the Manual's `the_call` entries. The room's intro and its three tips supplied the layer: the room that catches, the three-layer threshold, the floor as symptom, and lids hiding the drift. Nothing here is drawn from an outside author, method, or publication.

## Third-party IP
- No quotations from any external book, article, or speaker.
- No named methodology other than 6S itself and the Toyota lineage the book already establishes.
- No brand names, product names, model numbers, or retailer names anywhere in the package.
- No logos, no QR codes, no readable label text called for in any image prompt.
- The image prompts call for a family group in one figure; the b-roll notes specify ordinary rather than staged, and no identifying detail.

## Claims that need to stay defensible
1. Grit ground underfoot abrades a floor finish. Stated qualitatively, as the reason for the threshold layers.
2. A wet sole leaves a thin, hard-to-see film on hard flooring that persists for some minutes. Stated as the reason for tray placement, with no coefficient, timing, or injury data.
3. A hook rail loaded with wet coats can pull a fixing out of plasterboard. Stated as the reason for finding a stud or a proper anchor.
4. Damp wool packed away in spring develops a smell by autumn. Stated as the reason for drying before storage.
5. An unsealed pet food container admits pests. Stated as the reason for checking a lid seal.
6. Bleach and an ammonia based cleaner should never share a closed container. Stated as a storage rule, consistent with Chapters 43, 45, and 46.

No safety statistic was invented, and no claim is quantified.

## Liability posture, and the specific limits in this chapter
- **No veterinary content of any kind.** Flea, tick, and worming treatments appear once, as a height-and-containment rule, with no product named, no dose, no application method, and no advice about frequency. This is the tightest fence in the chapter and it is held in every asset, including the b-roll notes, which forbid filming any treatment close enough to read.
- **No fixing instruction beyond finding a stud or a proper anchor.** The chapter says a loaded rail will tear out of plasterboard and that fixings must land properly; it does not describe how to install one.
- No electrical work. The vacuum flex and floor socket are inspected and flagged, never opened.
- No mixing, no decanting, and no unlabelled containers.
- **Nobody is depicted on a wet hard floor in socks**, even as a hazard demonstration, because the demonstration is the hazard.

## A note on the honest negative
This is the first chapter to answer one of the five safety questions by saying the room has no meaningful hazard in that category. That is a deliberate editorial choice rather than an omission, it is stated in the text with its reasoning, and it is recorded here so that a later reviewer does not read it as a gap in the safety pass. The precedent matters for Chapters 48, 49, and 50, which are small rooms likely to have thin answers on more than one question.

## Cross-chapter reuse
- Uses and credits by reference: Chapters 14, 21, 24, 25, 28, and 31. None re-taught.
- **Kept deliberately distinct from Chapter 31, The Entryway**, which is the nearest neighbour and the other grit-at-a-threshold room. The entry mat set added for this chapter was mapped back to the Entryway, where Chapter 31 had named mats the library did not carry, so the two rooms now share a catalogued input without sharing prose.
- Does not borrow workshop (Ch 46) or hall closet (Ch 48) material.
- The before-and-after signature is the standard Part 9 close with no variation, documented in `chapter_47_signature.md`.

## Verdict
Low risk. Own source material throughout, no external IP, no invented statistics, and the one genuine trap in a room chapter that mentions animals, straying into veterinary advice, is closed completely.
