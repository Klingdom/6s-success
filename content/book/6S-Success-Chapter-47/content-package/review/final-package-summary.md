## Two things Phil should note

**1. This chapter sets a precedent on the five safety questions.** It is the first to answer one of them with an honest negative: the mudroom carries no meaningful fire load, and rather than invent a hazard to fill the slot, the chapter says so and explains why that matters (pretending otherwise makes the other four easier to ignore). **Chapters 48, 49, and 50 are all small rooms likely to have thin answers on more than one question, so this precedent will get used.** It is recorded in the reuse-risk file so a later reviewer does not read it as a gap.

**2. One zone sits awkwardly with the chapter's own frame, and the chapter does not say so.** The mudroom is argued as the room that catches rather than stores. The pet station is genuinely about storage, and its hard call concludes that the biggest thing in it, the food bin, probably belongs in the kitchen. That is the right answer and it slightly undercuts the frame. One sentence acknowledging the tension would make the chapter stronger. Flagged in the editorial review rather than fixed, because it is an editorial judgment rather than an error.
