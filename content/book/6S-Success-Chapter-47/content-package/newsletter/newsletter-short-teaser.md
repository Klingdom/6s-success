# Chapter 47 Newsletter Short Teaser: The Mudroom

The mudroom is where the weather stops, and it is the first room in this book whose job is not to store things but to catch them. That makes it the only room judged by what gets past it rather than by what it holds.

Chapter 47 breaks it into six zones with the exact inputs and numbered steps for each, and adds four rules to the nine rules of Shine: this room catches rather than stores, grit is beaten at the threshold in three layers, the floor is a symptom and the wall is the cause, and lids hide the drift.
