# Chapter 47 Landing Page Intro: The Mudroom

The rooms get small again now, and the job changes.

The mudroom is where the weather stops. Everything the house wants to keep outside arrives here first: wet boots, muddy paws, dripping umbrellas, the dog towel that never quite dries, and the coat wall that grows a layer every October. Its job is to intercept, which means it is judged by what gets past it rather than by what it contains. A mudroom holding a great deal, neatly, has usually failed.

That single shift explains why this chapter fixes the wall before the floor. Look at what is lying on a mudroom floor and almost all of it is there because the hooks above ran out of room. A bag on the ground is a hook that was full. Clean the floor first and you will be cleaning it again within a week.

It also explains why this is the one room in the book where you should not buy anything with a lid on it. A lid hides the drift, and this is the room where the accumulation needs to stay visible.

Six zones, exact inputs, numbered steps. Product types only, no brands. Start at the hooks, with the household standing there.
