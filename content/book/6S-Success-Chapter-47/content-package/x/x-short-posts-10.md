# X Short Posts: The Mudroom

Chapter 47 of *6S Success: Home Edition*. Ten standalone posts, each at or under 280 characters counting its number line. Plain numbering.

---

1/
The mudroom is where the weather stops. It is the first room in this book whose job is not to store things but to catch them, so it is judged by what gets past it rather than by what it holds.

(195 chars)

---

2/
Almost everything on your mudroom floor is there for one reason: the wall above it ran out of room. A bag on the ground is a hook that was full.

(147 chars)

---

3/
The floor is a symptom. The wall is the cause. Clean the floor first and you will be cleaning it again within a week.

(120 chars)

---

4/
Do the coat wall with the household standing there. A coat removed by somebody else is a coat that comes straight back. Hand each one to its owner and the decision sticks.

(174 chars)

---

5/
Give every person a strip of wall at their own reach. A child who can reach their own hook stops leaving their coat on the floor. Not because you asked. Because they can.

(173 chars)

---

6/
Grit does its damage underfoot, because grit ground into a floor sands the finish off it. Stop it at the door in three layers: scrape outside, soak inside, and a lipped tray for the drips.

(191 chars)

---

7/
A mat already full of grit has stopped being a mat. It has become a supply of grit sitting at your door. Shake it out and rinse the tray on the same day.

(156 chars)

---

8/
Do not buy storage with a lid for your entryway. A lid hides the drift, and this is the one room where the accumulation needs to stay visible.

(145 chars)

---

9/
A wet sole past the tray does not leave a puddle. It leaves an invisible film where everyone turns towards the kitchen, still there when the next person comes through in socks.

(179 chars)
