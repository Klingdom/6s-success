**This is the worst batch of the seven room chapters, and the reason is unusual: the content is
frequently excellent and the execution is almost universally disqualifying. Nineteen of twenty plates
carry a fake QR code, a rendered trademark, a baked em dash, or a spelling error. Several of the held
plates state this chapter's frozen reframes better than anything wired anywhere in Part Nine.**

Sixteen of the twenty carry a fake QR code. That single defect accounts for most of this chapter.

## HELD — the four that hurt most. Each states a frozen reframe and is killed by one thing.

### `ch36-image09` "Child's Eye Inspection" — **the highest-value regeneration in Part Nine**
This is the frozen floor-a-child-shares reframe rendered almost exactly. It says "get down on your
hands and knees", and it names the precise four objects the frozen string names: **the marble, the
snapped-off wheel, the stray die, the seed bead**, plus button batteries. Nothing else in the book
carries this idea visually.
**Held for a fake QR code and a baked em dash** ("from your child's point of view—then remove the
risks"). Two fixes, no rewriting.

### `ch36-image08` "Washing Toys the Right Way"
The frozen clean-and-dry rule, exactly: "DRY COMPLETELY. Moisture trapped inside toys can cause mold
and odors." Hard toys and soft toys, wash, rinse, dry each, and inspect after drying.
**Held for a fake QR code, and nothing else.** One fix.

### `ch36-image05` "Console Vent Cleaning"
The chapter's Quick Win in detail: brush the vents, vacuum outward, dry only, never liquids or
compressed air. **Held for a fake QR code, a baked em dash ("the direction of the airflow—outward"),
and a visible PlayStation logo on the console.**

### `ch36-image02` "The Six Family Room Zones"
All six zones present, correctly ordered, and it independently states two frozen safety rules right:
"Keep vents clear" on the media zone, and **"Devices charge safely on hard surfaces, not on couches or
rugs"** on the charging zone, which is the frozen heat rule verbatim in spirit.
**Held for a single spelling error: the traffic-flow legend reads "TRARFIC FLOW".** A misspelling
baked into a printed figure is not recoverable in layout. One character.

## HELD — invented systems and off-scope (10)

`image11` through `image20` are generic organizing and habit content rather than zone instruction, and
every one of them invents a numbered system. All ten carry a fake QR code.

- **A rival sort:** `image13` prints a **three-door "Keep, Donate, Store method"** against the frozen
  five doors (Donate, Sell, Store, Recycle, Throw).
- **A rival decision test:** `image11`'s **"TOY CHECK"** (do we love it, do we use it, does it fit,
  does it teach) competes with the frozen One Question and Use Test. "Do we love it" is KonMari
  register, which the book avoids.
- **Rival cadences and resets:** `image13` prints four cadences against the frozen three clocks;
  `image13`, `image16` and `image19` print five-minute, ten-minute and thirty-minute resets against the
  frozen fifteen.
- **Coined rules:** "One in, one out" (`image10`, `image11`), "Sunday Reset" (`image18`).
- **Habit trackers and progress charts** (`image15`, `image17`), both now on the Bible's AVOID list.
- **Minimalism framing** the book explicitly rejects: "Less clutter. More play." (`image11`), "Less
  clutter. More focus. Better play." (`image10`).
- **`image20`** re-teaches the whole of Sustain with an invented five-step system inside a room chapter.

### `image19` needs its own line
Titled **"Make It a Family System, Not Just a Mom's Job"**, and its roles chart assigns **"Mom, Kitchen
Lead"** and **"Dad, Garage Lead"**. The book is careful never to assume who the primary householder is;
Chapter 17 calls that person "whoever notices first and minds most", precisely so the reader is not
sorted by gender. This plate reintroduces the assumption and then illustrates it. Hold on voice
grounds independent of its QR code and em dashes.
