# Chapter 36 Reader Discussion Questions: The Family Room

1. When did you last actually wash the toy bins, rather than just tip them out and refill them?
2. How do you clean the vents on your television and games consoles? Did you know dust there cooks them?
3. Does anything in your home ever charge on a bed, a cushion, or under a blanket?
4. Are your toy and game shelves, and your television, strapped to the wall?
5. What is the choke-sized thing your crawling child has found on the floor that you did not know was there?
6. Which toys go back into their bins damp in your house, and where does the mildew smell come from?
7. Is there a games console kept unplugged behind the new one because of a save file? What is your plan for it?
8. Which throw does your family actually use, and which handsome one has never been unfolded?
9. Is there a half-finished craft project holding the good end of a work surface right now?
10. Which zone will you do first this weekend, the toy zone or the media zone, and what inputs do you need?
