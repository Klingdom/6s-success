# Chapter 36 Ebook Description: The Family Room

The sixth room playbook of Part Nine. The family room is the everyday, hardest-working soft room, lived in by the whole household, kids and pets and all, so the standard is not a showroom: it is a room that is genuinely clean, genuinely dry, and genuinely safe to sit on the floor in.

This chapter gives the exact inputs and the numbered steps for each of the room's six zones, with three habits running through all of them: clean means clean and dry (wash and dry the toys, bins, and throws, because a wet toy goes in a mouth and a damp bin breeds mildew), lift the dry mess before the wet cloth (dried glue and glitter defeat a wet cloth), and finish every zone on your hands and knees, sweeping the floor a crawling child shares of the choke-sized things it finds first.

You will learn the one thing that saves your consoles (the vents, not the screen), how to set up the toys so a child can put them away alone, the two fire risks unique to this room (a covered vent and charging on soft material), and the three tall things to strap to the wall. The chapter closes on a before-and-after room signature and the two photos to take.
