# The Family Room

*The family room is the room the family actually lives in, every day, kids and pets and all, and cleaning it is not about a showroom, it is about a room that is genuinely clean, genuinely dry, and genuinely safe to sit on the floor in. This playbook points the whole loop at it one zone at a time, and gives you, for each of the six zones, the exact inputs to gather and the precise, step-by-step way to clean and shine it, so a child can play on the floor and an adult can relax at the end of a long day.*

## How to read this chapter

This is a PLAYBOOK, not a method chapter. It applies the six-S loop you already own to one room, decomposed into six micro-zones, and its centerpiece is the per-zone clean-and-shine instructions with the exact inputs. It recaps but does not re-teach the nine-rule Shine method from Chapter 31; the new material is the family-room layer (clean means clean and dry; lift the dry mess first; finish every zone on your hands and knees on the floor a child shares; heat with nowhere to go) and the numbered steps for each zone.

Products are named as TYPES, never brands. The exhaustive product list is a separate appendix.

The friction meter, which reached its goal at Chapter 30, is retired for Part 9; the chapter closes on a before-and-after room signature and the two photos to take.
