# Chapter 36 Back Cover Copy: The Family Room

## Back-cover text
The family room is where your kids play on the floor, so wiping is not cleaning. A wet toy goes in a mouth and a damp bin breeds mildew, so clean has to mean clean and dry.

The Family Room breaks the room into six zones and gives you, for each, the exact inputs to gather and the numbered steps to follow. Wash and dry the toys and bins. Clear the vents that cook your consoles. Scrape the dried glue before you wipe. And finish every zone on your hands and knees, clearing the floor of the marble, the wheel, and the bead a crawler finds first.

Do one zone this weekend, and give the family a room that is genuinely clean and safe to live in.
