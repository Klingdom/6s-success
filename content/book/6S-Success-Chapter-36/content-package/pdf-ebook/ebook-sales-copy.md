# Chapter 36 Ebook Sales Copy: The Family Room

## Headline
The family room is where the kids play on the floor. Here is exactly how to clean it, so it is genuinely clean, dry, and safe.

## The promise
In the room your family lives in, wiping is not cleaning. A wet toy goes in a mouth and a damp bin breeds mildew, so clean has to mean clean and dry. This chapter gives you the exact inputs and the numbered steps for each of the room's six zones, plus the habits that make it work: lift the dry mess before the wet cloth, and finish every zone on your hands and knees on the floor a child shares.

## What you get in this chapter
- The family-room Shine method: clean means clean and dry; lift the dry mess first; sweep the floor a child shares; heat with nowhere to go.
- The six per-zone clean procedures, each with its inputs and numbered steps: the primary media zone, the toy and play zone, the board game and puzzle zone, the blanket and comfort zone, the charging and device zone, and the craft and activity zone.
- The one that saves your consoles: the vents and back panel, not the screen.
- The two fire risks, a covered vent and charging on soft material, and the three tall things to strap.
- The before-and-after room signature and the two photos to take.

## The common mistake it fixes
> Wiping the surfaces and calling the room done, so the console vents stay packed, the toys go back into damp bins, and the small parts stay on the floor a crawler shares.
