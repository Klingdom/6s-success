# Chapter 36 CTA and Companion Resources: The Family Room

## Primary call to action
Do one zone this weekend, the toy zone or the media zone, and give the family a room that is genuinely clean and genuinely safe to live in.

## Companion resources
- **The Family Room Reset card** (printable and phone wallpaper: the six zones, the done-looks-like for each, and the clean-and-dry, floor-a-child-shares order of work).
- **The Family Room Kit list** (printable: the cleaners, tools, and storage types the room needs, grouped by job, with the child-safety, wash-and-dry, and heat notes that matter here).

## Register and fences
- Warm, practical, expert, and clear-eyed about children and pets. Product types only, never brand names. No hype, no invented statistics or prices.
- Friction meter retired for Part 9; close on the before-and-after room signature.
- Previous: Chapter 35, The Living Room. Next: Chapter 37, The Primary Bedroom.

## Words to avoid
- No brand names (product types only). Never depict a screen being sprayed. Never suggest charging on soft material.
