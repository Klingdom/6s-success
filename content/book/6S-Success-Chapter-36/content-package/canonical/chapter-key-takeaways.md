# Chapter 36 Key Takeaways: The Family Room

- The heart of the chapter is the step-by-step way to clean each of the six family-room zones, with the exact inputs: the primary media zone, the toy and play zone, the board game and puzzle zone, the blanket and comfort zone, the charging and device zone, and the craft and activity zone.
- The family-room Shine layer, on top of the nine-rule method: clean means clean and dry; lift the dry mess first; finish every zone on your hands and knees, sweeping the floor a child shares.
- Clean and dry, both: wash the toys, bins, and throws and dry them fully, because a damp bin breeds mildew and a wet toy goes in a mouth.
- Lift the dry mess first: dried glue, glitter, and paint defeat a wet cloth, so scrape and brush them off before you wipe.
- The floor a child shares is the front line: the marble, the snapped-off wheel, the die, and the seed bead are all choke-sized, so sweep on your hands and knees.
- Save your consoles: the real cleaning at the media zone is the vents and the back panel, not the screen; the screen is dry-cloth-only.
- Two fire risks, both heat with nowhere to go: a covered console vent, and a device charging on soft material.
- Give the shelf to the child: open picture-labelled bins at child height let a child put the room away without an adult.
- Strap the three tall, top-heavy things a child pulls or climbs: the television, the toy shelf, and the game shelf.
