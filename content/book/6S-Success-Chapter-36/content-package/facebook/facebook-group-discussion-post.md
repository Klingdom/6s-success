# Facebook Group Discussion Post: The Family Room

For a home or parenting group. A useful tip, an open question, a soft close.

---

A family-room tip that matters more than it sounds.

In the room your kids play in, wiping is not cleaning. What lands on that floor goes in a small mouth, and anything you put away damp breeds mildew. So clean means two things at once here: genuinely clean, and genuinely dry. Wash the toys and the bins in soapy water, run the soft toys through the machine in a mesh bag, and dry everything fully before it goes back.

Two more that surprise people. First, your games console is probably dying of dust: the dust matted across its vents traps the heat and cooks it, so clean the vents, not just the screen. Second, never charge a tablet or a battery pack on a cushion or under a blanket, because it has no way to shed its heat.

And the most important step of all is on your hands and knees, sweeping the floor for the marble, the snapped-off wheel, and the bead a crawler would find first.
