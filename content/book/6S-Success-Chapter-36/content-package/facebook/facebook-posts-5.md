# Chapter 36 Facebook Posts: The Family Room

Five short standalone posts.

## 1. Clean and dry, both
In the room your kids play in, wiping is not cleaning. A wet toy goes in a mouth and a damp bin breeds mildew, so clean means clean and dry. Wash the toys and bins, run the soft toys through the machine in a mesh bag, and dry everything fully before it goes back.

## 2. Your console is dying of dust
Everyone cleans the screen. Nobody cleans the vents. But dust matted across a console vent traps the heat that cooks it. The real cleaning at the media zone is the vents and the back panel, not the screen. Brush every slot clear and they run cool again.

## 3. Lift the dry mess first
Dried glue, glitter, and paint defeat a wet cloth, which just smears them into a film. Scrape the glue with a plastic edge and brush the glitter out of the seams first, and only then wipe. The dry mess comes up clean; wetted, it becomes a job you fight.

## 4. Never charge on soft material
A tablet or a battery pack charging on a cushion or under a blanket cannot shed its heat, and that is a fire. Nothing in the family room charges on soft material, ever. One strip, on a hard surface, one named slot per device.

## 5. Finish on your hands and knees
The most important step of every zone is the sweep of the floor a crawling child shares. The marble, the wheel, the die, the bead are all choke-sized. You are not just tidying the floor, you are clearing it of the things a small child finds first. Chapter 36. Next: the primary bedroom.
