# Chapter 36 Image Generation Prompts: The Family Room

Warm, illustrated, flat-color editorial house style (paper #F7F2E9 ground, ink #2B2622 line, terracotta/honey/slate/green accents, Shine green #6E8B5B). No photorealism, no brand names or logos, no text baked into the art beyond the noted labels. Product types only.

## Prompt 1 (opener, two states)
A warm flat-illustration of one family room shown twice, side by side. Left, labelled "before": toys strewn across the floor, a dusty television over tangled console cables, a craft table buried under dried glue and scattered beads, a games shelf of leaning boxes, small parts on the floor. Right, labelled "after": open bins on a low shelf each with a picture label, the screen and vents clean with cables bundled and labelled, throws washed and folded in one basket, a cleared craft surface with one project tray, a clear play floor.

## Prompt 2 (six zones plan)
A warm overhead floor plan of a family room, gently shaded, with six numbered and labelled zones: primary media, toy and play, board game and puzzle, blanket and comfort, charging and device, craft and activity. Caption strip at the foot: "one room, six small jobs".

## Prompt 3 (method and kit, two panels)
A two-panel warm infographic. Left "the method": a toy and a bin through soapy water and drying, a plastic scraper lifting glue and a brush sweeping glitter, a hand gathering small choke-sized parts off the floor, a matted console vent with heat lines and a crossed-out tablet charging on a cushion. Right "the kit": labelled type icons for a vacuum with three attachments, a microfiber set with a flagged screen cloth, two cleaners, a detail brush, a plastic scraper, a mesh wash bag, mild dish soap, open picture-labelled bins, a controller dock, a project tray, an anti-tip anchor, and a surge protector.

## Prompt 4 (toy-zone step ribbon)
A warm step-ribbon titled "how to clean the toy zone": dust the shelf top, bin faces and labels, lift the bins and clean the boards, wash the bins and toys and dry fully, the play mat, the floor sweep. The wash-and-dry-fully step highlighted in the Shine green.

## Prompt 5 (before-and-after signature)
A warm matched pair of the family room's real life, the media wall, the toy shelf, and the play floor, shot from the doorway, before on the left and after on the right, with a small camera icon and the labels "the before, kept" and "the after, kept as the standard".
