# Chapter 36 Diagram Ideas: The Family Room

House palette (paper #F7F2E9, panel #FBF7EF, ink #2B2622, terracotta #BC4B2A, honey #DDA63A, slate #3C5A6B, green #6E8B5B for Shine). Warm, illustrated, not photoreal. Product types only, no brands or logos. Spark red reserved for the two true fire risks (a covered vent, charging on soft material).

## Diagram 1 (opener, figure 1): two states
The family room before and after, side by side. Before: toys strewn across the floor, a dusty television over tangled console cables, a craft table buried under dried glue and beads, a glue gun left plugged in, small parts everywhere. After: open picture-labelled toy bins a child can reach, the screen and vents clean with cables bundled and labelled, throws washed and folded in one basket, a cleared craft surface, a clear safe play floor.

## Diagram 2 (figure 2): the six zones plan
A warm overhead plan, six numbered zones marked and named: primary media, toy and play, board game and puzzle, blanket and comfort, charging and device, craft and activity. Footer: "one room, six small jobs".

## Diagram 3 (figure 3): the Shine method and kit
Two panels. Left, the method: clean and dry (a toy and a bin through soapy water and drying); lift the dry mess first (a plastic scraper on glue, a brush on glitter in a seam, beside a crossed-out wet cloth); the floor a child shares (a hand gathering a marble, a wheel, a die, a bead); heat with nowhere to go (a matted console vent, and a crossed-out tablet charging on a cushion). Right, the kit: a vacuum with three attachments, a microfiber set with a flagged screen cloth, two cleaners, a detail brush, a plastic scraper, a mesh wash bag, mild dish soap, open picture-labelled bins, a controller dock, a project tray, an anti-tip anchor, a surge protector. Footer: "clean and dry, dry mess first, sweep the floor a child shares".

## Diagram 4 (no HTML twin): the family-room Shine method
A standalone visual: clean and dry, lift the dry mess first, sweep the floor a child shares, heat with nowhere to go. Names the next chapter, the primary bedroom.

## Diagram 5 (no HTML twin): how to clean the toy zone
A step ribbon: dust the shelf top, wipe the bin faces and labels, lift the bins and clean the boards, wash the bins and toys and dry fully, the play mat both faces, sweep the shared floor. Highlight the wash-and-dry-fully step.

## Diagram 6 (before/after signature) and the photo brief
The Part 9 signature: a matched before-and-after pair of the media wall, the toy shelf, and the play floor from the doorway. **Best before-and-after photo brief:** stand in the doorway and frame the media wall, the toy shelf, and the open play floor together in one shot; same spot, angle, and light, before and after; do not tidy before the before (Ch 6); keep the after as the ideal-state standard (Ch 25). Secondary pairs: the toy shelf with its picture-labelled bins, and the craft surface cleared. The retired friction meter is not drawn.
