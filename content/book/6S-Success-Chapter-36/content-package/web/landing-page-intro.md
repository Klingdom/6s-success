# Chapter 36 Landing Page Intro: The Family Room

The family room is the room the family actually lives in, every day, with children on the floor, controllers on the sofa, snacks over the games, and the pet asleep on the throw. Cleaning it is a different job from the living room next door, because the standard is not a showroom. It is a room that is genuinely clean, genuinely dry, and genuinely safe to sit on the floor in.

So the method here adds a few things: you wash and dry things properly rather than just wiping them, because a wet toy goes in a mouth and a damp bin breeds mildew. You lift the dry mess, the glue, the glitter, the paint, before you ever reach for a wet cloth. And you finish every zone the same way, on your hands and knees, sweeping the floor a crawling child shares of the choke-sized things it finds first.

This chapter gives the exact inputs and numbered steps for each of the room's six zones, so a child can play safely on the floor and an adult can drop onto the sofa at the end of a long day.
