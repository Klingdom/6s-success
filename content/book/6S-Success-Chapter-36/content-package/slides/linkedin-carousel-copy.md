# Chapter 36 LinkedIn Carousel Copy: The Family Room

Thirteen slides, on-slide copy. Shine green accent. Footer: CLEAN, DRY, SAFE ON THE FLOOR. Product types only.

- Slide 1: The family room is not a showroom. It is where your kids play on the floor.
- Slide 2: So clean means two things at once: genuinely clean, and genuinely dry.
- Slide 3: A wet toy goes in a mouth. A damp bin breeds mildew. Wash and dry everything.
- Slide 4: Six zones: media, toys, board games, blankets, charging, craft. One at a time.
- Slide 5: Dried glue and glitter defeat a wet cloth. Scrape and brush the dry mess first.
- Slide 6: The most important step is on your hands and knees, clearing the choke-sized things.
- Slide 7: Your console is dying of dust. Clean the vents, not just the screen.
- Slide 8: Never charge on soft material. Heat with nowhere to go is a fire.
- Slide 9: Give the shelf to the child. Open picture-labelled bins a child can put away alone.
- Slide 10: Strap the three that tip: the TV, the toy shelf, the game shelf.
- Slide 11: A finished family room is one a child can play in on the floor and an adult can relax in.
- Slide 12: The before-and-after signature. Take the two photos.
- Slide 13 (CTA): Do one zone this weekend. Chapter 36. Next: the primary bedroom.
