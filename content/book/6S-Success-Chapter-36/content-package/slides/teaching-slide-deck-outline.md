# Chapter 36 Teaching Slide Deck Outline: The Family Room

Fifteen slides. House palette; Shine green #6E8B5B. Footer strip: CLEAN, DRY, SAFE ON THE FLOOR. Product types only.

1. Title: The Family Room. Part Nine, Room-by-Room Playbooks. The room the family lives in.
2. The problem: it is not a showroom. Kids on the floor, everything in small mouths.
3. What you will learn: the five outcomes.
4. The rule: clean means clean and dry; a wet toy goes in a mouth, a damp bin breeds mildew.
5. Six zones, not one room: with the overhead plan figure.
6. The definition: a room a child can play in on the floor and an adult can relax in.
7. The method (its own slide): clean and dry, lift the dry mess first, sweep the floor a child shares, heat with nowhere to go. Figure 3, method and kit.
8. Media zone: clean the vents, not just the screen. Inputs and key steps.
9. Toy zone: wash and dry the bins and toys; give the shelf to the child.
10. The rest (games, blankets, charging, craft): the key move for each.
11. Lift the dry mess: scrape the glue and brush the glitter first.
12. Safety: two fire risks (covered vent, charging on soft material); strap the three that tip.
13. The floor a child shares: finish on your hands and knees.
14. The signature: the before-and-after room card. The two photos to take. (Friction meter retired.)
15. Close and CTA: one idea to keep. Do one zone this weekend. Next: the primary bedroom.
