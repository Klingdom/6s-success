# Chapter 36 Visual Storyboard: The Family Room

## Design system
House palette: paper #F7F2E9, panel #FBF7EF, ink #2B2622, terracotta #BC4B2A, honey #DDA63A, slate #3C5A6B, green #6E8B5B (Shine). Fraunces display, Newsreader body, Inter labels. Footer strip: CLEAN, DRY, SAFE ON THE FLOOR.

## Recurring motif
The "clean and dry" cue: a hard toy and an open bin going through soapy water and drying fully. It ties the room's lesson together and reappears on the method slide.

## Two fences
- Never depict a screen being sprayed; never depict charging on soft material.
- Product types only, no brand names or real logos.

## Beats (thirteen)
1. Title over the two-state opener.
2. The room the family lives in; kids on the floor.
3. Clean means clean and dry.
4. The six zones overhead plan.
5. The definition: a room a child can play in and an adult can relax in.
6. The method and kit two-panel (figure 3).
7. Media zone: the vents, not the screen.
8. Toy zone: wash, dry, and give the shelf to the child.
9. Lift the dry mess: scrape the glue, brush the glitter.
10. Two fire risks; strap the three that tip.
11. The floor a child shares: hands and knees.
12. The before-and-after room signature.
13. Close: one idea to keep, and the Chapter 37 hand-off.

## Carrying it across the set
Every asset closes on the before-and-after room signature from the doorway (media wall, toy shelf, play floor). The friction meter is retired for Part 9. Register warm, practical, expert. Previous Ch 35, next Ch 37 The Primary Bedroom.
