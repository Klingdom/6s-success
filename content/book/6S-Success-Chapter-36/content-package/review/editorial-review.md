# Chapter 36 Editorial Review: The Family Room

## Verdict
Ready for Phil's review as a draft. The chapter meets the instruction-centric standard: every one of the six zones carries an Inputs list, numbered Steps, and an inspect-and-flag paragraph, and the family-room Shine layer is distinct from the five rooms before it.

## What works
- **The layer is genuinely its own.** Clean-means-clean-and-dry, lift-the-dry-mess-first, and the floor-a-child-shares sweep are specific to a room lived in by children and pets, and they do not repeat the living-room (dust-is-heat) or dining (finish-care) layers.
- **The centerpiece earns its length.** The media-zone vents, the wash-and-dry-fully toy procedure, and the craft-zone scrape-before-wipe are concrete, orderable, and correct.
- **Safety is integrated, not bolted on.** Two fire risks (covered vent, charging on soft material) and the three anti-tip shelves are surfaced in the Shine inspect-and-flag steps and resolved in the Safety pass, consistent with the Shine-finds-Safety-fixes rule.

## Watch items (minor, non-blocking)
- The chapter runs long (about 5,082 words), in line with the other room playbooks; the length cap is lifted for Part 9, so this is acceptable but worth a trim pass if Phil wants tighter.
- The board-game and craft zones overlap slightly in "small parts on the floor"; the text keeps them distinct by function (playable-tonight vs clears-for-dinner), which reads cleanly.
