# Chapter 36 Brand Voice Check: The Family Room

## Voice target
Warm, practical, expert, clear-eyed about children and pets, never preachy. The register of a knowledgeable friend who has actually cleaned this room, not a manufacturer.

## Scan results
- **Product types only, no brands**: PASS. A brand-name scan across the whole package (common household brands) returned zero hits. Everything is a type: microfiber cloth set, neutral pH multi-surface cleaner, mesh wash bag, anti-tip anchor, surge protector, open picture-labelled bins.
- **No hype, no invented numbers or prices**: PASS. No statistics, no fabricated study claims, no dollar figures.
- **Safety framed as care, not fear**: PASS. The fire-risk and choke-hazard language is direct but calm ("heat with nowhere to go", "the things a small child finds first"), consistent with the book's tone.
- **Dash and punctuation house style**: PASS. Middot for label separators; no em/en dashes; no prose spaced-hyphen separators.
- **Inclusive, non-gendered**: PASS. "A child", "an adult", "the family" throughout; no gendered assumptions about who cleans.

## Consistency with the series
- The zone-by-zone instruction voice matches Chapters 31 to 35.
- The one-idea-to-keep close and the before-and-after signature match the Part 9 pattern.
- The two fences held everywhere: never depict a screen being sprayed, never depict charging on soft material.

## Verdict
On voice. No changes needed.
