1/
Part Nine walks into the room the family actually lives in: the family room. Like the rooms before it, this chapter gives the exact inputs and steps to clean each of its six zones. But here the standard is not a showroom.

(224 chars)

---

2/
"The family room is the hardest-working soft room in the house, and here clean means two things at once: genuinely clean, and genuinely dry." A wet toy goes in a mouth; a damp bin breeds mildew.

(197 chars)

---

3/
So you wash and dry the toys, bins, and throws properly, you lift the dry mess before any wet cloth, and you finish every zone on your hands and knees, clearing the floor a child shares.

(189 chars)

---

4/
"The family room is six small zones, not one big job: the primary media zone, the toy and play zone, the board game and puzzle zone, the blanket and comfort zone, the charging and device zone, and the craft and activity zone."

(229 chars)

---

5/
The media zone: a console does not die of age, it dies of dust. The dust matted across its vents traps the heat and cooks it. So the real cleaning is the vents and the back panel, not the screen.

(198 chars)

---

6/
The screen itself gets a dry cloth only, in straight passes. Never a spray, never a paper towel. Brush and vacuum every vent slot on the TV, the consoles, the streaming box, and the soundbar.

(194 chars)

---

7/
The toy zone: wash every bin and hard toy in soapy water, run the soft toys through the machine in a mesh bag, and dry everything fully, because a damp bin keeps a damp block damp. Then lift each bin and clean the board beneath.

(231 chars)

---

8/
"Dried glue, glitter, and paint defeat a wet cloth, which just smears them into a film." So in the craft zone you scrape the glue with a plastic edge and brush the glitter from the seams first, then wipe.

(207 chars)

---

9/
The board games want to be complete and playable, so count them and check the corners of the box at the bottom of a stack. The blankets all go in the wash, even the clean-looking ones.

(187 chars)

---

10/
Two fire risks, both heat with nowhere to go: a blanket over a console vent, and a tablet charging on a cushion or under a blanket. Nothing in this room charges on soft material, ever.

(188 chars)

---

11/
Strap the three tall, top-heavy things a child pulls or climbs: the television, the toy shelf, and the game shelf. And keep the small parts and the blades up off the shared floor.

(183 chars)

---

12/
"In this room the last step of every zone is the most important, and it happens on your hands and knees: the sweep of the floor a crawling child shares." The marble, the wheel, the die, the bead are all choke-sized.

(219 chars)

---

13/
"A finished family room is one a child can play in on the floor and an adult can relax in at the end of a long day." Vents clear, toys in picture-labelled bins, throws washed, floor clear.

(192 chars)
