# X Short Posts: The Family Room

Chapter 36 of *6S Success: Home Edition*. Ten standalone posts, each at or under 280 characters counting its number line. Plain numbering.

---

1/
In the room your kids play in, wiping is not cleaning. A wet toy goes in a mouth and a damp bin breeds mildew. So clean means clean and dry: wash the toys and bins, and dry them fully.

(187 chars)

---

2/
Your games console is not dying of age. It is dying of dust. The dust matted across its vents traps the heat and cooks it. Clean the vents and the back panel, not just the screen.

(182 chars)

---

3/
Dried glue and glitter defeat a wet cloth, which just smears them into a film. Scrape the glue with a plastic edge and brush the glitter from the seams first, then wipe.

(172 chars)

---

4/
Never charge a tablet or a battery pack on a cushion or under a blanket. It cannot shed its heat, and that is a fire. Nothing in the family room charges on soft material, ever.

(179 chars)

---

5/
The most important cleaning step is on your hands and knees: the sweep of the floor a crawling child shares. The marble, the wheel, the die, the bead are all choke-sized.

(173 chars)

---

6/
Give the shelf to the child: five or six open bins with no lids on the lowest shelf, one category each, with a photo of the contents on the front. Then a child can put the room away alone.

(191 chars)

---

7/
Strap the three tall, top-heavy things a child pulls or climbs: the television, the toy shelf, and the game shelf. All three tip forward.

(140 chars)

---

8/
Wash the throws, even the ones that look clean. The blanket basket is where pet hair and popcorn oil concentrate. Then check the radiator and lamp shade a stray blanket drifted onto.

(185 chars)

---

9/
Six small zones, not one big job: media, toys, board games, blankets, charging, craft. One at a time. Start with the toy zone or the media zone for the biggest win.

(167 chars)

---

10/
A family room is not clean because the surfaces are wiped. It is clean because you washed and dried the toys, cleared the vents, and swept the floor a child shares. Chapter 36. Next . Chapter 37, The Primary Bedroom.
