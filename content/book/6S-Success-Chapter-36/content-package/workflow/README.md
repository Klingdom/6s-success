# Chapter 36 Content Package: The Family Room

The SIXTH room playbook of Part Nine (Chapters 31 to 50), built to the instruction-centric template. The chapter CORE is the detailed, step-by-step clean-and-shine instruction for each of the six family-room zones, with the exact inputs.

## The chapter in one line
The family room is the room the family lives in, so clean means clean and dry, you lift the dry mess first, and you finish every zone on your hands and knees, sweeping the floor a crawling child shares.

## The six zones
The primary media zone, the toy and play zone, the board game and puzzle zone, the blanket and comfort zone, the charging and device zone, and the craft and activity zone. Each carries an Inputs list, numbered Steps, and an inspect-and-flag list.

## The family-room Shine layer (nine rules from Chapter 31 plus)
- Clean means clean and dry: wash and dry the toys, bins, and throws.
- Lift the dry mess first: scrape glue, brush glitter, then wipe.
- Finish every zone on your hands and knees, on the floor a child shares.
- Heat with nowhere to go: clear the console vents; never charge on soft material.
