# Chapter 36 Repurposing Map: The Family Room

## Spine (the how-to-clean value, in priority order)
1. The family-room Shine method: clean and dry, lift the dry mess first, sweep the floor a child shares, heat with nowhere to go.
2. The zone-by-zone centerpiece: the exact inputs and numbered steps for the media, toys, games, blankets, charging, and craft.
3. The one that saves your consoles: the vents, not the screen.
4. The calls that matter: the save-file console, the unplayed gift, the incomplete game, the ugly favourite, the old phone, the half-finished project.

## Reuse cautions
- Types, not brands, everywhere. No brand names or invented prices.
- Never depict a screen being sprayed or a device charging on soft material.
- Every asset closes on the before-and-after signature (friction meter retired for Part 9).
- Stay in the family room: do not reuse living-room (Ch 35) or bedroom (Ch 37) material.
