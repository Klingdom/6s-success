# Chapter 36 YouTube Script (6 to 8 min): The Family Room

Alternating HOST and VISUAL. Product types only.

HOST: In the room your kids play in, wiping is not cleaning. Today we clean a whole family room the way it actually needs it, so it is genuinely clean, dry, and safe on the floor.
VISUAL: Two-state opener, toy-strewn before and clean after. A toy and a bin through soapy water.

HOST: The standard here is not a showroom. A wet toy goes in a mouth, a damp bin breeds mildew, so clean means clean and dry. And we lift the dry mess before any wet cloth, and finish on our hands and knees.
VISUAL: Washing bins and toys; a scraper on dried glue; a hand gathering small parts.

HOST: Six zones. Start with the media zone. A console does not die of age, it dies of dust. The real cleaning is the vents and the back panel, not the screen.
VISUAL: Brushing and vacuuming console vents; a dry cloth on the screen.

HOST: The toy zone. Wash every bin and toy, dry them fully, and lift each bin to clean the board beneath. Set them up in open picture-labelled bins so a child can put them away alone.
VISUAL: A mesh bag of soft toys into the machine; picture labels on bins.

HOST: The board games want to be complete and playable. The blankets all go in the wash, and you check the radiator and lamp shade a stray one drifts onto.
VISUAL: Counting a game; throws into the wash; a hand on a radiator top.

HOST: The charging station charges on a hard surface, never soft material, and you clear the lint from the ports. The craft table, you scrape the glue and brush the glitter first.
VISUAL: A brush clearing a charging port; a scraper on the craft surface.

HOST: Safety: strap the TV and the toy and game shelves. Then finish every zone on your hands and knees, clearing the floor of the marble, the wheel, the die, and the bead.
VISUAL: An anti-tip strap into a stud; the floor sweep.

HOST: A family room is not clean because the surfaces are wiped. It is clean where it counts, and safe on the floor. Do one zone this weekend. Next: the primary bedroom.
VISUAL: The before-and-after room signature from the doorway. End card.
