# Chapter 36 B-Roll and Visual Notes: The Family Room

Warm, natural light. Product types only, no brand labels on screen.

## Visual vocabulary
- The two-state opener: a cluttered, toy-strewn before and a genuinely clean, dry, safe after.
- The recurring "clean and dry" motif: a hard toy and an open bin going through soapy water and drying fully.

## Core shot list (seven shots)
1. The media zone: wiping the screen with a dry cloth, then the reveal, brushing and vacuuming the matted console vents and back panel.
2. The toy zone: washing bins and hard toys in soapy water, a mesh bag of soft toys going into the machine, then everything drying.
3. Lifting each bin to clean the shelf board beneath, where spills hide.
4. The craft table: scraping dried glue with a plastic edge and brushing glitter from the seams before any wet cloth.
5. The blanket zone: throws going into the wash, and a hand checking the radiator top and the lamp shade a stray blanket drifted onto.
6. The charging zone: brushing lint from a charging port, and a hand feeling a device for heat; a crossed-out shot of a tablet on a cushion.
7. The safety beat: an anti-tip strap into a stud behind the TV, the toy shelf, and the game shelf; then the hands-and-knees floor sweep gathering a marble, a wheel, a die, a bead.

## On-screen text bank (use sparingly)
- Clean means clean and dry.
- The real cleaning is the vents, not the screen.
- Lift the dry mess first.
- Never charge on soft material.
- Finish on your hands and knees.

## Lower thirds (rotate)
- Zone 1 to 6, one per segment.
- Give the shelf to the child.

## Editing notes
- Close each cut on the before-and-after room signature from the doorway (media wall, toy shelf, play floor).
- No presenter required; hands and surfaces only.
