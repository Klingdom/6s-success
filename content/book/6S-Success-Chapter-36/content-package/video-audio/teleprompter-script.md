# Chapter 36 Teleprompter Script: The Family Room

Short lines, one breath each. Warm and plain.

In the family room, wiping is not cleaning.
This is the room your kids play on the floor in.
So clean has to mean two things at once.
Genuinely clean. And genuinely dry.
A wet toy goes in a mouth.
A damp bin breeds mildew.
So wash the toys and bins in soapy water.
Run the soft toys through the machine in a mesh bag.
And dry everything fully before it goes back.
Next, the dry mess.
Dried glue and glitter defeat a wet cloth.
Scrape the glue. Brush the glitter out of the seams.
And only then wipe.
Now the consoles. They do not die of age. They die of dust.
So clean the vents, not just the screen.
Brush every slot on the TV and the consoles.
And never charge a tablet on a cushion or under a blanket.
Heat with nowhere to go is a fire.
Strap the three tall things: the TV, the toy shelf, the game shelf.
Then the most important step of all.
On your hands and knees, sweep the floor a child shares.
The marble. The wheel. The die. The bead.
All of them, off the floor.
Do one zone this weekend.
And give the family a room they can genuinely live in.
