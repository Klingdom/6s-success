# Chapter 36 Short Video Scripts (5): The Family Room

Each 30 to 90 seconds. Product types only.

## 1. A wet toy goes in a mouth (45s)
HOOK: In the room your kids play in, wiping is not cleaning.
SCRIPT: A wet toy goes straight in a mouth, and a damp bin breeds mildew that keeps a damp block damp. So clean means clean and dry. Wash the bins and hard toys in soapy water, run the soft toys through the machine in a mesh bag, and dry everything fully before it goes back.
ON-SCREEN: Clean means clean and dry.
CTA: Chapter 36. Do the toy zone this weekend.

## 2. Your console is dying of dust (40s)
HOOK: Your games console is not dying of age. It is dying of dust.
SCRIPT: The dust matted across its vents traps the heat and cooks it. So the real cleaning is the vents and the back panel, not the screen. Brush and vacuum every slot on the TV, the consoles, the streaming box, and the soundbar. The screen gets a dry cloth only.
CTA: Chapter 36. Clean the vents this weekend.

## 3. Lift the dry mess first (30s)
HOOK: Stop wiping dried glue and glitter.
SCRIPT: A wet cloth just smears them into a film. Scrape the dried glue with a plastic edge and brush the glitter out of the seams first, and only then wipe. The dry mess comes up clean; wetted, it becomes a job you fight.
CTA: Chapter 36, the craft zone.

## 4. Never charge on soft material (30s)
HOOK: Never do this with a tablet.
SCRIPT: A tablet or a battery pack charging on a cushion or under a blanket cannot shed its heat, and that is a fire. Nothing in the family room charges on soft material, ever. One strip, on a hard surface, one named slot per device.
CTA: Chapter 36. Move your chargers to a hard surface.

## 5. Finish on your hands and knees (40s)
HOOK: The most important cleaning step happens on your hands and knees.
SCRIPT: It is the sweep of the floor a crawling child shares. The marble, the snapped-off wheel, the stray die, and the seed bead are all choke-sized, and a cracked plastic edge is sharp enough to open a knee. You are not just tidying the floor, you are clearing it of what a small child finds first.
CTA: Chapter 36. Finish every zone on the floor.
