Hi friend,

We have done the rooms the whole house shares. This week, the room the family actually lives in, every day, kids and pets and all. Cleaning it is different, because the standard is not a showroom. It is a room that is genuinely clean, genuinely dry, and genuinely safe to sit on the floor in.

> The family room is the hardest-working soft room in the house, and here clean means two things at once: genuinely clean, and genuinely dry. What lands on this floor goes in a small mouth or under a bare knee, and anything put away damp, a toy, a bin, a throw, quietly breeds mildew. So you wash and dry things properly, you lift the dry mess before you reach for a wet cloth, and you finish every zone on your hands and knees, checking the floor a child shares.

The room is six zones, each with its exact inputs and numbered steps: the primary media zone, the toy and play zone, the board game and puzzle zone, the blanket and comfort zone, the charging and device zone, and the craft and activity zone.

Two you may not expect:

> Two quiet fire risks live in this room, and both are heat with nowhere to go. Dust matted across a console vent traps the heat that cooks it, exactly as it does a television, so the vents are the real cleaning. And a tablet or a battery pack charging on a cushion or under a blanket cannot shed its heat, so nothing in this room charges on soft material, ever.

## The toy zone, done properly (the worked example)

**Inputs:** a compact vacuum with the upholstery and crevice tools, a colour-coded microfiber set, a neutral pH multi-surface cleaner, mild dish soap, a mesh wash bag, your caddy.

**Steps:**

1. Dust the top of the shelf, dry, left to right.
2. Wipe the bin front faces and photo labels, damp then dry, so a child still reads the picture.
3. Lift each bin and clean the board beneath, where spills hide.
4. Empty and wash each bin in soapy water, rinse, and dry it fully.
5. Wash the hard toys in soapy water; run the soft toys through the machine in a mesh bag; dry everything.
6. Vacuum the play mat both faces, lift it, and vacuum the floor beneath.
7. Finish on your hands and knees, sweeping the floor for anything choke-sized.
