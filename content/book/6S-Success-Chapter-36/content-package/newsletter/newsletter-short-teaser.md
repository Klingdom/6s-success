**The Family Room: clean, dry, and safe on the floor.**

The family room is the room your kids actually play in, so wiping is not cleaning. A wet toy goes in a mouth and a damp bin breeds mildew, so clean means clean and dry: wash the toys and bins and dry them fully.

Chapter 36 gives the exact inputs and numbered steps for each of the room's six zones, plus the two habits that make it work: lift the dry mess before the wet cloth, and finish every zone on your hands and knees, sweeping the floor a crawling child shares.

[Read the chapter] and give the family a room that is genuinely clean and safe to live in.
