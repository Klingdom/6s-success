# Chapter 36 LinkedIn Carousel Outline: The Family Room

Twelve slides. Product types only.

- Slide 1 (hook): The family room is not a showroom. It is the room your kids play on the floor in.
- Slide 2: So the standard is different: clean, dry, and safe to sit on the floor in.
- Slide 3 (the rule): Clean means clean and dry. A wet toy goes in a mouth; a damp bin breeds mildew.
- Slide 4 (six zones): media, toys, board games, blankets, charging, craft. One at a time.
- Slide 5 (dry mess): Dried glue and glitter defeat a wet cloth. Scrape and brush the dry mess first.
- Slide 6 (the floor): The most important step is on your hands and knees, clearing the choke-sized things.
- Slide 7 (consoles): Your consoles are dying of dust. Clean the vents, not just the screen.
- Slide 8 (charging): Never charge on soft material. Heat with nowhere to go is a fire.
- Slide 9 (give the shelf to the child): Open picture-labelled bins let a child put the room away alone.
- Slide 10 (safety): Strap the three that tip: the TV, the toy shelf, the game shelf.
- Slide 11 (standard): A finished family room is one a child can play in on the floor and an adult can relax in.
- Slide 12 (CTA): Do one zone this weekend. Chapter 36. Next: the primary bedroom.
