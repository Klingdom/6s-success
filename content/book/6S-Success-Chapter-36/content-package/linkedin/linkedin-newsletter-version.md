# Clean, Dry, and Safe on the Floor

*A 6S Success: Home Edition newsletter, from Chapter 36, The Family Room.*

Hi friend,

The family room is the room the family actually lives in, every day, kids and pets and all. So cleaning it is not about a showroom. It is about a room that is genuinely clean, genuinely dry, and genuinely safe to sit on the floor in.

**The family room is the hardest-working soft room in the house, and here clean means two things at once: genuinely clean, and genuinely dry. What lands on this floor goes in a small mouth or under a bare knee, and anything put away damp, a toy, a bin, a throw, quietly breeds mildew.**

The room breaks into six zones, and each gets its exact inputs and numbered steps in the chapter: the media zone, the toys, the board games, the blankets, the charging station, and the craft table.

## To try this week

Do the toy zone. Empty and wash every bin in mild soapy water and dry it fully, wash the hard toys and run the soft ones through the machine in a mesh bag, wipe the shelf boards under the bins, and finish on your hands and knees, sweeping the floor a crawling child shares of anything choke-sized.
