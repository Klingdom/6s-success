# Chapter 36 LinkedIn Comment Prompts

1. When did you last actually wash the toy bins, not just tip them out and refill them?
2. How do you clean the vents on your TV and games consoles? Did you know dust there shortens their life?
3. Is anything in your home ever charging on a bed, a cushion, or under a blanket? It is a real fire risk.
4. Are your toy and game shelves strapped to the wall? If you have a climber, this matters.
5. What is the choke-sized thing your crawler has found on the floor that you did not know was there?
