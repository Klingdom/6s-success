## Batch-wide observations

1. **Branded packaging on 9 plates**, and unlike Ch 32's single Arm & Hammer box these are central and legible. A pantry chapter cannot be illustrated with real groceries.
2. **Fake QR codes on 13 plates**, up from 3 in Ch 32.
3. **An invented 14-zone taxonomy** replacing a frozen 5. This is the largest frozen-set failure yet.
4. **"THE RULES" panels on 11 plates**, all coining a system the book does not have.
5. **Contractions on every plate**, as in both previous batches.
6. Two recognisable **book covers** appear in `image10`, which the Bible forbids explicitly.
7. Notably, **the three plates that passed are the three that photograph a *process* rather than a *shelf full of product*.** That is the lesson for regeneration: shoot hands, cloths, tools and empty boards, not stocked shelves.
