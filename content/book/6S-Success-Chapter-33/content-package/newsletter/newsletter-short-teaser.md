# Newsletter Short Teaser: The Pantry

For a roundup or digest. 90 to 140 words.

---

If the kitchen is the room that matters most, the pantry is the room that hands your grocery money back: the storeroom that quietly decides how much food you throw in the bin. This edition, like the two before it, is about the clean. It carries the nine-rule Shine method from the door and adds the pantry's one overriding rule, dry before wet, always, because flour turns to paste the instant a wet cloth touches it: vacuum or brush first, wipe second, dry last, hunt the sticky ring, and read the clean as an inspection for pests and damp. Five small zones, not one big job, each with the exact inputs to gather and the precise, step-by-step way to clean it. A pantry is not clean because the shelves are wiped.
