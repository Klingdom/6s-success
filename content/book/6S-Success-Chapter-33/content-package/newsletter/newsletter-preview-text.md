# Newsletter Preview Text (Preheader): The Pantry

Ten options, each 35 to 90 characters, each paired with a subject line. Char counts noted.

1. Pairs with #1 (If the kitchen was the room that matters most, the pantry is the room that hands your grocery money back. This edition is about the clean.)
   "The room that hands your money back, cleaned dry-first, one shelf at a time." (75 chars)

2. Pairs with #2 (In a pantry, dust and damp are the whole job, and flour turns to paste. Dry before wet, without exception.)
   "Flour turns to paste. Dry before wet: vacuum first, wipe second, dry last." (73 chars)

3. Pairs with #3 (The pantry is five small zones, not one big job. Do them one at a time.)
   "Five small zones, not one big job. Do them one at a time." (56 chars)

4. Pairs with #4 (The pantry is the one room where cleaning is genuinely an inspection for safety.)
   "In the pantry, the wipe is an inspection: pests, damp, a bulging lid." (68 chars)

5. Pairs with #5 (The sticky ring under a honey jar is not just untidy, it is an invitation. Clean until it squeaks.)
   "The sticky ring is an invitation. Clean until the shelf squeaks." (63 chars)

6. Pairs with #6 (A pantry is not clean because the shelves are wiped. It is clean dry-first, dated, oldest to the front.)
   "Not clean because the shelves are wiped. Clean because you dried, dated, faced." (78 chars)

7. Pairs with #7 (A pantry does not go off all at once, it goes off one forgotten item at a time, at the back, in the dark.)
   "It goes off one forgotten item at a time. Let the shelf keep the rule." (69 chars)

8. Pairs with #8 (You know the nine rules of Shine from the door. The pantry adds one: dry before wet, always.)
   "The nine rules carry from the door. The pantry adds one: dry before wet." (72 chars)

9. Pairs with #9 (Do the dry goods shelf dry-first this weekend, and the shelf starts reading at a glance.)
   "Do one shelf dry-first: it stops smelling and starts reading at a glance." (72 chars)

10. Pairs with #10 (It is the room that quietly hands back a chunk of your grocery budget, and this is exactly how.)
    "The room that hands your grocery money back. Here is exactly how." (64 chars)
