It is the THIRD room playbook of Part Nine (Room-by-Room Playbooks), carrying the instruction-centric template proven in Chapters 31 and 32. The pantry is the kitchen's storeroom, and its defining challenge is not grease but dust, dates, damp, and the things that get in, so its governing principle is dry before wet. The CORE of the chapter is the "Zone by Zone: Exactly How to Clean It" section: for each of the five zones it gives the exact INPUTS to gather, the NUMBERED step-by-step clean-and-shine procedure, and an inspect-and-flag list, all governed by the universal nine-rule Shine method carried from Chapter 31 PLUS the pantry layer: dust and damp are the whole job and flour turns to paste the instant a wet cloth touches it, so dry before wet always, vacuum or brush first, wipe second, dry last, hunt the sticky ring, and read the clean as a genuine inspection for pests and damp. Shine finds the hazards, the safety pass fixes them. The friction meter, retired for Part 9, is replaced by a BEFORE-AND-AFTER room signature: a matched pair of the pantry shelves shot head-on from the doorway, plus the two photos to take (the before to keep, the after as the standard). The pantry is worked as five small zones, not one big job.
