2/
The one rule that overrides the rest: "in a pantry you clean dry before wet, always." Never wet a floury shelf, because flour turns to paste the instant a wet cloth touches it. Vacuum the dust off dry, then wipe, then take the board bone-dry. Read it free.

(259 chars)

3/
"The pantry is five small zones, not one big job: the dry goods shelves, the canned and jarred goods, the baking zone, the snack and lunch zone, and the backstock and bulk." Do them one at a time, each with a clear finish. Read it free.

(239 chars)

4/
"Do the dry goods shelf dry-first." Clear one board, vacuum the flour and grain dust off it while it is dry, then wipe it and take it bone-dry. Reseal or bin every open bag as it comes off, dating the keepers. One board, and the shelf reads at a glance.

(256 chars)

5/
"A finished pantry is one you can shop from at a glance": every open bag in a dated container you can read from the doorway, cans in rows with the label out and the oldest date at the front, one lift-out baking bin, a child-height snack bin. Read the free chapter.

(267 chars)

6/
"The sticky ring under a honey jar is not just untidy, it is an invitation. A tacky edge left on a shelf is exactly how ants find the row, so in the pantry you clean until the shelf squeaks, because clean here is the difference between a store cupboard and a feeding station."

(279 chars)

---

7/
"The pantry is the one room where cleaning is genuinely an inspection for safety." The chewed corner, the fine webbing in the flour, the bulging can lid, the soft dark patch of damp cardboard: you find them because your hands are on every surface. Read it free.

(264 chars)

---

8/
Wash the scoop, do not bury it damp. Dry before wet, every time, in this room: vacuum or brush first, wipe second, dry last. And "wash the scoop that lives in the flour rather than burying it back damp, because a damp scoop seeds a rock-hard clump."

(252 chars)

---

9/
"A pantry does not go off all at once, it goes off one forgotten item at a time, at the back, in the dark." The fix is a rule the shelf keeps for you: date every open bag the day you open it, and load every row from behind so the oldest stands at the front.

(260 chars)

---

10/
"A pantry is not clean because the shelves are wiped." It is clean because you took every board dry-first, dated every open bag, and faced the oldest to the front. Do one zone this weekend, the dry goods or the cans.
