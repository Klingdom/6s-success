### Card 1 · The dust rule (verbatim)
> "In a pantry, dust and damp are the whole job, and flour turns to paste the instant a wet cloth touches it. So the rule is dry before wet, without exception: vacuum or brush every board clean while it is dry, wipe only afterward, and take the board bone-dry before anything goes back, because a damp board plus grain dust is what starts a pantry smelling."

### Card 16 · The nine Shine rules (verbatim frozen list, carried from Chapter 31)
> - work top to bottom
> - dry or clear before wet
> - back to front and clean to dirty
> - mist the cloth not the surface
> - two cloths one to clean and one to dry
> - colour-code your cloths
> - match the cleaner to the surface
> - give it dwell time
> - clean to inspect and flag rather than fix

- Attribution: 6S Success: Home Edition · Chapter 33
- Design: The method card, the nine rules carried from the door that govern every pantry zone procedure, set as a
  numbered green list on panel. Small green `THE NINE RULES OF SHINE` eyebrow, the second rule (`dry or clear
  before wet`) given a small terracotta flag, because it is the one the pantry raises to an overriding principle.
  The list strings are byte-for-byte from the frozen nine Shine rules in `canonical/chapter-quotes.md`. The
  pantry keeps every one of these and adds dust, dates, pests, and damp.
