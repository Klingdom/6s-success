# Reuse Risk Check: Chapter 33 Content Package

When one chapter becomes fifty-plus assets, the risk is overexposure: the same line or image hitting the same follower until it feels canned. This check flags where that can happen. Chapter 33 is instruction-centric, so its most portable pieces are the five-zones framing, the dust rule, the nine-rule Shine method plus the dry-first pantry layer, and the per-zone procedures with their exact inputs.

## Same-day collision
The calendar avoids two assets sharing a hook on the same day. If your LinkedIn and Facebook audiences overlap heavily, push one back a day so the dust rule or the five-zones line does not land twice in one day.

## Cross-posting risk
- The LinkedIn carousel outline and the slides carousel copy cover the same zone-by-zone content. Post only one to LinkedIn (use the copy-ready slides file; keep the outline as planning).
- The LinkedIn newsletter and the email newsletter share structure; do not send both to the same subscriber the same day.

## Duplicate-content / SEO risk
- Web landing intro, ebook description, and LinkedIn article overlap in phrasing. Set the chapter page as the SEO canonical and mark syndicated copies with a canonical link.

## Cross-chapter risk (specific to Chapter 33)
- Chapter 33 is the THIRD chapter of Part Nine, the third of twenty room playbooks, following the instruction-centric format proven at the entryway and the kitchen. Keep it distinct from the method chapters behind it: it APPLIES the method to a real room and must NOT re-teach Sort, Straighten, Shine, Safety, Standardize, or Sustain (a one-line callback is fine, a re-teach is not). The nine-rule Shine method is applied here plus the pantry dry-first layer, not taught from scratch. Shine finds hazards and flags them; the safety pass fixes them, so assets must not slide into re-teaching the hazard hunt.
- Keep it distinct from Chapter 31 (The Entryway) and Chapter 32 (The Kitchen) behind it: Chapter 33 carries the same nine-rule Shine method but adds the pantry's own layer (dust, dates, damp, pests, and dry before wet, not grease), and its centerpiece is five zones, not the entryway's or the kitchen's zone lists. Do not blur the rooms' zone lists or reframes; in particular the kitchen's principle is grease hides upward, the pantry's is dry before wet.
- Do not let this chapter slide FORWARD into the next room. Ch 33 assets stay in the pantry and may hand off to Part 9's next room (Chapter 34, The Dining Room) in a single line, but must NOT teach it. This fence is held: the dining room is named only as where the loop goes next.
- The recurring-close beat is the BEFORE-AND-AFTER room signature, NOT the friction meter. The friction meter reached its goal at Chapter 30 and is RETIRED for Part 9; it does not appear in this chapter or any Part 9 chapter. Assets must close each room on its own concrete before-and-after transformation, governed by the frozen before-and-after caption.
- **No brand leaks.** The instruction-centric kit is given as product TYPES only (airtight dated containers, a handheld vacuum or soft brush, colour-coded microfiber cloths, a neutral pH multi-surface cleaner). Assets must never name a brand; the tone/brand scan confirms this held across the package, and the "Method" matches are the ordinary word "method" (the Shine method), not a brand.

## Originality and source-fidelity risk
- The chapter is a draft authored in the book voice to be instruction-centric, grounded in the CH33 canonical strings and brief. All derived assets are faithful to that draft; re-sync if the chapter is revised.
- Quote cards are verbatim: every card in `graphics/quote-card-copy.md` verified byte-identical to a quoted line in `canonical/chapter-quotes.md` (0 mismatches), including the dust rule and the nine-rule list. Nothing borrowed from outside the book. The dash scan returned CLEAN (0 em, 0 en, 0 real spaced-hyphen violations), the tone scan returned CLEAN (every match a negation or prohibition-list mention), and the brand-name scan returned CLEAN (product TYPES only, no brands).

## Recommendations
1. Rotate CTA wording (highest-value, lowest-effort fix; several posts close on "Read the free chapter").
2. Pick one carousel version for LinkedIn.
3. Set the chapter page as the SEO canonical.
4. Keep Chapter 33 from blurring into its neighbors: distinct from the method chapters behind it (it applies, does not re-teach), distinct from Chapter 32 (same method, but the pantry adds the dry-first layer and its own five zones, not grease), and distinct from Chapter 34 (The Dining Room) ahead of it. Chapter 33 is the instruction-centric pantry playbook: you do not clean the pantry, you clean the dry goods shelf, to the nine-rule Shine method plus the dry-first layer, with exact inputs, closing on the before-and-after room signature.
5. Re-sync all assets if the chapter changes after author review.
