3. Clean one pantry zone to the method this weekend (the chapter action)
   Before you close the book, pick one zone of the pantry and clean it to the method, start to finish. Gather the inputs into your caddy first, and remember the rule above every board, dry before wet, always, then vacuum or brush the dust off dry, wipe second, take it bone-dry, and date the keepers. Start with the dry goods shelf or the cans, because those two hold the most that can spoil and the most that hides and give the biggest win. It is the smallest possible proof of the whole idea, that a pantry is not clean because the shelves are wiped, it is clean because you took every board dry-first, dated every open bag, faced the oldest to the front, and flagged the chewed corner and the bulging lid you found on the way.
