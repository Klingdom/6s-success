## Motifs

- **A picture ends the argument.** "Tidy" stops being an opinion when there is a photo to match.
- **The ideal-state photo.** The positive cousin of the before photo; a space captured on the day it was exactly right.
- **A few frames beat a paragraph.** The storyboard carries a task's steps better than words.
- **Match-able or useless.** A visual standard's whole value is being seen at the point of use; shoot it from the angle and light of use.
