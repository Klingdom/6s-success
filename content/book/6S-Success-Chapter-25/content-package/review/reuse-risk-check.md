# Reuse Risk Check: Chapter 25 Content Package

When one chapter becomes fifty-plus assets, the risk is overexposure: the same line or image hitting the same follower until it feels canned. This check flags where that can happen.

## High-frequency elements
| Element | Appears in | Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Some things are easier to show than to say. When a picture carries the standard better than words can, the picture is the standard (the rule) | Canonical, CTA, LinkedIn, X, quote card, graphics, HTML | A follower on several platforms sees the hero rule repeatedly | Calendar staggers it; vary the phrasing around it per platform |
| Photograph a space when it is exactly right, and that photo becomes the standard ... at a glance (the pictures line) | Canonical, CTA, LinkedIn, X, FB, quote card, graphics | Medium to High | The flagship tool; the most portable line. Use across formats, not stacked on one day. |
| A written standard tells you what to do. A visual standard shows you what right looks like ... see and match (show-do-not-tell reframe) | Canonical, CTA, LinkedIn, X, FB, quote card | Medium to High | The spine of the chapter; vary the surrounding worked example (the tidy shelf, the entryway). |
| For a task with steps, a short strip of pictures beats a paragraph ... without reading a word (the storyboard line) | Canonical, LinkedIn, X, FB, quote card | Medium | The second visual standard; pair with a different worked task (folding table, cables, recycling) each time. |
| The most powerful standard in a home is often a single photograph ... matches it or it does not (photo-of-the-ideal-state reframe) | Canonical, LinkedIn, X, FB, quote card | Medium | The turn that makes tidy a picture; vary the space named (shelf, counter, entryway). |

## Same-day collision
The calendar avoids two assets sharing a hook on the same day. If your LinkedIn and Facebook audiences overlap heavily, push one back a day so the rule or the pictures line does not land twice in one day.

## Cross-posting risk
- The LinkedIn carousel outline (`linkedin/linkedin-carousel-outline.md`) and the slides carousel copy (`slides/linkedin-carousel-copy.md`) cover the same visual-standard content. Post only one to LinkedIn (use the copy-ready slides file; keep the outline as planning).
- The LinkedIn newsletter (`linkedin/linkedin-newsletter-version.md`) and the email newsletter share structure; do not send both to the same subscriber the same day.

## Duplicate-content / SEO risk
- Web landing intro, ebook description, and LinkedIn article overlap in phrasing. Set the chapter page as the SEO canonical and mark syndicated copies with a canonical link.

## Cross-chapter risk (specific to Chapter 25)
- Chapter 25 is the THIRD and FINAL chapter of Part Seven and the COMPLETE-THE-FIFTH-S chapter. Keep it distinct from Chapter 23 (which opened Standardize with the CONCEPT of a standard) and Chapter 24 (which gave standards their THREE TOOLS). Chapter 25 builds on both, but assets must NOT re-derive the concept of a standard, re-teach the three tools, re-teach Straighten's visual controls (labels, outlines, boundaries, the Glance Test; a one-line distinction, a label is a tiny visual standard, is fine, a re-teach is not), re-teach the before-photo technique from Prepare (referenced only as the "optimistic mirror image"), or re-teach Sort, Straighten, Shine, or Safety. Grep confirms the assets deliver the visual standard (the ideal-state photo and the storyboard) and do not rebuild the earlier material.
- Do not let this chapter slide FORWARD into the making-it-last work. Ch 25 assets keep it to the visual standard and must NOT build the long-term adherence and audit system (Sustain, Chapters 26 to 30). This fence is held: the making-it-last work is named in the package only as "the next part" and the sixth S still ahead, never built.
- The friction-meter beat is specific to this chapter, and it is a MILESTONE, not a hold. The live needle ticks a small, honest step FORWARD from (67.6,154.9), where Chapters 23 and 24 held it, to (67.4,154.95), reaching the STANDARDIZE COMPLETE mark, the fifth milestone on the dial after Sort, Straighten, Shine, and Safety complete; the forward tick is deliberately tiny because Standardize secured what was built rather than clearing new ground, and behind the needle the anti-backslide catch is now drawn FULLY ENGAGED and locked, its firmest state, with the GOAL crosshair a step ahead at (67,155). Five of the six S's are done. Do not understate this as a hold (unlike Chapter 24) and do not overstate it as reaching the goal; the goal stays a step ahead because one S remains, Sustain, which keeps a held home held over the years.

## Originality and source-fidelity risk
- The chapter is a draft authored in the book voice, grounded in the 6S source ("Steps to Complete Standardize": provide pictures of the ideal state, tied lightly to standard work and Toyota / TPS), not supplied verbatim source. All derived assets are faithful to that draft; re-sync if the chapter is revised.
- Quote cards are verbatim: every card in `graphics/quote-card-copy.md` (10 cards) verified byte-identical to a quoted line in `canonical/chapter-quotes.md` (0 mismatches). Nothing borrowed from outside the book; no brand-name products are named, and the Standardize step, standard work, Toyota / TPS origin, and the storyboard-from-filmmaking reference are used lightly, home-scaled, not overclaimed. The visual standard is framed as sharing clarity and ending arguments, warm and human; "match" and "right" are topic words used warmly, never enforce, comply, audit (punitive), or lock down (the force / control scan returned zero control uses across the whole package).

## Recommendations
1. Rotate CTA wording (highest-value, lowest-effort fix; several posts close on "Read the free chapter").
2. Pick one carousel version for LinkedIn.
3. Set the chapter page as the SEO canonical.
4. Keep Chapter 25 from blurring into its neighbors: distinct from Chapter 23 (the concept, already delivered) and Chapter 24 (the three tools, already delivered) behind it, and distinct from the making-it-last work ahead of it (Sustain). Chapter 25 is the COMPLETE-THE-FIFTH-S chapter: the visual standard (the ideal-state photo and the storyboard), the make-it-match-able craft, and the honest STANDARDIZE COMPLETE milestone with its fully engaged anti-backslide catch.
5. Re-sync all assets if the authored chapter changes after author review.
