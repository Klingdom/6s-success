# Newsletter Preview Text (Preheader): Pictures, Storyboards, and Visual Standards

Ten options, each 35 to 90 characters, each paired with a subject line. Char counts noted.

1. Pairs with #1 (Some things are easier to show than to say. When a picture carries the standard better than words can, the picture is the standard.)
   "Show it, don't say it: when a picture carries the standard, it is the standard." (79 chars)

2. Pairs with #2 (A written standard tells you what to do. A visual standard shows you what right looks like.)
   "You wrote the best way down. Where words fall short, show it in a picture." (74 chars)

3. Pairs with #3 (Photograph a space when it is exactly right, and that photo becomes the standard.)
   "Get a space exactly right, photograph it, and let the photo be the standard." (76 chars)

4. Pairs with #4 (The most powerful standard in a home is often a single photograph.)
   "Often the strongest standard in a home is a single honest photograph." (69 chars)

5. Pairs with #5 (For a task with steps, a short strip of pictures beats a paragraph.)
   "For a task with steps, a short strip of pictures beats any paragraph." (69 chars)

6. Pairs with #6 (A child too young to read the word tidy can match a shelf to a photo.)
   "A child too young to read tidy can still match a shelf to a photo." (66 chars)

7. Pairs with #7 (With this, the fifth S is complete. Your home runs on what you have made visible.)
   "The fifth S is complete. Your home runs on what you have made visible." (70 chars)

8. Pairs with #8 (The problem is not the people and not the standard. It is the medium.)
   "The card was right and still ignored. The problem was the medium, not you." (74 chars)

9. Pairs with #9 (The needle barely moved, and the grip behind it is complete.)
   "The needle barely moved, and the grip behind it is finally complete." (68 chars)

10. Pairs with #10 (Give one space a picture anyone can match, in about a minute.)
    "Photograph one space at its best and tape it up. About a minute." (64 chars)
