# Newsletter Short Teaser: Pictures, Storyboards, and Visual Standards

For a roundup or digest. 90 to 140 words.

---

The last two chapters you wrote the best way down and gave it tools: a checklist, a schedule, a name. But you have met the edge where words strain. Try to write down exactly what a tidy shelf looks like and you pile up clauses that still miss it. The problem is not the people and not the standard. It is the medium. So stop describing the image and simply show it. Photograph a space when it is exactly right, and that photo becomes the standard anyone can match at a glance; for a task with steps, a short strip of pictures beats a paragraph. With this, the fifth S is complete. Your home no longer runs on your memory. It runs on what you have made visible.
