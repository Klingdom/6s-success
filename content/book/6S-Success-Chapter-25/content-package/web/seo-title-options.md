# SEO Title Options: Chapter 25, Pictures, Storyboards, and Visual Standards

Five title options. Character counts are in parentheses. The canonical title is listed first.

1. Pictures, Storyboards, and Visual Standards (44)
   Angle: canonical chapter title. Use as the H1 and primary on-page heading, with "a visual standard," "a photo of the ideal state," "a storyboard," "show what right looks like," and "completes the fifth S" early in the body.

2. Some Things Are Easier to Show Than to Say (43)
   Angle: leads with the hero rule. Names the whole idea in a single line, a written standard is not always the right medium, and is quotable and snippet-ready for the reader who is tired of arguing about what "tidy" means.

3. When a Picture Is the Standard (30)
   Angle: leads with the hero device. Says the visual standard plainly, when a picture carries the standard better than words can, the picture is the standard, and answers a real search for how to keep a space right without writing instructions no one follows the same way.

4. Show What Right Looks Like (26)
   Angle: names the move in the reader's own words. Short, memorable, and true to the calm register, it maps the whole chapter to one promise: stop describing the ideal state and simply show it, so the room either matches the picture or does not.

5. Photograph the Ideal State (24)
   Angle: opens with the flagship move, the smallest provable proof of the idea. Concrete and outcome-led, photograph a space when it is exactly right and that photo becomes the standard anyone can match at a glance, and echoes the primary keyword.
