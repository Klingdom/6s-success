# Meta Description Options: Chapter 25, Pictures, Storyboards, and Visual Standards

Five options, each 140 to 160 characters, each with a primary or secondary keyword used naturally. Character counts are in parentheses.

1. Some things are easier to show than to say. Photograph a space at its best, and that photo becomes the standard anyone can match at a glance.

   Note: 141 characters.

2. How to keep a space right without arguing: photograph the ideal state, put the picture where the space is, and the room either matches it or does not.

   Note: 150 characters.

3. A written standard tells you what to do; a visual standard shows you what right looks like. Photograph the ideal state, or storyboard a task's steps.

   Note: 149 characters.

4. Why do written tidying instructions never work? The problem is the medium. Show the ideal state in a photo, and tidy stops being an argument.

   Note: 141 characters.

5. Some things resist description. When a picture carries the standard better than words can, the picture is the standard. This completes the fifth S.

   Note: 147 characters.
