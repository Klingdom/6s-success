# Quote Card Copy: Chapter 25

**"Pictures, Storyboards, and Visual Standards"**
*6S Success: Home Edition · Part Seven · Standardize*

Ten quote cards, each pulled from the frozen quotes in `content-package/canonical/chapter-quotes.md`.
Every quoted line below was diffed byte-for-byte against that file. Cards 1 through 10 are **verbatim**
(byte-identical to the frozen quotes). The alternates at the end are **verbatim** as well, each a
complete frozen line lifted whole. Nothing here is invented, paraphrased, or trimmed.

Each card uses the warm book palette (paper `#F7F2E9`, panel `#FBF7EF`, ink `#2B2622`) and the Fraunces
display face for the quote, Newsreader italic for the attribution. No em dashes, no en dashes, no
floating hyphen separators; labels use the middot. The tone is calm, warm, and anti-shame: a visual
standard makes right easier to reach, a gift and not a rule, so no card carries a corporate, controlling,
or inspection tone. A written standard people argue over is the normal case, never a failing, because the
problem is the medium and not the people.

### Card 3 (verbatim)

- Attribution: 6S Success: Home Edition · Chapter 25

### Card 4 (verbatim)

- Attribution: 6S Success: Home Edition · Chapter 25
