## Fix at next regeneration (none blocking)

### 1. Vocabulary drift on the hero device

"Prime zone" is printed on `image2`, `image3`, `image4` and `image5` only. Elsewhere the kept set
substitutes:

- `image1`: **PREMIUM / MODERATE / ECONOMY**
- `image7`: "prime space" and "prime real estate"
- `image8`: ordinal ranking (first, second, third, last)

None of these are wrong, and the real-estate framing is the chapter's own, but a single vocabulary
pass would help the set read as one system.

### 2. Zone names are inconsistent between plates

`image2` invents **four** zones: STRETCH / PRIME / BEND / DEEP. `image5` uses three but names the
middle one **NEAR ZONE**, where `image2` called that band **BEND ZONE**. The frozen spec has three
tiers: daily, weekly, rare.

The four-zone split is defensible, since it distinguishes high-rare from deep-rare, but the middle
band should carry one name across the chapter.

### 3. The frozen half-definition is missing

The frozen hero definition ends *"and measured from where you use the thing."* That clause appears
on no plate. It is the part that makes point-of-use follow from the prime zone, and `image2` is the
natural place for it.

### 4. `image7` re-opens Sort in one question

Question 4 reads **"IS IT THE RIGHT QTY? Keep what you use. Let the extra go."** That is a Sort
decision inside a Straighten chapter. The plate was kept because the chapter has a section named
*Audit Your Prime Space* and this plate is titled *Prime Space Audit*, so the fit is otherwise
exact. Delete or reword that one question at regeneration. Question 5, "Is this the best home?",
also echoes Chapter 12 and could be tightened to "best spot".

### 5. `image8` uses Chapter 12 language

The badge reads "We didn't get rid of anything. We just gave each item a better **home**." Homes
were assigned in Chapter 12; this chapter moves them. Change "home" to "spot" so the two chapters
stay distinct.

### 6. Contractions and one art bug

Contractions on `image2` ("That's"), `image7` (several: "don't", "WHAT'S", "isn't", "can't") and
`image8` ("didn't"). House voice uses none.

`image4` shows a **cutlery icon beside the "Keys" row** — an art bug, not a text error.

# SALVAGE PASS · 2026-07-21

A second pass over the eleven held images, run the same way as Chapter 12's.

**Result: none recovered. The chapter is unchanged.**

That is a real finding rather than a shrug, and the reason is structural.

## Why Chapter 12's rescue worked and this one does not

`ch12-image9` was recoverable because it was a **clean five-panel strip where each panel mapped to
exactly one chapter**. Panels 1 and 3 were Chapter 12, panel 2 was Chapter 13, panel 4 was Chapter
14. That let a caption honestly say "these two are today's work, those two are the chapters ahead",
which the frozen brief permits as a teaser.

Chapter 13's held plates do not have that structure. **The fences run through every panel and the
footer, not between them.** Two examined directly:

- **`ch13-image15` "Room by Room, Solutions that Stick"** looks like the same clean grid, six room
  panels. But each individual panel mixes chapters: the Kitchen panel alone contains "Keep daily
  items at eye level" (Chapter 13, correct), "Use clear containers and **labels**" (Chapter 14),
  while the Bedroom and Living Room panels say "One in, one out" (Sort) and the Entryway panel says
  "Give everything **a home**" (Chapter 12). The footer then adds "Build the habit. Live the life."
  (Sustain). No caption can assign a panel to a chapter when the panel is four chapters at once.
- **`ch13-image13` "Out of Sight, Not Out of Mind"** carries a genuine reach thread ("Can I reach it
  easily?", "Keep daily items within reach"), but its checklist asks "Is it labeled clearly?" and
  "Does it match my habits?", and its footer prescribes "MAKE IT A HABIT... One in, one out."
  The disqualifying content is in the furniture of the plate, not in a removable panel.

## The rule this pass established

**A caption may reframe an image's scope. It must not contradict the image's own printed labels.**

Reframing "these panels are the next chapters" is honest, because it adds context the plate does
not deny. Telling a reader that a plate's own six printed callout names are not really the controls
would be arguing with the artwork in front of them. That rules out caption-rescue for every plate
whose problem is renamed or invented terminology.

## Two further reasons not to force these in

**The chapter does not need more figures.** Chapter 13 already carries nine, one per section in
argument order.

**The chapter is badly over length.** The manuscript runs **4,707 words against a frozen target of
3,600 to 3,900** — between 800 and 1,100 words long, in a brief that says "Keep it TIGHT." Writing
new prose to justify more artwork would push it further the wrong way.
