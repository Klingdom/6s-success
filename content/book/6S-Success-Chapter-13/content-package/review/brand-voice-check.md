# Brand Voice Check: Chapter 13 Content Package

The voice of *6S Success: Home Edition* is warm, plain-spoken, and quietly confident. Chapter 13 is the second Straighten chapter, and its mood is light and practical: this is arranging, not soul-searching. The voice must stay encouraging and anti-shame, and it must make a slightly abstract idea (the value of easy-reach space) feel concrete and obvious.

## Voice fingerprint (from the authored final HTML and manuscript)
- **Warm and personal.** Talks to "you," returns to the entryway the reader already knows, then scales to the kitchen cabinet.
- **Concrete metaphor over abstraction.** Prime space is taught as real estate: "the corner shop on the busy street" versus "the storage unit across town." The mug at the back of a high shelf versus the novelty teapot front and center makes the cost visible.
- **Anti-shame.** The waffle iron in the good spot is "not a character flaw"; it "just landed and never moved, the way water sits wherever the ground is lowest." Wrong placement is "a first draft," "not a test you can fail."
- **Plain rule.** One line settles it: the best spot goes to the thing you reach for most.
- **Honest about scope.** Nothing leaves the house, so the friction move is justified on a real basis (reaching got cheaper for the busiest things), not overstated.

## Specific wins
- The real-estate metaphor gives an abstract idea (scarce easy-reach space) a concrete price, and it travels intact across channels.
- "The best spot goes to the thing you reach for most" is a clean, portable rule that anchors every asset.
- The audit reframe ("paying premium rent on a spot it did not earn") is the most shareable line and stays anti-shame.

## Voice risks to watch
- **Hero vocabulary is dense.** "prime," "reach," "space," "spot," and "frequency" recur heavily because they are the subject; that is expected, but vary the surrounding language when excerpting so a single post does not stack them.
- **CTA sameness.** Rotate the LinkedIn and X sign-offs.
- **Keep the mood light.** This is the easy, carpentry-like part of the book; do not let any asset drift into a formal or heavy register.

## Banned-language audit
- Widened dash scan (em dash, en dash, and spaced-hyphen " - " used as a separator, excluding markdown list bullets): clean across the package; labels use the middot "·".
- No hype words and no force/violence metaphors found in any shipped asset. The real-estate/tenant framing is drawn from the manuscript, not force imagery.

## Verdict
Voice is consistent and intact across all twelve packages, and the light, anti-shame, design-over-discipline job of Chapter 13 is honored throughout. Approve the voice. The chapter itself still needs author sign-off, since it was authored in the book voice rather than supplied from source.
