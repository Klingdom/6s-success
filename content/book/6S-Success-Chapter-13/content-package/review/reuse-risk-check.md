# Reuse Risk Check: Chapter 13 Content Package

When one chapter becomes fifty-plus assets, the risk is overexposure: the same line or image hitting the same follower until it feels canned. This check flags where that can happen.

## High-frequency elements
| Element | Appears in | Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| The best spot goes to the thing you reach for most | Web, LinkedIn, X, FB, newsletter, quote card, graphics | A follower on several platforms sees the rule repeatedly | Calendar staggers it; vary the phrasing per platform |
| Prime space is expensive (real-estate framing) | Hero infographic, carousel, deck, LinkedIn, X | Medium | The chapter's central metaphor; repetition reinforces. Vary the example (mug/teapot, kitchen, closet). |
| Paying premium rent on a spot it did not earn (audit reframe) | LinkedIn, FB, X, quote card | Medium | Strongest hook. Use across formats, not stacked on one day. |
| Store it where you use it (point of use) | LinkedIn, 6S Tip graphic, video | Low to Medium | Memorable; rotate placement. |
| The entryway keepers placed by frequency | Worked-pass graphic, carousel, video, longform | Low | Practical, meant to repeat; vary which keeper you lead with. |
| Soft-CTA sign-off wording | Most LinkedIn and X posts | Medium | Rotate phrasings; most likely thing to feel templated. |

## Same-day collision
The calendar avoids two assets sharing a hook on the same day. If your LinkedIn and Facebook audiences overlap heavily, push one back a day so the audit reframe does not land twice in one day.

## Cross-posting risk
- The LinkedIn carousel outline and the slides carousel copy cover the same Prime Zone content. Post only one to LinkedIn (use the copy-ready `slides/linkedin-carousel-copy.md`; keep the outline as planning).
- The LinkedIn newsletter and the email newsletter share structure; do not send both to the same subscriber the same day.

## Duplicate-content / SEO risk
- Web landing intro, ebook description, and LinkedIn article overlap in phrasing. Set the chapter page as the SEO canonical and mark syndicated copies with a canonical link.

## Cross-chapter risk (specific to Chapter 13)
- Chapters 12 to 15 are all Straighten. Keep each distinct: Ch 12 gives each thing a home, Ch 13 places those homes by frequency, Ch 14 makes each home obvious with a visual cue, Ch 15 maps the whole room. Ch 13 assets must not drift into re-assigning homes (Ch 12) or into labels/visual controls (Ch 14).
- Hold Ch 14's "make it obvious at a glance" as a teaser only, so Ch 13 and Ch 14 do not blur.
- The friction-meter "second Straighten step" is specific to this chapter; do not reuse the first-step or held-meter framing in Ch 13 assets.

## Originality and source-fidelity risk
- The chapter is a draft authored in the book voice, not supplied source. All derived assets are faithful to that draft; re-sync if the chapter is revised.
- Quote cards are verbatim (10 cards plus 5 alternates), grep-verified. Nothing borrowed from outside the book.

## Recommendations
1. Rotate CTA wording (highest-value, lowest-effort fix).
2. Pick one carousel version for LinkedIn.
3. Set the chapter page as the SEO canonical.
4. Keep the Part 4 chapters (12 to 15) from blurring, especially Ch 13 versus Ch 12 and Ch 14.
5. Re-sync all assets if the authored chapter changes after author review.
