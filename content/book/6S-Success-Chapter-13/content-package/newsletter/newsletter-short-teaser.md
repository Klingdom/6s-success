# Newsletter Short Teaser: Store by Frequency of Use

For a roundup or digest. 90 to 140 words.

---

Last issue gave every entryway keeper a home. But at the door they all hang at the same easy height, treated the same, when the keys go out a dozen times a day and the umbrella twice a month. This chapter fixes that without adding or removing a thing. Not all storage is equal: the easy-reach prime zone is small and expensive, so spend it on what you use most. The rule is plain, the best spot goes to the thing you reach for most. Sort each home into daily, weekly, and rare, place it to match, audit the best spots for things that never earned them, and the friction meter takes its second Straighten step.
