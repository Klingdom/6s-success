# Read Next Recommendations: Chapter 13, Store by Frequency of Use

1. Chapter 14, Visual Controls for the Home
   The next step, and the rest of Part Four. The homes are assigned and now well placed, but a good spot is obvious only to the person who set it up. You carry the whole arrangement in your head, so things still drift back and family members still ask where a thing goes. Chapter 14 makes each home clear at a glance, with a label, an outline, or a simple visual cue, so anyone can find and return a thing without having to ask.

2. Chapter 12, Straighten: A Place for Everything
   The setup. Before this chapter could run, every keeper needed a home. Chapter 12 opened Straighten and walked the entryway keepers into homes one at a time, judged by a single test, that putting a thing back is easier than putting it down anywhere else. What it left behind was a row of good homes all treated the same. This chapter places each one by how often your hand goes to it.

3. Make one swap (the chapter action)
   Open one drawer or cabinet you use every day. Find the thing in the easiest-to-reach spot that you almost never use, and trade it with a daily thing that is currently awkward to get to. One swap, and the space is faster.

4. The Prime Zone card (free resource)
   A printable plus phone wallpaper with the reach-to-frequency rule and the prime versus far zones, so the whole idea fits in a glance.

5. The Frequency Placement worksheet (free resource)
   A printable to list each keeper, how often you use it, and where its home should sit, so your best space goes to your busiest things.
