## You are here blurb
You are at Chapter 13, the second chapter of Part Four, Straighten, and the second step of the second S. Every keeper has a home now, but every home is treated the same, when the keys go out a dozen times a day and the umbrella twice a month. This chapter turns from assigning homes to placing them well: it does not re-assign homes (Chapter 12), add labels or visual cues (Chapter 14), map the whole room (Chapter 15), or re-open Sort. Its reframe is that prime space is expensive, so spend it on what you use every day and send the once-a-year things a step further off. It names the prime zone, the small band of easy reach measured from where you stand, and gives one rule, that the best spot goes to the thing you reach for most. The entryway keepers are placed by frequency, keys and leash prime, bag near, umbrella a step off, mail in the exit path, then scaled to the kitchen cabinet and the coat closet. Nothing left the house, yet the friction meter takes its second Straighten step, because reaching got cheaper for the things you reach for most.
