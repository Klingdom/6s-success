Five title options. Character counts are in parentheses. The canonical title is listed first.

1. Store by Frequency of Use (26)
   Angle: canonical book title. Use as the H1 and primary on-page heading, with "prime zone," "reach," and "how often you use it" early in the body.

2. The Best Spot Goes to the Thing You Reach for Most (50)
   Angle: leads with the one rule of the chapter. Names the whole idea in one line and is highly quotable.

3. Prime Space Is Expensive: Spend It on What You Use Most (54)
   Angle: opens with the core principle and the real-estate reframe. Warm and memorable.

4. Where Should Everyday Things Go? Store by Frequency of Use (57)
   Angle: matches the exact question readers arrive with. The answer is the prime zone for the daily things.

5. Store It Where You Use It, Not Where It Belongs (47)
   Angle: promises the point-of-use reframe at the heart of the audit. Reframes the whole habit in one line.
