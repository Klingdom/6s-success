# Facebook Longform Post: The Umbrella in the Keys' Good Spot

A longer, story-driven post from Chapter 13 of 6S Success: Home Edition, the second Straighten step. Conversational, like talking to a friend. Ends with a soft CTA. No em dashes.

---

Every thing by my door finally had a home. The keys on a hook, the leash beside them, the reusable bag on the next hook, the umbrella after that, the mail in a tray below. I was proud of it. And then I noticed every one of those homes was treated exactly the same.

Look at what my hand actually did there. I grabbed the keys a dozen times a day, every coming and going. I reached for the umbrella maybe twice a month, only when the sky turned. Yet both hung at the same easy arm's length, in the same good light, as though I needed them the same amount. A home can be well built and still be in the wrong place.

Here is the idea that fixed it. Not all storage is equal. A small band of space is genuinely pleasant to use, right in front of you at hand height, no bending and no stretching. Roughly hips to shoulders, within an arm's length, at the front. Call it the prime zone. It is the best storage you own, and there is never much of it.

So the rule writes itself. The best spot goes to the thing you reach for most. When two things want the same good spot, you do not weigh their size or tidiness. You ask which one your hand goes to more often, and that one wins.

I placed the door by that rule. Keys took the prime spot closest to the door, the first thing my hand meets. The leash stayed prime beside them. The reusable bag moved near, a hook to the side, since I grab it a few times a week, not a few times a day. The umbrella stepped a little off into a corner stand, close enough for a rainy day and out of the way the rest of the time.

Nothing left the house. The room just got quicker, because reaching got cheaper for the things I reach for most.

So here is your low-pressure homework for the comments. Open one drawer you use every day, find a rarely used thing hogging the easiest spot, and trade it with a daily thing that is currently awkward to reach.
