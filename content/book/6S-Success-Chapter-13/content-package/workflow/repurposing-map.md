# Chapter 13 · Repurposing Map

How the one chapter fans out into every channel, and which idea leads on each.

## The spine (reused across channels)
- The turn within Straighten: homes are already assigned; now place each home by how often you reach for it (the second Straighten step)
- The prime zone: the small band of genuinely easy reach, measured from where you use the thing
- The rule: the best spot goes to the thing you reach for most
- The principle as a budget: prime space is expensive, so spend it on daily things and send once-a-year things a step further off
- The audit reframe: a thing you touch twice a year is paying premium rent on a spot it did not earn
- Match the reach to the use: the daily / weekly / rare tiers to prime / near / a step off
- Point of use: store it where you use it, not where it belongs on a list
- The worked pass: the entryway keepers placed by frequency, then scaled to the kitchen cabinet and coat closet
- The second Straighten step on the friction meter (nothing added or removed, yet the busiest things became the easiest to reach)

## Cross-chapter / reuse cautions
- Keep Chapter 13 distinct from Chapter 12: Ch 12 assigned homes; Ch 13 places those same homes by frequency of use. Do not re-teach giving a thing a home.
- Hold the Chapter 14 idea (labels, outlines, color-coding, visual cues) as a teaser only, so Ch 13 and Ch 14 do not blur. Do not add visual cues here.
- Do not map or plan a whole room's storage at once; that is Chapter 15. Teaser only.
- The friction-meter "second Straighten step" is specific to Ch 13; do not reuse the first-step or Sort-complete framing here, and do not read it as arriving (Straighten is not finished).
- Set the chapter page as the SEO canonical; point syndicated copies (LinkedIn article, Medium) back to it.
