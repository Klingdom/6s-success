# Chapter 13 LinkedIn Comment Prompts: Store by Frequency of Use

Five prompts to start genuine discussion. Drop one in the comments of a post or article to invite replies. Anti-shame voice: wrong placement is a fixable first draft, never a character flaw.

---

1. Name the one thing your hand goes to most in your kitchen, the coffee, a favorite pan, a mug. Is it in your prime zone right now, front and center at hand height? Or is it doing a dozen jobs a day from an awkward spot?

2. Now the honest one: look at your best, easiest space and tell us what is sitting in it that you touch maybe twice a year. No blame owed, it just landed there and never moved. That is the expensive spot about to come free.

3. The best spot goes to the thing you reach for most. Run that rule on one shelf today and make one swap: trade the wasted best spot for a daily thing that is currently a stretch. What did you move, and did the shelf get faster?

4. Store it where you use it, not where it belongs on a list. What is your version of coffee living by the coffee maker, a thing that finally worked once you moved it to the point of use instead of its proper category?

5. Frequency placement is a first draft, not a test you can fail. What is one thing you placed wrong at first, then slid a shelf over once the room told you? The busiest things should be the least trouble to reach.
