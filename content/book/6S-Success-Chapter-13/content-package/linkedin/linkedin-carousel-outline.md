# Chapter 13 LinkedIn Carousel Outline: Store by Frequency of Use

Twelve slides. Slide 1 hooks, last slide carries the soft CTA. Each slide has a headline and one or two lines of body. Anti-shame voice, no force metaphors.

---

## Slide 1: Hook

**Headline:** Every keeper has a home. So why is the room still slow?

**Body:** Because a good home can still be in the wrong place. Swipe for the second Straighten step, and the one rule that decides where each thing goes.

---

## Slide 2: The same prime reach

**Headline:** Keys a dozen times a day. Umbrella twice a month. Same hook.

**Body:** Every home at the door gets the same easy arm's reach, as though you needed everything the same amount. You do not.

---

## Slide 3: Not all storage is equal

**Headline:** Your best storage is scarce.

**Body:** A small band is genuinely pleasant to use, right in front of you at hand height. A great deal is a nuisance, up high or at the back. The good part is small.

---

## Slide 4: The principle

**Headline:** Prime space is expensive.

**Body:** Spend it on what you use every day, and send the once-a-year things a step further off. Read it as a budget, not a rule of neatness.

---

## Slide 5: Think of it as real estate

**Headline:** The corner shop, or the storage unit across town.

**Body:** The front of the shelf at hand height is expensive and worth it. The high shelf and the deep back are cheap and a bother to visit. The only mistake is the wrong tenant in each.

---

## Slide 6: The prime zone

**Headline:** Name the good, easy reach: the prime zone.

**Body:** Hips to shoulders, within an arm's length, at the front. Measured from where you stand. The most valuable storage you own, and there is never very much of it.

---

## Slide 7: The rule

**Headline:** The best spot goes to the thing you reach for most.

**Body:** That is the entire rule. When two items want the same good spot, you ask which one your hand goes to more often, and that one wins.

---

## Slide 8: Three loose piles

**Headline:** Daily to the prime zone. Weekly near. Rare a step further off.

**Body:** A rough sort, not a spreadsheet. The more often you use a thing, the closer and easier its home should be.

---

## Slide 9: Not a test you can fail

**Headline:** A busy thing in a far spot will nag at you within a day.

**Body:** The room tells you where you guessed wrong, and sliding a thing one shelf over costs nothing. Frequency placement is a first draft, not a test you can fail.

---

## Slide 10: The audit

**Headline:** Premium rent on a spot it did not earn.

**Body:** Look at what is sitting in your best, easiest space right now. If it is something you touch twice a year, it is paying premium rent on a spot it did not earn. No blame owed; it just landed there and never moved.

---

## Slide 11: Store it where you use it

**Headline:** Store it where you use it, not where it belongs on a list.

**Body:** Coffee lives by the coffee maker, not across the kitchen. The point of use beats the tidy category every time, because you live at the point of use and only visit the category.

---
