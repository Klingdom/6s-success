# Chapter 42 Facebook Posts: The Guest Bathroom

Five short standalone posts.

## 1. The smell is a dry trap
That faint sewer smell in a guest bathroom is almost never dirt. A room nobody uses loses the water in its drain traps, and an empty trap lets the drain's smell into the room. Once a month, pour a mug of water down every drain to refill the seals. No scrubbing needed.

## 2. Nothing gets demoted in here
Your guest bathroom quietly becomes the home for the conditioner nobody liked and the towels that went thin. But a guest gets your standard, not your cast-offs. If it is not good enough for you, it is not good enough for a visitor, and it leaves the house rather than retiring here.

## 3. The good towels
Every house saves the nice towels and gives guests the worn ones. But towels thin whether they are used or not, so the good set ages untouched while visitors dry off on something gray. The guest bundle is built from the best towels you own.

## 4. Clean the surfaces a guest studies
A guest behind a shut door has nothing to do but look, and they look at the surfaces you skip: the tap collar, the underside of the seat, the base of the soap pump. Clean those as well as the ones on show. You are cleaning for someone who looks harder than you do.

## 5. The grout you cannot clean
You have scrubbed one grout line three times and it stayed gray. It is not dirt, it is mould in porous grout or a failed silicone bead, and no product will bring it back. Stop scrubbing a job that cannot succeed: one treatment, then re-grout or re-caulk it. Chapter 42. Next: the laundry room.
