# Facebook Group Discussion Post: The Guest Bathroom

For a home or hosting group. A useful tip, an open question, a soft close.

---

The guest-bathroom smell nobody can scrub away.

If your guest bathroom smells faintly of sewer no matter how hard you clean it, the cleaning is not the problem. A room nobody uses loses the water sitting in its drain traps, and an empty trap lets the drain's smell straight up into the room. So once a month, used or not, pour a mug of water down the sink, the tub, and the floor drain to refill the seals. You will fix a smell no scrubbing ever could.

Two more that keep it guest-worthy. First, nothing gets demoted in here: the disliked conditioner, the wrong soap, the thin towels. If it is not good enough for you, it is not good enough for a guest, so it leaves the house rather than retiring in the guest bathroom. Second, wash the stored towels hot even though nobody has touched them, because a towel folded all season smells musty the instant it gets wet.

What has been demoted into your guest bathroom that would not pass in your own?
