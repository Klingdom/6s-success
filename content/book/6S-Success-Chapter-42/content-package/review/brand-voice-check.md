# Chapter 42 Brand Voice Check: The Guest Bathroom

## Voice target
Warm, practical, expert, and honest about the word guest. The register of a knowledgeable friend who has hosted people and knows the difference between a room you show off and a room you apologise for.

## Scan results
- **Product types only, no brands**: PASS. A brand-name scan across the whole package returned no true hits. Everything is a type: bathroom grime and soap-film cleaner, neutral pH multi-surface cleaner, streak-free glass and mirror cleaner, toilet bowl cleaner and a dedicated bowl brush, dedicated mildew remover, stiff grout brush, colour-coded microfiber cloth set, shower squeegee, over-door hook rack, lockable medicine box, chemical storage bin, moisture absorber.
- **No hype, no invented numbers or prices**: PASS. No statistics, no fabricated study claims, no dollar figures.
- **Chemistry stated safely, never advises mixing**: PASS. The chapter repeats the never-mix-bleach-with-an-acid-cleaner rule and the latch-the-chemistry rule for a room a visiting child is sent to alone.
- **Dash and punctuation house style**: PASS. Middot for label separators; no em/en dashes; no prose spaced-hyphen separators.
- **Inclusive, non-gendered**: PASS. "A visitor", "a guest", "a stranger", "a small guest" throughout; no gendered assumptions.

## Consistency with the series
- The zone-by-zone instruction voice matches Chapters 31 to 41.
- The one-idea-to-keep close and the before-and-after signature match the Part 9 pattern.
- The no-demotion and guest-ready thinking echoes the guest bedroom (Ch 38) without repeating its material, which is the correct family resemblance.
- The two fences held everywhere: never mix two cleaners, and never demote the household's cast-offs into the guest bathroom.

## Verdict
On voice. No changes needed.
