# Chapter 42 Reuse and Risk Check: The Guest Bathroom

## Safety-claim review
- **Never mix cleaners / never use an acid cleaner after bleach**: correctly and clearly stated, with the reason (together they make a gas). This is the most safety-critical claim in the chapter and it is handled well. PASS.
- **Latch or remove the chemistry in a room a visiting child is sent to alone**: correct, mainstream child-safety guidance, sharpened by the true observation that guests are often sent to this room on their own. PASS.
- **Fit a real grab bar, not a towel bar; non-slip mat in an unfamiliar tub**: correct fall-prevention guidance for a stranger who does not know the room. The point that a towel bar or suction rail lets go under load is accurate and important. PASS.
- **Run-the-water for the dry trap**: sound plumbing hygiene; no repair instruction beyond pouring water to refill a trap seal. PASS.
- **The grout you cannot clean (re-grout or re-caulk failed grout/silicone)**: correct; it stops readers scrubbing indefinitely at mould that has penetrated porous grout. No hazardous method is suggested. PASS.
