## Source fidelity and differentiation from Chapter 41
The five zones, their inputs, their step order, and every inspect-and-flag list are folded directly from the Micro Zone Manual's Guest Bathroom content (five zones, each with shine_detail: surfaces, methods, products_used, inspect_as_you_clean) and the room's 28 mapped product types. The chapter deliberately takes the wet-room method from Chapter 41 as read rather than re-teaching it, and spends its distinctive weight on the guest-bathroom layer drawn from the Manual's intro and tips: run the water for the dry-trap smell, no demotion of the household's cast-offs, and clean-and-stock for a guest alone behind a shut door. The calls (whose bathroom this really is, the bag of hotel miniatures, the grout you cannot clean, the brush nobody wants to touch, the good towels) come straight from the Manual's the_call entries.
