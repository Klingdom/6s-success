# Chapter 42 Editorial Review: The Guest Bathroom

## Verdict
Ready for Phil's review as a draft. The chapter meets the instruction-centric standard: every one of the five zones carries an Inputs list, numbered Steps, and an inspect-and-flag paragraph, and it solves the hardest problem a second bathroom faces, differentiating from the first without re-teaching the wet-room method.

## What works
- **It differentiates cleanly from Chapter 41.** The single biggest risk with two bathrooms back to back was repetition, and the chapter avoids it by taking the wet-room method as read and building an entirely new layer on the two things unique to a guest bathroom: it is watched most closely and used least. That "two opposite problems" framing is the strongest structural idea in the chapter.
- **The run-the-water insight is genuinely useful.** The dry-trap explanation for the sewer smell no scrubbing cures is the kind of counter-intuitive, high-value fact that makes a chapter memorable, and it is drawn straight from the Manual's cleaning note.
- **The no-demotion rule is sharp and true.** Naming the guest bathroom as the household's retirement home for rejected products, and refusing it, is a clean, quotable principle that also does real decluttering work.
- **The Manual reset instructions are folded in faithfully.** The read-the-trap vanity storage, the spray-and-dwell shower with the grout-you-cannot-clean call, the cleanest-to-dirtiest toilet, and the wash-the-stored-linen shelf are carried straight from the source shine_detail.

## Watch items (minor, non-blocking)
- The chapter is long (about 6,130 words) for a five-zone room, because the calls section carries several distinct decisions; the length cap is lifted for Part 9, so this is acceptable, and the Calls section is the easiest trim if Phil wants tighter.
- The method section explicitly points back to Chapter 41 for the full wet-room method; this is deliberate and correct, but it does mean the two chapters should ship in order.

## Consistency checks
- Wet-room method referenced, not re-taught: PASS.
- Friction meter retired; before-and-after signature closes: PASS.
- Product types only: PASS.
- Hand-off to Chapter 43 (the laundry room) present and light: PASS.

## Recommendation
Approve as draft. No structural edits required.
