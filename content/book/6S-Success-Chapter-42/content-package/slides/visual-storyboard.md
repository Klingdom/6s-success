# Chapter 42 Visual Storyboard: The Guest Bathroom

## Design system
House palette: paper #F7F2E9, panel #FBF7EF, ink #2B2622, terracotta #BC4B2A, honey #DDA63A, slate #3C5A6B, green #6E8B5B (Shine). Fraunces display, Newsreader body, Inter labels. Footer strip: RUN THE WATER, NO DEMOTION, STOCK FOR THE SHUT DOOR.

## Recurring motif
The "run the water" cue: a mug of water poured down a drain, refilling the trap seal. It ties the room's lesson together and reappears on the method slide.

## Two fences
- Never depict two cleaners being mixed; never depict the household's cast-offs stocked in the guest bathroom as if they belong.
- Product types only, no brand names or real logos.

## Beats (twelve)
1. Title over the two-state opener.
2. The room watched most, used least.
3. Run the water: the mug down the drain.
4. The five zones overhead plan.
5. The definition: a room shown without apology.
6. The method and kit two-panel (figure 3).
7. No demotion: a cast-off leaving the house, not moving in.
8. The good towels: the best set going to the guest bundle.
9. Cleaning the surfaces a guest studies up close.
10. Stocking so a guest never asks.
11. The before-and-after room signature.
12. Close: one idea to keep, and the Chapter 43 hand-off.

## Carrying it across the set
Every asset closes on the before-and-after room signature from the doorway (the clear counter, the pink-line-free shower, the fresh towel bundle). The friction meter is retired for Part 9. Register warm, hospitable, honest. Previous Ch 41, next Ch 43 The Laundry Room.
