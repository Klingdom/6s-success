# Chapter 42 Back Cover Copy: The Guest Bathroom

## Back-cover text
The guest bathroom is the room a visitor studies most closely and the household uses least, which is why it can smell faintly of sewer no matter how hard you clean it. The cleaning was never the problem.

The Guest Bathroom keeps the wet-room method and breaks the room into five zones, giving you, for each, the exact inputs to gather and the numbered steps to follow. Run the water so the dry traps do not smell. Let nothing the household rejected retire in here. Clean the surfaces a guest studies up close, and stock the room so a visitor never has to ask.

Do one zone this week, and give the next visitor a room they can be left alone in without a second thought.
