# Chapter 42 Reader Discussion Questions: The Guest Bathroom

1. Does your guest bathroom have a faint smell you have blamed on cleaning? Could it be a dry drain trap?
2. What has been demoted into your guest bathroom that would not pass in your own?
3. Do your guests get the good towels, or the thin ones?
4. When did you last wash the stored guest towels, rather than keep them folded?
5. Is there a grout line you keep scrubbing that never comes clean?
6. Is your guest toilet brush a fixture you have stopped seeing, or a consumable you replace?
7. Can a guest find the spare roll, the tissues, and a wrapped soap without opening a cupboard?
8. Whose bathroom is this really: a used one, or a guest one the household stays out of?
9. How many hotel miniatures are you storing, and how many would a two-night guest actually use?
10. Which zone will you do first this week, the counter or the shower?
