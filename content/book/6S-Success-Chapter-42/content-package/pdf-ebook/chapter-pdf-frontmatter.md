# The Guest Bathroom

*The guest bathroom is the one room a visitor spends real time in alone, with the door shut and nothing to look at but your standards, and it is also the room nobody in the house uses, so cleaning it means both fighting the quiet problems of disuse and finishing every surface a guest studies up close. This playbook keeps the wet-room method from the last chapter and gives you, for each of the five zones, the exact inputs to gather and the precise, step-by-step way to clean and shine it, so you run the water, refuse the cast-offs, and stock it for a guest who never asks.*

## How to read this chapter

This is a PLAYBOOK, not a method chapter. It applies the six-S loop you already own to one room, decomposed into five micro-zones, and its centerpiece is the per-zone clean-and-shine instructions with the exact inputs. It takes the wet-room method from Chapter 41 as read; the new material is the guest-bathroom layer (run the water so the traps do not smell; let nothing be demoted in here; clean and stock for a guest who never asks) and the numbered steps for each zone.

Products are named as TYPES, never brands. The exhaustive product list is a separate appendix. Never mix cleaning products; one product per bowl.

The friction meter, which reached its goal at Chapter 30, is retired for Part 9; the chapter closes on a before-and-after room signature.
