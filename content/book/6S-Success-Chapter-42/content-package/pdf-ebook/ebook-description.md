# Chapter 42 Ebook Description: The Guest Bathroom

The twelfth room playbook of Part Nine, and the second wet room. The guest bathroom has two opposite problems at once: it is the room a visitor spends the most concentrated time in, alone behind a shut door, and it is the room nobody in the house uses.

This chapter keeps the wet-room method from Chapter 41 (spray first and let the chemistry work, treat drying as the real job, keep a toilet-only cloth colour) and gives the exact inputs and the numbered steps for each of the room's five zones, with three new rules on top: run the water (a room nobody uses loses the water in its drain traps and starts to smell, so once a month, used or not, run a mug of water down every drain and treat the bowl), no demotion (nothing the household rejected retires here, because if it is not good enough for you it is not good enough for a guest), and clean and stock for a guest who never asks (the spare roll and a wrapped soap found at a glance, and the surfaces a guest studies up close as clean as the ones on show).

You will learn the once-a-month fix for the dry-trap smell no scrubbing cures, the no-demotion rule that keeps the room worthy of the word guest, and how to keep a stranger safe in a room they do not know. The chapter closes on a before-and-after room signature.
