# Chapter 42 Ebook Sales Copy: The Guest Bathroom

## Headline
The room a visitor studies most closely and the household uses least. Here is how to fix its smell, refuse the cast-offs, and stock it for a guest who never asks.

## The promise
The guest bathroom can smell faintly of sewer no matter how hard you clean it, because the cleaning was never the problem. This chapter keeps the wet-room method and gives you the exact inputs and the numbered steps for each of the room's five zones, plus the three rules that make it work: run the water so the dry traps do not smell, let nothing be demoted in here, and clean and stock the room for a guest who studies it closely.

## What you get in this chapter
- The guest-bathroom layer on top of the wet-room method: run the water, no demotion, stock for the shut door.
- The five per-zone clean procedures, each with its inputs and numbered steps: the vanity counter, the vanity storage, the shower or tub, the toilet area, and the guest linen zone.
- The once-a-month run-the-water fix for the dry-trap smell no scrubbing cures.
- The no-demotion rule that keeps a guest bathroom from becoming a storage cupboard, and the good-towels call.
- The stock-it-so-no-one-asks setup, and the safety layer for a stranger who does not know the room.

## The common mistake it fixes
> Only cleaning the guest bathroom the hour before people arrive, so the traps have dried out and it smells however hard you scrub; letting it fill up with the products and towels the household rejected; and cleaning only the surfaces on show while the tap collar, the underside of the seat, and the base of the soap pump, the ones a guest studies up close, stay grimy.
