# Chapter 42 Landing Page Intro: The Guest Bathroom

The guest bathroom is a strange room, because it has two opposite problems at once. It is the room a visitor spends the most concentrated time in of anywhere in your house, alone behind a shut door with nothing to look at but your standards. And it is the room nobody in the house actually uses, which is a different problem entirely: its drain traps lose their water and start to smell, its towels go musty on the shelf, and it quietly fills up with everything the rest of the house rejected.

This chapter breaks the guest bathroom into five small zones and gives you, for each one, the exact inputs to gather and the numbered steps to clean and shine it: the vanity counter, the vanity storage, the shower or tub, the toilet area, and the guest linen zone. It keeps the wet-room method from the last chapter and adds the layer this room needs: run the water so the traps do not smell, let nothing be demoted in here, and clean and stock the room for a guest who will study it closely and never ask you where anything is.

Product types only, no brands. Start at the counter, and put the stored towels in the wash before you begin.
