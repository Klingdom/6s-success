# Chapter 42 Newsletter Short Teaser: The Guest Bathroom

A short digest blurb (about 120 words) with a read-the-chapter CTA.

---

**The Guest Bathroom: run the water, no demotion, ready for a shut door.**

The guest bathroom has two opposite problems at once: it is watched most closely, by a visitor alone behind a shut door, and it is used least, so it goes quietly wrong.

Chapter 42 keeps the wet-room method and gives the exact inputs and steps for the room's five zones, plus the three rules that make it work: run the water so the dry traps do not smell, let nothing the household rejected retire in here, and clean and stock the room for a guest who studies it closely and never asks.
