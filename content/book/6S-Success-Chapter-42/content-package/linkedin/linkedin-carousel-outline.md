# Chapter 42 LinkedIn Carousel Outline: The Guest Bathroom

Eleven slides. Product types only.

- Slide 1 (hook): The room watched most closely and used least.

- Slide 3 (the smell): the sewer note is a dry trap, not dirt. Run the water once a month.
- Slide 4 (five zones): counter, storage, shower, toilet, linen. Wash the linen first.
- Slide 5 (no demotion): nothing the household rejected retires here.
- Slide 6 (the good towels): the guest bundle is the best towels you own.
- Slide 7 (up close): clean the tap collar, seat underside, and soap-pump base a guest studies.
- Slide 8 (stock it): a spare roll and a wrapped soap found at a glance, so no one asks.
- Slide 9 (the grout you cannot clean): re-grout it, do not re-scrub a job that cannot succeed.
- Slide 10 (the brush): a brush is a consumable, not a fixture. Replace it.
