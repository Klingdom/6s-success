# The Guest Bathroom: Run the Water, No Demotion, Ready for a Shut Door

*A LinkedIn article from Chapter 42 of 6S Success: Home Edition.*

The guest bathroom is a strange room, because it has two opposite problems at once. It is the room a visitor spends the most concentrated time in of anywhere in your house, alone behind a shut door with nothing to look at but your standards. And it is the room nobody in the house actually uses, which is a different problem entirely.

> The guest bathroom is the one room a visitor spends real time in alone, with the door shut and nothing to look at but your standards, and it is also the room nobody in the house uses, which is its own problem. So you clean it two ways at once. You clean it to be looked at closely, from the underside of the seat to the chalky ring at the foot of the tap, because a guest behind a shut door sees everything. And you fight the quiet problems of a room that sits unused: the drain traps that lose their water and let the smell in, the towels that go musty on the shelf, and the rejected products the household keeps trying to retire in here. Nothing gets demoted into the guest bathroom, and the water gets run whether anyone has used the room or not.

## Five zones, not one room

> The guest bathroom is five small zones, not one big job: the guest vanity counter, the guest vanity storage, the shower or tub, the toilet area, and the guest linen zone. Do them one at a time, and start at the vanity counter, because it is the first thing anyone sees and the fastest thing to put right.

## The smell is a dry trap

> A bathroom nobody uses has a problem no busy room has: the water sitting in its drain traps slowly evaporates, and an empty trap is an open pipe that lets the drain's smell straight up into the room. That faint sewer note in a guest bathroom is almost never dirt, it is a dry trap.

## Nothing gets demoted in here

> This room quietly becomes the retirement home for everything the household rejected: the conditioner nobody liked, the soap that smells wrong, the towels that went thin, the shampoo left behind two summers ago. But a guest is offered your standard, not your cast-offs, so nothing gets demoted into the guest bathroom.

## Stock it for a shut door

> A guest behind a shut door will not open your cupboards and will not ask you where anything is, so the room has to answer every question before it is asked.
