# Chapter 42 LinkedIn Comment Prompts: The Guest Bathroom

1. Does your guest bathroom have a faint smell you have blamed on cleaning? It might be a dry drain trap.
2. Be honest: what has been demoted into your guest bathroom that would not pass in your own?
3. Do your guests get the good towels, or the thin ones you are quietly saving the nice ones from?
4. When did you last wash the stored guest towels, rather than just keep them folded on the shelf?
5. Is there a grout line you keep scrubbing that never comes clean? It may be time to re-grout it.
