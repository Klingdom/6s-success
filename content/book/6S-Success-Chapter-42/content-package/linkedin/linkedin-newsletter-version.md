# Run the Water, No Demotion, Ready for a Shut Door

*A 6S Success: Home Edition newsletter, from Chapter 42, The Guest Bathroom.*

Hi friend,

This week, the second wet room, and a strange one, because it has two opposite problems at once: it is watched most closely and used least.

**You clean it to be looked at closely, from the underside of the seat to the chalky ring at the foot of the tap, because a guest behind a shut door sees everything. And you fight the quiet problems of a room that sits unused: the drain traps that lose their water and let the smell in, the towels that go musty, and the rejected products the household keeps trying to retire in here.**

The room breaks into five zones, each with its exact inputs and numbered steps: the vanity counter, the vanity storage, the shower or tub, the toilet area, and the guest linen zone.

## To try this week

Fix the smell first. Once a month, used or not, pour a mug of water down the sink, the tub, and the floor drain to refill the drain traps that slowly dry out, and give the bowl a cleaner treatment with full dwell time. A dry trap, not dirt, is what that faint sewer note actually is, and no amount of scrubbing will fix it.
