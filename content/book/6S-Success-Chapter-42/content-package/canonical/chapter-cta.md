# Chapter 42 CTA and Companion Resources: The Guest Bathroom

## Primary call to action
Do one zone this week, the counter or the shower, and give the next visitor a room they can be left alone in without a second thought.

## Companion resources
- **The Guest Bathroom Reset card** (printable and phone wallpaper: the five zones, the done-looks-like for each, and the wash-the-linen-first, run-the-water order of work).
- **The Guest Bathroom Kit list** (printable: the cleaners, tools, and storage types the room needs, grouped by job, with the run-the-water, no-demotion, and latch-the-chemistry notes that matter here).
- **The Guest-Ready in Ten Minutes card** (the fast pass for a surprise arrival: run the water, wipe the counter and seat, put out a fresh towel and a spare roll).

## Register and fences
- Warm, practical, expert, and honest about the word guest. Product types only, never brand names. No hype, no invented statistics or prices. Never advise mixing cleaners.
- Friction meter retired for Part 9; close on the before-and-after room signature.
- Previous: Chapter 41, The Primary Bathroom. Next: Chapter 43, The Laundry Room.

## Words to avoid
- No brand names (product types only). Never suggest mixing bowl cleaner with bleach. Never demote the household's cast-offs into the guest bathroom.
