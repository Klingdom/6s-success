# Chapter 42 Content Package: The Guest Bathroom

The TWELFTH room playbook of Part Nine (Chapters 31 to 50), and the SECOND WET ROOM, built to the instruction-centric template. The chapter CORE is the detailed, step-by-step clean-and-shine instruction for each of the five guest-bathroom zones, with the exact inputs, folded directly from the Micro Zone Manual's Guest Bathroom reset content.

## The chapter in one line
The guest bathroom keeps the wet-room method from Chapter 41 but adds the problems of a room nobody uses: run the water so the dry traps do not smell, let nothing the household rejected retire here, and clean and stock it for a guest alone behind a shut door.

## The five zones
The guest vanity counter, the guest vanity storage, the shower or tub, the toilet area, and the guest linen zone. Each carries an Inputs list, numbered Steps, and an inspect-and-flag list.

## The guest-bathroom layer (wet-room method from Ch 41 plus)
- Judged up close: a visitor alone behind a shut door studies the tap collar, the seat underside, and the soap-pump base, so those are cleaned as well as the surfaces on show.
- Run the water: a room nobody uses loses the water in its drain traps and starts to smell, so once a month run a mug of water down every drain and treat the bowl.
- No demotion: nothing the household rejected retires here; if it is not good enough for you, it leaves the house.
- Stock for the shut door: the spare roll, the tissues, and a wrapped soap found at a glance, so a guest never asks.
