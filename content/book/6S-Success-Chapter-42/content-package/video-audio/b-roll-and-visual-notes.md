# Chapter 42 B-Roll and Visual Notes: The Guest Bathroom

Warm, natural light. Product types only, no brand labels on screen.

## Visual vocabulary
- The two-state opener: a cluttered, stale before and a clear, fresh after.
- The recurring "run the water" motif: a mug of water poured down a drain, refilling the trap seal.

## Core shot list (seven shots)
1. Putting the stored towel bundle into the washing machine before anything else, then starting the counter.
2. Pouring a mug of water down the sink, the tub, and the floor drain to refill the traps.
3. The counter: the tap collar coated and left to dwell, then worked with a folded cloth corner and buffed dry.
4. The vanity storage emptied out, the base wiped and dried so a single fresh drip would show, and a bottle neck wiped.
5. The shower: the grout sprayed and left to dwell, then worked with a stiff brush and rinsed.
6. The toilet: the bowl treated and left, the flush handle wiped, and the floor behind the pedestal done last with a toilet-only cloth.
7. The no-demotion beat: a thin gray towel and a cast-off bottle going into an out bag by the door, not onto the guest shelf.

## On-screen text bank (use sparingly)
- The sewer smell is a dry trap, not dirt.
- Run the water once a month.
- Nothing gets demoted in here.
- Clean the surfaces a guest studies up close.
- Stock it so no one has to ask.

## Lower thirds (rotate)
- Zone 1 to 5, one per segment.
- Do one zone this week.

## Editing notes
- Close each cut on the before-and-after room signature from the doorway (the clear counter, the pink-line-free shower, the fresh towel bundle).
- No presenter required; hands and surfaces only.
