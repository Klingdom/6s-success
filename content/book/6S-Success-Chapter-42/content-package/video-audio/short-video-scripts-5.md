# Chapter 42 Short Video Scripts (5): The Guest Bathroom

Each 30 to 90 seconds. Product types only.

## 1. The smell is a dry trap (40s)
HOOK: Your guest bathroom smells, and cleaning it harder will not fix it.
SCRIPT: A room nobody uses loses the water sitting in its drain traps, and an empty trap lets the drain's smell straight into the room. So once a month, pour a mug of water down the sink, the tub, and the floor drain to refill the seals. You will fix a smell no scrubbing ever could.
ON-SCREEN: The sewer smell is a dry trap, not dirt.
CTA: Chapter 42. Run the water this month.

## 2. Nothing gets demoted in here (35s)
HOOK: Stop demoting your cast-offs into the guest bathroom.
SCRIPT: The conditioner nobody liked, the wrong-smelling soap, the towels that went thin. A guest gets your standard, not your rejects. If it is not good enough for you, it is not good enough for a visitor, so it leaves the house rather than retiring in here.
CTA: Chapter 42, the no-demotion rule.

## 3. Give guests the good towels (30s)
HOOK: You are saving the good towels for guests who never get them.
SCRIPT: Towels thin whether they are used or not, so holding the good ones back means they age untouched while visitors dry off on something gray. The guest bundle is built from the best towels you own.
CTA: Chapter 42, the linen zone.

## 4. Clean the surfaces a guest studies (35s)
HOOK: A guest looks at the surfaces you skip.
SCRIPT: Behind a shut door, they have nothing to do but look, and they look at the collar at the foot of the tap, the underside of the seat, the base of the soap pump. Clean those as well as the ones on show. You are cleaning for someone who looks harder than you do.
CTA: Chapter 42. Clean up close.

## 5. The grout you cannot clean (30s)
HOOK: Stop scrubbing that grout line.
SCRIPT: You have scrubbed it three times and it stayed gray. It is not dirt, it is mould in porous grout or a failed silicone bead, and no product will bring it back. One proper treatment, then re-grout or re-caulk it, and stop spending the weekly clean on a job that cannot succeed.
CTA: Chapter 42. Next: the laundry room.
