# Chapter 42 YouTube Script (6 to 8 min): The Guest Bathroom

Alternating HOST and VISUAL. Product types only.

HOST: The guest bathroom can smell faintly of sewer no matter how hard you clean it, and the cleaning was never the problem. Today we solve that, and clean the second wet room properly.
VISUAL: Two-state opener, cluttered stale before and clear fresh after. A mug of water poured down a drain.

HOST: This room has two opposite problems: it is watched most closely, by a guest alone behind a shut door, and used least. So we keep the wet-room method and add three rules: run the water, no demotion, and stock it for a guest who never asks.
VISUAL: The trap refilled; a cast-off bottle leaving through a door; a shut door with a roll and soap inside.

HOST: Five zones, and you wash the stored towels first, because the linen shelf needs a wash cycle to finish. Then the counter: coat the tap collar and let it dwell, work it with a cloth corner, and buff dry.
VISUAL: Towels into the machine; the tap collar coated and buffed.

HOST: The vanity storage you empty right out, partly to read the trap. Wipe the base and dry it so a single fresh drip would show, and wipe every bottle neck, which is what keeps the cabinet from turning tacky.
VISUAL: The cabinet emptied; the base dried; a bottle neck wiped.

HOST: The shower keeps the spray-and-dwell method: spray the grout, walk away, come back and brush it. And the grout you cannot clean is not dirt, it is failed grout, so it gets re-grouted, not re-scrubbed.
VISUAL: The grout sprayed and left; a stiff brush; a re-caulk line.

HOST: The toilet gets its own cloth colour and the floor behind the pedestal, where the smell lives, done last. And clean the surfaces a guest studies: the seat underside, the base of the soap pump.
VISUAL: The toilet-only cloth; the floor behind the pedestal; the seat underside.

HOST: And nothing gets demoted in here. The thin towels and the wrong soap leave the house, not the guest shelf. Do one zone this week. Next: the laundry room.
VISUAL: A thin towel into an out bag; the before-and-after room signature from the doorway. End card.
