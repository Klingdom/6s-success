# Chapter 42 Teleprompter Script: The Guest Bathroom

Short lines, one breath each. Warm and practical.

The guest bathroom has two opposite problems at once.
It is watched most closely. A visitor, alone, behind a shut door.
And it is used least. Nobody in the house uses it.
So you clean it two ways at once.
First, the smell. It is not dirt.
A room nobody uses loses the water in its drain traps.
And an empty trap lets the drain's smell into the room.
So once a month, pour a mug of water down every drain.
The sink. The tub. The floor drain.
You will fix a smell no scrubbing ever could.
Then, no demotion.
This room fills up with what the household rejected.
The disliked conditioner. The wrong soap. The thin towels.
But a guest gets your standard, not your cast-offs.
If it is not good enough for you, it leaves the house.
The guest towels are the best towels you own.
Then, stock and clean for the shut door.
A guest will not open your cupboards or ask.
So put the roll and the soap where they see them.
And clean the surfaces they study up close.
The tap collar. The seat underside. The pump base.
Do one zone this week.
And give the next visitor a room they can be left alone in.
