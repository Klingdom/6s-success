# Chapter 42 Image Generation Prompts: The Guest Bathroom

Warm, illustrated, flat-color editorial house style (paper #F7F2E9 ground, ink #2B2622 line, terracotta/honey/slate/green accents, Shine green #6E8B5B). No photorealism, no brand names or logos, no text baked into the art beyond the noted labels. Product types only.

## Prompt 1 (opener, two states)
A warm flat-illustration of one guest bathroom shown twice, side by side. Left, labelled "before": a counter cluttered with toothbrushes, a pink line at the shower tray, a bowl with a ring, a bag of hotel miniatures, a shelf of thin gray towels. Right, labelled "after": a clear counter with a soap pump above half, a shower with no pink line, a bowl clean under the rim with a roll in plain sight, and one fresh folded towel bundle.

## Prompt 2 (five zones plan)
A warm overhead floor plan of a guest bathroom, gently shaded, with five numbered and labelled zones: guest vanity counter, guest vanity storage, shower or tub, toilet area, guest linen zone. Caption strip at the foot: "one room, five small jobs, wash the linen first".

## Prompt 3 (method and kit, two panels)
A two-panel warm infographic. Left "the method": a small spray bottle and squeegee for the kept wet-room method, a mug of water poured down a drain refilling a trap seal, a cast-off bottle leaving through a door rather than moving in, and a shut door with a spare roll, tissues, and a wrapped soap visible inside. Right "the kit": labelled type icons for a soap-film cleaner, a neutral pH cleaner, a streak-free glass cleaner, a bowl cleaner and dedicated brush, a mildew remover, a grout brush, a microfiber set with a toilet-only colour, a shower squeegee, two labelled bins, a lockable medicine box, a moisture absorber, and a washing machine.

## Prompt 4 (vanity-storage step ribbon)
A warm step-ribbon titled "how to clean the vanity storage": empty it out, vacuum the corners, wipe and dry the base, read the trap board dry, wipe every bottle neck, set back two labelled bins. The read-the-trap and wipe-the-necks steps highlighted in the Shine green.

## Prompt 5 (before-and-after signature)
A warm matched pair of the guest bathroom, the counter and shower and towel bundle, shot from the doorway, before on the left and after on the right, with a small camera icon and the labels "the before, kept" and "the after, kept as the standard".
