# Chapter 42 Diagram Ideas: The Guest Bathroom

House palette (paper #F7F2E9, panel #FBF7EF, ink #2B2622, terracotta #BC4B2A, honey #DDA63A, slate #3C5A6B, green #6E8B5B for Shine). Warm, illustrated, not photoreal. Product types only, no brands or logos. Spark red reserved only for a true hazard (two cleaners being mixed, crossed out).

## Diagram 1 (opener, figure 1): two states
The guest bathroom before and after, side by side. Before: a counter cluttered with the family's toothbrushes, a pink line at the shower tray, a bowl ring nobody flushed away, a bag of hotel miniatures, a shelf of thin gray demoted towels. After: a clear counter with a scale-free tap and a soap pump above half, a shower with no pink line, a bowl clean under the rim with a roll in plain sight, and one fresh towel bundle that smells of nothing at all.

## Diagram 2 (figure 2): the five zones plan
A warm overhead plan, five numbered zones marked and named: guest vanity counter, guest vanity storage, shower or tub, toilet area, guest linen zone. Footer: "one room, five small jobs, wash the linen first".

## Diagram 3 (figure 3): the Shine method and kit
Two panels. Left, the method: keep the wet-room method (a spray bottle and a squeegee, small); run the water (a mug of water poured down a drain, refilling the trap seal); no demotion (a cast-off bottle leaving the house through a door, not moving in); stock for the shut door (a shut door with a spare roll, tissues, and a wrapped soap visible inside). Right, the kit: a bathroom soap-film cleaner, a neutral pH cleaner, a streak-free glass cleaner, a bowl cleaner and dedicated brush, a mildew remover, a grout brush, a microfiber set with a toilet-only colour, a shower squeegee, two labelled bins, a lockable medicine box, a moisture absorber, and a washing machine. Footer: "run the water, no demotion, stock for the shut door".

## Diagram 4 (no HTML twin): the guest-bathroom layer
A standalone visual: run the water, no demotion, stock for the shut door. Names the next chapter, the laundry room.

## Diagram 5 (no HTML twin): how to clean the vanity storage
A step ribbon: empty it out, vacuum the corners, wipe and dry the base so a drip would show, read the trap board dry, wipe every bottle neck, set back two labelled bins. Highlight the read-the-trap and wipe-the-necks steps.

## Diagram 6 (before/after signature) and the photo brief
The Part 9 signature: a matched before-and-after pair of the counter, the shower, and the towel bundle, from the doorway. **Best before-and-after photo brief:** stand in the doorway and frame the counter, the shower, and the fresh towel bundle together in one shot; same spot, angle, and light, before and after; do not tidy before the before (Ch 6); keep the after as the ideal-state standard (Ch 25) the room matches before the next arrival. The retired friction meter is not drawn.
