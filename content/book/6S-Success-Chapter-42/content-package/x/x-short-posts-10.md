# X Short Posts: The Guest Bathroom

Chapter 42 of *6S Success: Home Edition*. Ten standalone posts, each at or under 280 characters counting its number line. Plain numbering.

---

1/
That faint sewer smell in a guest bathroom is almost never dirt, it is a dry drain trap. A room nobody uses loses the water in its traps. Once a month, pour a mug of water down every drain to refill the seals.

(212 chars)

---

2/
Your guest bathroom quietly becomes the home for the conditioner nobody liked and the towels that went thin. But a guest gets your standard, not your cast-offs. Nothing gets demoted in here; it leaves the house.

(214 chars)

---

3/
Every house saves the nice towels and gives guests the worn ones. But towels thin whether used or not, so the good set ages untouched while visitors dry off on gray. The guest bundle is the best towels you own.

(213 chars)

---

4/
A guest behind a shut door has nothing to do but look, and they look at the surfaces you skip: the tap collar, the underside of the seat, the base of the soap pump. Clean those as well as the ones on show.

(208 chars)

---

5/
A guest will not open your cupboards or ask where anything is. So stock the room so a visitor finds the spare roll, the tissues, and a wrapped soap at a glance, and never has to knock on your door.

(200 chars)

---

6/
Wash the stored guest towels hot even though nobody has touched them, because a towel folded all season carries a musty note the instant it gets wet. Put them in the machine before you start on the room.

(206 chars)

---

7/
You have scrubbed one grout line three times and it stayed gray. It is not dirt, it is mould in porous grout or a failed silicone bead. Stop scrubbing a job that cannot succeed: re-grout or re-caulk it.

(205 chars)

---

8/
The toilet brush in a guest bathroom is the object the household agreed not to look at: splayed bristles, standing in gray water. A brush is a consumable, not a fixture. Splayed bristles means replace it.

(207 chars)

---

9/
In the room a visiting child is sent to alone, latch the bowl cleaner, bleach, and drain opener away, and never use an acid cleaner after bleach in a small closed room, because together they make a gas.

(205 chars)

---

10/
A guest bathroom is not clean because you tidied it before people came. It is clean because you ran the water, refused the cast-offs, cleaned up close, and stocked it. Chapter 42. Next . Ch 43, The Laundry Room.

(215 chars)
