# Prompt 07 — Build the Quest and Time-Boxing Engine

## OBJECTIVE

Convert potentially dozens of opportunities into achievable sessions.

Support:

- 15 minutes
- 30 minutes
- 45 minutes
- 60 minutes
- 90 minutes
- custom duration

Support 1-10 players.

## ACTIVITY SEQUENCING

Prefer:

1. safety
2. remove obvious waste
3. relocate obvious misplaced objects
4. sort
5. clean empty surfaces
6. organize
7. label
8. define standard
9. record after condition
10. assign sustain task

Respect dependencies.

For example:

Do not recommend wiping a drawer before the drawer is emptied if emptying is required.

## QUEST OPTIMIZATION

Rank activities using factors such as:

- safety impact
- functional impact
- visual impact
- estimated duration
- dependencies
- supplies available
- user preferences
- number of players
- prior completion
- frustration reduction
- likelihood of finishing

## PARALLELIZATION

For multiple users identify independent workstreams.

Example:

Player A:
Sort shoes.

Player B:
Remove mail and paper from console.

Player C:
Vacuum entry floor once objects are cleared.

Do not assign dependent activities simultaneously.

## DELIVERABLES

Implement:

- Quest
- QuestTask
- dependency graph
- time budget optimizer
- multiplayer assignment
- progress tracking
- skip/defer/reassign behavior
- completion logic
- tests for sequencing and time fit
