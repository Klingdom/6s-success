# Newsletter Preview Text (Preheader): Safety: Protect People First

Ten options, each 35 to 90 characters, each paired with a subject line. Char counts noted.

1. Pairs with #1 (Ask not whether it is tidy, but whether it could hurt someone.)
   "The one question the first three S's never ask." (47 chars)

2. Pairs with #2 (Protect people first.)
   "Fix what could hurt someone worst and soonest, before anything else." (68 chars)

3. Pairs with #3 (A clean home is not a safe home)
   "Cleared, arranged, spotless, and still holding every danger it started with." (76 chars)

4. Pairs with #4 (Most hazards are perfectly tidy)
   "A hazard does not announce itself the way a mess does." (54 chars)

5. Pairs with #5 (The safety lens: one new question for the whole home)
   "Could this hurt someone? A question decides what you are able to see." (68 chars)

6. Pairs with #6 (Safety is love, not fear)
   "Handle the few real dangers once, so you can stop carrying them as worry." (73 chars)

7. Pairs with #7 (What lets a parent breathe out)
   "Safety done well is quiet: it is what lets a parent breathe out." (63 chars)

8. Pairs with #8 (Fix the one thing most likely to hurt someone, today)
   "Walk one room, find the single worst danger, and fix it today." (62 chars)

9. Pairs with #9 (Why isn't a tidy home always a safe home?)
   "Tidy and safe are different questions, and the gap can be dangerous." (67 chars)

10. Pairs with #10 (How to make your home safe, without living in fear)
    "This opens Safety, the fourth S, the plus one added to 5S." (58 chars)
