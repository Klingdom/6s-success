# Newsletter Short Teaser: Safety: Protect People First

For a roundup or digest. 90 to 140 words.

---

Your home is cleared, arranged, and kept clean and read, and it could still send someone to the hospital tonight. A home can be spotless and still hold every danger it started with, because tidy and safe are different questions. This chapter opens Safety, the fourth S, and hands you one new question to ask of the whole home: not is it tidy, but could it hurt someone. That is the safety lens. Because you cannot fix everything at once, you protect people first, ranking hazards by how badly and how soon they could hurt someone and fixing the worst and soonest first. Handle the few real dangers, and you trade worry for quiet. The friction meter takes its first Safety step.
