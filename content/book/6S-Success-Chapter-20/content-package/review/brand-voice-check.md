# Brand Voice Check: Chapter 20 Content Package

The voice of *6S Success: Home Edition* is warm, plain-spoken, and quietly confident. Chapter 20 is the FIRST chapter of Part Six and OPENS Safety, the fourth of the six S's, the "plus one" that American practice added to 5S. Its job is to turn from the state of the room to the people in it, so the register must be calm, loving attention, not alarm: not "your home is a minefield," but "look at the finished home through one new question, could this hurt someone, and handle the few real dangers worst and soonest first." Because this is a part opener that touches a subject people feel judged by, the voice must stay anti-shame (a home full of unnoticed hazards is the normal case, because adults habituate to their own space, not because anyone was negligent), hold the claim honest (Safety has only begun; a first high-value pass, not a thorough hunt), and keep the goal honestly ahead (a thorough hazard hunt, then Standardize and Sustain, still to come).

## Voice fingerprint (from the authored manuscript and final HTML)
- **Warm and personal.** Talks to "you," stands the reader in the home built across the book, and pictures ordinary, specific hazards: the rug curling at the top of the stairs, medicine at a toddler's eye level, the smoke alarm quietly dead since its battery gave out last spring, cleaning bottles at a child's height, a pan handle turned over the edge of the stove.
- **Concrete over abstract.** The idea is taught through the two-lenses image (the identical room asked "Is it tidy?" then "Could this hurt someone?"), the triage of what to fix today versus what waits, and a single slow walk that latches a cabinet, adds a battery, and tapes down a rug.
- **Anti-shame.** "Not because you did anything wrong. Because nothing you have done so far was ever aimed at that." The hazards "passed every test the first three S's could give, and they were dangerous the whole time." The failure is a question never asked, not a character flaw.
- **Calm, loving attention (the register distinct from earlier chapters).** "Safety is love, not fear." The fix is one new question and a short triage, not vigilance or dread: "It is what lets a parent breathe out."
- **Honest about the milestone and the meter.** The needle takes a real but small first Safety step and the chapter says so plainly: "The step is real and, you will notice, it is small, and both of those are true on purpose."

## Specific wins
- "Ask not whether it is tidy, but whether it could hurt someone." is the chapter's hero rule (frozen) and travels intact across channels (grep-confirmed present throughout the package, byte-verbatim on the quote cards).
- "Protect people first: fix what could hurt someone worst and soonest, before anything else." (frozen triage) gives the whole method a clean, quotable form (grep-confirmed verbatim in the quote set, the CTA file, and the cards).
- "A cabinet a child can open is a catastrophe waiting for a quiet afternoon." names the tidy-versus-safe gap in one line (grep-confirmed present in the manuscript and quote set).
- "A safe home is not a fearful one. It is the one where you have already done the worrying, once, with your hands, so you do not have to keep doing it with your mind." delivers the emotional core and lands as a shareable turn (grep-confirmed in the manuscript and quote set).

## Voice risks to watch
- **Hero vocabulary is dense.** "safety," "hazard," "home," "hurt," "danger," "lens" recur because they are the subject; expected, not a tic, but vary the surrounding language when excerpting, and watch "worst," "soonest," "first," "worry," and "tidy" as the payoff words most likely to stack.
- **CTA sameness.** Rotate the LinkedIn and X sign-offs; several close on "Read it free online" / "Read the free chapter."
- **Keep the register calm love, not alarm.** The chapter touches a job people feel judged by (a home that could hurt someone), so any asset must not tip from "handle the few real dangers and set the worry down" into fear or guilt. The frozen framing keeps it anti-shame (a home full of unnoticed hazards is the normal case; adults habituate to their own space) and keeps the goal honestly ahead (Safety opens here, but the thorough hunt, Standardize, and Sustain still follow).
- **Part-opener pull.** This is the chapter that opens an S and calls itself "the most important S in the book," so watch that the importance note stays quiet and honest and never becomes hype or an alarmist pitch; and keep Heinrich's Principle a calm reason to act now, never a threat, with its ratios not overclaimed. Held in the reviewed assets: the step is repeatedly told as real AND small, and the danger is handled, not dramatized.

## Banned-language audit (scans actually run, LC_ALL=C, across the content-package files)
- **Widened dash scan** (em dash, en dash, and spaced-hyphen " - " used as punctuation, excluding markdown list bullets): em **0**, en **0**, spaced-hyphen-as-punctuation **0**. The single raw " - " match in the package (`workflow/production-checklist.md` line 9) is inside a quoted description of the dash rule itself ("no ... spaced-hyphen \" - \" separator"), not a dash in use. - **Hype-word scan** (unlock, leverage, elevate, supercharge, seamless, game-changing, "almost magical", "fast-paced", sparkling, miracle, "dive in", "in conclusion"): **0** in all files.
- **Force / violence scan:** "attack" / "blitz" / "blast" / "purge" / "war on" tokens appear only across six editor-note files (`canonical/chapter-cta.md`, `graphics/diagram-ideas.md`, `graphics/image-generation-prompts.md`, `video-audio/b-roll-and-visual-notes.md`, `workflow/production-checklist.md`, `workflow/repurposing-map.md`), each a **NEGATED production/scope prohibition** of the form "Nothing is attacked, blitzed, blasted, waged war on, or purged; a few real dangers are simply handled" / "no war on hazards." None appears in any reader-facing asset as a metaphor in use. Not a voice violation; recorded for full honesty.
- **Alarmist-safety check:** no alarmist injury statistics, no graphic injury, and Heinrich's Principle is used as a calm reason to act now, not as a threat, with its ratios not overclaimed. Safety-is-love-not-fear register held throughout.
- **Brand / product-endorsement scan:** **0** brand-name products. The chapter says "a cabinet latch," "grip tape," "a fresh battery," never a brand.

## Verdict
Voice is consistent and intact across all eleven channels, and the calm, loving-attention, anti-shame, protect-people-first job of Chapter 20 is honored throughout. The part-opener importance note ("the most important S in the book") is present without hype or alarm, safety reads as love not fear, and the goal is kept honestly ahead. Approve the voice. The chapter itself still needs author sign-off, since it was authored in the book voice (grounded in the 6S source, "Principles of 6S: Safety Step," the 5S+1 history, the hazard definition, and Heinrich's Principle) rather than supplied from a verbatim source file.
