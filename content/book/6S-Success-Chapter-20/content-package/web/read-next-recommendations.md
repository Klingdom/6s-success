# Read Next Recommendations: Chapter 20, Safety: Protect People First

1. Chapter 21, Find and Remove Hazards
   The next step, and where Safety gets thorough. Chapter 20 changed the question and handled the worst few dangers today; this chapter takes the safety lens on a calm, room-by-room walk through the whole home to find and remove the hazards a first pass always misses. It builds the systematic hazard hunt and the full safety-questions checklist, so the lens you just picked up becomes a complete search. Chapter 20 opens Safety; Chapter 21 makes it thorough.

2. Chapter 19, The 15-Minute Reset
   The setup, and the marker just behind the needle. Sort cleared the home, Straighten arranged it, and Shine made the whole of it clean, read, and self-keeping on a rhythm, completing the third S at the Shine-complete milestone. Safety turns from that finished home to the people in it, because a home can be cleared, arranged, and spotless and still hold every danger it started with. Read how the reset closed Shine, then see why a clean home is still not a safe home.

3. Protect people first, today (the chapter action)
   Walk one room and find the single thing most likely to seriously hurt someone, the unlatched medicine, the rug that slides at the top of the stairs, the smoke alarm with a dead battery, and fix that one thing today. You just protected people first. It is the smallest possible proof of the whole idea.

4. The Safety Lens card (free resource)
   A printable and phone wallpaper carrying the one question, could this hurt someone, and the protect people first triage, worst and soonest first, so the whole idea is always in reach.

5. The Protect People First worksheet (free resource)
   A printable where you list the hazards you spot, rank each by how badly and how soon it could hurt someone, and fix from the top down, so a home full of findings becomes a short, ordered list instead of an overwhelming one.
