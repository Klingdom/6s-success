2. Protect People First (20)

   Angle: opens with the reframe the whole part rests on, the anti-shame insight that tidy and safe are different questions. Answers a real search for why a tidy home can still be dangerous.

4. Ask Whether It Could Hurt Someone (33)
   Angle: leads with the frozen rule, the safety lens as the device. Answers a real search for how to spot the dangers you have walked past for years.

5. How to Make Your Home Safe Without Fear (39)
