# Chapter 34 Content Package: The Dining Room

The FOURTH room playbook of Part Nine (Chapters 31 to 50) of *6S Success: Home Edition*, built to the instruction-centric template proven in Chapters 31 to 33. The chapter CORE is the detailed, step-by-step clean-and-shine instruction for each of the five dining-room zones, with the exact inputs.

## The chapter in one line
The dining room is a room of good finishes and things that matter, so you clean it gently: match the touch to the finish, lift and read the marks beneath every object, keep water off wood (damp then dry, in one motion), and hand-wash the good china.

## The five zones
The dining table, the sideboard surface, the sideboard storage, the china or display cabinet, and the beverage or coffee station. Each carries an Inputs list, numbered Steps in order, and an inspect-and-flag list.

## The dining-room Shine method (nine rules from Chapter 31 plus the dining layer)
- Match the touch to the finish: gentle, nothing harsh on wood, veneer, gilt, or glass.
- Lift every object and clean the surface itself, reading the marks beneath.
- On wood, damp then dry, in one motion, and never a wet surface left to sit.
- Hand-wash the good china in a towel-lined basin, never the machine for a gilt rim.
- Clean to inspect and flag; the safety pass fixes (the loose hinge, the sagging cabinet, the frayed cord).
