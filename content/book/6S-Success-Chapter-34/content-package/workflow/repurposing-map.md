# Chapter 34 Repurposing Map: The Dining Room

## Spine (the how-to-clean value, in priority order)
1. The dining-room Shine method: match the touch to the finish, lift and read, damp then dry on wood, hand-wash the good china.
2. The zone-by-zone centerpiece: the exact inputs and numbered steps for the table, the sideboard surface, the sideboard storage, the china cabinet, and the drinks station.
3. Clean-to-inspect-and-flag: the loose hinge, the heat bloom, the frayed strap, found on the way.
4. The calls that matter: the table-as-desk, and the good things that carry memory.
