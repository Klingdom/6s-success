3. Clean one dining-room zone to the method this weekend (the chapter action)
   Before you close the book, pick one zone of the dining room and clean it to the method, start to finish. Gather the inputs into your caddy first, and keep the two rules above every wooden surface, gentle, and dry, then lift every object to reach the ring beneath, wipe damp then dry so no water sits on the wood, and hand-wash the good things rather than trusting them to the machine. Start with the table for the biggest visible win, or the cabinet if the good china has been nagging at you. It is the smallest possible proof of the whole idea, that a dining room is not clean because the table is wiped, it is clean because you lifted every object to reach the ring beneath it, matched a gentle touch to each finish, hand-washed the good china, and flagged the loose hinge and the heat bloom you found on the way.
