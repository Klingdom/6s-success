That is why this chapter, like the three before it, is mostly about exactly how to clean each zone gently, with the precise inputs. And there is one governing principle here, said up front: match the touch to the surface, keep it gentle, and above all keep water off wood, so on wood you go damp then dry in one motion and never leave a wet surface to sit. The reason a whole dining room feels like too much to face is that you are looking at it as one job you have half given up on, and it is not: it is five small zones wearing a single name, and the trick to all of Part 9 is to stop seeing the room and start seeing the zones.
