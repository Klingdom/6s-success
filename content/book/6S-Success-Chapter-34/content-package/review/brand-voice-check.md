# Chapter 34 Brand Voice Check: The Dining Room

## Voice fingerprint
Warm, plain, confident, and here a touch more tender than the working rooms, because the dining room holds heirlooms and the good china. The register is caring: you look after this room rather than scrub it. The governing metaphor is gentleness and finish care, matched to the surface, and it holds across every asset.
