## In-chapter actions a reader can take (from the callouts)
- **Quick Win:** Do the table properly, not just the top where the plates sat. Clear it to bare wood, lift the centerpiece to get the ring beneath, then run a cloth wrapped over a fingernail down the leaf seam and along the apron rail where hands grip, wiping damp then drying at once. Finish by pushing the chairs out and vacuuming the footprint. Twenty minutes, and the room the house gathers in is ready for company.
- **6S Tip:** Match the cleaner to the finish and never leave water on wood: a barely damp cloth dried at once on sealed wood, streak-free glass cleaner sprayed onto the cloth and not the glass so nothing runs behind a frame, and the good china washed by hand in a basin, never the dishwasher for a gilt rim or hand painting. When in doubt, go gentler and drier.
- **Family Challenge:** Find the one activity that lands on the table more than three evenings a week, the homework, the laptop, or the craft, and give it a container and a home of its own off the table, because no table standard survives an activity with nowhere else to be. Then everyone agrees: whatever arrives on the table for a session leaves with it.
