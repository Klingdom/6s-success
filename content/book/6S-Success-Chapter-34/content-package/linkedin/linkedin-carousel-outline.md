# Chapter 34 LinkedIn Carousel Outline: The Dining Room

Twelve slides. Warm house style, product types only, no brands.

- **Slide 1 (hook):** You would never scrub your kitchen the way you should clean your dining room. Here is the difference.
- **Slide 2:** The dining room is not a working room. It is full of good finishes and things that matter: a wood table, a glass cabinet, the china that is really a person.
- **Slide 3 (the rule):** Match the touch to the finish. A cloth only just damp on sealed wood, dried at once. The good china by hand, never the machine. Nothing harsh on veneer, gilt, or glass.
- **Slide 4 (five zones):** Five small zones, not one big job: the table, the sideboard surface, the sideboard storage, the china cabinet, the drinks station. One at a time.
- **Slide 5 (lift and read):** Lift every object and clean the surface itself. The marks that tell the room's story, a cold-glass ring, a heat bloom, a woodworm pinhole, only show on the bare wood beneath.
- **Slide 6 (keep water off wood):** Damp then dry, in one motion. The ring you leave tonight is the ring you cannot lift next year.
- **Slide 7 (the table):** Clean the whole piece, not just the top: the leaf seam, the tacky apron rail, the chair rungs, the footprint. Lift the centerpiece for the ring beneath.
- **Slide 8 (the good china):** Wash by hand in a towel-lined basin, dry at once, set rim up. Then read the cabinet: cracked shelf, bent pin, sagging hinge, frayed strap.
- **Slide 9 (safety):** A loaded glass cabinet is the biggest tip-over risk in the house. Strap it to a wall stud, weight on the bottom shelf, no stack over eight plates.
- **Slide 10 (the table-as-desk):** If an activity lands on the table more than three evenings a week, it needs a home of its own off the table. No table standard survives an activity with nowhere else to be.
- **Slide 11 (the standard):** A finished dining room is one you could seat guests in on ten minutes' notice.
- **Slide 12 (CTA):** Do one zone this weekend, the table or the sideboard. Full numbered steps in Chapter 34 of 6S Success: Home Edition. Next: the living room.
