# Chapter 34 LinkedIn Comment Prompts

Five prompts to seed discussion under the Chapter 34 posts. Warm, practical, no hype.

1. Be honest: has your dining table quietly become the office, the homework desk, or the craft bench? What lives on it right now that is not a meal?
2. What is the one good thing in your dining room you are most afraid of damaging, the wood table, the inherited china, the silver? What has stopped you cleaning it properly?
3. Have you ever left a water ring or a heat mark on a wooden surface you could not lift afterward? What did you learn?
4. Does your china cabinet sit strapped to the wall, or free-standing and loaded? If you have small children, this one matters.
5. What would it take for your dining room to be ready to seat guests on ten minutes' notice? What is the gap?
