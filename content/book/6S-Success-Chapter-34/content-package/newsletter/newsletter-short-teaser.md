# Chapter 34 Newsletter Short Teaser

A short digest blurb (about 120 words) for a roundup newsletter, with a read-the-chapter CTA.

---

**The Dining Room: the room you clean gently.**

The kitchen you scrub. The dining room you do not, because it is full of good finishes and things that matter: a wood table, a glass cabinet, the china that is really a person. Clean it like a kitchen and you do quiet, expensive damage.

Chapter 34 gives the exact inputs and numbered steps for each of the room's five zones, matching a gentle touch to each finish. The one rule to carry: on wood, damp then dry, in one motion, and never a wet surface left to sit, because the ring you leave tonight is the ring you cannot lift next year.

[Read the chapter] and gather in a room that is ready for company.
