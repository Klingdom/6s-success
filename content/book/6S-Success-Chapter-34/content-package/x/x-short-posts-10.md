# X Short Posts: The Dining Room

Chapter 34 of *6S Success: Home Edition*. Ten standalone posts, each at or under 280 characters counting its number line. Plain numbering.
---

1/
The dining room is not a working room. You do not scrub it, you care for it. The touch is gentle: a cloth only just damp on sealed wood, dried at once, the good china by hand, nothing harsh on veneer, gilt, or glass.

(219 chars)

---

2/
The rule on wood: damp then dry, in one motion. A barely damp cloth to lift the mark, a dry one straight behind it, and never a wet surface left to sit. The ring you leave tonight is the ring you cannot lift next year.

(221 chars)

---

3/
Clean a dining room to read it. Lift every candlestick and frame rather than dusting around it. The marks worth finding, a cold-glass ring, a heat bloom, a woodworm pinhole, only show on the bare wood underneath.

(215 chars)

---

4/
Clean the whole table, not just the top: the leaf seam, the tacky apron rail, the chair rungs, the footprint. Lift the centerpiece for the ring beneath. Twenty minutes, and the room is ready for company.

(206 chars)

---

5/
The good china is a person. Wash it by hand in a towel-lined basin, dry at once, set it rim up. It outlasts you only if the cabinet does, so read the case: cracked shelf, bent pin, sagging hinge, frayed strap.

(212 chars)

---

6/
Small children? A loaded glass china cabinet is the biggest tip-over risk in the house. Strap it to a wall stud, weight on the bottom shelf, no plate stack over eight.

(170 chars)

---

7/
If an activity lands on the table more than three evenings a week, it is not clutter, it is an activity with nowhere else to be, and no table standard will ever survive it. Give it a home off the table.

(205 chars)

---

8/
Match the touch to the finish: streak-free glass cleaner sprayed onto the cloth, not the glass, so nothing runs behind a frame. A plastic card, not a blade, to lift candle wax. When in doubt, gentler and drier.

(213 chars)

---

9/
Five small zones, not one big job: the table, the sideboard surface, the sideboard storage, the china cabinet, the drinks station. One at a time. Start with the table for the biggest visible win.

(198 chars)

---

10/
A dining room is not clean because the table is wiped. It is clean because you lifted every object, matched a gentle touch to each finish, and hand-washed the good china. Chapter 34. Next · Chapter 35, The Living Room.
