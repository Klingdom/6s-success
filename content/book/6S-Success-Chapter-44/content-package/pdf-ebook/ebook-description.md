# Chapter 44 Ebook Description: The Home Office

The fourteenth room playbook of Part Nine.

Nothing in a home office is heavy. Nothing in it is dirty. By every measure that made the kitchen, the bathroom, and the laundry room hard, it should be the easiest room in the house, and it is almost always the one left longest. The reason is that it is not made of objects. It is made of decisions nobody has taken, and an unresolved decision is heavier than any box.

This chapter breaks the room into six zones and gives, for each, the inputs to gather and the exact order of steps to clean and shine it: the primary desk, the desk drawers and pedestal, the file storage, the bookshelf and reference zone, the printer and scanning station, and the supply cabinet. On top of the nine rules of Shine it adds four of its own, including the one that makes it unlike any other chapter in the book: here the damp cloth is the risk rather than the remedy, because everything in the room is either electronics or paper.

It also names the thing nobody says out loud about this room. It became the household's holding pen because it has a door that closes, and a closed door makes a decision disappear from view without ever being made.
