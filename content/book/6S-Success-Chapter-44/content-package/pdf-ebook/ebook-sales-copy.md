# Chapter 44 Ebook Sales Copy: The Home Office

**Your home office is not dirty. It is undecided.**

Chapter 44 of 6S Success: Home Edition takes the six-S loop into the room that defeats more people than any other, and explains why: it is not made of objects, it is made of decisions nobody has taken.

Inside:

- The six zones of a home office, with the exact inputs and the numbered steps for each, from the underside lip of the desk to the shelf-pin holes in the supply cabinet.
- The one move that unlocks the room: batching the decisions instead of taking them one item at a time, with the cable drawer worked end to end as the example.
- Why the desk is the only zone in the book that is also a tool, and why nothing else in the room can be done properly until it is clear.
- The rule that saves your equipment in the one room where a damp cloth is the hazard rather than the remedy.
- The three-way paper split, and the reprint test that finally clears the middle band of a filing drawer.
- The six hard calls: the half-finished thing, the cable bag, what you are actually required to keep, the shelf that says who you used to be, the backup printer, and the deal you got.
- A safety pass built for a room where everything looks light, and light gets mistaken for safe.

Product types, never brands. Six zones, four new rules, and a standard you check tonight rather than next season.
