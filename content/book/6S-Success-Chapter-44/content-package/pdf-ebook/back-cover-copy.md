# Chapter 44 Back Cover Copy: The Home Office

*Your home office is not dirty. It is undecided.*

Nothing in it is heavy. Nothing in it is dirty. And it is still the room you have left the longest, because it is not made of objects. It is made of decisions nobody has taken, and an unanswered question is the heaviest thing a house can hold.

Six zones. Four rules on top of the nine. The one move that unlocks a room full of postponed judgments. And the reason the damp cloth that rescued every other room is the one thing that will ruin this one.

**6S Success: Home Edition**, Chapter Forty-Four.
