# Chapter 44 Reader Discussion Questions: The Home Office

1. What is the oldest undecided thing in your home office, and what question has it been waiting for you to answer?
2. Which of the four batching jobs would take you longest: the cable drawer, the bookshelf, the supply count, or the filing drawer?
3. Is your desk's clear state a photograph, or is it currently a matter of opinion in your household?
4. What is in your office right now that is not office work, and how did it get there?
5. Which book on your shelf stands for a version of you that you have not formally retired?
6. What paperwork are you keeping that the issuer would simply reprint on request?
7. Have you ever damaged a device by cleaning it, and what would you do differently now?
8. If you reset the desk tonight rather than tomorrow morning, what would have to change?
