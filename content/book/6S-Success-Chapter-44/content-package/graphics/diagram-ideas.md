# Chapter 44 Diagram Ideas: The Home Office

1. **The weight of an undecided thing** (hero): two scales side by side. On one, a heavy box labelled "a full filing box". On the other, a single cable labelled "one cable you have not ruled on", and the cable side is lower. Caption: an unresolved decision is heavier than any box.
2. **One at a time against in one batch**: two timelines for the same cable drawer. The first shows nine sessions and no finish; the second shows one sitting and a finish. Caption: made one item at a time, it never gets made at all.
3. **The desk as a tool**: the six-zone plan with arrows from the other five zones all pointing onto the desk. Caption: the only zone in the book that is also equipment.
4. **Spray the cloth, not the thing**: two panels of a screen being cleaned, one with the bottle aimed at the glass and a drip running into the bezel seam, crossed out; one with the bottle aimed at a folded cloth, ticked.
5. **The three-way paper split**: permanent originals to a closed fireproof box, tax paper to one folder per year, and everything else through the reprint test. Caption: information does not get a folder.
6. **The reach map**: a shelf unit in elevation with bands marked, working reference at seated eye level, occasional reading at standing height, archive highest, heavy binders lowest.
7. **Taped inside the drawer**: the top drawer open, with the cleared-desk photo taped to the inside of the drawer front. Caption: the only standard in the book you check every evening.
