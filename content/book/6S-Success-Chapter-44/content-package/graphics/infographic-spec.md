# Chapter 44 Infographic Spec: The Home Office

## Figure 2: the six zones
- Canvas 1000 x 440, warm paper fill.
- Title in Fraunces, 22px: "The home office, drawn as its six zones".
- Six panels, two rows of three, each 270 x 130, rounded 8, panel fill with a rule-coloured stroke.
- Each panel carries a green numbered disc and a serif zone name: 1 Primary desk, 2 Drawers and pedestal, 3 File storage, 4 Bookshelf, 5 Printer station, 6 Supply cabinet.
- Footer in terracotta: the desk is the workbench the other five zones get emptied onto.

## Figure 3: the method and the kit
- Canvas 1000 x 480, two panels of 460 x 410.
- Left panel headed "The method": four rules, each a coloured lead line with grey gloss lines beneath. Lead lines: "Judgments, not lifts" (terracotta), "Here the wet cloth is the risk" (slate), "Clear one surface first" (green), "The door that closes" (terracotta).
- Right panel headed "The kit": nine lines of product types grouped by job, with the screen-safe electronics cleaner on the first line as this room's distinguishing input.
- Footer in terracotta: batch the decisions, spray the cloth, clear one surface, close the door on nothing.

## Figure 4: the signature
- Canvas 1000 x 430, matched before and after panels with a slate camera disc between them.
- The after panel carries an extra honey-outlined label reading "taped inside the top drawer", and the caption line beneath reads "the after, matched every evening". This is the variation unique to this chapter.
