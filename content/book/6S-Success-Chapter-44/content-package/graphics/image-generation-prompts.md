# Chapter 44 Image Generation Prompts: The Home Office

House style: warm paper background, editorial illustration, serif display type, muted terracotta, honey, slate, and green accents. No logos, no brand names, no readable screen or packaging text, no QR codes. No em dashes in any rendered text.

1. **Opener, two states.** A home office drawn twice side by side. Left: a desk buried under papers and a fringe of sticky notes around the monitor, a power strip on the floor with cables crossing the chair's path, an open drawer of tangled cables, a broken lamp in the corner. Right: the same room with a clear desk, monitor and keyboard squared, one notebook and one tray, the strip hooked up on the desk leg, and cables run and labelled.
2. **The six zones.** A warm overhead floor plan of a small home office with six numbered areas marked and labelled in a serif face.
3. **The cable batch.** Hands emptying a whole drawer of cables and adapters onto a cleared desk in one heap, mid-sort, with three small coiled and banded keepers set aside.
4. **Spray the cloth.** A close editorial pair: a spray bottle aimed at a monitor with a drip running into the bezel seam, and beside it the same bottle misting a folded microfiber cloth instead.
5. **The filing morning.** A person sitting on the floor beside an open filing drawer with three labelled piles, a closed document box, and a shredder alongside. Calm and methodical, not chaotic.
6. **The reach map.** A bookshelf in elevation with a seated figure at a desk beside it, showing which band of shelf sits at seated eye level.
7. **Taped inside the drawer.** A close crop of an open top drawer with a small printed photograph of a cleared desk taped to the inside of the drawer front.
