# Chapter 44 Facebook Posts: The Home Office

Five standalone posts.

## 1
Your home office is not dirty. It is undecided. Nothing in it is heavy, nothing in it is dirty, and that is exactly why it has beaten you for years. It is not made of objects. It is made of decisions nobody has taken.

## 2
The drawer of cables survives because each cable is too cheap to think about and too specific to throw away. Do not sort it. Empty the whole lot at once, name the device each one powers, and point at where that device is in this house. No device, no keep.

## 3
This is the one room where the damp cloth is the hazard, not the remedy. Everything here is electronics or paper. Power it down, spray onto the cloth and never onto the screen, and use a dry cloth on toner dust, because water turns loose toner into ink.

## 4
Most of your filing drawer is neither obviously permanent nor obviously rubbish, and that middle band is why the job stalls. Try this test: if the issuer will reprint it on request, or it already lives in an online account, it is information, not a record. Information does not get a folder.

## 5
Everything in your office that is not office work is there for one reason: this is the room with a door that closes, and shutting a door makes a decision disappear from view without ever being made.
