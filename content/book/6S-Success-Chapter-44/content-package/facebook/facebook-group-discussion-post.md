# Chapter 44 Facebook Group Discussion Post: The Home Office

**Question for the group: what is the oldest undecided thing in your home office right now?**

Not the messiest. The oldest *undecided*. The box you have not opened since the move. The project in the corner. The bag of cables.

Here is why I ask. The home office is the room where nothing is heavy and nothing is dirty, and that is exactly why it defeats people. It is not made of objects, it is made of decisions nobody has taken, and an unanswered question is the heaviest thing a house can hold.

Two more to talk about while we are here:

- **Is there a drawer of cables in your house that nobody can fully identify?** The fix is not to sort it, it is to refuse to do it one cable at a time. Empty the whole lot at once, name the device each one powers, and point at where that device is. No device, no keep.
- **What is in your office that is not office work?** It is there because this room has a door that can be shut, and shutting it makes the decision disappear without ever being made.

Tell us which one you are going to settle this week.
