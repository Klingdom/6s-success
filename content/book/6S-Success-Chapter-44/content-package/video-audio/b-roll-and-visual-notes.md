# Chapter 44 B-Roll and Visual Notes: The Home Office

## Must-get shots
- The desk cleared completely to bare wood, in one continuous take if possible. This is the image of the whole chapter.
- A whole drawer of cables emptied onto that cleared desk in one heap, then hands ruling on them one by one and setting three coiled keepers aside.
- The spray bottle misting a folded microfiber cloth, deliberately not the screen. Shoot this twice, tight.
- A keyboard turned upside down and shaken, then the vacuum crevice tool running between the key rows.
- The underside lip of the desk being wiped, with the cloth coming away visibly grey.
- The power strip being lifted off the floor and hooked onto the desk leg, and cables run and labelled.
- A filing drawer emptied to bare metal, the drawer bottom wiped, and folders going back with tabs in one straight line.
- Books tilted out and the vacuum dusting brush running along their top edges.
- The printer lid lifted and the scanner glass wiped in one direction.
- Masking tape and a printed photo of the cleared desk being taped inside the top drawer.

## Do not shoot
- Any readable brand name, logo, model number, or on-screen content. Types only.
- Any liquid being sprayed directly at a screen, a scanner, or a vent, even as a "wrong way" demo, unless it is clearly captioned and no liquid actually lands.
- Anyone standing on a rolling office chair, at any point, for any reason.
- Real names, addresses, account numbers, or identifiable documents in the filing sequence. Use blanks or dummies.
- Anything that reads as device repair: no opening cases, no removing panels.

## Tone
Calm and methodical. The register is recognition, not scolding. This room defeats almost everyone, and the film should say so.
