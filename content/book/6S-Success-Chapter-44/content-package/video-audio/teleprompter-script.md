# Chapter 44 Teleprompter Script: The Home Office

*Two minutes. Read at an even pace. Pause at each line break.*

Your home office is not dirty.

It is undecided.

Nothing in it is heavy. Nothing in it is dirty.

And it is still the room you have left the longest.

Because this room is not made of objects. It is made of decisions you have not taken.

Every thing in here asks a question before it will move.

What does this cable power. Are you the person who reads this book now.

None of those are hard to lift. All of them are hard to answer.

And an unanswered question is the heaviest object a house can hold.

So the move that unlocks this room is to stop deciding one item at a time.

Empty the whole cable drawer at once. Take every book off the shelf in one go.

Made in one batch, it is an afternoon. Made one item at a time, it never gets made at all.

Second: here the damp cloth is the risk, not the remedy.

Everything is electronics or paper. Spray onto the cloth. Never onto the thing.

Third: clear one surface first, and then defend it.

The desk is the only zone in this book that is also a tool.

And fourth: look at what is in this room that is not office work.

It is here because this room has a door that closes.

Six zones. Start at the desk.

Then photograph it clear, and tape the picture inside the top drawer.
