# Chapter 44 Short Video Scripts: The Home Office

Five scripts, 30 to 60 seconds each.

## 1. Not dirty, undecided
**Hook:** Your home office is not dirty. It is undecided.
**Body:** Nothing in it is heavy. Nothing in it is dirty. And it is still the room you have left the longest, because it is not made of objects. It is made of decisions nobody has taken.
**Close:** An unanswered question is the heaviest thing a house can hold.

## 2. Why the cable drawer survives
**Hook:** Here is why that drawer of cables has survived nine years.
**Body:** Each cable is too cheap to think about and too specific to throw away, so you never rule on any of them. Do not sort it. Empty the whole lot at once, pick up each one, name the device it powers, and point at where that device is in this house.
**Close:** No device, no keep.

## 3. Spray the cloth, not the thing
**Hook:** This is the one room where the damp cloth is the hazard.
**Body:** Everything here is electronics or paper. Power it down and let it cool. Spray onto the cloth, never onto the screen, the glass, or a vent, because liquid sprayed at a machine runs down inside and finds the electronics.
**Close:** And on toner dust, dry cloth only. Water turns loose toner into ink.

## 4. Information is not a record
**Hook:** Most of your filing drawer should not exist.
**Body:** It stalls because most of it is neither obviously permanent nor obviously rubbish. So use one test: if the issuer will reprint it on request, or it already lives in an online account, it is information, not a record.
**Close:** And information does not get a folder.

## 5. The door that closes
**Hook:** Why is there a broken lamp in your office?
**Body:** Because this is the room with a door that can be shut, and shutting a door makes a decision disappear from view without ever being made. That is how an office becomes the household's holding pen.
**Close:** Anything still there tomorrow is not temporary. It is furniture.
