# The Home Office: It Is Not Dirty, It Is Undecided

*A LinkedIn article from Chapter 44 of 6S Success: Home Edition.*

Walk into your home office and look honestly at what is stopping you. Nothing here is heavy. Nothing here is dirty. There is no grease, no lint, no damp, no grit walked in from outside. By every measure that made the other rooms in this book hard, this should be the easiest room in the house. And yet it is almost certainly the one you have left the longest, and the one where a previous attempt ran out of steam somewhere around the second drawer.

> The home office is the room where almost nothing is heavy and almost nothing is dirty, and that is precisely why it has sat untouched for years. Every other room asks you to lift, wipe, and carry. This one asks you to decide, of every object in it: this cable, this book, this sheet of paper, this half-finished thing in the corner. A room of decisions does not stall because the work is hard but because it is unresolved, and an unresolved decision is heavier than any box.

## The move that unlocks it

> The reason this room has defeated you is not that it is dirty or difficult. It is made almost entirely of postponed decisions, and postponed decisions are the heaviest objects in a house. A cable is not hard to lift, it is hard to rule on.

So you batch. Empty the entire cable drawer onto the desk at once and rule on all of it in one sitting. Take every book off the shelf in one go. Line up the whole supply cabinet so you can see the true count.

## The room where the method inverts

> In every other room in this book, water is your ally and the damp cloth does most of the work. In this one it is the hazard, because everything here is electronics or paper and both are ruined by it.

## The desk is a tool

> The desk is the only zone in this book that is also a piece of equipment. Every other zone gets emptied onto it: the drawer you tip out, the shelf you strip, the supply cabinet you count.

## And the door

> Look honestly at what is in this room that is not office work. The broken lamp. The unopened box from the last move. The bag of things with nowhere else to go. None of it is here because the office needed it. It is here because this is the room with a door that closes.
