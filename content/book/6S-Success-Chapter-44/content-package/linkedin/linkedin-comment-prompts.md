# Chapter 44 Comment Prompts: The Home Office

1. What is the oldest undecided thing in your home office right now?
2. Is there a drawer or bag of cables in your house that nobody can fully identify?
3. What is in your office that is not office work, and how long has it been there?
4. Which book on your shelf stands for a version of you that you have not formally retired?
5. Have you ever cleaned a screen or keyboard with something you later regretted?
6. Does your desk have a photographed clear state, or is clear a matter of opinion?
7. What paperwork are you keeping that the issuer would simply reprint on request?
8. Is there a backup printer in your house that has not been plugged in this year?
