# Chapter 44 LinkedIn Newsletter: The Home Office

**Subject:** Your home office is not dirty. It is undecided.

Nothing in your home office is heavy. Nothing in it is dirty. No grease, no lint, no damp. By every measure that made the other rooms hard, this should be the easiest room in the house.

It is almost certainly the one you have left the longest.

The reason is that this room is not made of objects. It is made of decisions you have not taken. Every thing in it asks a question before it will move: what does this cable power, are you the person who reads this book now, are you required to keep this piece of paper. None of those is hard to lift. All of them are hard to answer, and an unanswered question is the heaviest object a house can hold.

Six zones, and four rules on top of the nine rules of Shine:

- **Every item asks for a judgment, not a lift.** So batch the decisions. The whole cable drawer in one sitting, every book off the shelf at once, the entire supply cabinet lined up so you can see the true count.
- **Here the wet cloth is the risk.** Everything is electronics or paper. Spray onto the cloth, never onto the thing.
- **Clear one surface first, then defend it.** The desk is the only zone in the book that is also a tool.
- **The door that closes.** What is in this room that is not office work leaves today, or it becomes permanent furniture.

Do one zone this week, and make it the desk. Then photograph it clear and tape the picture inside the top drawer.
