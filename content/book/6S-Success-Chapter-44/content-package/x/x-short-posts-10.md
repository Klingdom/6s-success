# X Short Posts: The Home Office

Chapter 44 of *6S Success: Home Edition*. Ten standalone posts, each at or under 280 characters counting its number line. Plain numbering.

---

1/
Your home office is not dirty. It is undecided. Nothing in it is heavy and nothing in it is dirty, and it is still the room you have left the longest, because it is made of decisions nobody has taken.

(203 chars)

---

2/
A cable is not hard to lift. It is hard to rule on. A book from a job you left weighs nothing and costs an enormous amount to release. That is why this room defeats people.

(175 chars)

---

3/
Tidying advice fails in a home office because tidying moves things, and nothing in that room is waiting to be moved. It is waiting to be judged.

(147 chars)

---

4/
The drawer of cables survives because each one is too cheap to think about and too specific to throw away. Do not sort it. Empty the lot at once, name the device each powers, and point at it.

(194 chars)

---

5/
Made in one batch at a cleared desk, the cable drawer is a single sitting. Made one cable at a time, it never gets made at all.

(130 chars)

6/
The home office is the one room where the damp cloth is the hazard, not the remedy. Everything in it is electronics or paper. Spray onto the cloth, never onto the thing.

(172 chars)

7/
On toner dust, use a dry cloth only. Water turns loose toner into ink, and you will spend longer fixing that than you saved.

(127 chars)

8/

(196 chars)
