# Chapter 44 LinkedIn Carousel Copy: The Home Office

**Slide 1**
Your home office is not dirty.
It is undecided.

**Slide 2**
Nothing in it is heavy.
That is exactly why it has beaten you for years.

**Slide 3**
A cable is not hard to lift.
It is hard to rule on.

**Slide 4**
Six zones. The desk comes first,
because everything else gets emptied onto it.

**Slide 5**
Stop deciding one item at a time.
Batch, or it never happens.

**Slide 6**
The cable drawer, in one sitting.
Name the device. Point at it. No device, no keep.

**Slide 7**
The one room where the damp cloth
is the hazard, not the remedy.

**Slide 8**
Spray onto the cloth.
Never onto the screen, the glass, or a vent.

**Slide 9**
If the issuer will reprint it,
it is information, not a record. No folder.

**Slide 10**
What is in here that is not office work?
It leaves today.

**Slide 11**
Photograph the cleared desk.
Tape it inside the top drawer.
