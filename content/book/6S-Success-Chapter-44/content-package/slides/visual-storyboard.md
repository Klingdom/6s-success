# Chapter 44 Visual Storyboard: The Home Office

Four figures, matching the chapter's SVGs. Warm paper, terracotta, honey, slate, green. Types, not brands.

## Figure 1: the opener, two states
A full-width pair. Left, the room as it runs: a desk you would have to clear before starting anything, a fringe of sticky notes around the monitor, a power strip on the floor in the arc the chair rolls through, a drawer of unidentified cables, a broken lamp in the corner. Right, the same room: a surface you can sit straight down at, one spare of each cable you actually use, the strip hooked up off the floor, and nothing left that is not office work.

## Figure 2: the six zones
A warm overhead plan, six numbered cells in two rows of three: primary desk, drawers and pedestal, file storage, bookshelf, printer station, supply cabinet. Footer line: one room, six small jobs, and the desk is the workbench for the other five.

## Figure 3: the method and the kit
Two panels. Left, the four rules of the home-office layer with a short gloss under each. Right, the kit as product types grouped by job, with the screen-safe electronics cleaner leading, because it is the input that makes this room different.

## Figure 4: the before-and-after signature
A matched pair from the same doorway with the camera icon between them. The after panel carries a small honey-outlined label reading "taped inside the top drawer", marking the variation unique to this room: the standard lives where you will open it tomorrow, and it is matched every evening rather than every season.
