# Chapter 44 Teaching Slide Deck Outline: The Home Office

Sixteen slides, about 25 minutes.

1. Title: The Home Office. Part Nine, room-by-room playbooks.
2. The claim: this room is not dirty, it is undecided.
3. Nothing in it is heavy. Why that is the problem, not the consolation.
4. The heaviest object in a house is an unanswered question.
5. Why tidying fails here: nothing is waiting to be moved, it is waiting to be judged.
6. The six zones, and why the desk is first.
7. The desk as a tool: the only zone that is also equipment.
8. Rule one of the layer: judgments, not lifts. Batch or it never happens.
9. Worked example: the cable drawer, ruled on in one sitting.
10. Rule two: here the wet cloth is the risk. Spray the cloth, never the thing.
11. Rule three: clear one surface first, then defend it.
12. Rule four: the door that closes, and the household's holding pen.
13. The centerpiece: zone by zone, inputs and numbered steps (walk one zone live).
14. The six hard calls, from the half-finished thing to the deal you got.
15. Safety: the five questions in a room where light is mistaken for safe.
16. The signature: an after photo taped inside a drawer and matched every evening.
