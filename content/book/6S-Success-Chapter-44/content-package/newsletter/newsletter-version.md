# Chapter 44 Newsletter: The Home Office

**Subject:** Your home office is not dirty. It is undecided.
**Preview:** Nothing in it is heavy, and that is exactly the problem.

---

Walk into your home office and look honestly at what is stopping you.

Nothing here is heavy. Nothing here is dirty. There is no grease, no lint, no damp, no grit walked in from outside. By every measure that made the other rooms in this book hard, this should be the easiest room in the house.

And yet it is almost certainly the one you have left the longest, and the one where a previous attempt ran out of steam somewhere around the second drawer.

The reason is that this room is not made of objects. It is made of decisions you have not taken. Every thing in here asks a question before it will move. This cable: what does it power, and is that thing still in the house? This book: are you the person who reads this now? None of those questions are hard to lift. All of them are hard to answer, and an unanswered question is the heaviest object a house can hold.

**Six zones, and the desk comes first.** The primary desk, the desk drawers and pedestal, the file storage, the bookshelf and reference zone, the printer and scanning station, and the supply cabinet. The desk is the only zone in the book that is also a tool, because every other zone gets emptied onto it. Clear it completely before you open a single drawer, then defend it.

One warning on pacing: give the file storage a fresh morning of its own. It is hundreds of keep-or-shred calls in a row, and tired judgment is what stalls it.

**Four rules on top of the nine rules of Shine.**

*Every item asks for a judgment, not a lift.* So batch the decisions. Empty the entire cable drawer at once and rule on all of it in one sitting. Take every book off the shelf in one go. Line up the whole supply cabinet so you can see the true count.

*Here the wet cloth is the risk.* Everything is electronics or paper. Power down, let it cool, spray the cloth and never the screen or a vent, and use a dry cloth on toner because water turns loose toner into ink.

*Clear one surface first, then defend it.* The moment the desk refills, you have lost the only workbench you had.

*The door that closes.* Everything in this room that is not office work walks out today, or it becomes permanent furniture.

Do one zone this week, and make it the desk. Then photograph it clear and tape the picture inside the top drawer, because this standard is not checked at the change of season. It is checked tonight.
