# Chapter 44 Newsletter Short Teaser: The Home Office

The home office is the room where almost nothing is heavy and almost nothing is dirty, which is exactly why it has sat untouched for years. It is not made of objects. It is made of decisions nobody has taken.

Chapter 44 breaks it into six zones with the exact inputs and numbered steps for each, and adds four rules to the nine rules of Shine: every item asks for a judgment rather than a lift, here the wet cloth is the risk, clear one surface first, and the door that closes is what turned this room into the household's holding pen.

Start at the desk, and clear it completely before you open a single drawer.
