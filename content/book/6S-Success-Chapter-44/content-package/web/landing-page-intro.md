# Chapter 44 Landing Page Intro: The Home Office

Walk into your home office and look honestly at what is stopping you. Nothing here is heavy. Nothing here is dirty. There is no grease, no lint, no damp. By every measure that made the other rooms hard, this should be the easiest room in the house, and yet it is almost certainly the one you have left the longest.

The reason is that this room is not made of objects. It is made of decisions you have not taken. Every thing in it asks a question before it will move: what does this cable power, are you the person who reads this book now, are you required to keep this piece of paper. None of those is hard to lift. All of them are hard to answer, and an unanswered question is the heaviest object a house can hold.

This chapter breaks the home office into six small zones and gives you, for each one, the exact inputs to gather and the numbered steps to clean and shine it: the primary desk, the desk drawers and pedestal, the file storage, the bookshelf and reference zone, the printer and scanning station, and the supply cabinet. It adds four rules to the nine rules of Shine, including the one that makes this room unlike every other in the book: here the damp cloth is the risk rather than the remedy.

Product types only, no brands. Start at the desk, and clear it completely before you open a single drawer.
