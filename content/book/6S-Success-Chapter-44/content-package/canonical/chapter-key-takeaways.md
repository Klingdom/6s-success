# Chapter 44 Key Takeaways: The Home Office

- The home office is not dirty or difficult. It is made of postponed decisions, and postponed decisions are the heaviest objects in a house.
- The move that unlocks it is batching: rule on the whole cable drawer in one sitting, take every book off the shelf in one go, line up the entire supply cabinet so you can see the true count.
- Six zones, one at a time, starting at the primary desk, because every other zone gets emptied onto that surface.
- The desk is the only zone in the book that is also a tool. Clear it completely before you open a single drawer, then defend it.
- Here the wet cloth is the risk, not the remedy. Power down, spray the cloth and never the screen, glass, or vent, and use a dry cloth on toner because water turns loose toner into ink.
- Let a drawer bottom dry completely before the files go back, because paper wicks up any damp you leave.
- This room became the household's holding pen because it has a door that closes. Everything in it that is not office work walks out today or becomes permanent furniture.
- Give the file storage a fresh morning. It is hundreds of keep-or-shred calls in a row, and tired judgment is what stalls it.
- If the issuer will reprint it on request, or it already lives in an online account, it is information, not a record, and information does not get a folder.
- Safety here is quiet because everything looks light: paper is a fuel load, tall loaded things tip forward, and the rolling chair is not a step.
- Close on a before-and-after photo taped inside the top drawer, matched every evening when the laptop lid closes.
