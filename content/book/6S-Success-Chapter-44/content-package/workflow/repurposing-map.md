## Reuse cautions
- Types, not brands, everywhere. No invented prices.
- **Never state a retention period, a tax rule, or a jurisdiction.** The reader looks theirs up once and writes it on the card.
- Never depict liquid sprayed at a screen, scanner, or vent; never depict anyone standing on a rolling chair; never depict device repair.
- No real names, addresses, or account numbers in any filing sequence.
- Keep the register as recognition rather than scolding.
- Every asset closes on the before-and-after signature, and keeps the taped-inside-the-drawer detail in any depiction of the after.
- Stay in the home office: do not reuse laundry-room (Ch 43) or garage (Ch 45) material.
