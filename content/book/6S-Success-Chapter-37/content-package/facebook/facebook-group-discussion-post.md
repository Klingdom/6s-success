# Facebook Group Discussion Post: The Primary Bedroom

For a home or organizing group. A useful tip, an open question, a soft close.

---

A bedroom tip that matters more than it sounds.

The primary bedroom is the one room whose job is to help you stop, so you clean it for sleep and for air, not for tidiness. That means washing the layer that actually touches your face all night: not just the cases, but the pillow inside and the protector under it, which almost never get washed. Strip the bed right back, send the protectors through the wash, put the pillows themselves through when the seasons turn, and air the mattress and the duvet.

Two more that surprise people. First, the real dirt is where you never look: behind the headboard, under the bed, and on the closet's back wall, so pull the bed out and clear the closet to bare rod. Second, if you share the room, you clean, they cull: wipe the other person's side but remove nothing, and let the difference do the persuading.

And keep the strip of floor between the bed and the door bare, because it is what a bare foot finds at three in the morning.

When did you last actually wash your pillows, not the cases, the pillows themselves?
