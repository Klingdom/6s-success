# Chapter 37 Facebook Posts: The Primary Bedroom

Five short standalone posts.

## 1. Clean for sleep and air
The bedroom's whole job is to help you stop, so you clean it for sleep and air, not tidiness. You breathe against this bed for a third of your life, so wash the layer that touches your face and air out the night's moisture. Everything else in the room follows from that.

## 2. Wash the pillow, not just the case
The cases get washed. The pillow inside and the protector under it almost never do, though they are the layer against your face all night. Send the protectors through the wash too, put the pillows through when the seasons turn, and air the mattress and duvet. It changes what you breathe all night.

## 3. Clean where nobody looks
The real dirt in a bedroom is where you never look: behind the headboard, under the bed, along the closet rod, and on the closet's back wall. Pull the bed out, lift the drawers clear, clear the closet to bare rod, and clean the parts of the room you never see. That is where the trouble starts.

## 4. You clean, they cull
In a shared bedroom, every tidiness argument starts on the other person's side. The rule that ends it: you clean, they cull. You may wipe and vacuum their side, and you may not remove one object. Fix your own side to the standard and let the difference do the persuading.

## 5. Keep the dark strip bare
The strip of floor between your bed and the door is what a bare foot finds at three in the morning. A bin, a shoe, or a charging cable across it is a fall waiting in the dark. Keep it bare. Chapter 37. Next: the guest bedroom.
