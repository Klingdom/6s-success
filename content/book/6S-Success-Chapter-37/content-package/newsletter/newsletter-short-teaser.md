# Chapter 37 Newsletter Short Teaser: The Primary Bedroom

A short digest blurb (about 120 words) with a read-the-chapter CTA.

---

**The Primary Bedroom: clean for sleep and air.**

The primary bedroom is the one room whose whole job is to help you stop, so wiping it down is not the point. You breathe against this bed for a third of your life, so you wash the layer that touches your face and air out the night's moisture.

Chapter 37 gives the exact inputs and numbered steps for each of the room's six zones, plus the habits that make it work: clean where nobody looks (behind the headboard, under the bed, the closet back wall), and, on a shared bed, clean up to the middle and no further.

[Read the chapter] and wake to a room that helps you sleep.
