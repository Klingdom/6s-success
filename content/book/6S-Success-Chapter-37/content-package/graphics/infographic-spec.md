# Chapter 37 Infographic Spec: The Primary Bedroom Shine Method

## The single strongest image
The bedroom Shine method plus the kit, because the method (clean for sleep and air, clean where nobody looks, you clean they cull, the dark and the tip) is the chapter's centerpiece.

## Anchor string (byte-verbatim)
> The primary bedroom is the one room whose whole job is to help you stop, so here you clean for sleep and for air. You spend a third of your life with your face pressed to this bed, breathing what it holds, so when you strip it you wash the layer that sits against your face and air out what traps the night's moisture. You clean the surfaces the room hides, behind the headboard and under the bed and along the closet's back wall, because that is where dust, hair, and damp gather unseen. And on a bed two people share, you clean right up to the middle and no further.

## Layout, portrait (1080 x 1350)
- Title: The Primary Bedroom Shine Method.
- Band 1, "clean for sleep and air": a pillow to the wash, a mattress standing to air.
- Band 2, "clean where nobody looks": behind the headboard, under the bed, the closet back wall.
- Band 3, "you clean, they cull": one side wiped, the far side untouched.
- Band 4, "the dark and the tip": a bare floor strip, an anti-tip strap into a stud.
- Footer strip: clean for sleep and air, clean where nobody looks, stop at the middle.

Two panels: the method on the left, the kit on the right.

## Rules
Types only, no brands or logos. Never show reading glasses cleaned with window cleaner or the other sleeper's side reorganised. No em/en/spaced-hyphen dashes in any label.
