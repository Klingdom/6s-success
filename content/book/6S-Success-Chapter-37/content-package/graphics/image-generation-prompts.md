# Chapter 37 Image Generation Prompts: The Primary Bedroom

Warm, illustrated, flat-color editorial house style (paper #F7F2E9 ground, ink #2B2622 line, terracotta/honey/slate/green accents, Shine green #6E8B5B). No photorealism, no brand names or logos, no text baked into the art beyond the noted labels. Product types only.

## Prompt 1 (opener, two states)
A warm flat-illustration of one primary bedroom shown twice, side by side. Left, labelled "before": a bed made over a sagging unaired mattress, both nightstands buried under cables and glasses, a dresser drifted with loose change and receipts, a closet rod crammed end to end, shoes across the floor. Right, labelled "after": the bed stripped with the mattress standing to air and the pillows by the wash, both nightstands cleared to a lamp, a book, and a small dish, one valet tray on the dresser, a closet showing only this season, and a clear strip of floor.

## Prompt 2 (six zones plan)
A warm overhead floor plan of a primary bedroom, gently shaded, with six numbered and labelled zones: bed and bedding, your nightstand, their nightstand, dresser top, dresser drawers, primary closet. Caption strip at the foot: "one room, six small jobs".

## Prompt 3 (method and kit, two panels)
A two-panel warm infographic. Left "the method": a pillow and protector going to the wash beside a mattress standing to air, a crevice tool reaching behind a headboard and a hand under a bed, a cloth wiping one side of a bed while the far side stays untouched, and a bare floor strip with an anti-tip strap into a stud. Right "the kit": labelled type icons for a vacuum with three attachments, a microfiber set, a neutral pH cleaner and a streak-free glass cleaner, a small detail brush, laundry detergent, drawer dividers, a valet tray, matching hangers, a shoe rack, an under-bed bin, a moisture absorber, and anti-tip anchors.

## Prompt 4 (bed-zone step ribbon)
A warm step-ribbon titled "how to clean the bed and bedding zone": strip it right back, dust the headboard, vacuum behind it, air the pillows and duvet, vacuum the bare mattress and seams, wipe the frame, clear under the bed. The wash-the-face-layer and air-the-mattress steps highlighted in the Shine green.

## Prompt 5 (before-and-after signature)
A warm matched pair of the primary bedroom's real life, the bed, both nightstands, and the floor strip, shot from the doorway, before on the left and after on the right, with a small camera icon and the labels "the before, kept" and "the after, kept as the standard".
