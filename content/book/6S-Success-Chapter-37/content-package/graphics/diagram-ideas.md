# Chapter 37 Diagram Ideas: The Primary Bedroom

House palette (paper #F7F2E9, panel #FBF7EF, ink #2B2622, terracotta #BC4B2A, honey #DDA63A, slate #3C5A6B, green #6E8B5B for Shine). Warm, illustrated, not photoreal. Product types only, no brands or logos. Spark red reserved only for a true hazard (a cracked lamp cord, a bedside candle by the bedding).

## Diagram 1 (opener, figure 1): two states
The primary bedroom before and after, side by side. Before: a bed made over an unaired mattress, both nightstands buried under cables and glasses, a dresser top drifted with pocket sediment, a closet rod crammed end to end, shoes across the floor. After: the bed stripped and the face-layer washed with the mattress airing, both nightstands cleared to five things or fewer, one valet tray on the dresser, a closet showing only this season, and a clear strip of floor to cross in the dark.

## Diagram 2 (figure 2): the six zones plan
A warm overhead plan, six numbered zones marked and named: bed and bedding, your nightstand, their nightstand, dresser top, dresser drawers, primary closet. Footer: "one room, six small jobs".

## Diagram 3 (figure 3): the Shine method and kit
Two panels. Left, the method: clean for sleep and air (a pillow and protector to the wash, a mattress standing to air); clean where nobody looks (a crevice tool behind a headboard, a hand under the bed, a cloth down a closet back wall); you clean, they cull (a cloth wiping one side of a bed with the far side untouched); the dark and the tip (a bare floor strip, an anti-tip strap into a stud). Right, the kit: a vacuum with three attachments, a microfiber set (one cloth flagged glasses-dry-only), a neutral pH cleaner and a streak-free glass cleaner, a small detail brush, laundry detergent, drawer dividers, a valet tray, matching hangers, a shoe rack, an under-bed bin, a moisture absorber, and anti-tip anchors. Footer: "clean for sleep and air, clean where nobody looks, stop at the middle".

## Diagram 4 (no HTML twin): the bedroom Shine method
A standalone visual: clean for sleep and air, clean where nobody looks, you clean they cull, the dark and the tip. Names the next chapter, the guest bedroom.

## Diagram 5 (no HTML twin): how to clean the bed and bedding zone
A step ribbon: strip it right back including the protectors, dust the headboard, vacuum behind it, air the pillows and duvet, vacuum the bare mattress and its seams, wipe the frame, clear and vacuum under the bed. Highlight the wash-the-face-layer and air-the-mattress steps.

## Diagram 6 (before/after signature) and the photo brief
The Part 9 signature: a matched before-and-after pair of the bed, both nightstands, and the floor strip from the doorway. **Best before-and-after photo brief:** stand in the doorway and frame the bed, both nightstands, and the strip of floor you cross in the dark together in one shot; same spot, angle, and light, before and after; do not tidy before the before (Ch 6); keep the after as the ideal-state standard (Ch 25), taped inside the closet door. The retired friction meter is not drawn.
