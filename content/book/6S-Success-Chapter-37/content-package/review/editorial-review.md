# Chapter 37 Editorial Review: The Primary Bedroom

## Verdict
Ready for Phil's review as a draft. The chapter meets the instruction-centric standard: every one of the six zones carries an Inputs list, numbered Steps, and an inspect-and-flag paragraph, and the primary-bedroom Shine layer is distinct from the six rooms before it.

## What works
- **The layer is genuinely its own.** Clean-for-sleep-and-air, clean-where-nobody-looks, and you-clean-they-cull are specific to a room you sleep in and often share, and they do not repeat the family-room (clean-and-dry) or living-room (dust-is-heat) layers. This is the first chapter to open the private half of the house, and the tone shifts from busy to restful without losing the instructional spine.
- **The centerpiece earns its length.** The face-layer wash and mattress airing, the behind-the-headboard crevice pass, the water-glass ring, the pull-the-drawer-out runner clean, and the clear-to-bare-rod closet are concrete, orderable, and drawn straight from the Manual's shine_detail.
- **The shared-room material is handled with unusual care.** The "you clean, they cull" rule, with medication as the single named exception, turns a marriage-level friction point into a clean instruction, and it reads as wise rather than preachy.
- **Safety is integrated, not bolted on.** The dark walking strip and the two anti-tip pieces (dresser, closet rod) are surfaced in the Shine inspect-and-flag steps and resolved in the Safety pass, consistent with the Shine-finds-Safety-fixes rule.

## Watch items (minor, non-blocking)
- The chapter runs long (about 5,826 words), the longest room playbook so far, in line with the six-zone rooms; the length cap is lifted for Part 9, so this is acceptable but worth a trim pass if Phil wants tighter, most easily in the Calls section.
- Zones 2 and 3 (the two nightstands) are structurally parallel by design; the text keeps them distinct by making Zone 3 entirely about the shared-line discipline, which reads cleanly rather than repetitively.

## Consistency checks
- Nine Shine rules recapped, not re-taught: PASS.
- Friction meter retired; before-and-after signature closes: PASS.
- Product types only: PASS.
- Hand-off to Chapter 38 (the guest bedroom) present and light: PASS.

## Recommendation
Approve as draft. No structural edits required.
