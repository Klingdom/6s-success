# Chapter 37 Brand Voice Check: The Primary Bedroom

## Voice target
Warm, practical, expert, calm about sleep and rest, and clear-eyed about a shared room, never preachy. The register of a knowledgeable friend who has actually cleaned this room, not a manufacturer.

## Scan results
- **Product types only, no brands**: PASS. A brand-name scan across the whole package (common household brands) returned zero hits. Everything is a type: compact vacuum with attachments, colour-coded microfiber cloth set, neutral pH multi-surface cleaner, streak-free glass and mirror cleaner, small detail brush set, drawer divider set, jewelry and valet tray, matching or slim non-slip hanger set, moisture absorber or humidity monitor, anti-tip anchor and hardware.
- **No hype, no invented numbers or prices**: PASS. No statistics, no fabricated study claims, no dollar figures.
- **Rest framed with care, not judgement**: PASS. The shared-room and clothes-that-do-not-fit passages are handled with warmth ("kept the peace", "dress the body you have this morning") rather than shame.
- **Dash and punctuation house style**: PASS. Middot for label separators; no em/en dashes; no prose spaced-hyphen separators.
- **Inclusive, non-gendered**: PASS. "You", "the other sleeper", "two people who share a bed" throughout; no gendered assumptions about who cleans or who culls.

## Consistency with the series
- The zone-by-zone instruction voice matches Chapters 31 to 36.
- The one-idea-to-keep close and the before-and-after signature match the Part 9 pattern.
- The two fences held everywhere: never clean reading glasses with window cleaner, never reorganise the other sleeper's side.

## Verdict
On voice. No changes needed.
