# Chapter 37 Landing Page Intro: The Primary Bedroom

There is one room in your house whose only job is to help you stop, and it is the one that quietly absorbs everything the rest of the house refuses. Cleaning it well is not about tidiness. It is about a room you can walk into in the dark, cross without stepping over anything, and lie down in on clean-breathing linen.

This chapter breaks the primary bedroom into six small zones and gives you, for each one, the exact inputs to gather and the numbered steps to clean and shine it: the bed and bedding, your own nightstand, the other sleeper's nightstand, the dresser top, the dresser drawers, and the closet. It shows you the one pass almost nobody does (washing the layer that actually touches your face and airing the mattress), where the room's real dirt hides (behind the headboard, under the bed, along the closet's back wall), and how to clean a shared room without starting the argument that undoes it.

Product types only, no brands. Start tonight with your own nightstand, the smallest zone and the one you see first and last.
