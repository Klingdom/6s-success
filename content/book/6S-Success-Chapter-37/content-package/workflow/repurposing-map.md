# Chapter 37 Repurposing Map: The Primary Bedroom

## Spine (the how-to-clean value, in priority order)
1. The bedroom Shine method: clean for sleep and air, clean where nobody looks, you clean they cull, the dark and the tip.
2. The zone-by-zone centerpiece: the exact inputs and numbered steps for the bed, both nightstands, the dresser top, the drawers, and the closet.
3. The pass almost nobody does: wash the pillow and the protector, air the mattress.
4. The calls that matter: the third sheet set, the phone charger, the perfume you were given, the size you are not, the expensive mistake, and the shared side.
