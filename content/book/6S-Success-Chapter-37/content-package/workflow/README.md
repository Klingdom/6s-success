# Chapter 37 Content Package: The Primary Bedroom

The SEVENTH room playbook of Part Nine (Chapters 31 to 50), and the first of the private rooms, built to the instruction-centric template. The chapter CORE is the detailed, step-by-step clean-and-shine instruction for each of the six bedroom zones, with the exact inputs.

## The chapter in one line
The primary bedroom is the one room whose job is to help you stop, so you clean it for sleep and for air: wash the layer that touches your face, clean where nobody looks, and on a shared bed clean up to the middle and no further.

## The six zones
The bed and bedding zone, your own nightstand, the other sleeper's nightstand, the dresser top, the dresser drawers, and the primary closet. Each carries an Inputs list, numbered Steps, and an inspect-and-flag list.

## The bedroom Shine layer (nine rules from Chapter 31 plus)
- Clean for sleep and air: wash the pillow and protector, air the mattress and duvet.
- Clean where nobody looks: behind the headboard, under the bed, the closet rod and back wall.
- You clean, they cull: wipe the shared side, remove nothing; medication is the one exception.
- The dark walking strip and the furniture that tips: keep the strip bare; strap the dresser and the rod; one drawer at a time.
