# Chapter 37 LinkedIn Carousel Outline: The Primary Bedroom

Twelve slides. Product types only.

- Slide 1 (hook): The one room whose job is to help you stop. Clean it for sleep, not for show.
- Slide 2: So the standard is different: a room you can cross in the dark and lie down in on clean-breathing linen.
- Slide 3 (the rule): Clean for sleep and air. You breathe against this bed for a third of your life.
- Slide 4 (six zones): bed, your nightstand, their nightstand, dresser top, drawers, closet. One at a time.
- Slide 5 (the face-layer): wash the pillow and the protector, not just the case. Air the mattress.
- Slide 6 (hidden dirt): clean where nobody looks: behind the headboard, under the bed, the closet back wall.
- Slide 7 (vacuum the mattress): the upholstery tool does the real work, on the seams and the underside.
- Slide 8 (the shared side): you clean, they cull. Wipe their side, remove nothing.
- Slide 9 (the dark strip): keep the bed-to-door floor bare. It is what a bare foot finds at 3am.
- Slide 10 (anti-tip): strap the dresser and the rod; open one drawer at a time.
- Slide 11 (standard): a bedroom you can walk into and lie down in without moving one object.
- Slide 12 (CTA): Do one zone tonight, your own nightstand. Chapter 37. Next: the guest bedroom.
