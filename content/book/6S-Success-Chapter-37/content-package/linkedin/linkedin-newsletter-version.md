# Clean for Sleep and Air

*A 6S Success: Home Edition newsletter, from Chapter 37, The Primary Bedroom.*

Hi friend,

We have done the rooms the whole house shares. This week we cross into the private ones, starting with the room whose whole job is to help you stop.

**The primary bedroom is the one room whose whole job is to help you stop, so here you clean for sleep and for air. You spend a third of your life with your face pressed to this bed, breathing what it holds, so when you strip it you wash the layer that sits against your face and air out what traps the night's moisture.**

The room breaks into six zones, and each gets its exact inputs and numbered steps in the chapter: the bed and bedding, your own nightstand, the other sleeper's nightstand, the dresser top, the dresser drawers, and the closet.

## To try this week

Do your own nightstand tonight, in one sitting. It is the smallest zone in the room and the one you look at last thing and first thing. Lift everything off, wash the shade and the bulb, wipe the water-glass ring, divide the drawer, and clip the cord down the back leg so no cable crosses the top. Five things or fewer when you are done.

Two printables go with this one: The Primary Bedroom Reset card and The Primary Bedroom Kit list.
