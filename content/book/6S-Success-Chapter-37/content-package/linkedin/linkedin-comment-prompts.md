# Chapter 37 LinkedIn Comment Prompts: The Primary Bedroom

1. When did you last actually wash your pillows, not the cases, the pillows themselves?
2. Have you ever vacuumed your mattress? Most people never have. What stopped you, or what changed when you did?
3. In a shared bedroom, how do you handle the other person's side without starting an argument?
4. Is your dresser strapped to the wall? If you have a climber in the house, this one matters.
5. What is on the strip of floor between your bed and the door right now that a bare foot would find at 3am?
