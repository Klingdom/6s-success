Part Nine crosses into the private rooms, starting with the one whose whole job is to help you stop: the primary bedroom. Like the rooms before it, this chapter gives the exact inputs and steps for each zone. But here you clean for something new.

2/
"The primary bedroom is the one room whose whole job is to help you stop, so here you clean for sleep and for air." You breathe against this bed for a third of your life.

(173 chars)

---

3/
So you wash the layer that touches your face, you clean the surfaces the room hides, and on a bed two people share you clean right up to the middle and no further.

(166 chars)

---

4/
"The primary bedroom is six small zones, not one big job: the bed and bedding zone, your own nightstand, the other sleeper's nightstand, the dresser top, the dresser drawers, and the primary closet."

(202 chars)

---

5/
The bed: strip it right back. The cases get washed and the pillow inside almost never does, though it is the layer against your face all night. Send the protectors through the wash too, and air the mattress and the duvet.

(224 chars)

---

6/
With the mattress bare, run the vacuum's upholstery tool across the whole face and into the piped seams where dust and skin flakes settle, then rotate it and do the underside before the protector goes back.

(209 chars)

---

7/
"The surfaces that matter most in this room are the ones it hides." Dust and hair pack behind the headboard, grit gathers under the bed, a grey film builds on the closet rod, and damp starts on a back wall nobody wipes.

(222 chars)

---

8/
So you pull the bed out, lift the drawers clear, and clear the closet to bare rod, and you clean the parts of the room you never see, because that is where the trouble begins.

(178 chars)

---

9/
Your nightstand: five things or fewer. Lift everything off, wash the shade and wipe the bulb, treat the water-glass ring, and clean your reading glasses with a dry cloth only, never window cleaner.

(200 chars)

---

10/
"Half of this room is not yours to decide about." On the other sleeper's side you may wipe, dust, and vacuum, and you may not remove one object. The one exception is out-of-date medication.

(193 chars)

---

11/
The dresser and drawers: pull each drawer right out, brush the grit from the runner, and strap the tall chest to a stud. Open one drawer at a time, because two open at once tips the whole thing forward.

(206 chars)

---

12/
"Two quiet dangers live in a bedroom, and both come out at night." The strip of floor between the bed and the door is what a bare foot finds at three in the morning, so it stays bare.

(187 chars)

---

13/
"A finished primary bedroom is one you can walk into in the dark, cross to the bed without stepping over anything, and lie down in on clean-breathing linen."
