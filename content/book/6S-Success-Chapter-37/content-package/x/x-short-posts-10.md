# X Short Posts: The Primary Bedroom

Chapter 37 of *6S Success: Home Edition*. Ten standalone posts, each at or under 280 characters counting its number line. Plain numbering.

---

1/
The bedroom's whole job is to help you stop, so you clean it for sleep and air, not tidiness. You breathe against this bed for a third of your life. Wash the layer that touches your face, and air the mattress.

(212 chars)

2/
When did you last wash your pillow? Not the case, the pillow. The cases get washed and the pillow almost never does, though it is the layer against your face all night. Put the pillows through when the seasons turn.

(218 chars)

---

3/
Have you ever vacuumed your mattress? With it bare, run the upholstery tool across the face and into the piped seams where dust and skin flakes settle, then rotate it and do the underside.

(191 chars)

4/
The dirt in your bedroom is where you never look: behind the headboard, under the bed, along the closet rod, and on the closet back wall. Pull the bed out and clean the parts of the room you never see.

(204 chars)

---

5/
How to clean a shared bedroom without an argument: you clean, they cull. You may wipe and vacuum the other person's side, and you may not remove one object. Let the difference do the persuading.

(197 chars)

---

6/
Your nightstand holds five things or fewer: lamp, a lidded drink, one book, a small dish for your glasses. When a sixth turns up it is almost always a finished book or an empty glass. Start here tonight.

(206 chars)

---

7/
The phone should not sleep on the nightstand. You keep it there because it is your alarm. That is one job, and a small clock does it. Move the charger across the room and give the phone a spot you stand up to reach.

(218 chars)

---

8/
Keep the strip of floor between your bed and the door bare. It is what a bare foot finds at three in the morning, and a bin, a shoe, or a charging cable across it is a fall waiting in the dark.

(196 chars)

---

9/
Strap the tall dresser to a stud and anchor the closet rod. Both come down on whoever is underneath the moment a drawer is pulled or a child climbs it. Open one drawer at a time.

(181 chars)
