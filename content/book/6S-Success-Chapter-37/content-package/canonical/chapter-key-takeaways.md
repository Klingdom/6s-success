# Chapter 37 Key Takeaways: The Primary Bedroom

- The bedroom's whole job is to help you stop, so you clean it for sleep and for air, not for tidiness.
- Six zones, one at a time: the bed and bedding, your nightstand, the other sleeper's nightstand, the dresser top, the dresser drawers, the primary closet. Start with your own nightstand tonight.
- Wash the layer that touches your face: strip the bed right back, send the pillow protectors through the wash, put the pillows themselves through when the seasons turn, and air the mattress and duvet.
- Clean where nobody looks: behind the headboard, under the bed, behind the drawers, the grey film on the closet rod, and the damp back wall of the closet.
- On a shared bed you clean, they cull: wipe and vacuum the other person's side but remove nothing; the one exception is out-of-date medication.
- A vacuum with an upholstery and a crevice tool does most of the room's real work.
- Two night-time dangers: keep the bed-to-door strip bare, and strap the tall dresser and the closet rod; open one drawer at a time.
- Close on a before-and-after photo taped inside the closet door, the standard the room has to match from now on.
