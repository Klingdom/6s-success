# Chapter 37 Summary: The Primary Bedroom

The seventh room playbook of Part Nine, and the first of the private rooms. The primary bedroom is the one room whose whole job is to help you stop, so it is cleaned for sleep and for air: you wash the layer that touches your face all night, air out the night's moisture, clean the hidden surfaces the room keeps out of sight, and on a bed two people share, clean up to the middle and no further.

The room breaks into six zones, each with its exact inputs and numbered steps: the bed and bedding zone, your own nightstand, the other sleeper's nightstand, the dresser top, the dresser drawers, and the primary closet. The governing Shine layer adds four moves to the nine rules from Chapter 31: clean for sleep and air (wash the pillow protector and the pillow, air the mattress and duvet); clean where nobody looks (behind the headboard, under the bed, the closet rod and back wall); you clean, they cull (the shared side, wiped but never reorganised, with medication the one safety exception); and the two night-time dangers, the dark walking strip and the tall furniture that tips.

The chapter closes, as every Part Nine playbook does, on a before-and-after room signature and the two photos to take. Previous: Chapter 36, The Family Room. Next: Chapter 38, The Guest Bedroom.
