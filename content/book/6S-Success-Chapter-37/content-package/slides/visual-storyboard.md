# Chapter 37 Visual Storyboard: The Primary Bedroom

## Design system
House palette: paper #F7F2E9, panel #FBF7EF, ink #2B2622, terracotta #BC4B2A, honey #DDA63A, slate #3C5A6B, green #6E8B5B (Shine). Fraunces display, Newsreader body, Inter labels. Footer strip: CLEAN FOR SLEEP AND AIR.

## Recurring motif
The "clean for sleep and air" cue: a stripped bed with the pillow and protector going to the wash and the mattress standing to air. It ties the room's lesson together and reappears on the method slide.

## Two fences
- Never depict reading glasses cleaned with window cleaner; never depict the other sleeper's side being reorganised.
- Product types only, no brand names or real logos.

## Beats (thirteen)
1. Title over the two-state opener.
2. The room whose job is to help you stop.
3. Clean for sleep and air.
4. The six zones overhead plan.
5. The definition: a room you can cross in the dark and lie down in.
6. The method and kit two-panel (figure 3).
7. Bed and bedding: wash the face-layer, air the mattress.
8. Clean where nobody looks: behind the headboard, under the bed.
9. The nightstand: five things or fewer.
10. The shared side: you clean, they cull.
11. Safety: the dark strip; strap the dresser and rod.
12. The before-and-after room signature.
13. Close: one idea to keep, and the Chapter 38 hand-off.

## Carrying it across the set
Every asset closes on the before-and-after room signature from the doorway (bed, both nightstands, the floor strip). The friction meter is retired for Part 9. Register warm, calm, expert. Previous Ch 36, next Ch 38 The Guest Bedroom.
