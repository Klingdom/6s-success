# Chapter 37 Teaching Slide Deck Outline: The Primary Bedroom

Fifteen slides. House palette; Shine green #6E8B5B. Footer strip: CLEAN FOR SLEEP AND AIR. Product types only.

1. Title: The Primary Bedroom. Part Nine, Room-by-Room Playbooks. The room whose job is to help you stop.
2. The problem: it is not a showroom. It absorbs what the rest of the house refuses.
3. What you will learn: the five outcomes.
4. The rule: clean for sleep and air; you breathe against this bed for a third of your life.
5. Six zones, not one room: with the overhead plan figure.
6. The definition: a room you can cross in the dark and lie down in on clean-breathing linen.
7. The method (its own slide): clean for sleep and air, clean where nobody looks, you clean they cull, the dark and the tip. Figure 3, method and kit.
8. Bed and bedding: wash the face-layer, air the mattress, vacuum the seams, clean behind the headboard.
9. Your nightstand: five things or fewer; lift everything off; the water-glass ring.
10. The rest (their nightstand, dresser top, drawers, closet): the key move for each.
11. Clean where nobody looks: behind, beneath, the rod film, the back wall.
12. Safety: the dark walking strip; strap the dresser and the rod; one drawer at a time.
13. The shared side: you clean, they cull.
14. The signature: the before-and-after room card. The two photos to take. (Friction meter retired.)
15. Close and CTA: one idea to keep. Do one zone tonight. Next: the guest bedroom.
