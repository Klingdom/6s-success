# Chapter 37 Reader Discussion Questions: The Primary Bedroom

1. When did you last wash your pillows, rather than just the pillowcases?
2. Have you ever vacuumed your mattress or cleaned the wall behind your headboard? What did you find, or expect to find?
3. In a shared bedroom, how do you handle the other person's side without an argument?
4. Is your tall dresser strapped to the wall? Is your closet rod anchored into a stud?
5. What is on the strip of floor between your bed and the door right now that a bare foot would find at three in the morning?
6. How many sheet sets do you own for this bed, and how many do you actually use?
7. Is your phone charging on the nightstand tonight? What one job is keeping it there, and could a small clock do it?
8. How many fragrance bottles are standing on your dresser, and how many do you actually wear?
9. Are there clothes in your drawers or closet that do not fit the body you have this morning?
10. Which zone will you do first tonight, and what inputs do you need to gather before you start?
