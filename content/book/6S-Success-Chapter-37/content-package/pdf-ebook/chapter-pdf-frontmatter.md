# The Primary Bedroom

*The primary bedroom is the one room in the house whose whole job is to help you stop, so cleaning it is not about tidiness, it is about a room you can walk into in the dark, cross without stepping over anything, and lie down in on clean-breathing linen. This playbook points the whole loop at it one zone at a time, and gives you, for each of the six zones, the exact inputs to gather and the precise, step-by-step way to clean and shine it, so the room hands you back a good night.*

## How to read this chapter

This is a PLAYBOOK, not a method chapter. It applies the six-S loop you already own to one room, decomposed into six micro-zones, and its centerpiece is the per-zone clean-and-shine instructions with the exact inputs. It recaps but does not re-teach the nine-rule Shine method from Chapter 31; the new material is the bedroom layer (clean for sleep and air; clean where nobody looks; you clean, they cull; the dark walking strip and the furniture that tips) and the numbered steps for each zone.

Products are named as TYPES, never brands. The exhaustive product list is a separate appendix.

The friction meter, which reached its goal at Chapter 30, is retired for Part 9; the chapter closes on a before-and-after room signature and the two photos to take.
