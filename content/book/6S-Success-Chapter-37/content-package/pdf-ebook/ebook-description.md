# Chapter 37 Ebook Description: The Primary Bedroom

The seventh room playbook of Part Nine, and the first of the private rooms. The primary bedroom is the one room whose whole job is to help you stop, so the standard is not a showroom: it is a room you can walk into in the dark, cross without stepping over anything, and lie down in on clean-breathing linen.

This chapter gives the exact inputs and the numbered steps for each of the room's six zones, with four moves running through all of them: clean for sleep and air (wash the layer that touches your face and air out the night's moisture, because you breathe against this bed for a third of your life), clean where nobody looks (behind the headboard, under the bed, along the closet rod and the back wall), you clean, they cull (on a shared bed, wipe the other person's side but remove nothing, with medication the one safety exception), and the two night-time dangers (keep the bed-to-door strip bare, and strap the tall dresser and the closet rod).

You will learn the pass almost nobody does (washing the pillow and airing the mattress), how to clear a nightstand to five things or fewer, and how to clean a shared room without starting the argument that undoes it. The chapter closes on a before-and-after room signature and the two photos to take.
