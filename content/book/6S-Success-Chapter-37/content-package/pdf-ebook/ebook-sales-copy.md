# Chapter 37 Ebook Sales Copy: The Primary Bedroom

## Headline
The one room whose job is to help you stop. Here is exactly how to clean it, so it is clean for sleep and safe to cross in the dark.

## The promise
In the room you sleep in, wiping the surfaces is not the point. You breathe against this bed for a third of your life, so clean has to mean clean for sleep and for air. This chapter gives you the exact inputs and the numbered steps for each of the room's six zones, plus the habits that make it work: wash the layer that touches your face, clean where the dust hides, and, on a shared bed, clean up to the middle and no further.

## What you get in this chapter
- The bedroom Shine method: clean for sleep and air; clean where nobody looks; you clean, they cull; the dark walking strip and the furniture that tips.
- The six per-zone clean procedures, each with its inputs and numbered steps: the bed and bedding zone, your own nightstand, the other sleeper's nightstand, the dresser top, the dresser drawers, and the primary closet.
- The pass almost nobody does: washing the pillow and the protector and airing the mattress.
- The two night-time dangers, the dark walking strip and the tall furniture that tips, and how to fix them.
- The before-and-after room signature and the two photos to take.

## The common mistake it fixes
> Making the bed neatly over a pillow and a mattress that have not been washed or aired in a year, wiping only the surfaces you can see while the dust packs in behind the headboard, and quietly reorganising the other sleeper's side until it becomes the argument that undoes the whole room.
