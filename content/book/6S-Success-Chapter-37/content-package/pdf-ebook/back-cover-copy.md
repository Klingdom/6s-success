# Chapter 37 Back Cover Copy: The Primary Bedroom

## Back-cover text
The primary bedroom is the one room whose job is to help you stop, so wiping the surfaces is not the point. You breathe against this bed for a third of your life, so clean has to mean clean for sleep and for air.

The Primary Bedroom breaks the room into six zones and gives you, for each, the exact inputs to gather and the numbered steps to follow. Wash the layer that touches your face and air the mattress. Clean where the dust hides: behind the headboard, under the bed, the closet back wall. Clean up to the middle of a shared bed and no further. And keep the strip of floor you cross in the dark bare.

Do one zone tonight, and wake to a room that helps you sleep.
