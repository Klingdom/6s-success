# Chapter 37 B-Roll and Visual Notes: The Primary Bedroom

Warm, natural light. Product types only, no brand labels on screen.

## Visual vocabulary
- The two-state opener: a tired, buried before and a clean, aired, sleep-ready after.
- The recurring "clean for sleep and air" motif: a pillow and protector going to the wash and a bare mattress standing to air.

## Core shot list (seven shots)
1. The bed: stripping it right back, pulling the protectors off, and sending them to the wash with the sheets.
2. Airing the mattress and the duvet, then running the upholstery tool across the bare mattress face and into the piped seams.
3. The crevice tool down the gap behind the headboard, and a cloth down the skirting behind the frame.
4. The nightstand: lifting everything off, washing the shade and wiping the bulb, and treating the water-glass ring.
5. The shared side: wiping and vacuuming the other sleeper's top while leaving every object in place; a cloth stopping at the middle of the bed.
6. The dresser and drawers: pulling a drawer right out, brushing the runner, and an anti-tip strap going into a stud.
7. The closet: clearing to bare rod, wiping the grey film off the rod, checking the back wall for damp, and the shoes going onto a rack in pairs.

## On-screen text bank (use sparingly)
- Clean for sleep and air.
- Wash the layer that touches your face.
- Clean where nobody looks.
- You clean, they cull.
- Keep the dark strip bare.

## Lower thirds (rotate)
- Zone 1 to 6, one per segment.
- Do one zone tonight.

## Editing notes
- Close each cut on the before-and-after room signature from the doorway (bed, both nightstands, the floor strip).
- No presenter required; hands and surfaces only.
