# Chapter 37 Short Video Scripts (5): The Primary Bedroom

Each 30 to 90 seconds. Product types only.

## 1. Wash the pillow, not just the case (45s)
HOOK: When did you last wash your pillow? Not the case, the pillow.
SCRIPT: The cases get washed and the pillow inside almost never does, though it is the layer against your face all night. Strip the bed right back, send the protectors through the wash too, put the pillows themselves through when the seasons turn, and air the mattress and the duvet.
ON-SCREEN: Clean for sleep and air.
CTA: Chapter 37. Strip the bed all the way tonight.

## 2. Vacuum your mattress (40s)
HOOK: Have you ever vacuumed your mattress?
SCRIPT: With it bare, run the upholstery tool across the whole face and into the piped seams where dust and skin flakes settle, then rotate it and do the underside. While you are there, run the crevice tool down the gap behind the headboard where dust and hair pack against the wall.
CTA: Chapter 37. Do it next time you strip the bed.

## 3. Clean where nobody looks (35s)
HOOK: The dirt in your bedroom is where you never look.
SCRIPT: Behind the headboard, under the bed, along the closet rod, and on the closet's back wall. Pull the bed out, lift the drawers clear, clear the closet to bare rod, and clean the parts of the room you never see. That is where the damp and the dust start.
CTA: Chapter 37, clean where nobody looks.

## 4. You clean, they cull (40s)
HOOK: How to clean a shared bedroom without an argument.
SCRIPT: Every tidiness fight starts on the other person's side. The rule that ends it: you clean, they cull. You may wipe and vacuum their side, and you may not remove one object. Fix your own side to the standard and let the difference do the persuading.
CTA: Chapter 37. Clean your side to the standard.

## 5. Keep the dark strip bare (30s)
HOOK: The most important safety rule in your bedroom is on the floor.
SCRIPT: The strip between your bed and the door is what a bare foot finds at three in the morning. A bin, a shoe, or a charging cable across it is a fall waiting in the dark. Keep it bare, and strap the tall dresser while you are at it.
CTA: Chapter 37. Clear the strip tonight.
