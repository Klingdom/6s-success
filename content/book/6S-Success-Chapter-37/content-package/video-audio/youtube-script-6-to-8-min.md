# Chapter 37 YouTube Script (6 to 8 min): The Primary Bedroom

Alternating HOST and VISUAL. Product types only.

HOST: In the room you sleep in, wiping the surfaces is not the point. Today we clean a whole primary bedroom the way it actually needs it, for sleep and for air.
VISUAL: Two-state opener, buried before and clean after. A pillow going to the wash, a mattress airing.

HOST: The standard here is not a showroom. You breathe against this bed for a third of your life, so you wash the layer that touches your face and air out the night's moisture. And you clean where the room hides its dirt.
VISUAL: Stripping the bed; pulling the protectors off; the upholstery tool on the bare mattress.

HOST: Six zones. Start with the bed. Strip it right back, send the protectors through the wash, air the mattress and the duvet, then vacuum the whole face and into the seams.
VISUAL: The mattress standing to air; the vacuum working the piped seams.

HOST: Now the move nobody makes: the crevice tool down the gap behind the headboard, where dust and hair pack against the wall. That gap is the theme of the whole room.
VISUAL: The crevice tool behind the headboard; a cloth down the skirting.

HOST: Your nightstand: five things or fewer. Lift everything off, wash the shade and wipe the bulb, and treat the water-glass ring. Clean your reading glasses with a dry cloth only, never window cleaner.
VISUAL: The shade coming off; the water-glass ring; a dry cloth on glasses.

HOST: The other sleeper's side you clean but do not cull. Wipe the top, dust the lamp, vacuum the slot beside the bed, and remove nothing.
VISUAL: A cloth wiping one side, the far side untouched.

HOST: The dresser and drawers: pull each drawer right out, brush the runner, and strap the chest to a stud. The closet: clear to bare rod, wipe the grey film off the rod, and check the back wall for damp.
VISUAL: A drawer out and the runner brushed; an anti-tip strap; the closet cleared to bare rod.

HOST: Two dangers come out at night: keep the floor strip by the bed bare, and strap the tall furniture. A bedroom is not clean because the bed is made. Do one zone tonight. Next: the guest bedroom.
VISUAL: The before-and-after room signature from the doorway. End card.
