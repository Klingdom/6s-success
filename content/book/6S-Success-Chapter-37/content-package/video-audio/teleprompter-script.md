# Chapter 37 Teleprompter Script: The Primary Bedroom

Short lines, one breath each. Warm and calm.

The bedroom has one job. To help you stop.
So you do not clean it for tidiness.
You clean it for sleep. And for air.
You breathe against this bed for a third of your life.
So wash the layer that touches your face.
The cases get washed. The pillow inside almost never does.
Strip the bed right back.
Send the protectors through the wash too.
Put the pillows through when the seasons turn.
And air the mattress and the duvet.
Then clean where nobody looks.
Behind the headboard. Under the bed.
Along the closet rod. On the closet back wall.
Pull the bed out. Clear the closet to bare rod.
Clean the parts of the room you never see.
If you share the room, one rule keeps the peace.
You clean. They cull.
Wipe their side. Remove nothing.
Let the difference do the persuading.
And keep the strip of floor by the bed bare.
It is what a bare foot finds at three in the morning.
Strap the tall dresser. Anchor the rod.
Open one drawer at a time.
Do one zone tonight. Your own nightstand.
And wake to a room that helps you sleep.
