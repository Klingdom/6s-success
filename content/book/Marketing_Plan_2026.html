6S Success: Marketing Plan (2026)

1. Executive Summary

This marketing plan provides a high-level roadmap for launching the 6S Success ecosystem during the remainder of 2026. The objective is to establish authority, build an audience, validate products, and create recurring revenue through books, software, consulting, digital education, and retail recommendations.

2. Brand Positioning

Position 6S Success as the modern operating system for home organization, combining Lean thinking, behavioral science, AI coaching, and practical visual guidance.

3. Target Customers

  • Young professionals
  • Busy families
  • ADHD/neurodiverse households
  • DIY organizers
  • Professional organizers

4. Product Ecosystem

Book → Website → Email → AI App → Retail Products → Courses → Consulting → Certification.

5. Marketing Strategy

Focus on educational content, SEO, YouTube demonstrations, Reddit engagement, Pinterest, LinkedIn thought leadership, and strategic partnerships.

7. Launch Strategy

Soft launch with content, beta community, ARC readers, social proof, and coordinated launch across Amazon, website, and app.

8. Revenue Strategy

Books, consulting, AI subscriptions, affiliate revenue, retail reset kits, speaking, workshops, and future certification.

9. Aug–Dec 2026 Calendar

Complete book, launch website, grow email list, release MVP app, publish weekly educational content, begin consulting outreach.

10. KPIs

  • Email subscribers
  • Book sales
  • App downloads

  • Consulting leads
  • Affiliate revenue
