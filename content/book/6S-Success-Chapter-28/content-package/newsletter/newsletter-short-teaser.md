# Newsletter Short Teaser: Family Problem Solving

For a roundup or digest. 90 to 140 words.

---

You have the friendly check-in from the last chapter, and it works: it catches drift while it is small. But it keeps turning up a more stubborn finding, the same coats on the same chair, week after week. You keep fixing it, and it keeps coming back. A problem that keeps coming back is not a slip anyone is failing to correct. It is a signal that a small system is wrong, and a wrong system cannot be fixed by correcting it more often. So you stop patching the symptom and solve the cause, once, together. Two things make it work: you ask why it keeps happening and never who is to blame, and you solve it together, because people keep what they helped build.
