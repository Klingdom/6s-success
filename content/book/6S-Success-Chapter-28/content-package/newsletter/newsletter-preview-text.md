# Newsletter Preview Text (Preheader): Family Problem Solving

Ten options, each 35 to 90 characters, each paired with a subject line. Char counts noted.

1. Pairs with #1 (When the same problem keeps coming back, stop fixing it and start solving it: ask why it happens, not who is to blame, and change the system so it cannot easily happen again.)
   "Ask why it happens, not who is to blame, and change the system." (62 chars)

2. Pairs with #2 (A problem you have to fix more than twice is not a problem you are failing to fix. It is a problem you are failing to solve.)
   "Fixing puts the thing back; solving changes why it fell." (56 chars)

3. Pairs with #3 (Fixing is mopping the floor; solving is finding the leak.)
   "Stop mopping the same square of floor. Go and find the leak." (60 chars)

4. Pairs with #4 (Who finds a person to blame and changes nothing; why finds a system to fix and changes everything.)
   "One question changes nothing. The other changes everything." (59 chars)

5. Pairs with #5 (The coats are not on the chair because someone is lazy. They are on the chair because the hooks are in the wrong place.)
   "Not a lazy family, just a hook in the wrong place." (50 chars)

6. Pairs with #6 (The real cause is almost always a design flaw, not a character flaw.)
   "A missing hook, not a missing person. Look at the system first." (63 chars)

7. Pairs with #7 (You cannot easily change a person, but you can nearly always change a system.)
   "And that is genuinely good news for a tired household." (54 chars)

8. Pairs with #8 (A solution handed down is resented and quietly dropped; a solution the household worked out together is one it actually keeps.)
   "People keep what they helped build. So build it together." (57 chars)

9. Pairs with #9 (Bring one recurring annoyance to the household as "why does this keep happening?" and never "who keeps doing this?")
   "The best fix often comes from the person you were about to blame." (65 chars)

10. Pairs with #10 (When something keeps going wrong, do not look for someone to blame. Look, together, for the small system to fix.)
    "Fix the cause once, and you will never fix the symptom again." (60 chars)
