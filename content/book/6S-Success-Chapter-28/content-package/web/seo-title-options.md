# SEO Title Options: Chapter 28, Family Problem Solving

Five title options. Character counts are in parentheses. The canonical title is listed first.

1. Family Problem Solving (22)
   Angle: canonical chapter title. Use as the H1 and primary on-page heading, with "solve, do not just fix," "ask why, not who," "fix the system, not the person," "a design flaw, not a character flaw," and "solve it together" early in the body.

2. Stop Fixing It and Start Solving It (35)
   Angle: leads with the frozen rule. Names the whole move in a single line, that a problem which keeps coming back is not one you are failing to fix but one you are failing to solve, and answers the reader worn out by patching the same thing forever.

3. Ask Why, Not Who (17)
   Angle: leads with the method's heart. Says plainly where the looking points, at the system and not the person, so a recurring problem becomes a design flaw to change, never a character flaw to blame, and is quotable and snippet-ready.

4. Fix the System, Not the Person (30)
   Angle: names the no-blame move in the reader's own words. Reframes a returning problem from a family fault into a small system to change, and answers a real search for how to solve a household problem without blaming anyone.

5. A Problem You Fix Twice Is One You Failed to Solve (50)
   Angle: leads with the fixing-versus-solving logic at the heart of the chapter. Concrete and freeing, it says the thing a tired reader most needs to hear, that the exhaustion is not a discipline failure but a signal to stop patching the symptom and change the cause.
