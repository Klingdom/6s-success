3. Start solving one thing you are tired of fixing (the chapter action)
   Take the one thing in your home you are tired of fixing, the pile that always returns to the same spot, and ask out loud, "why does this keep happening here?", three or four times, until you reach something you can actually change. You just started solving it instead of fixing it. It costs about five minutes, and it is the smallest possible proof of the whole idea: a problem you have to fix more than twice is not a problem you are failing to fix, it is a problem you are failing to solve, and the moment you ask why instead of who, you are looking at the small system you can change.
