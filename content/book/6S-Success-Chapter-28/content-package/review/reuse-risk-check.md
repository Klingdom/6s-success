## Same-day collision
The calendar avoids two assets sharing a hook on the same day. If your LinkedIn and Facebook audiences overlap heavily, push one back a day so the rule or the "fixing is mopping the floor, solving is finding the leak" line does not land twice in one day.

## Cross-posting risk
- The LinkedIn carousel outline (`linkedin/linkedin-carousel-outline.md`) and the slides carousel copy (`slides/linkedin-carousel-copy.md`) cover the same family-problem-solving content. Post only one to LinkedIn (use the copy-ready slides file; keep the outline as planning).
- The LinkedIn newsletter (`linkedin/linkedin-newsletter-version.md`) and the email newsletter share structure; do not send both to the same subscriber the same day.

## Duplicate-content / SEO risk
- Web landing intro, ebook description, and LinkedIn article overlap in phrasing. Set the chapter page as the SEO canonical and mark syndicated copies with a canonical link.

## Cross-chapter risk (specific to Chapter 28)
- Chapter 28 is the THIRD chapter of Part Eight and the SECOND-CONCRETE-MECHANISM chapter. Keep it distinct from Chapter 27 behind it (which gave the friendly check-in that catches drift while it is small), from Chapter 26 (which opened Sustain with the concept and mindset), and from the later Sustain practices ahead of it. Chapter 28 uses the audit's findings from Chapter 27 (the audit finds the recurring problems; this chapter solves them) and the keeping mindset Chapter 26 built, but assets must NOT re-teach either; they reference the chosen-not-imposed ownership point from Chapter 24 in ONE light line only and must NOT re-teach it; and they must NOT re-teach Sort, Straighten, Shine, Safety, or Standardize (a one-line callback is fine, a re-teach is not). Grep confirms the assets deliver the shared, blameless problem-solving and its reframe (a returning problem is a signal that a small system is wrong; fixing is not solving; ask why, not who; solve together; the four-step method) and do not rebuild the earlier material.
- Do not let this chapter slide FORWARD into the later practices. Ch 28 assets keep it to the shared, occasional problem-solving (run when a recurring problem surfaces) and its reframe, and must NOT build the daily 6S rhythm (Chapter 29) or the recurring 6S event or book close (Chapter 30). This fence is held: the later practices are named in the package only as the chapters still ahead, never built.
- The friction-meter beat is specific to this chapter, and it is a small MOVE along the last-gap path, NOT a milestone. The live needle moves a short step from where Chapter 27 left it, at (67.3,154.96), to (67.2,154.97), a little further along the dashed last-gap path toward the GOAL crosshair at (67,155), tagged "Sustain, step 2," Chapter 27's position now a faint ghost just behind and Chapter 26's fainter still, and behind the needle the anti-backslide catch stays fully engaged and locked. Do not overstate this as a milestone and do not understate the move; the goal stays a step ahead, reached only at the very end of the book (Chapter 30), when all six S's are done.

All derived assets are faithful to that draft; re-sync if the chapter is revised.
- Quote cards are verbatim: every card in `graphics/quote-card-copy.md` (10 cards) verified byte-identical to a quoted line in `canonical/chapter-quotes.md` (0 mismatches). Nothing borrowed from outside the book; no brand-name products are named, and the source's Sustain step (utilize 6S to solve day-to-day problems, establish 6S problem-solving procedures) and the Toyota / TPS origin are used lightly, home-scaled, not overclaimed.
