## Full summary (about 250 words)

Chapter 28 is the third chapter of Part Eight and adds the second piece of keeping-machinery on top of Chapter 27's friendly audit. The audit is good at catching drift while it is small, but it keeps turning up a more stubborn kind of finding: not a one-off slip, but the same coats on the same chair, the same dishes by the sink, the same shoes in the same doorway, week after week. You keep fixing them, and they keep coming back, and a small, tired frustration builds. This chapter is the reframe that dissolves it.

A problem that keeps coming back is not a slip anyone is failing to correct; it is a signal that a small system is wrong, and a wrong system cannot be fixed by correcting it more often, any more than a leaking pipe can be fixed by mopping the floor more diligently. Fixing puts the thing back; solving changes why it fell, so you never put it back again. Two things make solving work, more than any technique. First, you ask why it keeps happening and never who is to blame, because who finds a person and changes nothing, while why finds a system and changes everything; the real cause is almost always a design flaw, not a character flaw. Second, you solve it together, because the person who lives with a problem knows why it happens and keeps the fix alive. The needle takes its honest second Sustain step: one returning source of friction removed for good.
