# Reuse Risk Check: Chapter 22 Content Package

When one chapter becomes fifty-plus assets, the risk is overexposure: the same line or image hitting the same follower until it feels canned. This check flags where that can happen.

## High-frequency elements
| Element | Appears in | Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Finding hazards makes a home safer once. Preventing them and preparing for them keep it safe for good (the rule) | Canonical, CTA, LinkedIn, X, quote card, graphics, infographic, HTML | A follower on several platforms sees the hero rule repeatedly | Calendar staggers it; vary the phrasing around it per platform |
| See it, remove it, prevent it, prepare for it (the four moves) | Canonical, CTA, LinkedIn, X, quote card, graphics, slides | Medium to High | The whole of Safety in four beats; the most portable line. Use across formats, not stacked on one day. |
| You can chase hazards one at a time forever ... raise the whole home's floor (baseline reframe) | Canonical, LinkedIn, X, FB, quote card | Medium to High | The turn the chapter rests on; the most shareable idea. Use across formats, not stacked on one day. |
| Some harm you cannot prevent, only prepare for ... Preparation is love for the day you hope never comes (preparation reframe) | Canonical, LinkedIn, X, FB, quote card | Medium | The emotional core; vary the surrounding worked example (alarm / extinguisher / walked exit). |
| A hunt clears the hazards that are here ... making the safe way the easy way (prevention reframe) | Canonical, LinkedIn, X, FB, quote card | Medium | The prevention logic; pair with a different one of the three habits each time. |

## Same-day collision
The calendar avoids two assets sharing a hook on the same day. If your LinkedIn and Facebook audiences overlap heavily, push one back a day so the rule or the baseline reframe does not land twice in one day.

## Cross-posting risk
- The LinkedIn carousel outline (`linkedin/linkedin-carousel-outline.md`) and the slides carousel copy (`slides/linkedin-carousel-copy.md`) cover the same Safety content. Post only one to LinkedIn (use the copy-ready slides file; keep the outline as planning).
- The LinkedIn newsletter (`linkedin/linkedin-newsletter-version.md`) and the email newsletter share structure; do not send both to the same subscriber the same day.

## Duplicate-content / SEO risk
- Web landing intro, ebook description, and LinkedIn article overlap in phrasing. Set the chapter page as the SEO canonical and mark syndicated copies with a canonical link.

## Cross-chapter risk (specific to Chapter 22)
- Chapter 22 is the THIRD and FINAL chapter of Part Six and the COMPLETE-THE-FOURTH-S chapter. Keep it distinct from the two chapters behind it: Chapter 20 opened Safety with the lens and a first pass on the obvious dangers; Chapter 21 ran the thorough, room-by-room hazard hunt and removed each danger. Chapter 22 adds the two forward moves removing cannot make, prevention and preparation, and completes the fourth S. Assets must USE the safety lens and the hunted home as the starting point but must NOT re-teach the lens, re-derive Heinrich's pyramid, re-run the hazard hunt, or re-tell the 5S+1 history. Grep confirms the assets run prevention and preparation and do not rebuild Chapters 20 or 21.
- Do not let this chapter slide FORWARD into making the whole home last. Ch 22 assets keep prevention and preparation LIGHT (safer defaults, simple habits, working alarms, a walked exit plan) and must NOT build documented standards or maintenance schedules (Standardize) or long-term adherence and audits (Sustain). This fence is held: the making-it-last work is named in the package only as "the next chapter" and "two S's remain," never built. The chapter is explicit that prevention here is "just the safe way, made easy enough that it happens on its own," and "not a schedule to be written down and enforced; that kind of documented standard is a later S."
- Do NOT re-open Sort, Straighten, or Shine, or re-teach cleaning or inspection (Chapters 16 to 19).
- The friction-meter beat is specific to this chapter: the needle REACHES the fourth milestone, SAFETY COMPLETE, at (67.6,154.9), a slate milestone marker beside Sort complete, Straighten complete, and Shine complete, with Chapter 21's second-Safety-step position (68.0,154.8) now the dashed ghost behind it and the GOAL crosshair still ahead at (67,155). This is a MILESTONE (four of six S's complete), not a step. The step onto the mark is small and that is stated honestly. Do not imply the goal has been reached or that the whole home is now made to last; the goal stays ahead because Standardize and Sustain still follow.

## Originality and source-fidelity risk
- The chapter is a draft authored in the book voice, grounded in the 6S source ("Principles of 6S: Safety Step": establish a goal of zero accidents and injuries, develop safety consciousness, safety integrated into all the S's, and the Tools for Improving Safety), not supplied verbatim source. All derived assets are faithful to that draft; re-sync if the chapter is revised.
- Quote cards are verbatim: every card in `graphics/quote-card-copy.md` verified byte-identical to a quoted line in `canonical/chapter-quotes.md` (0 mismatches). Nothing borrowed from outside the book; no brand-name products are named, and the Safety Step principles and Tools for Improving Safety origin are referenced lightly, home-scaled, not overclaimed, with no alarmist statistics, no graphic injury, and no graphic emergency detail. Preparation is framed as calm love, not dread; "prevent" and "remove / eliminate" are topic words used calmly, never a war on hazards (the force / violence scan returned zero files across the whole package).

## Recommendations
1. Rotate CTA wording (highest-value, lowest-effort fix; several posts close on "Read the free chapter").
2. Pick one carousel version for LinkedIn.
3. Set the chapter page as the SEO canonical.
4. Keep Chapter 22 from blurring into its neighbors: distinct from Chapters 20 and 21 behind it (the lens, the first pass, the hazard hunt) and distinct from the making-it-last work ahead of it (Standardize, Sustain). Chapter 22 is the COMPLETE-THE-FOURTH-S chapter: prevention, preparation, the four moves, and the Safety-complete milestone.
5. Re-sync all assets if the authored chapter changes after author review.
