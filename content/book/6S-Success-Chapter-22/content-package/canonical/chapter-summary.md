## Full summary (about 250 words)

Chapter 22 is the third and final chapter of Part Six and completes Safety, the fourth of the six S's. Chapter 20 opened Safety and taught the reader to see hazards; Chapter 21 taught the reader to remove them thoroughly, room by room. This chapter delivers the two forward-looking moves a hazard hunt cannot make, and with them the fourth S is whole.

It begins with an honest pair of facts. A home does not hold still: the hunt was a snapshot, and after the shutter clicks new cords, new heights, and dead batteries quietly flow back. And some harm cannot be removed in advance at all: a fire can still start, a person can still fall. So the chapter raises the whole home's baseline rather than chasing hazards one at a time.

Prevention keeps new hazards from taking hold by making the safe way the easy way, in three small habits: choose the safer default at the moment a thing arrives, give the dangerous things a home so away and safe are the same place, and re-hunt lightly now and then to catch drift. It stays light, never a documented schedule.

Preparation readies the home for the harm you cannot prevent: working smoke and carbon-monoxide alarms kept alive on every floor, a fire extinguisher within reach, a walked exit plan and one meeting spot, and the few numbers and steps everyone knows. Preparation is love for the day you hope never comes. Safety is four moves: see, remove, prevent, prepare. The needle reaches the SAFETY COMPLETE milestone; the goal is still ahead.
