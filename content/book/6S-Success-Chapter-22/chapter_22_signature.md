## Motifs
- **Removing versus improving.** Removing clears what is here; improving raises the floor against what is not here yet.
- **A home drifts.** It does not stay hunted; prevention is the defense that scales.
- **Make the safe way the easy way.** Prevention is a design choice, not vigilance.
- **Prepare for what you cannot prevent.** Some harm can only be readied for; the alarm, the exit, the plan.
- **Preparation is love.** Being ready for the day you hope never comes is a form of care.
- **Four moves, complete.** See, remove, prevent, prepare: the whole of Safety, and the fourth S done.
