### Card 1 (verbatim)
> "A visual control makes the right thing obvious without a word."

- Attribution: 6S Success: Home Edition · Chapter 14

### Card 2 (verbatim)
> "Could someone who has never lived here put this away correctly, at a glance, without opening it, reading a list, or asking? If they can, the home speaks for itself. If they would have to ask, it is still silent."

- Attribution: 6S Success: Home Edition · Chapter 14
- Design: The check card, the chapter's hero test. Slate Fraunces on panel, small slate `THE GLANCE
  TEST` eyebrow. Longer, give it width; best on landscape. Keep it calm and non-coercive; it is a
  test of the spot, not of the person.

### Card 3 (verbatim)
> "You were the label. The system lived in your head, so the room only worked when you were there to run it. A visual control moves that knowledge out of your head and onto the shelf, where everyone can read it, including you on a tired day."

- Attribution: 6S Success: Home Edition · Chapter 14
- Design: The reframe card, the emotional turn of the chapter. Ink Fraunces on paper, generous
  space, small terracotta `THE REFRAME` eyebrow. Longer, let it breathe. A relief, not a reproach;
  keep it warm.

### Card 4 (verbatim)
> "The home is right there, a foot away, correct in every respect. It simply never told anyone."

- Attribution: 6S Success: Home Edition · Chapter 14
- Design: The starting-point card, the gap this chapter closes. Ink Fraunces on paper, plenty of
  air, small terracotta `THE SILENT HOME` eyebrow. Names the problem in one plain line.

### Card 5 (verbatim)
> "A home can be well chosen and well placed and still keep its knowledge to itself, because the rule for what belongs there sits in exactly one place: your memory."

- Attribution: 6S Success: Home Edition · Chapter 14
- Design: The diagnosis card. Slate quote on panel, small slate `THE RULE LIVES IN ONE PLACE`
  eyebrow. Longer, give it width. Names why a good home can still leave everyone guessing.

### Card 6 (verbatim)
> "None of this is anyone failing. It is a design gap. The knowledge exists; it has just never been written down anywhere the thing itself can be seen."

- Attribution: 6S Success: Home Edition · Chapter 14
- Design: The anti-shame card, the most reassuring line in the chapter. Ink Fraunces on paper,
  generous space, small terracotta `NOT A FAILING, A DESIGN GAP` eyebrow. Keep it calm and
  non-coercive, no guilt cues.

### Card 7 (verbatim)
> "The label says what lives here; the outline also says whether it is home right now."

- Attribution: 6S Success: Home Edition · Chapter 14
- Design: The two-cues card. Slate on panel, small slate `LABEL AND OUTLINE` eyebrow. Short and
  clean; pair it with a tiny row of a labeled bin beside a hook with a drawn silhouette. Let the
  contrast land.

### Card 8 (verbatim)
> "One cue per spot, aimed at the one confusion that spot produces, is nearly always plenty."

- Attribution: 6S Success: Home Edition · Chapter 14
- Design: The discipline card. Ink Fraunces on paper, plenty of air, small terracotta `ONE CUE PER
  SPOT` eyebrow. Short and dry; let the sentence stand. The rule that keeps cues from piling up.

### Card 9 (verbatim)
> "Whenever you can choose a container whose form declares its job, you get the control for free, with nothing to print and nothing to draw."

- Attribution: 6S Success: Home Edition · Chapter 14
- Design: The umbrella-stand card, the best small lesson in the chapter. Green quote on panel, a
  green calm-dot, small green `THE CONTROL FOR FREE` eyebrow. Pair it with a tiny umbrella-stand
  glyph whose shape already says what it holds.

### Card 10 (verbatim)
> "A good home does not need you to explain it. Put the knowledge where the thing lives, a label, an outline, a clear jar, and the room starts telling everyone what belongs where, so keeping order stops being your job alone."

- Attribution: 6S Success: Home Edition · Chapter 14
- Design: The closing card, from One Idea to Keep. Ink on paper, warm and confident, plenty of air.
  Pair it with a tiny trio of a labeled bin, a hook with a drawn silhouette, and a clear jar.
  Eyebrow `ONE IDEA TO KEEP`.
