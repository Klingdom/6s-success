- The third Straighten step on the friction meter (nothing left the house; the room stopped leaning on one person's memory)

## Cross-chapter / reuse cautions
- Keep the Part 4 chapters distinct: Ch 12 gives a home, Ch 13 places it by frequency, Ch 14 makes it speak, Ch 15 maps the whole room.
- Hold the Chapter 15 idea (one shared map of the whole room) as a teaser only, so Ch 14 and Ch 15 do not blur.
- Do not let visual controls drift into the later Standardize S (routines, schedules, household standards). Ch 14 makes the place visible; keeping it up over time is a later S.
- The friction-meter "third Straighten step" is specific to Ch 14; do not reuse the first- or second-step framing here.
- Set the chapter page as the SEO canonical; point syndicated copies (LinkedIn article, Medium) back to it.
- The chapter avoids the factory term "shadow board" in favor of a plain description ("the workshop board," "painted shape"); keep repurposed copy consistent with that plain framing.
