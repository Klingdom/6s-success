# Brand Voice Check: Chapter 14 Content Package

The voice of *6S Success: Home Edition* is warm, plain-spoken, and quietly confident. Chapter 14 is the third Straighten chapter, and its job has an emotional core: it lifts the weight of being the household's memory off one person. The voice must stay light and anti-shame, and it must make an ordinary idea (label the shelf) feel like relief rather than another chore.

## Voice fingerprint (from the authored final HTML and manuscript)
- **Warm and personal.** Talks to "you," returns to the entryway, then imagines a friend locking up while you are away.
- **Concrete over abstract.** The visual control is taught through the workshop board of painted tool outlines (an empty outline shows a missing tool at a glance) and the charger-cable drawer that only you can read.
- **Anti-shame.** "None of this is anyone failing. It is a design gap." A silent home is a design gap, not a family's failing.
- **Weight-lifting reframe.** "You were the label." "You have been the living index of the house, consulted a dozen times a day." The chapter's kindest move is taking that job off the reader.
- **Honest about scope.** Nothing leaves the house, so the friction move is justified on a real basis: the room stops leaning on one person's memory.

## Specific wins
- "You were the label" is the most shareable, most human line in the chapter and travels intact across channels.
- The Glance Test ("could a stranger put this away without asking?") gives readers a concrete, testable check.
- The umbrella-stand point (a container whose form declares its job is a control for free) is a sophisticated idea kept plain.

## Voice risks to watch
- **Hero vocabulary is dense.** "control," "label," "home," "glance," "spot" recur because they are the subject; expected, not a tic, but vary the surrounding language when excerpting.
- **CTA sameness.** Rotate the LinkedIn and X sign-offs.
- **Keep it relief, not instruction.** The chapter's tone is a weight lifted; do not let any asset slide into a lecturing, do-this-now register.

## Banned-language audit
- Widened dash scan (em dash, en dash, and spaced-hyphen " - " used as a separator, excluding markdown list bullets): clean across the package; labels use the middot "·".
- No hype words and no force/violence metaphors in any shipped asset. Note the chapter avoids factory jargon: it teaches the concept as "the workshop board" with "painted shape" outlines, never the term "shadow board."

## Verdict
Voice is consistent and intact across all twelve packages, and the light, anti-shame, weight-lifting job of Chapter 14 is honored throughout. Approve the voice. The chapter itself still needs author sign-off, since it was authored in the book voice rather than supplied from source.
