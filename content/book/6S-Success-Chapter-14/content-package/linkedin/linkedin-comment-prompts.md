# Chapter 14 LinkedIn Comment Prompts: Visual Controls for the Home

Five prompts to start genuine discussion. Drop one in the comments of a post or article to invite replies. Anti-shame voice: a silent home is a design gap, never a family's failing.

---

1. Try the Glance Test on one spot right now. Pick a closed drawer or bin and ask: could someone who has never lived here put a thing away there correctly, at a glance, without opening it or asking? Name one spot in your home that passes, and one that quietly fails.

2. Here is the honest one. Which spot in your house only works because you are the one standing in it? The drawer whose logic lives entirely in your head, that a guest would have to text you about. No blame owed, it is a design gap, not a failing. Naming it is the first step to letting the shelf carry the rule instead of you.

3. You were the label. If you have ever felt that a room only holds together when you personally tend it, that everyone leans on you to know where the tape is, tell us about it. What is the one thing your household always asks you to locate?

4. Label the spot, not just the thing. What is one bin, hook, or shelf edge you could label this week so it greets the next thing that belongs there? Bonus: which container in your home is already a control by its shape, the way an umbrella stand needs no label at all?

5. One cue per spot, aimed at the one confusion that spot produces, is nearly always plenty. What does one of your mystery spots keep getting wrong, and which single cue would answer it: a label for what belongs, an outline for what is missing, or a boundary for when it is full?
