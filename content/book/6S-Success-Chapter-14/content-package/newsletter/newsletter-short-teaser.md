# Newsletter Short Teaser: Visual Controls for the Home

For a roundup or digest. 90 to 140 words.

---

Every entryway keeper has a home now, and every home is well placed. But each one is blank, obvious only to the person who built it, so a guest still stands there asking where a thing goes. This chapter fixes that without adding or removing a thing. A visual control makes the right thing obvious without a word: a label names what belongs, an outline shows whether it is home, a boundary shows when a spot is full. Judge each spot by the Glance Test, could a stranger put this away without asking? The reframe lands quietly: you were the label. Move the rule onto the shelf, and the friction meter takes its third Straighten step.
