# Facebook Group Discussion Post (Home and Organizing Groups)

Written for a home-and-organizing Facebook group. Leads with a genuinely useful nugget (the Glance Test and labeling the spot), then asks an open question to get people talking. Friendly group tone, soft CTA at the very end, and written so it does NOT read like an ad. No em dashes.

---

Okay, the small test that finally showed me which of my tidy spots were secretly useless, and then I want to hear about the spot in your house that only you understand.

For a long time I thought a spot was done once it was neat and everything had a home. My drawers were sorted, my shelves were arranged, and I could put anything away without thinking. What I missed is that I could do it because I built it. The knowledge lived in my head, and nowhere else.

Here is the test that showed me. Picture handing your house to someone who has never set foot in it, a pet sitter, a babysitter, a friend watering the plants. Then ask of any spot: could they put this away correctly, at a glance, without opening it, reading a list, or asking? If they can, the home speaks for itself. If they would have to text you a photo and wait, it is still silent, however tidy it looks.

Most of my spots failed, and they failed invisibly, because the ones that only work thanks to me feel obvious to me. The fix was smaller than I expected. A label on the bin, a clear jar instead of an opaque one, a little outline where a thing hangs. And one rule that changed everything: label the spot, not just the thing. A label on the item walks off with the item; a label on the shelf stays to greet the next thing that belongs there.

Said without blame, because nobody sets out to make a room only they can run. It just happens, one remembered spot at a time.

So here is what I am genuinely curious about.

What is the one spot in your house that only you understand, the drawer or shelf where everyone still asks you where things go? And if you gave it a single label or a clear container this week, do you think anyone else could finally use it without asking?

I will go first in the comments with my junk drawer, which I have been personally operating as a help desk for years. 😄
