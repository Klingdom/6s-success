# Chapter 14 Summary: Visual Controls for the Home

*6S Success: Home Edition · Part Four, Straighten*

## One-line summary

Chapter 14 takes the third Straighten step by making each already-placed home explain itself with a visual control, a label, an outline, a boundary, plus seeing-in and color, so the rule for what belongs where moves out of one person's head and onto the shelf, and anyone can find a thing and put it back at a glance, without asking.

## Short summary (about 100 words)

Every keeper has a home now, and every home sits in the right spot, but each one is silent, obvious only to the person who built it. Chapter 14 fixes that without adding or removing a thing. A visual control is any simple cue that makes the answer plain without a person, a conversation, or a list. ## Full summary (about 250 words)

Chapter 14 is the third chapter of Part Four, and it keeps working inside Straighten. Chapter 12 gave every keeper a home; Chapter 13 placed each home by how often you reach for it. Yet every home is still blank, so the only person who can use the room without guessing is the person who built it. A home can be well chosen and well placed and still keep its knowledge to itself, because the rule for what belongs there sits in exactly one place: your memory. A silent home is not a failing; it is a design gap. The chapter fences itself off plainly: it does not re-assign homes, re-place them by frequency, map the whole room, build routines for keeping order, or re-open Sort.

A visual control lifts the rule out of your memory and onto the thing itself, the way a workshop shadow board draws each tool's silhouette so a glance shows where everything goes and one empty shape shows what is missing. The hero check is the Glance Test: could someone who has never lived here put this away correctly, at a glance, without opening it, reading a list, or asking? Three simple cues cover most of a house, the label, the outline, and the boundary, with seeing-in and color as lighter helps. Match one cue to the one confusion a spot keeps producing, and stop there. The reframe lands: you were the label. The entryway keepers get voices, the cues scale to the pantry, kids' zone, junk drawer, and linen closet, and the friction meter takes its third Straighten step.
