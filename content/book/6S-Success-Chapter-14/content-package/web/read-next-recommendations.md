# Read Next Recommendations: Chapter 14, Visual Controls for the Home

1. Chapter 15, The Room Map
   The next step, and the one that completes Part Four. Each home now speaks for itself, but a newcomer still has to walk the whole house discovering them one at a time, because they meet each home alone, in the moment they happen to need it. There is no one picture yet, nothing that shows the arrangement all at once. Chapter 15 gives the household one shared map of where everything lives, so the whole room can be read in a single look, and Straighten is complete.

2. Chapter 13, Store by Frequency of Use
   The setup. Before this chapter could run, every home needed to sit in the right spot. Chapter 13 took the second Straighten step and placed each home by how often your hand goes to it, the daily things within easy reach and the rare things a polite step further off. What it left behind was a row of homes that were well chosen and well placed, and completely silent, obvious only to the person who built them. This chapter gives each one a way to explain itself.

3. Make one spot speak (the chapter action)
   Pick one bin, drawer, or shelf whose contents are a mystery from the outside, and give it one label or one clear container. Then ask someone else in the house to put a thing away there. If they get it right without asking, the home just started speaking.

4. The Glance Test card (free resource)
   A printable plus phone wallpaper with the one-glance check and the label, outline, and boundary trio, so the whole idea fits in a glance.

5. The Visual Controls checklist (free resource)
   A printable to walk each silent spot and pick one control, a label, an outline, a boundary, a clear container, or a color.
