Five title options. Character counts are in parentheses. The canonical title is listed first.

1. Visual Controls for the Home (29)
   Angle: canonical book title. Use as the H1 and primary on-page heading, with "visual control," "at a glance," and "put it away without asking" early in the body.

2. A Visual Control Makes the Right Thing Obvious Without a Word (60)
   Angle: leads with the frozen rule of the chapter. Names the whole idea in one line and is highly quotable.

3. The Glance Test: Could a Stranger Put This Away Without Asking? (61)
   Angle: opens with the hero check readers can run on their own rooms in one minute. Concrete and memorable.

4. Label the Spot, Not Just the Thing (34)
   Angle: leads with the single counterintuitive rule most labeling guides miss. Short and quotable.

5. Make Your Home Self Explaining, So Anyone Can Put Things Back (60)
   Angle: matches the outcome readers arrive wanting. Promises the payoff in plain words.
