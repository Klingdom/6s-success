## Variant B (held is not the same as kept)

- og:title: Held Is Not the Same as Kept.
- og:description: Standardize wrote the home down and showed it in pictures, so it holds its shape without leaning on your memory. But that only stops the passive slide, the quiet forgetting of the best way. It cannot stop the world, and the world is relentless: new things arrive, seasons change, people come and go, and no standard survives a busy season untended. A home is not a machine you set and leave running; it is a living thing, and living things have to be kept alive. That keeping is Sustain, the sixth S, and it is lighter than fixing: a home tended a little every day never needs the big dreaded overhaul.
