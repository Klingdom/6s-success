# SEO Title Options: Chapter 26, Sustain: Keeping the Gains

Five title options. Character counts are in parentheses. The canonical title is listed first.

1. Sustain: Keeping the Gains (26)
   Angle: canonical chapter title. Use as the H1 and primary on-page heading, with "Sustain," "the sixth S," "held is not the same as kept," "a practice, not a project," "habit, not willpower," and "second nature" early in the body.

2. You Do Not Finish 6S. You Keep It. (34)
   Angle: leads with the hero rule. Names the whole idea in a single line, that keeping a home is ongoing and not a thing you complete, and is quotable and snippet-ready for the reader who is tired of big pushes that collapse a few months later.

3. Held Is Not the Same as Kept (28)
   Angle: leads with the distinction the whole part turns on. Says plainly why an organized home still slides back, that the world is relentless and no standard survives untended, and answers a real search for why the house gets messy again no matter what you do.

4. Sustain Is a Practice, Not a Project (36)
   Angle: names the hero device in the reader's own words. Reframes keeping from a burden to be finished into an ordinary, ongoing way of living, and maps the whole chapter to one calm promise: you keep the home as you go.

5. Keeping Is Lighter Than Fixing (30)
   Angle: leads with the relief at the heart of the chapter. Concrete and freeing, it says the thing a tired reader most needs to hear, that a home tended a little every day never needs the big dreaded overhaul, so the household that keeps its home does less total work, not more.
