## Full summary (about 250 words)

Chapter 26 is the first chapter of Part Eight and opens Sustain, the sixth and final S. Five of the six S's are done: the home is cleared, arranged, clean, safe, and, with Standardize, written down and shown in pictures so it holds its shape without leaning on memory. And here is the last thing the book has to say. Held is not the same as kept. Standardize stopped the passive slide back, the quiet decay of people forgetting the best way. But it cannot stop the world, and the world is relentless: new things arrive, seasons turn, people come and go, and no standard survives untended. A home is not a machine you set and leave running; it is a living thing that has to be kept alive.

That keeping is Sustain, and it is different in kind. The first five S's are things you do and finish. Sustain is the one you never finish, because it is not a task with an end. You do not finish 6S; you keep it. And it runs on habit, not willpower, made small, normal, shared, and low enough in effort to survive the weeks nobody has energy to spare. Keeping is lighter than fixing, because a home tended a little every day never needs the dreaded overhaul. Kept long enough, 6S becomes simply how the home is: second nature, and nearly free. The needle holds, but a faint dashed path to the goal appears for the first time, the last gap Sustain closes.
