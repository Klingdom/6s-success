## Same-day collision
The calendar avoids two assets sharing a hook on the same day. If your LinkedIn and Facebook audiences overlap heavily, push one back a day so the rule or the held-is-not-kept line does not land twice in one day.

## Cross-posting risk
- The LinkedIn carousel outline (`linkedin/linkedin-carousel-outline.md`) and the slides carousel copy (`slides/linkedin-carousel-copy.md`) cover the same Sustain-mindset content. Post only one to LinkedIn (use the copy-ready slides file; keep the outline as planning).
- The LinkedIn newsletter (`linkedin/linkedin-newsletter-version.md`) and the email newsletter share structure; do not send both to the same subscriber the same day.

## Duplicate-content / SEO risk
- Web landing intro, ebook description, and LinkedIn article overlap in phrasing. Set the chapter page as the SEO canonical and mark syndicated copies with a canonical link.

## Cross-chapter risk (specific to Chapter 26)
- Chapter 26 is the FIRST chapter of Part Eight and the OPEN-THE-SIXTH-S chapter. Keep it distinct from Chapters 23 to 25 behind it (which built and completed Standardize) and from the concrete Sustain practices ahead of it. Chapter 26 uses the standards Standardize built, but assets must NOT re-teach the concept of a standard, the three tools, or the visual standard, and must NOT re-teach Sort, Straighten, Shine, or Safety (a one-line callback is fine, a re-teach is not). Grep confirms the assets deliver the Sustain mindset (held is not kept, practice not project, habit not willpower, second nature) and do not rebuild the earlier material.
- Do not let this chapter slide FORWARD into the concrete practices. Ch 26 assets keep it to the concept and mindset and must NOT build the gentle check-in / audit (Chapter 27), the family problem-solving method (Chapter 28), the daily 6S rhythm (Chapter 29, named only as "a little, often" in principle), or the recurring 6S event or book close (Chapter 30). This fence is held: the concrete practices are named in the package only as "the rest of Part Eight" still ahead, never built.
- The friction-meter beat is specific to this chapter, and it is a HOLD with a new last-gap path, NOT a milestone and NOT a move. The live needle stays exactly where Chapter 25 left it, at (67.4,154.95), the STANDARDIZE COMPLETE mark, the fifth on the dial, because a chapter about a mindset does not change a single thing in the rooms; behind the needle the anti-backslide catch stays fully engaged and locked. What is new, for the first time on the dial, is a faint DASHED path from the needle to the GOAL crosshair at (67,155), naming the last gap Sustain closes over the years. Do not overstate this as a milestone (unlike Chapter 25) and do not understate the new element; the goal stays a step ahead, now with its closing path shown, and is reached only at the very end of the book, when all six S's are done.

## Originality and source-fidelity risk
- The chapter is a draft authored in the book voice, grounded in the 6S source ("Principles of 6S: Sustain Step": sustain the gains, develop a sense of responsibility and self-discipline, utilize 6S to solve day-to-day problems, tied lightly to standard work and Toyota / TPS), not supplied verbatim source. All derived assets are faithful to that draft; re-sync if the chapter is revised.
- Quote cards are verbatim: every card in `graphics/quote-card-copy.md` (10 cards) verified byte-identical to a quoted line in `canonical/chapter-quotes.md` (0 mismatches). Nothing borrowed from outside the book; no brand-name products are named, and the Sustain principles (Shitsuke, sustain the gains, self-discipline, continuous improvement) and the Toyota / TPS origin are used lightly, home-scaled, not overclaimed. Keeping is framed as a little ongoing care and a shared choice, warm and human; "sustain" and "self-discipline" are topic words used warmly, never punitive discipline, enforce, comply, audit (punitive), or lock down (the force / control scan returned zero control uses across the whole package).

## Recommendations
1. Rotate CTA wording (highest-value, lowest-effort fix; several posts close on "Read the free chapter").
2. Pick one carousel version for LinkedIn.
3. Set the chapter page as the SEO canonical.
4. Keep Chapter 26 from blurring into its neighbors: distinct from Chapters 23 to 25 (Standardize, already delivered) behind it, and distinct from the concrete Sustain practices ahead of it. Chapter 26 is the OPEN-THE-SIXTH-S chapter: the concept and mindset (held is not kept, practice not project, habit not willpower, second nature) and the honest HOLD with its new last-gap path to the goal.
5. Re-sync all assets if the authored chapter changes after author review.
