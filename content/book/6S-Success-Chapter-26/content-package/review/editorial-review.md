# Editorial Review: Chapter 26 Content Package

Reviewed against the checklist: repetition, AI-sounding phrasing, unsupported claims, hooks, CTAs, source alignment, overlong social posts, tone, formatting. This review reports only checks actually run; every count below was measured with a scan, and every cited noun or quote was verified present in the manuscript or canonical strings before citing.

## Overall verdict
Publish-ready after author sign-off, with one standing caveat: the chapter is a draft (authored in the book voice, grounded in the 6S source "Principles of 6S: Sustain Step": sustain the gains, develop a sense of responsibility and self-discipline, utilize 6S to solve day-to-day problems, tied lightly to standard work and Toyota / TPS; no verbatim source file supplied). Findings below are minor.

- Cross-asset repetition of the spine (the held-is-not-kept distinction, the "done" trap, the practice-not-project rule, the definition box, the habit-not-willpower reframe, the second-nature reframe, and the honest hold with the new last-gap path) is by design and staggered by the calendar.
- The hero vocabulary runs high ("keep," "kept," "keeping," "habit," "willpower," "home," "sustain," "second nature," "relentless"), because it is the subject, not a tic. When excerpting, vary the surrounding language so a single post does not stack "keep," "kept," and "keeping."

## Unsupported claims
- No invented statistics, names, or dates. The concept is taught as the source's "sustain the gains" and "self-discipline," home-scaled into habit not willpower and second nature; the two graphs (willpower spiking then crashing, a project peaking then decaying) are offered as illustration of the mechanism, not as measured data; the garden and the after-dinner reset are illustration, not statistics.
- **Force / control scan returned 0 control uses across the whole package** (reader-facing and production files alike): no punitive "discipline" / "stay on top of it" / "no excuses" / "hold the line" / "crack down" / "lock down" / "enforce" / "comply" / "compliance" / "audit" (punitive) framing of Sustain anywhere. Keeping is framed as a little ongoing care, relief rather than obligation, and a shared choice of the whole household, never inspection or a compliance regime, and never a load left on one person.
- No brand-name products are endorsed anywhere; the chapter says "a surface," "the keys on the hook," "a garden," never a named app, planner, or product.
- Testimonials in `pdf-ebook/back-cover-copy.md` are clearly marked placeholders (three bracketed PLACEHOLDER lines under an explicit "do not publish as real quotes" instruction).
