- **The relentlessness reframe (frozen):** Nothing you have built will stay built on its own, and not because your standards are weak. - **The habit-not-willpower reframe (frozen):** You will not keep your home by resolving to try harder, any more than you kept it tidy by resolving to be neater. Sustain runs on habit, not willpower: small, normal, shared, and low enough in effort that it survives the weeks when nobody has any energy to spare.

That is the destination this whole book has been walking toward: not a home you are forever fixing, but one that mostly keeps itself, because keeping it has quietly become second nature.
- **One Idea to Keep (frozen close):** You do not finish 6S; you keep it. A home stays good the way a garden stays alive, by a little ongoing care from the people who love it, not by one heroic effort and then neglect. Sustain is that care, made small enough and shared enough to last for years.
