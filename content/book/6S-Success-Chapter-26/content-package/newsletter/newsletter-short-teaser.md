# Newsletter Short Teaser: Sustain: Keeping the Gains

For a roundup or digest. 90 to 140 words.

---

You have built a home that is sorted, arranged, clean, safe, and standardized, and Standardize held it against sliding back. But held is not the same as kept, because the world is relentless: new things arrive, seasons change, people come and go, and nothing stays good on its own. Sustain is the sixth S and the only one you never finish. You do not finish 6S; you keep it. And keeping runs on habit, not willpower, made small and shared enough to survive your worst weeks, until it becomes lighter than fixing and, kept long enough, simply how your home is. The needle holds this chapter, and for the first time the last gap to the goal appears.
