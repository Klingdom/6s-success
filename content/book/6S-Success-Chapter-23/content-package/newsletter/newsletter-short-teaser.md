# Newsletter Short Teaser: Standardize: The Best Way We Know Today

For a roundup or digest. 90 to 140 words.

---

Four S's have made your home sorted, arranged, clean, and safe, and yet almost all of it lives in one place: you. You know the best way to load the dishwasher and the order of the bedtime routine, and none of it was ever written down, so the home works beautifully right up until the day you are tired or away. This chapter opens Standardize, the fifth S, and closes that gap. A standard is not bureaucracy; it is a recipe, the best way you know today, written down plainly so it turns out right for anyone. The word today keeps it living: rewrite it the moment someone finds a better way. Write one plain standard, put it where the work happens. The needle holds, and for the first time it cannot slide back.
