2/
The home works beautifully right up until the day you are sick, away, or tired, and then it quietly stops. "Everything you have built so far lives in your head and your hands, which means it lasts exactly as long as your memory and your energy do."

(251 chars)

---

3/
Things drift back for a reason that is not carelessness. A way that lives in one head is invisible to everyone else, so everyone does it their own way. "It is just what happens when the best way is a secret."

(211 chars)

---

4/
This S has one blunt job: prevent reversion to the old way. Not by nagging, but by making the good way the easy, obvious one, so following it is simplest. "A standard is how you make the best way visible."

(208 chars)

---

5/
The word standard sounds cold, like clipboards. A home standard is warmer. "A standard is not bureaucracy. It is a recipe: the best way you know to make something turn out right, written down so it turns out right every time, for anyone, even on a tired day."

(262 chars)

---

6/
The whole of it: "A standard is the current best way to do a recurring task, written down plainly enough that anyone in the home can follow it and get the same result, and kept open enough that it is rewritten the moment a better way is found."

(247 chars)

---

7/
A good standard is usually a few plain lines about one task. Length is not the point. "The test of a standard is never how thorough it looks. It is whether the next person actually follows it and gets the result you would have gotten."

(238 chars)

---

8/
The word in the title doing the quiet work is today. "A standard is not the last word, it is the current best word, and the moment someone finds a better way, the standard is not broken. It is simply what you improve."

(221 chars)

---

9/
So it never becomes a cage. "A standard is not a rule carved in stone. It is the best way you have found so far, held in place only until you find a better one, and then rewritten without ceremony."

(201 chars)

---

10/
Without a written standard, a good new idea gets used once and forgotten. With one, the next person starts from there, not from scratch. "Held that way, a standard is not a constraint on improvement; it is the thing that makes improvement stick."

(250 chars)

---

11/
Write your first one today. Pick a small recurring task that only really works when you do it. Watch yourself do it once and write the best way in a few plain steps, most tasks need three to six, then stick it where the task happens, not in a binder.

(254 chars)

---

12/
You may not even have to invent it. "The best way usually already exists, fully formed, in somebody's hands." If someone does a task best, ask them to show you and write down what they do. A standard makes one person's good habit everyone's.

(245 chars)

The needle does not move this chapter, because nothing in the rooms changed. But for the first time something appears behind it: a catch, a ratchet, that holds it against the pull back toward friction. Every gain until now was quietly reversible.
