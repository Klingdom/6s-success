## Full summary (about 250 words)

Chapter 23 is the first chapter of Part Seven and opens Standardize, the fifth of the six S's. Four S's have made the home sorted, arranged, clean, and safe, and yet almost all of that lives in exactly one place: you. You know the best way to load the dishwasher, the order of the bedtime routine, the trick that keeps mornings calm, and none of it was written down, so the home works beautifully right up until the day you are sick, away, or out of patience, and then it quietly stops.

That is the gap the fifth S closes. Things drift back not because anyone is careless but because a way that lives in one head is invisible to everyone else, so everyone does it their own way and the best way stays a secret. A standard makes the best way visible so it stops depending on you.

The chapter's hero is that a standard is a recipe: the best way you know to make something turn out right, written down so it turns out right for anyone, even on a tired day. The word doing the quiet work is today: a standard is the current best word, not the last word, rewritten without ceremony the moment a better way is found, so it is a starting point, not a cage. Write one plain standard for a recurring task in a few steps and put it where the work happens. The needle holds, but for the first time a catch appears behind it that keeps the gain from sliding back.
