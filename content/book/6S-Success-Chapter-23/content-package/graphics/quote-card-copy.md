Ten quote cards, each pulled from the frozen quotes in `content-package/canonical/chapter-quotes.md`.

**verbatim** (byte-identical to the frozen quotes). The alternates at the end are **verbatim** as

Each card uses the warm book palette (paper `#F7F2E9`, panel `#FBF7EF`, ink `#2B2622`) and the

anti-shame: a standard is a recipe and a gift, not bureaucracy, so no card carries a corporate,
controlling, or clipboard tone. A home whose good ways live in one head is the normal case, never a
failing.

- (verbatim, the Quick Win)
  "Pick one recurring thing that only really works when you do it, loading the dishwasher, the bedtime routine, taking out the recycling, and write down the best way in five plain steps. Stick it where the task happens. One standard, today."

  "Ask the person who does a task best to teach it to everyone else, and write down what they do. The best way usually already lives in someone's hands; a standard just makes it everyone's."

- (verbatim, the 6S Tip)
  "Write the standard where the work happens, not in a binder. A note taped inside the cupboard door beats a perfect document nobody opens. The best standard is the one you cannot avoid seeing."

- (verbatim, the Common Mistake)
  "Writing a standard as a strict rule and then defending it forever. A standard you are not allowed to change becomes a cage, and people quietly route around a cage. Keep it the best way for now, and let anyone propose a better one."
