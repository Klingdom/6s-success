# Open Graph and Twitter Card Copy: Chapter 23, Standardize: The Best Way We Know Today

Two variants. Use the og:image concept noted under each.

## Variant A (a standard is a recipe)

- og:title: A Standard Is Not Bureaucracy. It Is a Recipe.
- og:description: Everything you have built so far lives in your head and your hands, so it lasts exactly as long as your memory and your energy do, and quietly drifts back the day you are tired or away. Standardize closes that gap by writing the best way down. A standard is not bureaucracy. It is a recipe: the best way you know to make something turn out right, written down so it turns out right for anyone, even on a tired day, and rewritten the moment someone finds a better way. That is all a standard is, and it is how the gains last.

## Variant B (the best way we know today)

- og:title: The Best Way We Know Today
- og:description: The word in the title doing the quiet work is today. A standard is not the last word, it is the current best word, and the moment someone finds a better way, the standard is not broken. It is simply what you improve. A standard is not a rule carved in stone. It is the best way you have found so far, held in place only until you find a better one, and then rewritten without ceremony.
