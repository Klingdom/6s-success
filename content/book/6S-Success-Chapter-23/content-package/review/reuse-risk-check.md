# Reuse Risk Check: Chapter 23 Content Package

When one chapter becomes fifty-plus assets, the risk is overexposure: the same line or image hitting the same follower until it feels canned. This check flags where that can happen.

## High-frequency elements
| Element | Appears in | Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| A standard is the best way you know today, written down (the rule) | Canonical, CTA, LinkedIn, X, quote card, graphics, HTML | A follower on several platforms sees the hero rule repeatedly | Calendar staggers it; vary the phrasing around it per platform |
| A standard is not bureaucracy. It is a recipe ... even on a tired day (recipe reframe) | Canonical, CTA, LinkedIn, X, FB, quote card, graphics | Medium to High | The hero the chapter rests on; the most portable line. Use across formats, not stacked on one day. |
| Everything you have built so far lives in your head and your hands ... stops depending on you (drift reframe) | Canonical, LinkedIn, X, FB, quote card | Medium to High | The problem the chapter opens on; the most relatable idea. Use across formats, not stacked on one day. |
| The word in the title doing the quiet work is today ... simply what you improve (today reframe) | Canonical, LinkedIn, X, quote card | Medium | The living-standard heart; vary the surrounding worked example (the teenager folding laundry, updating the card). |
| A standard is not a rule carved in stone ... rewritten without ceremony (living-standard line) | Canonical, LinkedIn, X, FB, quote card | Medium | The openness that answers rigidity; pair with a different framing (starting point / written in pencil) each time. |
| Pick one recurring thing ... write down the best way in five plain steps. One standard, today (Quick Win / primary CTA) | Canonical, CTA, LinkedIn, X, graphics | Medium | The frozen action; lead with a fresh hook before the full instruction. |
| The friction-meter caption ("The needle does not move this chapter ... a catch that keeps it from sliding back") | Canonical, graphics, HTML | Low to Medium | Long; it is the honest-HOLD beat, specific to this chapter. |
| Soft-CTA sign-off wording ("Read the free chapter", "Read it free online", "Read the chapter free in the online book") | Most LinkedIn and X posts | Medium | Rotate phrasings; most likely thing to feel templated. |

## Same-day collision
The calendar avoids two assets sharing a hook on the same day. If your LinkedIn and Facebook audiences overlap heavily, push one back a day so the rule or the recipe reframe does not land twice in one day.

## Cross-posting risk
- The LinkedIn carousel outline (`linkedin/linkedin-carousel-outline.md`) and the slides carousel copy (`slides/linkedin-carousel-copy.md`) cover the same Standardize content. Post only one to LinkedIn (use the copy-ready slides file; keep the outline as planning).
- The LinkedIn newsletter (`linkedin/linkedin-newsletter-version.md`) and the email newsletter share structure; do not send both to the same subscriber the same day.

## Duplicate-content / SEO risk
- Web landing intro, ebook description, and LinkedIn article overlap in phrasing. Set the chapter page as the SEO canonical and mark syndicated copies with a canonical link.

## Cross-chapter risk (specific to Chapter 23)
- Chapter 23 is the FIRST chapter of Part Seven and the OPEN-THE-FIFTH-S chapter. Keep it distinct from the chapters behind it: Part Six (Chapters 20 to 22) opened, ran, and completed Safety. Chapter 23 uses the finished home the first four S's built as its starting point, but assets must NOT re-teach Sort, Straighten, Shine, or Safety (a one-line callback is fine, a re-teach is not), re-derive any earlier device, or re-tell the 5S+1 history. Grep confirms the assets deliver the standard concept and the one written standard and do not rebuild the earlier S's.
- Do not let this chapter slide FORWARD into the standard TOOLS or the making-it-last work. Ch 23 assets keep it to the CONCEPT and ONE written standard and must NOT build the checklist, schedule, and responsibility system (Chapter 24), the visual and pictorial standards (Chapter 25), or the long-term adherence and audit system (Sustain, Chapters 26 to 30). This fence is held: the tools are named in the package only as "the next chapter" and the S's still ahead, never built. The package may note that a standard becomes habit through repetition but does not build the adherence or audit system.
- The friction-meter beat is specific to this chapter: the needle HOLDS at (67.6,154.9) where Safety left it and does NOT move, because writing down the best way changes nothing in the rooms; SAFETY COMPLETE is now a passed marker; and for the first time a NEW anti-backslide catch (a ratchet) appears behind the needle with a barred backslide arrow labeled "no backslide," with the GOAL crosshair still ahead at (67,155). This is a HOLD with a new anti-backslide motif, NOT a move and NOT a milestone. Do not imply the needle advanced, that a new milestone was reached, or that the goal is near; the goal stays ahead because Standardize has only begun and Sustain still follows.

## Originality and source-fidelity risk
- The chapter is a draft authored in the book voice, grounded in the 6S source (Standardize Step: prevent reversion back to the old way, and remember that it takes repetitive actions for something to become habit, plus the first Step to Complete Standardize, document standards for Sort, Straighten and Shine activities, tied lightly to standard work and Toyota), not supplied verbatim source. All derived assets are faithful to that draft; re-sync if the chapter is revised.
- Quote cards are verbatim: every card in `graphics/quote-card-copy.md` verified byte-identical to a quoted line in `canonical/chapter-quotes.md` (0 mismatches). Nothing borrowed from outside the book; no brand-name products are named, and the Standardize principles, standard work, and Toyota origin are referenced lightly, home-scaled, not overclaimed. A standard is framed as a recipe and a gift, warm and freeing; "standard" and "prevent reversion" are topic words used calmly, never enforce, comply, or lock down (the force / control scan returned zero control uses across the whole package).

## Recommendations
1. Rotate CTA wording (highest-value, lowest-effort fix; several posts close on "Read the free chapter").
2. Pick one carousel version for LinkedIn.
3. Set the chapter page as the SEO canonical.
4. Keep Chapter 23 from blurring into its neighbors: distinct from Part Six behind it (Sort, Straighten, Shine, Safety, all done) and distinct from the standard tools ahead of it (Chapter 24) and the making-it-last work (Chapter 25, Sustain). Chapter 23 is the OPEN-THE-FIFTH-S chapter: the concept of a standard, one written standard, and the honest HOLD with its first anti-backslide catch.
5. Re-sync all assets if the authored chapter changes after author review.
