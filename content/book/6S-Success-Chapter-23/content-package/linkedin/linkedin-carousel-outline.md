# Chapter 23 LinkedIn Carousel Outline: Standardize, The Best Way We Know Today

Twelve slides. Slide 1 hooks, last slide carries the soft CTA. Each slide has a headline and one or two lines of body. Warm, human, freeing voice; a standard is a gift and a relief, not bureaucracy or compliance; no force or control words, no hype, no brand names.

---

## Slide 1: Hook

**Headline:** You built a good home. Right now it lives in one head.

**Body:** Sorted, arranged, clean, and safe, and almost all of it lives in exactly one place: you. Which means it works beautifully until the day you are tired or away. Swipe for the fifth S, the one that fixes that.

---

## Slide 2: The quiet problem

**Headline:** The good you built lives in one place, and that place is you.

**Body:** You know the best way to load the dishwasher, the order of the bedtime routine, the trick with the launch pad. Nobody wrote it down, because you simply know it. So it lasts exactly as long as your memory and your energy do.

---

## Slide 3: The drift reframe

**Headline:** A standard is how a good way stops depending on you.

**Body:** Everything you have built so far lives in your head and your hands, which means it lasts exactly as long as your memory and your energy do. A standard takes it out of your head and puts it where anyone can reach it.

---

## Slide 4: Why things drift back

**Headline:** Drift is not carelessness. It is a secret.

**Body:** A way that lives only in one head is invisible to everyone else, so everyone does it their own way. Multiply that by every task and every person, and things slide back. It is just what happens when the best way is a secret.

---

## Slide 5: The blunt job

**Headline:** Standardize makes the best way visible.

**Body:** The people who first named these steps gave this S one job: keep the good way from quietly reversing, not through willpower or nagging but by making it visible and shared. A standard is how you make the best way visible.

---

## Slide 6: A standard is a recipe

**Headline:** A standard is not bureaucracy. It is a recipe.

**Body:** A standard is not bureaucracy. It is a recipe: the best way you know to make something turn out right, written down so it turns out right every time, for anyone, even on a tired day. A gift, not a clipboard.

---

## Slide 7: What it asks for

**Headline:** Measured by use, not by thoroughness.

**Body:** A good home standard is usually a few plain lines about one task. It does not ask for length or formality. The test is never how thorough it looks. It is whether the next person actually follows it and gets the result you would have gotten.

---

## Slide 8: The word today

**Headline:** The word doing the quiet work is today.

**Body:** A standard is not the last word, it is the current best word, and the moment someone finds a better way, the standard is not broken. It is simply what you improve. A starting point, not a finish line.

---

## Slide 9: A living standard

**Headline:** Not carved in stone. Written in pencil.

**Body:** A standard is not a rule carved in stone. It is the best way you have found so far, held in place only until you find a better one, and then rewritten without ceremony. That is what makes improvement stick instead of a cage.

---

## Slide 10: Write your first one

**Headline:** Watch yourself do it once. Write the steps.

**Body:** Pick one recurring task that only really works when you do it. Write the best way in a few plain steps, and stick it where the task happens, not in a binder. The best standard is the one you cannot avoid seeing.

---

## Slide 11: The shortcut

**Headline:** The best way already lives in someone's hands.

**Body:** If someone already does a task better than everyone else, do not invent a standard from scratch. Ask them to show you and write down what they do. A standard is often just the act of making one person's good habit into everyone's.

---

## Slide 12: Soft CTA

**Headline:** The needle holds, and for the first time cannot slide back.

**Body:** This is Chapter 23 of 6S Success: Home Edition, and it opens the fifth S. Nothing moved in the rooms, but a catch appeared behind the needle that keeps a gain from sliding back.
