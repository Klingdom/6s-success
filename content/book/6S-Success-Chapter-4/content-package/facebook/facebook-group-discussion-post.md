# Facebook Group Discussion Post

*For a home-and-organizing Facebook group. Useful nugget first, then an open question. Friendly, not an ad.*

---

A little reframe that genuinely changed how I pick where to start, and I would love to hear if it matches your experience.

Most of us choose our next organizing project based on guilt. We go straight for the space we feel worst about. The garage. The haunted closet. The room we apologize for when people come over.

But guilt is a terrible compass. It points at the hardest, heaviest, most emotional spaces, which are exactly the ones most likely to burn you out on day one.

The better question is not "which space do I feel guiltiest about." It is "which space costs me the most, most often?" That is friction, and it is usually pointing at something small and boring, like a junk drawer or the pile by the front door. Fix that and you actually feel it every single day.

So I am curious about this group's real-life experience:

Where did you start? And has starting big, like the garage or the whole basement, ever burned you out and made you quit for a while?

Did the small wins keep you going better than the big ones? Genuinely want to hear it, because I think a lot of us learned this the hard way. 🙂

(For full disclosure, this idea comes from Chapter 4 of a home organizing book called *6S Success: Home Edition*, which is free to read online. Not trying to sell anything, the friction-vs-guilt thing just stuck with me and I wanted to talk it through with people who actually do this.)
