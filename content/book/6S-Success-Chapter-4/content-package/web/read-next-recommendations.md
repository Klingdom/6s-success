# Read Next: How to Choose Your First Target Area

Suggested next reads and resources for someone finishing Chapter 4.

1. **Chapter 5 · The 6S Home Audit**
   The next step. Now that your target is chosen, look at it clearly before you touch a thing, so your effort lands where it actually helps.

2. **Chapter 3 · The Six Steps That Transform Any Space**
   The setup. This is the method you are about to point at your first target. Worth a quick reread if the six steps are not yet second nature.

3. **Choose your first target (the chapter's one action)**
   List three to five spaces that genuinely annoy you, score each on friction and effort, subtract effort from friction, and write down the winner. Do this before you move on.

4. **The First Target Map worksheet (free printable)**
   The blank friction vs effort two-by-two, so you can plot your own candidate spaces and see your sweet spot at a glance.

5. **The Target Scorecard (free printable)**
   A single tear-off page to list and score three to five candidate spaces, with the winner circled. The fastest way to turn the idea into a decision you can hold.
