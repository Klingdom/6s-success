# Landing Page Intro: How to Choose Your First Target Area

There is a specific kind of Saturday morning this chapter exists to rescue. You have learned the method, you have a second cup of coffee in your hand, and you have a dangerous amount of motivation. Then a thought arrives, bright and certain and completely wrong: I should do the garage.

Stop right there, gently. The garage feels like the brave choice, but it is the project most likely to eat your whole weekend and most of your enthusiasm. You will run out of daylight at the halfway point, and a quiet voice will tell you that you are just not an organized person. That voice is lying. You did not fail at organizing. You failed at choosing.

This chapter fixes the choosing. You will learn the only two questions that judge any space, friction and effort, and a one-minute map that finds your sweet spot: small enough to win, annoying enough to matter. By the end you will have one real target, named and written down, instead of a guilty wish.

Read Chapter 4 free online, and pick the space that gets you started.
