# Quote Card Copy: Chapter 4

**"How to Choose Your First Target Area" · 6S Success: Home Edition**

Ten quote cards, each pulled verbatim from chapter-quotes.md (which lifts directly from the manuscript and final HTML). No em dashes, nothing invented. Each quote is short enough to fill a 1080 x 1350 card comfortably. Attribution line is the same on every card. Design notes use the book palette and the chapter's motifs.

Palette quick reference: paper #F7F2E9, panel #FBF7EF, ink #2B2622, terracotta #BC4B2A, honey #DDA63A, slate #3C5A6B, green #6E8B5B, spark #CB4B36. Display type Fraunces, body Newsreader.

---

### Card 1
**Quote:** "The day after you learn the method is the day you are most likely to aim it at the wrong thing. Where you start decides whether you finish."
**Attribution:** 6S Success: Home Edition, Chapter 4
**Design note:** Warm paper ground, ink Fraunces. Motif: the tempting garage, a faint line-art garage glyph low in the corner with a single terracotta friction-spark. Slate footer rule.

### Card 2
**Quote:** "You did not fail at organizing. You failed at choosing. You aimed a beginner's budget of energy at an expert's project."
**Attribution:** 6S Success: Home Edition, Chapter 4
**Design note:** Panel #FBF7EF card. The two short sentences "You did not fail at organizing." and "You failed at choosing." set larger in ink, the rest in soft gray. Motif: friction meter holding, small and quiet at the base.

### Card 3
**Quote:** "Your first project is not really about a room. It is about proof."
**Attribution:** 6S Success: Home Edition, Chapter 4
**Design note:** Big airy Fraunces on paper, "proof" carried in terracotta. A single green calm-dot beside it as the visible win. This is a 6S Tip line, so use the slate tip accent on the footer.

### Card 4
**Quote:** "A win you can see is the most reliable fuel there is. It does not run out the way willpower does. It compounds."
**Attribution:** 6S Success: Home Edition, Chapter 4
**Design note:** Green is the lead color here. Motif: a small cluster of green calm-dots growing larger left to right, suggesting the win compounding. Ink type, honey underline on "compounds."

### Card 5
**Quote:** "Guilt is a terrible compass. It points at the hardest, heaviest, most emotional spaces, which are exactly the ones most likely to defeat a beginner."
**Attribution:** 6S Success: Home Edition, Chapter 4
**Design note:** Motif: the guilt versus friction compass. A simple line-art compass with the needle in spark red swinging toward a heavy, dim corner. Slate and ink type on paper. This is the Common Mistake line, so a thin spark left border fits.

### Card 6
**Quote:** "Stop asking which space you feel worst about. Start asking which space costs you the most, most often."
**Attribution:** 6S Success: Home Edition, Chapter 4
**Design note:** Two-line contrast layout: "Stop asking" set in muted gray, "Start asking" set in confident ink with a terracotta accent. Motif: the compass reorienting from guilt to friction.

### Card 7
**Quote:** "Friction is not about how bad a space looks. It is about how often it taxes your actual life."
**Attribution:** 6S Success: Home Edition, Chapter 4
**Design note:** Paper ground, ink Fraunces. Motif: a small daily-touch versus twice-a-year contrast, a tiny repeating four-dot mark (every meal) against a single far dot (once a season). Honey accent.

### Card 8
**Quote:** "The best first target is the smallest space that will still feel like a real win. High friction, low effort."
**Attribution:** 6S Success: Home Edition, Chapter 4
**Design note:** This is the canonical definition, so treat it as the flagship card. Slate-bordered card on panel, "High friction, low effort." set apart in small Inter caps below the main line. Motif: the Start Here quadrant lifted from the First Target Map, green-tinted with calm-dots.

### Card 9
**Quote:** "The garage is a graduation project, not a first lap."
**Attribution:** 6S Success: Home Edition, Chapter 4
**Design note:** Short and punchy, set large in Fraunces. Motif: the garage, a friendly line-art garage glyph with a small honey "Soon, Not First" tag, not mocked. Terracotta accent on "graduation project."

### Card 10
**Quote:** "Momentum is not a personality trait. It is a thing you build, one well-chosen target at a time."
**Attribution:** 6S Success: Home Edition, Chapter 4
**Design note:** Closing-note feel. Dark ink card (#2B2622) with paper-colored type, honey label, the way the "One Idea to Keep" box is styled in the HTML. Motif: a small row of green calm-dots, one brighter than the rest, as the next target.
