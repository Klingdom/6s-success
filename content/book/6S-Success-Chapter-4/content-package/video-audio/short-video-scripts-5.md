**Count:** 5 scripts, each 30 to 90 seconds
**Note:** Vertical 9:16. Captions on. Each script has a hook, spoken lines, on-screen text beats, visual direction, and a one-line CTA.

## SHORT 1: The first project is for proof (approx. 45 sec)

**Hook line:** "One organized drawer will not change your life. So why start there?"

[VISUAL: Close on a single messy junk drawer being opened, things sliding around.]

[SPOKEN]
One organized drawer will not change your life, and you know it. So why would I tell you to start there? Because your first project is not really about the room. It is about proof.

[ON SCREEN TEXT: "The first project is for YOU, not the room."]

[SPOKEN]
It exists to prove one quiet point to the part of your brain that has watched you quit a dozen organizing kicks before. This time is different, because this time it actually worked.

[VISUAL: The same drawer, now calm and sorted. A hand reaches in and grabs one thing instantly.]

[ON SCREEN TEXT: "A win you can see is the most reliable fuel there is."]

[SPOKEN]
People do not keep going because they are disciplined. They keep going because the last thing they tried worked, and they want that feeling again.

**CTA:** Start small enough to win. That is Chapter 4 of 6S Success: Home Edition.

## SHORT 2: Guilt vs friction (approx. 50 sec)

**Hook line:** "You are picking your first cleanup project with the wrong feeling."

[VISUAL: Split screen. Left, a dramatic messy garage. Right, a small annoying kitchen drawer.]

[SPOKEN]
You are about to pick the wrong project, and I know why. You are using guilt as your compass. Guilt points at the garage, the haunted closet, the room you apologize for when guests come over.

[ON SCREEN TEXT: "Guilt is a terrible compass."]

[SPOKEN]
But guilt points at the hardest, heaviest, most emotional spaces. Exactly the ones most likely to defeat a beginner. There is a better compass, and you already carry it. Friction.

[ON SCREEN TEXT: "Friction = how often a space actually costs you."]

[SPOKEN]
Stop asking which space you feel worst about. Start asking which space costs you the most, most often. Those are rarely the same place. And that difference is the whole secret.

[VISUAL: The small drawer gets a glowing green check. The garage fades back.]

**CTA:** Follow the friction, not the guilt. Full method in Chapter 4.

## SHORT 3: The First Target Map sweet spot (approx. 55 sec)

**Hook line:** "Two questions tell you exactly where to start. Under a minute."

[VISUAL: A clean two-by-two grid animates on screen, empty.]

[SPOKEN]
Where should you start? Two questions, answered in under a minute, about any space in your home.

[VISUAL: Vertical axis draws in.]

[ON SCREEN TEXT: "1. Friction: how often does it cost you?"]

[SPOKEN]
One. Friction. How much does this space cost you, and how often? The drawer you fight every evening is expensive. The crawl space you open twice a year is cheap, no matter how ugly it is.

[VISUAL: Horizontal axis draws in.]

[ON SCREEN TEXT: "2. Effort: how big is the job, honestly?"]

[SPOKEN]
Two. Effort. How big is the job, honestly? A drawer is fifteen minutes. The garage is a series of weekends.

[VISUAL: Top-left quadrant lights up bright green with dots, labeled "Start Here."]

[ON SCREEN TEXT: "Sweet spot: HIGH friction, LOW effort."]

[SPOKEN]
Where they meet is your answer. High friction, low effort. Big daily relief, small one-time cost. That green corner, top left, is where you start.

**CTA:** This is the First Target Map. Grab the free worksheet in Chapter 4.

## SHORT 4: Win where you will see it (approx. 50 sec)

**Hook line:** "Why fixing the right drawer beats fixing the right closet."

[VISUAL: A linen closet door closing. Then nothing. The win is hidden.]

[SPOKEN]
Here is a filter almost nobody uses when they pick a project. Choose a space you touch every single day.

[ON SCREEN TEXT: "A daily-touch space is a win that greets you every morning."]

[SPOKEN]
Fix a space you only see twice a year, and the win disappears the moment you close the door. Fix the drawer you open at every meal, and the win greets you four times a day.

[VISUAL: A kitchen drawer opening smoothly, a hand grabbing exactly what it needs, four quick cuts of the same easy motion.]

[ON SCREEN TEXT: "Same effort. Wildly different payoff."]

[SPOKEN]
Each time that drawer hands you what you need instead of fighting you, it quietly reminds you the method works. It recruits you for the next project without any willpower at all.

**CTA:** Win where you will see it. Chapter 4 of 6S Success: Home Edition.

## SHORT 5: Score it and choose (approx. 60 sec)

**Hook line:** "Stop agonizing over where to start. Do this math instead."

[VISUAL: A hand writing a short column of spaces on a notepad.]

[SPOKEN]
Stop agonizing over where to start. People turn it into a philosophy project so they never have to begin. Here is the fix. A list, and one piece of easy math.

[ON SCREEN TEXT: "Name 3 to 5 spaces that genuinely annoy you."]

[SPOKEN]
Walk your home and name three to five spaces that genuinely annoy you. Not the messiest. The most annoying. Trust the sigh. The sigh is friction announcing itself.

[VISUAL: Two columns appear next to the list. Scores fill in one to five.]

[ON SCREEN TEXT: "Score friction 1 to 5. Score effort 1 to 5."]

[SPOKEN]
Score each one twice. Friction, how often it bites, one to five. Effort, how big the job, one to five. Then subtract effort from friction.

[VISUAL: Kitchen drawer 4 minus 1 equals 3. Garage 3 minus 5 equals negative 2. The drawer glows green.]

[ON SCREEN TEXT: "Highest number = your first target."]

[SPOKEN]
The highest number is your target. The humble drawer beats the dramatic garage every time. Now write it down, because a target in your head is a wish. A target on paper is a plan.

**CTA:** Pick one. Write it down. That is the whole assignment in Chapter 4.
