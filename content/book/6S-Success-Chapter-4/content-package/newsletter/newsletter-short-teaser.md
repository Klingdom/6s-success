# Chapter 4 Short Teaser (for a roundup or digest)

Fresh off a little momentum, most people aim it at the garage. It feels like the brave choice. It is usually the one that burns the whole weekend and most of the enthusiasm, then leaves a quiet voice saying you are just not an organized person. That voice is lying. You did not fail at organizing. You failed at choosing.

Your first project is not really about a room. It is about proof, one win you can see. Chapter 4 gives you a one-minute way to find it: score your spaces on friction (how often they cost you) and effort (how big the job is), then start in the high-friction, low-effort sweet spot. Guilt is a bad compass. Friction is a good one.
