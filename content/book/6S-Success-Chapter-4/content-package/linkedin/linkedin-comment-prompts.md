# LinkedIn Comment Prompts: How to Choose Your First Target Area

Five prompts to start a genuine conversation. Drop one into the comments of any post above, or use as a standalone question.

---

1. Without overthinking it: what space in your home annoyed you most this past week? Not the messiest, the most annoying, the one that made you sigh. That feeling is usually pointing straight at a good first target.

2. Be honest about a time you started too big. Did you ever charge the garage, the basement, or the whole closet on a wave of motivation and stall halfway? What did that teach you?

3. Guilt and friction often point at different spaces. Which space do you feel worst about, and which one actually costs you the most every day? Are they the same place or not?

4. If you scored your home on friction minus effort, what humble little space do you think would win? A catch-all drawer? An entryway drop zone? A bathroom counter? Name yours.

5. Naming a target out loud makes it real. What is the one small, mostly-yours space you could win in a single session this week? Say it in the comments and that is basically a promise to yourself.
