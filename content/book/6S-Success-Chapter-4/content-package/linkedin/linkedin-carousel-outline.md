# LinkedIn Carousel Outline: How to Choose Your First Target Area

Eleven slides. Square or portrait. Warm, plain. Slide 1 hooks, slide 11 is the soft CTA. The First Target Map is the visual spine, slides 6 through 8.

---

## Slide 1 (Hook)
**Headline:** Do not start with the garage.
**Body:** The most tempting place to start is usually the worst. Where you point your first project decides whether you finish it, or quit and blame yourself.

---

## Slide 2 (The trap)
**Headline:** Motivation is a budget, not a battery.
**Body:** It does not last and it does not refill on command. Spend it all on the most expensive room in your house and you stall at the halfway point.

---

## Slide 3 (The reframe)
**Headline:** You did not fail at organizing. You failed at choosing.
**Body:** A beginner's budget of energy aimed at an expert's project. The fix is not more willpower. It is a smaller, smarter target.

---

## Slide 4 (Proof, not the room)
**Headline:** Your first project is for proof, not the room.
**Body:** A single organized drawer will not change your life. The win it gives you changes what you do next. A visible win compounds where willpower runs out.

---

## Slide 5 (The compass)
**Headline:** Guilt is a bad compass. Friction is a good one.
**Body:** Stop asking which space you feel worst about. Start asking which space costs you the most, most often. Those are rarely the same place.

---

## Slide 6 (Question one)
**Headline:** Question one: friction.
**Body:** How much does this space cost me, and how often? The drawer you fight every evening is expensive. The crawl space you open twice a year is cheap, however ugly.

---

## Slide 7 (Question two)
**Headline:** Question two: effort.
**Body:** How big is the job, honestly? A drawer is fifteen minutes. A closet is an afternoon. The garage is a series of weekends. Be honest, not hopeful.

---

## Slide 8 (The map)
**Headline:** The First Target Map.
**Body:** Friction up one side, effort along the bottom. The sweet spot is top-left: high friction, low effort. Big daily relief, small one-time cost. That is your first target.

---

## Slide 9 (The garage)
**Headline:** The garage is a graduation project, not a first lap.
**Body:** High effort, usually shared, often seasonal, and its friction is lower than its guilt. You do not stand in it every morning. Worth doing. Just not first.

---

## Slide 10 (Three filters)
**Headline:** Three filters pick the winner.
**Body:** Win where you will see it, daily-touch. Pick a space you control, mostly yours. Skip the emotional landmines for now. Earn that muscle on a junk drawer.

---

## Slide 11 (Soft CTA)
**Headline:** Write it down. One target.
**Body:** List three to five annoying spaces, score friction minus effort, pick the highest, and write it where you will see it. The full map is Chapter 4 of 6S Success: Home Edition, free in the online book.
