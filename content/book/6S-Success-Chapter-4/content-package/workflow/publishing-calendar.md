# Publishing Calendar: 14-Day Release Sequence

A two-week rollout for Chapter 4, "How to Choose Your First Target Area". This chapter closes Part One and helps the reader choose a winnable first target. The goal is to get readers to pick one small space and write it down. Publish the home base (the web chapter) first, then drip social and email toward it. Dates are relative (Day 1 = the day the chapter page goes live).

Pacing principle: one anchor asset per day, plus one or two light reshares. The "should you start with the garage" question and the First Target Map are your strongest hooks. Lead with them.

## After Day 14

- Recycle the First Target Map graphic and the strongest quote cards monthly.
- Keep the chapter page updated with an FAQ block for SEO (see `canonical/chapter-seo.md`).
- When Chapter 5 publishes, link this chapter from its "previously" section, and link forward from here. This chapter closes Part 1, so it is a natural bridge into Part 2.

## Hero CTA reminder

This chapter's best conversion event is the decision itself. Wherever you can, end with "score your spaces on friction and effort, pick the high-friction, low-effort one, and write it down." A reader who does that has a target and is ready for Part 2.
