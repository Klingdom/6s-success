## Back cover

The hardest part of getting organized is not the work. It is knowing where to start.

Most people aim their motivation at the biggest, guiltiest room in the house, usually the garage, and burn out before they finish. Then they decide they are just not organized. They are wrong. They did not fail at organizing. They failed at choosing.

"6S Success: Home Edition" gives you a method that begins with a better choice. In Chapter 4 you learn a one-minute way to pick a first target you can actually win: judge any space on how much it costs you daily and how big the job is, and start where friction is high and effort is low. Pick one small, annoying, winnable space, finish it, and let that win carry you to the next.

You do not need more motivation. You need a smaller, smarter first target.
