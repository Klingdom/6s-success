# Ebook Store Description

*For the book "6S Success: Home Edition," foregrounding what Chapter 4 delivers. Honest, benefit-led, no hype.*

---

You learned the method. Now comes the question that decides whether any of it sticks: where do you start?

Most people answer it badly. Fresh off a burst of motivation, they charge the hardest room in the house, usually the garage, run out of energy halfway, and walk away with a bigger mess and a quiet voice that says they are just not an organized person. That voice is wrong. They did not fail at organizing. They failed at choosing.

Chapter 4 of "6S Success: Home Edition" fixes the choosing. It gives you a one-minute way to pick a first target you can actually win, using two honest questions about any space: how much does it cost you, and how often (friction), and how big is the job, really (effort). The sweet spot is high friction and low effort, a space that annoys you every day but takes very little to fix. That is your first target, and the book's First Target Map and Target Scorecard walk you straight to it.

You will also learn why the garage is a graduation project, not a first lap, why guilt is a bad compass and friction is a good one, and how to win where you will see it so the result keeps paying you back. The chapter ends with one small action: name your target and write it down.

This is the closing chapter of Part One. Part Two begins with the audit in Chapter 5, where you see your target clearly before you touch it.
