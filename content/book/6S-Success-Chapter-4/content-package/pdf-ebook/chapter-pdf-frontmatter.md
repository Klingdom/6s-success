# How to Choose Your First Target Area

Not the biggest room, and not the one you feel worst about. The smallest space that will still feel like a real win.

## One-line chapter descriptor

A one-minute way to pick a first target you can actually win, so the next project gets easier on its own.

---

## How to read this chapter

This is the closing chapter of Part One. You will not move a single object here, and that is on purpose. This chapter is about choosing, not doing. Read it once through, in order. When you reach "Make Your Shortlist," keep a piece of paper or your phone nearby, because the chapter ends with one small action: naming your first target and writing it down. Take the two infographics seriously. The First Target Map and the Target Scorecard are decision tools you will reuse on every room for the rest of the book. The work itself begins in Part Two, with the audit in Chapter 5.
