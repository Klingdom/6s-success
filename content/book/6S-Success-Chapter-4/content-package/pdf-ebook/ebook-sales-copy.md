# Sales Page Copy

*For "6S Success: Home Edition," leading with Chapter 4. Honest and direct. No hype verbs.*

---

## You were motivated. Then you picked the garage.

You know the feeling. You read a method, something clicks, and a Saturday arrives with a second cup of coffee and a dangerous amount of energy. So you open the garage door, pull everything out, and run out of daylight at the halfway point. Now the garage is worse than when you started, your back hurts, and a quiet voice says you are just not an organized person.

That voice is lying to you. You did not fail at organizing. You failed at choosing. You aimed a beginner's budget of energy at an expert's project.

Almost every organizing book hands you a method and then leaves you alone with the hardest question: where do I actually start? Get that wrong and the best method in the world cannot save you.

## A one-minute way to choose a target you can win

Chapter 4 of "6S Success: Home Edition" answers the where-to-start question with a simple decision, not a pep talk. You judge any space in your home on two honest questions: how much does it cost you, and how often (friction), and how big is the job, really (effort). The space that costs you a lot every day and very little to fix once is your first target. The First Target Map shows you the sweet spot in about a minute.

## What you get in this chapter

- The First Target Map: a friction-versus-effort tool you reuse on every room in the book.
- A clear, kind case for why the garage is a graduation project, not a first lap.
- Three filters for the best target: win where you will see it, pick a space you control, and skip the emotional landmines for now.
- The Target Scorecard, a small scoring method that does the choosing for you.
- One small closing action: name your target and write it down.

You do not need more motivation. You need a smaller, smarter first target. Win where you will see it, and the next project gets easier on its own.

## Start here

Read Chapter 4, pick one target, and write it down. Then Chapter 5 helps you see that target clearly before you touch it.
