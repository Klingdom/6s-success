### Slide 1 (Hook)

**ON-SLIDE COPY:**

You learned the method.
Now you want to do the garage.

Don't.

Where you start decides whether you finish.

### Slide 2

**ON-SLIDE COPY:**

Motivation is a budget.
Not a renewable resource.

The garage spends all of it, then leaves you stalled at the halfway point with a sore back and a mess.

[VISUAL] A battery or fuel gauge draining from full to empty over a small garage silhouette. Slate line art on warm paper.

### Slide 3

**ON-SLIDE COPY:**

If you stall, a quiet voice says:
"You're not really an organized person."

That voice is lying.

You didn't fail at organizing.
You failed at choosing.

[VISUAL] Plain panel (#FBF7EF), the last two lines large in Fraunces. "choosing" underlined in honey. Calm and uncluttered.

### Slide 4

**ON-SLIDE COPY:**

Your first project isn't about a room.

It's about proof.

One win you can see, to prove the method actually works. That win is fuel. It compounds where willpower runs out.

[VISUAL] A small drawer with a green calm-dot, an arrow to a second and third project growing in size. Momentum as a chain.

---

### Slide 5

**ON-SLIDE COPY:**

Guilt is a bad compass.

It points at the hardest, heaviest, most emotional spaces.

Friction is a good compass.

Don't ask which space you feel worst about. Ask which one costs you the most, most often.

[VISUAL] A slate compass with two needles: "Guilt" pointing at a dramatic closet, "Friction" pointing at a humble junk drawer. Friction needle carries a small red spark.

---

### Slide 6

**ON-SLIDE COPY:**

Two questions pick your target.

1. Friction: how much does this space cost me, and how often?

2. Effort: how big is the job, honestly?

Where they meet is your answer.

[VISUAL] Two cards side by side. FRICTION (red spark) and EFFORT (slate weight). A meeting-point arrow pointing to the next slide.

---

### Slide 7

**ON-SLIDE COPY:**

The First Target Map.

Friction up the side. Effort across the bottom.

The sweet spot is top-left: high friction, low effort.

Start here.

[VISUAL] The hero 2x2 infographic. Top-left "Start Here" quadrant filled green with six green calm-dots, "Small to fix. Big daily relief." Top-right "Soon, Not First" holds a small garage icon. Bottom row "Fine, Not Now" and "Avoid for Now." Slate axis labels.

### Slide 8

**ON-SLIDE COPY:**

So why not the garage?

It's high effort. Usually shared. Often seasonal.

And its friction is lower than its guilt. You don't stand in it every morning.

The garage is a graduation project, not a first lap.

[VISUAL] The garage icon parked in the "Soon, Not First" quadrant with three tags: high effort, shared, seasonal. Affectionate, not mocking.

---

### Slide 9

**ON-SLIDE COPY:**

Score your shortlist.

List 3 to 5 spaces that annoy you.
Rate friction (1 to 5) and effort (1 to 5).
Subtract effort from friction.

Kitchen drawer: 4 - 1 = +3
Entryway drop zone: 5 - 2 = +3
Garage: 3 - 5 = -2

The humble drawer wins.

[VISUAL] The Target Scorecard table. Drawer and entryway rows highlighted with a green calm-dot at the top; the garage row muted at the bottom. Slate headers.

**ON-SLIDE COPY:**

You don't need more motivation.
You need a smaller, smarter first target.

Pick one. Write it down where you'll see it.
One space. One session.
