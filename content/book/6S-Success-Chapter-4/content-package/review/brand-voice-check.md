# Brand Voice Check: Chapter 4 Content Package

The voice of *6S Success: Home Edition* is warm, plain-spoken, and quietly confident. Chapter 4 adds a specific job: it protects the reader from overwhelm. It talks a motivated person out of a doomed big start and into a small, smart one, and it does so with affection, never scolding.

## Voice fingerprint (from the drafted final HTML and manuscript)
- **Warm and personal.** Talks to "you," uses an exact, recognizable moment (Saturday morning, one cup of coffee, the garage doorway).
- **Kind, not scolding.** "That voice is lying to you. You did not fail at organizing. You failed at choosing."
- **Plain over technical.** Explains the friction-vs-effort decision without jargon.
- **Concrete and specific.** A wall of bins, one cup of coffee, a kitchen catch-all drawer, the entryway drop zone.
- **Gently witty.** "An expert's project." "A respectable way to never begin."
- **Honest about scope.** Small space, small win. The friction meter does not move because no objects moved.

## Specific voice wins
- The affectionate garage treatment is preserved everywhere. The chapter never mocks the reader for wanting the garage; it sequences the garage for later. That tone carries into LinkedIn, X, FB, and the podcast.
- "You did not fail at organizing. You failed at choosing." survives intact as the chapter's sharpest line, across the thread, a quote card, and the article.
- "Guilt is a terrible compass. Friction is a good one" is used as a clean, repeatable refrain.

## Voice risks to watch
- **Contractions on X.** The book uses few; X uses several. Deliberate platform choice. Revise only if brand guidelines forbid contractions everywhere.
- **CTA sameness.** Rotate the sign-offs so they do not feel templated.
- **Keep the garage affectionate.** A few "where to start" angles could tip into mocking people who love their garage project. Spot-checked: the assets stay on the kind side. Keep it that way in any edits.
- **Emoji on Facebook.** Used sparingly. Confirm it matches your brand's emoji policy.

## Banned-language audit
- No em dashes, en dashes, or spaced hyphens (" - ") used as separators anywhere (verified, zero hits; Markdown list bullets and arithmetic minus signs are not separators).
- No prohibited hype words as filler. "Transform" does not appear at all.
- No "in today's fast-paced world" or similar.

## Verdict
Voice is consistent and intact across all twelve packages, and the anti-overwhelm job of Chapter 4 is honored throughout. Approve the voice. The chapter itself still needs author sign-off, since it was drafted rather than supplied.
