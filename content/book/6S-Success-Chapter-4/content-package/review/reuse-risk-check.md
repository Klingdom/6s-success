# Reuse Risk Check: Chapter 4 Content Package

When one chapter becomes fifty assets, the risk is overexposure: the same line, hook, or image hitting the same follower too many times until the message feels canned. This check flags where that can happen and how the calendar manages it.

## High-frequency elements (used in many assets)

| Element | Appears in | Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| The tempting garage | LinkedIn post 1, X thread 1, FB longform, YouTube open, podcast open, landing intro, garage og:image, image prompt | A follower on several platforms sees the same opening repeatedly | Calendar staggers these across Days 1 to 10. Each asset retells it slightly differently. |
| "You did not fail at organizing. You failed at choosing." | X thread, LinkedIn post, quote card, article | Medium | The sharpest line in the chapter. Spread it out; do not stack it twice in one week on one channel. |
| The First Target Map | Hero infographic, carousel, deck, LinkedIn post, X post | Medium | The map is the chapter's hero device, so repetition reinforces. Vary the caption and which quadrant you emphasize. |
| Guilt vs friction (the compass) | LinkedIn post, FB group, X post, quote card, the compass diagram | Medium | Strong refrain. Use across formats, not stacked on the same day. |
| The choose-and-write-it-down CTA | Most assets (it is the hero action) | Low to Medium | This is the chapter's main conversion event, so frequent use is intended. Vary the wording. |
| Soft-CTA sign-off wording | Most LinkedIn and X posts | Medium | Rotate phrasings from the CTA bank. Most likely thing to feel templated. |

## Same-day collision check
The calendar was built to avoid two assets sharing the same hook on the same day. Confirmed clear at the planned spacing. If your LinkedIn and Facebook audiences overlap heavily, push FB post 1 (Day 4) to Day 5 so the garage hook does not land twice in one day.

## Platform cross-posting risk
- The LinkedIn carousel outline (`linkedin/`) and the slides carousel copy (`slides/`) cover the same First Target Map content. Post only one to LinkedIn. Use the copy-ready version (`slides/linkedin-carousel-copy.md`) and keep the outline as a planning doc.
- The LinkedIn newsletter version and the email newsletter version share structure. Fine for different lists, but do not send both to the same subscriber the same day.

## Duplicate-content / SEO risk
- The web landing intro, ebook description, and LinkedIn article overlap in phrasing. For SEO, the canonical chapter page should be the only indexed full version. Set syndicated copies (LinkedIn article, Medium, etc.) to point back with a canonical link.

## Cross-chapter risk (specific to Chapter 4)
- Chapter 3 ended with a fifteen-minute first lap, and Chapter 4 is about choosing a first target. The two are adjacent and both touch "where and how to start." Keep their angles distinct when both are live: Chapter 3 is "walk the loop once on a small space," Chapter 4 is "choose which space, and avoid the garage." Do not run both chapters' "start small" content the same week.
- The garage appears as the wrong-choice example here, and the garage is a real later chapter in Part 9. When that chapter publishes, make sure the social does not contradict this one. Here the garage is "not yet." There it is "now, and here is how." Frame the later one as the graduation the reader earned.

## Originality and source-fidelity risk
- The chapter itself is a draft authored in the book voice, not supplied source. All derived assets are faithful to that draft. If the author revises the chapter, refresh the assets so they do not drift from the published version.
- Nothing is borrowed from outside the book. All quotes are from the drafted chapter.

## Recommendations
1. Rotate CTA wording across posts (highest-value, lowest-effort fix).
2. Pick one carousel version for LinkedIn, not both.
3. Set the chapter page as the SEO canonical and mark syndicated copies.
4. Keep Chapter 3 and Chapter 4 social angles distinct when both are live, and reconcile the garage framing with the future garage chapter.
5. Re-sync all assets if the drafted chapter changes after author review.
