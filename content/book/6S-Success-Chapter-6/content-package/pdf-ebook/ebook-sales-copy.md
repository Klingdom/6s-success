# Photograph Before You Fix (Sales Page Copy)

## You stopped seeing your own mess. And the proof of your work keeps disappearing.

There is a pile in your home you no longer notice. Not because it is hidden, but because your brain habituates to anything that stays the same and quietly filed it under "normal, ignore." A guest sees it the second they walk in. You have not really seen it in months.

So when you finally clean a space, something frustrating happens. A week later it looks ordinary again, the old voice says you were never really an organized person, and you have nothing to point to. You did the work, but it left no proof, because you skipped the before photo. Or you took one after straightening up first, which made your real, hard-won after look like barely any change at all.

## An honest before is what makes the after worth it.

Chapter 6 of *6S Success: Home Edition* fixes this. The camera never habituates. It records your space the way it actually is, which is the first step to changing it. And the before and after pair is the most motivating thing in the whole method. Its power lives entirely in the gap between the two pictures, and that gap only exists if your before is honest.

## What you get in this chapter

- Why your eye stops seeing the messes you live with, and why a photo breaks the spell
- Why the embarrassing before is the valuable one, and the staged before robs you
- The matched-pair technique: same spot, whole area, good light, do not tidy first
- How to mark your spot so the after lines up with the before
- How to save your photos in a "6S Before" album, and keep them private if you want to

The before is yours. It serves you, not an audience. You never have to show it to anyone.
