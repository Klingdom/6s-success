# Photograph Before You Fix

**6S Success: Home Edition**

Part Two, Prepare · Chapter Six

---

## Chapter descriptor

Your eye made peace with the mess months ago. The camera never did. Record an honest starting point before you change a thing.

---

## How to read this chapter

This is the middle chapter of Part Two. It follows the audit in Chapter 5, where you put a number on your space, and it hands off to Chapter 7, where you decide what the space is actually for.

This chapter does not fix anything. Its whole job is to record an honest before photo of the space you already chose and audited. Read it through once, then do the one small action at the end: stand in a fixed spot, frame the whole area, and take the picture. It takes about a minute. Do not tidy first.

If photographing your own mess feels uncomfortable, read the section "Keep It, and Keep It Yours" first. The before is yours. You never have to show it to anyone.
