# 6S Success: Home Edition (Ebook Description)

There is a pile somewhere in your home you cannot see anymore. It is in plain sight, you walk past it a dozen times a day, and your brain has quietly filed it under "normal, ignore." A guest sees it instantly. You have not really seen it in months.

*6S Success: Home Edition* is a practical method for getting your home in order one small space at a time. Chapter 6, "Photograph Before You Fix," is the chapter most organizing advice skips.

Your brain habituates to anything that stays the same, so the mess you live with becomes invisible to the one person most able to fix it, which is you. The camera is the cure. It never habituates. It records what is actually there, sharply, with all the friction your eye has learned to smooth away.

In this chapter you will learn why you stop seeing your own clutter, why the embarrassing before photo is the valuable one, and how to shoot a matched pair so the after lines up with the before and proves the change really happened. You will get the full technique: same spot, whole area, good light, and the one rule people break every time, do not tidy first.

The before is yours. Keep it private if you wish. It serves you, not an audience. Take the picture you would rather not take. It is the one that will prove, later, that you did this.
