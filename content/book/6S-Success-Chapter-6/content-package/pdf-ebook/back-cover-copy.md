# Back Cover Copy

## Photograph Before You Fix

There is a pile in your home you cannot see anymore. Your brain habituates to anything that stays the same, so the mess you walk past every day becomes invisible to the one person most able to fix it, which is you. A guest sees it instantly.

The camera is the cure, because it never habituates. Before you fix the space you audited, you photograph it. Not to shame yourself. To record an honest starting point, and to give your future self the one thing that makes all of this feel worth it: proof.

This chapter teaches the matched pair, so your after lines up with your before and shows the change plainly. Same spot, whole area, good light, and the one rule people break every time, do not tidy first. The embarrassing before is the valuable one. And it is yours to keep private if you wish.

Take the picture you would rather not take. It is the one that will prove, later, that you did this.
