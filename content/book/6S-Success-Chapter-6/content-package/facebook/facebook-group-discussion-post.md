# Facebook Group Discussion Post: Photograph Before You Fix (Chapter 6)

For a home-and-organizing Facebook group. Friendly, useful, conversation-first. Not an ad.

---

Here is a small thing I learned that changed how I look at my own house. 🏠

You stop seeing a mess about three days after it arrives. Your brain habituates to anything that stays the same, so the pile you walk past every day quietly becomes scenery. It is not laziness. Your brain is saving its attention for whatever is new. The catch is that a guest walks in and sees the clutter in a second, while you genuinely cannot anymore.

The fix that surprised me was just a phone camera. The lens never habituates, so a photo shows your space the way a guest sees it, which is the way it actually is. The first time I did it I was a little shocked, and that shock was weirdly motivating.

So I am curious, and I would love to hear from this group:

What is one mess in your home that you have completely stopped noticing? The chair that became a wardrobe, the drop zone by the door, that one drawer? No judgment here, we have all got at least one. 🙂

Drop yours in the comments. I have a feeling we will all recognize each other's.

(For anyone who wants the deeper version of this, the idea comes from Chapter 6 of a home-organizing book called 6S Success: Home Edition, which you can read free online. Sharing because the "you stop seeing your own clutter" bit genuinely helped me, not to sell anything.)
