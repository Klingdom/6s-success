# Visual Storyboard: Photograph Before You Fix

**Book:** 6S Success: Home Edition · Chapter 6
**Slug:** photograph-before-you-fix

This storyboard ties the teaching deck and the LinkedIn carousel to the book's own design system, drawn straight from the chapter's HTML art. Every beat reuses the book's palette, type, and the green calm-dot vs red friction-spark motif so the slides read as the same family as the chapter.

## Design system reference (from chapter_06_final.html)

Palette and roles:
- Paper `#F7F2E9`: slide backgrounds, the warm base.
- Panel `#FBF7EF`: cards, figure frames, the inside of phone screens.
- Ink `#2B2622`: primary text, the camera frame outline.
- Terracotta `#BC4B2A`: eyebrows, the floor mark, primary accent.
- Honey `#DDA63A`: art notes, "keep this" tags, secondary accent.
- Slate `#3C5A6B`: the goal crosshair, labels, the quiet/private cues, the GOAL marker.
- Green calm-dot `#6E8B5B`: the eye side, the "after" dots, the calm side of the friction meter.
- Red friction-spark `#CB4B36`: the camera side, the friction marks, the NOW side of the meter.

Type:
- Fraunces (display) for titles and pull lines.
- Newsreader (body) for captions and italic asides.
- A clean sans (Inter) only for small uppercase labels and tag text, matching the chapter's figure labels.

The recurring motif in one sentence: anything seen by the forgiving eye gets a soft green wash and a calm dot; anything seen by the honest camera gets sharp ink lines and a red spark. Every key beat below leans on that one contrast.

## Beat 1 · The phone-photographing opener
**Used on:** Teaching slide 1, Carousel slide 1.

A hand holds a phone up to a cluttered entryway drop zone. The room behind the phone is rendered soft, in `#C9B79A` outlines on a warm dawn gradient (`#FBE9C8` to panel `#FBF7EF`), the way the eye sees it. The phone screen shows the same drop zone but sharp: ink-black outlines, a slate bag, and two red friction-spark marks `#CB4B36` floating over the worst of it. This is the whole chapter in one image, the soft room and the sharp screen, side by side.

Palette cues: dawn gradient background, ink phone frame, panel screen fill, two red sparks. Motif: the room is the eye (soft, no sparks), the screen is the camera (sharp, sparks lit).

Caption line (Newsreader italic): "The same clutter, twice. In the room your eye softens it. On the screen it is sharp and undeniable."

## Beat 2 · The fade into scenery (habituation)
**Used on:** Teaching slides 2 and 3, Carousel slide 2.

A three-frame timeline showing the same pile on Day 1, Day 2, Day 3. Day 1 is sharp ink with a red spark. By Day 3 the same pile has drifted into a soft green wash `#6E8B5B` at 7 percent opacity, outlines faded to `#C9B79A`, the spark gone, a single calm green dot in its place. Palette cues: progressive green wash, fading outlines. Motif: red spark on Day 1 dissolving into a green calm-dot by Day 3, the eye taking over from the camera.

## How the motif recurs across the deck

- Soft green wash `#6E8B5B` appears on every "eye" or "calm" or "privacy" beat (Beats 2, 3 left, 9), signaling forgiveness, habit, and the reader's control.
- Red friction-spark `#CB4B36` appears on every "camera" or "friction" beat (Beats 1 screen, 3 right, 4, 5, 8), signaling honest, sharp, undeniable seeing.
- The spark-to-calm-dot swap (Beats 5 and 6) is the visual shorthand for the whole transformation, the same friction point shown first as a red spark, then as a green dot.
- Terracotta `#BC4B2A` is reserved for the one fixed human anchor, the floor mark, so the warmest color always points to "stand here, both times."
- The closing friction meter (Beat 10) gathers the entire spectrum, green calm to red friction, into one dial, and wraps the NOW point in photo-corner brackets to land the chapter's promise: measured, pictured, impossible to deny.
