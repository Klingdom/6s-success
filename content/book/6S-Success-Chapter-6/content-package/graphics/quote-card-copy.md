# Quote Card Copy: Chapter 6, "Photograph Before You Fix"

Ten quote cards for social. Every quote is pulled verbatim from
`content-package/canonical/chapter-quotes.md`, trimmed only to fit a card, with no em
dashes. Attribution is the same line on every card. Each card has one design note
naming the palette accent and the motif it should carry.

Shared style: warm paper background `#F7F2E9`, Fraunces for the quote, Newsreader
italic for any sub-line, Inter small caps for the attribution in soft gray `#6A625A`.
Square 1080 x 1080 and portrait 1080 x 1350 both work; keep the quote to four lines or
fewer.

---

### Card 1
**Quote:** "The eye forgives. The camera does not."
**Attribution:** 6S Success: Home Edition, Chapter 6
**Design note:** The hero line. Split the card down the middle with the dashed rule
from the Eye vs the Camera motif: left half a faint green wash (`#6E8B5B` at 7 percent),
right half crisp on panel `#FBF7EF` with one spark-red friction mark (`#CB4B36`).

### Card 2

**Attribution:** 6S Success: Home Edition, Chapter 6

## Notes for the designer

- Keep every card to four lines of quote or fewer. Cards 8 and 9 are the longest; if a
  card feels cramped, drop to the square 1080 x 1080 and reduce the line below it.
- Only one accent color does the emotional work per card. Spark red for friction and
  warnings, green for calm and afters, slate for evidence and the starting line.
- Attribution line is identical on all ten: `6S Success: Home Edition, Chapter 6`, set
  small in Inter, soft gray, bottom-left or centered under the quote.
