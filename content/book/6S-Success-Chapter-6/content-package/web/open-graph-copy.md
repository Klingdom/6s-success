# Open Graph and Twitter Card Copy: Chapter 6, Photograph Before You Fix

Two variants for social sharing. Use Variant A for the psychology angle, Variant B for the practical how-to angle.

## Variant A: The honest witness

- **og:title:** Photograph Before You Fix
- **og:description:** Your eye made peace with the mess months ago. The camera never did. Take an honest before photo before you change a thing, and see why it matters.
- **twitter:title:** Why You Stop Seeing Your Own Clutter
- **twitter:description:** Your brain quietly files the pile under "normal." The camera does not. Here is how to record an honest before, and why the embarrassing one is the valuable one.
- **og:image concept:** The Eye vs the Camera split. Left half shows the drop zone soft, faded, and forgiving, the way your eye sees it. Right half shows the same drop zone sharp and framed inside a phone screen, with the friction points marked. Caption line: "The eye forgives. The camera does not."

## Variant B: Take the picture

- **og:title:** The Before Photo You Would Rather Not Take
- **og:description:** It is the one that proves, later, that you did this. Learn the matched-pair technique: same spot, whole area, good light, do not tidy first.
- **twitter:title:** How to Take Before and After Photos That Prove It
- **twitter:description:** The power is in the gap between the two images, and that gap only exists if you captured an honest before. Take the picture, keep it private if you want.
- **og:image concept:** A hand holding a phone up to a real entryway drop zone. On the phone screen the clutter is crisp and undeniable; in the room beyond it is softer. A small floor mark shows where the photographer stands, "both times."
