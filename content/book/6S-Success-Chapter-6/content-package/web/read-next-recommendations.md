# Read Next Recommendations: Chapter 6, Photograph Before You Fix

1. **Chapter 5 · The 6S Home Audit**
   The setup for this chapter. You put a number on your space there; the before photo is the picture that sits next to that number.

2. **Chapter 7 · Define the Purpose of the Area**
   The next step. You have measured it and pictured it. Now the most clarifying question in Part Two: what is this space actually for?

3. **Take the before photo (action)**
   Open a "6S Before" album, stand in one fixed spot, frame the whole audited space, take a wide shot and a detail shot, and do not tidy first. About a minute of doing.

4. **The Before and After Photo Guide (companion resource)**
   A printable of the matched-pair technique, the floor-mark and same-spot rules, and a place to log the date and your audit score.

5. **The "6S Before" Album Setup Card (companion resource)**
   How to make and name the album, with a simple naming convention (space plus date) so the right before lines up with the right after later.
