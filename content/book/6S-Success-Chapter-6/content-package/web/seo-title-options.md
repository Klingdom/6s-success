# SEO Title Options: Chapter 6, Photograph Before You Fix

Five title options for the web page. Character counts are for the title text shown below. Aim is under about 60 characters where the angle allows.

1. **Photograph Before You Fix** (26 chars)
   Angle: Canonical book title. Clean, distinctive, and on-brand for the series. Best as the primary H1 and page title.

2. **Why You Stop Seeing Your Own Clutter** (37 chars)
   Angle: Leads with the habituation hook, a genuinely searched and underserved question. Pulls in readers curious about the psychology, not just the how-to.

3. **How to Take Before and After Photos That Prove It** (50 chars)
   Angle: Practical how-to for the matched-pair technique. Targets the strong "before and after photos" keyword and signals a real method, not just advice.

4. **The Before Photo You Would Rather Not Take** (43 chars)
   Angle: Emotional and counterintuitive, drawn from the One Idea to Keep. Good for click-through; pairs the embarrassment with the payoff.

5. **Photograph Before You Fix: The Honest Before Photo** (51 chars)
   Angle: Canonical title plus a keyword-rich subtitle. Carries the brand while adding "before photo" search signal for ranking.
