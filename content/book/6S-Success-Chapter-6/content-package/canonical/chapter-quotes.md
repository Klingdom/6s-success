# Chapter 6 Quotes: Photograph Before You Fix

Pull quotes lifted directly from the manuscript and final HTML, ready for quote cards, social posts, and graphics. All are verbatim or lightly trimmed. Nothing here is invented.

## Verbatim lines (book voice)

1. "There is a pile somewhere in your home that you cannot see anymore."

2. "Your eye made peace with this mess months ago. The camera never did."

3. "Your brain habituates to anything that stays the same, so it can save its attention for what is new and what might matter."

4. "The mess you live with becomes invisible to the one person most able to fix it, which is you. A guest walks in and sees it instantly."

5. "Your eye is not a passive window. It is an editor, and a generous one."

6. "Stopped being news and stopped being a problem feel identical from the inside, and they are not the same thing at all."

7. "That small shock is not a feeling to avoid. It is accurate vision returning."

8. "The eye forgives. The camera does not."

9. "A before photo is not vanity and it is not for show. It is evidence."

10. "The power lives entirely in the gap between the two images, and that gap only exists if you captured an honest before."

11. "The embarrassing before is the valuable one."

12. "A modest before makes a modest after. An honest, slightly mortifying before makes an after that genuinely moves you."

13. "You are not taking a photo, you are taking the first half of a matched pair."

14. "Mark where you stood. The after photo only lands if it is shot from the exact same spot, so the room, not the camera, is what changed."

15. "A staged before is a lie you tell only yourself, and it fools you in the worst possible direction."

16. "The most satisfying afters in the world sit next to befores that made their owners cringe. Cringe a little. It is worth it."

17. "The photo serves you, not an audience. You do not owe the world your drop zone."

18. "The starting line is now drawn, in ink, and the only direction left to go is forward."

## Definition box (canonical)

"A before photo is not vanity and it is not for show. It is evidence. It is the visual baseline that, paired with your audit number, proves the change you are about to make actually happened."

## One Idea to Keep (canonical close)

"The after only means something next to an honest before. Take the picture you would rather not take. It is the one that will prove, later, that you did this."

## The technique (for graphics)

The Matched Pair: shoot the before and the future after from the same marked spot, same frame, same light. Rules: same spot, whole area, good light, do not tidy first.
