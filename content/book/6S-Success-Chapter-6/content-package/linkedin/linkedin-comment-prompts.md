# LinkedIn Comment Prompts: Photograph Before You Fix (Chapter 6)

Five prompts to spark genuine discussion in the comments. Pair one with any post above.

1. What is one mess in your home you have completely stopped seeing? The one a guest would notice in two seconds but you walked past this morning without a thought.

2. Be honest: do you take before photos, or do you skip them? And if you skip, is it the time, or is it that you would rather not look?

3. Have you ever caught yourself tidying up "just a little" before taking a before photo? What made you want to clean up the very thing you were trying to document?

4. What is the most satisfying before and after you have ever pulled off, in your home or anywhere? Did the honest before make the after land harder?

5. Where do you land on sharing: do you post your messy befores publicly for accountability, keep them in a private album just for you, or send them to one trusted person? What works for how you are wired?
