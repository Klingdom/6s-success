# LinkedIn Carousel Outline: Photograph Before You Fix (Chapter 6)

Ten slides. Built around the two hero ideas: the Eye vs the Camera, and the Matched Pair.
Format note: square or 4:5 slides. Keep body lines short and readable on mobile.

---

## Slide 1 (Hook)
**Headline:** There is a pile in your home you cannot see anymore.
**Body:** Not hidden. In plain sight, somewhere you walk past every day. Your brain just stopped reporting it.

## Slide 2
**Headline:** This is not a flaw. It is habituation.
**Body:** Your brain tunes out anything that stays the same, to save attention for what is new. A new pile becomes scenery in about three days.

## Slide 3
**Headline:** A guest sees it instantly. You have not seen it in months.
**Body:** The mess you live with becomes invisible to the one person most able to fix it. Which is you.

## Slide 4
**Headline:** The eye forgives. The camera does not.
**Body:** Your eye is a generous editor. The lens has no history with your home, no tolerance built up. It just records what is there.

## Slide 5
**Headline:** A photo is an honest witness.
**Body:** It flattens your forgiven space into a flat rectangle of stuff you can finally see the way a stranger would. That small shock is accurate vision returning.

## Slide 6
**Headline:** The before is for the after.
**Body:** A tidy space alone is forgettable. The power of a before and after lives in the gap between the two images. That gap only exists if the before is honest.

## Slide 7
**Headline:** The embarrassing before is the valuable one.
**Body:** A modest before makes a modest after. A slightly mortifying before makes an after that genuinely moves you. Cringe a little. It is worth it.

## Slide 8
**Headline:** Shoot a matched pair.
**Body:** Same spot. Whole area. Good light. Then the room is the only thing that changes between the two photos.

## Slide 9
**Headline:** Mark where you stood.
**Body:** A scuff, a tile line, a note in your phone. The after only lands if it is shot from the exact same spot, so the room, not the camera, is what moved.

## Slide 10
**Headline:** Do not tidy first.
**Body:** Every item you straighten before the before is transformation you erase in advance. The messy truth is the point of the photo, not a side effect.

## Slide 11
**Headline:** Keep it, and keep it yours.
**Body:** Save it in a "6S Before" album. Post it or never show a soul. The photo serves you, not an audience. One trusted person is plenty for accountability.

## Slide 12 (Soft CTA)
**Headline:** Take the picture you would rather not take.
**Body:** It is the one that will prove, later, that you did this. Read Chapter 6 of *6S Success: Home Edition* free in the online book.
