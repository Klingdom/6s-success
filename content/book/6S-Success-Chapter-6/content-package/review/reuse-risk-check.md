# Reuse Risk Check: Chapter 6 Content Package

When one chapter becomes fifty assets, the risk is overexposure: the same line, hook, or image hitting the same follower too many times until the message feels canned. This check flags where that can happen and how the calendar manages it.

## High-frequency elements (used in many assets)

| Element | Appears in | Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| You stop seeing your own mess (habituation) | Web landing intro, LinkedIn post, X thread 1, FB longform, YouTube open, podcast open, quote card | A follower on several platforms sees the same reframe repeatedly | Calendar staggers these across Days 1 to 8. Vary the phrasing per platform. |
| The Eye vs the Camera | Hero infographic, carousel, deck, LinkedIn post, X post | Medium | The split is the chapter's hero device, so repetition reinforces. Vary the caption and which side you lead with. |
| The embarrassing before is the valuable one | LinkedIn post, FB post, short video, quote card | Medium | Strongest hook in the chapter. Use across formats, not stacked on the same day. |
| The matched pair (same spot, mark it) | The Matched Pair diagram, carousel, X posts, short video | Low to Medium | Practical technique, meant to repeat. Vary which rule you emphasize. |
| Do not tidy first | LinkedIn post, FB post, short video, quote card, discussion question | Medium | Memorable rule, but it also appears in Chapter 1. Keep this chapter's framing about the photo specifically. |
| Take-the-before-photo CTA | Most assets (it is the hero action) | Low to Medium | This is the chapter's main conversion event, so frequent use is intended. Vary the wording. |
| Soft-CTA sign-off wording | Most LinkedIn and X posts | Medium | Rotate phrasings from the CTA bank. Most likely thing to feel templated. |

## Same-day collision check
The calendar was built to avoid two assets sharing the same hook on the same day. Confirmed clear at the planned spacing. If your LinkedIn and Facebook audiences overlap heavily, push FB post 1 (Day 4) to Day 5 so the habituation hook does not land twice in one day.

## Platform cross-posting risk
- The LinkedIn carousel outline (`linkedin/`) and the slides carousel copy (`slides/`) cover the same Eye-vs-Camera content. Post only one to LinkedIn. Use the copy-ready version (`slides/linkedin-carousel-copy.md`) and keep the outline as a planning doc.
- The LinkedIn newsletter version and the email newsletter version share structure. Fine for different lists, but do not send both to the same subscriber the same day.

## Duplicate-content / SEO risk
- The web landing intro, ebook description, and LinkedIn article overlap in phrasing. For SEO, the canonical chapter page should be the only indexed full version. Set syndicated copies (LinkedIn article, Medium, etc.) to point back with a canonical link.

## Cross-chapter risk (specific to Chapter 6)
- "Do not tidy first" and the before photo both first appeared in Chapter 1 as the gentle first move. Chapter 6 is the full treatment. Keep the angles distinct when both are live: Chapter 1 is "take a before photo to prove you started," Chapter 6 is "here is why your eye lies and how to shoot a matched pair." Do not run the same "do not tidy first" post from both chapters in the same week.
- Chapters 5, 6, and 7 are all Part 2 (Prepare): audit, photo, purpose. They form a natural set, but each captures the starting point differently (number, image, intent). Keep their "capture your starting point" content from colliding in the same week.

## Originality and source-fidelity risk
- The chapter itself is a draft authored in the book voice, not supplied source. All derived assets are faithful to that draft. If the author revises the chapter, refresh the assets so they do not drift from the published version.
- Nothing is borrowed from outside the book. All quotes are from the authored chapter.

## Recommendations
1. Rotate CTA wording across posts (highest-value, lowest-effort fix).
2. Pick one carousel version for LinkedIn, not both.
3. Set the chapter page as the SEO canonical and mark syndicated copies.
4. Keep Chapter 1 and Chapter 6 "do not tidy first" and before-photo angles distinct when they run together, and keep the Part 2 trio (5, 6, 7) from colliding.
5. Re-sync all assets if the authored chapter changes after author review.
