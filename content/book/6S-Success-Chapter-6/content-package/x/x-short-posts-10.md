# X Short Posts: Photograph Before You Fix

Chapter 6 of "6S Success: Home Edition" (slug: photograph-before-you-fix). 10 standalone posts. Each stands alone and is at or under 280 characters. Character counts are approximate. Soft CTA on a few, not all.

---

1.
There is a pile in your home you cannot see anymore. Not hidden, just walked past so often your brain filed it under normal. A guest sees it instantly. You have not really seen it in months. The fix is a camera. (Chapter 6, 6S Success: Home Edition)

(~249 chars)

---

2.
Your brain habituates to anything that stays the same. Handy for ignoring the fridge hum, less handy when the mess goes invisible to the one person who can fix it. You stop seeing a pile about three days after it arrives.

(~221 chars)

---

3.
The eye forgives. The camera does not. Your eye softened the clutter into scenery months ago. A photo hands it back to you sharp and undeniable. That small shock is just accurate vision returning.

(~196 chars)

---

4.
A before photo is not vanity and it is not for show. It is evidence. Paired with your audit number, it proves the change you are about to make actually happened.

(~161 chars)

---

5.
A before photo doubles as a second inspection. Flattened into a frame, a space shows you the cord you would trip on and the drawer that never closes. The audit named the weakest spot. The photo shows where it lives.

(~215 chars)

---

6.
The before is for the after. A tidy space on its own is forgettable. All the power of a before and after lives in the gap between the two images, and that gap only exists if the before was honest.

(~196 chars)

---

7.
The embarrassing before is the valuable one. A modest before makes a modest after. An honest, slightly mortifying before makes an after that genuinely moves you. Take the picture you would rather not take.

(~205 chars)

---

8.
Shoot a matched pair. Pick one spot and mark it, a tile line or a scuff or a note in your phone. Frame the whole area in good light. Shoot the after from that same spot, so the room is the only thing that changed. (Chapter 6)

(~225 chars)

---

9.
The one rule that beats all the technique: do not tidy first. Every item you straighten before the before is a piece of the transformation you erase in advance. Cringe a little. It is worth it.

(~193 chars)

---

10.
Your before photo is yours. Keep it in a private album, use it as your own evidence, delete it whenever you like. The photo serves you, not an audience. Full chapter free in the online book: 6S Success: Home Edition.

(~216 chars)
