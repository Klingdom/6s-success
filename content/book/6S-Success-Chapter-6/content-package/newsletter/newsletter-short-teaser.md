# Chapter 6 Short Teaser: Photograph Before You Fix

For a roundup or digest. 90 to 140 words.

---

There is a pile somewhere in your home you cannot see anymore. Not hidden, just invisible to you. Your brain habituates to anything that stays the same, so after about three days the mess stops registering and becomes scenery. A guest sees it instantly. You have not really looked at it in months.

Chapter 6 hands you the cure: a camera, which never habituates. Before you fix the space you audited, take an honest before photo. Shoot a matched pair from one marked spot, frame the whole area, and do not tidy first, because the embarrassing before is the valuable one. Keep it private if you like. The before is what makes the after feel earned.
