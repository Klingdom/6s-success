# Publishing Calendar: 14-Day Release Sequence

A two-week rollout for Chapter 6, "Photograph Before You Fix". This chapter is the middle of Part Two and gets the reader to take an honest before photo of their audited space. The goal is to get readers to actually shoot the photo. Publish the home base (the web chapter) first, then drip social and email toward it. Dates are relative (Day 1 = the day the chapter page goes live).

Pacing principle: one anchor asset per day, plus one or two light reshares. The "you stopped seeing your own mess" hook and the Eye vs the Camera split are your strongest. Lead with them.

## After Day 14

- Recycle the Eye vs the Camera graphic and the strongest quote cards monthly.
- Keep the chapter page updated with an FAQ block for SEO (see `canonical/chapter-seo.md`). The Before and After Photo Guide is a strong lead magnet.
- When Chapter 7 publishes, link this chapter from its "previously" section, and link forward from here. Chapters 5, 6, and 7 are all Part 2 (Prepare), so the audit, the photo, and the purpose are a natural set.

## Hero CTA reminder

This chapter's best conversion event is the photo. Wherever you can, end with "stand on your spot, frame the whole space, do not tidy first, and take the picture." A reader who does that has an honest before and is ready for the work.
