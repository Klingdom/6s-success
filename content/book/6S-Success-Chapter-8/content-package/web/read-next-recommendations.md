# Read Next Recommendations: Chapter 8, Sort: Remove What Does Not Belong

1. Chapter 9, Necessary vs. Unnecessary
   The next step. You have removed what does not belong here. Now we look at what is left and ask a harder question: not where it lives, but whether you need it at all.

2. Chapter 7, Define the Purpose of the Area
   The setup. Before this pass could work, you gave the space one job in a single sentence. That purpose is the judge every object is measured against here.

3. Run your first Sort pass (the chapter action)
   Stand in your space, say its one job out loud, and ask the One Question of every object. Carry out everything that answers no. Even one object is a legal, complete Sort.

4. The One Question card (free resource)
   A printable and phone wallpaper of the single Sort question and the belongs-here versus belongs-somewhere-else split, for quick reference on any space you tackle next.

5. The First Sort Pass sheet (free resource)
   A printable with two columns to list what belongs here and what goes to a better home, so you can run a clean pass on one space without agonizing over anything.
