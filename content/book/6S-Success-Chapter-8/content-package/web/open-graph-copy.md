# Open Graph and Twitter Card Copy: Chapter 8, Sort: Remove What Does Not Belong

Two variants. Use the og:image concept noted under each.

## Variant A (the One Question hook)

- og:title: One Question That Sorts Any Space
- og:description: Hold up each object and ask one thing: does this help the space do its job? Yes stays, no goes to a better home. That is a full Sort, no shopping required.
- twitter:title: One Question That Sorts Any Space
- twitter:description: Does this help the space do its job? Yes belongs here, no belongs somewhere else. One question sorts a drop zone before you buy a single bin.
- og:image concept: The One Question gate. Small drop-zone objects flow toward a central gate labeled "Does this help the space do its job?" and split. One branch goes to a green zone labeled "belongs here" (keys, umbrella, leash, a folded bag). The other follows an arrow out to a slate zone labeled "a better home" (magazines, junk mail, a charger, homework). Caption line: One question, two outcomes.

- twitter:description: You are not deciding what it is worth. You are only deciding it does not live here. An address change, not a verdict. From Chapter 8 of 6S Success: Home Edition.
