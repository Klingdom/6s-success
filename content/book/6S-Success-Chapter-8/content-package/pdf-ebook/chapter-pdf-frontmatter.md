# Sort: Remove What Does Not Belong

## Chapter descriptor

The first S, and the first time you actually move a thing. Hold every object up to one question, does this help the space do its job, and carry out everything that answers no.

---

## How to read this chapter

This is the opening chapter of Part Three, Sort, and the first doing chapter in the book. By now you have chosen a space, put an honest number on it, taken a before photo, and written it a one-sentence purpose. This chapter turns that preparation into the first physical move.

Read it through once for the idea, then stand in your chosen space and run a real pass. Say the space's one job out loud, pick up the first thing your eye lands on, and ask the One Question: does this help the space do its job? If not, carry it to where it actually belongs. The clear items go to their real homes; the honest maybes wait in one spot while you finish. Nothing gets bought and nothing gets scrubbed. This chapter removes what does not belong here. It does not yet decide what you need to own at all, which is the next chapter.

If you only do one thing: ask the One Question of everything on one surface and carry out the noes. Even one object is a legal, complete Sort.
