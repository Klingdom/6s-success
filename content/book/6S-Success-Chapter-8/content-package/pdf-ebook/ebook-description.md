# 6S Success: Home Edition (Ebook Description)

Most decluttering advice hands you a checklist and a headache. *6S Success: Home Edition* hands you one question, and Chapter 8 is where the preparing finally turns into doing.

"Sort: Remove What Does Not Belong" opens Part Three and is the first action chapter in the book. After four chapters of choosing a space, scoring it, photographing it, and giving it a job, you finally pick something up, and it leaves. Sort has one aim: separate what belongs in a space from what does not, then remove what does not. It is not tidying, which just rearranges a pile, and not cleaning, which comes later. It is the structural step before both, the one that decides what actually lives here.

The tool is the One Question, and you point it at every object: does this help the space do its job? Yes means it belongs here and stays. No means it belongs somewhere else and goes to a better home, wherever it already lives. You will watch a worked pass through a real entryway drop zone, where keys and the umbrella stay and the magazines, junk mail, and a stray charger go back to their real addresses, and the surface gets lighter with nothing bought.

The chapter also handles the thing that stalls most people, which is not the objects but the guilt. Removing is not throwing away. Sorting an item out of a space is only an address change inside your own home, ordinary, kind, and completely reversible. You are not deciding what it is worth. You are only deciding it does not live here.

For the first time in the book, the friction meter moves. It steps toward calm because objects actually moved, and it stops partway because Sort is only the first of six S's. Ask one question. Carry out the noes. Most of the mess walks out the door before you buy a single bin.
