# Back Cover Copy: Sort: Remove What Does Not Belong

## Back-cover text

You have decluttered that spot before. You made piles, you agonized over each object, and by evening it was rearranged but just as full. The problem was never that you are bad at this. It is that the usual method asks the wrong thing of every object: do I still like this, will I use it someday, who gave it to me.

Chapter 8 of *6S Success: Home Edition* opens Part Three with the first doing step in the book, and it replaces that whole checklist with one question. Sort has one aim: separate what belongs in a space from what does not, then carry the ones that do not somewhere else. You point one question at each object: does this help the space do its job? Yes, it belongs here and stays. No, it belongs somewhere else and goes to a better home. You will watch a real entryway drop zone get lighter with nothing bought, and you will see why removing is not throwing away. You are not deciding what it is worth. You are only deciding it does not live here.

Ask one question. Carry out the noes. Most of the mess walks out the door before you buy a single bin.
