## Search intent
Action-oriented, with an undertone of relief. Readers arriving here want to actually start, not read more theory, and many carry guilt about getting rid of things. The chapter answers with a single, concrete decision rule (the One Question) and a reframe that lowers the emotional stakes (removing is an address change, not throwing away). The "sort before you buy storage" angle directly counters the common instinct to shop first, and the Seiri / first-S framing captures method searchers.
