## Sequencing logic

1. Lock `canonical/` first. If the title, slug, the One Question wording, the purpose sentence, or "One Idea to Keep" changes, it ripples everywhere.
2. Build `web/` next, because the chapter URL is the destination every soft CTA points to.
3. Then social and email, which drive traffic to that URL.
4. Video, audio, slides, and graphics are higher-effort and can trail the text assets by a few days.
5. The One Question card and the "sort first, before you buy a bin" hook are the two highest-value assets. Seed the card early and lead social with the counterintuitive hook.
