# Publishing Calendar: 14-Day Release Sequence

A two-week rollout for Chapter 8, "Sort: Remove What Does Not Belong". This chapter opens Part Three · Sort and is the first action chapter in the book. It gets the reader to run a first real Sort pass on their space, testing every object against one question and carrying out what does not belong. The goal is to get readers to actually make one move. Publish the home base (the web chapter) first, then drip social and email toward it. Dates are relative (Day 1 = the day the chapter page goes live).

Pacing principle: one anchor asset per day, plus one or two light reshares. The "sort first, before you buy a single bin" hook and the One Question card are your strongest. Lead with them.

## After Day 14

- Recycle the One Question card and the strongest quote cards monthly.
- Keep the chapter page updated with an FAQ block for SEO (see `canonical/chapter-seo.md`). The First Sort Pass sheet is a strong lead magnet.
- When Chapter 9 publishes, link this chapter from its "previously" section, and link forward from here. This chapter opens Part 3, so it is the natural doorway into the sorting work.

## Hero CTA reminder

This chapter's best conversion event is one real move. Wherever you can, end with "stand in your space, say its one job out loud, pick up the first thing your eye lands on, and ask: does this help the space do its job? If not, carry it to where it belongs. One object is a complete Sort." A reader who makes one move has started.
