# Content Package: Chapter 8, Sort: Remove What Does Not Belong

This folder is a complete content and packaging workflow for Chapter 8 of *6S Success: Home Edition*. One chapter, turned into a full set of assets for web, social, email, video, audio, slides, print, and graphics.

Chapter 8 opens Part Three · Sort. It is the first action chapter in the book. Chapters 4 to 7 chose a space, audited it, photographed it, and gave it a purpose. Nothing physically moved. In Chapter 8 the reader runs a first real Sort pass on that same space, the entryway drop zone, testing every object against one question and carrying out what does not belong. The friction meter, held on the friction side for four chapters, takes its first honest step toward calm. It does not arrive. Sort is only the first of six S's.

## The chapter in one screen

- **Hero device · The One Question.** Every object in the space passes through one gate: does this help the space do its job? Yes means it belongs here. No means it belongs somewhere else, and it goes to a better home.
- **The judge · the purpose sentence from Chapter 7.** This entryway is for getting everyone out the door fast and landing softly on the way back in, so that mornings start calm instead of frantic. The purpose is the standard; the One Question is how you apply it, object by object.
- **The worked pass.** The drop zone is judged item by item. Keys, outgoing mail, sunglasses, the leash, a folded bag, the umbrella, and a small tray stay. Old magazines, junk mail, a desk charger, school papers, a stray screwdriver, a closet sweater, and the coin-collecting bowl go to a better home. The surface gets lighter.
- **Sort by address, not by fate.** Removing an item only decides it does not live here. It does not decide whether you keep it or let it go. That is a later chapter. You are not deciding what a thing is worth. You are only deciding it does not live here.
- **The needle moves.** Nothing was bought and nothing was cleaned. Objects simply left, and the space is already lighter. The meter steps toward the goal and stops partway, because five S's are still to come.

## Scope note

Chapter 8 sorts by belonging, not by need. It asks only whether an object belongs in this space. It does not ask whether you need to own it (that is Chapter 9), it does not build a red-tag or holding-area system (that is Chapter 10), and it does not decide donate, sell, or discard (that is Chapter 11). "A better home" means where the item lives now, not a giveaway pile. The chapter sets up those later decisions without making them.

## House rules used throughout

- No em dashes, no en dashes, no spaced hyphen as a separator. Labels use the middot (·).
- Plain, warm, calm, concrete, non-prescriptive voice. Removing is not throwing away, and Sort is not a verdict on any object's worth.
- No force or violence metaphors. No hype verbs.
- Every social post is useful on its own, with a soft CTA to the chapter or online book.
- Nothing is invented beyond the chapter. Inferences are labeled.
