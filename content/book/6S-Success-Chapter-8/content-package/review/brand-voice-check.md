# Brand Voice Check: Chapter 8 Content Package

The voice of *6S Success: Home Edition* is warm, plain-spoken, and quietly confident. Chapter 8 opens Part 3 (Sort) and carries a new job: it is the first action chapter, the first time an object actually leaves. The voice has to make that feel satisfying without ever tipping into force or shame. It does. Removing is framed as an address change, not a verdict, and the reader is never told what to keep.

## Voice fingerprint (from the authored final HTML and manuscript)
- **Warm and personal.** Talks to "you," returns to the same scene the reader already knows (the entryway drop zone they chose, scored, photographed, and gave a purpose).
- **Non-prescriptive and anti-shame.** The reassurance line, verbatim in the manuscript, is "You are not deciding what it is worth. You are only deciding it does not live here." It reframes stalling as guilt, not a character flaw.
- **Plain over clever.** "The clutter is not a filing problem. It is an occupancy problem." (verbatim)
- **Concrete and specific.** Real objects carry the writing: keys, the dog's leash, the umbrella, a folded reusable bag, a phone charger, a stray screwdriver, junk mail, finished homework, a decorative bowl that collects coins. Each of these strings was grep-verified in the manuscript.
- **Gently witty, never mean.** "notice the squatters and show them the way out" and "You are not judging the charger. You are just checking its address" (both verbatim).
- **Honest about the gauge.** This is the landmark: the friction meter moves for the first time in the book, and the voice is careful to say it moved partway, not all the way. "The needle stops in the middle, pointed at the goal but not arrived" (verbatim).

## Specific voice wins
- **The One Question survives intact everywhere.** "Does this help the space do its job?" is the operational form of Chapter 7's purpose, and it carries the thread, the quote cards, the article, and the infographics as the same question, not a paraphrase.
- **The anti-shame spine holds.** No asset shames the reader for the mess. "You are not deciding what it is worth. You are only deciding it does not live here" appears as the chapter's reassurance and is reused as a quote card, a LinkedIn post, and an X post, always in the same non-prescriptive register.
- **The scope discipline reads as voice, not a rulebook.** "Sort by address, not by fate" (verbatim 6S Tip) keeps removal from sounding like disposal, and the maybes get one light forward-teaser without building Chapter 10's system.
- **The landmark is handled with restraint.** The friction-meter payoff is written as an event ("for the first time in this entire book, it has something to report") but immediately tempered ("far enough to feel real, short of a finish you have not yet earned"). That honesty is the brand.

## Voice risks to watch
- **"belongs" runs hot.** The word "belongs" appears about 25 times in the manuscript (counted). This is the chapter's frozen outcome vocabulary (belongs here / belongs somewhere else), so the repetition is structural rather than filler, but watch it in the social assets so no single post stacks it three or four times.
- **Contractions on X.** The book uses few; X uses several. Deliberate platform choice. Revise only if brand guidelines forbid contractions everywhere.
- **CTA sameness.** The soft-CTA bank in `canonical/chapter-cta.md` holds six phrasings (counted). Rotate them so LinkedIn and X sign-offs do not feel templated.
- **Emoji on Facebook.** One emoji at the end of the longform post. Confirm it matches your brand's emoji policy.

## Banned-language audit
- **Widened dash scan, run for this review.** I ran a scan for em dash (U+2014), en dash (U+2013), and the spaced-hyphen " - " used as a separator (excluding markdown list bullets at line start) across all 52 package files plus `chapter_08_manuscript.md`, `chapter_08_final.html`, and `chapter-08-publishable.html`. Result: zero em dashes, zero en dashes, zero spaced-hyphen separators. The package uses the middot ("·") for masthead and label separators. For full transparency, the only em/en dashes anywhere in the chapter folder are four inside the working brief `CH8_CANONICAL_STRINGS_AND_BRIEF.md`, which is not a shipped package file.
- **Hype-word scan.** Zero hits across the package for "unlock," "leverage," "seamless," "elevate," "supercharge," "game-changing," "delve," "dive in," "in conclusion," "fast-paced," "in today's," "effortless," "revolutionize" (scan run).
- **No force or violence framing.** No "purge," "attack the pile," "war on clutter," or "pull the trigger." Removal is consistently an address change.

## Verdict
Voice is consistent and intact across all channels, and the two things that make this chapter different (the first physical move and the honest partial swing of the friction meter) are honored throughout. Approve the voice. The chapter itself still needs author sign-off, since it was authored in the book voice rather than supplied from source.
