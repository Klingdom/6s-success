# Newsletter Short Teaser: Sort: Remove What Does Not Belong

For a roundup or digest. 90 to 140 words.

---

For four chapters you prepared and did not touch a thing. This one, you finally pick something up and it leaves. Sort is the first of the six S's: separate what belongs in a space from what does not, then carry the noes somewhere else. It is not tidying and not cleaning. The tool is one question you point at every object: does this help the space do its job? Keys stay, the umbrella stays, the stack of magazines and the stray charger go to a better home. No shopping, no scrubbing, no guilt, because you are only deciding it does not live here, not what it is worth. Do that, and for the first time the friction meter moves.
