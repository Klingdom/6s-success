# X Short Posts: Sort: Remove What Does Not Belong

Chapter 8 of *6S Success: Home Edition*. Ten standalone posts. Each is at or under 280 characters and makes sense on its own. Soft CTA on a few, not all.

---

1/
Four chapters of preparing, then this: Sort, the first S, and the first time you actually move a thing. Its one aim is to separate what belongs in a space from what does not, and carry the noes somewhere else. Chapter 8 of 6S Success: Home Edition, free online.

(approx. 254 chars)

---

2/
Sort is not tidying; a tidied pile is still a pile. Sort is not cleaning either. It is the blunt step before both, and it asks one thing of every object: does this live here, yes or no? The yeses stay. The noes leave.

(approx. 210 chars)

---

3/
The clutter is not a filing problem. It is an occupancy problem, and step one is simply to notice the squatters and show them the way out.

(approx. 137 chars)

---

4/
One question sorts any space: Does this help the space do its job? Yes means it belongs here and stays. No means it belongs somewhere else and goes to a better home. The purpose you wrote becomes a tool you can point at things.

(approx. 223 chars)

---

5/
A phone charger is genuinely useful, and in the entryway the answer is still no, because charging phones is not what the space is for. You are not judging the charger. You are just checking its address.

(approx. 200 chars)

---

6/
Sort has two halves. First separate what belongs from what does not. Then actually remove. People love the first and skip the second, which is why so many organizing afternoons end with the room just as full, only rearranged.

(approx. 222 chars)

---

7/
A better home is where the thing already lives. The charger to the desk, the screwdriver to the toolbox, the sweater to the closet. You are not inventing homes. You are returning things to the ones they wandered from. Chapter 8, free online.

(approx. 237 chars)

---

8/
What stalls a Sort is not the objects. It is guilt, when a simple move starts to feel like a verdict. Keep this close: You are not deciding what it is worth. You are only deciding it does not live here.

(approx. 199 chars)

---

9/
Removing is not throwing away. Sorting a thing out of a space is only an address change inside your own home, not a decision to donate, sell, or discard it. Sort by address, not by fate.

(approx. 184 chars)

---

10/
The friction meter moves for the first time, and stops partway. Objects actually moved, so the needle travels toward calm, done by hand with no money spent. It stops in the middle because Sort is only the first of six S's. Chapter 8, free online.
