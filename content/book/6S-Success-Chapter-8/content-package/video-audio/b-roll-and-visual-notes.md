# B-Roll and Visual Notes: Sort: Remove What Does Not Belong

**Chapter 8, 6S Success: Home Edition**
**Slug:** sort-remove-what-does-not-belong
**Use:** Shot list, on-screen text moments, and lower-third suggestions for the YouTube, short-form, and any companion video cuts.

**Visual vocabulary to keep consistent (from the chapter):**
- Green calm-dots glow on objects that belong here (they help the space do its job).
- Red friction-sparks fire on objects that do not belong (they leave for a better home).
- The One Question is the recurring device: a central gate that objects flow toward and split at.
- The friction meter MOVES this chapter, for the first time in the book. The needle steps off the friction side into the honey middle, toward calm. A faint ghost marks where it sat. The GOAL crosshair stays ahead on the calm side, still carrying its purpose tag.
- Warm morning light, lived-in homes, real households. Not a styled catalog look.
- Nothing is bought and nothing is cleaned on camera. The only thing that happens is that some objects leave.

### Shot 5: The console before and after
- The same surface, two states. Before: drowning, holding all of it. After: holding only a keys tray, a leash, an umbrella, a bag, and tomorrow's mail.

### Shot 6: It is not throwing away (the anti-shame beat)
- A hand lifts the decorative bowl, pauses, almost sets it back down, then steadies and carries it calmly out of frame.
- The tension is the guilt tug and its release. Removing is an address change, not a verdict.
- Optional insert: the charger being set down at a desk, safe and sound, clearly not discarded.
- **On-screen text moment:** "You are not deciding what it is worth." then "You are only deciding it does not live here."
- **Lower third:** Removing is not throwing away

### Shot 7: The maybes wait in one spot
- A hand sets one genuinely uncertain object into a single labeled box or corner, then keeps moving to the next clear call.
- Shows the discipline: do not stall on the hard ones, park them in one place and finish the clear majority.
- **On-screen text moment:** "Not sure? One spot. Keep moving." then "The maybes get their own system soon."

## EDITING NOTES

- This is the first action chapter. It should feel like an event after four chapters of prep. Let the first object lift land as a small payoff.
- Keep the cut calm and unhurried. The tone is deciding and moving, not frantic. No fast montage of "purging."
- Do show objects leaving the space this chapter. That is the whole point, and it is the promise Chapter 7 set up. But never show anything being thrown in the trash, donated, or sold. Items only move to another room inside the home. Disposal belongs to a later chapter.
- Avoid any force or violence framing in visuals: no dramatic sweeping-off of surfaces, no aggressive dumping. Objects are carried, calmly, to their real homes.
- The friction-meter move in Shot 8 is the landmark. Give it room. It must look visibly different from the four "held" chapters.
- Color and warmth should match Chapters 1 through 7: warm paper tones, terracotta and honey accents, slate for any data overlays, green for the calm-dots.
