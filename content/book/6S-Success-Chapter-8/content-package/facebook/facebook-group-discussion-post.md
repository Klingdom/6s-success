# Facebook Group Discussion Post (Home and Organizing Groups)

Written for a home-and-organizing Facebook group. Leads with a genuinely useful nugget (one question sorts any space), then asks an open question to get people talking. Friendly group tone, soft CTA at the very end, and written so it does NOT read like an ad.

---

Okay, the single most useful decluttering trick I have picked up in years, and then I want to hear how your worst spot would survive it.

For the longest time I thought my entryway table was just messy and that I was just bad at keeping it clear. Then I stopped asking whether I liked each thing on it and started asking one blunt little question instead: does this help the space do its job?

My entryway has one job, get everyone out the door fast and land them softly on the way back in. So I pointed that question at everything on the table. Keys, yes. Leash, yes. Umbrella, yes. The magazines I already read? No. The phone charger? Useful, but it lives better at a desk, so no. The decorative bowl full of coins nobody counts? No.

Here is the part that surprised me. I was not deciding whether any of that stuff was good or worth keeping. I was only deciding it does not live here. The charger did not get thrown out, it just went back to the desk where it belongs. Sort by address, not by fate. That reframe took all the guilt out of it, and I actually finished for once.

So here is what I am genuinely curious about:

What is the one job of your messiest little space? And if you asked every object on it "does this help the space do its job," what would stay, and what would you walk back to where it actually lives?

I will go first in the comments with my coin bowl of shame. 😄
