# Chapter 8 LinkedIn Carousel Outline: Sort: Remove What Does Not Belong

Twelve slides. Slide 1 hooks, last slide carries the soft CTA. Each slide has a headline and one or two lines of body.

---

## Slide 1: Hook

**Headline:** Before you buy a single bin, do this.

**Body:** Sort first, and most of the mess walks out the door before you spend a cent. Swipe for the whole move.

---

## Slide 2: The trap

**Headline:** Storage bought first just locks the clutter in.

**Body:** A neat bin around a room that was already holding too much is not progress. It is contained overload.

---

## Slide 3: The reframe

**Headline:** It is an occupancy problem.

**Body:** Clutter is not a filing problem. Most of the chaos is the sheer presence of things that were never supposed to be there.

---

## Slide 4: What Sort is

**Headline:** Separate what belongs, remove what does not.

**Body:** That is the whole aim of Sort, the first of six S's. Buy nothing, clean nothing, just decide what lives here.

---

## Slide 5: The One Question

**Headline:** One question tests every object.

**Body:** Does this help the space do its job? Yes, it belongs here. No, it belongs somewhere else.

---

## Slide 6: Check the address

**Headline:** You are checking the address, not judging the object.

**Body:** A charger is genuinely useful. For the entryway the answer is still no, because charging phones is not the job.

---

## Slide 7: Do the second half

**Headline:** Separating is thinking. Removing changes the room.

**Body:** People love the split and skip the carry-out, which is why rooms end up rearranged, not lighter. Do the removing for real.

---

## Slide 8: A better home

**Headline:** Most things already have a home.

**Body:** Charger to the desk, screwdriver to the toolbox, sweater to the closet. You are returning things to the ones they wandered from.

---

## Slide 9: Park the maybes

**Headline:** Do not let one hard object stall the pass.

**Body:** Set the honest maybes in one spot and keep moving. They get their own system a couple of chapters on.

---

## Slide 10: Removing is not throwing away

**Headline:** You are not deciding what it is worth.

**Body:** You are only deciding it does not live here. Nothing donated, sold, or thrown out. It moved. Completely reversible.

---

## Slide 11: The needle moves

**Headline:** For the first time, the meter moves.

**Body:** Objects actually left, so the friction needle steps toward calm. It stops partway, because Sort is only the first of six S's.

---

## Slide 12: Soft CTA

**Headline:** You sorted by belonging. Next, by need.
