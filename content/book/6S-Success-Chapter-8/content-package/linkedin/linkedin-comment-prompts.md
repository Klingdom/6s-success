# Chapter 8 LinkedIn Comment Prompts: Sort: Remove What Does Not Belong

Five prompts to start genuine discussion. Drop one in the comments of a post or article to invite replies.

---

1. Point the One Question at your messiest surface: does this help the space do its job? Name one thing sitting there right now that answers a clear no.

2. What is one object in your home that belongs somewhere else and just got lazy? Tell us where it actually lives, then go make the ten-second walk.

3. Which stalls you more when you declutter: figuring out where things go, or the guilt of moving them out? Be honest, no judgment here.

4. Have you ever bought bins to fix a mess, then realized you had just organized the clutter and locked it in? What happened next?

5. What is your honest maybe, the object you can never decide on? Park it in the comments instead of on the counter, and let the pass keep moving.
