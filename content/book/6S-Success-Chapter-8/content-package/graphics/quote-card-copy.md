# Quote Card Copy: Chapter 8

**"Sort: Remove What Does Not Belong"**
*6S Success: Home Edition · Part Three · Sort*

Ten quote cards, each pulled from `chapter_08_manuscript.md`. Every quoted line below was
grep-verified as present in the manuscript. Cards 1 through 10 are **verbatim** (byte-identical
to the manuscript). The two alternates at the end are marked where a line is **lightly trimmed**
(a lead-in dropped or a leading capital added), and are labeled as such. Nothing here is invented.

Each card uses the warm book palette (paper `#F7F2E9`, panel `#FBF7EF`, ink `#2B2622`) and the
Fraunces display face for the quote, Newsreader italic for the attribution. No em dashes, no en
dashes, no floating hyphen separators; labels use the middot.

Suggested attribution for every card: **6S Success: Home Edition · Chapter 8**
