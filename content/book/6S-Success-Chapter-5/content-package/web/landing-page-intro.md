# Landing Page Intro: Chapter 5, The 6S Home Audit

Nobody has ever been excited to hear the word audit. It is a cold word, a clipboard word, the kind that shows up with bad news and reading glasses. So let us hand it back as something friendlier, because what this chapter asks of you is genuinely small and genuinely kind.

You already chose your target space last chapter. Maybe it is the entryway drop zone, where the keys and the mail and the one stray glove all go to argue. The urge right now is to skip straight to fixing it. Resist that for ten more minutes, because one quick step makes every minute of fixing land where it actually helps.

The audit is not a test you can fail. It is a flashlight. You score your space zero to five on each of the six S's, plot the numbers on the 6S Snapshot, and read the lopsided shape to find the one S that is really dragging the space down. A messy space is never equally broken in all six ways, and the lowest score is not an insult. It is a map pointing straight at the cheapest relief in the room.

Spend two honest minutes here before you move a single thing. Read the chapter free in the online book and take your baseline.
