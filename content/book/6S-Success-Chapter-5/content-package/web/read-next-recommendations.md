# Read Next Recommendations: Chapter 5, The 6S Home Audit

Suggested next steps and companion resources, each with a one-line reason.

1. **Chapter 4: How to Choose Your First Target Area**
   The setup for this chapter. If you have not picked a space yet, choose one small, high-friction spot first, then come back and audit it.

2. **Chapter 6: Photograph Before You Fix**
   The next step. You have the number; now take the picture, because the camera sees what the eye has learned to forgive.

3. **Take your baseline (the action)**
   Score your target zero to five on each S, total it out of thirty, circle the lowest score, and write it down with today's date. Two minutes, and you are ready to fix the right thing.

4. **The Home 6S Audit Form**
   The printable companion: the six questions, the zero-to-five scale, space for your total and date, and a blank 6S Snapshot radar to plot.

5. **The 6S Snapshot card**
   A printable and phone wallpaper of the blank radar, so you can score any space on the fly.
