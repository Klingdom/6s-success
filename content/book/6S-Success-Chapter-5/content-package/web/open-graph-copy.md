- **og:title:** A Low 6S Score Is Good News
- **og:description:** Score your space zero to five on each of the six S's and add it up. A space that scores a fourteen has sixteen points of relief sitting in it, waiting. The worst spaces give the best wins.
- **twitter:title:** A Low 6S Score Is Good News
- **twitter:description:** Run the 6S audit on one space, find its weakest S, and write down your baseline. The lowest number is not a verdict. It is relief waiting to be claimed.
