# Publishing Calendar: 14-Day Release Sequence

A two-week rollout for Chapter 5, "The 6S Home Audit". This chapter opens Part Two and gives the reader a way to measure their chosen space before fixing it. The goal is to get readers to score one space and write down a baseline. Publish the home base (the web chapter) first, then drip social and email toward it. Dates are relative (Day 1 = the day the chapter page goes live).

Pacing principle: one anchor asset per day, plus one or two light reshares. The "a low score is good news" reframe and the 6S Snapshot radar are your strongest hooks. Lead with them.

## After Day 14

- Recycle the 6S Snapshot graphic and the strongest quote cards monthly.
- Keep the chapter page updated with an FAQ block for SEO (see `canonical/chapter-seo.md`). The Home 6S Audit Form is a strong lead magnet.
- When Chapter 6 publishes, link this chapter from its "previously" section, and link forward from here. Chapter 5 and Chapter 6 are both Part 2 (Prepare), so the audit and the photo are a natural pair.

## Hero CTA reminder

This chapter's best conversion event is the audit itself. Wherever you can, end with "score your space zero to five on each S and write down the baseline." A reader who does that has a number and is ready to beat it.
