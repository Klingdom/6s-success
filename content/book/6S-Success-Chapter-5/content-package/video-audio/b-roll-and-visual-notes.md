# B-Roll and Visual Notes: The 6S Home Audit

**Chapter 5, 6S Success: Home Edition**
**Slug:** the-6s-home-audit
**Purpose:** A shot list and visual reference for the YouTube, short-form, and any explainer cuts. Built from the chapter's own imagery: the calm-audit opener, the 6S Snapshot radar, the Home 6S Audit scorecard, the "14 / 30" notebook, and the friction meter holding with NOW and GOAL markers.

**Visual style:** Carry the book's design system. Warm paper tones, terracotta and honey accents, slate for data and for the Safety row. Green calm-dots for strong axes, red friction-sparks for weak ones. Warm morning light on all live action. Calm and curious, never stressed.

---

## SHOT LIST (live action)

### 1. The calm-audit opener (hero)
- **Shot:** A person crouched on their heels in front of a real entryway drop zone. A small notebook in one hand, a coffee on the floor beside them. They are just looking, not touching anything.
- **Expression:** Curiosity, not dread. Relaxed shoulders.
- **Light:** Calm morning light, soft and warm.
- **Coverage:** Wide establishing, then a slow push-in to the face, then an over-the-shoulder of what they see (the cluttered drop zone).
- **Use:** Cold open, channel intro, podcast thumbnail.

### 2. The "not yet" beat
- **Shot:** Hands hovering eagerly over the clutter, then pulling back in a gentle "not yet" gesture. Notebook comes up instead.
- **Use:** Pairs with "resist the urge to fix it" and "measure before you move."

### 3. The drop zone itself
- **Shot:** Tight detail passes of the clutter. Keys, a pile of mail, sunglasses, a single stray glove, tangled charging cables. One detail of scissors sitting at grabbing height (Safety).
- **Note:** Scatter a few mixed signals in frame so the space reads as "some of this is fine, some is not." Some items are clearly used daily (calm), some clearly homeless (friction).
- **Use:** Establishing clutter, the "never equally broken in all six ways" line, the Safety question.

### 4. The wrong-fix sequence
- **Shot A:** The person purging items fast into a donation box, satisfied, a little ruthless.
- **Shot B (match cut, weeks later):** The same space drifted right back to chaos.
- **Optional rewind effect:** Reverse the purge, then show them calmly scoring instead and adding one key dish and one hook.
- **Use:** "You fixed the wrong thing" / "less stuff and the same chaos."

### 5. The notebook score
- **Shot:** A calm hand writing the six scores in a row, then writing "14 / 30" and circling the lowest numbers. Space softly out of focus behind.
- **Detail insert:** A green calm-dot drawn in the margin beside the number.
- **Use:** "Write it down," the baseline CTA, short-form #2.

### 6. The stand-up close
- **Shot:** The person stands from the drop zone, notebook closed, a little lighter. They glance back at the space with a plan, not dread.
- **Use:** Outro, "you have done the prepared thing."

## MOTION GRAPHICS (the hero diagnostics)

### A. The 6S Snapshot radar (build axis by axis)
- A clean six-spoke radar, a hexagon. One axis per S, arranged Sort at top, then clockwise Straighten, Shine, Safety (in slate), Standardize, Sustain.
- Center is zero, the rim is five.
- **Animation:** Each axis lights up as it is named, a dot lands at the example score, and the polygon draws itself point by point.
- **Example reading:** Sort 4, Straighten 3, Shine 3, Safety 2, Standardize 1, Sustain 1.
- **Final state:** A lopsided polygon, full on top, caved in at the bottom. A green calm-dot glows on the strong Sort axis. Red friction-sparks flicker on Standardize and Sustain.
- **Center note:** "The dents are where the work goes."
- **Corner note:** "Baseline 14 / 30."

### B. The Home 6S Audit scorecard
- A clean six-row scorecard, one row per S, each with its plain question and a 0 to 5 score for the example drop zone.
  - Sort: "How much of what is here do you actually use?" 4
  - Straighten: "Does everything have an obvious home you can find fast?" 3
  - Shine: "Is it clean, with no hidden dust, leaks, or wear?" 3
  - Safety: "Could anything here hurt someone, especially a child?" 2 (row tinted slate)
  - Standardize: "Is the right way obvious to anyone, or only in your head?" 1
  - Sustain: "Is any habit keeping it up, or does it just drift?" 1
- **Bold total row:** "Baseline 14 / 30."
- **Animation:** Rows fill one at a time as the host reads each question.

### C. The three shapes gallery (read the shape)
- Three small radar silhouettes side by side, each with a one-line read:
  - Caved in on Sort: "Buried. Start by removing."
  - Full on Sort, low on Straighten: "No homes. Assign homes, do not purge."
  - Good early, collapsing on Standardize and Sustain: "Organized before. Keeps drifting. Build a habit."
- **Note:** Highlight the third as "the most common shape."

### D. The friction meter (holds, with NOW and GOAL)
- The recurring friction meter. The needle holds steady, because no objects moved.
- A small slate marker labeled **NOW** sits on the friction side, at the space's current reading.
- A crosshair labeled **GOAL** rests on the calm (green) side, carried over from Chapter 4.
- The gap between NOW and GOAL glows softly.
- **Caption beat:** "Still no objects moved, so the needle holds. NOW marks where your space is today. GOAL marks where it is going. The gap is the work."
