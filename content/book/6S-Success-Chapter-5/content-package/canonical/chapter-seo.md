## Search intent
A mix of practical and method-curious. Some readers want a 6S or 5S audit tool for the home; others are stuck and want a way to diagnose a space before they start. The chapter serves both with a concrete tool (the 6S Snapshot radar and the six-question scorecard) and a strong reframe (a low score is good news, not a grade). The "audit a home" angle is underserved compared with workplace 5S audit content, which is the differentiator.

## Content gaps this page fills (vs typical search results)
- Gives a home-friendly 6S audit (most audit content is industrial and intimidating)
- Provides a visual diagnostic (the radar snapshot) instead of a long checklist
- Reframes a low score as opportunity, which addresses the shame that stops beginners
- Teaches reading the shape, so the reader knows which step to do, not just that the space is bad
