# Chapter 5 Quotes: The 6S Home Audit

Pull quotes lifted directly from the manuscript and final HTML, ready for quote cards, social posts, and graphics. All are verbatim or lightly trimmed. Nothing here is invented.

## Verbatim lines (book voice)

1. "An audit is not a test you can fail. It is a flashlight."

2. "You cannot improve what you have not honestly seen. Two minutes of looking saves an hour of fixing the wrong thing."

3. "A messy space is never equally broken in all six ways. One or two of the S's are usually fine. One or two are the real problem."

4. "Without a baseline, better is just a feeling, and feelings fade."

5. "It turns a vague, guilty 'this space is a mess' into a specific, almost cheerful 'this space is a fourteen, and the low scores are Standardize and Sustain.'"

6. "A number protects you from fixing the wrong thing."

7. "You are not stalling. You are aiming."

8. "The two minutes you spend scoring your space is the measure-twice of home organizing."

9. "A gut number you write down in ten seconds beats a perfect number you never get around to."

10. "Your lowest score is not a failure. It is a map."

11. "The lowest score is not an insult. It is pointing directly at the cheapest relief in the room."

12. "A flattering audit is a lie you tell only yourself, and it points your effort in the wrong direction."

13. "A low baseline is good news."

14. "A space that scores a fourteen has sixteen points of relief sitting in it, available, mostly cheap."

15. "The worst spaces give the best wins."

16. "The audit is per area. The house gets audited one target at a time, the same way it gets fixed."

17. "The eye is generous, and the eye forgives, and the eye quietly edits out the pile you have stopped noticing. The camera does not."

## Definition box (canonical)

"Score your space zero to five on each S, add it up, and write the number down. The lowest score is your biggest opportunity. The total is the baseline you will beat."

## One Idea to Keep (canonical close)

"An audit is not a grade you take. It is a flashlight you aim. The lowest number is not bad news about you. It is good news about how much relief is sitting there, waiting to be claimed."

## The diagnostic (for graphics)

The 6S Snapshot: a six-spoke radar, one axis per S (Sort, Straighten, Shine, Safety, Standardize, Sustain), scored 0 to 5. Example drop zone: 4, 3, 3, 2, 1, 1. Baseline 14 / 30. The dents are where the work goes.
