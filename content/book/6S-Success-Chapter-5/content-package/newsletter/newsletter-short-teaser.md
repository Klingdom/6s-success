# Newsletter Short Teaser: The 6S Home Audit

For a roundup or digest. 90 to 140 words.

---

Nobody likes the word "audit." So Chapter 5 of 6S Success: Home Edition hands it back as something friendlier. An audit at home is a flashlight, not a grade. Before you fix the space you chose, you spend two honest minutes scoring it zero to five on each of the six S's. Connect the numbers and the lopsided shape shows where the space is starving, because a messy space is never equally broken in all six ways. Measure before you move, or you might fix the wrong thing. And a low score is good news: the worst spaces give the best wins. Score one space this week and write down the baseline.
