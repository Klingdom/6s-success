# Reuse Risk Check: Chapter 5 Content Package

When one chapter becomes fifty assets, the risk is overexposure: the same line, hook, or image hitting the same follower too many times until the message feels canned. This check flags where that can happen and how the calendar manages it.

## High-frequency elements (used in many assets)

| Element | Appears in | Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Audit as a flashlight, not a grade | Web landing intro, LinkedIn post, X thread 1, FB longform, YouTube open, podcast open, quote card | A follower on several platforms sees the same reframe repeatedly | Calendar staggers these across Days 1 to 8. Vary the phrasing per platform. |
| The 6S Snapshot radar | Hero infographic, carousel, deck, LinkedIn post, X post | Medium | The radar is the chapter's hero device, so repetition reinforces. Vary the caption and which axis you emphasize. |
| A low score is good news | LinkedIn post, FB post, short video, quote card, newsletter subject | Medium | Strongest hook in the chapter. Use across formats, not stacked on the same day. |
| The six questions | The scorecard graphic, carousel, X posts, discussion questions | Low | Reference content, meant to repeat. Consistency is a feature. |
| Take-your-baseline CTA | Most assets (it is the hero action) | Low to Medium | This is the chapter's main conversion event, so frequent use is intended. Vary the wording. |
| Soft-CTA sign-off wording | Most LinkedIn and X posts | Medium | Rotate phrasings from the CTA bank. Most likely thing to feel templated. |

## Same-day collision check
The calendar was built to avoid two assets sharing the same hook on the same day. Confirmed clear at the planned spacing. If your LinkedIn and Facebook audiences overlap heavily, push FB post 1 (Day 4) to Day 5 so the flashlight reframe does not land twice in one day.

## Platform cross-posting risk
- The LinkedIn carousel outline (`linkedin/`) and the slides carousel copy (`slides/`) cover the same 6S Snapshot content. Post only one to LinkedIn. Use the copy-ready version (`slides/linkedin-carousel-copy.md`) and keep the outline as a planning doc.
- The LinkedIn newsletter version and the email newsletter version share structure. Fine for different lists, but do not send both to the same subscriber the same day.

## Duplicate-content / SEO risk
- The web landing intro, ebook description, and LinkedIn article overlap in phrasing. For SEO, the canonical chapter page should be the only indexed full version. Set syndicated copies (LinkedIn article, Medium, etc.) to point back with a canonical link.

## Cross-chapter risk (specific to Chapter 5)
- Chapter 1 already had a "before photo" first move, and Chapter 6 is "Photograph Before You Fix." Chapter 5 (the audit) sits between them. Keep their angles distinct: Chapter 1 is the gentle first action, Chapter 5 is the numeric baseline, Chapter 6 is the visual baseline. When all three are live, do not run "capture your starting point" content from all three in the same week.
- The six S's appear here as audit lenses and are taught deeply later (Parts 3 to 8). Keep this chapter's framing as "score and diagnose," distinct from the later "how to do each S" chapters.
- The entryway drop zone is this chapter's worked example and is the first target you chose in Chapter 4 (it tied on Chapter 4's scorecard, and the tie was broken in its favor because you see it every day). That continuity is intentional. Keep it consistent if both chapters' assets run together.

## Originality and source-fidelity risk
- The chapter itself is a draft authored in the book voice, not supplied source. All derived assets are faithful to that draft. If the author revises the chapter, refresh the assets so they do not drift from the published version.
- Nothing is borrowed from outside the book. All quotes are from the authored chapter.

## Recommendations
1. Rotate CTA wording across posts (highest-value, lowest-effort fix).
2. Pick one carousel version for LinkedIn, not both.
3. Set the chapter page as the SEO canonical and mark syndicated copies.
4. Keep Chapters 1, 5, and 6 "starting point" angles distinct when they run together.
5. Re-sync all assets if the authored chapter changes after author review.
