# X Thread: The 6S Home Audit

Chapter 5 of *6S Success: Home Edition*. One thread, 10 posts. Each post is under 280 characters and reads on its own. Approx character counts noted per post.

---

**1/**
An audit is not a test you can fail. It is a flashlight.

Before you touch a messy space, you look at it on purpose and put a few honest numbers on it. That is the whole thing. No clipboard. No grade. Just seeing it clearly, on purpose. (1/)

*(approx 240 chars)*

---

**2/**
A messy space is never equally broken in all six ways.

One or two of the six S's are usually fine. One or two are the real problem. If you cannot tell which, you pour energy into the parts that were never the issue. The audit tells you which. (2/)

*(approx 245 chars)*

---

**3/**
Measure before you move.

Without a baseline, better is just a feeling, and feelings fade. You spend a satisfying hour, step back, feel good, and three weeks later have no honest way to tell if the space actually improved or if you just remember it fondly. (3/)

*(approx 261 chars)*

---

**4/**
A baseline protects you from fixing the wrong thing.

A drop zone that feels chaotic might have a fine amount of stuff. The real problem might be that nothing has a home. Purge it and you get less stuff and the same chaos, then wrongly decide 6S does not work. (4/)

*(approx 265 chars)*

---

**5/**
The 6S Snapshot is one question per S, scored 0 to 5.

Sort: how much of what is here do you actually use?
Straighten: does everything have an obvious home you can find fast?
Shine: is it clean, with no hidden leaks, dust, or wear? (5/)

*(approx 234 chars)*

---

**6/**
Three more questions for the 6S audit:

Safety: is there anything here that could hurt someone?
Standardize: is the right way obvious to anyone, or only in your head?
Sustain: is any habit keeping this up, or does it just drift? (6/)

*(approx 232 chars)*

---

**7/**
Now connect the six scores into a shape.

The lopsided shape tells you more in one glance than an hour of staring. A space is never just bad. It is bad in particular ways, and the dents in the shape are exactly where the work goes. (7/)

*(approx 234 chars)*

---

**8/**
Read the shape to find the real problem.

Caved in on Sort? The space is buried. Remove first. Fine on Sort but low on Straighten? It has no homes. Assign them. Good early then collapsing on Standardize and Sustain? It was organized before and keeps drifting. (8/)

*(approx 262 chars)*

---

**9/**
A low baseline is good news.

Read that again, because it runs against every instinct. A space that scores a 14 has 16 points of relief sitting in it, available, mostly cheap. A 28 is already pretty good. The worst spaces give the best wins. (9/)

*(approx 243 chars)*

---

**10/**
That is the whole audit: see your space honestly, score it 0 to 5 on each S, add it up, write the number and the date down.

The lowest score is your biggest opportunity. The total is the baseline you will beat.

Read Chapter 5 free in the online book. (10/)

*(approx 258 chars)*
