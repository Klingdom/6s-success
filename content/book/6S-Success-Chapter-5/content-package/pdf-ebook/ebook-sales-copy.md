# Measure Before You Move

## Sales copy for 6S Success: Home Edition, featuring Chapter 5, The 6S Home Audit

You have done this before. You spend a satisfying hour on a messy space, step back, feel good, and three weeks later it has quietly drifted back to exactly how it was. Or worse, you pour your energy into the wrong fix entirely. You ruthlessly throw things away when the real problem was that nothing had a home, so you end up with less stuff and the same chaos, and you decide organizing just does not work for you.

It works. You just aimed at the wrong thing, because you never measured.

Chapter 5 of 6S Success: Home Edition fixes that with a two-minute audit. Not a clipboard. Not a grade. A flashlight you aim at the space you already chose, so that for the first time you see exactly which part is broken and which parts are fine.

Here is what you get.

- The 6S Snapshot: a simple six-spoke radar that turns six quick scores into one shape, so you can see at a glance where a space is starving.
- Six plain questions, one per S, that you can answer honestly in about ten seconds each.
- A worked example on a real entryway drop zone, scored start to finish, so you can copy the method exactly onto your own space.
- A baseline total out of thirty: a number to beat after the work, and proof that the work held.
- A way to read the shape, so you know whether to remove, to assign homes, or to build a habit, before you lift a finger.

And the part nobody tells you: a low score is good news. A space that scores low has the most relief sitting in it, mostly cheap to claim. The worst spaces give the best wins.

Score one space today. Write down the number. Then read the chapter and find out where to aim.
