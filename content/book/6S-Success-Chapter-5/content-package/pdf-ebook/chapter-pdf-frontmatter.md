## How to read this chapter

This is the opening chapter of Part Two, Prepare. It follows the target you picked in Chapter 4 and hands off to Chapter 6, where you photograph the space before you fix it.

This chapter measures. It does not fix. You will not move a single object here, and that is on purpose. The work begins later in the book. For now your only job is to look.

Read it once straight through to meet the tool. Then, when you are ready, go back to the two infographics, the 6S Snapshot radar and the Home 6S Audit scorecard, and run them on the space you actually chose. Scoring from memory is fine for a first pass. You can always re-score with the space in front of you.

Keep a notebook or a note on your phone nearby. You will write down one number and today's date, and you will want to find it again after the work.
