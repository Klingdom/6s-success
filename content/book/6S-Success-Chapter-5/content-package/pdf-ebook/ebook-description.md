# 6S Success: Home Edition

Most home organizing advice tells you to start. This book teaches you to start in the right place, on the right thing, so the work actually lasts.

6S Success: Home Edition adapts the 6S method (Sort, Straighten, Shine, Safety, Standardize, Sustain) for real homes and the busy people who keep them. It is warm, honest, and practical. No shame, no all-weekend purge.

Chapter 5, The 6S Home Audit, is where the preparation gets concrete. Before you fix the space you chose, you spend two honest minutes seeing it clearly. The chapter takes the dread out of the word "audit" and hands it back as something friendlier: a flashlight, not a grade. You score your space zero to five on each of the six S's using one plain question each, plot the scores on the 6S Snapshot, a simple six-spoke radar, and read the lopsided shape to find the one S that is actually dragging the space down.

A messy space is never equally broken in all six ways. The audit shows you which part is the real problem, so you do not spend an hour fixing what was never wrong. You finish with a baseline total out of thirty, a number to beat after the work.

Here is the part that surprises people: a low score is good news. The worst spaces hold the most relief, and it is mostly cheap to claim.

Start with one space. Measure it before you move it.
