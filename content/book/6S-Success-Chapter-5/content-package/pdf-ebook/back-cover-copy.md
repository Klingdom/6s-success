# Back-Cover Copy: The 6S Home Audit

## Back-cover copy

Nobody has ever been excited to hear the word "audit." So this chapter takes it away and hands it back as something friendlier: a flashlight, not a grade.

Before you fix the space you chose, you spend two honest minutes seeing it clearly. You score it zero to five on each of the six S's, plot the scores on the 6S Snapshot, and read the lopsided shape to find the one S that is actually dragging the space down. A messy space is never equally broken in all six ways, so the audit tells you exactly where an hour of effort will buy the most relief.

You finish with a baseline total out of thirty, a number to beat after the work.

And here is the part that surprises people: a low score is good news. The worst spaces give the best wins. Measure before you move.
