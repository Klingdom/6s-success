# Image Generation Prompts: The 6S Home Audit

**Chapter 5, "The 6S Home Audit"**
**6S Success: Home Edition, Part Two, Prepare**

Five prompts for photographic and illustrated images that match the book's warm, editorial, hand-made feel. Each is specific about subject, composition, lighting, materials, palette, and mood. The book palette is paper `#F7F2E9`, panel `#FBF7EF`, ink `#2B2622`, terracotta `#BC4B2A`, honey `#DDA63A`, slate `#3C5A6B`, green `#6E8B5B`, friction red `#CB4B36`. Keep people relaxed and real, never staged or glossy. Each prompt ends with a short "avoid" note.

---

## Prompt 1: The calm audit (chapter opener, hero photo)

A person in their thirties crouched on their heels in front of a small entryway console table, the household drop zone where keys, mail, sunglasses, and a single stray glove pile up. They hold a small notebook in one hand and have a mug of coffee resting on the floor beside them. They are just looking at the space, not touching anything, head tilted in mild curiosity. Early morning side light from a nearby window, soft and warm, long gentle shadows across a wood floor. Shot at eye level from a few feet back, shallow but not extreme depth of field, the table sharp and the room softly falling off. Warm cream and oatmeal walls, a terracotta-toned rug, small slate-blue accents on a jacket or shoe. Editorial lifestyle photography, the mood unhurried and kind, the feeling of curiosity rather than dread.
**Avoid:** no stress or frowning, no clipboard, no clinical inspector vibe, no neon or cold blue tones, no glossy magazine retouching, no clutter so extreme it reads as a hoarding scene.

## Prompt 2: The hand and the number (companion photo)

A close, warm shot of a hand writing a score in a small open notebook, the numbers "14 / 30" in honest, unpolished handwriting, a column of single digits above it. A simple pencil or fine pen. The drop-zone table and its contents are softly out of focus in the warm background. Natural daylight from the left, gentle and directional, catching the paper grain and the slight texture of the notebook. Composition tight on the hand and page, lower third of frame, the blurred space breathing behind it. Palette of cream paper, ink-dark text, a touch of terracotta from a nearby object, a slate cuff. Quiet, intimate, the small satisfying moment of putting a real number on something.
**Avoid:** no perfect calligraphy, no stock-photo manicured hand, no laptop or spreadsheet, no cold lighting, no oversaturated colors.

## Prompt 3: The 6S Snapshot as a hand-drawn radar (illustration)

A hand-drawn six-spoke radar chart on warm cream paper, drawn in confident ink line with roughly even stroke weight and slightly rounded joins, like a thoughtful notebook sketch rather than a software chart. Six labeled axes around a hexagon: Sort at top, then clockwise Straighten, Shine, Safety, Standardize, Sustain. A lopsided polygon connects the scored points, filled with a light terracotta wash. One axis carries a small green hand-inked circle, two axes carry small red spark marks. Labels lettered in a warm serif. Flat, printed-zine quality, subtle paper texture, no 3D, no gloss. Palette strictly terracotta, slate, honey, green, friction red on cream. The mood is friendly and analog, a diagnostic you would actually draw at the kitchen table.
**Avoid:** no glossy infographic rendering, no glassy gradients or drop shadows, no corporate dashboard look, no rainbow palette, no 8k render language.

## Prompt 4: Aim before you act, the wrong fix (editorial illustration)

A two-panel editorial line illustration of the same entryway table, drawn flat with about a 3px ink stroke and rounded corners on warm cream. Left panel: the table nearly empty after an over-eager purge, but the few remaining items still scattered and a small red spark mark hovering, conveying "less stuff, same chaos." Right panel: the same table with a key dish, a mail tray, and a wall hook added, every object resting in its place, a small green dot of calm. A thin slate divider with a small arrow between the panels. Limited flat color from the book palette, terracotta and honey objects, slate accents. The tone is gently instructive, a touch of wit, hand-made and warm.
**Avoid:** no photorealism, no heavy shading or 3D, no busy backgrounds, no cold grey, no text labels baked into the art beyond the two small dot marks.

## Prompt 5: A flashlight on the space (conceptual cover-style image)

A quiet conceptual image: a small handheld flashlight resting on a warm wood surface, its soft beam falling across a modest entryway drop zone and gently illuminating one corner of it, the rest of the scene in calm low light. The metaphor is seeing clearly, not interrogating. Materials are honest, a brass-and-cream flashlight, a cream wall, a terracotta dish, a slate-toned tray. Warm low-key lighting, a single soft beam, deep but gentle shadows, no harsh spotlight. Composition leaves quiet negative space for a title in the upper area. Editorial still-life mood, contemplative and warm, the feeling that the room is being understood rather than judged.
**Avoid:** no dramatic film-noir interrogation lamp, no harsh white beam, no cold blue, no lens flare spectacle, no cluttered busy frame.
