## Layout notes for the set

- Square 1080 x 1080 for the feed, and a 1080 x 1350 portrait variant for stories and Pinterest.
- Keep one consistent footer on every card: a thin rule, then `6S Success: Home Edition  ·  Chapter 5` in Inter small caps, soft grey `#6A625A`.
- Rotate the motif so the set does not look repetitive: cards 3, 6, and 10 lean on the radar, cards 4 and 5 on the friction meter, cards 1 and 7 on the calm-audit and notebook.
- Honey and green are accent-only. Never tint a whole card honey or green. Reserve slate for the tip cards and the Safety idea, keeping the book's color logic intact.
