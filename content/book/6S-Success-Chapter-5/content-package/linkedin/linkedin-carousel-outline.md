# LinkedIn Carousel: The 6S Home Audit

A 10-slide carousel built around the 6S Snapshot and the six questions. Slide 1 hooks, the last slide carries the soft CTA.

---

## Slide 1 (Hook)
**An audit is not a grade. It is a flashlight.**

Nobody likes the word audit. But at home it is just two honest minutes of looking, taken before you fix anything.

---

## Slide 2
**A messy space is never equally broken in all six ways.**

One or two S's are usually fine. One or two are the real problem. The audit tells you which, so you stop guessing.

---

## Slide 3
**Measure before you move.**

Without a baseline, "better" is a feeling that fades. A number turns "this space is a mess" into "this space is a fourteen, and I know exactly why."

---

## Slide 4
**A baseline keeps you from fixing the wrong thing.**

Purge a space that only needed homes, and you get less stuff and the same chaos. Then you blame the method. The method was fine. The aim was off.

---

## Slide 5
**The 6S Snapshot: six questions, zero to five each.**

Sort. Straighten. Shine. Safety. Standardize. Sustain. Ten seconds per question. Connect the scores into a shape.

---

## Slide 6
**The six questions, in plain language.**

Sort: how much of this do you use? Straighten: does it have a home? Shine: is it clean? Safety: could it hurt someone? Standardize: is the right way obvious? Sustain: is a habit keeping it up?

---

## Slide 7
**The shape tells the story.**

Caved in on Sort means buried, so remove. Low on Straighten means no homes, so assign them. Strong early, then dropping on Standardize and Sustain means it keeps drifting.

---

## Slide 8
**A worked example: the entryway drop zone.**

Sort 4, Straighten 3, Shine 3, Safety 2, Standardize 1, Sustain 1. Baseline 14 out of 30. The dents point straight at the cheapest relief in the room.

---

## Slide 9
**Your lowest score is a map, not an insult.**

A space that scores a fourteen has sixteen points of relief sitting in it, mostly cheap. A low baseline is good news. The worst spaces give the best wins.

---

## Slide 10 (Soft CTA)
**Take two honest minutes. Write down a number you can beat.**

Score the one space you chose, zero to five on each S. Circle the lowest. This is Chapter 5 of 6S Success: Home Edition, free in the online book.
