# LinkedIn Comment Prompts: The 6S Home Audit

Five prompts to start genuine discussion in the comments. Each is one or two sentences.

---

1. Picture your messiest room and run the six S's over it for a second. Which one would score lowest, Sort, Straighten, Shine, Safety, Standardize, or Sustain?

2. Have you ever fixed the wrong thing, like purging a space that really just needed homes? Tell me what you threw away that did not actually solve it.

3. Most of us have a space that we keep re-organizing and it keeps drifting back. Which spot in your home has been "organized" three times and still slides?

4. The idea that a low score is good news is a tough sell. Does it land for you, or does an ugly number still feel like a verdict?

5. If you scored one space right now, from memory, in two honest minutes, which space would you pick and what number do you think it would come back with?
