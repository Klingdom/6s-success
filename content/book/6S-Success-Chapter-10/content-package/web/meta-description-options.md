# Meta Description Options: Chapter 10, Red Tags, Holding Areas, and Sorter's Remorse

Five options, each 140 to 160 characters, each with a primary keyword used naturally. Character counts are in parentheses.

1. For the thing you honestly cannot call, do not force a guess. Red-tag it with what it is and a decide-by date, set it in a holding area, and let time decide. (157)

   Note: 157 characters.

2. Red tag decluttering for the maybes: tag the item, give it a decide-by date, and hold it out of the way. A holding area without a deadline is just a junk pile. (159)

   Note: 159 characters.

3. Stuck on what you cannot decide to keep or toss? Give it a decide-by date in a holding area and let time answer, so no maybe stalls the whole sorting pass. (155)

   Note: 155 characters.

4. Sorter's remorse in two kinds, answered. You almost never regret what you let go; the fear is louder than the loss. Tag the maybes and let time decide. (151)

   Note: 151 characters.

5. You do not have to be sure today. Red-tag the maybes, match a decide-by date to each thing, and let the holding area empty itself on schedule. (142)

   Note: 142 characters.
