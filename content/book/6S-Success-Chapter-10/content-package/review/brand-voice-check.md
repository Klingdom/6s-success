# Brand Voice Check: Chapter 10 Content Package

The voice of *6S Success: Home Edition* is warm, plain-spoken, and quietly confident. Chapter 10 is the third Sort chapter, and it carries the touchiest job of the three. Chapter 8 sorted by belonging and Chapter 9 sorted by use; this one takes the two things those passes could not resolve, the honest maybes and the second thoughts, and hands them a calm structure instead of a verdict forced under pressure. The voice has to make "you are allowed to not know" read as a real tool rather than a dodge, and it does. Not knowing is framed as accurate information about an object, uncertainty is given a system rather than a shove, and the reader is never told they lack nerve.

## Voice fingerprint (from the authored final HTML and manuscript)
- **Warm and personal.** Talks to "you," returns to the same scene the reader already knows (the cleared console holding its five working things, the emptied overflow basket, the out box waiting in its corner). "You are almost done with the entryway, and the last two things on your hands are the two you could not answer." (verbatim)
- **Non-prescriptive and anti-shame.** The permission line, verbatim in the manuscript, is "You are allowed to not know." and the reframe that carries it is "A maybe is not a failure of nerve. It is honest, and honesty gets a system, not a shove." (verbatim). It reframes stalling as an honest state, not a character flaw.
- **Plain over clever.** The hero device is described in three plain lines, verbatim: "what it is, the date you tagged it, and a decide-by date." The bluntest line in the chapter, "A holding area without a deadline is just a nicer junk pile." (verbatim), does the heavy lifting without a flourish.
- **Concrete and specific.** Real objects carry the writing: a good umbrella that was a gift, a folding rain poncho used exactly once, a scrap of paper or a strip of masking tape for the tag, a snow shovel that "cannot prove itself in July." Each of these strings was grep-verified in the manuscript.
- **Gently witty, never mean.** "Then you closed the holding area and went to make coffee, which is the correct next step" and "a deadline you do not honor is not a deadline; it is a wish." (both verbatim) keep the tone light rather than stern.
- **Honestly attributed.** The red tag is not overclaimed as the book's invention. The chapter says, verbatim, "The tool is a tag, and the idea is borrowed from the factory floor. In the workplaces where this method grew up..." That candor about the Lean origin is on-brand.
- **Honest about the gauge.** The friction meter does not move this chapter, and the voice says so plainly: "The meter only moves when the room does." (verbatim) and "a gauge that swings when nothing has physically changed is just flattery, and this book would rather be accurate than flattering." (verbatim)

## Specific voice wins
- **The red tag survives intact everywhere.** The three lines ("what it is, the date you tagged it, and a decide-by date") carry the thread, the quote cards, the article, and the infographics as the same three lines, not a paraphrase. The reframe "A maybe is not a failure of nerve..." rides with it.
- **The anti-shame spine holds.** No asset shames the reader for not being able to decide. "You are allowed to not know." appears as the chapter's reassurance and is reused as a quote card and social copy, always in the same non-prescriptive register.
- **The two-kinds split reads as clarity, not jargon.** Anticipated remorse (the fear before) and real remorse (the rare regret after) are named plainly and each gets its own answer: the holding area for the first, the plain arithmetic for the second. The voice keeps both in kitchen-table language.
- **The friction-meter hold is handled with restraint and honesty.** The chapter has the meter stay put ("The needle holds") because nothing new left the room, and it explains why in-voice: "You built understanding today, not an emptier entryway, and understanding does not move the physical needle." (verbatim). Chapter 9's position is kept as the live needle, and a new faint dashed forward arrow marks where the needle will step when the decide-by dates pass (present in both HTML files, verified). Holding the gauge honest is the brand, and the hold is written as a strength, not a stall.

## Voice risks to watch
- **"maybe," "tag," and "decide-by" run hot.** In the manuscript "maybe" and "maybes" appear 35 times, tag word-forms (tag, tags, tagged, tagging) 66 times, "decide-by" 25 times, "holding area" 22 times, and "out box" 19 times (all counted). This is the chapter's frozen working vocabulary, so the repetition is structural rather than filler, but watch it in the social assets so no single post stacks the same term four or five times.
- **Contractions on X.** The book uses few; X uses several. Deliberate platform choice. Revise only if brand guidelines forbid contractions everywhere.
- **CTA sameness.** The soft-CTA bank in `canonical/chapter-cta.md` holds six phrasings (counted). Rotate them so LinkedIn and X sign-offs do not feel templated.
- **Emoji on Facebook.** One emoji sits at the end of the longform post (counted: one). Confirm it matches your brand's emoji policy.

## Banned-language audit
- **Widened dash scan, run for this review.** I ran a scan for em dash (U+2014), en dash (U+2013), and the spaced-hyphen " - " used as a separator (excluding markdown list bullets at line start) across all 52 package files plus `chapter_10_manuscript.md` and `chapter_10_final.html` (54 files scanned; the publishable HTML is one of the 52 package files). Result: zero em dashes, zero en dashes, zero spaced-hyphen separators. The package uses the middot ("·") for masthead and label separators. For full transparency, the only em dashes anywhere in the chapter folder are three inside the working brief `CH10_CANONICAL_STRINGS_AND_BRIEF.md` (counted), which is not a shipped package file.
- **Hype-word scan.** Zero hits across the package for "unlock," "leverage," "seamless," "elevate," "supercharge," "game-changing," "delve," "dive in," "in conclusion," "fast-paced," "in today's," "effortless," "revolutionize" (scan run, each returned zero files).
- **No force or violence framing.** No "purge," "attack the pile," "war on clutter," or "pull the trigger." The whole move is framed as postponing a decision on purpose and letting time, not willpower, make the call.

## Verdict
Voice is consistent and intact across all channels, and the two things that make this chapter distinct (the gentle red-tag system for the maybes, and the honest friction-meter hold with its dashed forward projection) are honored throughout. Approve the voice. The chapter itself still needs author sign-off, since it was authored in the book voice rather than supplied from source.
