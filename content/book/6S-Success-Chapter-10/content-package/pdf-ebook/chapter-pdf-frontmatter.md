# Red Tags, Holding Areas, and Sorter's Remorse

**Part Three, Sort**
Chapter 10 of *6S Success: Home Edition*

## Chapter descriptor

The third Sort chapter. For the things you honestly cannot call, you do not force a guess; you tag them. A red tag carries what it is, the date you tagged it, and a decide-by date. The tagged maybe waits in a holding area out of the daily flow until time answers, and the second thoughts get a plan of their own.

---

## How to read this chapter

This is the third chapter of Part Three, Sort, and it picks up exactly where the last pass left off, with the two things that pass could not resolve. It is short and specific. It is not about the easy yeses or the easy noes; you made those already. It is about the honest maybes still in your hands and the second thoughts that circle them.

Read it through once for the idea, then set two things ready: a way to make a tag, a scrap of paper or a strip of tape, and one spot out of the daily flow to serve as a holding area. Pick up each thing you honestly cannot call and tag it: what it is, the date you tagged it, and a decide-by date. Match the date to the thing, about a month for an everyday object, a season for a seasonal one. Set it in the holding area and get on with your day. Reach for it before the date and it earned its keep, so untag it. Let the date pass untouched and the maybe became a no, and it joins the out box with the other unnecessary things.

Nothing gets forced and nothing gets thrown while you decide. When a decide-by date comes up, keep the appointment: open the holding area and settle whatever it governs. Where the out box itself goes is a later chapter, and where to store the keepers is later still. Today is only the maybes and the second thoughts.

If you only do one thing: red-tag one honest maybe right now. Write what it is and a decide-by date, set it in one spot out of the way, and let time make the call.
