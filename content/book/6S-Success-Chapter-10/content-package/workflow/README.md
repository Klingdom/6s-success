## The chapter in one screen

- **Hero device · The Red Tag.** For an item you honestly cannot call, you do not force a guess. You tag it. A red tag carries three things: what it is, the date you tagged it, and a decide-by date. The tagged item goes to a holding area, out of the daily flow, and time answers for you. Reach for it before the decide-by date and it earned its keep, so untag it. Let the date pass untouched and the maybe just became a no, so it joins the out box.
- **The holding area · one known spot with a deadline.** A labeled box, a bin, or a shelf out of the daily flow, where red-tagged maybes wait for their decide-by date. Match the deadline to the item's natural cycle: about a month for everyday things, a season for seasonal ones. A holding area without a deadline is just a nicer junk pile.
- **Sorter's remorse · two kinds of second thought.** Anticipated remorse ("what if I need it?") is felt before anything is gone; the holding area is its cure, because while a thing waits under a red tag nothing is actually gone yet. Real remorse, the rare case where you truly miss a released thing, gets the honest reframe: you almost never regret what you let go, you fear you will, and the fear is louder than the loss.
- **The worked pass · the entryway maybes.** A good umbrella that was a gift and a second folding rain poncho used once. Red-tag each with a decide-by date and set both in the holding area. No forced guess; you set up the one judge that cannot be argued with, which is time.
- **A maybe is not a failure of nerve.** It is honest, and honesty gets a system, not a shove. Uncertainty is normal. The system decides kindly.
- **The needle holds.** Nothing new left the room today, so the meter stays where Chapter 9 left it. A faint dashed arrow marks where it steps next, when the decide-by dates pass and the untouched tags convert to the out box. The goal crosshair is still further ahead. The meter only moves when the room does.

## Scope note

Chapter 10 handles the two leftovers from Chapter 9: the genuine maybes and sorter's remorse. It builds the red tag, the holding area, and the decide-by date, and it quiets the second thoughts. It does not re-teach the Use Test or re-litigate necessary vs. unnecessary (that was Chapter 9), it does not route the out box or expired-tag items to a destination (that is Chapter 11), and it does not teach where to store keepers by frequency of use (that is Chapter 13). The holding area is a temporary, time-boxed limbo, not a storage-placement scheme. When a tag expires untouched, the item joins the out box; where the out box goes is Chapter 11. This chapter sets up that handoff without making it.
