# Publishing Calendar: 14-Day Release Sequence

A two-week rollout for Chapter 10, "Red Tags, Holding Areas, and Sorter's Remorse". This is the third chapter of Part Three · Sort. Chapter 9 made the clear, use-based calls; this chapter builds a system for the items you honestly could not call and quiets the second thoughts that follow letting go. The goal is to get readers to red-tag one honest maybe: write what it is and a decide-by date, set it in one spot out of the way, and let time make the call. Publish the home base (the web chapter) first, then drip social and email toward it. Dates are relative (Day 1 = the day the chapter page goes live).

Pacing principle: one anchor asset per day, plus one or two light reshares. The Red Tag and the "a holding area without a deadline is just a nicer junk pile" line are your strongest. Lead with them.

## After Day 14

- Recycle The Red Tag and the strongest quote cards monthly.
- Keep the chapter page updated with an FAQ block for SEO (see `canonical/chapter-seo.md`). The Holding Area card is a strong lead magnet.
- When Chapter 11 publishes, link this chapter from its "previously" section, and link forward from here. This chapter is the third stop in the sorting work, so it hands off the settled out box to its real destinations, which Chapter 11 routes.

## Hero CTA reminder

This chapter's best conversion event is one honest red tag. Wherever you can, end with "pick one thing you honestly cannot call, write what it is and a decide-by date on a slip of tape, stick it on, and set the thing in one spot out of the way. If you reach for it before the date, it stays. If not, the decision already made itself." A reader who tags one maybe has started.
