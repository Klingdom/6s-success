# Chapter 10 LinkedIn Carousel Outline: Red Tags, Holding Areas, and Sorter's Remorse

Twelve slides. Slide 1 hooks, last slide carries the soft CTA. Each slide has a headline and one or two lines of body.

---

## Slide 1: Hook

**Headline:** For the thing you honestly cannot decide.

**Body:** You made the easy calls. One or two things refused to answer. Swipe for the gentlest tool in the whole method, the one that lets you not know.

---

## Slide 2: The honest maybe

**Headline:** Not clutter. Not a keeper.

**Body:** You do not use it, exactly, but you cannot say to let it go. That is an honest maybe. Setting it apart was right, but apart is not a plan.

---

## Slide 3: The trap

**Headline:** You cannot force a maybe.

**Body:** Force a keep and it reopens next month. Force a release and that is how remorse is made: not from letting go, but from letting go before you had an answer.

---

## Slide 4: The reframe

**Headline:** A maybe is not a failure of nerve.

**Body:** It is honest, and honesty gets a system, not a shove. A genuine maybe does not have an answer yet. You are allowed to not know.

---

## Slide 5: The red tag

**Headline:** Three lines, and only three.

**Body:** What it is · the date you tagged it · a decide-by date. A scrap of tape will do. Neatness does not matter. Those three lines do.

---

## Slide 6: The line people skip

**Headline:** Write the date you tagged it.

**Body:** It looks redundant, but weeks later it tells you at a glance whether a thing has waited a long time, or only went in last week. It keeps the area honest.

---

## Slide 7: Two ways it ends

**Headline:** Then time decides.

**Body:** Reach for it before the decide-by date and it earned its keep, so untag it. Let the date pass untouched and the maybe became a no. It joins the out box.

---

## Slide 8: The holding area

**Headline:** One spot, out of the daily flow.

**Body:** A labeled box, a bin, a reserved drawer. What matters is that it is one spot, off the path you walk, and everything in it is wearing a date.

---

## Slide 9: The whole game

**Headline:** A holding area without a deadline is just a nicer junk pile.

**Body:** Undated maybes are the same indecision in a tidier box. The deadline is what eventually forces an answer.

---

## Slide 10: Match the date

**Headline:** About a month for everyday. A season for seasonal.

**Body:** Long enough for real life to test the thing, short enough that the maybe cannot become permanent by default. A deadline you do not honor is only a wish.

---

## Slide 11: Sorter's remorse

**Headline:** The fear is louder than the loss.

**Body:** The fear before is answered by the holding area: a tagged thing is not gone, it is one shelf away. The rare regret after is answered by the odds. You almost never regret what you let go.

---

**Headline:** You do not have to be sure about everything today.
