# Chapter 10 LinkedIn Comment Prompts: Red Tags, Holding Areas, and Sorter's Remorse

Five prompts to start genuine discussion. Drop one in the comments of a post or article to invite replies.

---

1. What is your honest maybe, the thing that sits in your hand and refuses to answer? Name it here, no judgment. Sometimes saying it out loud is the start of the tag.

2. If you red-tagged one maybe today, what decide-by date would you give it: about a month, or a full season? Tell us the thing and the window.

3. Be honest about the fear before. What is one thing you keep only because letting it go feels risky, even though you never actually reach for it?

4. Think back over everything you have ever cleared from a home. What did you genuinely, materially miss afterward? For most of us the honest number is close to zero.

5. Where would your holding area live? A labeled box, a bin, a reserved drawer. Describe the one spot, out of your daily flow, where a tagged maybe could quietly wait out its deadline.
