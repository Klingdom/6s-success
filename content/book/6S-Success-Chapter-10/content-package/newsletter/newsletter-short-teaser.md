# Newsletter Short Teaser: Red Tags, Holding Areas, and Sorter's Remorse

For a roundup or digest. 90 to 140 words.

---

Last issue you sorted by need and filled an out box. What is left in your hands are the honest maybes, the one or two things you genuinely cannot call. This chapter gives them a system. You do not force a guess; you red-tag the thing with what it is, the date you tagged it, and a decide-by date, set it in a holding area out of the daily flow, and let time make the call. A holding area without a deadline is just a nicer junk pile, so the date is the whole point. Then it quiets sorter's remorse, the fear before and the rare regret after, because you almost never regret what you let go. The friction meter holds, with a dashed arrow showing the next step.
