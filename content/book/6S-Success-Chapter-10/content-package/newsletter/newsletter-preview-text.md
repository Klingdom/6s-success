# Newsletter Preview Text (Preheader): Red Tags, Holding Areas, and Sorter's Remorse

   "You do not force a guess. You give it a tag and a date." (55 chars)

2. Pairs with #2 (Red tags, holding areas, and sorter's remorse)

3. Pairs with #3 (A maybe is not a failure of nerve)

6. Pairs with #6 (A holding area without a deadline is just a nicer junk pile)

9. Pairs with #9 (Stop forcing the maybes; tag them instead)

10. Pairs with #10 (The second thoughts, before and after)
