# Facebook Longform Post: The Things You Cannot Call

A longer, story-driven post from Chapter 10 of 6S Success: Home Edition. Conversational, like talking to a friend. Ends with a soft CTA to red-tag one honest maybe. No em dashes.

---

I finished sorting the basket by my front door, and I was proud of it, until I noticed the two things still in my hands. Not clutter, not keepers. A good umbrella that was a gift, better than the one I actually grab, that I never grab. And a folding rain poncho I used exactly once.

I stood there trying to be firm. Just decide, I told myself. Be brave, make the call, move on. And I nearly forced it, because forcing feels braver than pausing.

Here is what stopped me. A genuine maybe does not have an answer yet. That is what makes it a maybe. Push harder and you do not get a decision, you get a guess made only to end the discomfort. Force a keep and it goes back on the shelf undecided in everything but name. Force a release and you send off a thing you were not ready to lose, which is exactly how regret is manufactured.

So I stopped forcing, and I borrowed a trick from the factory floor. When something on the line could not be judged on the spot, nobody agonized over it. They hung a red tag on it, set it aside for a stretch of time, and let the days decide.

A red tag carries three lines: what it is, the date you tagged it, and a decide-by date. So I tagged both. The gift umbrella, decide-by one season, because an umbrella needs weather to prove itself. The poncho, decide-by a month or the next trip. Both went on a shelf out of the way.

That shelf is a holding area, and it only works because of the dates. A holding area without a deadline is just a nicer junk pile. The date is what eventually forces the answer. Reach for a thing before its date and it earned its keep. Let the date pass untouched and the maybe just became a no.

The best part is what it did to the fear. Nothing is actually gone while it waits under a tag. It is one shelf away, retrievable. A maybe is not a failure of nerve. It is honest, and honesty gets a system, not a shove.

So here is your low-pressure homework for the comments, if you want it. Pick one thing you cannot call, write what it is and a decide-by date on a slip of tape, and set it in one spot out of the way. If you reach for it before the date, it stays. If not, the decision already made itself.
