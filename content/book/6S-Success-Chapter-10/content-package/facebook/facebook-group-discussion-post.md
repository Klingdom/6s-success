# Facebook Group Discussion Post (Home and Organizing Groups)

Written for a home-and-organizing Facebook group. Leads with a genuinely useful nugget (the red tag and the holding area), then asks an open question to get people talking. Friendly group tone, soft CTA at the very end, and written so it does NOT read like an ad.

---

Okay, the trick that finally got me past the stuff I "cannot decide," and then I want to hear what is stalling your sort.

For years the maybes killed every pass I ever ran. You know the ones. Not clutter, not keepers. The good umbrella that was a gift that I never actually grab. The poncho I used once. I would stand there trying to force a call, and either shove it back on the shelf undecided or send off something I was not ready to lose and then quietly regret it.

Here is what changed. A genuine maybe does not have an answer yet, so I stopped trying to supply one out of willpower. Instead I borrowed the red tag from the factory floor. You write three lines on a slip of tape: what it is, the date you tagged it, and a decide-by date. Then the thing goes to a holding area, one spot out of the way, and time makes the call. Reach for it before the date and it earned its keep. Let the date pass untouched and the maybe just became a no.

The one warning that saved me: a holding area without a deadline is just a nicer junk pile. The date is the whole point. Without it you have not decided anything, you have only moved the indecision into a tidier box.

And the part that took the fear out. While a thing waits under a tag, nothing is actually gone. It is one shelf away. So the what-if has nothing to grip, and honestly, you almost never regret what you let go. The fear is just louder than the loss.

So here is what I am genuinely curious about:

What is your version of the maybe? The thing you cannot quite call, that you would tag rather than force? And if you gave it a decide-by date today, what date would you write, a month, a season, the next trip?

I will go first in the comments with my gift umbrella. 😄
