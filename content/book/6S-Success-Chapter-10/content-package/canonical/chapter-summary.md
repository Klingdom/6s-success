## Full summary (about 250 words)

Chapter 10 is the third chapter of Part Three and the one that clears the two loose ends Chapter 9 named. The first, the out box in the corner, simply waits; where it goes is Chapter 11, not today. The second, the honest maybes still in your hands, is the whole job. A maybe cannot be forced: pushing a verdict onto an uncertain thing either reopens the question next month or, worse, releases a thing you were not ready to let go, which is precisely how remorse is manufactured. The honest move is to stop trying to feel certain on a schedule and build a system instead.

The hero device is the red tag, borrowed and scaled down from the factory floor. A red tag carries three lines: what it is, the date you tagged it, and a decide-by date. The tagged thing goes to a holding area, one known spot out of the daily flow, and time decides. Reach for it before the date and it earned its keep; let the date pass untouched and the maybe just became a no, joining the out box. The deadline is the whole point, because a holding area without a deadline is just a nicer junk pile. A short worked pass tags the two entryway maybes, the gift umbrella and the folding poncho.

Then the chapter meets sorter's remorse in its two kinds: anticipated fear, answered by the holding area, since a tagged thing is not gone; and rare real regret, answered by the honest odds. The friction meter holds, with a dashed arrow projecting the next step for when the dates pass.
