## Search intent
Decision-paralysis, with an undertone of fear. Readers arriving here have already made the easy keep-and-release calls and are stuck on the honest maybes, the items they genuinely cannot decide, and many are held back by the fear that letting something go will turn into regret. The chapter answers with a concrete system (the red tag: what it is, the date you tagged it, and a decide-by date), a place to run it (the holding area, out of the daily flow), and a clear-eyed treatment of sorter's remorse that splits the fear before from the rare regret after and answers each. The "you cannot force a maybe" and "a holding area without a deadline is just a nicer junk pile" framings capture the exact traps that stall an otherwise good pass.

## Content gaps this page fills (vs typical search results)
- Gives a specific tool (the red tag, three lines) for the undecidable items most declutter advice just tells you to "power through"
- Names the holding area as a time-boxed limbo with a required deadline, not a permanent maybe box
- Insists the decide-by date is the whole point (a holding area without a deadline is just a nicer junk pile), the step most maybe-box advice omits
- Splits sorter's remorse into anticipated fear (cured by the holding area) and rare real regret (answered by the odds), instead of treating regret as one undifferentiated dread
- Reframes the fear honestly: you almost never regret what you let go, and most released things are cheap to replace
- Keeps scope tight: expired maybes join the out box, but where the out box goes is Chapter 11, not here
