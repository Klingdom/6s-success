## The red tag's three lines (frozen)

"what it is, the date you tagged it, and a decide-by date."

## Definition box (canonical)

"Red-tagging is how you decide the things you cannot decide: label an uncertain item with a decide-by date, set it in a holding area out of the daily flow, and let the deadline answer. Reach for it in time and it earned its keep. Let the date pass untouched and the maybe just became a no."

## One Idea to Keep (canonical close)

"You do not have to be sure about everything today. Tag the maybes, give them a decide-by date, and let the one thing you cannot argue with, time, make the call for you."

## 6S Tip (canonical)

"A holding area without a deadline is just a nicer junk pile. The date is the whole point; without it, the maybe waits forever and you have decided nothing."

## Common Mistake (canonical)

"Letting the holding area become permanent. If you never set the decide-by date or never honor it, you have not made a decision, you have only moved the indecision into a tidier box."

## Quick Win (canonical)

"Red-tag one honest maybe right now. On a slip of tape write what it is and a decide-by date, stick it on, and set the thing in one spot out of the way. If you reach for it before the date, it stays. If not, the decision already made itself."

## Family Challenge (canonical)

"Everyone red-tags one \"I am not sure\" thing with the same decide-by date, and you all agree not to argue about anyone else's tags until the date comes. Whatever no one reached for by then goes, no debate."

## The friction-meter caption (for graphics)

"The needle holds. Nothing new left the room today; you gave the maybes a system and quieted the second thoughts, which is real work but not a clearing. The dashed arrow shows where it steps next, when the decide-by dates pass and the untouched tags convert. The meter only moves when the room does."

## The Red Tag (for graphics)

The hero device: for an item you honestly cannot call, you do not force a guess, you tag it. A red tag carries three lines, "what it is, the date you tagged it, and a decide-by date." The tagged thing goes to a holding area, out of the daily flow, and time answers: reach for it before the decide-by date and it earned its keep (untag it); let the date pass untouched and the maybe just became a no (it joins the out box).
