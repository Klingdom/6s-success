2/
Every other space in it is somewhere you keep the weather out of. You spend forty-nine chapters working on the inside of a boundary, and the whole time that boundary is doing quiet work for you.

(197 chars)

3/

4/
Out here none of that is true. The patio is the only room where the weather is not something you keep out. It is the reason you are there.

(141 chars)

---

5/
So the strategy that worked everywhere else, control the environment then arrange things inside it, does not work at all. You cannot make it stop raining on the deck box.

(173 chars)

---

6/
Indoors, the cable you never ruled on is still a cable. It annoyed you, and it is exactly as good the day you finally decide as it was the day you did not. The cost of postponing was irritation.

(197 chars)

7/
Out here, the cushion you meant to bring in is not the same cushion in April. Something has been happening to it every night since September, in the dark.

(157 chars)

---

8/
That is the clock. Sun makes plastic brittle, damp gets into fabric and end grain, and a season of putting things off arrives in spring as a bill.

(149 chars)

---

9/
So one question settles the whole space. Not is this useful, not do I love it, not have I used it this year.

Can it take a night of rain.

(141 chars)

---

10/
A cushion cannot. A cardboard box inside the deck box cannot, and it is remarkable how many deck boxes contain one. Each is fine for a week, and that is precisely the trap.

(176 chars)

---

11/
Where to start is not the cushions. It is the boards and the railing, on a dry morning, before you touch anything soft. If the structure is not sound, everything you arrange on top of it is decoration.

(205 chars)

---

12/
Rake the gaps between the boards until you can see daylight through them. Least visible job on the list, buys the most time. A deck rots from the end grain outward, in a gap you cannot see into.

(198 chars)

---

13/
And this is the room where the oldest idea in the book gets serious. You cannot see a soft board from a chair. You can feel it through a deck brush.

(152 chars)

---

14/
The only room whose standard visibly ages. Weather will undo part of it, and that is not a failure, it is the reason there is a standard.

Fifty chapters. Twenty rooms. Go and push the rail.
