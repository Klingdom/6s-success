# Chapter 50 LinkedIn Carousel Copy: The Patio or Deck

**Slide 1**
One room left.
And it has no roof.

**Slide 2**
Every other room is one
you keep the weather out of.

**Slide 3**
This is the only one where
the weather is why you are there.

**Slide 4**
So you cannot exclude the conditions.
You decide what survives them.

**Slide 5**
And it is the only room
with a clock running.

**Slide 6**
Indoors, postponing costs convenience.
Out here it is billed.

**Slide 7**
One question settles it:
can it take a night of rain?

**Slide 8**
Start with the boards.
Not the cushions.

**Slide 9**
If the structure is not sound,
everything on it is decoration.

**Slide 10**
Rinse first. Grit does not
lift under a cloth. It grinds.

**Slide 11**
You cannot see a soft board
from a chair.

**Slide 12**
The only standard that visibly ages.
That is the last lesson.
