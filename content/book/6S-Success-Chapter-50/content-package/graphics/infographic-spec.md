# Chapter 50 Infographic Spec: The Patio or Deck

## Figure 2: the six zones
- Canvas 1000 x 500, warm paper fill.
- Title in Fraunces, 22px: "The patio, drawn as its six zones".
- **A single large rounded container, 880 x 360, stroked in terracotta**, representing the surface, rail, and safety zone. It carries a terracotta numbered disc, the zone name in a serif face, and a small grey subline: "the whole floor and the rail line, underneath everything else".
- **Five smaller panels, 250 x 78, sit inside that container**, each with a green numbered disc and a serif zone name: 2 Outdoor seating, 3 Outdoor dining, 4 Grill and cooking, 5 Garden and plants, 6 Outdoor storage.
- **This nesting is the point and must not be flattened into a six-cell grid.** Every other chapter's zone plan is a grid of equals; this one is a base plane with five zones on top, and the drawing is what carries the argument for the zone order.
- Footer in terracotta: the boards come first, because everything else is arranged on top of them.

## Figure 3: the method and the kit
- Canvas 1000 x 480, two panels of 460 x 410.
- Left, "The method": four rules with a gloss under each. Lead lines: "The weather is the point" (terracotta), "Postponement is billed" (slate), "A night of rain settles it" (terracotta), "Cleaning is how you read it" (green).
- Right, "The kit": nine lines, and the last reads "Everything else here is furniture". **The kit should read as mostly brushes**, with only two liquids, because the chapter argues that outdoors is agitation and rinsing rather than chemistry.
- Footer in terracotta: outdoors is agitation and rinsing, not chemistry.

## Figure 4: the signature, and the first photograph
- Canvas 1000 x 470. Matched before and after panels across the top with a slate camera disc between them.
- **Below the after panel, a dashed honey connector drops to a wide honey-outlined box** reading "and the first photograph you ever took", with the subline "fifty chapters ago, of a room you could not yet read".
- Captions beneath: "the before, kept" and "the after, and the standard that ages".
- **This is the only figure in the book that reaches back to another chapter's artefact.** The connector must read as a link across time, not as a third photo in the same set.
