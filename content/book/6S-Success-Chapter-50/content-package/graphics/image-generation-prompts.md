# Chapter 50 Image Generation Prompts: The Patio or Deck

House style: warm paper background, editorial illustration, serif display type, muted terracotta, honey, slate, and green accents. No logos, no brand names, no readable label text, no QR codes. No em dashes in any rendered text.

1. **Opener, two states.** A domestic deck drawn twice side by side, both shot from the back door. Left: a folded lounger across the route to the steps, cushions under a towel darker underneath, saucers full of water, a deck box lid half open in rain, green film on the shaded treads. Right: the same deck, boards bare with daylight through the gaps, table clear but for an umbrella and one tray, deck box closed flat.
2. **The six zones.** A warm plan view with the house wall along one edge, zone one drawn as the whole tinted floor plane and rail line, the other five as panels sitting on top of it.
3. **The gap.** A close cutaway of two deck boards with a putty knife raking the gap clear, daylight beginning to show through.
4. **The rail push.** Hands on a top rail at a post, pushing outward and down, drawn from the side so the direction of force is legible.
5. **The deck box.** A lid closing flat over a single layer of cushions, with two labelled totes visible beneath them.
6. **The kit.** A row of mostly brushes on warm paper: long-handled deck brush, stiff scrubbing brush, detail brush, bristle-free grate scraper, coiled hose, gloves, two bottles.
7. **The two photographs.** Two framed images on a warm surface, a fresh one and an older faded one, side by side.

**Do not depict:** anyone working under a raised structure, any repair in progress, a pressure washer, a lit grill being cleaned, a wire grill brush in use, or any child near the deck box hinge.
