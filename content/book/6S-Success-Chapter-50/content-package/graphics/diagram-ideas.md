# Chapter 50 Diagram Ideas: The Patio or Deck

1. **The clock** (hero): two identical objects side by side, one indoors and one outdoors, with a dial behind the outdoor one. Caption: indoors the thing waits for you.
2. **The boundary**: a simple section through a wall, with the indoor side annotated "temperature held, rain off, decisions wait" and the outdoor side blank. Caption: what a wall was quietly doing for you.
3. **The night-of-rain sort**: a row of patio objects each with a yes or no beneath it, the noes routed to a door. Cushion no, cardboard box no, charcoal bag no, citronella bucket yes.
4. **The zone stack**: an exploded view showing the surface, rail, and safety zone as a base plane with the other five zones lifted off it. Caption: everything else is arranged on top of them.
5. **The gap**: a cutaway of two boards with a packed gap holding water against the end grain, beside a raked gap with daylight through it.
6. **The inversion**: two panels, indoors dry-then-wet and outdoors rinse-then-scrub, with a magnified grit particle acting as an abrasive in the wrong version.
7. **The soft board decision tree**: one soft spot leads to replace the board; two adjacent soft spots or softness at the wall leads to stop and call someone. Drawn so the second branch is visibly the wider one.
8. **The two photographs**: the last after photo and the first before photo, connected across a line marked fifty chapters.
