# Chapter 50 CTA and Companion Resources: The Patio or Deck

## Primary call to action
Go outside now and push the top rail hard at every post, outward and then down. Ninety seconds. Any movement, any softness, any proud screw head is a stop and fix today.

## Secondary call to action
Walk the patio with whoever else uses it and ask, of every object on it, whether it could take a night of rain. Anything that gets a no has a home behind a door, or it does not belong out there.

## Companion resources
- **The Patio Reset card.** The six zones in order, with the boards and the rail first, sized for a pocket.
- **The Night-of-Rain card.** The single sorting question and what to do with each answer.
- **The Season Card.** What comes in, what is drained, and what is checked before the first cook and before the first frost.
- **The Patio Kit list.** The short outdoor kit, mostly brushes, with the bristle-free grate scraper flagged as a replacement rather than an addition.

## Closing the book
This is the last chapter. The reader is asked to put the patio's after photograph beside the very first photograph they took in Chapter 6, and to notice that the difference between them is not tidiness but the ability to read a room.
