# Chapter 50 Comment Prompts: The Patio or Deck

1. What is on your patio right now that could not take a night of rain?
2. When did you last push your deck rail hard at every post?
3. Can you see daylight through the gaps between your boards?
4. What is in your deck box that is not a cushion?
5. Is there a board out there you step over without thinking about it?
6. How many part-used garden concentrates are on your shelf, and can you read all the labels?
7. Does your grill brush shed a bristle if you pull at it?
8. If you have read the whole book: put your patio's after photo beside your very first before photo. What changed?
