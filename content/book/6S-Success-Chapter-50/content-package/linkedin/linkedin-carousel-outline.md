# Chapter 50 LinkedIn Carousel Outline: The Patio or Deck

Twelve slides. Product types only.

- Slide 1 (hook): One room left, and it has no roof.
- Slide 2: Every other room is one you keep the weather out of.
- Slide 3: This is the only one where the weather is the reason you are there.
- Slide 4: So you cannot exclude the conditions. Only decide what survives them.
- Slide 5: And it is the only room with a clock running.
- Slide 6: Indoors, postponing costs convenience. Out here it is billed.
- Slide 7: One question settles it: can it take a night of rain?
- Slide 8: Start with the boards, not the cushions.
- Slide 9: If the structure is not sound, everything on it is decoration.
- Slide 10: Rinse first. Grit does not lift under a cloth, it grinds.
- Slide 11: You cannot see a soft board from a chair.
- Slide 12 (close): The only standard that visibly ages. That is the last lesson.
