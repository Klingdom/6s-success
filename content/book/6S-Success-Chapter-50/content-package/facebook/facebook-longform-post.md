# Chapter 50 Facebook Longform Post: The Patio or Deck

---

This is the last chapter of the book, and it is about the only room in your house with no roof.

That sounds like a small difference. It is not.

Every other space you have worked on is somewhere you keep the weather out of. And the whole time, that boundary has been doing quiet work for you. It meant a thing you put down and forgot about was, six months later, still more or less the thing you put down.

Out here, none of that is true.

The patio is the only room where the weather is not something you keep out. It is the reason you are there. Nobody sits outside in spite of the conditions.

Which means the approach that worked in every other room, control the environment and then arrange things inside it, does not work at all. You cannot make it stop raining on the deck box. What you can do is decide, with your eyes open, what is allowed to live in those conditions, and put everything else behind a door.

Here is the part worth sitting with. Indoors, the thing you never got round to deciding about is still fine. The cable in the drawer annoyed you, and it will be exactly as good on the day you finally decide as it was on the day you did not. The cost of postponing was irritation.

Out here, the cushion you meant to bring in is not the same cushion in April. Something has been happening to it every night since September, in the dark, at no charge to your attention and considerable charge to your wallet.

That is the clock. Sun makes plastic brittle, damp gets into fabric and end grain, and a season of putting things off arrives in spring as a bill.

So one question settles the whole space. Not is this useful. Not do I love it. Can it take a night of rain?

And one thing to go and do today, which takes ninety seconds. Go outside and push the top rail hard at every post, outward and then down. Any movement at all, any softness under your thumb, any screw head standing proud is a stop and fix today.

Because if the structure is not sound, everything you arrange on top of it is decoration.

Chapter 50, and the last chapter of the book.
