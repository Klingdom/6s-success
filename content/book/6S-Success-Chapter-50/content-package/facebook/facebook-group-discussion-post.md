# Chapter 50 Facebook Group Discussion Post: The Patio or Deck

**The last chapter. Question for the group: what is on your patio right now that could not take a night of rain?**

That is the whole sorting question for outdoor space, and the useful thing about it is that it is not arguable. Not is this useful, not do I love it. Can it take a night of rain, tonight, and be no worse in the morning?

A cushion cannot. A cardboard box inside the deck box cannot, and it is remarkable how many deck boxes contain one. A bag of charcoal on the ground cannot. Each of them is fine for a week, and that is precisely the trap.

Two more while we are here:

- **Go and push your deck rail hard at every post, outward and then down.** Ninety seconds. Any movement is a stop and fix today.
- **Can you see daylight through the gaps between your boards?** A deck rots from the end grain outward, in a gap you cannot see into, not from the top down.

Tell us what is out there.
