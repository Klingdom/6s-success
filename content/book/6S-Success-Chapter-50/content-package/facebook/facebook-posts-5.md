# Chapter 50 Facebook Posts: The Patio or Deck

Five standalone posts.

## 1
The patio is the only room where the weather is not something you keep out. It is the reason you are there. So you do not win it by excluding conditions, you win it by deciding what can survive them.

## 2
Indoors, a decision you postpone costs you convenience and the thing waits for you. Out here it does not wait. Sun makes plastic brittle, damp gets into end grain, and a season of putting things off arrives in spring as a bill.

## 3
One question settles a patio. Not is this useful, not do I love it. Can it take a night of rain? Anything that gets a no has a home behind a door.

## 4
Start with the boards and the rail, not the cushions. If the structure is not sound, everything you arrange on top of it is decoration.

## 5
You cannot see a soft board from a chair. You can feel it through a deck brush. That is what cleaning is actually for.
