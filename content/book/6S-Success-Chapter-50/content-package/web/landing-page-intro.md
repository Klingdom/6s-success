# Chapter 50 Landing Page Intro: The Patio or Deck

There is one room left, and it has no roof.

Every other space in this book is somewhere you keep the weather out of. The patio is the only one where the weather is the reason you are there, which means you cannot solve it by excluding conditions. You solve it by deciding what can survive them.

It is also the only room with a clock running. Indoors, a decision you postpone costs you convenience and the thing waits for you. Out here it does not wait, and a season of putting things off arrives in spring as a replacement cost.

Chapter 50 breaks the patio into six zones with the exact inputs and numbered steps for each, starting with the boards and the railing on a dry morning, because if the structure is not sound then everything you arrange on top of it is decoration.

It is the last chapter of the book, and the only room whose standard visibly ages.
