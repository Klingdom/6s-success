# Chapter 50 Newsletter Short Teaser: The Patio or Deck

The patio is the last room in the book and the only one with no roof, which makes it the only space you are not defending against the weather but visiting it in, and the only one running a clock on everything you postpone.

Chapter 50 breaks it into six zones with the exact inputs and numbered steps for each, and adds four rules to the nine rules of Shine: the weather is the point rather than the enemy, postponement is billed, anything that cannot take a night of rain belongs behind a door, and out here cleaning is how you read the structure.

Start with the boards and the rail. Everything else is arranged on top of them.
