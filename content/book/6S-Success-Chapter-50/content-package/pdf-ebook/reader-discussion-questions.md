# Chapter 50 Reader Discussion Questions: The Patio or Deck

1. What is on your patio right now that could not take a night of rain?
2. Go outside and push the rail hard at every post. What did you find?
3. Can you see daylight through the gaps between your boards? When were they last raked clear?
4. What is in your deck box that is not a cushion, and where would it actually live?
5. Is there a board out there you step over without thinking about it? What is that worth admitting?
6. How many part-used concentrates are on your shelf, and can you read every label?
7. The chapter says postponement is billed outdoors. What has already been billed at your house without you noticing?
8. Put the patio's after photograph beside the very first before photograph you took, back in Chapter 6. What is actually different between them?
9. The chapter argues that a standard which visibly ages is more honest than one that does not. Do you agree?
10. Fifty chapters on: which room in your house would you start with if you began again tomorrow?
