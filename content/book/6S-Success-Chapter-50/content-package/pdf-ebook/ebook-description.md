# Chapter 50 Ebook Description: The Patio or Deck

The twentieth and final room playbook of Part Nine, and the last chapter of the book.

Every other space in this book is somewhere you keep the weather out of. The patio is the only one where the weather is the reason you are there, and that single inversion drives everything: you cannot solve this room by excluding conditions, only by deciding what can live in them.

It is also the only room with a clock running. Indoors, a postponed decision costs you convenience and the thing waits for you. Out here it does not wait. Sun makes plastic brittle, damp gets into fabric and end grain, and a season of putting things off arrives in spring as a replacement cost.

One rule settles the whole space, taken unchanged from the Micro Zone Manual: if it cannot take a night of rain, it belongs behind a door.

The room breaks into six zones and 46 shine surfaces, the highest surface count of any six-zone room in the book. The surface, rail, and safety zone comes first, on a dry morning, before a single cushion is touched, because if the structure is not sound then everything arranged on top of it is decoration.

And it is where the oldest idea in the book arrives at its most serious use. Cleaning is inspection, and out here the thing you are cleaning is the thing holding you up.
