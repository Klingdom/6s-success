# Chapter 50 Back Cover Copy: The Patio or Deck

*There is one room left, and it has no roof.*

Every other space in this book is somewhere you keep the weather out of. This is the only one where the weather is the reason you are there, so you do not win it by excluding conditions. You win it by deciding what can survive them.

It is also the only room with a clock running. Indoors a postponed decision costs you convenience. Out here it is billed.

Six zones. One question that settles all of them. And the last photograph in the book, which goes beside the first one you ever took.

**6S Success: Home Edition**, Chapter Fifty, and the last chapter.
