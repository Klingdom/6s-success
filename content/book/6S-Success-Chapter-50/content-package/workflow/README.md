## Fences for this chapter
- Product **types** only, never brand names.
- **No structural, carpentry, electrical, or gas-fitting instruction.** Boards, rails, fasteners, hoses and regulators are tested and a verdict is reached; how to repair them is never taught. On bubbles at a propane fitting the verdict is always replace, never tighten.
- **No pressure washing, sealing, staining, or refinishing.** That is maintenance, not 6S, and pressure washing destroys soft wood. The chapter does not go there and neither should any derived asset.
- **Never depict anyone under a raised structure**, and never depict a repair in progress.
- **No horticultural advice.** Pots are containers here, not a growing guide.
- **No medical and no veterinary content.** The pellet hazard to a dog is named; treatment is not. A swallowed grill bristle is called a genuine emergency and nothing further.
- Garden concentrates go to **household hazardous waste collection, never down a drain.** Nothing is ever decanted out of its original container.
- The kit is mostly brushes and should look that way in every asset.
- Friction meter retired for Part 9; close on the before-and-after signature plus the first photograph from Chapter 6.
