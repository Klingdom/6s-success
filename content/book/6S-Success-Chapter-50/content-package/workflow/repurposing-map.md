## Reuse cautions
- Types, not brands. No invented prices or statistics.
- **No structural, carpentry, electrical, or gas-fitting instruction anywhere.** Test, verdict, stop. On a propane fitting the verdict is replace, never tighten.
- **No pressure washing, sealing, staining, or refinishing** in any asset, including b-roll.
- **Never depict anyone under a raised structure or any repair in progress.**
- No horticultural advice, no medical content, no veterinary content.
- Concentrates go to household hazardous waste, never a drain, and are never decanted.
- Keep the kit looking like brushes. Padding it with chemistry contradicts the chapter.
- **The Chapter 6 callback only works for readers who have the book.** In standalone assets, present it as an invitation rather than an instruction.
- This chapter hands off to nothing. Any asset that ends with a next-chapter tease is wrong.
