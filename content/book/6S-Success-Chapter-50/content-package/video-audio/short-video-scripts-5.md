# Chapter 50 Short Video Scripts: The Patio or Deck

Five scripts, 30 to 60 seconds each.

## 1. The room with no roof
**Hook:** This is the only room in your house where the weather is not the enemy.
**Body:** Every other space you keep the weather out of. The patio is the one where the weather is the reason you are there. So you cannot solve it by excluding conditions. You solve it by deciding what can survive them.
**Close:** And everything that cannot take a night of rain belongs behind a door.

## 2. The clock
**Hook:** Indoors, putting something off is free. Outdoors it is billed.
**Body:** The cable in the drawer is just as good the day you finally decide about it. The cushion you meant to bring in is not the same cushion in April. Sun makes plastic brittle, damp gets into end grain.
**Close:** A season of putting things off arrives in spring as a replacement cost.

## 3. Start with the boards
**Hook:** Do not start with the cushions.
**Body:** Do the boards and the railing first, on a dry morning, before you touch anything soft. Push the top rail hard at every post, outward and then down. Any movement is a stop and fix today.
**Close:** If the structure is not sound, everything you arrange on top of it is decoration.

## 4. The gap you cannot see into
**Hook:** A deck does not rot from the top down.
**Body:** It rots from the end grain outward, in the gap between two boards, where packed leaf litter holds water against the board ends. Rake each gap clear with a putty knife until you can see daylight through it.
**Close:** Least visible job on the list. Buys the most time.

## 5. You cannot see a soft board from a chair
**Hook:** Here is what cleaning is actually for.
**Body:** You will never find a popped fastener, a soft board end, or a spider web in a burner tube while sitting down. You find them with a brush in your hand and your face close to the thing.
**Close:** You cannot see a soft board from a chair. You can feel it through a deck brush.
