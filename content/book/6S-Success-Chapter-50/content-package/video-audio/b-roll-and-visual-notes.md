# Chapter 50 B-Roll and Visual Notes: The Patio or Deck

## Must-get shots
- The deck shot from the back door with everything on it. Hold on it.
- The same shot after clearing. The two frames are the chapter.
- A putty knife raking a gap between boards, with daylight appearing through it. This is the shot most viewers will not have seen before.
- Hands on the top rail at a post, pushing outward and down.
- A screwdriver tip going into a board end near the house.
- A deck brush working with the grain, in low sun so the wet line is visible.
- Green algae film on a shaded tread, before and after the stiff brush.
- A cushion standing on its edge with both faces open to the air.
- A deck box lid closing flat, then being latched rather than lowered.
- A grate scraper on a cooling grate, clearly bristle-free.
- The last shot of the series: two photographs side by side on a warm surface, one fresh and one faded.

## Do not shoot
- **Anyone under a raised structure**, at any time, for any reason.
- **Any repair in progress**: no board being replaced, no rail being refixed, no hose or regulator being worked on.
- **A pressure washer.** The chapter does not discuss one and the footage would read as an endorsement.
- A wire grill brush in use, or any brush on a grate that could shed a bristle.
- A lit grill being cleaned, or any flame at all near a cleaning sequence.
- A child near the deck box hinge, even to illustrate the hazard.
- Any readable brand name, logo, or label text, including on chemical containers, which should be shot from behind or out of focus.

## Tone
Unhurried, and warmer than the rest of Part Nine. This is the last film in the series and it should feel like an ending: more natural light, more time on the wide shots, and a final beat on the two photographs with no voiceover over it.
