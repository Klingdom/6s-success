# Chapter 50 Teleprompter Script: The Patio or Deck

*Two minutes. Read at an even pace. This is the last one, so do not rush the ending.*

There is one room left.

And it has no roof.

Every other space in this book is one you keep the weather out of.

The whole time, that boundary was working for you.

It meant a thing you put down was still that thing six months later.

Out here, none of that is true.

The patio is the only room where the weather is not something you keep out.

It is the reason you are there.

So you cannot win it by excluding conditions.

You win it by deciding what can survive them.

And it is the only room with a clock running.

Indoors, postponing a decision costs you patience.

Out here, it is billed.

Sun makes plastic brittle. Damp gets into end grain.

A season of putting things off arrives in spring as a bill.

So one question settles the whole space.

Can it take a night of rain?

Anything that cannot belongs behind a door.

Start with the boards and the rail.

Not the cushions.

Because if the structure is not sound,

everything you arrange on top of it is decoration.

You cannot see a soft board from a chair.

You can feel it through a deck brush.

Fifty chapters. Twenty rooms.

Go and push the rail.
