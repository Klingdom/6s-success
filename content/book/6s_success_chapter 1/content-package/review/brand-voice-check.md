# Brand Voice Check: Chapter 1 Content Package

The voice of *6S Success: Home Edition* is warm, plain-spoken, and quietly confident. Chapter 1 adds a specific job: it removes guilt. It tells the reader the mess is not a flaw in them, it is a system that has not kept up. The voice must stay kind and never scold.

## Voice fingerprint (from the final HTML)
- **Warm and personal.** Talks to "you," uses everyday scenes (a Tuesday morning, a key dish, a junk drawer).
- **Kind, not scolding.** "None of the friction in your house is evidence of a flaw in you."
- **Plain over technical.** Explains systems and friction without jargon.
- **Concrete and specific.** A warranty for an appliance you no longer own, one chopstick, the teenager who would lose his own elbows.
- **Gently witty.** "Has anyone seen my keys since roughly the last presidential election."
- **Honest about scope.** Asks for a five-minute before photo, not a transformed life.

## Specific voice wins
- The guilt-removing thread is preserved everywhere. "Rooms are not messy people. They have friction points" carries into LinkedIn, X, FB, and the discussion questions. That line is the heart of the chapter and it survived repurposing.
- "You need fewer decisions, not more discipline" is used as a memorable refrain.
- "Effortless is a system, not a personality" is the spine claim and appears, intact, as the title-level idea across assets.

## Voice risks to watch
- **Contractions on X.** The book uses few; X uses several. Deliberate platform choice. Revise only if brand guidelines forbid contractions everywhere.
- **CTA sameness.** Rotate the sign-offs so they do not feel templated.
- **Emoji on Facebook.** Used sparingly. Confirm it matches your brand's emoji policy.
- **Do not let any asset tip into scolding.** A few organizing-niche phrasings can sound preachy. Spot-checked: the posts stay on the kind side. Keep it that way in any future edits.

## Banned-language audit
- No em dashes anywhere (verified, zero hits).
- No prohibited hype words as filler. One photography term ("seamless background") was reworded out of an avoid-list.
- No "in today's fast-paced world" or similar.

## Verdict
Voice is consistent and intact across all twelve packages, and the guilt-removing job of Chapter 1 is honored throughout. Approve, with the CTA-rotation and contractions notes above.
