# Facebook Group Discussion Post: Home & Organizing Groups

For a home-and-organizing Facebook group. Leads with a genuinely useful nugget, then asks an open question to spark replies. Friendly group tone. Soft CTA at the very end, kept small so it does not read like an ad.

---

A little reframe that helped me stop feeling guilty about the same pile, over and over.

Almost every home has one surface that collects "I'll deal with it later" stuff. The kitchen counter. A chair nobody actually sits in. The table by the front door. Things land there because they have nowhere else to go, and the pile grows one reasonable decision at a time. Nobody plans it. A spare key, then the mail, then the batteries, then a warranty card for something you got rid of years ago.

Here is the part that took the pressure off for me. That surface is not proof that I am messy. It is just a spot where stuff with no home ends up. Which means it is a findable, fixable thing, not a personality flaw. You do not need more discipline to clear it. You usually just need to give those items an actual home so the right move becomes the easy move.

So I am curious about everyone here:

Where is your "deal with it later" surface? And has anyone found a fix that actually stuck past the first few weeks, or does yours quietly fill back up like mine used to?

Genuinely want to hear the real ones, not the Pinterest-perfect ones. 🙂

(For full honesty: this idea comes from Chapter 1 of a book called *6S Success: Home Edition*, which is free to read online. Not trying to sell anything, the reframe was just too good not to share, and I would love the group's takes on it.)
