# Publishing Calendar: 14-Day Release Sequence

A two-week rollout for Chapter 1, "Why Some Homes Feel Effortless". This is the opening chapter, so the goal is to hook new readers and get them to do the one small first move: an honest before photo. Publish the home base (the web chapter) first, then drip social and email toward it. Dates are relative (Day 1 = the day the chapter page goes live).

Pacing principle: one anchor asset per day, plus one or two light reshares. Lead with the before-photo CTA wherever it fits, because a reader who acts is a reader who continues.
