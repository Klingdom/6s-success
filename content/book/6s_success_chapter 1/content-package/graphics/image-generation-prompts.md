# Image Generation Prompts: Chapter 1

*6S Success: Home Edition, "Why Some Homes Feel Effortless"*

Five prompts built for the book's warm, editorial, hand-made feel. Each is specific about subject, composition, light, materials, palette, and mood. The palette to hold throughout: warm oat paper `#F7F2E9`, soft panel cream `#FBF7EF`, dark walnut ink `#2B2622`, terracotta `#BC4B2A`, honey gold `#DDA63A`, slate blue `#3C5A6B`, sage green `#6E8B5B`, and a single warm spark red `#CB4B36` used sparingly. Each prompt has a short "avoid" note.

---

## Prompt 1: The calm entryway (hero / opener)

A real, lived-in home entryway photographed in soft early-morning side light. A low wooden console table holds a small slate-blue ceramic dish with two sets of keys resting in it. Above the table, a simple wall rail with three iron hooks, one holding a packed terracotta canvas backpack. A shallow woven basket on the floor with two pairs of shoes paired neatly inside. A paper wall calendar at eye height. The walls are warm oat-paper cream, the wood is honey-toned, the metal is matte dark. Light rakes in from a window to the left, catching dust and warming the wood. Editorial interiors photography, shallow depth of field, gentle film grain, calm and unstaged. Palette: oat cream, honey, slate blue, terracotta, walnut. Mood: quiet, ordered, kind, the look of an easy morning.

**Avoid:** showroom perfection, glossy magazine staging, cold blue light, plastic organizers, visible brand logos, wide-angle distortion, anything sterile or empty.

---

## Prompt 2: The junk drawer, honest and close

An overhead flat-lay of an open kitchen drawer, photographed honestly in warm soft daylight. Inside: a tangle of charging cables, a single AA battery, a takeout menu, rubber bands, a spare key, a warranty card, a dead pen. The drawer is real wood with a worn slate-blue handle. The surrounding counter is warm cream stone. Nothing arranged, nothing styled, just the ordinary truth of a drawer that filled up one "for now" at a time. Natural top light, soft shadows, slight film grain, documentary honesty with a warm editorial finish. Palette: oat cream, honey wood, slate blue, muted everyday colors, one small spark of warm red on the menu. Mood: familiar, a little funny, not shameful.

**Avoid:** color-coded perfect drawer organizers, harsh flash, top-down clinical product lighting, oversaturation, fake clutter that looks arranged, pure white background.

---

## Prompt 3: The key dish, the system in one object

A tight, intimate still life of a small handmade slate-blue ceramic dish on a honey-toned wood table, holding a single set of brass keys catching warm light. Behind it, soft and out of focus, the suggestion of a hook rail and a cream wall. Late-afternoon window light from one side, long gentle highlights on the brass, the ceramic showing a real glazed texture and a thumbprint of imperfection. Editorial product-as-story photography, macro warmth, shallow focus, film grain. Palette: slate blue, honey, brass gold, oat cream, walnut shadow. Mood: small, dependable, the quiet hero of a calm home.

**Avoid:** stocky studio product lighting, pure black or pure white studio backdrop, chrome or plastic dish, hyper-glossy reflections, cold tones, anything that looks like an ad.

---

## Prompt 4: Two mornings, one split frame

A hand-drawn editorial illustration split down the middle into two halves of the same entryway on the same morning. Left half: calm and ordered, a backpack on a hook, paired shoes in a basket, keys in a dish, drawn with confident dark ink lines and warm flat fills, three small sage-green dots marking where things belong. Right half: the same room undone, keys on the counter, one shoe astray, a fan of mail on a chair, a few small spark-red marks where life snags. Flat hand-made illustration style, visible pen texture, warm paper background, limited palette, generous negative space, a thin dashed line dividing the two. Palette: oat paper, walnut ink, terracotta, honey, slate blue, sage green for calm, spark red for friction. Mood: gently instructive, warm, a touch witty.

**Avoid:** 3D render, vector clip-art slickness, gradients and drop shadows, photorealism, cluttered composition, neon colors, cartoon mascots.

---

## Prompt 5: The honest before photo

A casual phone-style photo of a person's hand holding a phone, framing a small cluttered entry table to take a "before" picture, in warm natural living-room light. On the phone screen, the same table is visible mid-capture. The real table holds scattered mail, a stray glove, a set of keys, a coffee mug ring. Nothing tidied, nothing staged, an honest snapshot of one small area. Soft window light, slight motion-of-life imperfection, warm film tones, documentary and relatable. Palette: oat cream walls, honey wood, slate blue accents, terracotta glove, walnut shadows. Mood: low pressure, real, the small first step that takes five minutes.

**Avoid:** professional model posing, studio lighting, spotless staged set, heavy retouching, cold tones, oversharpened phone-ad look, any sense of homework or shame.
