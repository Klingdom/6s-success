## Notes for the set

- Keep every card on the warm book palette. No pure white, no pure black. Ink is `#2B2622`, paper is `#F7F2E9`.
- One accent color per card, never two competing. Green for calm and systems, spark red for friction and mistakes, slate for steadiness, honey for warmth and ornament.
- Quote in Fraunces, attribution in Inter, small and uppercase-tracked, in honey or soft gray.
- Leave wide margins. The book's whole feel is unhurried. Let the cards breathe.
