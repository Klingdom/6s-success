# LinkedIn Comment Prompts: Why Some Homes Feel Effortless

Five prompts to spark genuine discussion in the comments. Use them under any of the posts.

---

1. What is the one surface in your home that collects "I'll deal with it later" items? The counter, a chair, the table by the door. No judgment, we all have one.

2. Be honest: what is your version of "has anyone seen my keys"? The thing your household hunts for almost every single day.

3. Tell me about a junk drawer in your home. What is the strangest thing currently living in it that nobody remembers putting there?

4. Have you ever organized a closet beautifully, only to watch it drift back in three weeks? What do you think actually went wrong?

5. If you took an honest before photo of one small area right now, no tidying first, which area would you pick, and what would the photo embarrass you into noticing?
