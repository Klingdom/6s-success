# Visual Storyboard

**Chapter 1 - Why Some Homes Feel Effortless**
*6S Success: Home Edition*

This storyboard ties the teaching deck and the LinkedIn carousel to the book's existing design system. Every key beat reuses an infographic that already lives in the final chapter HTML, so the slides feel like the book rather than a separate brand. Below: the design system, then a beat-by-beat shot list, then how the green calm-dot / red friction-spark motif recurs across the whole sequence.

---

## Design system (carry across every slide)

**Palette**
- Paper background: #F7F2E9 (warm) and panel #FBF7EF for figure frames
- Ink text: #2B2622, soft gray text: #6A625A
- Terracotta #BC4B2A: primary accent, step badges 1 to 3, the "organizing" bar, the backpack motif
- Honey #DDA63A: arrows, loop lines, labels, soft CTA underlines, the "cleaning" bar
- Slate #3C5A6B: the key dish, the steady "systems" line, step badges 4 to 5, the 6S card
- Green calm-dot #6E8B5B: calm, things that have a home, the "6S lasts" bar, step badge 6
- Friction spark #CB4B36: friction, hunting, the chaos in the junk drawer, the "?" marks

**Type**
- Display headlines and quotes: Fraunces (600 to 900 weight)
- Body and captions: Newsreader
- Small labels, eyebrows, and key lists: an Inter-style sans, uppercase, wide letter-spacing (matches the book's .kick and label styles)

**Frame**
- Figures sit in a rounded panel card (#FBF7EF) with a 1px #E2D8C4 rule, the book's `.figframe` look. Keep that frame on illustrated slides so the deck reads as one object with the chapter.

**Voice in captions**
- Warm, plain, kind. No hype. Short sentences. The caption explains the picture, it does not sell.

## How the green calm-dot / red friction-spark motif recurs

The two marks are the visual through-line of the entire sequence. Treat them as a consistent vocabulary, not decoration:

- **Green calm-dot (#6E8B5B):** placed wherever something has a home and an action is effortless. It appears over the key dish, the hooks, the basket, and the calendar (Beat A, B, D), as the row of dots on the "systems" line (Beat C), as the full-width 6S bar (Beat G), and as the Sustain step badge (Beat I). It is the reward state.
- **Red friction-spark (#CB4B36):** placed wherever life snags. It appears over the empty counter and scattered shoes (Beat B, D), creeping into the junk drawer (Beat E), and at full density as the eight numbered markers in the find-the-friction scene (Beat J). It is the diagnosis, never a judgment.
- **The arc of the deck** moves from a balanced split (Beat B, dots vs sparks side by side), through a stretch where sparks dominate (Beats E, F, J, the problem made visible), to a resolution where the meter needle swings back toward green (Beat L). The viewer literally watches calm-dots win.
- **Keep the marks small and consistent in size** across slides so they read as the same symbol every time. A calm-dot is always a soft solid circle. A friction-spark is always a short four-line burst or an X. By the end of the carousel the audience should recognize a spark on sight, which is exactly the "you cannot unsee it" skill the chapter teaches.

**Soft CTA placement:** the only sales line in the whole sequence sits on the final friction-meter slide, in honey, beneath the close. Everything before it earns trust by being useful and kind, in keeping with the chapter's job of removing guilt rather than adding pressure.
