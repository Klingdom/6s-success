## Short 1: Two Tuesdays
**Length:** about 45 seconds
**Hook line:** "Same Tuesday morning. Two families. One leaves at 7:55, one is still looking at 8:09."

**Spoken script:**
Same Tuesday. Two families trying to get out the door. In the first home, the bags are on hooks, the shoes are paired in a basket, the keys are in a little dish by the door. The door closes at 7:55, calm. In the second home, the keys are not on the table, because there is no table for the keys. One shoe shows up. Its partner does not. They leave at 8:09, frayed. Here is the part that matters. The second family is not lazier. They are doing more, on a problem the first family quietly solved. The first family did not get lucky. They built a system.

**[ON SCREEN TEXT] beats:**
- 0:00 "Same Tuesday. Two families."
- 0:08 "OUT AT 7:55"
- 0:20 "STILL LOOKING AT 8:09"
- 0:34 "Not lazier. No system."
- 0:40 "They built a system."

**[VISUAL] direction:**
Split-screen feel, cut back and forth. Left side: calm entryway, keys into the dish, door closing. Right side: hands opening and shutting drawers, one lonely shoe, papers on the dining chairs. Add tiny green dots over the calm home, tiny red sparks over the frantic one.

## Short 2: A system, not a personality
**Length:** about 40 seconds
**Hook line:** "You are not a messy person. You are a person without a system."

**Spoken script:**
We explain other people's homes with personality. She is just naturally organized. And we explain our own less kindly. I am just messy, I have no discipline. It is almost entirely wrong. Putting the keys away is effortless for the tidy family, not because of their character, but because the dish is right there. The system makes the right action the easy action. The other family has to invent a new plan for the keys every single day, while tired. That is not a willpower problem. So if you have been blaming yourself, stop. You do not need more discipline. You usually just need fewer decisions.

**[ON SCREEN TEXT] beats:**
- 0:00 "Not a messy person."
- 0:10 "Effortless = a system, not a personality."
- 0:24 "The system makes the right action the easy action."
- 0:34 "You don't need discipline. You need fewer decisions."

**[VISUAL] direction:**
Host to camera, kitchen background. Cutaway to a key dish by the door, a hand dropping keys without looking. Keep it intimate and slow. No frantic energy.

## Short 3: How a junk drawer is born
**Length:** about 50 seconds
**Hook line:** "Nobody ever decides to build a junk drawer. Here is how you end up with one anyway."

**Spoken script:**
Nobody decides to build a junk drawer. It happens one reasonable choice at a time. A spare key goes in, just for now. Then a takeout menu. Then the batteries, the rubber bands, the warranty card for an appliance you do not even own anymore. Every single addition made sense in the moment. The result makes sense to no one. Here is why it happens. Stuff flows into your home fast, but the systems update slowly. New things arrive every day. New homes for them almost never do. So things pool in the gaps. That drawer is not a character flaw. It is just a system that has not kept up with your life.

**[ON SCREEN TEXT] beats:**
- 0:00 "Nobody builds a junk drawer on purpose."
- 0:08 "Just for now."
- 0:18 "...one for-now at a time."
- 0:30 "Stuff flows in fast. Systems update slowly."
- 0:44 "Not a flaw. A system that fell behind."

**[VISUAL] direction:**
Open on an empty drawer. Drop in one item per line, faster each time, until it is full chaos. Then a real junk drawer slides open. End on a slow pull-back, no judgment.

**CTA (one line):** Why it happens, and what fixes it, is Chapter 1. Free to read online.

## Short 4: Spot the friction
**Length:** about 60 seconds
**Hook line:** "Once you can see friction in your home, you cannot unsee it. Let's find eight."

**Spoken script:**
Friction is the small daily resistance your space adds to your life. The half-second of hunting. The extra trip across the room. The where-does-this-go pause. It hides because it is never dramatic. Look at one ordinary room. Keys on the counter, nowhere near the door, so every exit starts with a hunt. A nest of charging cables with no home. A stack of mail hiding the one envelope that mattered. Shoes in the walkway. The remote on the floor, because it lives nowhere, so it lives everywhere. A coat over the chair back, because there is no hook. A drop-zone table, a home for everything and nothing. And the scissors, which exist in three rooms and none when you need them. Eight friction points, one normal room. Now you will see them everywhere.

**[ON SCREEN TEXT] beats:**
- 0:00 "What is friction?"
- 0:10 "Small daily resistance. It hides."
- 0:18 Count up on screen as each is named: "1 keys ... 2 cables ... 3 mail ... 4 shoes ... 5 remote ... 6 coat ... 7 drop-zone ... 8 scissors"
- 0:52 "8 friction points. 1 normal room."

**[VISUAL] direction:**
One illustrated or real room. A red spark pops on each item as it is named, with the number. Quick, satisfying rhythm. End wide with all eight sparks glowing.

**CTA (one line):** Train your eyes with Chapter 1, free in the online book.

## Short 5: Your first move (the before photo)
**Length:** about 40 seconds
**Hook line:** "Do not reorganize your house this weekend. Do this five-minute thing instead."

**Spoken script:**
Please do not reorganize your whole house this weekend. Big bursts feel great and almost always end in a bigger mess and a sore back. Do the opposite. Start so small it feels silly, because small is what lasts. Your first move is one honest before photo. Pick one tiny area. A drawer. The table by the door. One bathroom counter. Stand in one spot, frame the whole thing, and here is the important part. Do not tidy first. The mess is the point. An honest before is what makes the after mean something. Save it in a folder called 6S Before, with the date. Under five minutes, no cleaning. That is the whole assignment.

**[ON SCREEN TEXT] beats:**
- 0:00 "Don't reorganize your house this weekend."
- 0:08 "Start small. Small is what lasts."
- 0:16 "Take ONE honest before photo."
- 0:26 "Do NOT tidy first. The mess is the point."
- 0:34 "Folder: 6S Before. Under 5 min."

**[VISUAL] direction:**
A phone framing a cluttered drawer. Shutter sound, freeze the shot, stamp "BEFORE" in the corner. Show a hand resisting the urge to straighten an item, then pulling back. Keep it honest and a little funny.

**CTA (one line):** That before photo is your first move. The chapter walks you through it. Read Chapter 1 free online.
