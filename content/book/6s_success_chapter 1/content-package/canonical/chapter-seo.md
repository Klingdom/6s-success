## Search intent
Mostly informational and emotional. People searching these terms are frustrated and often blaming themselves. The chapter answers with relief: it is a system problem, not a character flaw. That reframe is the hook and the differentiator. Pair it with the practical "first move" (a before photo) to satisfy people who also want a next step.
