# Chapter 1 Calls to Action: Why Some Homes Feel Effortless

This chapter is the gentle opener of the book. Its strongest CTA is built right in: take an honest before photo. The CTAs below match the chapter's kind, low-pressure tone.

## Primary CTA
Take your first move. "Pick one small area, a single drawer or the table by the door, and take an honest before photo. Do not tidy first. That is the entire first step, and it takes under five minutes. Read Chapter 1 free in the online book."

## Secondary CTA
Read the chapter (or the next one). "If your mornings feel like a search party, Chapter 1 explains why, and it is not what you think. Read it free in the online book, then Chapter 2 shows where the method came from."

## In-chapter actions a reader can take (from the callouts)
- **Quick Win:** Name one surface that collects "I'll deal with it later" items. You just found your first project.
- **Quick Win:** Your first move takes under five minutes, no cleaning required.
- **Family Challenge:** Read the six steps out loud together once. Bet someone names all six by dinner.

## CTA writing notes
- The honest before photo is the hero CTA. It is concrete, free, and takes five minutes. Lead with it.
- Keep the tone kind. The chapter's whole job is to remove guilt, so the CTAs must not add pressure.
- Avoid hype and big promises. Keep claims the size of the chapter, which is a calm, encouraging one.
