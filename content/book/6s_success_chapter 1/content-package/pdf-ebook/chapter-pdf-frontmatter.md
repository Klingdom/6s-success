# Why Some Homes Feel Effortless

The ones that feel easy to live in are not tidier by nature. They run on something you can build too.

---

## Chapter descriptor

A single line for the table of contents or the chapter opener:

> Two families, one Tuesday morning. The difference between calm and chaos is not character. It is a system, and a system is something you can build.

---

## How to read this chapter

This is the opening chapter, so it asks nothing hard of you. You do not need supplies, a free weekend, or a single garbage bag yet. Read it the way you would read a letter from a friend who has been where you are.

Two things will help:

- Read it once, all the way through, before you change anything. This chapter is about seeing your home clearly, not fixing it. The fixing comes later, room by room.
- Near the end there is one small assignment: take an honest before photo of one small area. It takes under five minutes and no cleaning. If you do only that, you have started.

There is no test, no streak to keep, and nothing to feel behind on. Read at your own pace and be kind to yourself while you do.
