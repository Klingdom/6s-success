## You know the morning.

It is 7:42 on a Tuesday and the house has turned into a search party. The keys are not where they should be, because there is no where they should be. One shoe shows up and its partner does not. The permission slip was signed, everyone agrees it was signed, and it is gone. You leave late, a little frayed, and somewhere in the back of your mind you think the same tired thought: what is wrong with me.

Here is the truth this book opens with. Nothing is wrong with you.

## It is a system, not you.

Down the street, another family left at 7:55 without raising their voice once. They are not tidier by nature. They are not more disciplined, more organized, or luckier. They built a system, quietly, a while ago, and then forgot they ever had a problem. The keys live in a dish. The bags live on hooks. Putting things away costs them no thought and half a second, because the system makes the right action the easy action.

That is the whole idea, and it is good news. If a calm home came from a personality you do not have, you would be stuck for life. But a system is something you can build, borrow, copy, and improve. It does not care whether you are a "naturally organized person."

## What you get in Chapter 1

The opening chapter does not hand you a chore list. It changes how you see your home. You will learn to spot friction, the small daily resistance your space adds to your life. You will finally understand why your past organizing never lasted, and why that was never a flaw in you. You will meet the six steps of the method in plain language: Sort, Straighten, Shine, Safety, Standardize, Sustain. And you will take one small first move that takes under five minutes, with no cleaning required.

You do not need special skills, expensive bins, or to become a minimalist. Later chapters walk you through the house room by room. This first one helps you start, gently, today.
