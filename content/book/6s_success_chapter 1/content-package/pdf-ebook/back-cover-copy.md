# 6S Success: Home Edition

**Back-cover copy**

---

Some homes feel easy to live in. Bags on hooks, shoes in a basket, keys in the same dish they have lived in for years. The family gets out the door without a search party.

If that is not your house, this book begins by taking the blame off your shoulders. The calm home is not tidier by nature, more disciplined, or luckier. It runs on a system, and a system is something you can build.

In this opening chapter you will learn to see the friction in your own home, the small daily resistance that quietly taxes every ordinary task. You will understand why cleaning and organizing never seem to last, and how 6S is the missing part that makes them stick. You will meet the six steps. And you will take one small first move, in under five minutes, with no cleaning required.

You do not need special skills, expensive storage, or to become a minimalist. You only need to start small, and this book shows you how.
