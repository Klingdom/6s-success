# SEO Title Options: Chapter 1

Five title options for the web page. The canonical title is listed first. Character counts are noted so you can stay near the ~60 character target where it matters.

1. **Why Some Homes Feel Effortless** (31 chars)
   Canonical title. The reframe in plain words. Calm, curious, no hype.

2. **Why Some Homes Feel Effortless (It Is the System)** (50 chars)
   Canonical title plus the payoff. Hints at the answer to pull the click without overpromising.

3. **Effortless Homes Run on Systems, Not Willpower** (47 chars)
   Leads with the core idea. Speaks straight to readers who blame their own discipline.

4. **Why You Cannot Stay Organized (and How to Fix It)** (50 chars)
   Question angle that matches a real search. Names the frustration, then promises relief.

5. **Cleaning vs Organizing vs 6S: Why Order Never Lasts** (52 chars)
   Comparison angle for search. Targets people wondering why their tidy-ups keep drifting back.
