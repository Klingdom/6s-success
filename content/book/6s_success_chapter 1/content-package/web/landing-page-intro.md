# Landing Page Intro: Chapter 1

It is 7:42 on a Tuesday, and two families are trying to get out the door. In the first home, the backpacks are on their hooks, the shoes are paired in a basket, and the keys are in the dish they have lived in for three years. The door closes at 7:55 and the house goes quiet. In the second home, the same morning is a search party. The keys are missing, one shoe has no partner, the signed permission slip has vanished into the papers on the dining chairs, and everyone leaves at 8:09, a little frayed.

Here is the part that matters. The second family is not lazier or less loving. Most mornings they are doing more, not less. The first family did not get lucky. They built a system.

That is the whole idea of this chapter. Homes that feel effortless are not run by people with a tidiness gene the rest of us missed. They run on systems, and a good system makes the right action the easy action. Systems can be learned and copied, even if you have never managed to stay organized in your life.

Read Chapter 1 free, and if you want to start right now, your first move is one honest before photo of a single small area. Under five minutes. No tidying first.
