# SEO Title Options: Chapter 2, "What Is 6S?"

Primary keyword: what is 6S. Canonical display title: What Is 6S?

1. **What Is 6S?** (19 chars)
   The canonical title. Exact-match for the primary search query, clean and direct. Use this as the H1 and page title.

2. **What Is 6S? The Six S's, in Plain English** (43 chars)
   Keeps the exact-match question and promises a plain-language answer, which matches the informational intent of most searchers.

3. **What Is 6S? Sort, Straighten, Shine, Safety, Standardize, Sustain** (64 chars)
   Names all six steps for readers who are searching the words themselves. Slightly long, but front-loads the keyword and earns the click by answering the question in the title.

4. **What Is 6S, and Why It Belongs in Your Home** (44 chars)
   Leads with the keyword, then adds the home angle that sets this page apart from the workplace and manufacturing results that dominate the query.

5. **What Is 6S? From the Toyota Factory to Your Kitchen Drawer** (58 chars)
   Story-driven angle that hooks with the origin and the bottle-opener-drawer motif, good for social and for readers drawn to the "why" before the "how."
