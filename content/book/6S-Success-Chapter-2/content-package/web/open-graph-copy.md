## Suggested og:image concept

A top-down shot of a simple, well-organized kitchen drawer in warm light. A small tray near the top holds a bottle opener, corkscrew, and can opener together; sorted utensils run in clear lanes beside them. This is the chapter's "findability" hero image. Keep it real but tidy, warm paper tones, nothing sterile. Optional small overlay text: "What Is 6S?" in the lower third, matching the book's Fraunces display type. Recommended ratio 1.91:1 (1200 by 630) for Open Graph; a 1200 by 1200 square crop works for Twitter and feed sharing.
