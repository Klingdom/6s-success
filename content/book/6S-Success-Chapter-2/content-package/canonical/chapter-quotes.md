# Chapter 2 Quotes: What Is 6S?

Pull quotes lifted directly from the chapter, ready for quote cards, social posts, and graphics. All are verbatim or lightly trimmed from the manuscript and final HTML. Nothing here is invented.

## Verbatim lines (book voice)

1. "A good space answers questions. A cluttered one asks them. You can feel the difference long before you can name it."

2. "Some drawers answer your question. Others just ask more. The difference is a method, and it has a name."

3. "What you felt was findability. The drawer was arranged so the answer came to you, fast, with no effort and no thinking."

4. "You only notice findability when it is missing, the way you only notice a chair when it is not there and you sit down hard on the floor."

5. "They could not win by spending more, because they did not have more. They had to win by wasting less."

6. "Make the right thing easy and the wrong thing obvious is not a car idea. It is a human idea."

7. "Helping ordinary people do good work under pressure, without having to be heroes about it. Which, if you think about it, is a fairly accurate description of a Tuesday morning with three kids and one missing shoe."

8. "The words are just handles. What matters is the idea behind each one, and the order they come in."

9. "Workplaces have inspectors and rules. Your home has you."

10. "We are borrowing the logic of 6S, not the look of a factory."

11. "Same logic underneath. Completely different feeling on top."

12. "A well-run home is not an empty one. It is not a minimalist showroom and it is not a museum where nothing may be touched."

13. "We are not trying to make your house quieter than a hospital. We are trying to make your Tuesday mornings quieter, which is a very different goal."

14. "You do not learn a different system for every room. You learn one system and point it wherever you like."

15. "The drawer you organize tonight is teaching you the exact moves you will use on the garage in spring."

16. "You already understand more about this method than most people ever will, and you have not moved a single object yet."

17. "6S was built so ordinary people could do good work under pressure. That is also a pretty good description of running a home."

## The one-sentence definition (canonical)

"A simple, repeatable method for making the right thing easy to find, easy to use, and safe, in any space, and keeping it that way."

## The six words (for badges and cards)

Sort. Straighten. Shine. Safety. Standardize. Sustain.
Three make space, one keeps people safe, two make it last.
