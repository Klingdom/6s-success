# Chapter 2 Calls to Action: What Is 6S?

The chapter itself is gentle and teaching-first, so the CTAs match that tone. Nothing here pushes hard. Each one gives the reader something to do or somewhere to go without pressure.

## Primary CTA
Read the chapter (or the next one). "If the bottle-opener drawer rang a bell, Chapter 2 explains exactly why, and Chapter 3 walks all six steps start to finish. Read the chapter free in the online book."

## Secondary CTA
Try the thirty-second Safety walk. "Tonight, walk the path you take in the dark, bedroom to bathroom. Move anything you would trip on. That is your first thirty seconds of 6S, and you have not even started organizing yet."

## In-chapter actions a reader can take (from the callouts)
- **Quick Win:** Open any drawer and ask, does this answer me or interrogate me?
- **Quick Win:** Walk the dark path from bed to bathroom and clear the floor.
- **Family Challenge:** Each person names the most and least findable spot in the house. The loser's pick becomes a future project.

## Soft CTAs for social posts (rotate these)
- "The full chapter is free to read in the online book."
- "This is from Chapter 2 of 6S Success: Home Edition. Read it free online."
- "Want the rest of the story? Chapter 2 is up in the online book."
- "If this helped, Chapter 3 walks all six steps in order."
- "Grab the free Six Words card and stick it on your fridge."

## Lead-magnet / resource CTAs (planned companion assets)
- **The Six Words card:** a printable and phone wallpaper with the six S's and their one-line home meanings.
- **6S Origin one-pager:** the short history on a single shareable page, for readers who want to explain it to family.

## CTA writing notes
- Keep it honest. The chapter does not promise transformation, so the CTAs should not either.
- Lead with usefulness, then point to the book. The reader should get value even if they never click.
- Avoid hype verbs and big promises. Keep claims the size of the chapter, which is a calm, useful one.
