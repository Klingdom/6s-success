# Publishing Calendar: 14-Day Release Sequence

A two-week rollout for Chapter 2, "What Is 6S?". The shape: publish the home base first (the web chapter), then drip social and email toward it, then layer in video, audio, and graphics. Dates are relative (Day 1 = the day the chapter page goes live). Adjust to your real calendar and time zone.

Pacing principle: one anchor asset per day, plus one or two light reshares. Do not dump everything at once. Each post should earn its own attention.
