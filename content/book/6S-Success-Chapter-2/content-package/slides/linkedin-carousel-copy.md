# LinkedIn Carousel: Copy-Ready

**Title:** What Is 6S, and Why It Belongs at Home
**Source:** Chapter 2 of *6S Success: Home Edition*. Slug: what-is-6s.
**Format:** 10 slides. This is the on-slide copy, written out and ready to set in the template. (Complements the framing in linkedin-carousel-outline.md; this file is the copy.)
**Voice:** warm, plain, concrete. No hype. Slide 1 hooks, slide 10 is a soft CTA.
**Design notes:** warm paper background, Fraunces for headlines, Newsreader for body, terracotta and honey accents, slate for data, green calm-dots as the recurring signature.

---

## Slide 1 (Hook)

**Headline:** Some drawers answer you. Others just ask more.

**Body:**
You reach in for the bottle opener.
It is right on top, in a little tray.
Two seconds. No digging.

That was not luck.

[VISUAL] Top-down shot of a simply organized kitchen drawer, bar tools grouped in one tray. Green calm-dots on the items that have a home. Warm paper border.

---

## Slide 2

**Headline:** That feeling has a name.

**Body:**
It is called findability.
A space arranged so the answer comes to you, fast, with no thinking.

You only notice it when it is gone.

[VISUAL] The word "findability" large in Fraunces on paper, a single green calm-dot beside it. Quiet and centered.

---

## Slide 3

**Headline:** Findable is not the same as tidy.

**Body:**
A drawer can be neat and still useless.
You can line up junk very neatly and still not find what matters.

Aim for findable, not just tidy.

[VISUAL] Split frame. Left, a neat-but-useless drawer in slate (no dots). Right, the findable version with green calm-dots glowing.

---

## Slide 4

**Headline:** It started in a car factory.

**Body:**
Postwar Toyota could not win by spending more.
They had to win by wasting less.

That pressure produced five practices, later named 5S.

[VISUAL] The four-node timeline, trimmed: Postwar Japan to Toyota system to worldwide to home. Honey arrows, terracotta rings, the home node in slate.

---

## Slide 5

**Headline:** The big idea: make normal visible.

**Body:**
Set a space up so a missing tool leaves an obvious gap.
Then problems show up early, instead of festering.

A labeled basket by the door does the same job at home.

[VISUAL] A pegboard with one empty tool outline next to a row of entry hooks with one bag missing. Slate line art on paper, one honey arrow linking them.

---

## Slide 6

**Headline:** Six words carry the whole thing.

**Body:**
Sort. Straighten. Shine. Safety. Standardize. Sustain.

Three make space.
One keeps people safe.
Two make it last.

[VISUAL] Six tiles in a row, Fraunces caps. Five honey, the Safety tile in slate. A small green "6S" badge above.

---

## Slide 7

**Headline:** Five became six because of Safety.

**Body:**
Workplaces have inspectors and rules.
Your home has you.

The stairs in the dark. The medicine in reach.
At home, Safety may be the most important S.

[VISUAL] The 5S plus Safety equals 6S growth diagram. Honey tiles, a slate shield tile for Safety, the full set of six on the right.

---

## Slide 8

**Headline:** Borrow the logic, not the look.

**Body:**
No yellow floor tape required.
Make the right action easy. Make normal visible. Protect the people.

Your home gets to dress that logic in its own warm clothes.

[VISUAL] The factory-bench vs warm-counter split. Left, pegboard and floor tape. Right, a plant, a fruit bowl, a mug. The same green calm-dots glow under both.

---

## Slide 9

**Headline:** One method. Any size.

**Body:**
The same six steps fix a drawer in fifteen minutes
or a garage over a few weekends.

The scale changes. The steps do not.
Every small project is practice for the big ones.

[VISUAL] Nested scale graphic: drawer, shelf, room, garage growing larger, each stamped with the same small green "6S" loop badge.

---

## Slide 10 (Soft CTA)

**Headline:** You already understand more than most people. You have not moved a single object.

**Body:**
6S in one sentence:
make the right thing easy to find, easy to use, and safe, in any space, and keep it that way.

This is Chapter 2 of *6S Success: Home Edition*.
Read it free in the online book. Chapter 3 walks all six steps, start to finish.
