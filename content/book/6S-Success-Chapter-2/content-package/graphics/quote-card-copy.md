# Quote Card Copy: Chapter 2, "What Is 6S?"

Ten quote cards, all pulled verbatim from `chapter-quotes.md`. None contain em dashes.
Each is short enough to sit comfortably on a card. Attribution line is consistent.
Design notes reference the book palette and the recurring chapter motifs.

**Palette reference:** paper `#F7F2E9`, panel `#FBF7EF`, ink `#2B2622`, terracotta
`#BC4B2A`, honey `#DDA63A`, slate `#3C5A6B`, green calm-dot `#6E8B5B`, friction spark
`#CB4B36`. Display type Fraunces, body Newsreader.

**Standard attribution line (use on every card):**
*6S Success: Home Edition, Chapter 2*

---

### Card 1
> "A good space answers questions. A cluttered one asks them. You can feel the difference long before you can name it."

- **Attribution:** 6S Success: Home Edition, Chapter 2
- **Design note:** Paper background, ink quote in Fraunces. Set "answers" in green
  `#6E8B5B` and "asks" in friction spark `#CB4B36` to stage the contrast. A single green
  calm-dot in the lower corner as the closing mark.

### Card 2
> "Some drawers answer your question. Others just ask more. The difference is a method, and it has a name."

- **Attribution:** 6S Success: Home Edition, Chapter 2
- **Design note:** Pair with the bottle-opener drawer motif as a faint top-down line
  drawing behind the panel. Quote in ink, the phrase "it has a name" in terracotta
  `#BC4B2A`. This is the lead card for the set.

### Card 3
> "What you felt was findability. The drawer was arranged so the answer came to you, fast, with no effort and no thinking."

- **Attribution:** 6S Success: Home Edition, Chapter 2
- **Design note:** "findability" in terracotta `#BC4B2A`, italic Newsreader, everything
  else ink. Three green calm-dots `#6E8B5B` in a row beneath the quote to echo the
  drawer's sorted lanes.

### Card 4
> "You only notice findability when it is missing, the way you only notice a chair when it is not there and you sit down hard on the floor."

- **Attribution:** 6S Success: Home Edition, Chapter 2
- **Design note:** Warm paper, quiet layout, generous margins. Keep it all ink so the
  small joke lands plainly. One honey `#DDA63A` underline stroke under "missing." No
  illustration, let the line breathe.

### Card 5
> "They could not win by spending more, because they did not have more. They had to win by wasting less."

- **Attribution:** 6S Success: Home Edition, Chapter 2
- **Design note:** The origin-story card. Slate `#3C5A6B` panel with paper text, nodding
  to the factory-to-home timeline. Set "wasting less" in honey `#DDA63A`. A small
  four-node timeline tick mark sits along the bottom as a subtle motif.

### Card 6
> "The words are just handles. What matters is the idea behind each one, and the order they come in."

- **Attribution:** 6S Success: Home Edition, Chapter 2
- **Design note:** Reference the bilingual mapping motif. Six tiny terracotta `#BC4B2A`
  dots in a row across the top, standing for the six steps in order. Quote in ink
  Fraunces. "the order they come in" in terracotta.

### Card 7
> "Workplaces have inspectors and rules. Your home has you."

- **Attribution:** 6S Success: Home Edition, Chapter 2
- **Design note:** The Safety card. Short and punchy, so set it large in Fraunces on a
  panel `#FBF7EF`. "you" in slate `#3C5A6B`, the book's Safety color. A small slate
  shield glyph (from the 5S-to-6S growth diagram) in the corner.

### Card 8
> "Same logic underneath. Completely different feeling on top."

- **Attribution:** 6S Success: Home Edition, Chapter 2
- **Design note:** Use the factory-bench-vs-warm-counter split as a thin band behind the
  quote, a dashed `#D9CDB8` divider down the middle. Shared green calm-dots `#6E8B5B`
  run along the baseline under both halves.

### Card 9
> "You do not learn a different system for every room. You learn one system and point it wherever you like."

- **Attribution:** 6S Success: Home Edition, Chapter 2
- **Design note:** The scale card. Four nested target outlines (drawer, shelf, room,
  garage) faintly in honey `#DDA63A` behind the panel, each stamped with a tiny green
  "6S" badge. Quote in ink, "one system" in terracotta `#BC4B2A`.

### Card 10
> "6S was built so ordinary people could do good work under pressure. That is also a pretty good description of running a home."

- **Attribution:** 6S Success: Home Edition, Chapter 2
- **Design note:** The closing, warmest card. Ink `#2B2622` background, paper text, the
  same treatment as the "One Idea to Keep" box in the chapter. "running a home" in honey
  `#DDA63A`. Suited as the last post in a carousel or the sign-off graphic.

---

## Notes for the designer

- Keep one consistent grid across all ten so they read as a set in a feed.
- Cards 2, 5, 7, 8, 9 carry a specific motif and can anchor a carousel; cards 1, 3, 4,
  6, 10 are quieter and work as standalone posts.
- For Instagram, export each at 1080 x 1350 (portrait) and 1080 x 1080 (square).
- Friction spark `#CB4B36` appears on Card 1 only. Everywhere else the mood stays calm.
