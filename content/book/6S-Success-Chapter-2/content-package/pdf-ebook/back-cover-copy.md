# Back-Cover Copy

**6S Success: Home Edition**

Some drawers answer your question. Others just ask more. The difference is a method, and it has a name.

6S is a simple, repeatable way to make the right thing easy to find, easy to use, and safe in any space, and then keep it that way. It came out of a car factory in postwar Japan, where Toyota learned to compete by wasting less rather than spending more. The same idea later moved into hospitals, airlines, and offices, and it works at home for the same reason it worked everywhere else.

This book teaches the one method, then points it at real rooms. You will learn all six steps, Sort, Straighten, Shine, Safety, Standardize, Sustain, in plain English, why Safety matters even more at home than at work, and why organized never has to mean bare or cold. You are borrowing the logic of 6S, not the look of a factory.

Learn the loop once. Use it on everything from a junk drawer to the garage.
