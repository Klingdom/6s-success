# Sales Page Copy

**6S Success: Home Edition**

## You know the drawer.

Eleven takeout menus, a dead battery, a warranty for a blender you no longer own, and exactly one chopstick. You open it looking for the bottle opener, and instead of handing you the answer, the drawer interrogates you. A good space answers your questions. A cluttered one just asks more of them.

Now picture the opposite. A drawer at a friend's house where the bottle opener sits right on top, lined up with the corkscrew and the can opener like three friends waiting for the same bus. Two easy seconds. That is not luck, and it is not a personality trait you were born without. Somebody set that drawer up to behave that way, using a method that has a name.

## One method, not a hundred tips.

That method is 6S. It is a simple, repeatable way to make the right thing easy to find, easy to use, and safe in any space, and then keep it that way. It was worked out on a factory floor in postwar Japan, refined in hospitals and offices around the world, and it works at home for the same reason it worked everywhere else. It is built around how busy, tired people actually behave, not how we wish they would.

The best part is that you do not learn a different system for every room. You learn one loop, Sort, Straighten, Shine, Safety, Standardize, Sustain, and point it wherever you like. The drawer you organize tonight is teaching you the exact moves you will use on the garage in spring.

## What you get in this book.

- A plain-English explanation of all six steps, including why Safety matters more at home than at work.
- The short, genuinely interesting story of where the method came from.
- Reassurance that organized does not mean bare or cold. You are borrowing the logic, not the look of a factory.
- Room-by-room playbooks in the second half, so the method meets your actual kitchen, bathroom, and garage.

This book will not promise to change your life. It will give you one method that works on everything you own, starting with a single drawer.
