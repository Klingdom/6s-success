## How to Read This Chapter

This is a teaching chapter, not a doing chapter. You will not move a single object here, and that is on purpose. The goal is to leave you knowing what the six S words mean, where they came from, and why they work at home. Read it straight through. It is short. When you reach a callout box, treat it as a place to pause, not homework you have to finish before turning the page. The hands-on work begins in Chapter 3, where we walk all six steps in order.
