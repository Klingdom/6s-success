1/
A drawer can be perfectly tidy and still useless. Neat junk is still junk you can't find. The goal isn't tidy, it's findable: a space that answers your question before you finish asking it. That's the whole heart of 6S.
(~219 chars)

2/
You only notice findability when it's missing. Like a chair you never think about until it isn't there and you sit down hard on the floor. A good space just quietly works. 6S is how you build that on purpose, then keep it.
(~222 chars)

3/
6S started on a factory floor, not in a home. Postwar Toyota couldn't out-spend bigger rivals, so they out-wasted them. Less searching, less motion, less time. The method that came out of it works just as well on a kitchen drawer. More in 6S Success: Home Edition.
(~264 chars)

4/
The big idea borrowed from the factory: make normal visible, so abnormal stands out. A missing tool leaves an obvious gap. A box in the aisle looks wrong. At home, it means you catch the mess as it starts, instead of after it has taken over.
(~241 chars)

5/
The six S's, in order: Sort, Straighten, Shine, Safety, Standardize, Sustain. Three make space, one keeps people safe, two make it last. If you forget a name, keep the order. The sequence is the method. The labels are just handles.
(~231 chars)

6/
Pick up three books on this and you'll find three slightly different sets of S words. Set in Order, or Straighten, or Systematize. Shine, or Sweep. Don't let it rattle you. They're translations of Japanese words. The idea and the order are what matter.
(~252 chars)

7/
Workplaces have inspectors and rules. Your home has you. The stairs in the dark, the knives in the drawer, the medicine within a toddler's reach. Nobody audits those for you. That's why Safety is one of the six S's, and at home it may be the most important one.
(~261 chars)

8/
A 30-second Safety win: walk the path you take in the dark, bedroom to bathroom. Anything on the floor you'd trip on? Move it. That's the Safety S, done. No big project required.
(~178 chars)

9/
6S at home isn't yellow floor tape and a label maker gone rogue. You borrow the logic, not the look. Make the right action easy, make problems show up early, protect the people. Your home gets to dress that logic in its own warm clothes.
(~237 chars)

10/
You don't need a different system for every room. One method points anywhere: a drawer in fifteen minutes, a garage over a few weekends. Same six steps every time. The drawer you fix tonight is practice for the garage in spring. Chapter 2 of 6S Success: Home Edition.
(~267 chars)
