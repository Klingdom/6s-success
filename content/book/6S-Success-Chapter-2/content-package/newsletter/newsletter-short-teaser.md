# Short Teaser: What Is 6S?

You reach into a friend's kitchen drawer for the bottle opener, and it is right on top, sitting with the corkscrew and the can opener. Two easy seconds, no digging. That is not luck. It is findability, built on purpose, and it has a name: 6S.

Chapter 2 explains where it came from (a postwar car factory in Japan) and names the whole method in six plain words: Sort, Straighten, Shine, Safety, Standardize, Sustain. Three make space, one keeps people safe, two make it last. A method built for factories, and a surprisingly good fit for a busy home.
