# Brand Voice Check: Chapter 2 Content Package

The voice of *6S Success: Home Edition* is warm, plain-spoken, and quietly confident. It teaches without lecturing, uses small domestic images, and respects the reader's intelligence. This check confirms the package holds that voice.

## Voice fingerprint (from the manuscript)
- **Warm and personal.** Talks to "you," uses everyday scenes (a friend's kitchen, a Tuesday morning, a junk drawer).
- **Plain over technical.** Explains 5S and 6S without jargon. "The words are just handles."
- **Reassuring, not bossy.** "Please relax. That is not where we are going."
- **Concrete and specific.** Eleven menus, one chopstick, a basket by the door, a hook for each kid's bag.
- **Gently witty.** "Like friends waiting for the same bus." "Possibly the last cookie."
- **Honest about scope.** This chapter teaches; it does not promise a transformed life.

## Specific voice wins
- The "Tuesday morning with three kids and one missing shoe" line is preserved across several assets. It is the most on-brand sentence in the chapter and it survived repurposing intact.
- "Borrow the logic, not the look" is used as a consistent, memorable refrain.
- The Safety section keeps "Workplaces have inspectors and rules. Your home has you," which is the chapter's sharpest, most human line. It carries into LinkedIn, X, and the discussion questions.

## Voice risks to watch
- **Contractions on X.** The book uses few contractions; X uses several. This is a deliberate platform choice, not a slip. If brand guidelines forbid contractions everywhere, revise the X copy. Otherwise leave it.
- **CTA sameness.** Repeated identical sign-offs can start to feel like a brand template rather than a person. Rotate them (see editorial review).
- **Emoji on Facebook.** Used sparingly (0 to 2 per post) and only where natural. Confirm this matches the brand's emoji policy before publishing.

## Banned-language audit
- No em dashes anywhere (verified by character scan, zero hits).
- None of the prohibited hype words appear as filler in the published copy.
- No "in today's fast-paced world" or similar throat-clearing openers.

## Verdict
Voice is consistent and intact across all twelve packages. The author would recognize this as theirs. Approve, with the minor CTA-rotation and contractions note above.
