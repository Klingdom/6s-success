# Teleprompter Script: What Is 6S?

**Source:** Chapter 2, "What Is 6S?" from *6S Success: Home Edition*
**Length:** about 650 words, roughly 4 to 5 minutes read aloud
**Note:** Narration only. Short lines for easy reading. Read slowly and warmly. Let the pauses breathe.

---

You are at a friend's place for dinner.

You offer to help.

She says, could you grab the bottle opener?

And tips her head toward a drawer.

You open it.

And there it is.

Right on top.

Sitting with the corkscrew and the can opener.

You did not have to ask which drawer.

You did not have to dig past the takeout menus.

The drawer just handed you the thing you came for.

Two seconds.

Maybe three.

That tiny moment is what 6S is about.

The feeling of a space answering your question

before you even finish asking it.

///

Now, here is the part that surprises people.

That feeling was not invented for kitchens.

It was worked out on a factory floor.

After the Second World War,

a car company in Japan called Toyota

was trying to compete with much bigger rivals.

They could not win by spending more.

So they won by wasting less.

Less searching.

Less motion.

Less wasted time.

Out of that came an idea called the visual workplace.

Set a space up well,

and anyone can glance at it

and instantly see if something is off.

The tool is outlined on the board,

so a missing tool leaves an obvious gap.

Normal becomes visible.

Which means problems get caught early.

///

They built it on five practices.

Each one starts, in Japanese, with the letter S.

Later it got a name.

5S.

And it spread.

Into hospitals.

Into airlines.

Into offices.

And eventually, into homes.

Because the real idea was never about cars.

It was about helping ordinary people

do good work under pressure.

Which is a pretty good description

of a Tuesday morning

with three kids and one missing shoe.

///

So let me give you the six words.

Sort.

Separate what you need from what you do not.

And remove what you do not.

Straighten.

Give everything that stays a clear home.

Daily things in the easy spots.

Shine.

Clean it in a way that lets you notice problems.

The leak. The loose hinge.

While your hands are already there.

Safety.

Spot the wobbly shelf.

The overloaded outlet.

The chemicals stored above the cereal.

Standardize.

Make the good setup the normal setup.

So it survives a busy week.

And Sustain.

The small habits that keep it from sliding back.

Six words.

Sort, Straighten, Shine, Safety, Standardize, Sustain.

Three make space.

One keeps people safe.

Two make it last.

///

Now, a quick reassurance.

This does not mean yellow tape on your floor.

It does not mean a sterile, empty house.

We borrow the logic of 6S.

Not the look of a factory.

A well-run home is not an empty one.

The fruit bowl on the counter.

The kids' art on the fridge.

The worn spot on the couch.

Those are not failures.

That is the life the method exists to protect.

Same logic underneath.

Completely different feeling on top.

///

And here is the best part.

You only learn one system.

The same six steps that fix a junk drawer

will fix a closet, a bedroom, the whole garage.

The scale changes.

The steps do not.

So every small project you do tonight

is practice for the big ones later.

///

So, what is 6S?

A simple, repeatable method

for making the right thing easy to find,

easy to use,

and safe,

in any space,

and then keeping it that way.

And here is the wild part.

You already understand it.

And you have not moved a single object yet.

If you want one tiny thing to do tonight,

walk the path you take in the dark,

from your bed to the bathroom.

Anything on the floor you would trip on?

Move it.

That is your first thirty seconds of 6S.
