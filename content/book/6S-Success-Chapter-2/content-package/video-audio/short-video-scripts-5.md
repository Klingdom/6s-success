## Short 1: The Two Drawers (the bottle-opener test)
**Length:** ~45 seconds
**Topic:** Findability vs tidiness

**HOOK LINE (spoken + on screen, first 2 seconds):**
"Your junk drawer is not messy. It is asking you questions."

**SPOKEN SCRIPT:**
You ask a friend for the bottle opener. She nods at a drawer. You open it, and it is right on top, sitting with the corkscrew and the can opener. Two easy seconds. Now picture your drawer. Eleven takeout menus, a dead battery, one lonely chopstick. Here is the thing. The good drawer is not tidier. It is more findable. A space can be neat and still useless. Findability is when the answer comes to you before you finish asking. You only notice it when it is gone.

**[ON SCREEN TEXT] beats:**
- 0:00 "The bottle-opener test"
- 0:10 "Drawer A: 2 seconds"
- 0:20 "Drawer B: still digging..."
- 0:30 "Tidy =/= findable"
- 0:38 "Findability is the point"

**[VISUAL] direction:**
Top-down on a clean drawer with the tray of openers. Hard cut to a chaotic junk drawer, hand digging. Split screen for the comparison. Small green dots appear over the items in the good drawer.

## Short 2: It Came From a Car Factory
**Length:** ~60 seconds
**Topic:** The origin story

**HOOK LINE:**
"The reason your kitchen can feel calm was figured out in a car factory."

**SPOKEN SCRIPT:**
After World War Two, Japan was rebuilding, and Toyota was trying to compete with much bigger, richer car companies. They could not win by spending more, because they did not have more. So they won by wasting less. Less searching, less motion, less wasted time. Out of that came an idea called the visual workplace. Set a space up well, and anyone can glance at it and instantly see if something is off. The tool is outlined on the board, so a missing tool leaves a gap. They built it on five practices, each starting with S in Japanese. Later it got the name 5S. And it spread. Hospitals, airlines, offices, and eventually homes. Because making the right thing easy and the wrong thing obvious was never really about cars. It is a human idea.

**[ON SCREEN TEXT] beats:**
- 0:00 "Postwar Japan, 1950s"
- 0:08 "Win by wasting less"
- 0:20 "The visual workplace"
- 0:30 "A missing tool leaves a gap"
- 0:42 "5 practices, each starts with S = 5S"
- 0:52 "Factory to hospital to home"

**[VISUAL] direction:**
Warm archival-style factory footage. Animated shadow board with one tool missing, gap highlighted in red. Simple four-node timeline sliding from factory to front door.

## Short 3: What Do the Six S's Actually Mean?
**Length:** ~75 seconds
**Topic:** The six words in plain English

**HOOK LINE:**
"6S has six S words. Most explanations make them sound harder than they are."

**SPOKEN SCRIPT:**
Here they are in plain English. Sort. Separate what you need from what you do not, and remove what you do not. Straighten. Give what stays a clear home, with daily things in the easy spots. Shine. Clean it, and clean it in a way that lets you notice problems, the leak, the loose hinge, while your hands are already there. Safety. Spot the wobbly shelf, the overloaded outlet, the chemicals stored above the cereal. Standardize. Make the good setup the normal setup, out in the open, so it survives a busy week. Sustain. The small habits that stop it all from sliding back. Six words. Three make space, one keeps people safe, two make it last. And if you blank on a name, do not worry. The words are just handles. The order is the method.

**[ON SCREEN TEXT] beats:**
- 0:00 "The 6 S's, plain English"
- Each step as it is spoken: "1 Sort", "2 Straighten", "3 Shine", "4 Safety", "5 Standardize", "6 Sustain"
- 1:00 "3 make space. 1 keeps you safe. 2 make it last."
- 1:08 "Forget a word? Keep the order."

**[VISUAL] direction:**
Fast, satisfying cuts. One real action per S: pulling out unused items, placing things in clear spots, wiping a shelf, tightening a loose bracket, a labeled basket, a quick evening tidy. Numbered tile animates in for each S.

## Short 4: The Thirty-Second Safety Walk
**Length:** ~40 seconds
**Topic:** The Safety S, do it tonight

**HOOK LINE:**
"At work, an inspector checks for hazards. At home, that is just you."

**SPOKEN SCRIPT:**
The sixth S is Safety, and at home it might be the most important one. Workplaces have inspectors and rules. Your home has you. The stairs in the dark. The knives in the drawer. The medicine within a toddler's reach. Nobody is going to audit those for you. So here is a thirty-second version you can do tonight. Walk the path you take in the dark, bedroom to bathroom. Is there anything on the floor you would trip on? Move it. That is it. You just did your first thirty seconds of 6S, and you have not even started organizing yet.

**[ON SCREEN TEXT] beats:**
- 0:00 "The Safety S"
- 0:08 "Workplaces have inspectors. Your home has you."
- 0:18 "Tonight: walk it in the dark"
- 0:26 "Trip hazard? Move it."
- 0:34 "Your first 30 seconds of 6S"

**[VISUAL] direction:**
A dim hallway, a person walking it at night, low light. POV down at the floor. A bag or toy on the floor gets a red glow, then a hand moves it and the glow turns green. Calm.

**CTA (one line):**
Do the walk tonight. Then read why it matters in Chapter 2, free online.

## Short 5: Organized Does Not Mean Cold
**Length:** ~50 seconds
**Topic:** Reassurance, borrow the logic not the look

**HOOK LINE:**
"You do not have to turn your home into a sterile little warehouse. Please do not."

**SPOKEN SCRIPT:**
When people hear "organizing method from a factory," they picture yellow floor tape, every spatula traced on a pegboard, a label maker gone rogue. Relax. That is not the goal. We borrow the logic of 6S, not the look of a factory. The logic is gentle. Make the right action easy. Make problems easy to spot. Protect the people who live there. A well-run home is not an empty one. The fruit bowl on the counter, the kids' art on the fridge, the worn spot on the couch where everyone sits. Those are not failures. That is the life the method exists to protect. Same logic underneath, completely different feeling on top. We are not making your house quieter than a hospital. Just your Tuesday mornings.

**[ON SCREEN TEXT] beats:**
- 0:00 "Organized =/= cold"
- 0:10 "Borrow the logic, not the look"
- 0:24 "A home is not a showroom"
- 0:36 "Same logic. Different feeling."
- 0:44 "Calmer Tuesdays, not a hospital"

**[VISUAL] direction:**
Quick comic flash of a sterile, taped-up "warehouse kitchen," then warm reveal of a real lived-in kitchen with a plant, a fruit bowl, kids' drawings. Green calm-dots glow softly under the warm version.
