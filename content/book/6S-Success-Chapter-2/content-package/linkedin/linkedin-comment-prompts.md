# Comment Prompts: What Is 6S?

Five open questions to start real discussion under a post. Each ties to a Chapter 2 idea.

1. What is the single most findable spot in your home right now, the one that hands you what you need in two seconds, and what is the least findable spot you keep losing battles with?

2. Think of a drawer or shelf that answers you instantly. Did you set it up on purpose, or did it just happen that way over time?

3. If you walked the path you take in the dark from your bed to the bathroom, what is the one thing on the floor you would trip on tonight?

4. Where in your home does Safety matter more than tidiness, the spot you would secure first if you only had ten minutes?

5. Which feels truer for you: organized makes a home feel calm, or organized makes a home feel cold, and what changed your mind?
