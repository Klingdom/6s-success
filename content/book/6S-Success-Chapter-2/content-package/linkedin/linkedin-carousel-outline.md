# Carousel Outline: What Is 6S, and Why It Belongs at Home

10 slides. Drawn from Chapter 2 of 6S Success: Home Edition. Slide 1 hooks, slide 10 is a soft CTA.

---

## Slide 1 (Hook)
**Headline:** Some drawers answer your question. Others just ask more.
**Body:** You ask for the bottle opener. It is right there on top, in a little tray. Two seconds, no digging. That was not luck.

## Slide 2
**Headline:** That feeling has a name.
**Body:** It is called findability. A space arranged so the answer comes to you, fast, with no thinking. You only notice it when it is missing.

## Slide 3
**Headline:** Findable is not the same as tidy.
**Body:** A drawer can be neat and still useless. You can line up junk very neatly and still not find what matters. Aim for findable.

## Slide 4
**Headline:** It started in a car factory.
**Body:** Postwar Toyota could not win by spending more. They had to win by wasting less. That pressure produced a set of five practices, later named 5S.

## Slide 5
**Headline:** The big idea: make normal visible.
**Body:** Set a space up so a missing tool leaves an obvious gap. Then problems show up early instead of festering. A labeled basket by the door does the same job at home.

## Slide 6
**Headline:** Six words carry the whole thing.
**Body:** Sort. Straighten. Shine. Safety. Standardize. Sustain. Three make space, one keeps people safe, two make it last.

## Slide 7
**Headline:** Five became six because of Safety.
**Body:** Workplaces have inspectors and rules. Your home has you. The stairs in the dark, the medicine in reach. At home, Safety may be the most important S.

## Slide 8
**Headline:** Borrow the logic, not the look.
**Body:** No yellow floor tape required. Make the right action easy, make normal visible, protect the people. Your home gets to dress that logic in its own warm clothes.

## Slide 9
**Headline:** One method, any size.
**Body:** The same six steps fix a drawer in fifteen minutes or a garage over a few weekends. Every small project is practice for the big ones.

## Slide 10 (Soft CTA)
**Headline:** You already understand more than most people, and you have not moved a single object.
**Body:** This is Chapter 2 of 6S Success: Home Edition. Read it free in the online book. Chapter 3 walks all six steps, start to finish.
