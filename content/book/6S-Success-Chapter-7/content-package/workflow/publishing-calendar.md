# Publishing Calendar: 14-Day Release Sequence

A two-week rollout for Chapter 7, "Define the Purpose of the Area". This chapter closes Part Two and gets the reader to write a one-sentence purpose for their space. The goal is to get readers to actually write that sentence. Publish the home base (the web chapter) first, then drip social and email toward it. Dates are relative (Day 1 = the day the chapter page goes live).

Pacing principle: one anchor asset per day, plus one or two light reshares. The "a space cannot be good at everything" hook and the Purpose Statement template are your strongest. Lead with them.

## After Day 14

- Recycle the Purpose Statement template and the strongest quote cards monthly.
- Keep the chapter page updated with an FAQ block for SEO (see `canonical/chapter-seo.md`). The Purpose Statement worksheet is a strong lead magnet.
- When Chapter 8 publishes, link this chapter from its "previously" section, and link forward from here. This chapter closes Part 2, so it is a natural bridge into Part 3, where the sorting begins.

## Hero CTA reminder

This chapter's best conversion event is the written purpose. Wherever you can, end with "name your space's one job and write it as a sentence: this space is for ___, so that ___." A reader who does that is fully prepared to sort.
