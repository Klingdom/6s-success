# Newsletter Subject Lines: Define the Purpose of the Area

Ten options. Mix of curiosity, plain clarity, and benefit angles. Honest, no clickbait.

1. Your space is not messy. It is confused. (curiosity)
2. Give your messy spot one job (plain)
3. Why your organizing never lasts (benefit)
4. The one sentence that sorts a space for you (curiosity)
5. Decide what a space is for before you sort it (plain)
6. A space cannot be good at everything (curiosity)
7. Write one sentence, make every sorting decision easy (benefit)
8. The everything-space, and how to fix it (plain)
9. Five jobs at once is why your drop zone fails (curiosity)
10. One clear job, and the space finally works (benefit)
