# Newsletter Preview Text (Preheader): Define the Purpose of the Area

Ten options, each 35 to 90 characters, each paired with a subject line. Char counts noted.

1. Pairs with #1 (Your space is not messy. It is confused.)
   "Decide its one job in a sentence, and the rest gets easy." (56 chars)

2. Pairs with #2 (Give your messy spot one job)
   "Five minutes, mostly thinking. No moving anything yet." (53 chars)

3. Pairs with #3 (Why your organizing never lasts)
   "It was tidy. It was also optimized for nothing." (47 chars)

4. Pairs with #4 (The one sentence that sorts a space for you)
   "This space is for one job, so that ___. Fill in the blanks." (59 chars)

5. Pairs with #5 (Decide what a space is for before you sort it)
   "The purpose is the rule. Sorting just follows it." (49 chars)

6. Pairs with #6 (A space cannot be good at everything)
   "Pick the one job that matters most. Let the rest go." (52 chars)

7. Pairs with #7 (Write one sentence, make every sorting decision easy)
   "Does this help the space do its job? Done." (42 chars)

8. Pairs with #8 (The everything-space, and how to fix it)
   "Mail, keys, charging, homework, decor. All at once. All losing." (63 chars)

9. Pairs with #9 (Five jobs at once is why your drop zone fails)
   "Give it one job and it finally starts working." (46 chars)

10. Pairs with #10 (One clear job, and the space finally works)
    "Write it for the home you have, not the one you wish for." (57 chars)
