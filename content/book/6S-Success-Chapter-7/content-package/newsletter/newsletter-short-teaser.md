# Newsletter Short Teaser: Define the Purpose of the Area

For a roundup or digest. 90 to 140 words.

---

Here is a thought before you organize anything: that messy spot in your home is not failing because it is messy. It is messy because it is failing to be any one thing. The drop zone by your door became the mail station, the key depot, the charging hub, and the homework landing pad, all at once, and so it is good at none of them. Chapter 7 fixes that with one sentence. Decide the space's single job, write it as "this space is for one job, so that an outcome you want," and every keep-or-go decision after that gets easy. Match it to the home you actually live in, not the one you wish for.
