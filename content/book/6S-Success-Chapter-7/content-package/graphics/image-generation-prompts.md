# Image Generation Prompts: Chapter 7

**"Define the Purpose of the Area"**
Five prompts written for the book's warm, editorial, hand-made feel. Palette to hold
across all five: warm paper `#F7F2E9`, panel cream `#FBF7EF`, deep ink brown `#2B2622`,
terracotta `#BC4B2A`, honey `#DDA63A`, slate blue `#3C5A6B`, soft green `#6E8B5B`, a
single warm red `#CB4B36` reserved for tension. Natural soft daylight, shallow but not
showy depth, real textures over polish.

---

## 1. Placing the purpose card (the opener)

A close, low-angle photograph of a hand setting a small handwritten card on a wooden
console table by a front door at early morning. The card reads, in plain ink
handwriting, "the launch pad". Behind it the entryway is still genuinely cluttered:
scattered mail, a tangle of keys, a phone charger cable, a child's homework folder. Warm
low dawn light rakes in from a side window, long soft shadows across the wood grain.
Palette of warm paper cream, ink brown, a touch of honey on the brass keys, slate blue
in the shadowed wall. Editorial cookbook-style photography, shallow focus on the card,
the clutter soft behind it, calm and human, not staged.

- Mood: quiet resolve, the first decision before the work.
- Avoid: glossy real-estate staging, perfect symmetry, cold blue tints, floating text
  overlays, plastic-looking surfaces, anything that reads as a stock photo.

## 2. One job, the launch pad working

A tidy, lived-in entryway console photographed in soft morning light, doing exactly one
job well. On it: a small bowl of keys, one canvas tote per person lined up, a single
umbrella in a narrow stand. Warm cream wall, honey-toned wood, a slate blue runner. No
mail, no chargers, no clutter. The frame feels calm because the surface is allowed to be
good at one thing. Natural window light, gentle shadows, a faint sprig of green for
life. Hand-made editorial style, warm and reassuring, real wear on the wood.

- Mood: calm competence, a space that knows its job.
- Avoid: showroom minimalism, sterile white, harsh flash, decorative clutter sneaking
  back in, oversaturated color, lens-flare gimmicks.

## 3. The everything-space, five jobs colliding

An honest overhead photograph of a small entryway shelf overwhelmed by five competing
jobs at once: a stack of unopened mail, a heap of keys, a charging phone with a tangled
cable, a spread of homework papers, and one dusty decorative object. Things overlap and
crowd; nothing has room. Flat soft daylight from above, warm paper and ink-brown tones
with a single warm-red object catching the eye as a note of tension. Documentary,
unstyled, a little weary. The viewer should feel the friction of a surface asked to do
everything.

- Mood: gentle frustration, good intentions piled up.
- Avoid: disgust or grime, dramatic mess for shock value, neon colors, tilt-shift
  toy-camera effect, a cold clinical look.

## 4. The wish vs the real, a diptych

A two-panel editorial diptych of the same entryway. Left panel: the catalog fantasy, a
serene console with one elegant tray and a single stem in a slim vase, cool slate-blue
light, beautiful and empty. Right panel: the real household, four bags and four pairs of
shoes by the door, an umbrella, a basket per person, warm honey daylight, full and
busy and alive. The two halves share a composition but feel opposite in temperature.
Matte, film-like photography, warm cream throughout, honest texture on every surface.

- Mood: affection for real life, a quiet argument for honesty over fantasy.
- Avoid: mocking the messy side, glamorizing the empty side, hard borders that feel like
  a slide template, harsh contrast, glossy magazine retouching.

## 5. The bouncer at the door

A warm, slightly storybook photograph of a doorway treated as a gentle checkpoint. A
soft terracotta rope curves across a slate-framed door. On the inside, a calm cream
space holds only what belongs: keys and one daily bag. On the outside, a small pile of
old mail and a stray charger are politely set aside, not let in. Low warm side light,
shallow depth, a hand-lettered tag near the rope reading "does this serve the job?".
Hand-made editorial feel, real paper and wood and rope textures, friendly rather than
strict.

- Mood: calm guardianship, a quiet rule keeping the space good.
- Avoid: literal nightclub or velvet-rope cliche, intimidating security imagery, cartoon
  exaggeration, cold blue lighting, busy backgrounds that pull focus.
