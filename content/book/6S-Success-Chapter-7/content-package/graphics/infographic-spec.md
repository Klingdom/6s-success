# Infographic Spec: The Purpose Statement

**Chapter 7, "Define the Purpose of the Area"**
**6S Success: Home Edition, Part Two, Prepare**

This is the single strongest image in the chapter. It carries the whole idea in one
glance: fill two blanks, and the space sorts itself. The concept pairs the hero
**Purpose Statement** template with a compact **One Job Beats Five** strip underneath,
so the viewer sees both the tool and the reason it works.

## Layout notes

- Keep the two blanks in terracotta and nothing else competing for that color near
  them, so the eye lands on the words a reader is meant to fill.
- The filled example earns the only pure-white card on the page. That small contrast
  against the warm panel fills signals "here is the answer."
- The collision X marks are the only spark red in the top two-thirds. Save spark for
  friction, save green for calm. That color discipline is the chapter's whole argument
  in two hues.
- Hand-made feel: a faint paper grain on the background, slightly soft corners, the
  filled-example sentence set as if jotted rather than typeset.
