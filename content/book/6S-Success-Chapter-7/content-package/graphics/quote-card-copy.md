# Quote Card Copy: Chapter 7

**"Define the Purpose of the Area"**
Ten quote cards, pulled verbatim from `chapter-quotes.md`. None contain em dashes.
Each card uses the warm book palette (paper `#F7F2E9`, panel `#FBF7EF`, ink `#2B2622`)
and the Fraunces display face for the quote, Newsreader italic for the attribution.

Suggested attribution for every card: **6S Success: Home Edition, Chapter 7**

---

### Card 1
> "Before you decide what stays and what goes, you have to decide what this space is for."

- Attribution: 6S Success: Home Edition, Chapter 7
- Design: Ink quote on paper, small terracotta `THE LAST QUESTION` eyebrow. Purpose Statement motif, a single blank card glyph in the corner.

### Card 2
> "The space is not failing because it is messy. It is messy because it is failing to be any one thing."

- Attribution: 6S Success: Home Edition, Chapter 7
- Design: Slate quote on panel. Everything-space motif, faint cluster of overlapping gray shapes behind the text.

### Card 3
> "Organizing without a purpose is just moving things around neatly."

- Attribution: 6S Success: Home Edition, Chapter 7
- Design: Ink on paper, generous space. One small spark-red dot as the only accent. Quiet, dry tone to match the line.

### Card 4
> "The purpose is what makes tidy mean something."

- Attribution: 6S Success: Home Edition, Chapter 7
- Design: Fraunces large on panel, terracotta underline beneath "purpose". Minimal, confident.

### Card 5
> "A space is allowed to have one job."

- Attribution: 6S Success: Home Edition, Chapter 7
- Design: One Job Beats Five motif. A single green `LAUNCH PAD` pill sits below the quote, green calm dots beside it.

### Card 6
> "Excellence at one thing requires not being mediocre at four others."

- Attribution: 6S Success: Home Edition, Chapter 7
- Design: Split feel. Four faded gray words on one side, one solid green word on the other. Ink quote across the top.

### Card 7
> "A space cannot be good at everything. Give it one clear job, and it will finally be good at that."

- Attribution: 6S Success: Home Edition, Chapter 7
- Design: The definition-box look. Panel card with a 2 px slate border, ink Fraunces text. Anchor card for the set.

### Card 8
> "A plain, slightly clumsy sentence that is true beats a beautiful one that is vague."

- Attribution: 6S Success: Home Edition, Chapter 7
- Design: Newsreader italic on paper, as if handwritten. Purpose Statement motif, a soft honey blank line under the words.

### Card 9
> "A good purpose works with your life. A vain one fights it."

- Attribution: 6S Success: Home Edition, Chapter 7
- Design: Aspirational-vs-real contrast. Left half warm and lived-in, right half cool slate. Quote centered on the seam.

### Card 10
> "A defined purpose gives every stray object a bouncer at the door."

- Attribution: 6S Success: Home Edition, Chapter 7
- Design: Bouncer-at-the-door motif. A simple doorway shape, one item turned away outside it. Terracotta eyebrow `THE ONE QUESTION: DOES THIS SERVE THE JOB?`
