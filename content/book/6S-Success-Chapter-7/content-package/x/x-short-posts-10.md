# X Short Posts: Define the Purpose of the Area

Chapter 7 of *6S Success: Home Edition*. Ten standalone posts. Each is at or under 280 characters and makes sense on its own. Soft CTA on a few, not all.

---

1/
The space is not failing because it is messy. It is messy because it is failing to be any one thing. Most troubled spaces in a home were never assigned a job. They accumulated three or five by default, and now those jobs compete.

(approx. 227 chars)

---

2/
Before you decide what stays and what goes, decide what the space is for. Skipping that one question is the quiet reason so much organizing falls apart a few weeks later.

(approx. 168 chars)

---

3/
Organizing without a purpose is just moving things around neatly. Decide the space's job first, and then the sorting finally has something to be right about. Chapter 7 of 6S Success: Home Edition is free in the online book.

(approx. 220 chars)

---

4/
Meet the everything-space: a surface asked to do five jobs at once, mail, keys, charging, homework, and decor, and therefore good at none. The mail buries the keys. The fix is not a better system for five jobs. It is picking one.

(approx. 226 chars)

---

5/
A space is allowed to have one job. In fact it is the only way a space ever gets genuinely good, because excellence at one thing requires not being mediocre at four others. One primary job, one supporting job at most. Chapter 7, free online.

(approx. 239 chars)

---

6/
The Purpose Statement, fill in two blanks: This space is for [one primary job], so that [an outcome you actually want]. The first blank is the job. The second is why it matters. A plain true sentence beats a vague pretty one.

(approx. 223 chars)

---

7/
A worked purpose for an entryway: This space is for getting everyone out the door fast and landing softly on the way back in, so mornings start calm instead of frantic. Hold any object against it. The keys serve it. The old mail does not.

(approx. 236 chars)

---

8/
Do not write the purpose for the home you wish you had. A space defined for an imaginary, tidier family will be wrong for the real one every single morning. Define it for the people who actually live there. A good purpose works with your life. A vain one fights it.

(approx. 262 chars)

---

9/
Test your purpose at the worst moment, not the best. The frantic Tuesday. The rainy game day. If it only works when everyone behaves like a catalog model, it is the wrong purpose. This also gives you permission to stop apologizing for the life you have.

(approx. 252 chars)

---

10/
A clear purpose sets a space's limits. Once it has one job, you know exactly how much belongs: as much as the job requires, no more. It becomes a bouncer at the door against re-cluttering. The full chapter is free in the online book.

(approx. 231 chars)
