# Define the Purpose of the Area

**Part Two, Prepare**
Chapter 7 of *6S Success: Home Edition*

---

## Chapter descriptor

A space cannot be good at everything. Before you move a single thing, give it one clear job in one honest sentence.

---

## How to read this chapter

This is the closing chapter of Part Two, Prepare. By now you have chosen a space, put an honest number on it, and taken a before photo. This chapter adds the last and least physical step: deciding what the space is actually for.

You do not sort anything here. You write one sentence. Read it through once for the idea, then come back to the section called "Write Yours" and spend about five minutes naming the one job your space exists to do. Keep that sentence with your audit number and your before photo. When Part Three begins and the sorting starts, that sentence is the rule that makes every keep-or-go decision easy.

If you only do one thing: write the purpose sentence for the space you have chosen.
