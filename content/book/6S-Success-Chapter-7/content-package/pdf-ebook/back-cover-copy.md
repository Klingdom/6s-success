# Back Cover Copy: Define the Purpose of the Area

## Back-cover text

Before you decide what stays and what goes, you have to decide what the space is for.

Most troubled spaces in a home were never given a job. They took on five by default, mail and keys and charging and homework and decor, and ended up good at none of them. A space is not failing because it is messy. It is messy because it is failing to be any one thing.

Chapter 7 of *6S Success: Home Edition* gives you the fix, and it is one sentence long. Decide the space's one primary job. Write it down. Now every keep-or-go decision has a yardstick: does this help the space do its job? You will see a cluttered entryway become a launch pad, so mornings start calm. You will learn to write the purpose for your real life, not an ideal one. And you will see how a clear job sets a natural limit that keeps the space good long after you finish.

A space cannot be good at everything. Give it one clear job, and it will finally be good at that.
