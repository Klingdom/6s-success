# 6S Success: Home Edition (Ebook Description)

Most organizing advice starts with sorting. *6S Success: Home Edition* starts a step earlier, and that step is why its results last.

In Chapter 7, "Define the Purpose of the Area," you learn the move that makes every later decision easy: decide what a space is for before you touch a single object. Most troubled spaces in a home were never given a job. They quietly took on five at once, mail and keys and charging and homework and decor, and ended up good at none of them. A space is not failing because it is messy. It is messy because it is failing to be any one thing.

This chapter gives you a plain tool to fix that. It is a one-sentence Purpose Statement: this space is for one primary job, so that an outcome you actually want. You will see it worked through on a real entryway, where a cluttered drop zone becomes a launch pad, so mornings start calm instead of frantic. You will also learn to write the purpose for the people who actually live in your home, not for an ideal version of them, and to test it against your worst, most frantic day.

Then comes the quiet payoff. A space with one clear job has a natural limit. It knows how much belongs there, so it keeps a bouncer at the door and turns away the clutter that creeps back in.

Decide the one job. Write the one sentence. The sorting gets easy from there.
