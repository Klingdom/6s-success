- **Secondary device:** One Job Beats Five (a space asked to do five jobs is good at none; one clear job makes it good at that).

- **Hero CTA:** Write your purpose sentence. Name the one job your space exists to do, write it as "this space is for ___, so that ___," keep it to one primary job, and match it to the people who actually live there.
