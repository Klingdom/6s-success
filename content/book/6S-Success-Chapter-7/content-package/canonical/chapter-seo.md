## Search intent
Practical and slightly philosophical. Readers want a decision rule for what to keep, or want to understand why their organizing keeps failing. The chapter answers with a concrete tool (the one-sentence Purpose Statement) and a strong diagnosis (the everything-space). The "give a room one job" framing is a fresh, underserved angle compared with generic decluttering tips, and it pairs naturally with the re-cluttering problem people actively search.
