# Chapter 7 Quotes: Define the Purpose of the Area

Pull quotes lifted directly from the manuscript and final HTML, ready for quote cards, social posts, and graphics. All are verbatim or lightly trimmed. Nothing here is invented.

## Verbatim lines (book voice)

1. "Before you decide what stays and what goes, you have to decide what this space is for."

2. "The space is not failing because it is messy. It is messy because it is failing to be any one thing."

3. "Organizing without a purpose is just moving things around neatly."

4. "A defined purpose turns every keep-or-go decision into a single, fast, repeatable question: does this thing help the space do its job?"

5. "The purpose is what makes tidy mean something."

6. "It is the everything-space. The surface or drawer or room that has quietly been assigned five jobs at once and is therefore good at none of them."

7. "A space is allowed to have one job."

8. "Excellence at one thing requires not being mediocre at four others."

9. "A space cannot be good at everything. Give it one clear job, and it will finally be good at that."

10. "A plain, slightly clumsy sentence that is true beats a beautiful one that is vague."

11. "Tidiness is not a purpose. It is a side effect of a space doing its real job well."

12. "Writing a purpose for the home you wish you had. A space defined for an imaginary, tidier family will be wrong for the real one every single morning."

13. "A good purpose works with your life. A vain one fights it."

14. "It gives you permission to stop apologizing for the life you have."

15. "Once a space has a single defined job, it also has a natural capacity."

16. "A defined purpose gives every stray object a bouncer at the door."

17. "The number told you where the space stood. The photo recorded it. The purpose is the one that keeps it good."

## Definition box (canonical)

"A space cannot be good at everything. Give it one clear job, and at most one supporting job, and it will finally be good at that. Everything that does not serve the job has a better home elsewhere."

## One Idea to Keep (canonical close)

"A space cannot be good at everything. Decide its one job, in a single sentence, and you have just made every sorting decision that follows almost automatic."

## The template (for graphics)

The Purpose Statement: "This [space] is for [one primary job], so that [an outcome you actually want]." Worked example: "This entryway is for getting everyone out the door fast and landing softly on the way back in, so that mornings start calm instead of frantic." One primary job, one supporting job at most.
