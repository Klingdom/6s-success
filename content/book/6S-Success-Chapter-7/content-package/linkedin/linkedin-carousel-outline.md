# Chapter 7 LinkedIn Carousel Outline: Define the Purpose of the Area

Ten slides. Slide 1 hooks, last slide carries the soft CTA. Each slide has a headline and one or two lines of body.

---

## Slide 1: Hook

**Headline:** Your messiest space is not failing because it is messy.

**Body:** It is messy because it is failing to be any one thing. Swipe to fix the real cause.

---

## Slide 2: The default job

**Headline:** No space was ever decided. It accumulated.

**Body:** The drop zone became where mail, keys, chargers, and homework all land at once. None of it chosen. All of it competing.

---

## Slide 3: Decide before you sort

**Headline:** Decide the job before you move a thing.

**Body:** One sentence. Five minutes. The least physical step in organizing, and one of the most useful.

---

## Slide 4: The yardstick

**Headline:** One question makes sorting easy.

**Body:** Does this thing help the space do its job? The umbrella stays. The read magazines find a better home.

---

## Slide 5: Tidy can still fail

**Headline:** A space can be neat and still wrong.

**Body:** Sort without a purpose and you optimize for nothing. The purpose is what makes tidy mean something.

---

## Slide 6: The everything-space

**Headline:** Meet the enemy: the everything-space.

**Body:** One surface asked to do five jobs at once: mail, keys, charging, homework, decor. Good at none of them.

---

## Slide 7: One job beats five

**Headline:** A space is allowed to have one job.

**Body:** Pick the job that matters most. Excellence at one thing requires not being mediocre at four others.

---

## Slide 8: The Purpose Statement

**Headline:** Write the one sentence.

**Body:** This space is for one primary job, so that an outcome you actually want. The job, and the reason it matters.

---

## Slide 9: The worked example

**Headline:** Watch it sort the space for you.

**Body:** This entryway is for getting everyone out the door fast, so that mornings start calm. Keys serve it. Three weeks of mail do not.

---

## Slide 10: Match it to real life

**Headline:** Write it for the home you have, not the one you wish for.

**Body:** Test it on the frantic Tuesday. If it only works when everyone behaves like a catalog model, it is the wrong purpose.

---

## Slide 11: Purpose sets the limits

**Headline:** Your space's bouncer at the door.

**Body:** One job means you know exactly how much belongs. New clutter drifts in, the purpose asks: does this serve the job? If not, turned away.

---

## Slide 12: Soft CTA

**Headline:** Decide its one job. The rest gets almost automatic.
