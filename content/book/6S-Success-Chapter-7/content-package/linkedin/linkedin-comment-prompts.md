# Chapter 7 LinkedIn Comment Prompts: Define the Purpose of the Area

Five prompts to start genuine discussion. Drop one in the comments of a post or article to invite replies.

---

1. What is the one job of your messiest space? Try to say it in a single sentence, and notice how hard it is to keep it to one job.

2. What space in your home is trying to do five jobs at once? Name the five and tell us which one you would keep if you could only pick one.

3. Have you ever organized a space beautifully and still felt it was wrong? What was it actually for that you only realized later?

4. Fill in the blanks for one space: this space is for ___, so that ___. Share yours and we will pressure-test whether it is one honest job or three in a trench coat.

5. What is one object sitting in your space right now that has nothing to do with its real job? You do not have to move it yet, just name it.
