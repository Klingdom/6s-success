# Chapter 40 Landing Page Intro: The Nursery

The nursery is the one room in this book where getting it wrong is not a matter of an untidy shelf. It is the room where safety outranks everything, because the person it is built for can reach nothing, hold nothing, and ask for nothing, and the person running it is usually exhausted and working one-handed at two in the morning.

This chapter breaks the nursery into six small zones and gives you, for each one, the exact inputs to gather and the numbered steps to clean and shine it: the crib and sleep zone, the changing station, the baby clothing zone, the feeding station, the diaper and care backstock, and the books and quiet play zone. It shows you why the crib holds one fitted sheet and nothing else ever, how to build every zone so the essentials sit inside one arm's sweep, and how to clean the surfaces a mouth and bare skin touch after every single use, with fragrance-free, infant-safe cleaners and nothing ever put away wet.

Product types only, no brands. Start with the crib, the quickest zone and the one where getting it wrong matters most.
