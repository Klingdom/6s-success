# The Nursery

*The nursery is the one room in the house where safety outranks everything else, and where the person using it is usually exhausted and working one-handed at two in the morning, so cleaning it is not about a tidy shelf, it is about a room that adult can run in the dark and trust completely. This playbook points the whole loop at it one zone at a time, and gives you, for each of the six zones, the exact inputs to gather and the precise, step-by-step way to clean and shine it, so a bare crib, one arm's sweep, and a fragrance-free wipe hold every night.*

## How to read this chapter

This is a PLAYBOOK, not a method chapter. It applies the six-S loop you already own to one room, decomposed into six micro-zones, and its centerpiece is the per-zone clean-and-shine instructions with the exact inputs. It recaps but does not re-teach the nine-rule Shine method from Chapter 31; the new material is the nursery layer, in which safety leads every zone (the bare crib, one arm's sweep, cleaned after every use with fragrance-free cleaners), and the numbered steps for each zone.

Products are named as TYPES, never brands. The exhaustive product list is a separate appendix. Nothing in this chapter is medical advice; it reflects established safe-sleep and safe-storage practice, and you should follow your own health service's current guidance.

The friction meter, which reached its goal at Chapter 30, is retired for Part 9; the chapter closes on a before-and-after room signature, and here the after is the safe state the room resets to every night.
