# Chapter 40 Ebook Sales Copy: The Nursery

## Headline
The one room where safety outranks everything. Here is how to build and clean a nursery an exhausted adult can run one-handed at 2 a.m. and trust completely.

## The promise
In the nursery, cleaning and safety are the same job, because the baby it protects can reach nothing and the person running it is depleted. This chapter gives you the exact inputs and the numbered steps for each of the room's six zones, plus the three rules that run through all of them: the crib holds one fitted sheet and nothing else ever, everything a change or a feed needs sits inside one arm's sweep, and the surfaces skin and a mouth touch are cleaned after every use.

## What you get in this chapter
- The nursery Shine method, with safety leading every zone: the bare crib, one arm's sweep, cleaned after every use, nothing put away wet.
- The six per-zone clean procedures, each with its inputs and numbered steps: the crib and sleep zone, the changing station, the baby clothing zone, the feeding station, the diaper and care backstock, and the books and quiet play zone.
- The safe-sleep standard you never round up, and how to hold it with the people who give you gifts.
- The one-arm's-sweep setup for the changing and feeding stations, and the after-every-use habits that stop mould.
- The safety layer, cords, weight, the dropped mattress, strapped furniture, locked medicines, and the before-and-after signature.

## The common mistake it fixes
> Buying ahead, a bigger diaper size, next winter's clothes, a second bottle brand in case the first fails, so the room fills with supplies bought for a baby who never arrived in that shape; and softening the bare-crib rule for one cold night, which is the night it matters most.
