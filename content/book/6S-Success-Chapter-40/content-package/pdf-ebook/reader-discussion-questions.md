# Chapter 40 Reader Discussion Questions: The Nursery

1. Is your crib truly bare, one fitted sheet and nothing else, or has something crept back over the rail?
2. Can you reach every diaper, wipe, and cream from the changing pad without turning your back on the baby?
3. Do you clean the changing pad after every change, or save it for the weekly reset?
4. How do you dry the bottle parts, and where does a damp valve end up in your kitchen or nursery?
5. Are the monitor and blind cords looped short and clipped an arm's length back from the crib?
6. Is anything heavy stored on a shelf above or beside the crib right now?
7. Has the crib mattress been dropped, or will be, the day your baby can pull to stand?
8. Are the creams, gels, and infant medicines in a lockable box up high?
9. What did you buy ahead that your baby skipped straight past?
10. Which loving gift is hardest to keep out of the crib, and how will you say so to the giver?
