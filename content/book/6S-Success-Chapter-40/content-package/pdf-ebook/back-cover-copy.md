# Chapter 40 Back Cover Copy: The Nursery

## Back-cover text
The nursery is the one room where safety outranks everything, because the baby it protects can reach nothing and the person running it is exhausted at two in the morning. So cleaning it and keeping it safe are the same job.

The Nursery breaks the room into six zones and gives you, for each, the exact inputs to gather and the numbered steps to follow. The crib holds one fitted sheet and nothing else, ever. Everything a change or a feed needs sits inside one arm's sweep. And the surfaces a mouth and bare skin touch are cleaned after every use, with fragrance-free cleaners and nothing put away wet.

Do one zone tonight, the crib, and give the person who runs this room in the dark a room they can trust without looking.
