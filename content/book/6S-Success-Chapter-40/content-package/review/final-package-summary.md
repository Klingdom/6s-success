## Source fidelity
The six zones, their inputs, their step order, and every inspect-and-flag list are folded directly from the Micro Zone Manual's Nursery content (six zones, each with shine_detail: surfaces, methods, products_used, inspect_as_you_clean) and the room's 30 mapped product types (including the lockable medicine box present in all six zones). The reset instructions were carried through faithfully: the strip-clean-inspect crib pass with the mouthed rail and the frame bolts, the one-arm's-sweep changing station with the pad wiped after every change, the empty-drawer clothing pass, the nothing-goes-away-wet feeding station with the descale and the under-rack surface, the read-every-date backstock, and the mouthing-surface books and play pass. The intro's safety-outranks-everything and built-for-2-a.m. framing and the buy-ahead trap are the source of the chapter's layer, and the calls (the quilt, the half-station, the unworn outfit, the rejected bottle, the good deal in the wrong size, the books you bought for yourself) come straight from the Manual's the_call entries.

## Note on scope
Nothing in this chapter is medical advice; it reflects established safe-sleep and safe-storage practice, and the chapter says so and points readers to their own health service's current guidance.
