# Chapter 40 Editorial Review: The Nursery

## Verdict
Ready for Phil's review as a draft. The chapter meets the instruction-centric standard: every one of the six zones carries an Inputs list, numbered Steps, and an inspect-and-flag paragraph, and the nursery Shine layer is distinct from the nine rooms before it, most obviously in that safety leads every zone rather than coming last.

## What works
- **The layer is genuinely its own, and it is the right one for this room.** Safety-outranks-everything, the bare crib as a number you never round up, one arm's sweep, and clean-after-every-use are all specific to a room whose occupant can protect themselves not at all, and none of them repeats the kids-bedroom (at-their-height) or guest-bedroom (freshness) layers. Restructuring so safety frames all five passes rather than being one of them is the correct and honest move.
- **The Manual reset instructions are folded in faithfully.** The strip-and-inspect crib pass, the one-arm's-sweep changing station, the nothing-goes-away-wet feeding station, and the read-every-date backstock are carried straight from the source shine_detail and elevated into numbered procedures.
- **The safety framing is calm, not alarmist.** The chapter is unflinching about the hazards (loose bedding, long cords, weight above the crib, the high mattress) but states them plainly and gives a clear fix for each, which is the right register for a room parents are already anxious about.
- **The gift-and-good-deal calls land.** The quilt someone made, the rejected bottle, and the bulk buy in the wrong size turn the room's real emotional friction into clean rules, and the "say it once, plainly" guidance is genuinely useful.

## Watch items (minor, non-blocking)
- The chapter is the longest room playbook so far (about 6,270 words), because the safety framing, the six-zone instruction set, and the calls all carry weight; the length cap is lifted for Part 9, so this is acceptable, and the Calls section is the easiest trim if Phil wants tighter.
- The chapter correctly disclaims that it is not medical advice and points to the reader's health service; Phil may want to confirm the exact wording of that disclaimer against house legal preference before print.

## Consistency checks
- Nine Shine rules recapped, not re-taught, with the fragrance-free change noted: PASS.
- Friction meter retired; before-and-after signature closes, with the after as the nightly safe state: PASS.
- Product types only: PASS.
- Hand-off to Chapter 41 (the primary bathroom, first of the wet rooms) present and light: PASS.

## Recommendation
Approve as draft. No structural edits required; confirm the medical-disclaimer wording before print.
