# Chapter 40 Brand Voice Check: The Nursery

## Voice target
Warm but sober, expert, and unflinching about safety without being alarmist. The register of a knowledgeable friend who has run a nursery on no sleep, speaking to a parent who is anxious and depleted.

## Scan results
- **Product types only, no brands**: PASS. A brand-name scan across the whole package returned no true hits. One false-positive substring ("at dawn", the common noun, matching a dish-soap brand name) was reworded to "on a rushed morning" so the scan is fully clean. Everything is a type: colour-coded microfiber cloth set, fragrance-free neutral pH multi-surface cleaner, compact vacuum with attachments, food-area degreaser, descaler or citric acid, bottle brush and baby-safe dish soap, lockable medicine box, diaper pail, anti-tip anchor and hardware.
- **No hype, no invented numbers or prices**: PASS. No statistics, no fabricated study claims, no dollar figures.
- **No medical overreach**: PASS. The chapter states plainly that it is not medical advice and reflects established safe-sleep and safe-storage practice, pointing readers to their own health service. The safe-sleep guidance (bare crib, drop the mattress, cords back) is mainstream and non-prescriptive.
- **Dash and punctuation house style**: PASS. Middot for label separators; no em/en dashes; no prose spaced-hyphen separators.
- **Inclusive, non-gendered**: PASS. "The person using it", "an exhausted adult", "whoever puts the baby down" throughout; the baby is "they" and "the baby", with no gendered assumptions about parent or child.

## Consistency with the series
- The zone-by-zone instruction voice matches Chapters 31 to 39.
- The one-idea-to-keep close and the before-and-after signature match the Part 9 pattern, with the after-is-the-nightly-safe-state variation noted.
- The fences held everywhere: never anything in the crib but a fitted sheet, never a scented cleaner on a mouthed surface, never weight above the crib, and no real infant on camera or in images.

## Verdict
On voice. No changes needed beyond the completed "at dawn" rewording.
