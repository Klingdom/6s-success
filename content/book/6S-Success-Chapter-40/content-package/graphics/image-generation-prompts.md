# Chapter 40 Image Generation Prompts: The Nursery

Warm but sober, illustrated, flat-color editorial house style (paper #F7F2E9 ground, ink #2B2622 line, terracotta/honey/slate/green accents, Shine green #6E8B5B). No photorealism, no brand names or logos, no text baked into the art beyond the noted labels. Product types only. No real infant depicted; suggest scale, not a face.

## Prompt 1 (opener, two states)
A warm flat-illustration of one nursery shown twice, side by side. Left, labelled "before": a crib holding a bumper, a quilt, and a plush toy, cords looping down within reach, supplies stacked out of reach, cartons on a shelf above the crib. Right, labelled "after": a bare crib with one tight fitted sheet, a changing pad with supplies inside an arm's arc, cords clipped short and back, a lockable box up high, and furniture strapped to the wall.

## Prompt 2 (six zones plan)
A warm overhead floor plan of a nursery, gently shaded, with six numbered and labelled zones: crib and sleep, changing station, baby clothing, feeding station, diaper backstock, books and play. Caption strip at the foot: "one room, six small jobs, safety leads each one".

## Prompt 3 (method and kit, two panels)
A two-panel warm infographic. Left "the method": a shield over the room, a crib with one sheet and nothing else, an arc of reach around a changing pad with every supply inside it, and a cloth on a pad beside a drying bottle part with a crossed-out scented spray. Right "the kit": labelled type icons for a microfiber set, a fragrance-free cleaner, plain water or a baby-safe wipe, a vacuum with attachments, a food-area degreaser, a descaler, a bottle brush and baby-safe soap, a lockable medicine box, a diaper pail, front-facing book bins, picture-label toy bins, a moisture absorber, and anti-tip anchors.

## Prompt 4 (crib step ribbon)
A warm step-ribbon titled "how to clean the crib": dust the monitor and clip the cord back, clean the mouthed rail with plain water, wipe every slat, lift the mattress and check the bolts, hot-wash the sheet, vacuum beneath, one tight sheet back. The mouthed-rail and one-sheet-back steps highlighted in the Shine green.

## Prompt 5 (before-and-after signature)
A warm matched pair of the nursery, the crib and the changing pad and the cords, shot from the doorway, before on the left and after on the right, with a small camera icon and the labels "the before, kept" and "the after, the nightly standard".
