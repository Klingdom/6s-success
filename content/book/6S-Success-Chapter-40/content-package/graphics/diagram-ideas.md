# Chapter 40 Diagram Ideas: The Nursery

House palette (paper #F7F2E9, panel #FBF7EF, ink #2B2622, terracotta #BC4B2A, honey #DDA63A, slate #3C5A6B, green #6E8B5B for Shine). Warm but sober, illustrated, not photoreal. Product types only, no brands or logos. Spark red reserved only for a true hazard (a long grabbable appliance cord, loose bedding in the crib).

## Diagram 1 (opener, figure 1): two states
The nursery before and after, side by side. Before: a bumper, a quilt, and a plush toy in the crib, cords looping down within reach, supplies stacked out of reach, cartons on the shelf above the crib, five bottle brands and a wet rack. After: a bare crib with one tight sheet, every change and feed inside one arm's sweep, cords short and clipped back, medicines in a lockable box up high, and every tall thing strapped to the wall.

## Diagram 2 (figure 2): the six zones plan
A warm overhead plan, six numbered zones marked and named: crib and sleep, changing station, baby clothing, feeding station, diaper backstock, books and play. Footer: "one room, six small jobs, safety leads each one".

## Diagram 3 (figure 3): the Shine method and kit
Two panels. Left, the method: safety leads (a shield over the room); the bare crib (a crib with one sheet and nothing else); one arm's sweep (an arc of reach around a changing pad, everything inside it); fragrance-free after every use (a cloth on a pad, a bottle part drying, a crossed-out scented spray). Right, the kit: a microfiber set, a fragrance-free cleaner, plain water or a baby-safe wipe, a vacuum with attachments, a food-area degreaser, a descaler, a bottle brush and baby-safe soap, a lockable medicine box, a diaper pail, front-facing book bins and picture-label toy bins, a moisture absorber, and anti-tip anchors. Footer: "safety leads, crib bare, arm's sweep, nothing wet".

## Diagram 4 (no HTML twin): the nursery Shine method
A standalone visual: safety leads, the bare crib, one arm's sweep, clean after every use. Names the next chapter, the primary bathroom.

## Diagram 5 (no HTML twin): how to clean the crib
A step ribbon: dust the monitor and clip the cord back, clean the mouthed rail with plain water, wipe every slat feeling for a split, lift the mattress and check the bolts, hot-wash the sheet, vacuum the floor beneath, one tight sheet back. Highlight the mouthed-rail and one-sheet-back steps.

## Diagram 6 (before/after signature) and the photo brief
The Part 9 signature: a matched before-and-after pair of the crib, the changing pad, and the cords, from the doorway. **Best before-and-after photo brief:** stand in the doorway and frame the crib, the changing pad, and the cords together in one shot; same spot, angle, and light, before and after; do not tidy before the before (Ch 6); keep the after as the nightly safe state the room resets to (Ch 25). The retired friction meter is not drawn.
