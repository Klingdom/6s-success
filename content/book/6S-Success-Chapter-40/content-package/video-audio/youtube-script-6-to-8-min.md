# Chapter 40 YouTube Script (6 to 8 min): The Nursery

Alternating HOST and VISUAL. Product types only. No real infant on camera; suggest scale.

HOST: There is one room where cleaning and safety are the same job, because the baby it protects can reach nothing and the person running it is exhausted at two in the morning. Today, the nursery.
VISUAL: Two-state opener, hazard-filled before and bare-crib after. One tight sheet going onto a bare mattress.

HOST: Here safety does not come last, it leads every zone. Three rules run through the room: the crib holds one fitted sheet and nothing else, everything a change needs sits inside one arm's sweep, and the mouthed and skin surfaces are cleaned after every use.
VISUAL: A crib stripped to one sheet; an arm reaching every supply at a pad.

HOST: Six zones. Start with the crib. Clean the mouthed rail with plain water, draw your palm down every slat to feel for a split, lift the mattress and check the frame bolts, then one tight sheet back and nothing more.
VISUAL: The rail wiped; a palm down a slat; the mattress lifted and bolts checked.

HOST: The changing station is built for one hand. The diapers, wipes, and cream are all touchable without moving your feet, and the pad gets wiped after every single change.
VISUAL: The arm's-sweep reach; the pad wiped after a change.

HOST: The feeding station: wash the parts, sterilize them, and stand every one separately to air dry, because a damp valve in a closed bin is where mould starts. Descale the kettle, and coil each cord short and back.
VISUAL: Bottle parts drying separately; the surface under the rack wiped; a cord coiled short.

HOST: Safety runs through the whole room. Cords short and clipped back, nothing heavy above the crib, the mattress dropped when they pull to stand, furniture strapped, and every medicine in a lockable box up high.
VISUAL: The monitor cord clipped back; a carton moved off the shelf above the crib; an anti-tip strap.

HOST: And do not buy ahead, because babies skip sizes and reject teats. Do one zone tonight, the crib. Next: the primary bathroom.
VISUAL: The before-and-after room signature from the doorway. End card.
