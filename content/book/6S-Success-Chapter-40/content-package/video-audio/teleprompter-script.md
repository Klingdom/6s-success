# Chapter 40 Teleprompter Script: The Nursery

Short lines, one breath each. Warm but sober.

There is one room where safety outranks everything.
The nursery.
The baby it protects can reach nothing, and ask for nothing.
And the person running it is exhausted, one-handed, at two in the morning.
So every standard is built for that person, and that hour.
Start with the crib.
It holds one fitted sheet, pulled tight to the corners.
And nothing else. Ever.
No bumper. No pillow. No loose blanket. No plush.
This is the one standard you never round up.
Not for a cold night. Not for a gift.
Then the changing pad.
Everything a change needs sits inside one arm's sweep.
Because the moment you turn to reach a shelf,
is the moment a baby rolls off the pad.
Wipe the pad after every change.
Wash the bottle parts, and stand each one to air dry.
A damp valve in a closed bin is where mould starts.
Fragrance-free cleaners only. Nothing put away wet.
Loop the cords short. Clip them back from the crib.
Keep the weight low. Lock the medicines up high.
Drop the mattress the day they pull to stand.
Do one zone tonight. The crib.
And give the person who runs this room in the dark a room they can trust.
