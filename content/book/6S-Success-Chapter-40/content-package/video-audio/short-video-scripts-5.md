# Chapter 40 Short Video Scripts (5): The Nursery

Each 30 to 90 seconds. Product types only. No real infant on camera; suggest scale.

## 1. The bare crib (40s)
HOOK: The crib holds one thing, and nothing else.
SCRIPT: A fitted sheet pulled tight to the corners, and nothing else over the rail, ever. No bumper, no pillow, no loose blanket, no plush, because loose bedding is a suffocation risk to a baby who cannot roll off it. This is the one standard you never round up, not even for a cold night.
ON-SCREEN: The crib holds one sheet and nothing else.
CTA: Chapter 40. Strip the crib to one sheet tonight.

## 2. One arm's sweep (35s)
HOOK: Never turn your back at the changing pad.
SCRIPT: Every change is done one-handed, with your other hand on the baby. So the diapers, wipes, and cream must all be touchable from the pad without moving your feet, because the moment you turn to reach a shelf is the moment a baby rolls off it.
CTA: Chapter 40. Set up your pad for one arm's sweep.

## 3. Nothing goes away wet (30s)
HOOK: This is where nursery mould starts.
SCRIPT: A damp valve in a closed bin. So wash the bottle parts, sterilize them, and stand every one separately to air dry before it goes in the clean bin. Wipe the changing pad after every change, and use fragrance-free cleaners only. Nothing in this room goes away wet.
CTA: Chapter 40, the feeding station.

## 4. Drop the mattress (30s)
HOOK: The day your baby pulls to stand, do this.
SCRIPT: Drop the crib mattress to its lowest setting. Left high, the rail sits below their chest, and the fall is over the side onto a hard floor. It is a two-minute job that closes the most predictable fall in the room.
CTA: Chapter 40, the crib.

## 5. Do not buy ahead (35s)
HOOK: Stop buying ahead for the baby.
SCRIPT: Babies skip sizes and reject teats, so the nursery fills with stock for a baby who never arrived in that shape. Buy no more than one size ahead and two packs at most, whatever the price, and move outgrown stock out fast.
CTA: Chapter 40. Next: the primary bathroom.
