# Chapter 40 B-Roll and Visual Notes: The Nursery

Warm but sober, natural light. Product types only, no brand labels on screen. No real infant on camera; suggest scale, not a face.

## Visual vocabulary
- The two-state opener: a hazard-filled before and a bare-crib, safety-led after.
- The recurring "bare crib" motif: one tight sheet and nothing else, and an arm reaching every supply without leaving the pad.

## Core shot list (seven shots)
1. The crib: stripping it bare, cleaning the mouthed rail with plain water, and drawing a palm down each slat.
2. Lifting the mattress to check the frame bolts and the support base, then one tight sheet going back and nothing more.
3. The changing station: an arm reaching diapers, wipes, and cream without the feet moving, and the pad wiped after a change.
4. The feeding station: bottle parts washed and each one stood separately on the rack to air dry, and the surface under the rack wiped.
5. Descaling the kettle or sterilizer, and coiling each appliance cord short and setting it back behind the unit.
6. The backstock: reading a date on a cream tube and placing the lockable medicine box up high.
7. The safety beat: clipping the monitor cord an arm's length back, moving a carton off the shelf above the crib, and an anti-tip strap into a stud.

## On-screen text bank (use sparingly)
- Safety leads every zone.
- The crib holds one sheet and nothing else.
- One arm's sweep, never turn your back.
- Nothing goes away wet.
- Do not buy ahead.

## Lower thirds (rotate)
- Zone 1 to 6, one per segment.
- Do one zone tonight, the crib.

## Editing notes
- Close each cut on the before-and-after room signature from the doorway (the bare crib, the arm's-sweep pad, the clipped cords).
- No presenter required; hands and surfaces only.
