Hi friend,

We finish the sleeping rooms with the one where getting it wrong is not about an untidy shelf. It is the room where safety outranks everything.

> The nursery is the one room in the house where safety outranks everything else, and where the person using it is usually exhausted and working one-handed at two in the morning. So every standard here is built for that person and that hour rather than for a tidy shelf: the crib holds one fitted sheet and nothing else, ever; everything a change or a feed needs sits inside one arm's sweep so you never turn your back on the baby; and the surfaces a mouth and bare skin touch get cleaned after every single use, with fragrance-free, infant-safe cleaners and nothing put away wet. In this room you do not round a safety number up, and you do not leave the baby to fetch a thing you forgot to keep in reach.

The room is six zones, each with its exact inputs and numbered steps: the crib and sleep zone, the changing station, the baby clothing zone, the feeding station, the diaper and care backstock, and the books and quiet play zone.

The standard you hold exactly:

> The crib is the one surface in the house where your baby is left alone, so it holds a fitted sheet pulled tight to the corners and nothing else, ever. No bumper, no pillow, no loose blanket, no plush toy, and nothing draped over the top rail, because loose bedding is a suffocation risk to an infant who cannot roll off it. This is the one standard in the whole book you never round up: not for a cold night, not for a nap, and not for the beautiful quilt somebody made, which goes on the wall above the crib and never inside the rails.

## The crib, done properly (the worked example)

**Inputs:** a colour-coded microfiber set, a fragrance-free neutral pH cleaner, plain warm water for the mouthed rail, a compact vacuum, a hot wash, your caddy.

**Steps:**

1. Dust and wipe the wall-mounted monitor and run the cloth down the clipped cable, checking it stays an arm's length back.
2. Clean the top and teething rail with warm water, a light pass of fragrance-free cleaner, then plain water again.
3. Wipe every slat, drawing your palm down each to feel for a rough edge or split.
4. Lift the mattress, wipe the cover seams and zip, and look at the frame bolts while they are exposed.
5. Hot-wash the sheet and sleep sack, vacuum the floor under the crib, then put back one tight sheet and nothing else.

## To try tonight

Do one zone tonight, the crib, and give the person who runs this room at two in the morning a room they can trust without looking.
