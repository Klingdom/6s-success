# Chapter 40 Newsletter Short Teaser: The Nursery

A short digest blurb (about 120 words) with a read-the-chapter CTA.

---

**The Nursery: safe sleep, one arm's sweep, after every use.**

The nursery is the one room where safety outranks everything, because the baby it protects can reach nothing and the person running it is exhausted at two in the morning. So every standard is built for that person and that hour.

Chapter 40 gives the exact inputs and numbered steps for each of the room's six zones, plus the three rules that run through all of them: the crib holds one fitted sheet and nothing else ever, everything a change or feed needs sits inside one arm's sweep, and the surfaces skin and a mouth touch are cleaned after every use.

[Read the chapter] and build a nursery you can trust without looking.
