# Chapter 40 Content Package: The Nursery

The TENTH room playbook of Part Nine (Chapters 31 to 50), and the last of the sleeping rooms, built to the instruction-centric template. The chapter CORE is the detailed, step-by-step clean-and-shine instruction for each of the six nursery zones, with the exact inputs, folded directly from the Micro Zone Manual's Nursery reset content.

## The chapter in one line
The nursery is the one room where safety outranks everything, so every standard is built for an exhausted adult at 2 a.m.: a bare crib, everything inside one arm's sweep, and the surfaces skin and a mouth touch cleaned after every use.

## The six zones
The crib and sleep zone, the changing station, the baby clothing zone, the feeding station, the diaper and care backstock, and the books and quiet play zone. Each carries an Inputs list, numbered Steps, and an inspect-and-flag list.

## The nursery Shine layer (nine rules from Chapter 31 plus, with safety leading)
- Safety leads every zone: it is the test every other pass has to satisfy, not a final check.
- The bare crib: one fitted sheet, nothing else over the rail, ever, a number you never round up.
- One arm's sweep: everything a change or a feed needs is touchable without turning your back on the baby.
- Fragrance-free, after every use, nothing put away wet: clean the mouthed and skin-touched surfaces after every single use.
