# Chapter 40 Repurposing Map: The Nursery

## Spine (the safety-and-how-to value, in priority order)
1. The nursery Shine method: safety leads, the bare crib, one arm's sweep, clean after every use.
2. The zone-by-zone centerpiece: the exact inputs and numbered steps for the crib, changing, clothing, feeding, backstock, and play.
3. The safe-sleep standard and the safety layer: cords, weight, the dropped mattress, strapped furniture, locked medicines.
4. The calls that matter: the quilt someone made, the half-station, the unworn outfit, the rejected bottle, the bulk buy.

## Reuse cautions
- Types, not brands, everywhere. No brand names or invented prices. No medical claims beyond established safe-sleep and safe-storage practice.
- Never depict anything in the crib but a fitted sheet, a scented cleaner on a mouthed surface, or weight above the crib.
- No real infant on camera or in images; suggest scale, not a face.
- Every asset closes on the before-and-after signature (friction meter retired for Part 9), the after as the nightly safe state.
- Stay in the nursery: do not reuse kids-bedroom (Ch 39) or primary-bathroom (Ch 41) material.
