# Chapter 40 Visual Storyboard: The Nursery

## Design system
House palette: paper #F7F2E9, panel #FBF7EF, ink #2B2622, terracotta #BC4B2A, honey #DDA63A, slate #3C5A6B, green #6E8B5B (Shine). Fraunces display, Newsreader body, Inter labels. Footer strip: SAFETY LEADS, CRIB BARE, ARM'S SWEEP, NOTHING WET.

## Recurring motif
The "bare crib" cue: a crib holding one tight sheet and nothing else, and an arm reaching every supply without leaving the baby. It ties the room's lesson together and reappears on the method slide.

## Two fences
- Never depict anything inside the crib but a fitted sheet; never depict a scented cleaner on a mouthed surface, a long grabbable cord, or weight stored above the crib.
- Product types only, no brand names or real logos.

## Beats (twelve)
1. Title over the two-state opener.
2. The one room where safety leads.
3. The bare crib: one tight sheet.
4. The six zones overhead plan.
5. The definition: a room an exhausted adult can trust.
6. The method and kit two-panel (figure 3).
7. One arm's sweep at the changing pad.
8. After every use: the pad wiped, the bottle parts drying.
9. Cords short, weight low, medicines locked.
10. Drop the mattress; strap the furniture.
11. The before-and-after room signature, the after as the nightly safe state.
12. Close: one idea to keep, and the Chapter 41 hand-off.

## Carrying it across the set
Every asset closes on the before-and-after room signature from the doorway (the bare crib, the arm's-sweep changing pad, the clipped cords). The friction meter is retired for Part 9. Register warm but sober, safety-first, expert. Previous Ch 39, next Ch 41 The Primary Bathroom.
