# Chapter 40 Teaching Slide Deck Outline: The Nursery

Fifteen slides. House palette; Shine green #6E8B5B. Footer strip: SAFETY LEADS, CRIB BARE, ARM'S SWEEP, NOTHING WET. Product types only. Sober tone.

1. Title: The Nursery. Part Nine, Room-by-Room Playbooks. The one room where safety leads.
2. The problem: the person it protects can reach nothing; the person running it is exhausted.
3. What you will learn: the five outcomes.
4. The rule: safety outranks everything; every standard built for 2 a.m. one-handed.
5. Six zones, not one room: with the overhead plan figure. Start with the crib.
6. The definition: a room an exhausted adult can run one-handed in the dark and trust.
7. The method (its own slide): safety leads, the bare crib, one arm's sweep, after every use. Figure 3, method and kit.
8. The bare crib: one fitted sheet, nothing else, ever. A number you never round up.
9. One arm's sweep: the changing station and the feeding station, everything touchable.
10. After every use: wipe the pad, dry every bottle part, nothing put away wet.
11. Safety across the room: cords short, weight low, mattress dropped, furniture strapped, medicines locked.
12. The zones: crib, changing, clothing, feeding, backstock, play; the key move for each.
13. The calls: the quilt, the half-station, the unworn outfit, the rejected bottle, the bulk buy.
14. The signature: the before-and-after room card; the after is the nightly safe state. (Friction meter retired.)
15. Close and CTA: one idea to keep. Do one zone tonight, the crib. Next: the primary bathroom.
