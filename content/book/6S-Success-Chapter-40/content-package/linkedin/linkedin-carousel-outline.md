# Chapter 40 LinkedIn Carousel Outline: The Nursery

Twelve slides. Product types only. Sober, safety-first tone.

- Slide 1 (hook): The one room in the house where safety outranks everything.
- Slide 2: Because the person it protects can reach nothing, and the person running it is exhausted.
- Slide 3 (the bare crib): one fitted sheet, nothing else over the rail, ever. A number you never round up.
- Slide 4 (six zones): crib, changing, clothing, feeding, backstock, play. Start with the crib.
- Slide 5 (one arm's sweep): everything a change needs, touchable without turning your back.
- Slide 6 (after every use): wipe the pad after every change; nothing goes away wet.
- Slide 7 (fragrance-free): infant-safe cleaners only; plain water on a mouthed rail.
- Slide 8 (cords and weight): cords short and clipped back; nothing heavy above the crib.
- Slide 9 (drop the mattress): the day they pull to stand, the mattress drops to its lowest setting.
- Slide 10 (do not buy ahead): babies skip sizes and reject teats; one size ahead, two packs at most.
- Slide 11 (standard): a room an exhausted adult can run one-handed in the dark and trust.
- Slide 12 (CTA): Do one zone tonight, the crib. Chapter 40. Next: the primary bathroom.
