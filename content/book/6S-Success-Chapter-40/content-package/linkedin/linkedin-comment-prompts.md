# Chapter 40 LinkedIn Comment Prompts: The Nursery

1. Is your crib truly bare, one fitted sheet and nothing else, or has something crept back over the rail?
2. Can you reach every diaper, wipe, and cream from the changing pad without turning your back on the baby?
3. Do you clean the changing pad after every change, or save it for the weekly reset?
4. Are the monitor and blind cords looped short and clipped an arm's length back from the crib?
5. What did you buy ahead for the nursery that your baby skipped straight past?
