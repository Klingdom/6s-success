# The Nursery: Safe Sleep, One Arm's Sweep, After Every Use

*A LinkedIn article from Chapter 40 of 6S Success: Home Edition.*

The nursery is the one room in this book where getting it wrong is not a matter of an untidy shelf. It is the room where safety outranks everything, because the person it is built for can reach nothing, hold nothing, and ask for nothing, and the person running it is usually exhausted and working one-handed at two in the morning.

> The nursery is the one room in the house where safety outranks everything else, and where the person using it is usually exhausted and working one-handed at two in the morning. So every standard here is built for that person and that hour rather than for a tidy shelf: the crib holds one fitted sheet and nothing else, ever; everything a change or a feed needs sits inside one arm's sweep so you never turn your back on the baby; and the surfaces a mouth and bare skin touch get cleaned after every single use, with fragrance-free, infant-safe cleaners and nothing put away wet. In this room you do not round a safety number up, and you do not leave the baby to fetch a thing you forgot to keep in reach.

## Six zones, not one room

> The nursery is six small zones, not one big job: the crib and sleep zone, the changing station, the baby clothing zone, the feeding station, the diaper and care backstock, and the books and quiet play zone. Do them one at a time, and start with the crib, because it is the quickest zone in the room and the one where getting it wrong matters most.

## The bare crib

> The crib is the one surface in the house where your baby is left alone, so it holds a fitted sheet pulled tight to the corners and nothing else, ever. No bumper, no pillow, no loose blanket, no plush toy, and nothing draped over the top rail, because loose bedding is a suffocation risk to an infant who cannot roll off it.

## One arm's sweep

> Every task in this room is done one-handed, with your other hand on the baby, so nothing a task needs may sit outside your arm's sweep. The diapers, the wipes, and the cream all have to be touchable from the changing pad without moving your feet or lifting your hand, because the moment you turn to reach a shelf is the moment a baby rolls off the pad.

## Safety leads every zone

> Safety is not a pass at the end in this room, it runs through every zone, because the person it protects can reach nothing and ask for nothing. Cords on the monitor, the blind, and the feeding appliances are looped short and clipped an arm's length back from the crib, so nothing can be grabbed or pull boiling water down.
