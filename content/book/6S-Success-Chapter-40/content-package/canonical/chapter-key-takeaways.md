# Chapter 40 Key Takeaways: The Nursery

- The nursery is the one room where safety outranks everything, so safety leads every zone rather than coming last.
- Every standard is built for an exhausted adult working one-handed at two in the morning.
- Six zones, one at a time: the crib and sleep zone, the changing station, the baby clothing zone, the feeding station, the diaper and care backstock, and the books and quiet play zone. Start with the crib.
- The bare crib is a number you never round up: one fitted sheet pulled tight, and nothing else over the rail, ever, not for a cold night and not for a gift.
- Everything a change or a feed needs sits inside one arm's sweep, so you never turn your back on the baby to fetch a thing.
- Clean the surfaces skin and a mouth touch after every single use, with fragrance-free, infant-safe cleaners, and put nothing away wet.
- Cords looped short and clipped back, nothing heavy above the crib, the mattress dropped when the baby can pull to stand, tall furniture strapped, creams and medicines in a lockable box up high.
- Do not buy ahead: babies skip sizes and reject teats, and the room fills with stock for a baby who never arrived in that shape.
- Close on a before-and-after photo; here the after is the safe state the room resets to every night.
