# Chapter 40 CTA and Companion Resources: The Nursery

## Primary call to action
Do one zone tonight, the crib, and give the person who runs this room at two in the morning a room they can trust without looking.

## Companion resources
- **The Nursery Reset card** (printable and phone wallpaper: the six zones, the done-looks-like for each, and the bare-crib, one-arm's-sweep, after-every-use order of work).
- **The Nursery Kit list** (printable: the fragrance-free cleaners, tools, and safe-storage types the room needs, grouped by job, with the bare-crib, anti-tip, and cord-length notes that matter here).
- **The Safe-Sleep Standard card** (a single card for the crib: one fitted sheet, nothing else over the rail, ever, taped where a helper can see it).

## Register and fences
- Warm, calm, expert, and unflinching about safety. Product types only, never brand names. No hype, no invented statistics or prices. No medical advice beyond established safe-sleep and safe-storage practice.
- Friction meter retired for Part 9; close on the before-and-after room signature, with the after as the nightly safe state.
- Previous: Chapter 39, The Kids Bedroom. Next: Chapter 41, The Primary Bathroom.

## Words to avoid
- No brand names (product types only). Never soften the bare-crib rule. Never place anything heavy above the crib or a scented cleaner on a mouthed surface.
