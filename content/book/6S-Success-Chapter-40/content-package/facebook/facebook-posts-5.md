# Chapter 40 Facebook Posts: The Nursery

Five short standalone posts.

## 1. The bare crib
The crib holds one fitted sheet and nothing else, ever. No bumper, no pillow, no loose blanket, no plush, because loose bedding is a suffocation risk to a baby who cannot roll off it. This is the one standard you never round up, not even for a cold night.

## 2. One arm's sweep
Every change in a nursery is done one-handed, with your other hand on the baby. So the diapers, wipes, and cream must all be touchable from the pad without moving your feet, because the moment you turn to reach a shelf is the moment a baby rolls off it.

## 3. Nothing goes away wet
Wash the bottle parts, sterilize them, and stand every one separately to air dry, because a damp valve in a closed bin is where mould starts. Wipe the changing pad after every change. Fragrance-free cleaners only, and nothing in this room put away wet.

## 4. Drop the mattress
A crib mattress left high after your baby can pull to stand puts the rail below their chest, and the fall is over the side onto a hard floor. The day they pull up is the day the mattress drops to its lowest setting. A two-minute job that closes a predictable fall.

## 5. Do not buy ahead
Babies skip sizes and reject teats, and the nursery fills with stock bought for a baby who never arrived in that shape. Buy no more than one size ahead and two packs at most, whatever the price, and move outgrown stock out fast. Chapter 40. Next: the primary bathroom.
