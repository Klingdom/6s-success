# Facebook Group Discussion Post: The Nursery

For a parenting or new-baby group. A calm tip, an open question, a soft close.

---

A nursery tip that is really a safety habit.

In the nursery, cleaning and safety are the same job, because the person it protects can reach nothing and the person running it is exhausted at two in the morning. So three rules run through the whole room.

The crib holds one fitted sheet and nothing else, ever, no bumper, pillow, blanket, or plush, because loose bedding is a suffocation risk to a baby who cannot roll off it. This is the one standard you never round up, even for a cold night.

Everything a change needs sits inside one arm's sweep, so you never turn your back on the baby to fetch a wipe. And the surfaces a mouth and bare skin touch, the changing pad, the bottle parts, the mouthed rail, get cleaned after every use with fragrance-free cleaners, and nothing goes away wet, because a damp valve in a closed bin is where mould starts.

Is your crib truly bare, one fitted sheet and nothing else, or has something crept back over the rail?
