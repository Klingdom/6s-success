2/
"The nursery is the one room in the house where safety outranks everything else, and where the person using it is usually exhausted and working one-handed at two in the morning."

(181 chars)

3/
So every standard is built for that person and that hour: the crib holds one fitted sheet and nothing else ever, everything sits inside one arm's sweep, and the mouthed surfaces are cleaned after every use.

(209 chars)

---

4/
"The nursery is six small zones, not one big job: the crib and sleep zone, the changing station, the baby clothing zone, the feeding station, the diaper and care backstock, and the books and quiet play zone."

(211 chars)

---

5/
Start with the crib. It is one armful of work, and the standard is a number you cannot argue with: one fitted sheet, and nothing else over the rail.

(151 chars)

---

6/
"The crib is the one surface in the house where your baby is left alone, so it holds a fitted sheet pulled tight to the corners and nothing else, ever."

(155 chars)

---

7/
No bumper, no pillow, no loose blanket, no plush, because loose bedding is a suffocation risk to an infant who cannot roll off it. This is the one standard you never round up, not even for a cold night.

(205 chars)

---

8/
"Every task in this room is done one-handed, with your other hand on the baby, so nothing a task needs may sit outside your arm's sweep."

(140 chars)

---

9/
The diapers, the wipes, and the cream are all touchable from the pad without moving your feet, because the moment you turn to reach a shelf is the moment a baby rolls off the pad.

(182 chars)

---

10/
"The surfaces that matter most in a nursery are the ones a mouth and bare skin touch, and they are cleaned after every single use." Wipe the pad after every change.

(168 chars)

---

11/
Wash the bottle parts, sterilize them, and stand every one separately to air dry, because a damp valve in a closed bin is where mould starts. Fragrance-free throughout, and nothing goes away wet.

(199 chars)

---

12/
"Safety is not a pass at the end in this room, it runs through every zone." Cords looped short and clipped back, nothing heavy above the crib, medicines locked in a box up high.

(181 chars)

---

13/
Drop the crib mattress the day the baby can pull to stand, strap the chest and the ledge to the wall, and do not buy ahead, because babies skip sizes and reject teats.
