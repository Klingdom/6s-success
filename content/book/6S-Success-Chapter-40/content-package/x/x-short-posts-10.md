# X Short Posts: The Nursery

Chapter 40 of *6S Success: Home Edition*. Ten standalone posts, each at or under 280 characters counting its number line. Plain numbering.

---

1/
The nursery is the one room where safety outranks everything, because the baby it protects can reach nothing and the person running it is exhausted at 2am. So every standard is built for that person and that hour.

(216 chars)

---

2/
The crib holds one fitted sheet and nothing else, ever. No bumper, no pillow, no loose blanket, no plush, because loose bedding is a suffocation risk to a baby who cannot roll off it. Never round this one up.

(211 chars)

---

3/
Every change in a nursery is done one-handed, with your other hand on the baby. So the diapers, wipes, and cream must all be touchable from the pad without moving your feet. Never turn your back to reach a shelf.

(215 chars)

---

4/
A damp valve in a closed bin is where nursery mould starts. Wash the bottle parts, sterilize them, and stand every one separately to air dry. Wipe the changing pad after every change. Nothing goes away wet.

(209 chars)

---

5/
The day your baby can pull to stand, drop the crib mattress to its lowest setting. Left high, the rail sits below their chest and the fall is over the side. A two-minute job that closes a predictable fall.

(208 chars)

---

6/
Loop the monitor, blind, and appliance cords short and clip them an arm's length back from the crib, so nothing can be grabbed or pull boiling water down. And never store anything heavy on a shelf above the crib.

(215 chars)

---

7/
Keep every cream, gel, and infant medicine in a lockable box up high, out of a standing child's reach. In a nursery, safety is not the last pass. It leads every zone.

(169 chars)

---

8/
Do not buy ahead for the baby. Babies skip sizes and reject teats, so the nursery fills with stock for a baby who never arrived in that shape. One size ahead, two packs at most, whatever the price.

(200 chars)

---

9/
The quilt someone made for the crib cannot go in it. Hang it on the wall above the crib or fold it into the keepsake box, and say so once, plainly, as you show them where you hung it. A bare crib is the safest sleep.

(219 chars)

---

10/
A nursery is not safe because it looks calm and clean. It is safe because the crib holds one sheet, every change sits inside one arm's sweep, and the medicines are locked up high. Chapter 40. Next . Ch 41, The Primary Bathroom.
