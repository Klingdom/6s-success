The game

Each player runs a reset practice on a personal house board, drafting product cards from a central market to complete micro-zone contracts. The real business moat is the strategy: a lean shared core powers many zones, and certified zones pay calm points every round, unless neglect lets them slide back.

How it plays
  • Draft product cards into zone kits (Sort & Straighten).
  • Flip a zone clean and secure its hazard to certify it (Shine & Safety).
  • Lock finished zones so they keep scoring (Standardize).
  • Pay upkeep, or entropy claims your best rooms (Sustain).
Why it sells

  • Highly replayable with 100+ zones and a deep product deck; premium price point.
  • Earns credibility with the hobby and strategy-gamer audience.
