# Chapter 39 Diagram Ideas: The Kids Bedroom

House palette (paper #F7F2E9, panel #FBF7EF, ink #2B2622, terracotta #BC4B2A, honey #DDA63A, slate #3C5A6B, green #6E8B5B for Shine). Warm, illustrated, not photoreal. Product types only, no brands or logos. Spark red reserved only for a true hazard (a frayed nightlight flex, a loose button battery).

## Diagram 1 (opener, figure 1): two states
The kids bedroom before and after, side by side. Before: toys tipped across the floor, the rod too high for the child to reach, bins labelled with words, the desk buried, the launch corner a heap by the door. After: the floor clear enough to lie down on, picture-labelled bins at shoulder height, the rod lowered, a packed bag on a low hook, and a clear path from the pillow to the door.

## Diagram 2 (figure 2): the six zones plan
A warm overhead plan, six numbered zones marked and named: bed and sleep, toy storage, study desk, clothing closet, dresser drawers, school launch. Footer: "one room, six small jobs, start on the floor".

## Diagram 3 (figure 3): the Shine method and kit
Two panels. Left, the method: their height and reading level (a low rod, a shoulder-height bin, a photo label); with them, not for them (a child at a parent's side sorting toys into a dated box); the two-song clean (a music note, a damp cloth on a surface, a hand picking up the floor); a room a child uses alone (a small part going up high, a cleated blind cord, an anti-tip strap into a stud). Right, the kit: a vacuum with three attachments, a microfiber set, a neutral pH cleaner and a floor cleaner and a food-area degreaser, a small detail brush and a lint brush, open toy bins with picture labels, front-facing book bins, child-height hangers, low hooks and a low shoe rack, a dated rotation box, a memory box, and anti-tip anchors. Footer: "their height, with them, short enough to finish".

## Diagram 4 (no HTML twin): the kids room Shine method
A standalone visual: their height and reading level, with them, the two-song clean, a room a child uses alone. Names the next chapter, the nursery.

## Diagram 5 (no HTML twin): how to clean the toy zone
A step ribbon: wipe the rotation box, wipe the bin faces and photo labels, wash the hard toys and dry fully, empty and check each bin, vacuum the skirting corners, vacuum the play floor. Highlight the wash-the-hard-toys and skirting-corners steps.

## Diagram 6 (before/after signature) and the photo brief
The Part 9 signature: a matched before-and-after pair of the floor, the toy bins, and the pillow-to-door path, from the doorway, with the child holding the camera for the after. **Best before-and-after photo brief:** stand in the doorway and frame the floor, the toy bins, and the strip of floor from the bed to the door together in one shot; same spot, angle, and light, before and after; do not tidy before the before (Ch 6); let the CHILD take the after and keep it as the standard (Ch 25). The retired friction meter is not drawn.
