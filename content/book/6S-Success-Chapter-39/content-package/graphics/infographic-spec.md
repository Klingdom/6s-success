# Chapter 39 Infographic Spec: The Kids Bedroom Shine Method

## The single strongest image
The kids-room Shine method plus the kit, because the method (their height and reading level, with them, the two-song clean) is the chapter's centerpiece.

## Anchor string (byte-verbatim)
> The kids bedroom is the first space a child gets to run themselves, so here you build it and clean it at their height and their reading level rather than yours, and you do it with them beside you. Every hook, rod, and bin has to work for a child's reach and be labelled with a picture a child who cannot yet read can follow, because a room built for your reach is a room you will still be tidying when they are fifteen. And a child keeps only what a child chose, so you do the dull cleaning alone but make every keep-or-go call with them standing next to you, and you keep the clean short enough that they can finish it before their attention runs out.

## Palette
paper #F7F2E9, panel #FBF7EF, ink #2B2622, terracotta #BC4B2A, honey #DDA63A, slate #3C5A6B, green #6E8B5B (Shine). Fraunces headings, Inter labels.

## Layout, portrait (1080 x 1350)
- Title: The Kids Bedroom Shine Method.
- Band 1, "their height, their reading level": a low rod, a shoulder-height bin, a photo label.
- Band 2, "with them, not for them": a child sorting toys into a dated box at a parent's side.
- Band 3, "the two-song clean": a music note, surfaces during song one, floor during song two.
- Band 4, "a room a child uses alone": choke-sized up high, a cleated cord, an anti-tip strap.
- Footer strip: their height, with them, short enough to finish.

## Layout, landscape (1200 x 675)
Two panels: the method on the left, the kit on the right.

## Rules
Types only, no brands or logos. Never show storage labelled in words a child cannot read, or the keep-or-go calls made without the child. No em/en/spaced-hyphen dashes in any label.
