# Chapter 39 Image Generation Prompts: The Kids Bedroom

Warm, illustrated, flat-color editorial house style (paper #F7F2E9 ground, ink #2B2622 line, terracotta/honey/slate/green accents, Shine green #6E8B5B). No photorealism, no brand names or logos, no text baked into the art beyond the noted labels. Product types only.

## Prompt 1 (opener, two states)
A warm flat-illustration of one kids bedroom shown twice, side by side. Left, labelled "before": toys tipped across the floor, a closet rod set too high for a child, bins labelled with written words, a buried desk, a heap of bags by the door. Right, labelled "after": the floor clear, open bins at a child's shoulder height each with a photo label, a lowered rod, a packed bag on a low hook, and a clear path to the door.

## Prompt 2 (six zones plan)
A warm overhead floor plan of a kids bedroom, gently shaded, with six numbered and labelled zones: bed and sleep, toy storage, study desk, clothing closet, dresser drawers, school launch. Caption strip at the foot: "one room, six small jobs, start on the floor".

## Prompt 3 (method and kit, two panels)
A two-panel warm infographic. Left "the method": a low rod and a shoulder-height bin with a photo label, a child at a parent's side sorting toys into a dated box, a music note over a damp cloth and a hand picking up the floor, and a small part going up onto a high shelf beside a cleated blind cord and an anti-tip strap. Right "the kit": labelled type icons for a vacuum with three attachments, a microfiber set, a neutral pH cleaner and a floor cleaner and a food-area degreaser, a detail brush and a lint brush, open toy bins with picture labels, front-facing book bins, child-height hangers, low hooks, a low shoe rack, a dated rotation box, a memory box, and anti-tip anchors.

## Prompt 4 (toy-zone step ribbon)
A warm step-ribbon titled "how to clean the toy zone": wipe the rotation box, wipe the bin faces and photo labels, wash the hard toys and dry fully, empty and check each bin, vacuum the skirting corners, vacuum the play floor. The wash-the-hard-toys and skirting-corners steps highlighted in the Shine green.

## Prompt 5 (before-and-after signature)
A warm matched pair of the kids bedroom, the floor and the toy bins and the pillow-to-door path, shot from the doorway, before on the left and after on the right, with a small camera icon in a child's hand over the after and the labels "the before, you" and "the after, the child".
