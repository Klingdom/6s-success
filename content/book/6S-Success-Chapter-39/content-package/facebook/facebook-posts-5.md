# Chapter 39 Facebook Posts: The Kids Bedroom

Five short standalone posts.

## 1. Build it at their height
A child cannot maintain what a child cannot reach. Lower the rod so a five year old reaches a hanger without a stool, drop the hooks, and keep the bins at shoulder height. Build it for your reach and you will still be tidying it when they are fifteen.

## 2. Labels are pictures, not words
A child who cannot yet read cannot use a bin labelled in your handwriting. Put a photo of the contents on the front of every bin and drawer, and a small child can put the blocks back in the right place without asking anyone. Words are for you; pictures are for them.

## 3. Clean it with them, not for them
It is quicker to clean the room while they are at school, and it costs you the point. A child will not maintain a room they did not choose, and they notice exactly which animal went missing. Do the dull parts alone, but make every keep-or-go call with the child beside you.

## 4. The mould is in the water bottle lid
The dirtiest thing in a kids room is the water bottle lid and the lunchbox seals, where mould grows unseen. Scrub the lid threads and the drinking valve with a detail brush, run it into the lunchbox seal groove, rinse well, and stand both open to dry, never closed wet.

## 5. Make the reset a two-song job
A child sustains only what they can finish. Make their share of the cleaning a two song job: one song for surfaces, one song for the floor, and stop when the music does. A job with an end they can see is a job they will do again next week. Chapter 39. Next: the nursery.
