# Facebook Group Discussion Post: The Kids Bedroom

For a parenting or home group. A useful tip, an open question, a soft close.

---

A kids-room tip that ends the daily tidy-up.

The reason you are still tidying your child's room is usually that it was built for your reach, not theirs. A child cannot hang a coat on a rod they cannot reach, or use a bin labelled in handwriting they cannot read. So lower the rod, drop the hooks, keep the bins at shoulder height, and make every label a photo of what goes there. Then a small child can put the room away without calling down the stairs.

Two more that make it stick. First, clean it with them, not for them: do the dull parts alone, but make every keep-or-go call with the child beside you, because a child will not maintain a room they did not choose. Second, keep their share short: make the reset a two song job, one song for surfaces and one for the floor, and stop when the music does.

Is your child's closet rod at their height or yours, and who ends up hanging the clothes?
