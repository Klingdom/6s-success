## Source fidelity
The six zones, their inputs, their step order, and every inspect-and-flag list are folded directly from the Micro Zone Manual's Kids Bedroom content (six zones, each with shine_detail: surfaces, methods, products_used, inspect_as_you_clean) and the room's 35 mapped product types. The reset instructions were carried through faithfully: the strip-vacuum-turn-under-the-bed sequence, the wash-the-hard-toys and hunt-the-skirting-corners toy pass, the bare-wood desk and cable nest, the empty-closet back-wall check, the pull-the-drawers-out dresser, and the water-bottle-lid and lunchbox-seal launch corner. The intro's build-at-their-height principle, the two-song cleaning note, and the do-it-with-them trap are the source of the chapter's three-part layer, and the calls (the unslept animals, the tipped-out set, the schoolwork memory box, the outgrown clothes, who packs the bag) come straight from the Manual's the_call entries.
