# Chapter 39 Editorial Review: The Kids Bedroom

## Verdict
Ready for Phil's review as a draft. The chapter meets the instruction-centric standard: every one of the six zones carries an Inputs list, numbered Steps, and an inspect-and-flag paragraph, and the kids-bedroom Shine layer is distinct from the eight rooms before it.

## What works
- **The layer is genuinely its own, and it is the strongest hook in Part Nine so far.** Building at the child's height and reading level, sorting with them rather than for them, and the two-song clean are all specific to a room a child is meant to run, and none of them repeats the primary-bedroom (sleep and air) or guest-bedroom (freshness and overflow) layers. The reframe that a room built for your reach is one you will still be tidying at fifteen lands hard.
- **The Manual reset instructions are folded in faithfully.** The strip-and-turn-the-mattress bed pass, the wash-the-hard-toys and hunt-the-skirting-corners toy pass, the bare-wood desk, and the water-bottle-lid launch corner are carried straight from the source shine_detail and elevated into numbered procedures.
- **The child-ownership thread is handled with unusual care.** The dated-box method, "nothing leaves the house the day it leaves the bed", and letting the child take the after photo turn a common parenting friction into clean, respectful instruction.
- **Safety is correctly elevated.** The chapter states plainly that this is the most important safety pass in the house because a small child is in the room without you, and the toilet-roll-tube test, the cleated cords, and the strapped dresser are all resolved in the Safety pass per the Shine-finds-Safety-fixes rule.

## Watch items (minor, non-blocking)
- The chapter is the longest room playbook so far (about 6,125 words), because it carries both a full six-zone instruction set and a heavier-than-usual set of calls; the length cap is lifted for Part 9, so this is acceptable, and the Calls section is the easiest trim if Phil wants tighter.
- The two-song clean is a genuinely novel idea and could carry even more weight; it is currently one reframe among three, which is the right balance for a playbook but worth remembering as a strong standalone social hook.

## Consistency checks
- Nine Shine rules recapped, not re-taught: PASS.
- Friction meter retired; before-and-after signature closes, with the child taking the after: PASS.
- Product types only: PASS.
- Hand-off to Chapter 40 (the nursery) present and light: PASS.

## Recommendation
Approve as draft. No structural edits required.
