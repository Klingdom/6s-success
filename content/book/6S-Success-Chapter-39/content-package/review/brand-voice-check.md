# Chapter 39 Brand Voice Check: The Kids Bedroom

## Voice target
Warm, practical, expert, and respectful of the child's ownership of the room, never bossy about parenting. The register of a knowledgeable friend who has set up a child's room that the child actually keeps.

## Scan results
- **Product types only, no brands**: PASS. A brand-name scan across the whole package (common household and kids-storage brands) returned zero hits. Everything is a type: compact vacuum with attachments, colour-coded microfiber cloth set, neutral pH multi-surface cleaner, floor-material-compatible cleaner, food-area degreaser, small detail brush set, lint or clothes brush, open toy bins with picture labels, front-facing book bins, matching child-height hanger set, anti-tip anchor and hardware.
- **No hype, no invented numbers or prices**: PASS. No statistics, no fabricated study claims, no dollar figures. The "days between washes plus two" is a method, not a cited figure.
- **Respectful of the child, not preachy to the parent**: PASS. The child is treated as the owner of the room ("a child keeps only what a child chose", "let the child take the after"), and the parent guidance is practical rather than judgemental.
- **Dash and punctuation house style**: PASS. Middot for label separators; no em/en dashes; no prose spaced-hyphen separators.
- **Inclusive, non-gendered**: PASS. "A child", "a five year old", "they" throughout; no gendered assumptions about the child or the parent.

## Consistency with the series
- The zone-by-zone instruction voice matches Chapters 31 to 38.
- The one-idea-to-keep close and the before-and-after signature match the Part 9 pattern, with the child-takes-the-after variation noted.
- The two fences held everywhere: never label a child's storage in words a child cannot read, never make the keep-or-go calls without the child. The video and image guidance also adds show-hands-and-scale-not-faces for any child on camera.

## Verdict
On voice. No changes needed.
