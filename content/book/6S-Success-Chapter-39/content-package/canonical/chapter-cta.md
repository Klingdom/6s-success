# Chapter 39 CTA and Companion Resources: The Kids Bedroom

## Primary call to action
Do one zone this weekend with your child, the toy zone or the floor, and give them a room they can actually run themselves.

## Companion resources
- **The Kids Room Reset card** (printable and phone wallpaper: the six zones, the done-looks-like for each, and the floor-first, two-song order of work, drawn for a child to follow).
- **The Kids Room Kit list** (printable: the cleaners, tools, and child-scale storage types the room needs, grouped by job, with the picture-label, child-height, and anti-tip notes that matter here).
- **The Two-Song Reset** (a short picture card a child keeps by the door: surfaces during song one, floor during song two, done when the music stops).

## Register and fences
- Warm, practical, expert, and respectful of the child's ownership of the room. Product types only, never brand names. No hype, no invented statistics or prices.
- Friction meter retired for Part 9; close on the before-and-after room signature, with the child taking the after.
- Previous: Chapter 38, The Guest Bedroom. Next: Chapter 40, The Nursery.

## Words to avoid
- No brand names (product types only). Never label a child's storage in words a child cannot read. Never make the keep-or-go calls without the child.
