# Chapter 39 Key Takeaways: The Kids Bedroom

- The kids bedroom is the first room a child runs themselves, so you build it and clean it at their height and reading level, not yours.
- Six zones, one at a time: the bed and sleep zone, the toy storage zone, the study desk, the clothing closet, the dresser drawers, and the school and activity launch zone. Start on the floor, out of the toy storage zone.
- Build it at their height and reading level: lower the rod, keep the bins at shoulder height, put the hooks low, and label everything with a picture a child who cannot read can follow.
- Do it with them, not for them: do the dull cleaning alone, but make every keep-or-go call with the child beside you, because a child keeps only what a child chose.
- Keep the clean short: a child sustains only what they can finish, so make their share a two-song job, floor first, with an end they can see.
- A vacuum with an upholstery, crevice, and brush tool does most of the adult work; the picture labels do the child's.
- Safety is the most important pass here: choke-sized things and button batteries up high, blind cords cleated, the dresser strapped, the pillow-to-door path clear.
- Close on a before-and-after photo, and let the child take the after, because it is their room and their standard.
