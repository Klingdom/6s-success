# Chapter 39 Teleprompter Script: The Kids Bedroom

Short lines, one breath each. Warm and practical.

Here is why you are still tidying your child's room.
You built it for your reach, not theirs.
A child cannot maintain what a child cannot reach.
So build it at their height, and their reading level.
Lower the rod. A five year old should reach a hanger without a stool.
Keep the bins at shoulder height. Drop the hooks.
And label everything with a picture, not a word.
A child who cannot read still needs to find the block bin.
Then clean it with them, not for them.
Do the dull parts alone. The runners. The skirting corners.
But make every keep-or-go call with the child beside you.
A child keeps only what a child chose.
Nothing leaves the house the day it leaves the bed.
The drift goes in a dated box for six weeks.
What nobody asks for by name goes quietly.
And keep their share short.
Make the reset a two song job.
One song for surfaces. One song for the floor.
Stop when the music does.
Start on the floor. It is what everything else is failing onto.
And keep the room safe, because a small child is in here alone.
Choke-sized things go up high. Strap the dresser.
Do one zone this weekend with your child.
And give them a room they can run themselves.
