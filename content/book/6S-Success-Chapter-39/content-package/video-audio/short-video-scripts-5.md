# Chapter 39 Short Video Scripts (5): The Kids Bedroom

Each 30 to 90 seconds. Product types only. Show hands and scale, not faces.

## 1. Build it at their height (40s)
HOOK: Here is why you are still tidying your child's room.
SCRIPT: You built it for your reach, not theirs. A child cannot hang a coat on a rod they cannot reach. Lower the rod so a five year old reaches a hanger without a stool, drop the hooks, and keep the bins at shoulder height. Then a small child can put the room away without calling down the stairs.
ON-SCREEN: Build it at their height.
CTA: Chapter 39. Lower the rod this weekend.

## 2. Labels are pictures, not words (30s)
HOOK: Your kid's bins are labelled wrong.
SCRIPT: A child who cannot yet read cannot use your tidy handwriting. Put a photo of the contents on the front of every bin. A picture of blocks on the block bin, and a small child puts the blocks back in the right place without asking anyone. Words are for you. Pictures are for them.
CTA: Chapter 39, the toy zone.

## 3. Sort it with them, not for them (40s)
HOOK: Stop decluttering your kid's room while they are at school.
SCRIPT: It is quicker, and it costs you the point. A child will not maintain a room they did not choose, and they notice exactly which animal went missing. Make every keep-or-go call with them beside you, and put the drift in a dated box for six weeks. What is not asked for goes quietly.
CTA: Chapter 39. Sort it together.

## 4. The mould is in the bottle lid (30s)
HOOK: The dirtiest thing in your kid's room is not the floor.
SCRIPT: It is the water bottle lid and the lunchbox seals, where mould grows unseen. Scrub the lid threads and the drinking valve with a detail brush, run it into the lunchbox seal groove, rinse well, and stand both open to dry, never closed wet.
CTA: Chapter 39, the launch zone.

## 5. Make the reset a two-song job (35s)
HOOK: Your kid can clean their room in two songs.
SCRIPT: A child sustains only what they can finish. So make their share a two song job: one song for surfaces with a damp cloth, one song for picking the floor up, and stop when the music does. A job with an end they can see is a job they will do again next week.
CTA: Chapter 39. Next: the nursery.
