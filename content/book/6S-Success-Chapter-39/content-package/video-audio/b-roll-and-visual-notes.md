# Chapter 39 B-Roll and Visual Notes: The Kids Bedroom

Warm, natural light. Product types only, no brand labels on screen. Where a child appears, show hands and scale, not faces.

## Visual vocabulary
- The two-state opener: a toy-strewn, adult-height before and a floor-clear, child-height after.
- The recurring "at their height" motif: a small hand reaching a low bin with a photo label.

## Core shot list (seven shots)
1. The bed: stripping it, vacuuming the mattress and turning it, and clearing the long strip of dust and lost toys under the slats.
2. The toy zone: washing hard toys in warm soapy water and drying them on a towel, then the crevice tool tight into the skirting corners.
3. Setting up: a rod being lowered, bins placed at shoulder height, and a photo label pressed onto a bin front.
4. Sorting with the child: toys going into a dated box, the child picking the keepers, the lid dated with a marker.
5. The two-song reset: a phone starting a song, a damp cloth on surfaces, then the floor picked up, and the music stopping.
6. The launch zone: scrubbing the water bottle lid and the lunchbox seal groove with a detail brush.
7. The safety beat: a small part and a button battery going up onto a high shelf, a blind cord cleated, an anti-tip strap into a stud.

## On-screen text bank (use sparingly)
- Build it at their height.
- Labels are pictures, not words.
- Sort it with them, not for them.
- Make the reset a two-song job.
- The mould is in the bottle lid.

## Lower thirds (rotate)
- Zone 1 to 6, one per segment.
- Do one zone this weekend with your child.

## Editing notes
- Close each cut on the before-and-after room signature from the doorway, with the child holding the camera for the after.
- No presenter required; hands and surfaces only.
