# Chapter 39 YouTube Script (6 to 8 min): The Kids Bedroom

Alternating HOST and VISUAL. Product types only. Show hands and scale, not faces.

HOST: If you are still tidying your child's room every day, the room is built wrong. Today we set up a room a child can actually run themselves.
VISUAL: Two-state opener, toy-strewn before and floor-clear after. A small hand reaching a low bin.

HOST: Whether a child can keep their room is not willpower, it is height and reading level. So we build it at the child's scale, clean it with them, and keep their share short enough to finish.
VISUAL: A rod being lowered; a photo label pressed onto a bin.

HOST: Six zones. Start on the floor, out of the toy zone, because the floor is what everything else has been failing onto. Wash the hard toys and dry them, then vacuum tight into the skirting corners where the small parts hide.
VISUAL: Hard toys washed and drying; the crevice tool in the corners.

HOST: The bed: strip it, vacuum the mattress and turn it, and clear the long strip under the slats. The desk: take the top to bare wood, lift the glue and marker, and clean the cable nest.
VISUAL: The upholstery tool on the mattress; the degreaser on dried glue.

HOST: Then set it up at their height. Lower the rod so they reach a hanger without a stool. Keep the bins at shoulder height. And put a photo on every bin and drawer.
VISUAL: The lowered rod; picture labels going on.

HOST: Now sort it with them, not for them. Make every keep-or-go call with the child beside you, and put the drift in a dated box for six weeks. And make the reset a two song job, floor first.
VISUAL: Toys into a dated box; a song starting; the floor picked up.

HOST: Safety is the most important pass here. Choke-sized things up high, button batteries included, cords cleated, the dresser strapped. Do one zone this weekend with your child. Next: the nursery.
VISUAL: A small part going up high; an anti-tip strap; the before-and-after signature, child taking the after. End card.
