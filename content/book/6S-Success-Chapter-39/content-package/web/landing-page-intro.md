# Chapter 39 Landing Page Intro: The Kids Bedroom

The kids bedroom is the first space in the house a child gets to run themselves, and that changes the whole job. Whether a five year old can put their own blocks away, hang their own coat, and find their own socks is not about willpower. It is about whether the room was built for their reach or for yours.

This chapter breaks the kids bedroom into six small zones and gives you, for each one, the exact inputs to gather and the numbered steps to clean and shine it: the bed and sleep zone, the toy storage zone, the study desk, the clothing closet, the dresser drawers, and the school and activity launch zone. It shows you how to build the room at the child's height and reading level (the rod down, the bins low, every label a picture), how to sort it with them rather than for them so they will actually keep it, and how to make their share of the cleaning a two-song job with an end they can see.

Product types only, no brands. Start on the floor, where everything else in the room is failing onto.
