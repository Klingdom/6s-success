**The Kids Bedroom: a room a child can run themselves.**

The kids bedroom is the first space a child gets to run themselves, so whether they can keep it is not about willpower. It is about whether the room was built for their reach or for yours.

Chapter 39 gives the exact inputs and numbered steps for each of the room's six zones, plus the three moves that make it work: build it at the child's height with picture labels, make every keep-or-go call with them rather than for them, and keep their share of the clean short enough to finish.
