# Chapter 39 Visual Storyboard: The Kids Bedroom

## Design system
House palette: paper #F7F2E9, panel #FBF7EF, ink #2B2622, terracotta #BC4B2A, honey #DDA63A, slate #3C5A6B, green #6E8B5B (Shine). Fraunces display, Newsreader body, Inter labels. Footer strip: THEIR HEIGHT, WITH THEM, SHORT ENOUGH TO FINISH.

## Recurring motif
The "at their height" cue: a low rod, a shoulder-height bin, and a photo label a small hand reaches. It ties the room's lesson together and reappears on the method slide.

## Two fences
- Never depict storage labelled in words a child cannot read; never depict the keep-or-go calls being made without the child.
- Product types only, no brand names or real logos.

## Beats (thirteen)
1. Title over the two-state opener.
2. The first room a child runs themselves.
3. Their height: the lowered rod, the low bins.
4. Picture labels: a photo on every bin.
5. The six zones overhead plan.
6. The method and kit two-panel (figure 3).
7. With them: a child at your side for the calls.
8. The dated box for the drift.
9. The two-song clean: floor first.
10. The bottle lid and lunchbox seals.
11. Safety: choke-sized up high, the dresser strapped.
12. The before-and-after room signature, the child taking the after.
13. Close: one idea to keep, and the Chapter 40 hand-off.

## Carrying it across the set
Every asset closes on the before-and-after room signature from the doorway (the clear floor, the low bins, the pillow-to-door lane), with the child taking the after.
