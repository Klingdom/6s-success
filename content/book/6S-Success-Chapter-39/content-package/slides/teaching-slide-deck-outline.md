# Chapter 39 Teaching Slide Deck Outline: The Kids Bedroom

Fifteen slides. House palette; Shine green #6E8B5B. Footer strip: THEIR HEIGHT, WITH THEM, SHORT ENOUGH TO FINISH. Product types only.

1. Title: The Kids Bedroom. Part Nine, Room-by-Room Playbooks. The first room a child runs themselves.
2. The problem: built for your reach, tidied by you forever.
3. What you will learn: the five outcomes.
4. The rule: build and clean it at the child's height and reading level, with them.
5. Six zones, not one room: with the overhead plan figure. Start on the floor.
6. The definition: a room a child can run and reset themselves.
7. The method (its own slide): their height and reading level, with them, the two-song clean. Figure 3, method and kit.
8. Their height: lower the rod, drop the hooks, bins at shoulder height, picture labels.
9. With them: every keep-or-go call with the child; the dated box for six weeks.
10. The two-song clean: floor first, one song surfaces, one song floor, stop with the music.
11. The zones: bed, toys, desk, closet, drawers, launch corner; the key move for each.
12. Safety: choke-sized up high, cords cleated, dresser strapped, the dark path clear.
13. The calls: the animals, the tipped-out set, the schoolwork box, who packs the bag.
14. The signature: the before-and-after room card, the child takes the after. (Friction meter retired.)
15. Close and CTA: one idea to keep. Do one zone this weekend with your child. Next: the nursery.
