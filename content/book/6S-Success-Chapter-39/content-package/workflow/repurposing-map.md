# Chapter 39 Repurposing Map: The Kids Bedroom

## Spine (the how-to-clean value, in priority order)
1. The kids-room Shine method: their height and reading level, with them not for them, the two-song clean.
2. The zone-by-zone centerpiece: the exact inputs and numbered steps for the bed, the toys, the desk, the closet, the drawers, and the launch corner.
3. The child-scale setup: lower the rod, bins at shoulder height, picture labels a child can follow.
4. The calls that matter: the unslept animals, the tipped-out set, the schoolwork box, the outgrown clothes, who packs the bag.

## Reuse cautions
- Types, not brands, everywhere. No brand names or invented prices.
- Never depict storage labelled in words a child cannot read, or the keep-or-go calls made without the child.
- Where a child appears in video or images, show hands and scale, not faces.
- Every asset closes on the before-and-after signature (friction meter retired for Part 9), child taking the after.
- Stay in the kids bedroom: do not reuse guest-bedroom (Ch 38) or nursery (Ch 40) material.
