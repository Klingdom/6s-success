# Chapter 39 Content Package: The Kids Bedroom

The NINTH room playbook of Part Nine (Chapters 31 to 50), built to the instruction-centric template. The chapter CORE is the detailed, step-by-step clean-and-shine instruction for each of the six kids-bedroom zones, with the exact inputs, folded directly from the Micro Zone Manual's Kids Bedroom reset content.

## The chapter in one line
The kids bedroom is the first room a child runs themselves, so you build it and clean it at their height and reading level, with them beside you, and short enough to finish, so the room mostly holds itself.

## The six zones
The bed and sleep zone, the toy storage zone, the study desk, the clothing closet, the dresser drawers, and the school and activity launch zone. Each carries an Inputs list, numbered Steps, and an inspect-and-flag list.

## The kids-bedroom Shine layer (nine rules from Chapter 31 plus)
- Build it at the child's height and reading level: the rod lowered, the bins at shoulder height, every label a picture a child who cannot read can follow.
- Do it with them, not for them: the dull cleaning is yours, but every keep-or-go call is the child's; the drift goes in a dated box for six weeks.
- The two-song clean: keep the child's share short enough to finish, floor first, with an end they can see.
- A room a small child uses alone: choke-sized things and button batteries up high, blind cords cleated, the dresser strapped, the dark path clear.
