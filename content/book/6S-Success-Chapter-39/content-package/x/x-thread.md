Part Nine opens the door a child closes: the kids bedroom. Like the rooms before it, this chapter gives the exact inputs and steps for each zone. But this room a child, not you, is meant to run, and that changes everything.

"The kids bedroom is the first space a child gets to run themselves, so here you build it and clean it at their height and their reading level rather than yours, and you do it with them beside you."

(201 chars)

3/
A room built for your reach is a room you will still be tidying when they are fifteen, because a child cannot maintain what a child cannot reach, and will not maintain a home they did not choose.

(198 chars)

---

4/
"The kids bedroom is six small zones, not one big job: the bed and sleep zone, the toy storage zone, the study desk, the clothing closet, the dresser drawers, and the school and activity launch zone."

(203 chars)

---

5/
Start on the floor, out of the toy zone, because the floor is what everything else in this room is failing onto. Clear it and the room stops looking like one enormous job.

(174 chars)

---

6/
"Every hook, rod, and bin in this room has to work at the child's height and their reading level, not yours." The rod comes down so a five year old reaches a hanger without a stool.

(184 chars)

---

7/
And every label is a photo of what goes there, not a word, so a child who cannot yet read can still put the blocks back in the right bin. Words are for you; pictures are for them.

(182 chars)

---

8/
"A child will not maintain a home they did not choose, and they will notice exactly which animal went missing." So make every keep-or-go call with the child standing next to you.

(181 chars)

---

9/
Nothing leaves the house the day it leaves the bed. The drift goes into a dated box for six weeks, and what nobody asks for by name goes quietly. The box does the deciding, not the argument.

(193 chars)

---

10/
"A child sustains only what they can finish while their attention lasts." So make their share a two song job: one song for surfaces, one song for the floor, and stop when the music does.

(190 chars)

---

11/
The dirtiest thing in a kids room is not the floor, it is the water bottle lid and the lunchbox seals, where mould grows unseen. Scrub the valve and threads with a detail brush, rinse, and dry open.

(202 chars)

---

12/
Safety is the most important pass here, because a small child is in this room without you. Anything that fits through a toilet roll tube goes up high, button batteries included. Strap the dresser.
