# X Short Posts: The Kids Bedroom

Chapter 39 of *6S Success: Home Edition*. Ten standalone posts, each at or under 280 characters counting its number line. Plain numbering.

---

1/
The kids bedroom is the first room a child runs themselves, so build it at their height, not yours. A child cannot maintain what a child cannot reach. Lower the rod, drop the hooks, bins at shoulder height.

(209 chars)

---

2/
A child who cannot yet read cannot use a bin labelled in your handwriting. Put a photo of the contents on the front of every bin and drawer, and a small child can put the blocks back without asking anyone.

(208 chars)

---

3/
Stop decluttering your kid's room while they are at school. A child will not maintain a room they did not choose, and they notice which animal went missing. Make every keep-or-go call with them beside you.

(208 chars)

---

4/
Nothing leaves the house the day it leaves the bed. The drift goes into a dated box for six weeks. What nobody asks for by name goes quietly; what they ask for by name comes back. The box decides, not the argument.

(217 chars)

---

5/
A child sustains only what they can finish. Make the reset a two song job: one song for surfaces, one song for the floor, stop when the music does. A job with an end they can see is a job they will do again.

(210 chars)

---

6/
Start on the floor in a kids room, out of the toy zone, because the floor is what everything else is failing onto. Clear it and the strip under the bed, the closet floor, and under the desk all get easier to judge.

(217 chars)

---

7/
The dirtiest thing in a kids room is the water bottle lid and the lunchbox seals, where mould grows unseen. Scrub the lid threads and the drinking valve with a detail brush, rinse, and stand both open to dry.

(211 chars)

---

8/
Strap the dresser. An unanchored chest tips forward the moment a child opens the drawers and climbs them. Fix an anti-tip strap to a wall stud and keep the top bare of anything worth climbing for.

(199 chars)

---

9/
Anything in a kids room that fits through a toilet roll tube is choke sized and goes up high, out of a crawler's reach, button batteries included. It is the most important safety rule in the house.

(200 chars)

---

10/
