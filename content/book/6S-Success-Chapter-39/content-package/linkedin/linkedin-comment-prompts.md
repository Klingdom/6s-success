# Chapter 39 LinkedIn Comment Prompts: The Kids Bedroom

1. Is your child's closet rod at their height or yours? Who ends up hanging the clothes?
2. Do you label kids' storage with words or with pictures? What changed when you switched?
3. Be honest: do you make the keep-or-go calls with your child, or quietly while they are at school?
4. When did you last scrub the water bottle lid, where the mould you cannot see is growing?
5. Is your child's dresser strapped to the wall? It is the piece most likely to tip when a drawer is climbed.
