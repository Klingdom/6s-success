# A Room a Child Can Run Themselves

*A 6S Success: Home Edition newsletter, from Chapter 39, The Kids Bedroom.*

Hi friend,

We have done the rooms adults sleep in. This week, the first room a child gets to run themselves, which changes the whole job.

**A room built for your reach is a room you will still be tidying when they are fifteen, because a child cannot maintain what a child cannot reach, and a child will not maintain a home they did not choose.**

The room breaks into six zones, each with its exact inputs and numbered steps: the bed and sleep zone, the toy storage zone, the study desk, the clothing closet, the dresser drawers, and the school and activity launch zone.

## To try this week

Do the toy zone with your child. Wash the hard toys in warm soapy water and dry them fully, wipe each bin inside and check the base, vacuum the skirting corners where the small parts hide, and put every toy back in an open bin at shoulder height with a photo of the contents on the front. Make the keep-or-go calls with your child beside you, and put the drift in a dated box for six weeks.
