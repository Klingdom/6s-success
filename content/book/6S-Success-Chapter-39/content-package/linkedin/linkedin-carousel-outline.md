# Chapter 39 LinkedIn Carousel Outline: The Kids Bedroom

Twelve slides. Product types only.

- Slide 1 (hook): The first room a child gets to run themselves. Build it for their reach, or tidy it forever.
- Slide 2: Whether a child can keep it is not willpower. It is whether it was built for their height or yours.
- Slide 3 (their height): lower the rod, drop the hooks, keep the bins at shoulder height.
- Slide 4 (picture labels): a child who cannot read cannot use your handwriting. Every label is a photo.
- Slide 5 (six zones): bed, toys, desk, closet, drawers, launch corner. Start on the floor.
- Slide 6 (with them): make every keep-or-go call with the child beside you. Owned is kept.
- Slide 7 (the box): the drift goes in a dated box for six weeks. What is not asked for goes quietly.
- Slide 8 (two-song clean): one song surfaces, one song floor, stop when the music does.
- Slide 9 (the bottle lid): the mould is in the water bottle lid. Scrub the valve and threads.
- Slide 10 (safety): choke-sized up high, cords cleated, dresser strapped, the dark path clear.
- Slide 11 (standard): a room a child can run and reset themselves.
- Slide 12 (CTA): Do one zone this weekend with your child. Chapter 39. Next: the nursery.
