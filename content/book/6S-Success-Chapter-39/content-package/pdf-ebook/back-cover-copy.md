# Chapter 39 Back Cover Copy: The Kids Bedroom

## Back-cover text
The kids bedroom is the first room a child gets to run themselves, so whether they can keep it is not about willpower. It is about whether the room was built for their reach or for yours.

The Kids Bedroom breaks the room into six zones and gives you, for each, the exact inputs to gather and the numbered steps to follow. Build it at the child's height with picture labels. Sort it with them, not for them, because a child keeps only what a child chose. And make the reset a two-song job with an end they can see.

Do one zone this weekend with your child, and give them a room they can actually run themselves.
