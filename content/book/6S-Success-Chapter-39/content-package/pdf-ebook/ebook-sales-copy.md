# Chapter 39 Ebook Sales Copy: The Kids Bedroom

## Headline
The first room a child gets to run themselves. Here is how to build it and clean it at their height, with them, so they can actually keep it.

## The promise
In the room your child is meant to run, cleaning it perfectly while they are at school costs you the point. This chapter gives you the exact inputs and the numbered steps for each of the room's six zones, plus the three moves that make a child able to keep it: build it at their height with picture labels, sort it with them rather than for them, and make the clean a job short enough to finish.

## What you get in this chapter
- The kids-room Shine method: build it at the child's height and reading level; do it with them, not for them; the two-song clean.
- The six per-zone clean procedures, each with its inputs and numbered steps: the bed and sleep zone, the toy storage zone, the study desk, the clothing closet, the dresser drawers, and the school and activity launch zone.
- The picture-label and child-height setup that lets a small child put the room away without asking.
- The dated-box method for sorting toys with a child, and the two-song reset that a child can finish.
- The safety pass that matters most in the house, and the before-and-after signature the child takes.

## The common mistake it fixes
> Doing the whole room while they are at school because it is quicker, building every bin and rod at your height instead of theirs, and labelling with words a child cannot yet read, so you end up with a tidy room the child cannot run and will not maintain, and you are still tidying it when they are fifteen.
