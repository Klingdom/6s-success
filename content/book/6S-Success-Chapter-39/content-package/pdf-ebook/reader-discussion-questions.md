# Chapter 39 Reader Discussion Questions: The Kids Bedroom

1. Is your child's closet rod at their height or yours, and who ends up hanging the clothes?
2. Do you label kids' storage with words or with pictures? What would change if you switched?
3. Do you make the keep-or-go calls with your child, or quietly while they are at school?
4. What toy set gets tipped out and abandoned in your house, and what would the rotation-box test show?
5. When did you last scrub the water bottle lid and the lunchbox seals, where the mould hides?
6. Is your child's dresser strapped to the wall, and is the top bare of anything worth climbing for?
7. How many shirts and socks does your child actually need between washes, plus two?
8. Who packs the school bag in your house, and what would it take to hand it over for good?
9. Are there clothes on the rod being kept for a sibling you cannot yet name a season for?
10. Which zone will you do first with your child this weekend, the toy zone or the floor?
