# The Kids Bedroom

*The kids bedroom is the first space a child gets to run themselves, so cleaning it is not about tidiness, it is about building a room a child can reset on their own, at their height and their reading level, and then keeping it that way with them rather than for them. This playbook points the whole loop at it one zone at a time, and gives you, for each of the six zones, the exact inputs to gather and the precise, step-by-step way to clean and shine it, so a five year old can put the blocks away without calling down the stairs.*

## How to read this chapter

This is a PLAYBOOK, not a method chapter. It applies the six-S loop you already own to one room, decomposed into six micro-zones, and its centerpiece is the per-zone clean-and-shine instructions with the exact inputs. It recaps but does not re-teach the nine-rule Shine method from Chapter 31; the new material is the kids-room layer (build it at the child's height and reading level; do it with them, not for them; the two-song clean) and the numbered steps for each zone.

Products are named as TYPES, never brands. The exhaustive product list is a separate appendix.

The friction meter, which reached its goal at Chapter 30, is retired for Part 9; the chapter closes on a before-and-after room signature, and here the child takes the after.
