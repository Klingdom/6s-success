# Chapter 39 Ebook Description: The Kids Bedroom

The ninth room playbook of Part Nine. The kids bedroom is the first space a child gets to run themselves, so the standard is not a room you cleaned perfectly while they were out: it is a room a child can reset on their own.

This chapter gives the exact inputs and the numbered steps for each of the room's six zones, with three moves running through all of them: build it at the child's height and reading level (the rod lowered, the bins at shoulder height, every label a picture a child who cannot read can follow), do it with them rather than for them (a child keeps only what a child chose, so the dull cleaning is yours but every keep-or-go call is theirs), and the two-song clean (a child sustains only what they can finish, so the floor comes first and the job has an end they can see).

You will learn how to set the room up so a small child can put it away without asking, how to sort toys and clothes with a child using a dated box, and how to make the safety pass that matters most in the house. The chapter closes on a before-and-after room signature, and here the child takes the after.
