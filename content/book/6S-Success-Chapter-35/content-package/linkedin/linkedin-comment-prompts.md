# Chapter 35 LinkedIn Comment Prompts

1. When did you last clean the vents on your television or games console? Be honest.
2. How many decorative cushions do you move onto the floor every time you sit down?
3. Is your television or your tall bookcase strapped to the wall? If you have small children, this matters.
4. Walk your living room from door to door with a full laundry basket. Can you pass without turning sideways?
5. What is the one hidden spot in your living room you have never cleaned, the sofa deck, the top of the bookcase, under the rug?
