# Chapter 35 LinkedIn Carousel Outline: The Living Room

Twelve slides. Product types only.

- Slide 1 (hook): Your living room looks clean. Your TV vents are quietly cooking your electronics.
- Slide 2: In the living room, dust is not just untidy. It is heat, allergens, and lost light.
- Slide 3 (the rule): Clean where nobody looks, and match the tool to the surface.
- Slide 4 (six zones): Sofa, coffee table, media center, bookshelves, side tables and lighting, floor and route. One at a time.
- Slide 5 (dust is heat): A device does not die of age, it dies of dust. The real cleaning is the vents and back panel, not the screen.
- Slide 6 (the screen): Never spray a screen. A dry microfiber cloth in slow straight passes is the whole method.
- Slide 7 (the sofa): Lift every cushion. Vacuum the deck and seams, where the crumbs, coins and pet hair hide.
- Slide 8 (the bulb): A dusty bulb steals the light. Wipe it off and cold, then the shade, then the ring marks under the tray.
- Slide 9 (safety): Strap the two tall, top-heavy things, the television and the bookcase, to the wall.
- Slide 10 (the floor): Keep a route you can cross in the dark carrying a basket. If two adults cannot pass, it is too much furniture.
- Slide 11 (standard): A finished living room is one anyone can walk in, cross in the dark, and sit down in without moving a thing.
- Slide 12 (CTA): Do one zone this weekend, the sofa or the media center. Chapter 35. Next: the family room.
