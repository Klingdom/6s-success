# X Short Posts: The Living Room

Chapter 35 of *6S Success: Home Edition*. Ten standalone posts, each at or under 280 characters counting its number line. Plain numbering.
---

1/
In the living room, dust is not just untidy, it is heat, allergens, and lost light: it mats the vents that cool your electronics, settles deep in the upholstery, and films the bulb. Clean where nobody looks.

(210 chars)

---

2/
Your TV is not dying of age, it is dying of dust. The felt matted across a vent slot traps the heat the fan is moving. The real cleaning here is the vents and the back panel, not the screen. Brush them clear.

(211 chars)

---

3/
Never spray a screen. A dry microfiber cloth in slow straight passes is the whole method. A wet cloth streaks it, a paper towel scratches the coating, a spray runs down inside the bezel. Match the tool to the surface.

(220 chars)

---

4/
The sofa hides its dirt in the seams and the deck under the cushions. Lift every cushion, vacuum both faces, then the crevice tool into the deck. Beat the throw pillows and launder the throws. That is where a sofa is cleaned.

(228 chars)

---

5/
The dust on a bookcase is on the very top and along the top edges of the books, where nobody looks. Work down shelf by shelf, pulling each row of books forward to reach the band behind them.

(193 chars)

---

6/
A dusty bulb steals the light. Wipe it first, off and fully cold, with a dry cloth, then the shade, then the ring marks under the tray. Smallest job in the room, big payoff.

(176 chars)

---

7/
Small children? Strap the two tall, top-heavy things in the living room, the television and the bookcase, to the wall. Both tip forward onto a child who pulls or climbs.

(172 chars)

---

8/
Keep tidying the floor and it keeps failing? Walk it door to door with a full laundry basket. If two adults cannot pass, the room holds more furniture than floor, not more clutter.

(183 chars)

---

9/
Six small zones, not one big job: the sofa, the coffee table, the media center, the bookshelves, the side tables and lighting, the floor and route. One at a time. Start with the sofa or the media center.

(206 chars)

---

10/
A living room is not clean because the middle is tidy. It is clean because you vacuumed the sofa deck, brushed the vents, dusted the bookcase, wiped the bulb, and cleared the route. Chapter 35. Next · Chapter 36, The Family Room.
