1/
Part Nine walks into the room the house relaxes in: the living room. Like the rooms before it, this chapter gives the exact inputs and steps to clean each of its six zones. But here the dirt that matters is the dirt you cannot see.

(234 chars)

---

2/
"In the living room, dust is not just untidy, it is heat, allergens, and lost light: it mats the vents that keep your electronics cool, it settles deep in the upholstery, and it films the bulb until the lamp barely lights."

(226 chars)

---

3/
So you clean two ways the other rooms did not ask for: you clean where nobody looks, and you match the tool to the surface, a dry cloth on a screen, a soft brush on a vent, the upholstery tool deep in the seams.

(214 chars)

---

4/
"The living room is six small zones, not one big job: the sofa and seating, the coffee table, the media center, the bookshelves and display, the side tables and lighting, and the floor and circulation path."

(210 chars)

---

5/
The big one: "A television or a games console does not usually die of age, it dies of dust." Dust matted across a vent slot traps the heat the fan is moving, and a device left to run hot is on its way out.

(208 chars)

---

6/
So the real cleaning at the media center is not the screen, it is the vents and the back panel. Brush and vacuum every slot. If dust has matted solid, loosen it with a soft detail brush, then lift it with the vacuum.

(219 chars)

---

7/
The screen itself: "A dry microfiber cloth in slow, straight passes is the whole method, because a wet cloth streaks it, a paper towel scratches the coating, and a spray runs down inside the bezel." Never spray a screen.

(223 chars)

---

8/
The sofa hides its dirt in the seams and the deck under the cushions. Lift every cushion, vacuum both faces, then run the crevice tool into the deck, back to front. Beat the throw pillows and launder the throws.

(214 chars)

---

9/
Bookshelves: the dust is on the very top of the unit and along the top edges of the books, where nobody looks. Work down shelf by shelf, pulling each row of books forward to reach the band behind them.

(204 chars)

---

10/
A dusty bulb steals the light this table exists to give. Wipe the bulb first, off and fully cold, with a dry cloth, then the shade, then the ring marks hiding under the tray. Smallest job in the room, big payoff.

(216 chars)

---

11/
Safety: strap the two tall, top-heavy things, the television and the bookcase, to the wall, because both tip forward onto a child who pulls or climbs. And keep drinks off the console and away from the power strip.

(217 chars)

---

12/
The floor is the route you cross in the dark carrying something. Clean the whole run, move the furniture legs and do underneath, lift the rug and check the grip. Keep a route wide enough to pass with a basket.

(213 chars)

---

13/
The standard: "A finished living room is one anyone can walk into, cross in the dark, and sit down in without moving a thing." Every seat sittable, vents clear, shelves strapped, a clear route door to door.

(210 chars)
