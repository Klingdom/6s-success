# Chapter 35 Newsletter Short Teaser: The Living Room

A short digest blurb (about 120 words) for a roundup, with a read-the-chapter CTA.

**The Living Room: clean where nobody looks.**

Your living room looks fine after a quick tidy. But the dirt that matters hides: matted in the vents that cook your electronics, deep in the sofa seams, filmed on the bulb that steals the light.

Chapter 35 gives the exact inputs and numbered steps for each of the room's six zones, with two habits running through all of them: clean where nobody looks, and match the tool to the surface, a dry cloth on a screen, a soft brush on a vent, the upholstery tool deep in the seams.
