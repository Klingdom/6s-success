# Newsletter: The Room You Clean Where Nobody Looks

Hi friend,

We have done the rooms that feed the house and the room it eats in. This week we walk into the room the house relaxes in, and cleaning it well is oddly different, because in the living room the dirt that matters is the dirt you cannot see.

The open middle looks fine after a quick tidy. Meanwhile the real trouble is soft and hidden: dust matted across the vents that keep your electronics cool, grime worked deep into the sofa seams, a grey film on the bulb stealing the light, pet hair packed under the rug and behind the sofa.

> In the living room, dust is not just untidy, it is heat, allergens, and lost light: it mats the vents that keep your electronics cool, it settles deep in the upholstery, and it films the bulb until the lamp barely lights. So you clean where nobody looks, and you match the tool to the surface: a dry cloth on a screen, a soft brush on a vent, the upholstery tool deep in the seams.

The room breaks into six zones, each with its exact inputs and numbered steps: the sofa and seating, the coffee table, the media center, the bookshelves and display, the side tables and lighting, and the floor and circulation path.

The single most important one to know:

> A television or a games console does not usually die of age, it dies of dust. The felt of dust matted across a vent slot traps the heat the fan is trying to move, and a device left to run hot is a device on its way out. So in this room the real cleaning is not the screen, it is the vents and the back panel, where the dust you cannot see is quietly cooking the electronics you paid for.

## The media center, done properly (the worked example)

**Inputs:** a compact vacuum with the brush head, a colour-coded microfiber cloth set including a dry cloth kept only for the screen, a neutral pH multi-surface cleaner, a small detail brush, your caddy.

**Steps:**

1. Wipe the top of the unit, the ledge where dust settles thickest.
2. Wipe the screen with the dry screen cloth only, in slow straight passes. Never spray, never a paper towel.
3. Vacuum the whole back panel and bezel with the brush head.
4. Brush and vacuum every vent slot. If dust has matted solid, loosen it with the detail brush first, then lift it.
5. Wipe the shelves, moving devices forward rather than around them.
6. Wipe the cable bundle and the strip, keeping moisture off the sockets, then vacuum the floor behind.
7. Wipe the controllers and remotes, the grime you hand person to person.

## To try this week

Do the media center to the method above, and while the unit is pulled out, strap the television and any tall bookcase to the wall.

> A living room is not clean because the middle is tidy. It is clean because you lifted the cushions and vacuumed the deck, brushed the dust out of the vents, dusted the top of the bookcase and the edges of the books, wiped the bulb, and cleared the route from door to door. Do one zone this weekend, the sofa or the media center, and relax in a room that is clean where it counts.
