# Chapter 35 Teaching Slide Deck Outline: The Living Room

Fifteen slides. House palette; Shine green #6E8B5B. Footer strip: CLEAN WHERE NOBODY LOOKS. Product types only.

1. **Title:** The Living Room. Part Nine, Room-by-Room Playbooks. The room the house relaxes in.
2. **The problem:** A quick tidy cleans the middle. The dirt that matters is the dirt you cannot see.
3. **What you will learn:** the five outcomes.
4. **The rule (dust is heat):** In the living room, dust is not just untidy, it is heat, allergens, and lost light. Clean where nobody looks, and match the tool to the surface.
5. **Six zones, not one room:** the six-zones line with the overhead plan figure.
6. **The definition:** a finished living room, one anyone can walk in, cross in the dark, and sit down in without moving a thing.
7. **The method (its own slide):** dust is heat; match the tool to the surface (dry cloth on a screen, brush on a vent, upholstery tool in the seams); clean where nobody looks. Figure 3, method and kit.
8. **Media center:** the real cleaning is the vents and the back panel, not the screen. Inputs and key steps.
9. **The screen:** never spray, never a paper towel. A dry cloth in slow straight passes.
10. **The sofa:** lift every cushion, vacuum the deck and seams. Inputs and key steps.
11. **The rest (coffee table, bookshelves, side tables, floor):** one line of the key move for each.
12. **Clean-to-inspect-and-flag:** Shine finds the fault, the safety pass fixes it. Strap the TV and the bookcase.
13. **The hard calls:** the pillows, the book, the dead electronics, the medicine, too much furniture.
14. **The signature:** the before-and-after room card. The two photos to take. (Friction meter retired for Part 9.)
15. **Close and CTA:** one idea to keep. Do one zone this weekend. Next: the family room.
