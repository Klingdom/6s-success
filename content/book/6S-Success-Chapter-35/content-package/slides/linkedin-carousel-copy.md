# Chapter 35 LinkedIn Carousel Copy: The Living Room

Thirteen slides, on-slide copy. Shine green accent. Footer: CLEAN WHERE NOBODY LOOKS. Product types only.

- **Slide 1:** Your living room looks clean. Your TV vents are quietly cooking your electronics.
- **Slide 2:** In the living room, dust is not just untidy. It is heat, allergens, and lost light.
- **Slide 3:** So you clean two ways: where nobody looks, and matching the tool to the surface.
- **Slide 4:** Six zones, not one room: sofa, coffee table, media center, bookshelves, side tables and lighting, floor and route.
- **Slide 5:** A device does not die of age. It dies of dust. The real cleaning is the vents and the back panel.
- **Slide 6:** Never spray a screen. A dry microfiber cloth in slow straight passes is the whole method.
- **Slide 7:** The sofa hides its dirt in the seams and the deck. Lift every cushion. Vacuum both faces.
- **Slide 8:** Bookshelves: the dust is on the very top of the unit and the top edges of the books.
- **Slide 9:** A dusty bulb steals the light. Wipe it off and cold, then the shade, then the ring marks.
- **Slide 10:** Strap the two tall, top-heavy things, the television and the bookcase, to the wall.
- **Slide 11:** Keep a route you can cross in the dark carrying a basket. Two adults cannot pass? Too much furniture.
- **Slide 12:** A finished living room is one anyone can walk in, cross in the dark, and sit down in without moving a thing.
- **Slide 13 (CTA):** Do one zone this weekend, the sofa or the media center. Chapter 35. Next: the family room.
