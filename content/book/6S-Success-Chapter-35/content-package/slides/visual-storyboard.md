# Chapter 35 Visual Storyboard: The Living Room

## Design system
House palette: paper #F7F2E9, panel #FBF7EF, ink #2B2622, terracotta #BC4B2A, honey #DDA63A, slate #3C5A6B, green #6E8B5B (Shine). Fraunces display, Newsreader body, Inter labels. Footer strip: CLEAN WHERE NOBODY LOOKS.

## Recurring motif
The "dust is heat" cue: a matted vent slot with faint heat lines, cleared to run cool. It ties the room's lesson together and reappears on the method slide.

## Two fences
- Never spray a screen (dry cloth only). No harsh chemistry on soft furnishings.
- Product types only, no brand names or real logos.

## Beats (thirteen)
1. Title over the two-state opener.
2. The hidden dirt: vents, seams, bulb, under the rug.
3. Clean where nobody looks, and match the tool to the surface.
4. The six zones overhead plan.
5. The definition, a room you can cross in the dark.
6. The method and kit two-panel (figure 3).
7. Media center: vents and back panel, not the screen.
8. The screen, dry cloth only.
9. The sofa: lift every cushion, the deck and seams.
10. Bookshelves, side tables, floor: one key move each.
11. Clean to inspect and flag; strap the TV and the bookcase.
12. The before-and-after room signature.
13. Close: one idea to keep, and the Chapter 36 hand-off.

## Carrying it across the set
Every asset closes on the before-and-after room signature from the doorway (sofa, coffee table, media wall). The friction meter is retired for Part 9. Register warm, practical, expert. Previous Ch 34, next Ch 36 The Family Room.
