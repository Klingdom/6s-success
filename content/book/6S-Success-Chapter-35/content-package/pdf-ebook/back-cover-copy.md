# Chapter 35 Back Cover Copy: The Living Room

## Back-cover text
Your living room looks clean after a quick tidy. But the dirt that matters is hiding: matted in the vents that cook your electronics, deep in the sofa seams, filmed on the bulb that steals the light.

The Living Room breaks the room into six zones and gives you, for each, the exact inputs to gather and the numbered steps to follow. Two habits carry it: clean where nobody looks, and match the tool to the surface, a dry cloth on a screen, a soft brush on a vent, the upholstery tool deep in the seams. Clear the vents so the electronics run cool. Lift every cushion. Strap the two things that tip. Keep a route you can cross in the dark.

Do one zone this weekend, and relax in a room that is clean where it counts.
