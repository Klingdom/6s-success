# Chapter 35 Ebook Description: The Living Room

The fifth room playbook of Part Nine. The living room is where the house relaxes, and cleaning it well is different from every room so far, because here the dirt that matters is the dirt you cannot see: dust matted across the vents that keep your electronics cool, grime worked deep into the sofa seams, a film on the bulb that steals the light, pet hair under the rug and behind the sofa.

This chapter gives the exact inputs and the numbered steps for each of the room's six zones, with two habits running through all of them: clean where nobody looks (lift the cushions, pull the books forward, reach behind and underneath) and match the tool to the surface (a dry cloth on a screen, a soft brush on a vent, the upholstery tool deep in the seams).

You will learn the one thing that saves your electronics (the vents and the back panel, not the screen), how to clean a screen safely, how to lift and vacuum a sofa properly, why a dusty bulb steals your light, and how to strap the two things that tip, the television and the tall bookcase. The chapter closes on a before-and-after room signature and the two photos to take.
