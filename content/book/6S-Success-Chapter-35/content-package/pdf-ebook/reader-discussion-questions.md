# Chapter 35 Reader Discussion Questions: The Living Room

1. When did you last clean the vents on your television or games console? Did you know dust in the vents shortens their life?
2. What is the one hidden spot in your living room you have never cleaned, the sofa deck, the top of the bookcase, under the rug, behind the sofa?
3. How do you clean your TV screen now? Have you ever sprayed it or used a paper towel?
4. How many decorative cushions do you move onto the floor every time you sit down? Which of them actually stay in place?
5. Is your television or your tall bookcase strapped to the wall? What has stopped you doing it?
6. Walk your route from door to door with a full laundry basket. Can two adults pass? Is it clutter, or too much furniture?
7. How many remotes and controllers are scattered around your living room right now? Where could one labelled bin live?
8. Is there a working device in your media unit that nobody has switched on in a year? What is keeping it there?
9. Is any medicine sitting on a side table within reach of where a child could play?
10. Which zone will you do first this weekend, the sofa or the media center, and what inputs do you need to gather?
