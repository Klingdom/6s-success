# Chapter 35 Ebook Sales Copy: The Living Room

## Headline
The living room is where the house relaxes. Here is exactly how to clean it, where the dust actually hides.

## The promise
Most people clean the open middle and stop. But in the living room the dirt that matters is the dirt you cannot see: matted in the vents that cook your electronics, deep in the sofa seams, filmed on the bulb that steals the light. This chapter gives you the exact inputs and the numbered steps for each of the room's six zones, plus the two habits that make it work: clean where nobody looks, and match the tool to the surface.

## What you get in this chapter
- The living-room Shine method: dust is heat, allergens, and lost light; clean where nobody looks; match the tool to the surface; screens dry-cloth-only.
- The six per-zone clean procedures, each with its inputs and numbered steps: the sofa and seating, the coffee table, the media center, the bookshelves and display, the side tables and lighting, and the floor and circulation path.
- The one that saves your electronics: the vents and the back panel, not the screen.
- The room's hard calls, and the two big safety items, the television and the tall bookcase, both strapped to the wall.
- The before-and-after room signature and the two photos to take.

## The common mistake it fixes
> Wiping the open surfaces and calling the room done, so the dust stays where it does the damage: matted in the vents cooking the electronics, deep in the sofa seams, on the top of the bookcase, and under the rug.
