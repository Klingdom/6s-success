# The Living Room

*The living room is where the house relaxes, and its enemies are soft and hidden: the dust matted in the vents that quietly cooks your electronics, the grime deep in the sofa seams, the film on a bulb that steals the light. This playbook points the whole loop at it one zone at a time, and gives you, for each of the six zones, the exact inputs to gather and the precise, step-by-step way to clean and shine it, matching the tool to each surface, so the room is genuinely clean where it counts and easy to walk into and sit down in.*

## How to read this chapter

This is a PLAYBOOK, not a method chapter. It applies the six-S loop you already own to one room, decomposed into six micro-zones, and its centerpiece is the per-zone clean-and-shine instructions with the exact inputs. It recaps but does not re-teach the nine-rule Shine method from Chapter 31; the new material is the living-room layer (dust is heat, allergens, and lost light; clean where nobody looks; match the tool to the surface; screens dry-cloth-only) and the numbered steps for each zone.

Products are named as TYPES, never brands. The exhaustive product list is a separate appendix.

The friction meter, which reached its goal at Chapter 30, is retired for Part 9; the chapter closes on a before-and-after room signature and the two photos to take.
