# Chapter 35 Content Package: The Living Room

The FIFTH room playbook of Part Nine (Chapters 31 to 50), built to the instruction-centric template. The chapter CORE is the detailed, step-by-step clean-and-shine instruction for each of the six living-room zones, with the exact inputs.

## The chapter in one line
In the living room the dirt that matters is the dirt you cannot see, so you clean where nobody looks (lift, reach behind, under) and match the tool to the surface (a dry cloth on a screen, a soft brush on a vent, the upholstery tool deep in the seams).

## The six zones
The sofa and seating, the coffee table, the media center, the bookshelves and display, the side tables and lighting, and the floor and circulation path. Each carries an Inputs list, numbered Steps, and an inspect-and-flag list.

## The living-room Shine layer (nine rules from Chapter 31 plus)
- Dust is heat, allergens, and lost light: it mats the vents, settles deep in the upholstery, and films the bulb.
- Clean where nobody looks; match the tool to the surface.
- The media center: the real cleaning is the vents and the back panel, not the screen.
- The screen is dry-cloth-only; never spray, never a paper towel.
- Strap the two biggest tip-over risks, the television and the tall bookcase.
