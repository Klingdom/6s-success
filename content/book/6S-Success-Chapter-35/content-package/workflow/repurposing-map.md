# Chapter 35 Repurposing Map: The Living Room

## Spine (the how-to-clean value, in priority order)
1. The living-room Shine method: dust is heat, clean where nobody looks, match the tool to the surface.
2. The zone-by-zone centerpiece: the exact inputs and numbered steps for the sofa, coffee table, media center, bookshelves, side tables and lighting, and floor.
3. The one that saves your electronics: the media vents and back panel, not the screen.
4. The calls that matter: the pillows, the book, the dead electronics, the medicine, too much furniture.

## Reuse cautions
- Types, not brands, everywhere. No brand names or invented prices.
- Never depict a screen being sprayed. No harsh chemistry on soft furnishings.
- Every asset closes on the before-and-after signature (friction meter retired for Part 9).
- Stay in the living room: do not reuse dining-room (Ch 34) or family-room (Ch 36) material.
