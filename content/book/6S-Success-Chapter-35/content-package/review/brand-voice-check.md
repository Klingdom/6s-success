# Chapter 35 Brand Voice Check: The Living Room

## Voice fingerprint
Warm, plain, confident, practical. The room-specific lens is the hidden dirt, dust that becomes heat, and the register keeps a slight urgency about the electronics without tipping into alarm. The governing metaphors (dust is heat; clean where nobody looks; match the tool to the surface) hold across every asset.

## Frozen-string wins (verified byte-verbatim, manuscript and HTML)
- The dust-and-touch rule ("In the living room, dust is not just untidy, it is heat, allergens, and lost light...").
- The six zones line.
- The definition box ("A finished living room is one anyone can walk into...").
- The dust-is-heat, clean-where-nobody-looks, and screen reframes.
- One Idea to Keep and the before-and-after caption.

## Banned-language and brand audit (checks run)
- Dash scan: CLEAN (0 em/en, 0 real spaced-hyphen; only "> - " nine-rule bullets and the self-referential dash-rule literal in the checklist).
- Brand-name leak scan: CLEAN. Product TYPES only; no device brands, no cleaner brands, no console names.
- No hype words; no invented statistics or prices.

## Risks
- The dust-is-heat claim must stay accurate and calm, framed as maintenance guidance rather than a scare; checked in the manuscript.
- The screen guidance (never spray, dry cloth only) is stated as method, not a product claim.

## Not run
- No lab verification of electronics-cooling claims beyond the manuscript's own maintenance guidance; flagged as guidance, not a measured claim.
