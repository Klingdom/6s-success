A living-room tip that might save you an expensive replacement.

Your television and games console do not usually die of age. They die of dust. A felt of it matts across the vent slots, traps the heat the fan is trying to move, and the device slowly cooks itself. And it is dust you never see, because everyone cleans the screen and nobody cleans the vents.

So once in a while, pull the media unit out and, with a soft brush and the vacuum, clear every vent slot and the whole back panel of the TV and the console. If dust has matted solid in a slot, loosen it with the brush first, then lift it with the vacuum. The screen itself gets a dry microfiber cloth only, never a spray and never a paper towel.

While you are back there: strap the television and any tall bookcase to the wall, because both tip forward onto a child who pulls or climbs.

When did you last clean the vents on your electronics, and did you know it mattered?
