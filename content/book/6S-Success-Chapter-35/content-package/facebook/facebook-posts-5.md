# Chapter 35 Facebook Posts: The Living Room

Five short standalone posts.

## 1. The dirt you cannot see
A living room looks fine after a quick tidy. But the dirt that matters hides: in the vents cooking your electronics, deep in the sofa seams, on the bulb stealing the light. In the living room, dust is not just untidy, it is heat, allergens, and lost light. Clean where nobody looks.

## 2. Your TV is dying of dust
A television or a games console does not usually die of age, it dies of dust. The dust matted across a vent slot traps the heat the fan is trying to move. The real cleaning in this room is the vents and the back panel, not the screen. Brush them clear and they run cool again.

## 3. Never spray a screen
A screen is the one surface you never spray and never scrub. A dry microfiber cloth in slow straight passes is the whole method. A wet cloth streaks it, a paper towel scratches the coating, and a spray runs down inside the bezel. Match the tool to the surface.

## 4. Lift every cushion
Most of what a sofa swallows lives in the seams and the deck under the cushions: crumbs, coins, a season of pet hair. Lift every cushion, vacuum both faces, then run the crevice tool into the deck and seams. Beat the throw pillows and launder the throws, even the clean-looking ones.

## 5. Clean where it counts
A living room is not clean because the middle is tidy. It is clean because you vacuumed the sofa deck, brushed the vents, dusted the top of the bookcase, wiped the bulb, and cleared the route from door to door. Do one zone this weekend. Chapter 35. Next: the family room.
