# Chapter 35 Short Video Scripts (5): The Living Room

Each 30 to 90 seconds. Product types only.

## 1. Your TV is dying of dust (45s)
HOOK: Your television is not dying of age. It is dying of dust.
SCRIPT: Everyone cleans the screen. Nobody cleans the vents. But the dust matted across a vent slot traps the heat the fan is trying to move, and the device slowly cooks itself. So pull the unit out and, with a soft brush and the vacuum, clear every vent slot and the whole back panel. Matted solid? Loosen it with the brush first, then lift it. The screen gets a dry cloth only.
ON-SCREEN: The real cleaning is the vents, not the screen.
CTA: Chapter 35. Do the media center this weekend.

## 2. Never spray a screen (30s)
HOOK: Stop spraying your TV screen.
SCRIPT: A screen is the one surface you never spray and never scrub. A wet cloth streaks it, a paper towel scratches the coating, and a spray runs down inside the bezel. The whole method is a dry microfiber cloth in slow, straight passes. Gentle and dry, and nothing else.
CTA: Match the tool to the surface. Chapter 35.

## 3. Lift every cushion (60s)
HOOK: This is where a sofa actually gets cleaned.
SCRIPT: Not the open middle. The seams and the deck under the cushions, where the crumbs, the coins, and a season of pet hair collect. Work headrest down. Lift every seat cushion and vacuum both faces. Then run the crevice tool into the deck and the seams, back to front. Beat the throw pillows outside and launder the throws, even the clean-looking ones. Then the outer back against the wall, months untouched.
CTA: Do the sofa this weekend. Chapter 35.

## 4. A dusty bulb steals your light (30s)
HOOK: Your room is dimmer than it should be, and here is why.
SCRIPT: A film of dust on the bulb quietly steals the light the lamp exists to give. So clean the bulb first, off and fully cold, with a dry cloth. Then the shade, inside and out. Then the ring marks hiding under the tray. Smallest job in the room, and the lamp gives back its full light.
CTA: Chapter 35, the side tables and lighting.

## 5. Strap the two things that tip (40s)
HOOK: Two things in your living room can fall on a child.
SCRIPT: The television on its unit, and the tall bookcase. Both tip forward when a child pulls or climbs, and both are among the biggest tip-over risks in the house. So while the shelves are bare and the media unit is pulled out for cleaning, strap both to a wall stud, with the weight low. Cleaning finds the fault. This is the fix.
CTA: Chapter 35. Strap them this weekend.
