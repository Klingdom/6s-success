# Chapter 35 B-Roll and Visual Notes: The Living Room

Warm, natural light. Product types only, no brand labels on screen.

## Visual vocabulary
- The two-state opener: a cluttered before (cushions to move, buried coffee table, dusty TV over tangled cables, crammed shelf) and a clean after (every seat sittable, a tray on the coffee table, clear vents, a strapped shelf, a clear route).
- The recurring "dust is heat" motif: a close macro of a matted vent slot with faint heat lines, then the same vent cleared.

## Core shot list (seven shots)
1. The sofa: lifting every cushion, vacuuming both faces, then the crevice tool into the deck and seams. A coin surfacing.
2. The coffee table: cleared to bare, a glass top taking two passes, one glass-cleaner and one dry, fingerprints vanishing by lamplight.
3. The media center: the screen wiped with a dry cloth only; then the reveal, the real work, brushing and vacuuming the matted vents and back panel.
4. Bookshelves: dusting the very top of the unit and drawing a brush along the top edges of the books; a hand checking the wall strap.
5. Side table: wiping a cold bulb with a dry cloth, and the lamp visibly brightening.
6. The floor: moving the sofa out to vacuum underneath, lifting the rug to vacuum the underside, pressing the gripper pad.
7. Safety beat: an anti-tip strap going into a wall stud behind the TV and the bookcase.

## On-screen text bank (use sparingly)
- Dust is heat, allergens, and lost light.
- The real cleaning is the vents, not the screen.
- Never spray a screen. Dry cloth only.
- Clean where nobody looks: lift, reach behind, under.
- Strap the TV and the bookcase to the wall.

## Lower thirds (rotate)
- Zone 1 to 6, one per segment.
- Match the tool to the surface.

## Editing notes
- Close each cut on the before-and-after room signature from the doorway (sofa, coffee table, media wall).
- No presenter required; hands and surfaces only.
