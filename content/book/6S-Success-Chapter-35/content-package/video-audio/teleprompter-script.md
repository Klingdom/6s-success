# Chapter 35 Teleprompter Script: The Living Room

Short lines, one breath each. Warm and plain.

The living room looks fine after a quick tidy.
But in this room, the dirt that matters is the dirt you cannot see.
Dust matted in the vents that cool your electronics.
Grime deep in the sofa seams.
A film on the bulb, stealing the light.
So you clean two ways the other rooms did not ask for.
You clean where nobody looks.
And you match the tool to the surface.
A dry cloth on a screen.
A soft brush on a vent.
The upholstery tool, deep in the seams.
Here is the one that saves your electronics.
A television does not die of age. It dies of dust.
The dust matted across a vent traps the heat the fan is moving.
So the real cleaning is the vents and the back panel.
Not the screen.
Brush every slot. If the dust has gone solid, loosen it first.
The screen itself gets a dry cloth. Never a spray. Never a paper towel.
On the sofa, lift every cushion.
Vacuum both faces, then the crevice tool into the deck.
On the bookcase, the dust is on the very top, and the top edges of the books.
Wipe the bulb, off and cold, and the lamp gives back its light.
Then strap the two tall things, the TV and the bookcase, to the wall.
And keep a route you can cross in the dark, carrying a basket.
Do one zone this weekend.
The sofa, or the media center.
And relax in a room that is clean where it counts.
