# Chapter 35 Canonical Quotes: The Living Room

Frozen strings, byte-verbatim. Use exactly as written. Product types only, no brands.

## The rule and the structural line
> The living room is six small zones, not one big job: the sofa and seating, the coffee table, the media center, the bookshelves and display, the side tables and lighting, and the floor and circulation path. Do them one at a time and the room the house relaxes in comes back to you a zone at a time.

> In the living room, dust is not just untidy, it is heat, allergens, and lost light: it mats the vents that keep your electronics cool, it settles deep in the upholstery, and it films the bulb until the lamp barely lights. So you clean where nobody looks, and you match the tool to the surface: a dry cloth on a screen, a soft brush on a vent, the upholstery tool deep in the seams.

## The definition
> A finished living room is one anyone can walk into, cross in the dark, and sit down in without moving a thing: every seat sittable, the coffee table clear to its tray, the screen dust-free and the vents clear, the bookshelves dusted and strapped to the wall, a lamp that gives its full light, and a clear route from door to door wide enough to pass carrying a basket.

## The reframes
> A television or a games console does not usually die of age, it dies of dust. The felt of dust matted across a vent slot traps the heat the fan is trying to move, and a device left to run hot is a device on its way out. So in this room the real cleaning is not the screen, it is the vents and the back panel, where the dust you cannot see is quietly cooking the electronics you paid for.

> Most of what a living room needs cleaning is exactly where you never look: in the seams and the deck under the sofa cushions, on the top of the tallest bookcase, along the top edges of the books, on the outer back of the sofa against the wall, and under the rug. A surface wipe of the open middle leaves all of it. You clean a living room by lifting, pulling forward, and reaching behind.

> A screen is the one surface in the house you never spray and never scrub. A dry microfiber cloth in slow, straight passes is the whole method, because a wet cloth streaks it, a paper towel scratches the coating, and a spray runs down inside the bezel. Gentle and dry, and nothing else.

## The nine Shine rules (from Chapter 31, carried here)
> - work top to bottom
> - work dry before wet
> - work back to front, clean to dirty
> - mist the cloth, not the surface
> - use two cloths, one to clean and one to dry
> - colour-code your cloths and never use fabric softener
> - match the cleaner to the surface
> - give the cleaner a moment to work
> - clean to inspect, and flag, do not fix

## The callouts
> **Quick Win**
> Do the one thing that saves your electronics: brush and vacuum every vent slot and the back panel of the television and the console. If dust has matted solid in a slot, loosen it with a soft detail brush, then lift it with the vacuum. Wipe the screen with a dry cloth only. Twenty minutes, and the devices run cool instead of cooking.

> **Family Challenge**
> Walk the route from door to door with a full laundry basket, and if you cannot pass without turning sideways, the room holds more furniture than floor. Clear the route to full width, put every remote and controller in one labelled bin, and agree the floor is for furniture legs and the rug, and nothing else.

> **6S Tip**
> Match the tool to the surface: a dry microfiber cloth on a screen, never a spray or a paper towel; a soft brush and the vacuum on a vent, because dust there is heat; the upholstery tool deep in the sofa seams; a dry cloth on a cold bulb, because a dusty bulb steals the light. And strap the two tall, top-heavy things in this room, the television and the bookcase, to the wall.

> **Common Mistake**
> Wiping the open surfaces and calling the room done, so the dust stays where it does the damage: matted in the vents cooking the electronics, deep in the sofa seams, on the top of the bookcase, and under the rug. And spraying cleaner on the screen, which streaks it and runs inside the bezel. Lift, reach behind, brush the vents, and keep the screen dry.

## One Idea to Keep
> A living room is not clean because the middle is tidy. It is clean because you lifted the cushions and vacuumed the deck, brushed the dust out of the vents, dusted the top of the bookcase and the edges of the books, wiped the bulb, and cleared the route from door to door. Do one zone this weekend, the sofa or the media center, and relax in a room that is clean where it counts.

## The before-and-after caption

Take the before photo before you touch a thing, and take the after as the standard, because a living room this easy to walk into and sit down in is the one the house will actually relax in, and the photo is what it has to match from now on.
