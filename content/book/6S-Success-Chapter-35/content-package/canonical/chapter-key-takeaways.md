# Chapter 35 Key Takeaways: The Living Room

- The heart of the chapter is the step-by-step way to clean each of the six living-room zones, with the exact inputs: the sofa and seating, the coffee table, the media center, the bookshelves and display, the side tables and lighting, and the floor and circulation path.
- The living-room Shine layer, on top of the nine-rule method: dust is heat, allergens, and lost light, so clean where nobody looks and match the tool to the surface.
- Clean where nobody looks: lift the cushions and vacuum the deck and seams, dust the top of the bookcase and the top edges of the books, reach behind the sofa against the wall, and lift the rug.
- Save your electronics: the real cleaning at the media center is the vents and the back panel, not the screen. Dust matted across a vent cooks the device.
- The screen is dry-cloth-only: never a spray, never a paper towel, which scratches the coating.
- A dusty bulb steals the light, so wipe it while it is off and cold.
- The two biggest tip-over risks in the room, the television and the tall bookcase, both get strapped to the wall.
- Keep a clear route from door to door wide enough to cross in the dark carrying a basket; if two adults cannot pass, the room holds more furniture than floor.
- The friction meter is retired for Part 9; close on the before-and-after pair, the before kept and the after taped up as the standard.
