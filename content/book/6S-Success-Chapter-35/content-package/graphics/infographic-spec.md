# Chapter 35 Infographic Spec: The Living Room Shine Method

## The single strongest image
The living-room Shine method plus the kit, because the method (clean where nobody looks, match the tool to the surface, and the dust-is-heat idea) is the chapter's centerpiece.

## Anchor string (byte-verbatim)
> In the living room, dust is not just untidy, it is heat, allergens, and lost light: it mats the vents that keep your electronics cool, it settles deep in the upholstery, and it films the bulb until the lamp barely lights. So you clean where nobody looks, and you match the tool to the surface: a dry cloth on a screen, a soft brush on a vent, the upholstery tool deep in the seams.

## Palette
paper #F7F2E9, panel #FBF7EF, ink #2B2622, terracotta #BC4B2A, honey #DDA63A, slate #3C5A6B, green #6E8B5B (Shine). Fraunces headings, Inter labels.

## Layout, portrait (1080 x 1350)
- Title: The Living Room Shine Method.
- Band 1, "dust is heat, allergens, and lost light": three cues (a matted vent with heat lines, a dust-filled sofa seam, a filmed bulb).
- Band 2, "match the tool to the surface": dry cloth on a screen, soft brush on a vent, upholstery tool in a seam.
- Band 3, "clean where nobody looks": lift, pull forward, reach behind.
- Footer strip: dust is heat, match the tool to the surface, clean where nobody looks.

## Layout, landscape (1200 x 675)
Two panels: the method on the left, the kit on the right (a vacuum with three attachments, a microfiber set with a flagged screen cloth, three cleaners, a detail brush, an upholstery spot cleaner, a throw basket, a remote tray, a cable kit with labels, an anti-tip strap).
