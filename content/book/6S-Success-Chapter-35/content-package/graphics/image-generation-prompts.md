# Chapter 35 Image Generation Prompts: The Living Room

Warm, illustrated, flat-color editorial house style (paper #F7F2E9 ground, ink #2B2622 line, terracotta/honey/slate/green accents, Shine green #6E8B5B). No photorealism, no brand names or logos, no text baked into the art beyond the noted labels. Product types only.

## Prompt 1 (opener, two states)
A warm flat-illustration of one living room shown twice, side by side. Left, labelled "before": a sofa piled with cushions and throws, a coffee table buried under mugs and charging cables, a television standing over a tangle of cables with dust on its vents, a tall bookcase crammed and leaning off the wall, a cluttered floor. Right, labelled "after": every sofa seat clear with one cushion each and throws in a basket, a coffee table clear to a tray, the television clean with cables bundled to one strip, the bookcase dusted and strapped to the wall, a clear route across the floor.

## Prompt 2 (six zones plan)
A warm overhead floor plan of a living room, gently shaded, with six numbered and labelled zones: sofa and seating, coffee table, media center, bookshelves and display, side tables and lighting, and a marked clear route from the doorway across the room. Caption strip at the foot: "one room, six small jobs".

## Prompt 3 (method and kit, two panels)
A two-panel warm infographic. Left panel "the method": a matted television vent with faint heat lines, a cut-away sofa seam showing hidden dust, a filmed bulb, and three tool-to-surface pairings (dry cloth on a screen, soft brush on a vent, upholstery tool in a seam). Right panel "the kit": labelled type icons for a vacuum with three attachments, a microfiber set with a flagged screen cloth, three cleaners, a detail brush, an upholstery spot cleaner, a throw basket, a remote tray, a cable kit with labels, and an anti-tip strap.

## Prompt 4 (media center step ribbon)
A warm step-ribbon illustration titled "how to clean the media center": top of the unit, screen with a dry cloth, back panel and every vent slot (drawn as the most important step, with a soft brush and a vacuum), shelves and cables, controllers. The vents highlighted in the Shine green.

## Prompt 5 (before-and-after signature)
A warm matched pair of the living room's face, the sofa, coffee table, and media wall, shot from the doorway, before on the left and after on the right, with a small camera icon and the labels "the before, kept" and "the after, kept as the standard".
