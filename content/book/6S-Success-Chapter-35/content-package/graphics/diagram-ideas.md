# Chapter 35 Diagram Ideas: The Living Room

House palette (paper #F7F2E9, panel #FBF7EF, ink #2B2622, terracotta #BC4B2A, honey #DDA63A, slate #3C5A6B, green #6E8B5B for Shine). Warm, illustrated, not photoreal. Product types only, no brands or logos. Spark red reserved for the two true hazards (the tip-over TV and bookcase).

## Diagram 1 (opener, figure 1): two states
The living room before and after, side by side. Before: a sofa piled with cushions and throws, a coffee table buried under mugs and cables, a dusty TV over tangled cables, a crammed unstrapped bookcase, a floor you weave through. After: every seat sittable, the coffee table clear to a tray, the screen and vents clear with cables bundled and labelled, the shelves dusted and strapped, a clear route from door to door.

## Diagram 2 (figure 2): the six zones plan
A warm overhead plan of a living room, six numbered zones marked and named: sofa and seating, coffee table, media center, bookshelves and display, side tables and lighting, floor and circulation path. Footer: "one room, six small jobs".

## Diagram 3 (figure 3): the Shine method and kit
Two panels. Left, the method: three cues for "dust is heat, allergens, and lost light" (a matted vent with heat lines, a dust-filled sofa seam, a filmed bulb); "match the tool to the surface" (dry cloth on a screen, brush on a vent, upholstery tool in a seam); "clean where nobody looks" (lift, pull forward, reach behind). Right, the kit: a vacuum with upholstery, crevice, and brush heads; a microfiber set with a screen cloth flagged; multi-surface, glass, and floor cleaners; a detail brush; an upholstery spot cleaner; plus a throw basket, a remote tray, a cable kit with cord labels, and an anti-tip strap. Footer: "dust is heat, match the tool to the surface, clean where nobody looks".

## Diagram 4 (no HTML twin): the living-room Shine method
A standalone visual of the method: dust is heat, allergens, and lost light; clean where nobody looks; match the tool to the surface; screens dry-cloth-only. Names the next chapter, the family room.

## Diagram 5 (no HTML twin): how to clean the media center
A step ribbon: top of the unit, screen (dry cloth only), back panel and every vent slot (dust is heat), shelves and cables, controllers. Highlight the vents as the step that matters most.

## Diagram 6 (before/after signature) and the photo brief
The Part 9 signature: a matched before-and-after pair of the sofa, coffee table, and media wall from the doorway. **Best before-and-after photo brief:** stand in the doorway and frame the sofa, the coffee table, and the media wall together in one shot, because that is the room's face; same spot, angle, and light, before and after; do not tidy before the before (Ch 6); keep the after as the ideal-state standard (Ch 25). Secondary pairs: the media unit with its cables, and the bookshelves. The retired friction meter is not drawn.
