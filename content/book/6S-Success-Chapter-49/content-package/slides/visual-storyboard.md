# Chapter 49 Visual Storyboard: The Stair Landing

Four figures, matching the chapter's SVGs. Warm paper, terracotta, honey, slate, green. Types, not brands.

## Figure 1: the opener, two states
A full-width pair. Left, the flight as it really is: folded laundry on step four, a box staged on the bottom three steps, a coat over the newel post so the handrail runs out before the bottom, things pushed to the stair-side edge of a console, and a dark patch at the top of the flight. Right, the same flight: every tread bare from nose to riser, the rail running unbroken from newel to newel, both ends lit, and one basket on the landing floor standing empty.

## Figure 2: the three zones
A warm diagram of a flight and landing, three numbered areas marked and named in a serif face: stair and floor path, landing console, wall and display. Only three cells, the fewest in the book. Footer line: the flight comes first, because it sets the limit on what the other two may hold.

## Figure 3: the method and the kit
Two panels. Left, the four rules of the stair landing layer with a gloss under each. Right, the kit, which is deliberately the shortest in the book and should look sparse on the page. Footer line: the shortest kit in the book, and that is the argument.

## Figure 4: the before-and-after signature
A matched pair shot up the flight from the bottom, with the camera icon between them, plus an honey-outlined label reading "then look from the fall's view". The after caption reads "the after, plus a check you stand in". This is the only Part 9 close whose extra element is a position rather than a photograph.
