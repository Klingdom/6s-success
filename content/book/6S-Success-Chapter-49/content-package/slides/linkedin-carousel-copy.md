# Chapter 49 LinkedIn Carousel Copy: The Stair Landing

**Slide 1**
The only space in this book
that is not a room.

**Slide 2**
A stair landing is a route.
Nobody ever stops in it.

**Slide 3**
So it is judged by passage.
Not by what it holds.

**Slide 4**
The correct amount of storage here
is very nearly none.

**Slide 5**
A workshop hazard hurts you
while you are paying attention.

**Slide 6**
A stair hazard waits in the dark
for somebody who is not.

**Slide 7**
Nobody approaches a staircase carefully.
A staircase is not a task.

**Slide 8**
The trap is a reasonable sentence:
I will take it up next time I go.

**Slide 9**
So give it a basket, not an argument.
Emptied nightly, full or not.

**Slide 10**
The handrail: most touched,
least washed. Underside included.

**Slide 11**
Stand at the bottom, in the dark.
That is the view the fall would have.
