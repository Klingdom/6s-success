# Chapter 49 Teaching Slide Deck Outline: The Stair Landing

Thirteen slides, about 18 minutes. The shortest deck in Part Nine, appropriately.

1. Title: The Stair Landing. Part Nine, room-by-room playbooks.
2. The only space in this book that is not a room.
3. A route: nobody stops here, nobody keeps anything here on purpose.
4. So it is judged by passage, not by contents.
5. The correct amount of storage here is very nearly none.
6. Attended against unattended: workshop hazard, stair hazard.
7. A staircase is not a task. It is the thing you do on the way to one.
8. The trap: a completely reasonable sentence.
9. Give it a basket, not an argument. And the three-nights test.
10. The handrail: most touched, least washed. Underside included.
11. The centerpiece: zone by zone, inputs and numbered steps (walk the flight live).
12. The bulb you cannot see, and the only place you can see it from.
13. The close: a check you stand in rather than a photograph.
