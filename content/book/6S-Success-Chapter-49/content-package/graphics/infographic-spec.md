# Chapter 49 Infographic Spec: The Stair Landing

## Figure 2: the three zones
- Canvas 1000 x 440, warm paper fill.
- Title in Fraunces, 22px: "The stair landing, drawn as its three zones".
- **Three panels on a single row**, each 270 x 130, rounded 8, panel fill with a rule-coloured stroke. This is the only chapter with a single-row zone plan, and the sparseness is appropriate.
- Each panel carries a green numbered disc and a serif zone name: 1 Stair and floor path, 2 Landing console, 3 Wall and display.
- Footer in terracotta: the flight comes first, because it sets the limit on what the other two may hold.

## Figure 3: the method and the kit
- Canvas 1000 x 480, two panels of 460 x 410.
- Left panel headed "The method": four rules with a gloss under each. Lead lines: "A route, not a room" (terracotta), "The hazard waits, unattended" (slate), "The trap is a reasonable sentence" (terracotta), "Most touched, least washed" (green).
- Right panel headed "The kit": nine short lines, and the last reads "Nothing else belongs here". **The kit panel should read as noticeably sparse compared with every other chapter**, because that is the chapter's argument.
- Footer in terracotta: the shortest kit in the book, and that is the argument.

## Figure 4: the signature
- Canvas 1000 x 430, matched before and after panels shot up the flight, with a slate camera disc between them.
- The after panel carries an honey-outlined label reading "then look from the fall's view", and the caption beneath reads "the after, plus a check you stand in".
- **This is the only Part 9 close whose extra element is a position rather than a photograph.** The figure should make that distinction visible.
