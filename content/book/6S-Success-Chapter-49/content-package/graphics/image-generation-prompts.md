# Chapter 49 Image Generation Prompts: The Stair Landing

House style: warm paper background, editorial illustration, serif display type, muted terracotta, honey, slate, and green accents. No logos, no brand names, no readable label text, no QR codes. No em dashes in any rendered text.

1. **Opener, two states.** A domestic staircase drawn twice side by side, both shot up the flight from the bottom. Left: folded laundry on a middle tread, a box on the bottom steps, a coat over the newel post, a dark landing at the top. Right: the same flight entirely bare, rail clear, both ends lit warmly, one empty basket on the landing floor.
2. **The three zones.** A warm diagram of a flight and landing with three numbered areas marked and labelled in a serif face.
3. **The two positions.** A stair elevation with two small figures, one at the bottom looking up and one at the top looking down, drawn in silhouette with sight lines.
4. **The handrail.** A close crop of a hand wiping the underside of a handrail with a cloth, the underside clearly the subject.
5. **The tread crevice.** A close cutaway illustration of one tread meeting its riser with a vacuum crevice nozzle going into the join.
6. **The basket.** A single basket on a landing floor at the end of a console nearest the top step, empty, in evening light.
7. **The frame diagonal.** A stairway wall of frames climbing the stair's diagonal, each the same distance above the tread nose below it.

**Do not depict:** anyone standing on a tread to reach a frame or a light fitting, anyone carrying a load they cannot see over, or a fall of any kind.
