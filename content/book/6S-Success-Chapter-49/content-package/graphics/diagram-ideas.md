# Chapter 49 Diagram Ideas: The Stair Landing

1. **Attended against unattended** (hero): two panels. Left, a person at a bench with their attention visibly on a blade. Right, a person on a stair at night looking straight ahead over a basket. Caption: one hazard has your attention, the other does not.
2. **The two positions**: a flight in elevation with two small figures marked, one at the bottom looking up and one at the top looking down, and every other viewing position greyed out. Caption: the two views nobody stands in on purpose.
3. **The reasonable sentence**: a small pile on a landing console with a speech bubble reading "next time I go", repeated four times fainter behind it. Caption: true in principle, false in practice.
4. **The basket rule**: one basket drawn at the end of the console nearest the top step, with a nightly arrow up and an empty arrow back.
5. **The tread crevice**: a cutaway of one tread and riser with the crevice highlighted and grit shown collecting in it. Caption: this is what polishes a runner slick.
6. **The frame diagonal**: a stairway wall with frames spaced by a constant measurement from each frame centre to the tread nose below, so the gallery climbs the stair's diagonal.
7. **The rail run**: two elevations, one with a coat over the newel post and the usable rail visibly shortened, one clear from newel to newel.
