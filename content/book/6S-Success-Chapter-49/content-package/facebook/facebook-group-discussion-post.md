# Chapter 49 Facebook Group Discussion Post: The Stair Landing

**Question for the group: what is on your stairs right now, and how long has it been there?**

Almost every house has something, and it usually arrived by way of a completely reasonable sentence: I will take it up next time I go. Everyone says it, several times a day, and every time it is true in principle and false in practice.

That one sentence is how a landing console fills and how the bottom three steps become a shelf. The fix is not willpower, it is a container: one basket, emptied every night whether it is full or not.

Two more while we are here:

- **Go and stand at the bottom of your flight tonight and look up at the top step.** A dead bulb there is the most dangerous thing a staircase can hold, and that is the only position you can see it from.
- **When did you last wipe your handrail, underside included?** It is one of the most touched and least washed surfaces in the house.

Tell us what is on your stairs.
