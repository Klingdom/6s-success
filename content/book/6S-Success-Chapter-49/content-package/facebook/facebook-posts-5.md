# Chapter 49 Facebook Posts: The Stair Landing

Five standalone posts.

## 1
A stair landing is not a room, it is a route. Nobody ever stops in it, which means it is judged by whether you can get through it rather than by what it holds. The correct amount of storage on a staircase is very nearly none.

## 2
A workshop hazard hurts you while you are paying attention to it. A stair hazard waits in the dark for somebody thinking about something else, carrying a basket they cannot see over.

## 3
Nobody approaches a staircase carefully, because a staircase is not a task. It is the thing you do on the way to the task. That is why small oversights matter more there than anywhere else.

## 4
The trap is not laziness. It is a reasonable sentence: I will take it up next time I go. You will not defeat it, so give it a basket instead, emptied every night whether it is full or not.

## 5
Stand at the bottom of your stairs in the dark and look up at the top step. A dead bulb there is the most dangerous thing a staircase can hold, and that is the only place you can see it from.
