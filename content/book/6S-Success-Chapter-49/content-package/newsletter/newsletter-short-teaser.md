# Chapter 49 Newsletter Short Teaser: The Stair Landing

The stair landing is not a room at all but a route, the only space in this book you never stop in, and the one place where a small oversight stops being untidiness and becomes a real injury.

Chapter 49 breaks it into three zones with the exact inputs and numbered steps for each, and adds four rules to the nine rules of Shine: this is a route rather than a room, the hazard here arrives unattended, the trap is a reasonable sentence, and the handrail is the most touched and least washed surface in the house.

Start with the flight itself, even though it looks the least cluttered.
