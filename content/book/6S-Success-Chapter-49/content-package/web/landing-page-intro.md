# Chapter 49 Landing Page Intro: The Stair Landing

This is the only space in the book that is not a room.

A stair landing is a route. Nobody sits down here, nobody works here, nobody keeps anything here on purpose. It exists to be passed through, several times a day, by every person in the house, which means it is judged by an entirely different question from every other space: not what does it hold, but can a person get through it. And the honest answer to how much storage belongs on a staircase is very nearly none.

That would make this the simplest chapter in the book, except for one thing. This is also the place where a small oversight stops being untidiness and becomes a real injury.

The workshop was dangerous too, and the difference matters. A workshop hazard hurts you while you are paying attention to it. A stair hazard waits, in the dark, for somebody thinking about something else entirely, carrying a basket they cannot see over. Nobody approaches a staircase carefully, because a staircase is not a task.

Three zones, exact inputs, numbered steps. Product types only, no brands, and the shortest kit list in the book.
