# Chapter 49 CTA and Companion Resources: The Stair Landing

## Primary call to action
Do one zone this week, and make it the flight itself, because bare treads and working light at both ends set the limit on everything else this landing is allowed to hold.

## Companion resources
- **The Stair Landing Reset card** (printable: the three zones, the done-looks-like for each, and the flight-first order of work).
- **The Two Positions card** (the check to run monthly: stand at the bottom in the dark and look up at the top step, then stand at the top and look down).
- **The Nightly Basket card** (the rule to agree out loud, plus the three-nights test for anything that will not land).
- **The Stair Landing Kit list** (the shortest kit in the book, which is itself the argument).

## Register and fences
- Warm and practical, but this is the chapter that takes its safety material most seriously, and the register holds level rather than dramatic.
- Product types only, never brand names. No invented statistics, and no injury or fall data of any kind.
- **No structural, electrical, or carpentry instruction.** Loose treads, loose brackets, and runner fixing are flagged or decided, never described as a procedure.
- Bulb changing is named as a task done from the landing on a properly footed ladder, never from a tread, and never described step by step.
- Friction meter retired for Part 9; close on the before-and-after signature plus the two-position check.

## Words to avoid
- No brand names. No fall statistics. No repair, wiring, or joinery instruction. Never depict anyone working from a tread.
