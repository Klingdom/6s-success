# Chapter 49 Teleprompter Script: The Stair Landing

*Two minutes. Read at an even pace, a little slower than usual. Pause at each line break.*

This is the only space in the book that is not a room.

A stair landing is a route.

Nobody sits down here. Nobody works here.

Nobody keeps anything here on purpose.

So it is judged by a different question.

Not what does it hold. Can a person get through it.

And the correct amount of storage on a staircase is very nearly none.

That would make this simple, except for one thing.

This is where a small oversight becomes a real injury.

The workshop was dangerous too. The difference matters.

A workshop hazard hurts you while you are paying attention to it.

A stair hazard waits. In the dark.

For somebody thinking about something else entirely.

Nobody approaches a staircase carefully.

Because a staircase is not a task.

It is the thing you do on the way to the task.

And what fills a landing is not laziness.

It is a reasonable sentence. I will take it up next time I go.

True in principle. False in practice.

So do not argue with it. Give it a basket.

One basket, emptied every night, whether it is full or not.

Then go and stand at the bottom of your stairs, in the dark.

And look up at the top step.
