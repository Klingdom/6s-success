# Chapter 49 Short Video Scripts: The Stair Landing

Five scripts, 30 to 60 seconds each.

## 1. Not a room
**Hook:** This is the only space in your house that is not a room.
**Body:** A stair landing is a route. Nobody sits there, nobody works there, nobody keeps anything there on purpose. So it is judged by whether you can get through it, not by what it holds.
**Close:** The correct amount of storage on a staircase is very nearly none.

## 2. A staircase is not a task
**Hook:** Here is why stairs are more dangerous than a workshop.
**Body:** A workshop hazard hurts you while you are paying attention to it. You are holding the tool, your care is switched on. A stair hazard waits in the dark for somebody thinking about something else.
**Close:** Nobody approaches a staircase carefully, because a staircase is not a task.

## 3. The reasonable sentence
**Hook:** One sentence fills every landing in the country.
**Body:** I will take it up next time I go. Everyone says it, several times a day, and every time it is true in principle and false in practice. Nobody is lying. The trip just does not happen with that thing in hand.
**Close:** So give it a basket, not an argument.

## 4. The bulb you cannot see
**Hook:** The most dangerous thing on your stairs is a bulb.
**Body:** Not from the landing, because you are standing under it. Not from halfway up, because you are looking at your feet. Only from the bottom, looking up, in the dark, which is a position nobody stands in on purpose.
**Close:** Go and stand there tonight. Ten minutes and a bulb.

## 5. The handrail
**Hook:** When did you last wash your handrail?
**Body:** It is one of the most touched and least washed surfaces in the house. Every hand that has ever used those stairs has left something on it, and almost nobody wipes it because it does not look dirty.
**Close:** End to end, every week. Underside included.
