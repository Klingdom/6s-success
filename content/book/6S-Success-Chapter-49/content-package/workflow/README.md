## Fences for this chapter
- Product **types** only, never brand names.
- **No structural, electrical, or carpentry instruction.** Loose treads, loose brackets and lifted runners are flagged or decided, never repaired on the page.
- **Never depict anyone working from a tread.** If height is needed, the ladder is footed on the flat landing and that is visible in frame.
- **Never depict a fall, a stumble, or a near-miss**, staged or otherwise, and never depict a person carrying a load they cannot see over on stairs.
- **No fall statistics or injury data.** The argument is made structurally, not with numbers.
- The kit is deliberately the shortest in the book and should look sparse in every asset. Do not pad it.
- Friction meter retired for Part 9; close on the before-and-after signature plus the two-position check.
