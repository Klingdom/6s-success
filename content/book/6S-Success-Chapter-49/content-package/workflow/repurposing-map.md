## Reuse cautions
- Types, not brands. No invented prices, and **no fall statistics or injury data anywhere**.
- **Never depict anyone working from a tread.** Ladders are footed on the flat landing, visibly.
- **Never depict a fall, stumble, or near-miss**, or a person carrying a load they cannot see over on stairs.
- No repair, wiring, or joinery content. Runners, brackets and treads are flagged or decided, never fixed on the page.
- Keep the kit sparse in every asset. Padding it contradicts the chapter.
- Every asset closes on the before-and-after signature plus the two-position check, which is a position rather than a photograph.
- Stay on the landing: do not reuse hall closet (Ch 48) or patio (Ch 50) material, and keep the workshop contrast (Ch 46) as a contrast rather than a repeat.
