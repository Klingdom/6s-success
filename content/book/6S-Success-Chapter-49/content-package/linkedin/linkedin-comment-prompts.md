# Chapter 49 Comment Prompts: The Stair Landing

1. What is on your stairs right now, and how long has it been there?
2. When did you last wipe your handrail, underside included?
3. Are both ends of your flight lit tonight? Go and check from the bottom.
4. Has a runner corner in your house lifted and been pressed back down?
5. Is anything hanging on a newel post, so the rail runs out early?
6. How many things are staged on your bottom three steps?
7. Does the sentence "I will take it up next time I go" get said in your house?
8. Is there a picture on your stairway wall hanging from a single nail?
