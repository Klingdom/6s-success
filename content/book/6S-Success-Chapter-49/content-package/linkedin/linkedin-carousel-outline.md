# Chapter 49 LinkedIn Carousel Outline: The Stair Landing

Eleven slides. Product types only.

- Slide 1 (hook): The only space in this book that is not a room.
- Slide 2: A stair landing is a route. Nobody ever stops in it.
- Slide 3: So it is judged by passage, not by what it holds.
- Slide 4: The correct amount of storage here is very nearly none.
- Slide 5: A workshop hazard hurts you while you are paying attention.
- Slide 6: A stair hazard waits in the dark for someone who is not.
- Slide 7: Nobody approaches a staircase carefully. It is not a task.
- Slide 8: The trap is a reasonable sentence: I will take it up next time.
- Slide 9: So give it a basket, not an argument. Emptied nightly.
- Slide 10: The handrail: most touched, least washed. Underside included.
- Slide 11 (close): Look from the bottom in the dark. That is the fall's view.
