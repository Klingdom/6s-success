# X Short Posts: The Stair Landing

Chapter 49 of *6S Success: Home Edition*. Ten standalone posts, each at or under 280 characters counting its number line. Plain numbering.

---

1/
A stair landing is not a room, it is a route. Nobody ever stops in it, so it is judged by whether you can get through it rather than by what it holds.

(153 chars)

---

2/
The correct amount of storage on a staircase is very nearly none.

(68 chars)

---

3/
A workshop hazard hurts you while you are paying attention to it. A stair hazard waits in the dark for somebody thinking about something else.

(145 chars)

---

4/
Nobody approaches a staircase carefully, because a staircase is not a task. It is the thing you do on the way to the task.

(125 chars)

---

5/
What fills a landing is not laziness. It is a reasonable sentence: I will take it up next time I go. True in principle, false in practice, every single time.

(160 chars)

---

6/
You will not defeat that sentence, so give it a basket instead. One basket, emptied every night whether it is full or not.

(125 chars)

---

7/
If something rides that basket three nights running, it is not waiting for a trip. It has no home on the destination floor.

(126 chars)

---

8/
The handrail is one of the most touched and least washed surfaces in the house. End to end, every week, underside included.

(126 chars)

---

9/
The most dangerous thing on your stairs is a dead bulb over the top step, and you can only see it from the bottom of the flight, looking up, in the dark.

(156 chars)

10/
