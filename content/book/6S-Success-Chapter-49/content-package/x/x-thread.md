# X Thread: The Stair Landing

Chapter 49 of *6S Success: Home Edition*, the nineteenth room playbook of Part Nine and the shortest chapter in the run. One thread, 14 posts. Each at or under 280 characters, counting its number line. Plain "1/" numbering on its own line.

---

1/
Part Nine reaches the only space in the whole book that is not a room: the stair landing.

(92 chars)

---

2/
A stair landing is a route. Nobody sits down there, nobody works there, nobody keeps anything there on purpose. It exists to be passed through, several times a day, by everyone in the house.

(193 chars)

---

3/
Which means it gets judged by a completely different question. Not what does it hold. Can a person get through it.

(117 chars)

---

4/
And the honest answer to how much storage belongs on a staircase is very nearly none.

(88 chars)

---

5/
That would make this the simplest chapter in the book, except that it is also where a small oversight stops being untidiness and becomes a real injury.

(154 chars)

---

6/
We did the workshop three chapters ago, and it was genuinely dangerous. The difference matters. A workshop hazard hurts you while you are paying attention to it: you are holding the tool, your care is switched on.

(216 chars)

---

7/
A stair hazard does the opposite. It waits, in the dark, for somebody thinking about something else entirely, carrying a basket they cannot see over, at the end of a long day.

(178 chars)

---

8/
Nobody approaches a staircase carefully, because a staircase is not a task. It is the thing you do on the way to the task.

(125 chars)

---

9/
Now, what actually fills a landing, and it is not laziness. It is a completely reasonable sentence: I will take it up next time I go.

(136 chars)

---

10/
Everyone says it, several times a day, and every time it is true in principle and false in practice. Nobody is lying. The trip was going to happen. It just did not happen with that thing in hand.

(199 chars)

---

11/
So do not try to defeat it. Give it a container. One basket, at the end nearest the top step, emptied every night whether it is full or not.

(144 chars)

---

12/
And if an item rides that basket three nights running without landing anywhere, it is not waiting for a trip. It has no home on the destination floor.

(154 chars)

---

13/
One thing to do tonight. Stand at the bottom of your flight in the dark and look up at the top step. A dead bulb there is the most dangerous thing a staircase can hold, and that is the only position you can see it from.

(223 chars)

---

14/
Not from the landing, you are under it. Not from halfway up, you are looking at your feet. Ten minutes and a bulb. Chapter 49.
