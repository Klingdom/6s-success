# Chapter 49 Reader Discussion Questions: The Stair Landing

1. What is on your stairs right now, and which trip was it waiting for?
2. Go and stand at the bottom of your flight in the dark. What can you see at the top?
3. When did you last wipe your handrail, underside included?
4. Has a runner corner in your house lifted and been pressed back down? What would refixing it properly actually involve?
5. Is anything hanging on a newel post, so the rail runs out before the bottom?
6. Who in your household would carry the nightly basket, and would they agree to it?
7. Is there anything on your stairway wall you already suspect is too heavy for what is holding it?
8. When somebody in your house carries a laundry basket downstairs, can they see their feet?
