# Chapter 49 Back Cover Copy: The Stair Landing

*A stair landing is not a room. It is a route.*

Nobody ever stops in it, which means it is judged by whether a person can get through it rather than by what it holds. The correct amount of storage on a staircase is very nearly none.

And it is the one place where a small oversight becomes a real injury. A workshop hazard hurts you while you are paying attention to it. A stair hazard waits in the dark for somebody who is thinking about something else.

Three zones. One reasonable sentence that fills every landing in the country. And the shortest kit list in the book.

**6S Success: Home Edition**, Chapter Forty-Nine.
