# Chapter 49 Ebook Description: The Stair Landing

The nineteenth room playbook of Part Nine, the shortest chapter in the run, and the only space in the book that is not a room.

A stair landing is a route. Nobody sits down there, nobody works there, nobody keeps anything there on purpose. It exists to be passed through, several times a day, by every person in the house, which means it is judged by an entirely different question: not what does it hold, but can a person get through it. The honest answer to how much storage belongs on a staircase is very nearly none.

That would make it the simplest chapter in the book, except that it is also the place where a small oversight stops being untidiness and becomes a real injury. And the chapter is precise about why that differs from the workshop: a workshop hazard hurts you while you are paying attention to it, while a stair hazard waits in the dark for somebody thinking about something else entirely.

The room breaks into three zones, the fewest in Part Nine: the stair and floor path, the landing surface or console, and the wall and display zone. The flight comes first even though it looks least cluttered, because bare treads and working light set the limit on what the other two may hold.
