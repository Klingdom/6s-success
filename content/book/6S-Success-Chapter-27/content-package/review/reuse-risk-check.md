## Same-day collision
The calendar avoids two assets sharing a hook on the same day. If your LinkedIn and Facebook audiences overlap heavily, push one back a day so the rule or the "a home audit is not an inspection" line does not land twice in one day.

## Cross-posting risk
- The LinkedIn carousel outline (`linkedin/linkedin-carousel-outline.md`) and the slides carousel copy (`slides/linkedin-carousel-copy.md`) cover the same friendly-audit content. Post only one to LinkedIn (use the copy-ready slides file; keep the outline as planning).
- The LinkedIn newsletter (`linkedin/linkedin-newsletter-version.md`) and the email newsletter share structure; do not send both to the same subscriber the same day.

## Duplicate-content / SEO risk
- Web landing intro, ebook description, and LinkedIn article overlap in phrasing. Set the chapter page as the SEO canonical and mark syndicated copies with a canonical link.

## Cross-chapter risk (specific to Chapter 27)
- Chapter 27 is the SECOND chapter of Part Eight and the FIRST-CONCRETE-MECHANISM chapter. Keep it distinct from Chapter 26 behind it (which opened Sustain with the concept and mindset), from Chapter 5 (which introduced the one-time baseline audit), and from the later Sustain practices ahead of it. Chapter 27 uses the keeping mindset Chapter 26 built, but assets must NOT re-teach it; they reference the Chapter 5 baseline audit in ONE light line only (you can reuse its simple snapshot) and must NOT re-teach the 6S Snapshot radar, the 0 to 5 scoring, or the out-of-30 baseline; and they must NOT re-teach Sort, Straighten, Shine, Safety, or Standardize (a one-line callback is fine, a re-teach is not). Grep confirms the assets deliver the recurring, drift-catching check-in and its reframe (a check-in, not an inspection; the four things; the no-blame rule; catch it early) and do not rebuild the earlier material.
- Do not let this chapter slide FORWARD into the later practices. Ch 27 assets keep it to the recurring check-in and its reframe and must NOT build the family problem-solving method (Chapter 28), the daily 6S rhythm (Chapter 29), or the recurring 6S event or book close (Chapter 30). This fence is held: the later practices are named in the package only as the chapters still ahead, never built.
- The friction-meter beat is specific to this chapter, and it is a small MOVE along the last-gap path, NOT a milestone. The live needle moves a short step from where Chapter 26 held it, at (67.4,154.95), to (67.3,154.96), a little way along the dashed last-gap path toward the GOAL crosshair at (67,155), tagged "Sustain, step 1," the first movement since Standardize; Chapter 26's position is now a faint ghost just behind, and behind the needle the anti-backslide catch stays fully engaged and locked. The move is honestly small, because an audit clears nothing on its own; it scrubs no surface and removes no clutter, but it makes the home a little more able to keep itself. Do not overstate this as a milestone and do not understate the move; the goal stays a step ahead, reached only at the very end of the book (Chapter 30), when all six S's are done.

## Originality and source-fidelity risk
- The chapter is a draft authored in the book voice, grounded in the 6S source ("Steps to Complete Sustain": schedule 6S audits, the audit as a routine of continuous improvement, tied lightly to Toyota / TPS), not supplied verbatim source. All derived assets are faithful to that draft; re-sync if the chapter is revised.
- Quote cards are verbatim: every card in `graphics/quote-card-copy.md` (10 cards) verified byte-identical to a quoted line in `canonical/chapter-quotes.md` (0 mismatches). Nothing borrowed from outside the book; no brand-name products are named, and the source's Sustain step (schedule 6S audits, continuous improvement) and the Toyota / TPS origin are used lightly, home-scaled, not overclaimed. The audit is framed as a kindness and a health check, warm and human; "audit" is a topic word used warmly, "inspection" appears only as the negated non-thing, and no force, control, or surveillance framing appears (the scan returned zero control uses across the whole package).

## Recommendations
1. Rotate CTA wording (highest-value, lowest-effort fix; several posts close on "Read the free chapter").
2. Pick one carousel version for LinkedIn.
3. Set the chapter page as the SEO canonical.
4. Keep Chapter 27 from blurring into its neighbors: distinct from Chapter 26 (the mindset, already delivered) and Chapter 5 (the one-time baseline audit) behind it, and distinct from the later Sustain practices ahead of it. Chapter 27 is the FIRST-CONCRETE-MECHANISM chapter: the recurring, drift-catching check-in and its reframe (a check-in, not an inspection; the four things; the no-blame rule; catch it early) and the honest first Sustain step along the last-gap path.
5. Re-sync all assets if the authored chapter changes after author review.
