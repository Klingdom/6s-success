# Newsletter Preview Text (Preheader): 6S Audits That Do Not Feel Like Inspections

Ten options, each 35 to 90 characters, each paired with a subject line. Char counts noted.

1. Pairs with #1 (A home audit is not an inspection. It is a check-in, a quick, warm look at the house, not the people.)
   "Same walk, same rooms, opposite feeling: a check-in, not an inspection." (71 chars)

2. Pairs with #2 (An audit checks the home, not the household. What it finds is always a system to fix, never a person to blame.)
   "It finds a system to fix, never a person to blame." (50 chars)

3. Pairs with #3 (The word audit makes everyone flinch. Same walk through the same rooms, opposite feeling.)
   "The word makes us flinch. A home audit is the gentle opposite." (62 chars)

4. Pairs with #4 (Keep it about the shelf, not the shelf-slipper. A drifted shelf is a system to fix, never a person to blame.)
   "A drifted shelf is not a character flaw. It is a system to top up." (66 chars)

5. Pairs with #5 (A drift caught this week is a two-minute fix; the same drift caught in six months is a lost weekend.)
   "Two minutes now, or a lost weekend later. Catch drift while it is small." (72 chars)

6. Pairs with #6 (You do not audit the home to judge it. You audit it so it never again has to be rescued.)
   "You do not audit a home to judge it, but to spare it a rescue." (62 chars)

7. Pairs with #7 (Keep it short and regular: a five-minute check every week beats a two-hour reckoning once a year.)
   "A five-minute check beats a yearly reckoning, because it actually happens." (74 chars)

8. Pairs with #8 (Let each person check their own zone. A self-audit you own beats an inspection you resent.)
   "Check your own zone, and it becomes quiet pride, not being inspected." (69 chars)

9. Pairs with #9 (It notices what is working, not only what has slipped, so you leave it encouraged, not scolded.)
   "It notices what is working, so you leave encouraged, not scolded." (65 chars)

10. Pairs with #10 (An audit is a kindness you do for your home, not a judgment you pass on your family.)
    "An audit is a kindness for your home, not a judgment on your family." (68 chars)
