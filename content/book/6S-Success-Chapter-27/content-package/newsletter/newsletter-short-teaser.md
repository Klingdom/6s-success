# Newsletter Short Teaser: 6S Audits That Do Not Feel Like Inspections

For a roundup or digest. 90 to 140 words.

---

You have the keeping mindset from the last chapter: a home is kept, not finished, on small shared habits. Now here is the first concrete way to keep the gains, a regular check-in on the home that catches drift while it is still small. But the word audit makes everyone flinch, because it sounds like a test with an inspector and a grade. A home audit is the opposite: a quick, regular, friendly look at the house, not the people, to see what is still working and catch small drift before it grows. It checks the home, never the household, so a drift it finds is a system to fix, not a person to blame. A drift caught this week is a two-minute fix; the same drift caught in six months is a lost weekend.
