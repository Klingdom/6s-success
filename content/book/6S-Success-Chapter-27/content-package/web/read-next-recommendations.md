3. Do one two-minute audit right now (the chapter action)
   Walk a single room and ask, of the room and not of anyone in it, "what has drifted since we set this up?" Fix the first small thing you find. That is a whole audit, and it was not frightening at all. It costs about two minutes, and it is the smallest possible proof of the whole idea: a home holds its shape when someone, now and then, simply looks at it with clear eyes and catches what has begun to slip while the slipping is still small.
