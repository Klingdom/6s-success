# Meta Description Options: Chapter 21, Find and Remove Hazards

Five options, each 140 to 160 characters, each with a primary or secondary keyword used naturally. Character counts are in parentheses.

1. A first pass catches the obvious dangers and walks past the rest. Make safety thorough: hunt room by room, ask the five safety questions, and fix each one.

   Note: 155 characters.

2. How to find hazards in your home without fear: run a hazard hunt, carry the same five safety questions into every room, and find it, and fix it now.

   Note: 148 characters.

3. The obvious hazards are the few; the hidden ones are the many. A room by room hazard hunt, five plain questions, and one rule: find it, and fix it now.

   Note: 151 characters.

4. Most home hazards hide in plain sight as ordinary objects. Hunt them room by room with five safety questions, and remove each one the moment you find it.

   Note: 153 characters.

5. Make your home thoroughly safe, room by room, today. Carry the five safety questions into every space, catch the hidden hazards, and fix it now, not later.

   Note: 155 characters.
