# SEO Title Options: Chapter 21, Find and Remove Hazards

Five title options. Character counts are in parentheses. The canonical title is listed first.

1. Find and Remove Hazards (23)
   Angle: canonical chapter title. Use as the H1 and primary on-page heading, with "the hazard hunt," "room by room," and "find it, and fix it now" early in the body.

2. The Hazard Hunt (15)
   Angle: leads with the hero device. Names the whole method in three words and is highly quotable and snippet-ready.

3. Find It, and Fix It Now (23)
   Angle: opens with the frozen rule, the line that makes the hunt end in action rather than in a list. Answers a real search for what to actually do about a hazard once you spot it.

4. The Five Safety Questions (25)
   Angle: leads with the secondary device, the short list you carry into every room. Answers a real search for what questions to ask to find hazards at home.

5. A Room by Room Safety Check (27)
   Angle: opens with the reader's real search, a repeatable way to walk the whole home. Concrete and outcome-led, and true to the calm, anti-shame register.
