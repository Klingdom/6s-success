# Read Next Recommendations: Chapter 21, Find and Remove Hazards

1. Chapter 22, Improve Overall Safety
   The next step, and where Safety is made to last. Chapter 21 found and removed the hazards already in the home, room by room; this chapter turns a one-time sweep into a home that stays safe, because homes do not hold still. It builds the safeguards that prevent new hazards, calm preparation for the few emergencies you cannot prevent, and the habit that keeps the home safe long after today's hunt. Chapter 21 makes Safety thorough; Chapter 22 completes the fourth S.

2. Chapter 20, Safety: Protect People First
   The setup, and the first-Safety-step position just behind the needle. Chapter 20 opened Safety, changed the question to could this hurt someone, triaged what it found, and handled the worst few dangers on one first pass. This chapter is the search that first pass was not, because a first pass catches the dangers that are obvious the moment you look and walks straight past the hidden many. Read how the lens opened Safety, then see how the hunt makes it thorough.

3. Give one room a full hunt today (the chapter action)
   Pick one room and give it a full hunt right now. Ask the five questions, and fix everything you can in ten minutes before you walk out: the cord tucked away, the pills moved up high, the rug taped down, the heavy thing brought off the top shelf. One room, thoroughly safe, today. It is the smallest possible proof of the whole idea.

4. The Hazard Hunt checklist (free resource)
   A printable carrying the five safety questions with room to tick found-and-fixed, a block per room, so a whole-home sweep can be run and nothing skipped.

5. The Room-by-Room Safety Sweep (free resource)
   A printable, a simple home map or ordered room list to walk in sequence, with the five questions repeated per room, so the hunt covers the whole home in order and finding and fixing stay one motion.
