# Newsletter Preview Text (Preheader): Find and Remove Hazards

Ten options, each 35 to 90 characters, each paired with a subject line. Char counts noted.

1. Pairs with #1 (Find it, and fix it now.)
   "Turn each hazard you find into one that is simply gone." (55 chars)

2. Pairs with #2 (Go room by room. Ask the same few questions in each. Fix what you find before you leave.)
   "The whole method, in a single line, for the whole home." (55 chars)

3. Pairs with #3 (A first pass finds what is obvious. A hunt finds the rest.)
   "The obvious dangers are the few. The hidden ones are the many." (62 chars)

4. Pairs with #4 (Most of what could hurt someone at home is hiding in plain sight)
   "Camouflaged as ordinary objects doing a quiet second job." (57 chars)

5. Pairs with #5 (The hazard hunt: one calm method for the whole home)
   "Calm, room by room, carrying the same five questions into each." (63 chars)

6. Pairs with #6 (The five questions that find the dangers a glance walks past)
   "Ask all five in every room, and the hidden hazards surface." (59 chars)

7. Pairs with #7 (A hazard you write down is still a hazard)
   "A hazard found but not fixed is not handled. It is scheduled." (61 chars)

8. Pairs with #8 (Trade a low hum of worry for a home whose dangers are simply gone)
   "Safety done thoroughly is quiet: it lets a parent breathe out." (62 chars)

9. Pairs with #9 (Why a clean, tidy home can still be full of hidden dangers)
   "Tidy and safe are different questions, and a glance misses the gap." (67 chars)

10. Pairs with #10 (How to make your whole home safe, room by room, today)
    "The second Safety step: the whole home hunted, not just glanced at." (67 chars)
