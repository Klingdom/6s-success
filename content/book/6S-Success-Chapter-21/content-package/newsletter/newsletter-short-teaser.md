# Newsletter Short Teaser: Find and Remove Hazards

For a roundup or digest. 90 to 140 words.

---

Last issue opened Safety and handled the obvious dangers, the ones that light up the moment you finally look. This chapter finds the rest, and the rest are the majority. A first pass finds the dangers that are obvious the moment you look; a hunt finds the ones that never will be, because familiarity hides the dangerous objects right along with the harmless ones. So you run a hazard hunt: a calm, room-by-room walk that carries the same five questions into every space. Could someone fall, could something burn, could something poison or choke or strangle, could something fall or cut or crush, could water and electricity meet. Find it, and fix it now, because a danger is only handled once it is gone. The friction meter takes a second Safety step.
