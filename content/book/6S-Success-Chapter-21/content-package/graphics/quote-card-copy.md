### Card 3 (verbatim)

### Card 5 (verbatim)

### Card 8 (verbatim)
> "A hazard you have found but not fixed is not handled. It is scheduled. And a hazard on a schedule is still a hazard, still able to hurt someone on any ordinary day before its turn comes up. The only safe place for a danger is gone."

- Attribution: 6S Success: Home Edition · Chapter 21
- Design: The found-not-fixed card, the rule's whole logic. Slate Fraunces on panel, small slate

### Card 9 (verbatim)

  "Pick one room and give it a full hunt right now. Ask the five questions, and fix everything you can in ten minutes before you walk out: the cord tucked away, the pills moved up high, the rug taped down, the heavy thing brought off the top shelf. One room, thoroughly safe, today."

- (verbatim, the 6S Tip)
  "Hunt every room twice, once standing and once low. Crouch to a small child's height and look again: the outlet at ankle level, the tablecloth a toddler can pull, the cleaning spray under the sink, the wobbling table leg. A whole layer of hazards lives in the band of space adults never occupy."

- (verbatim, the Common Mistake)
  "Walking the home with a notepad, listing hazards to deal with later, and calling that safety. The list is not the fix. Every danger you write down and leave in place is still live. Carry a roll of tape and a box instead of a notepad, and fix as you find."

  "Turn the hunt into a game. Give each person a room and the five questions and send everyone off to find hazards at once, and the younger the sharper for the low ones. Then come back together and fix the worst finds as a team. A home searched by five pairs of eyes misses far less than one."
