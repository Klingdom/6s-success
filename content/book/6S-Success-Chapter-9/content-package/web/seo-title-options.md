# SEO Title Options: Chapter 9, Necessary vs. Unnecessary

Five title options. Character counts are in parentheses. The canonical title is listed first.

1. Necessary vs. Unnecessary (25)
   Angle: canonical book title. Use as the H1 and primary on-page heading, with "use test" and "when did you last use it" early in the body.

2. The Use Test: When Did You Last Use It? (40)
   Angle: leads with the hero device and its exact question. Concrete, repeatable, very searchable.

3. Useful Is Not the Same as Used (30)
   Angle: the memorable reframe that names the confusion. Quotable and clarifying.

4. How to Stop Keeping Things Just in Case (39)
   Angle: speaks to the exact pattern readers get stuck on. Practical and specific.

5. Letting Go Is Not Wasting: Declutter Without Guilt (50)
   Angle: answers the sunk-cost block people actually feel, and promises relief in one line.
