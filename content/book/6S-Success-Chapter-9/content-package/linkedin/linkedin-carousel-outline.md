# Chapter 9 LinkedIn Carousel Outline: Necessary vs. Unnecessary

Twelve slides. Slide 1 hooks, last slide carries the soft CTA. Each slide has a headline and one or two lines of body.

---

## Slide 1: Hook

**Headline:** The clutter you cannot see is the useful kind.

**Body:** You cleared the surface. The basket beside it is still full of things that all belong here and never get used. Swipe for the one question that finds them.

---

## Slide 2: The gap

**Headline:** Everything left belongs here.

**Body:** That is not the same as needing it. The last chapter asked where a thing lives. This one asks whether you use it at all.

---

## Slide 3: The trap

**Headline:** A test that passes everything is not a test.

**Body:** Almost anything is useful in some imaginable circumstance. So "could this ever be useful" keeps everything. You need a narrower question.

---

## Slide 4: The reframe

**Headline:** Useful is not the same as used.

**Body:** Useful is a claim about the future. Used is a fact about the past. One is a story you tell about an object. The other is a record of what it has done.

---

## Slide 5: The Use Test

**Headline:** One question tests what stayed.

**Body:** When did you last use it? A fast, specific memory means necessary, and it stays. A shrug means unnecessary, and it goes in the out box.

---

## Slide 6: The two words

**Headline:** Necessary and unnecessary describe you, not the object.

**Body:** Necessary means you use it, or would replace it if it vanished. Unnecessary means it is only kept just in case. Neither judges the object's quality.

---

## Slide 7: The tiebreaker

**Headline:** Stuck on a hard one?

**Body:** If it vanished today, would you buy it again? It strips away the sunk cost. An easy yes keeps it. A slow no is your answer.

---

## Slide 8: Duplicates

**Headline:** You do not need three umbrellas.

**Body:** You need the one you actually grab. Necessary quietly includes a sensible quantity. Keep your favorite and let the rest go.

---

## Slide 9: The backup trap

**Headline:** The spare that guards nothing.

**Body:** If two things do one job, one of them is unnecessary, usually the one you never reach for. Keep the one your hand goes to. Let the understudy retire.

---

## Slide 10: The out box

**Headline:** Decide today, route later.

**Body:** The out box is a set-aside spot, not a destination. Where things go from there is a later chapter. Today you decide. Later you route.

---

## Slide 11: Letting go is not wasting

**Headline:** The money was spent the day you bought it.

**Body:** Keeping the thing unused does not earn it back. The waste, if any, already happened. Letting go is the end of paying twice.

---

**Headline:** Keep what earns its place by getting used.
