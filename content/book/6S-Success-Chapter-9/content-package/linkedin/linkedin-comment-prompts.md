# Chapter 9 LinkedIn Comment Prompts: Necessary vs. Unnecessary

Five prompts to start genuine discussion. Drop one in the comments of a post or article to invite replies.

---

1. Point the Use Test at one thing you keep just in case. When did you last actually use it? Name it here, no judgment.

2. What is the object you own several of and only need one of? Umbrellas, tote bags, scissors, cables. Which one would you actually keep?

3. Try the tiebreaker on something you are unsure about. If it vanished today, would you buy it again? Tell us the honest answer.

4. Be honest about the guilt. What is one unused thing you keep only because it cost money? Naming it out loud is often the whole release.

5. What is your genuine maybe, the thing that sits in your hand and refuses to answer? Park it here instead of on the shelf, and let the rest of the pass keep moving.
