# Newsletter Preview Text (Preheader): Necessary vs. Unnecessary

Ten options, each 35 to 90 characters, each paired with a subject line. Char counts noted.

1. Pairs with #1 (When did you last use it?)
   "That one question keeps what you use and sets the rest aside." (60 chars)

2. Pairs with #2 (Necessary vs. unnecessary)
   "Everything left belongs here. That is not the same as needing it." (64 chars)

3. Pairs with #3 (Useful is not the same as used)
   "One is a claim about the future. The other is a fact." (52 chars)

4. Pairs with #4 (One question keeps what you actually reach for)
   "No shopping, no scrubbing, just an honest look." (47 chars)

5. Pairs with #5 (Everything left belongs here. You still may not need it.)
   "Belonging got you a clear surface. Use gets you the rest." (56 chars)

6. Pairs with #6 (The second Sort question, and the harder one)
   "Not where a thing lives, but whether you use it at all." (54 chars)

7. Pairs with #7 (Keep what you use, set the rest aside)
   "The whole move is subtraction, and it costs nothing." (51 chars)

8. Pairs with #8 (The hardest clutter to see is the useful kind)
   "It is not the junk. It is the sensible surplus." (47 chars)

9. Pairs with #9 (If it vanished today, would you buy it again?)
   "The tiebreaker that strips away the sunk cost." (46 chars)

10. Pairs with #10 (Letting go of what you do not use is not wasting)
    "The money was spent when you bought it, not when you let go." (59 chars)
