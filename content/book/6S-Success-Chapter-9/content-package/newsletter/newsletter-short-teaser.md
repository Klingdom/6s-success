# Newsletter Short Teaser: Necessary vs. Unnecessary

For a roundup or digest. 90 to 140 words.

---

Last issue you cleared the console. It is still clear. But the basket beside it is full of things that all belong at an entryway and mostly never get used. That is the gap this chapter closes. Chapter 8 sorted by belonging; this one sorts by need. The reframe: useful is not the same as used. The tool is one honest question you ask of each thing that stayed: when did you last use it? A fast, specific memory means it is necessary, and it stays. A shrug means it is unnecessary, and it goes in the out box, a set-aside spot, not a destination. No shopping, no guilt, because letting go of what you do not use is not wasting. The friction meter takes an honest second step.
