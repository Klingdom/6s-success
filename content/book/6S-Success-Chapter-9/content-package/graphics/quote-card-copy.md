# Quote Card Copy: Chapter 9

**"Necessary vs. Unnecessary"**
*6S Success: Home Edition · Part Three · Sort*

Ten quote cards, each pulled from `chapter_09_manuscript.md`. Every quoted line below was
grep-verified as present in the manuscript. Cards 1 through 10 are **verbatim** (byte-identical
to the manuscript). The alternates at the end are **verbatim** unless explicitly marked
**lightly trimmed** (a lead-in dropped or a leading capital added), and are labeled as such.
Nothing here is invented.

Each card uses the warm book palette (paper `#F7F2E9`, panel `#FBF7EF`, ink `#2B2622`) and the
Fraunces display face for the quote, Newsreader italic for the attribution. No em dashes, no en
dashes, no floating hyphen separators; labels use the middot.

Suggested attribution for every card: **6S Success: Home Edition · Chapter 9**

### Card 3 (verbatim)
> "If it vanished today, would you buy it again?"

- Attribution: 6S Success: Home Edition · Chapter 9
- Design: The tiebreaker. Honey-outlined card, Newsreader italic, small honey eyebrow `FOR THE HARD CASES`. Quieter and warmer than Card 2.

### Card 4 (verbatim)
> "Last chapter asked where a thing lives. This chapter asks whether you need to keep it at all."

- Attribution: 6S Success: Home Edition · Chapter 9

### Card 5 (verbatim)
> "The just-in-case thing is a plausibly useful object kept for a someday that rarely comes, quietly taking the place of the things you use now."

- Attribution: 6S Success: Home Edition · Chapter 9
- Design: Naming card. Slate quote on panel, a faint honey mark like the just-in-case tags in the opener art. Eyebrow `NAME THE PATTERN`.

### Card 6 (verbatim)
> "The hardest clutter to notice is not the junk. It is the sensible surplus, each piece defensible, that together buries the handful of things you reach for every day."

- Attribution: 6S Success: Home Edition · Chapter 9
- Design: Ink on paper, best on landscape. A faint cluster of overlapping gray shapes behind the text, a few green-dotted keepers showing through at the front. Longer, give it width.

### Card 7 (verbatim)
> "That fast answer is the sound of a necessary thing."

- Attribution: 6S Success: Home Edition · Chapter 9
- Design: Ink on paper, calm rhythm. A single green calm-dot at the end of the line to mark the keeper.

### Card 8 (verbatim)
> "If two things do one job, one of them is surplus, and it is almost always the one you never reach for."

- Attribution: 6S Success: Home Edition · Chapter 9
- Design: The duplicates card. Two identical small glyphs, one with a green calm-dot, the other on a slate out-arrow toward a small `OUT BOX` tag. Eyebrow `DUPLICATES AND SURPLUS`.

### Card 9 (verbatim)
> "The waste, if there is any, already happened. Letting go is not the waste. It is the end of paying twice."

- Attribution: 6S Success: Home Edition · Chapter 9
- Design: The sunk-cost anchor. Panel card with a 2 px slate border, ink Fraunces text, generous space. The reframe the whole pass turns on.

### Card 10 (verbatim)
> "There is nothing to be ashamed of in owning a thing you no longer need."

- Attribution: 6S Success: Home Edition · Chapter 9
- Design: The anti-shame close. Ink on paper, dry and kind, plenty of air. No accent color; let the sentence stand alone.
