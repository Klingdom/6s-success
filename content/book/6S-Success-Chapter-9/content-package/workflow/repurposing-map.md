## Sequencing logic

1. Lock `canonical/` first. If the title, slug, the Use Test wording, "useful is not the same as used," the definition box, or "One Idea to Keep" changes, it ripples everywhere.
2. Build `web/` next, because the chapter URL is the destination every soft CTA points to.
3. Then social and email, which drive traffic to that URL.
4. Video, audio, slides, and graphics are higher-effort and can trail the text assets by a few days.
5. The Use Test card and the "useful is not the same as used" reframe are the two highest-value assets. Seed the card early and lead social with the reframe.
