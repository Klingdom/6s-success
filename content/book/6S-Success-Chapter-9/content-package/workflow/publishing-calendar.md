# Publishing Calendar: 14-Day Release Sequence

A two-week rollout for Chapter 9, "Necessary vs. Unnecessary". This is the second chapter of Part Three · Sort. Chapter 8 cleared what did not belong; this chapter picks up what stayed and asks whether you actually use it. The goal is to get readers to run one honest Use Test on a single thing they have been keeping just in case. Publish the home base (the web chapter) first, then drip social and email toward it. Dates are relative (Day 1 = the day the chapter page goes live).

Pacing principle: one anchor asset per day, plus one or two light reshares. The "useful is not the same as used" reframe and the Use Test card are your strongest. Lead with them.

## After Day 14

- Recycle the Use Test card and the strongest quote cards monthly.
- Keep the chapter page updated with an FAQ block for SEO (see `canonical/chapter-seo.md`). The Necessary vs. Unnecessary sheet is a strong lead magnet.
- When Chapter 10 publishes, link this chapter from its "previously" section, and link forward from here. This chapter is the second stop in the sorting work, so it hands off to the system for the items you honestly cannot call.

## Hero CTA reminder

This chapter's best conversion event is one honest release. Wherever you can, end with "pick up one thing you have been keeping just in case, ask when you last used it, and if you cannot remember, put it in an out box today. One honest release is a full necessary pass." A reader who sets one thing aside has started.
