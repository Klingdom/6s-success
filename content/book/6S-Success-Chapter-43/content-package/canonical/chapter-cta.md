# Chapter 43 CTA and Companion Resources: The Laundry Room

## Primary call to action
Do one zone this week, and make it the machines, because every other zone in this room is waiting on them. Then write this month on a strip of tape and stick it to the side of the dryer.

## Companion resources
- **The Laundry Room Reset card** (printable and phone wallpaper: the six zones, the done-looks-like for each, and the machines-first order of work).
- **The Laundry Room Kit list** (printable: the cleaners, tools, and storage types the room needs, grouped by job, with the two machine-specific inputs called out and the never-mix note).
- **The Dated Tape card** (the one-page standard for the duct: what to clear, how often, what to write, and what a foil accordion duct should be replaced with).

## Register and fences
- Warm, practical, expert, and plain about fire without alarmism. Product types only, never brand names. No hype, no invented statistics or prices. Never advise mixing cleaners.
- Friction meter retired for Part 9; close on the before-and-after room signature, which in this room contains a detail that expires.
- Previous: Chapter 42, The Guest Bathroom. Next: Chapter 44, The Home Office.

## Words to avoid
- No brand names (product types only). Never suggest mixing bleach with an ammonia based cleaner. Do not present duct clearing as optional maintenance, and do not give repair instructions beyond clearing, looking, and flagging.
