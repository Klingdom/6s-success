# Chapter 43 Brand Voice Check: The Laundry Room

## The register
Warm, practical, expert, plain. Second person. Short declaratives carrying the rules, longer sentences carrying the reasoning. No exclamation marks, no rhetorical questions used as filler (the two questions in the chapter, "can you name the month" and "think about where yours stalls", are both doing work).

## Tone risks specific to this chapter, and how they were handled

**Fire.** The obvious failure mode was alarm copy. The chapter states the physical facts plainly (most heat, most fine dry tinder, a metre apart), gives one concrete test, and moves on. The strongest sentence, "the failure it prevents burns the room it happens in", is placed once, at the end of the reframe, and is not repeated in any social asset except quote card 3 and the podcast, where it closes a section rather than opening one. No asset leads with fear.

**Condescension.** The chapter tells readers they have been telling themselves the dryer is slow, and that they are keeping a bag of socks for a reunion that will not happen. That register only works because it is consistently first-person-plural in spirit and never says "you should have". It reads as recognition, not correction. Checked, and it holds.

**The child-and-pod line.** "A pod looks exactly like a sweet to a toddler and bursts in the mouth" is the bluntest sentence in the chapter. It is taken from the Manual's own watch_for text and is kept verbatim in the safety frozen string because softening it would defeat its purpose. It appears once in the chapter and once in the X shorts, never in a headline position.

## Fences observed
- Product types only, never brands.
- Friction meter retired; the chapter closes on the before-and-after signature.
- Stays in the laundry room; does not re-teach the wet-room method (Ch 41), visual controls (Ch 14), named responsibility (Ch 24), or the ideal-state photo (Ch 25), all of which it uses and credits by reference.
- The word "utility" is used for the room class, not as a euphemism for unimportant.

## Verdict
On voice. No leaks, no hype, no blame, no repair advice.
