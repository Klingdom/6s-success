# Chapter 43 Infographic Spec: The Laundry Room

## Figure 2: the six zones
- Canvas 1000 x 440, warm paper fill.
- Title in Fraunces, 22px: "The laundry room, drawn as its six zones".
- Six panels, two rows of three, each 270 x 130, rounded 8, panel fill with a rule-coloured stroke.
- Each panel carries a green numbered disc and a serif zone name: 1 Washer and dryer, 2 Detergent zone, 3 Sorting and hampers, 4 Folding surface, 5 Air-dry rack, 6 Utility corner.
- Footer in terracotta: "one room, six small jobs, and five of them wait on the machines".

## Figure 3: the method and the kit
- Canvas 1000 x 480, two panels of 460 x 410.
- Left panel headed "The method": four rules, each a coloured lead line with grey gloss lines beneath. Lead lines: "Lint is fuel, not fluff" (terracotta), "Nothing is put away wet" (slate), "Clean the machine that cleans" (green), "The flow jams at the counter" (terracotta).
- Right panel headed "The kit": nine lines of product types, grouped by job. The drum cleaning tablet and duct brush share one line as this room's only unshared inputs.
- Footer in terracotta: "clear the lint, dry everything, clean the machine, keep the counter bare".

## Figure 4: the signature
- Canvas 1000 x 430, matched before and after panels with a slate camera disc between them.
- The after panel carries an extra honey-outlined tape label reading "duct cleared: this month". This element is unique to this chapter and marks the standard that expires.
