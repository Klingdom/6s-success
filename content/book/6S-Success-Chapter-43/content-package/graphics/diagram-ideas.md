# Chapter 43 Diagram Ideas: The Laundry Room

1. **The flow with four stages** (hero): dirty, washed, dry, away, drawn as four linked boxes with an owner's name slot under each. The fourth slot is empty, and the arrow out of it is greyed. Caption: a flow stops at whichever stage nobody owns.
2. **The three hiding places**: a cutaway washing machine with three call-outs, the gasket folds, the detergent drawer and its cavity, and the drum.
3. **The six zones plan**: two rows of three, with the five downstream zones tinted and the machines zone highlighted as the head of the room.
4. **The duct, foil against rigid**: two short pipe runs side by side, the accordion foil packed grey at the bend, the rigid metal clear. Caption: same room, same load, different amount of fuel.
5. **The counter's two states**: empty, or one load in progress. A third panel crossed out. Caption: there is no third state.
6. **The dated tape**: a strip of masking tape with a month written on it, drawn at scale on the side of a dryer, with the note that this is the only standard in the book that expires.
7. **Nothing goes away wet**: six small icons, door ajar, dried gasket folds, drawer, rack folded flat, mop hung, liner in the wash.
