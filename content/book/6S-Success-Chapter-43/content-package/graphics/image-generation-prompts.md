# Chapter 43 Image Generation Prompts: The Laundry Room

House style: warm paper background, editorial illustration, serif display type, muted terracotta, honey, slate, and green accents. No logos, no brand names, no readable packaging text, no QR codes. No em dashes in any rendered text.

1. **Opener, two states.** A domestic laundry room drawn twice side by side. Left: both machine lids covered in bottles, a shelf of mismatched containers with dried drips, a counter piled with an unfolded basket, a broom leaning against a wall. Right: the same room, both lids clear, the washer door propped slightly open, containers standing on a shallow tray, a bare counter, a folding rack flat against the wall, a broom hanging on a wall rail.
2. **The six zones.** A warm overhead floor plan of a small laundry room with six numbered areas marked and labelled in a serif face.
3. **The three hiding places.** A clean editorial cutaway of a front-loading washing machine with three areas gently highlighted: the rubber door seal folds, the pull-out detergent drawer and the cavity behind it, and the drum.
4. **Behind the dryer.** A dryer eased forward from the wall, the flexible duct visible at the bend, a person kneeling with a long brush. Warm, calm, instructional, not alarming.
5. **The folding counter, two states.** A bare counter with empty baskets stacked beneath and spare hangers on a rail; beside it the same counter holding one load mid-fold.
6. **The dated tape.** A close crop of a strip of masking tape on the painted side panel of a dryer with a month handwritten on it in pencil.
7. **The signature pair.** A matched before-and-after of the whole room from the doorway, same framing, same light.
