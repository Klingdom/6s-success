# Chapter 43 LinkedIn Carousel Outline: The Laundry Room

Eleven slides. Product types only.

- Slide 1 (hook): You are not doing more laundry. You are doing the same laundry twice.
- Slide 2: The first process room in the book. Not a place you live, a machine you live with.
- Slide 3: The flow has four stages: dirty, washed, dry, away. It stalls at whichever one nobody owns.
- Slide 4: Six zones, and the order matters, because five of them wait on the machines.
- Slide 5: Lint is fuel, not fluff. The screen every load, the duct once a year.
- Slide 6: The test: can you name the month you last cleared the duct? If not, that is today's job.
- Slide 7: Nothing goes away wet. Door ajar, folds dried, rack folded flat, mop hung to dry.
- Slide 8: A washer is not self-cleaning. Three hiding places: the gasket, the drawer, the drum.
- Slide 9: The counter is empty or holds exactly one load. There is no third state.
- Slide 10: The safety pass here is about fire, and it is the reason the chapter exists.
- Slide 11 (close): Do the machines first. Then write this month on tape and stick it to the dryer.
