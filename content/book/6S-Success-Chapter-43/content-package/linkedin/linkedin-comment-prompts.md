# Chapter 43 LinkedIn Comment Prompts: The Laundry Room

Use one per post to invite replies without asking for engagement outright.

1. Can you name the month you last cleared the duct behind your dryer?
2. Which stage does your laundry actually stall at: dirty, washed, dry, or away?
3. Has anyone in your house ever been named as the person who carries the clean clothes out?
4. How many sorting streams does your household genuinely honour, and how many just wait?
5. When did you last pull the detergent drawer right out and wash it?
6. What is currently parked on your folding counter that was never laundry?
7. How big has your rag supply quietly become?
8. Is there a garment on your drying rack you have washed more often than you have worn?
