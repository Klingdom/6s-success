# X Short Posts: The Laundry Room

Chapter 43 of *6S Success: Home Edition*. Ten standalone posts, each at or under 280 characters counting its number line. Plain numbering.

---

1/
You are not doing more laundry than you used to. You are doing the same laundry twice. Every dry load that goes back in for a second tumble because the counter was full is work you paid for once already.

(206 chars)

---

2/
Your dryer has not got slower. It is blocked. If you cannot name the month you last pulled it out and cleared the duct, that is today's job, and there is no debate about it.

(176 chars)

---

3/
While the dryer is out and the duct is off, look at what it is made of. A flexible foil accordion duct traps far more lint than smooth rigid metal. Replace it now, because there is rarely a next time.

(203 chars)

---

4/
A washing machine is not self-cleaning. It hides its dirt in three places: the folds of the door gasket, the detergent drawer and the cavity behind it, and the drum. None is the part you can see.

(198 chars)

---

5/
That faint smell in your clean laundry is not the detergent. It is the machine. You have been washing every load in the residue of every load before it.

(155 chars)

---

6/
Your folding counter is not the problem. Laundry has four stages: dirty, washed, dry, away. Most homes own the first three. Nobody was ever named to do the fourth. That is the whole jam.

(189 chars)

---

7/
Every other room gets dirty. The laundry room gets damp, and damp spreads. Door ajar between loads, gasket folds dried and not just wiped, rack folded flat, mop hung to dry. Nothing goes away wet.

(199 chars)

---

8/
A stream earns its own hamper only if it has run as a full load, by itself, in the last month. A bag that waits three weeks is not sorting. It is clothes you have taken out of circulation.

(191 chars)

---

9/
Detergent pods go latched or above shoulder height, without exception. A pod looks exactly like a sweet to a toddler, and it bursts in the mouth.

(148 chars)

---

10/
Do one zone this week, and make it the machines, because every other zone waits on them. Then write this month on tape and stick it to the dryer. Chapter 43. Next . Ch 44, The Home Office.

(192 chars)
