# X Thread: The Laundry Room

Chapter 43 of *6S Success: Home Edition*, the thirteenth room playbook of Part Nine and the first utility room. One thread, 14 posts. Each at or under 280 characters, counting its number line. Plain "1/" numbering on its own line.

---

1/
Part Nine reaches the first utility room, and the first room in the book that is not a place you live: the laundry room. It is a machine you live with, and it fails in ways no bedroom does.

(192 chars)

---

2/
If your household feels like it is drowning in laundry, you are almost certainly not doing more washing than you used to. You are doing the same washing twice.

(162 chars)

---

3/
Laundry is a flow with four stages: dirty, washed, dry, away. It is not finished until the fourth one happens, and it can stall at any of them. Every stall costs you the work again.

(184 chars)

---

4/
The biggest stall is the folding counter. But here is the surprise: the counter is not the problem. A flow stops at whichever stage nobody owns.

(147 chars)

---

5/
Most households own the first three stages without discussing them. Somebody loads it. Somebody moves it. Somebody tips it out. Then it sits, because stage four was never given to a person by name.

(200 chars)

---

6/
That is the whole jam. Not laziness, not the folding. An unowned step. Give it a name, put one basket per bedroom under the counter so folding and delivering are one motion, and the counter empties.

(201 chars)

---

7/
Six zones: the machines, the detergent shelf, the sorting bags, the folding counter, the drying rack, the utility corner. The order is not a suggestion. Five of the six wait on the machines.

(193 chars)

---

8/
Second idea: a washing machine is not self-cleaning. It hides its dirt in three places, and none of them is the part you can see.

(132 chars)

---

9/
The gasket, whose folds you peel back one at a time to find coins, hairpins and grey slime. The detergent drawer, which lifts right out, over a cavity often filmed with black mould. And the drum.

(198 chars)

---

10/
So that faint smell in your clean laundry is not the detergent. It is the machine, and you have been washing every load in the residue of every load before it.

(163 chars)

---

11/
Now the part that matters most. Outside the kitchen this is the hottest room in your house and the one with the most fine dry tinder, and those two facts sit about a metre apart behind the dryer.

(199 chars)

---

12/
The lint screen you clear every load is the part you can see. It is not the part that burns. The fuel that matters is the soft grey packing where the duct bends behind the machine.

(184 chars)

---

13/
One question: can you name the month you last pulled the dryer out and cleared that duct? If you cannot, that is today's job. And check what it is made of. Foil traps far more lint than rigid metal.

(202 chars)

---

14/
Do one zone this week, and make it the machines. Then write this month on a strip of tape and stick it to the dryer. Full steps for all six zones in Chapter 43.
