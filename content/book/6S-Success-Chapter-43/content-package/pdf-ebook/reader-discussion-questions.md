# Chapter 43 Reader Discussion Questions: The Laundry Room

1. Of the four stages, dirty, washed, dry, and away, which one does your laundry actually stall at? Who owns each of the other three?
2. Has anyone in your household ever been named, out loud, as the person who carries the clean clothes out of the room? What would change if someone were?
3. Can you name the month you last cleared the duct behind your dryer? If not, when will you?
4. Have you ever run an empty hot cycle with a drum cleaning tablet, or pulled the detergent drawer right out to wash it?
5. What is on your folding counter right now that was never laundry, and how long has it been there?
6. How many sorting streams does your household genuinely honour, and how many bags just wait?
7. Which of the six calls in this chapter is the one you have been postponing longest?
8. What in your laundry room is currently being put away damp?
