# Chapter 43 Ebook Description: The Laundry Room

The thirteenth room playbook of Part Nine, and the first utility room.

Every room in 6S Success so far has been a place where somebody does something. The laundry room is the first one that is not: a process room, judged by exactly one thing, which is whether the work gets through it without stopping. Laundry arrives dirty, sits wet, and is not finished until it is back in a drawer, and it can stall at any one of those points.

This chapter breaks the room into six zones and gives, for each, the inputs to gather and the exact order of steps to clean and shine it: the washer and dryer, the detergent and treatment zone, the sorting and hamper zone, the folding surface, the hanging and air-dry zone, and the utility and cleaning zone. The order matters more here than in any other room, because five of the six are downstream of the machines.

On top of the nine rules of Shine it adds four of its own, and one of them makes this chapter unlike any other in the book: outside the kitchen, this is the hottest room in the house and the one with the most fine dry tinder in it, so the safety pass is not a tidy-up. It is the reason the chapter exists, and it is why this room's standard carries a date.
