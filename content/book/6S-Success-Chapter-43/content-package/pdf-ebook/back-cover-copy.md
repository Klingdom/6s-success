# Chapter 43 Back Cover Copy: The Laundry Room

*You are not doing more laundry. You are doing the same laundry twice.*

The laundry room is the first room in this book that is not a place you live. It is a machine you live with, and it is judged by whether the work flows through it: dirty, washed, dry, away.

Six zones. Four rules on top of the nine. The three places a washing machine hides its own dirt. And one question that decides whether today has a job in it: can you name the month you last cleared the duct behind your dryer?

**6S Success: Home Edition**, Chapter Forty-Three.
