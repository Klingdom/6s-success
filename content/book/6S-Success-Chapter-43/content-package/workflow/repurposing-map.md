## Reuse cautions
- Types, not brands, everywhere. No brand names or invented prices. Never advise mixing cleaners.
- Never depict two cleaners being mixed, anyone reaching into a running machine, or work behind a machine that is still plugged in.
- Do not turn the fire material into alarm copy. It is maintenance, delivered plainly.
- Every asset closes on the before-and-after signature (friction meter retired for Part 9), and keeps the dated tape in any depiction of the after.
- Stay in the laundry room: do not reuse guest-bathroom (Ch 42) or home-office (Ch 44) material.
