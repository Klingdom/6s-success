# Chapter 43 LinkedIn Carousel Copy: The Laundry Room

**Slide 1**
You are not doing more laundry.
You are doing the same laundry twice.

**Slide 2**
The laundry room is not a place you live.
It is a machine you live with.

**Slide 3**
Laundry has four stages: dirty, washed, dry, away.
A flow stops at whichever stage nobody owns.

**Slide 4**
Six zones. Start at the machines.
Five of the six are waiting on them.

**Slide 5**
Lint is fuel, not fluff.
The screen every load. The duct once a year.

**Slide 6**
The test: can you name the month you last cleared the duct?
If not, that is today's job.

**Slide 7**
Nothing goes away wet.
Door ajar. Folds dried. Rack folded flat.

**Slide 8**
A washer is not self-cleaning.
Three hiding places: the gasket, the drawer, the drum.

**Slide 9**
The counter is empty, or it holds exactly one load.
There is no third state.

**Slide 10**
The jam is not the folding.
It is the job nobody was ever given.

**Slide 11**
Do the machines this week.
Then write this month on tape and stick it to the dryer.
