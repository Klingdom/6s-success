# Chapter 43 Visual Storyboard: The Laundry Room

Four figures, matching the chapter's SVGs. Warm paper, terracotta, honey, slate, green. Types, not brands.

## Figure 1: the opener, two states
A full-width pair. Left, the room as it runs: both machine lids buried under bottles, a washer door shut on a damp drum, a shelf of half used stain removers weeping onto the wood, a counter holding yesterday's basket, a broom leaning behind the door. Right, the same room: both drums empty with the door propped ajar, one product per job on a wipeable tray, a bare counter, the rack folded flat, and a strip of tape on the dryer with a month written on it.

## Figure 2: the six zones
A warm overhead plan, six numbered cells in two rows of three: washer and dryer, detergent zone, sorting and hampers, folding surface, air-dry rack, utility corner. Footer line: one room, six small jobs, and five of them wait on the machines.

## Figure 3: the method and the kit
Two panels. Left, the four rules of the laundry layer stacked with a short gloss under each. Right, the kit as product types, grouped by job, with the two machine-specific inputs on their own line.

## Figure 4: the before-and-after signature
A matched pair from the same doorway, with the camera icon between them. The after panel carries one extra element the other room chapters do not have: a small tape label reading "duct cleared: this month", drawn in honey, marking the one detail in the photo that expires.
