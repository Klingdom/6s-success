# Chapter 43 Teaching Slide Deck Outline: The Laundry Room

Sixteen slides, about 25 minutes.

1. Title: The Laundry Room. Part Nine, room-by-room playbooks.
2. The claim: you are not doing more laundry, you are doing the same laundry twice.
3. The first process room: not a place you live, a machine you live with.
4. Laundry as a flow: dirty, washed, dry, away. A flow stops where nobody owns it.
5. The three stall points, and which one your household has.
6. The six zones, and why the order is not a suggestion.
7. The nine rules of Shine, taken as read from Chapter 31.
8. Rule one of the laundry layer: lint is fuel, not fluff.
9. The duct test: name the month, or it is today's job. Foil versus rigid metal.
10. Rule two: nothing is put away wet. Damp as the failure that spreads.
11. Rule three: clean the machine that cleans. The three hiding places.
12. Rule four: the flow jams at the counter, and the jam is an unnamed job.
13. The centerpiece: zone by zone, inputs and numbered steps (walk one zone live).
14. The six hard calls, from the duct to the rag pile.
15. Safety: the five questions in the hottest room in the house.
16. The signature: a before-and-after whose after contains a detail that expires.
