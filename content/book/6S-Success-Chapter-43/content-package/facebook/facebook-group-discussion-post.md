# Chapter 43 Facebook Group Discussion Post: The Laundry Room

**Question for the group: can you name the month you last cleared the duct behind your dryer?**

No judgement, because most people cannot, and that is exactly the point. The lint screen we clear every load is the part we can see. The fuel that actually matters is the soft grey packing where the duct bends behind the machine, in the room that combines the most heat with the most fine dry tinder outside the kitchen.

If the dryer seems to have got a bit slower, and towels seem to need two cycles now, that is not age. That is a blocked duct.

Two more to talk about while we are here:

- **Where does your laundry actually stall?** Dirty, washed, dry, or away? Almost everyone stalls at away, and it is usually because nobody was ever named as the person who carries the clean clothes out of the room.
- **Have you ever pulled the detergent drawer right out and washed it in the sink?** There is a cavity behind it, and the roof of that cavity is often filmed with black mould.

Tell us which one you are going to do this week.
