# Chapter 43 Facebook Longform Post: The Laundry Room

---

Here is something worth knowing about laundry.

When a household says it is drowning in laundry, it is almost never doing more washing than it used to. It is running the same washing twice.

The laundry room is a process room, judged by one thing: whether the work gets through it without stopping. Laundry arrives dirty, sits wet, and is not finished until it is back in a drawer. Every stall costs you the work again.

The biggest stall is the folding counter, and the surprise is that the counter is not the problem. Laundry has four stages: dirty, washed, dry, away. Most households own the first three without thinking. Somebody loads it, somebody moves it, somebody tips it out. Then the clean clothes sit, because the last stage, carrying them out and putting them away, has never been given to a person by name. That is the whole jam. Give it a name and a basket per bedroom, so folding and delivering are one motion, and the counter empties.

The second thing worth knowing is that a washing machine is not self-cleaning. It hides its dirt in three places you have probably never opened: the folds of the rubber door gasket, the detergent drawer and the cavity behind it, and the drum. If your clean laundry has a faint smell you cannot place, that is not the detergent. That is the machine.

And the third is the one that matters most. Outside the kitchen, this is the hottest room in your house and the one with the most fine dry tinder in it, and those two facts sit about a metre apart behind the dryer. The lint screen you clear every load is the part you can see, and it is not the part that burns. The fuel that matters is the soft grey packing where the duct bends behind the machine.

So here is the test. Can you name the month you last pulled the dryer out and cleared the duct? If you cannot, that is today's job. And while the machine is out, look at what the duct is made of, because a flexible foil accordion duct traps far more lint than smooth rigid metal, and there is rarely a next time.

Once a year is a cheap habit, and the failure it prevents burns the room it happens in.

Do one zone this week, and make it the machines. Then write this month on a strip of tape and stick it to the side of the dryer.
