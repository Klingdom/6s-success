# Chapter 43 Facebook Posts: The Laundry Room

Five standalone posts.

## 1
When a household says it is drowning in laundry, it is almost never doing more washing than it used to. It is running the same washing twice. A dry load goes back in for a second tumble because the counter was full, and that second tumble is pure waste.

## 2
Your dryer has not got slower. It is blocked. If you cannot name the month you last pulled the machine out and cleared the duct, that is today's job, and while it is out, check whether the duct is the flexible foil kind. Foil traps far more lint than rigid metal.

## 3
A washing machine is not self-cleaning. It hides its dirt in three places: the folds of the door gasket, the detergent drawer and the cavity behind it, and the drum. That faint smell in your clean laundry is not the detergent. It is the machine.

## 4
The folding counter is not the problem. Laundry has four stages: dirty, washed, dry, away. Most households own the first three. The last one has never been given to a person by name, and that is the whole jam.

## 5
Nothing in a laundry room gets put away wet. The washer door stands ajar, the gasket folds get dried and not just wiped, the rack is folded flat, and the mop hangs to dry. It is the single change that stops a laundry room smelling like a laundry room.
