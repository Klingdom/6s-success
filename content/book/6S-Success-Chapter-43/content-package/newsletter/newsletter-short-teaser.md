# Chapter 43 Newsletter Short Teaser: The Laundry Room

The laundry room is the first room in this book that is not a place you live. It is a process room, and it is judged by one thing: whether the work gets through it without stopping.

Chapter 43 breaks it into six zones with the exact inputs and numbered steps for each, and adds four rules to the nine rules of Shine: lint is fuel, nothing is put away wet, the machine that cleans needs cleaning, and the flow jams at the counter.

Start at the machines. Every other zone in the room is waiting on them.
