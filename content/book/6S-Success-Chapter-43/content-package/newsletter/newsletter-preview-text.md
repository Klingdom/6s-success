# Chapter 43 Newsletter Preview Text: The Laundry Room

1. The first process room in the book, and the one with the most heat and the most tinder.
2. Lint is fuel, damp spreads, and the whole household jams at one counter.
3. Three places a washing machine hides its own dirt, and none of them are the drum you can see.
4. Why the folding counter is full, and why it is not about folding.
5. The one standard in this room that expires and has to be earned again.
