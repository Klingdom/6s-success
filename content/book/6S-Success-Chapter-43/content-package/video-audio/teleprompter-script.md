# Chapter 43 Teleprompter Script: The Laundry Room

*Two minutes. Read at an even pace. Pause at each line break.*

The laundry room is the first room in this book that is not somewhere you live.

It is a machine you live with, and it is judged by one thing only.

Whether the work flows through it.

Laundry arrives dirty. It sits wet. And it is not finished until it is back in a drawer.

It can stall at any one of those points, and every stall costs you the work again.

So we clean this room for the flow, not for the look of it.

First: lint is fuel, not fluff.

The screen you clear every load is the part you can see. It is not the part that burns.

The fuel that matters is where the duct bends behind the dryer.

So here is the test. Can you name the month you last cleared it?

If you cannot, that is today's job.

Second: nothing is put away wet.

The washer door stands ajar. The gasket folds are dried, not just wiped. The rack is folded flat.

Third: clean the machine that cleans.

A washer hides its dirt in three places. The gasket. The drawer. The drum.

And fourth: the flow jams at the counter, and the jam is not about folding.

It is that carrying the clothes out and putting them away is the one stage nobody was ever given.

Six zones. Start at the machines, because the other five are waiting on them.

Then write this month on a strip of tape, and stick it to the side of the dryer.
