# Chapter 43 B-Roll and Visual Notes: The Laundry Room

## Must-get shots
- Both machine lids cleared, and the washer door being propped ajar. This is the image of the whole chapter.
- Hands peeling back a rubber door gasket fold, one at a time. Shoot close and slow.
- The detergent drawer pulled out to its stop and lifted free, then washed in a sink.
- The empty drawer cavity, torch-lit, showing the roof of it.
- A dryer eased out from the wall on its feet; the duct disconnected; a long brush going through.
- The two ducts side by side: flexible foil accordion against smooth rigid metal.
- Masking tape torn, a month written on it in pencil, and the strip pressed onto the dryer's side panel.
- The counter cleared right down, then wiped before folding starts, not after.
- Baskets stacked underneath, one per bedroom, and a load being folded straight into them.
- The rack being folded flat against the wall, and the drip tray rinsed and dried.
- A broom being hung heads up on a wall rail, with a painted outline behind it.

## Do not shoot
- Any readable brand name, logo, or packaging text. Types only.
- Two cleaners near each other in a way that implies mixing.
- Anyone reaching into a machine that is running, or working behind a machine still plugged in.
- Anything that looks like an appliance repair. This is clear, look, and flag only.

## Tone
Calm and workmanlike, not alarming. The fire material is delivered plainly, at normal pace, as maintenance rather than warning.
