# Chapter 43 Short Video Scripts: The Laundry Room

Five scripts, 30 to 60 seconds each.

## 1. You are doing it twice
**Hook:** You are not doing more laundry. You are doing the same laundry twice.
**Body:** Laundry has four stages: dirty, washed, dry, away. Every time a dry load goes back in for a second tumble because the counter was full, you paid for that work twice. The volume never changed. The jam did.
**Close:** Fix the jam, not the workload.

## 2. Can you name the month?
**Hook:** One question tells you whether your dryer is a fire risk.
**Body:** Can you name the month you last pulled it out and cleared the duct? Not the lint screen. The duct behind the machine, where the soft grey packing builds at the bend. If you cannot name the month, that is today's job.
**Close:** Once a year is a cheap habit.

## 3. Three places a washer hides its dirt
**Hook:** Your washing machine is not self-cleaning.
**Body:** One: the folds of the rubber door gasket, peeled back one at a time. Two: the detergent drawer, which lifts right out, and the cavity behind it. Three: the drum, which needs a hot empty cycle with a cleaning tablet.
**Close:** That smell in your clean laundry is not the detergent.

## 4. The jam is not the folding
**Hook:** Your folding counter is not the problem.
**Body:** A flow stops at whichever stage nobody owns. Somebody loads the washer. Somebody moves it to the dryer. Somebody tips it out. Nobody was ever named to carry it out of the room and put it away.
**Close:** Give the last stage a name and the counter empties.

## 5. Nothing goes away wet
**Hook:** Here is why a clean laundry room still smells.
**Body:** Every other room gets dirty. This one gets damp. The washer door goes ajar between loads, the gasket folds get dried and not just wiped, the rack folds flat so both faces get air, and the mop hangs to dry.
**Close:** Nothing in this room gets put away wet.
