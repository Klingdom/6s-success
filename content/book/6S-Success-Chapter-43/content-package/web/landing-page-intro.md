# Chapter 43 Landing Page Intro: The Laundry Room

Every room in this book so far has been a place where somebody does something. The laundry room is the first one that is not. It is a process room, judged by exactly one thing: whether the work gets through it without stopping. Laundry arrives dirty, sits wet, and is not finished until it is back in a drawer, and it can stall at any of those three points. When a household says it is drowning in laundry, it is almost never doing more washing than it used to. It is running the same washing twice.

This chapter breaks the laundry room into six small zones and gives you, for each one, the exact inputs to gather and the numbered steps to clean and shine it: the washer and dryer, the detergent and treatment zone, the sorting and hamper zone, the folding surface, the hanging and air-dry zone, and the utility and cleaning zone. The order matters more here than anywhere else, because five of the six are downstream of the machines. On top of the nine rules of Shine it adds four of its own: lint is fuel, nothing is put away wet, the machine that does the cleaning needs cleaning, and the flow jams at the counter.

Product types only, no brands. Start the empty hot cycle before you touch anything else, and work the machines first.
