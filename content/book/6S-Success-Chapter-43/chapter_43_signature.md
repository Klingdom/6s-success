# Chapter 43 Signature: The Laundry Room

The Part 9 recurring close. The friction meter is retired (it reached GOAL at Chapter 30); each room playbook ends instead on a matched before-and-after room signature: the before kept per Chapter 6, the after held as the ideal-state standard per Chapter 25, with a copy taped inside a cupboard door as the standard the room matches.

## The before-and-after room signature
Stand in the doorway. In one frame, capture both machines, the folding counter, and the corner where the broom lives. Take the before photo before you touch a thing, and do not tidy first, because the point of the photo is the room as it actually runs. Take the after as the standard.

## The variation unique to this room
This is the only signature in the book whose after photo contains a detail that expires. Every other room's after can be restored in an evening. This one carries a strip of masking tape on the side of the dryer with a month written on it, and that month has to be earned again next year. The photo holds the state of the room; the tape holds the one standard that time can quietly cancel.

## The before-and-after caption (frozen, byte-verbatim)
> This is the laundry room the whole loop was for: the same room you started with, both drums empty and the washer door propped ajar, nothing on either lid, the counter bare, the rack folded flat against the wall, and every handle hanging heads up over its outline. Take the before photo before you touch a thing, and take the after as the standard. Then look at the one detail in the after photo that expires: the strip of tape on the side of the dryer with a month written on it. Every other thing in this picture you can restore in an evening. That one you have to earn again next year, and it is the one that keeps the room from burning.

## One Idea to Keep (frozen, byte-verbatim)
> A laundry room is not clean because the surfaces are wiped. It is clean because the work flows through it: the lint is out, nothing was put away wet, the machine that does the cleaning has itself been cleaned, and the counter is bare because somebody by name carries the clean clothes out. Do one zone this week, and make it the machines, because every other zone in this room is waiting on them. Then write this month on a strip of tape and stick it to the side of the dryer.
