# Chapter 15 LinkedIn Comment Prompts: The Room Map

Five prompts to start genuine discussion. Drop one in the comments of a post or article to invite replies. Anti-shame voice: a private, in-your-head map is a natural stage, never a family's failing.

---

1. Group by what you do, not by what things are. Look at one busy corner of your home and name, in a single word, the activity that happens there. What is the one zone your household clearly runs on, and what does it need gathered into it?

2. Here is the honest one. What is the one piece of your home's arrangement that lives only in your head, the logic everyone else follows but nobody else can see? No blame owed; a private map is a natural stage, not a failing. Naming it is the first step to drawing it.

3. The coffee zone test: pick a task you do every day and count how many places its pieces live. Coffee scattered across three cupboards and a shelf, or gathered at the machine? Tell us one task in your house that is still spread out, and where its zone should be.

4. The keys, leash, bag, and umbrella by the door were never six separate spots. They were one zone all along: the launch pad. What would you name yours, and what belongs in it?

5. A map that lives in one person's head is a private map, and a private map fails the moment that person steps out. Have you ever drawn your home's layout and walked the household through it? What changed in who the room belonged to afterward?
