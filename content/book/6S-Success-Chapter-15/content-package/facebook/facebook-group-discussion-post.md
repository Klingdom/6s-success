# Facebook Group Discussion Post (Home and Organizing Groups)

Written for a home-and-organizing Facebook group. Leads with a genuinely useful nugget (group by activity, not category) then asks an open question to get people talking. Friendly group tone, soft CTA at the very end, and written so it does NOT read like an ad. No em dashes.

---

Okay, the one small shift that finally made my house make sense to everyone in it, and then I want to hear where your home keeps colliding.

For years I organized by category. All the kitchen things in one cupboard, all the office stuff in one drawer. It felt tidy, and it quietly failed me every single day, because I do not actually live by category. I live by activity.

Here is the thing that flipped it for me. Nobody stands in the kitchen thinking, time to interact with my kitchen things. They think, I want coffee, and then they need the mug and the beans and the grinder and the filters, four items that a category-minded house happily scatters across three cupboards and a shelf. Gather those four at the machine and making a cup collapses from a lap of the kitchen to a single reach. Nothing bought, nothing thrown out. The same objects just gathered around the thing you do with them.

That one spot is a zone. Group by what you do, not by what things are. Name it by the verb: the coffee zone, the homework corner, the charging station, the launch pad by the door where the keys and leash and bag all live. And the best part is the groups are already there. Watch your household for a day and the zones announce themselves by where people keep colliding.

Said without any blame, because a house organized by category is just what happens when you build one shelf at a time. It is a completely natural stage to reach.

So here is what I am genuinely curious about.

Where in your home do people keep colliding, the counter or corner where several things pile up because one activity happens there? And if you named that spot in a single word and gathered everything that activity needs into it, what would you call the zone?

I will go first in the comments with my kitchen counter, which has secretly been a coffee zone for years without anyone admitting it. 😄
