# Chapter 15 · Repurposing Map

How the one chapter fans out into every channel, and which idea leads on each.

## The spine (reused across channels)
- Good homes, no shared picture: assigned, placed, and speaking, yet the view lives only behind one person's eyes
- A room map is a single shared picture of a space, organized into zones by activity
- The rule: group by what you do, not by what things are
- A zone gathers everything one activity needs into the place that activity happens (coffee at the machine, launch pad by the door)
- The payoff: the entryway was one zone all along, the launch pad, and the home is just a handful of zones
- Draw it and share it: a napkin sketch, then walk the household through it, from your room to their room
- The STRAIGHTEN COMPLETE mark on the friction meter (two of six S's done; nothing bought, nothing added; the goal is still ahead)

## Cross-chapter / reuse cautions
- Keep the Part 4 chapters distinct: Ch 12 gives a home, Ch 13 places it by frequency, Ch 14 makes it speak, Ch 15 gathers the homes into shared zones and completes Straighten.
- Do not re-teach the earlier three moves; Ch 15 only groups the existing homes into zones and makes the picture shared.
- Hold the Chapter 16 · Shine idea (cleaning as inspection) as a teaser only, so Ch 15 and Ch 16 do not blur. Do not clean, scrub, or teach cleaning here.
- Do not let zones drift into the later Standardize S (routines, schedules, household standards) or Sustain (habits, backsliding). The room map is a one-time arranging act, structure plus sharing, not upkeep.
- The friction-meter "STRAIGHTEN COMPLETE milestone" is specific to Ch 15; it is the second mark on the dial, after SORT COMPLETE. Be honest that it is a real, earned milestone but not the finish line.
- Set the chapter page as the SEO canonical; point syndicated copies (LinkedIn article, Medium) back to it.
- Keep the framing plain: "zone," "launch pad," "room map," "shared picture." Group by the verb, not the noun.
