# Read Next Recommendations: Chapter 15, The Room Map

1. Chapter 16, Shine: Cleaning as Inspection
   The next step, and the one that opens Part Five. Straighten is complete: the room is cleared and fully arranged, but it is not yet cared for. The surfaces still gather dust, the corners still need wiping. Chapter 16 begins the third S, Shine, and turns the simple act of cleaning into a way of noticing what needs attention before it becomes a problem. Sort cleared the room and Straighten arranged it; now Shine cleans and cares for what remains.

2. Chapter 14, Visual Controls for the Home
   The setup. Before the homes could gather into a shared map, each one had to be able to explain itself. Chapter 14 took the third Straighten step and made each placed home self-explaining, so a label, an outline, or a boundary tells anyone what belongs in a spot and whether it is there, without asking you. What it left behind was a row of homes that each spoke clearly and still stood as separate clever spots, with the picture that ties them together living only in one person's head. This chapter draws that picture and shares it.

3. Draw your first zone (the chapter action)
   Pick one busy corner and name, in a single word, the activity that happens there. Gather everything that activity needs into that spot, and move out anything that belongs to a different activity. You just drew your first zone.

4. The Room Map worksheet (free resource)
   A printable to sketch your home and block in its zones by activity, so the picture in your head lands on paper where everyone can see it.

5. The Zone starter list (free resource)
   A printable of common home zones, launch pad, coffee, homework, charging, and mail-and-admin, to spark your own.
