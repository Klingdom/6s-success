## You are here blurb
You are at Chapter 15, the fourth and final chapter of Part Four, Straighten, and the last step of the second S. Every keeper has a home now, each home sits where your hand wants it, and each home speaks for itself, yet the arrangement still reads as separate clever spots, because each home was built one at a time and arranged around itself, not around the errands you actually run, and the picture that ties them together lives only in one person's head. This chapter gathers the homes into a few zones by activity and turns the whole arrangement into one shared map: it does not re-choose homes (Chapter 12), re-place them by frequency (Chapter 13), or re-mark them (Chapter 14), does not clean or teach cleaning (Chapter 16, Shine), does not build routines or standards for keeping order (the later Standardize S), and Sort is not re-opened. A room map is a single shared picture of a space, organized into zones by the activities that happen there, and the rule is to group by what you do, not by what things are. A zone gathers everything one activity needs into the place that activity happens, named by the verb and not the noun, so a coffee zone at the machine beats a kitchen things cupboard. The running entryway example is revealed as one zone all along, the launch pad, then the home zooms out to a handful of zones: coffee, homework corner, charging station, and mail-and-admin. Two small acts make the map shared, sketch it as a napkin diagram and walk the household through it, which hands the design to everyone, so it stops being your room that they live in and becomes their room that they run. Nothing left the house, yet the friction meter reaches the Straighten-complete mark, the second milestone on the dial after Sort complete. Two of the six S's are done and the goal is still ahead, because Shine, Safety, Standardize, and Sustain remain.
