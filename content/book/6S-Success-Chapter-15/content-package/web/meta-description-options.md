# Meta Description Options: Chapter 15, The Room Map

Five options, each 140 to 160 characters, each with a primary or secondary keyword used naturally. Character counts are in parentheses.

1. Good homes still add up to a scatter until they cohere. The room map gathers them into zones by activity and makes the picture shared. Straighten complete. (155)

   Note: 155 characters.

2. Group by what you do, not by what things are. Organize your home into a few zones by activity, then draw one shared map the whole household can read. (149)

   Note: 149 characters.

3. The keys, leash, and bag by the door were one zone all along: the launch pad. Learn to zone your home by what you do and draw a shared family map. (146)

   Note: 146 characters.

4. A map in one person's head fails the moment they step out. Gather your home into zones by activity and draw one shared picture everyone can read. (145)

   Note: 145 characters.

5. Finish straightening your home. Gather well-placed homes into a handful of activity zones, sketch a simple map, and walk the household through it. (146)

   Note: 146 characters.
