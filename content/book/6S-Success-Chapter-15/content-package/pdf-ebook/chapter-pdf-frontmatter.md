## Chapter descriptor

The fourth and final chapter of Part Four, and it completes Straighten, the second S. Chapter 12 gave every keeper a home; Chapter 13 placed each home by how often you reach for it; Chapter 14 made each home explain itself; now every home is well chosen, well placed, and self-explaining, yet the whole arrangement still reads as separate clever spots and the picture that ties them together lives only in the head of the person who built it. ## How to read this chapter

This is the fourth and final chapter of Part Four, Straighten, and it picks up exactly where the last one left off, with every keeper in a home, every home well placed, and every home now able to speak, yet the arrangement still living as scattered pieces in one person's head. It is not about re-choosing whether a thing gets a home; you made those calls in Chapter 12. It is not about re-placing homes by frequency; you did that in Chapter 13. It is not about marking each home so it explains itself; that was Chapter 14. It is about one job: gather the homes you already have into a few natural groups, draw that grouping as one view, and share it.

Read it through once for the idea, then watch your household for a day and let the zones announce themselves by where people keep colliding: the spot everyone passes leaving the house, the counter where the morning coffee ritual happens, the table that becomes homework central at four o'clock, the tangle of cables where every device charges overnight, the surface where the mail piles up. Follow one rule as you look: group by what you do, not by what things are. A zone gathers everything one activity needs into the place that activity happens, so a task is served by one spot instead of scattered across the room, and you name it by the verb, not the noun. Then two small acts make the map shared: sketch the rough shape of your home no straighter than a napkin diagram and block in the zones, and walk the household through it, standing in each zone and saying its name out loud.

Nothing here re-chooses homes; the keepers are settled and stay. Nothing here re-places homes by frequency; that was Chapter 13. Nothing here re-marks a home so it speaks; that was Chapter 14. Nothing here cleans or teaches cleaning; that is Shine, the third S, and Chapter 16, and we only point to it. Nothing here builds routines, schedules, or standards for keeping order over time; that is a later S. Nothing re-opens Sort. Today has one job: gather the finished homes into zones and make the picture shared, so the arrangement stops living in your head and starts living in the whole household. Then the second S is done.

If you only do one thing: pick one busy corner and name, in a single word, the activity that happens there. Gather everything that activity needs into that spot, and move out anything that belongs to a different activity. You just drew your first zone.
