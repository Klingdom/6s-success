## Key extracted assets
- **Title:** The Room Map
- **Slug:** the-room-map
- **Part:** Part Four, Straighten (the fourth and final Straighten chapter; it completes Straighten)
- **Hero device:** the room map (the homes gathered into a few zones by activity, drawn once and shared)
- **The rule:** Group by what you do, not by what things are.
- **Room map (definition):** A room map is a single shared picture of a space, organized into zones by the activities that happen there, so anyone in the household can see where everything lives, and why, all at once.
- **Zone (definition):** A zone gathers everything one activity needs into the place that activity happens, so a task is served by one spot instead of scattered across the room.
- **The payoff:** It was one zone all along. The entryway (keys, leash, bag, umbrella, outgoing mail, shoes) is revealed as the launch pad.
- **The reframe:** The arrangement was yours. Drawing the map together hands the design to the whole household, so it stops being your room that they live in and becomes their room that they run.
- **One Idea to Keep:** Group the homes into a few zones by what you do, and draw the map once with everyone, so the arrangement stops living in your head and starts living in the whole household. A room whose logic is shared is a room that runs itself, which is exactly where Straighten set out to go.
