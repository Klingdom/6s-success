# Reuse Risk Check: Chapter 15 Content Package

When one chapter becomes fifty-plus assets, the risk is overexposure: the same line or image hitting the same follower until it feels canned. This check flags where that can happen.

## High-frequency elements
| Element | Appears in | Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Group by what you do, not by what things are | Web, LinkedIn, X, FB, newsletter, quote card, graphics, slides (35 files) | A follower on several platforms sees the rule repeatedly | Calendar staggers it; vary the phrasing per platform |
| The room-map definition | Canonical, LinkedIn, X, quote card, graphics (25 files) | Medium | The chapter's hero device; repetition reinforces. Vary the surrounding setup. |
| A zone gathers everything one activity needs... | Canonical, X, quote card, graphics (29 files) | Medium | The woven definition; pair with a different worked zone each time. |
| It was one zone all along | LinkedIn, FB, X, quote card | Medium to High | Strongest, most shareable hook. Use across formats, not stacked on one day. |
| The private-map / shared-map pull-quote | LinkedIn, X, FB, quote card (24 files) | Medium | Vary which half you lead with (the private-map failure vs. the household drawing it together). |
| Soft-CTA sign-off wording ("Free online", "the online book") | Most LinkedIn and X posts | Medium | Rotate phrasings; most likely thing to feel templated. |

## Same-day collision
The calendar avoids two assets sharing a hook on the same day. If your LinkedIn and Facebook audiences overlap heavily, push one back a day so "It was one zone all along" does not land twice in one day.

## Cross-posting risk
- The LinkedIn carousel outline (`linkedin/linkedin-carousel-outline.md`) and the slides carousel copy (`slides/linkedin-carousel-copy.md`) cover the same room-map content. Post only one to LinkedIn (use the copy-ready slides file; keep the outline as planning).
- The LinkedIn newsletter and the email newsletter share structure; do not send both to the same subscriber the same day.

## Duplicate-content / SEO risk
- Web landing intro, ebook description, and LinkedIn article overlap in phrasing. Set the chapter page as the SEO canonical and mark syndicated copies with a canonical link.

## Cross-chapter risk (specific to Chapter 15)
- Chapters 12 to 15 are all Straighten. Keep each distinct: Ch 12 gives each thing a home, Ch 13 places those homes by frequency, Ch 14 makes each home self-explaining, Ch 15 gathers them into zones and shares the whole-room map. Ch 15 assets must not drift into re-assigning homes (Ch 12), re-placing by frequency (Ch 13), or re-marking them (Ch 14): nothing moves house here.
- Do not let the room-map idea slide into the next S (Shine: cleaning and inspection) or into the later Standardize/Sustain S's (routines, schedules, checklists, household standards for keeping order over time). Ch 15 is a one-time arranging act of structure plus sharing; keeping it up is a later S. Grep confirms the reader-facing copy holds this fence (the only "routine/schedule/checklist" hits are a CSS class name and negated production notes).
- The friction-meter STRAIGHTEN COMPLETE milestone is specific to this chapter (the second milestone, after SORT COMPLETE); do not reuse the earlier per-step Straighten framing in Ch 15 assets, and keep the goal honestly ahead (four S's remain: Shine, Safety, Standardize, Sustain).

## Originality and source-fidelity risk
- The chapter is a draft authored in the book voice, not supplied source. All derived assets are faithful to that draft; re-sync if the chapter is revised.
- Quote cards are verbatim: 10 cards plus 14 alternates, each verified byte-identical to the manuscript. Nothing borrowed from outside the book; the Straighten/Seiton origin (an overall workplace layout and activity zoning) is referenced lightly, home-scaled, not overclaimed.

## Recommendations
1. Rotate CTA wording (highest-value, lowest-effort fix; several posts close on "Free online").
2. Pick one carousel version for LinkedIn.
3. Set the chapter page as the SEO canonical.
4. Keep the Part 4 chapters (12 to 15) from blurring, especially Ch 14 (the individual home's cue) versus Ch 15 (the room-wide shared map), and keep the room map distinct from the later Shine and Standardize S's.
5. Re-sync all assets if the authored chapter changes after author review.
