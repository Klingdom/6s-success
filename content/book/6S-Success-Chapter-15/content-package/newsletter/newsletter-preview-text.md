# Newsletter Preview Text (Preheader): The Room Map

Ten options, each 35 to 90 characters, each paired with a subject line. Char counts noted.

1. Pairs with #1 (Group by what you do, not by what things are)
   "You live by activity, so arrange the room that way." (52 chars)

2. Pairs with #2 (The entryway was a zone all along)
   "Keys, leash, bag, umbrella: one launch pad all along." (53 chars)

3. Pairs with #3 (Your home is really just a handful of zones)
   "Name the few your life actually runs on." (40 chars)

4. Pairs with #4 (The picture that ties your home together lives in one head)
   "Let's get it on paper where everyone can see it." (48 chars)

5. Pairs with #5 (The last Straighten step: gather the homes into shared zones)
   "Nothing new added; the homes just gather and share." (51 chars)

6. Pairs with #6 (Draw the map together, and the room starts to run itself)
   "A sheet of paper and ten honest minutes at the table." (53 chars)

7. Pairs with #7 (Nothing moves house today, and Straighten is complete)
   "Two of the six S's are done. The goal is still ahead." (53 chars)

8. Pairs with #8 (Draw your first zone this week)
   "Name one corner by what happens there, and gather." (50 chars)

9. Pairs with #9 (Get the map out of your head and onto a sheet of paper)
   "So the room works even when you step out." (41 chars)

10. Pairs with #10 (The room map: zones, plus a picture the whole household holds)
    "One shared view, read at a glance instead of hunted." (52 chars)
