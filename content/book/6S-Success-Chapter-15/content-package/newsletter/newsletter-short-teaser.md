# Newsletter Short Teaser: The Room Map

For a roundup or digest. 90 to 140 words.

Every home is well placed and self-explaining now, yet the entryway is still a row of separate clever spots, and the only picture that ties them together lives behind your eyes. This chapter closes that gap without moving a thing. The move is zones: group by what you do, not by what things are, so a coffee zone at the machine beats a "kitchen things" cupboard. The running entryway example is revealed as one zone all along, the launch pad. Then two small acts make the map shared: sketch it like a napkin diagram, and walk the household through it, from your room to their room. The friction meter reaches the Straighten-complete mark. Two of the six S's are done, and Straighten is complete.
