## Full summary (about 250 words)

Chapter 18 is the third chapter of Part Five and continues Shine, the third of the six S's. Chapter 16 reframed cleaning as inspection and did one first pass; Chapter 17 built the Shine list, targets by kind with a name on every line, and held the meter with a dashed forward projection. This chapter carries out the plan: it gives each target a method so the list gets done instead of postponed.

The reason a job does not get done is usually not that you are lazy or busy; it is that the method you are using is slow and horrible, and a slow horrible job is one any sane person postpones. A method that works has to do two things at once. It has to be effective, so the target comes clean and you can read its condition, and it has to be easy, so you will genuinely do it again. The best method is the one you will actually do.

The chapter teaches five working methods: right tool for the surface; top to bottom, dry before wet; fewer, simpler products; keep the tool where the target is; and clean in passing. It reframes dread as a method problem, not a character flaw, keeps the methods serving inspection so a target is cleaned and read in the same pass, and applies the treatment to the existing list.

Because the targets actually get cleaned this chapter, the friction meter follows Chapter 17's dashed projection and takes its next Shine step. The goal is still ahead; Shine completes in Chapter 19.
