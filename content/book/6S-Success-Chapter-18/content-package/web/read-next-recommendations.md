3. Fix your worst cleaning job (the chapter action)
   Take the one cleaning job you dread most and find a faster method for it: a better tool, fewer products, a different order. Do it once the new way and time it. Dread usually turns out to be a method problem.

4. The Working Methods card (free resource)
   A printable and phone wallpaper carrying the five working methods, right tool for the surface, top to bottom, dry before wet, fewer, simpler products, keep the tool where the target is, and clean in passing, so the kit is always in reach.

5. The Method Swap sheet (free resource)
   A printable where, for each target you dread, you note the faster method you found, so the household builds a shared toolkit instead of everyone working it out alone.
