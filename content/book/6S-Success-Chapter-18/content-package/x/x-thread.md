2/
Here is the gap. A job can be perfectly assigned to a perfectly willing person and still not happen, for the oldest reason there is: it is a pain to do. Nobody skips the bathroom because they forgot whose job it was. They skip it because, done their way, it is slow.

3/
Say it plainly:

"the reason a job does not get done is usually not that you are lazy, or busy, or disorganized. It is that the method you are using is slow and horrible, and a slow horrible job is one any sane person postpones."

(232 chars)

4/
So several lines on your list already give you a small sinking feeling. The oven that means an hour and a caustic spray. The shower screen that never comes clear. The target is owned. It is also dreaded. And dread, quietly, beats ownership almost every time.

(261 chars)

5/
The missing piece is not more willpower and not a stricter chart. It is a better way to do each job. A method that works has to do two things at once, and most cleaning advice only ever gives you one. It has to be effective. It also has to be easy.

(251 chars)

---

6/
Effective means the target actually comes clean and you can read it. Easy means quick and painless enough that you will do it again without a fight. Easy but not effective gets done and does nothing. Effective but not easy works once and never again.

(253 chars)

---

7/
"A working method is the quickest effective way to clean and read a given target: effective enough that the surface actually comes clean and shows what it needs, and easy enough that you will do it again without dreading it."

(228 chars)

---

8/
Which points at the most useful rule in the book:

"The best method is the one you will actually do."

Thorough is worth nothing if it does not happen. The home is kept by what you actually do, not by what you know you should.

(229 chars)

---

9/
Five working methods lift almost any target toward quick and good. Right tool for the surface: most struggle at the sink is really the wrong tool applied with extra effort. Top to bottom, dry before wet: order is free speed, so nothing gets dirtied twice.

(258 chars)

---

10/
Fewer, simpler products: a cupboard of sprays is slower, not faster. A small handful covers almost everything. Keep the tool where the target is: a cloth kept by the sink gets used; the same cloth in a cupboard down the hall does not. Starting takes no effort.

(264 chars)

---

11/
Clean in passing: the cheapest clean is the one you never had to make time for, because you did it while you were already there. A wipe of the shower wall while the water runs, the counter while the pasta boils. Folded into a moment you were spending anyway.

(262 chars)

12/
Now the kind part:

"You do not have a cleaning problem. You have a method problem."

The moment the method gets quick, the avoidance stops on its own, without any change in your character. Nobody has to become a more disciplined person to keep a clean home.
