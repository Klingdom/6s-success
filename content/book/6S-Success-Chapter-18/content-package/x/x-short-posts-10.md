5/
Five methods make almost any target quick to clean well:

Right tool for the surface.
Top to bottom, dry before wet.
Fewer, simpler products.
Keep the tool where the target is.
Clean in passing.

None is a trick or a special product. Read the free chapter.

(259 chars)

6/
"Order is free speed."

Dust and crumbs fall, so clean the high things before the low ones, and do the dry jobs before the wet ones. Work the other way and you dirty what you just cleaned and do half of it twice.
