- **The five working methods (frozen):** right tool for the surface; top to bottom, dry before wet; fewer, simpler products; keep the tool where the target is; clean in passing.
- **The reframe (frozen):** You do not have a cleaning problem. You have a method problem. - **Methods still serve inspection (frozen):** A working method still leaves you having looked. Do not spray a surface and walk away; the point of Shine was never only a clean surface but a read one, so the best method cleans the target and shows you its condition in the same easy pass.
