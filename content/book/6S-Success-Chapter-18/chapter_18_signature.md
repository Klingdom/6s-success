## Hero device: The working method (effective and easy)
For each target, the method that is BOTH effective (cleans the surface and lets you read it) and easy (quick enough you will do it again). The overlap of the two is the working method; a method that is only easy leaves dirt, a method that is only effective never gets repeated. Source: Seiso "determine cleaning methods", home-scaled. Referenced lightly, no overclaiming.

## Emotional purpose
Relief and permission, practical and warm. This is the most hands-on chapter of Shine; it removes shame from cleaning by locating the problem in the method, not the person. The reader is not lazy; they were handed slow methods. The payoff is that dread drains out of a job the moment the method gets quick, with no change in character required.
