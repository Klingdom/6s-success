# X Short Posts: Straighten: A Place for Everything

Chapter 12 of *6S Success: Home Edition*. Ten standalone posts. Each is at or under 280 characters, counting its number line, and makes sense on its own. Soft CTA on a few, not all.

---

1/
Chapter 12 of 6S Success: Home Edition opens Part Four, the second S: Straighten. Sort decided what stays. Now every keeper gets one clear home, a single spot where it lives and returns to. The surface stays clear because the room holds it, not you. Free online.

(approx. 256 chars)

---

2/
"A place for everything, so everything can find its way back."

That is the whole of the second S. The point of a home is not neatness, it is the return trip: when you are done with a thing, where it goes is already decided. Read the free chapter.

(approx. 241 chars)

---

3/
Why do things not get put away? Not because you are lazy or messy. Think of the forks, or the coat hook, or the spot the remote returns to. Those go back every time, because their home is right there. The problem is never you. It is the missing home.

(approx. 245 chars)

---

4/
"You were never messy. Your things simply had nowhere to go. Give them a home, and putting them away stops taking willpower and starts taking a second."

The pile by the door was never a verdict on you. It was information about the door.

(approx. 233 chars)

---

5/
"A home works when putting the thing back is easier than putting it down anywhere else. If returning it takes more than one easy motion, the home is wrong, not you."

Call it the Home Test, and hold every home up to it.

(approx. 216 chars)

---

6/
The problem was never your willpower. The problem was the design. And a design problem has a design fix, which is good news: you can change a home far more easily than you can change your character.

"Move the home, not your willpower."

(approx. 232 chars)

---

7/
Give the entryway keepers homes. Keys · a tray at the door. Leash · a hook, grabbed going out and hung coming back. Bag · a hook beside it. Umbrella · a stand, not the back of a closet. Mail · an outgoing slot. Each one a single easy motion from where it is used.

(approx. 258 chars)

---

8/
The umbrella is the one people get wrong. The closet feels like the proper place, but a home you open a door and reach past three coats to use will fail the test. Put it where you actually pass on the way out, and it goes back where it goes.

(approx. 237 chars)

---

9/
You did not become tidier today. You built a handful of small homes, each close enough and open enough to win the quiet contest against just setting the thing down. From now on those homes do the tidying that used to fall to you. Read the chapter free online.

(approx. 255 chars)

10/
"Tidy is not a chore you repeat forever. It is a set of homes good enough that things find their own way back. Build the homes once, and the room helps keep itself."

That is the second S. Next, Chapter 13 places each home by how often you reach for it.
