# Read Next Recommendations: Chapter 12, Straighten: A Place for Everything

1. Chapter 13, Store by Frequency of Use
   The next step, and the rest of Part Four. Every keeper has a home now, but not every home is in the best spot yet. The tray of keys you touch a dozen times a day and the umbrella you reach for once a week are sitting at the same door, treated the same. Chapter 13 puts the daily things within quick reach and the rare things a little out of the way, so the homes you just built work as hard as they can.

2. Chapter 11, What to Donate, Sell, Store, Recycle, or Throw Away
   The setup. Before this chapter could run, Sort had to finish. Chapter 11 routed the out box through five doors and carried the last of the clutter out the door, which closed Part Three. What it left behind was the short list of keepers, sitting loose where they landed. This chapter picks them up and gives each one a home.

3. Give one thing a home (the chapter action)
   Pick the one thing you use every day and reach for most, and give it a single home within arm's reach of where you use it. Return it there for three days. Watch how fast putting it back stops being a decision.

4. The Home Test card (free resource)
   A printable plus phone wallpaper with the one-motion-return test and the good-home versus bad-home contrast, so the whole test fits in a glance.

5. The Give-It-a-Home worksheet (free resource)
   A printable to list each keeper in a space and the one home you assigned it, so a cleared surface stays clear on its own.
