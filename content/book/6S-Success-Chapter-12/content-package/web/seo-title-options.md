# SEO Title Options: Chapter 12, Straighten: A Place for Everything

Five title options. Character counts are in parentheses. The canonical title is listed first.

1. Straighten: A Place for Everything (34)
   Angle: canonical book title. Use as the H1 and primary on-page heading, with "a place for everything," "home," and "put away" early in the body.

2. A Place for Everything, So Everything Can Find Its Way Back (58)
   Angle: leads with the core principle. Names the whole idea in one line and is highly quotable.

3. You Were Never Messy: Give Everything a Home (44)
   Angle: opens with the anti-shame reframe and the primary teaching move. Warm and memorable.

4. Why You Never Put Things Away (and How to Fix It) (49)
   Angle: matches the exact question readers arrive with. The fix is a home, not more willpower.

5. Organizing Is Design, Not Willpower (35)
   Angle: promises the reframe at the heart of the chapter. Reframes the whole problem in one line.
