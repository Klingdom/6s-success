## You are here blurb
You are at Chapter 12, the first chapter of Part Four, Straighten, and the opening of the second S. Sort emptied the entryway, but the keepers are still sitting loose where they landed, with no places of their own. This chapter makes the turn from removing to arranging: it does not re-open Sort, and it does not yet place homes by frequency, add labels, or map the room. Its principle is a place for everything, so everything can find its way back, and its one tool is the Home Test, that a home works when putting the thing back is easier than putting it down anywhere else, in one easy motion. The entryway keepers each get a home, keys to a tray or hook, leash to a hook, umbrella to a stand, bag to a hook, mail to an outgoing slot. Nothing left the house, yet the friction meter takes its first Straighten step past the Sort-complete milestone, because the room got easier to keep.
