# Chapter 12 LinkedIn Comment Prompts: Straighten: A Place for Everything

Five prompts to start genuine discussion. Drop one in the comments of a post or article to invite replies. Anti-shame voice: the mess is a design problem, never a character flaw.

---

1. Name one thing in your home that always gets put back without a thought, the forks, the remote, the good coat's hook. What makes that home so easy to use? That is the whole model for every other home.

2. Now the honest one: what is the thing that lives on the counter no matter how many times you move it? Where is its home right now, and how far is it from where you actually set the thing down?

3. Run the Home Test on one item today: is putting it back easier than putting it down anywhere else? If it takes more than one easy motion, the home is wrong, not you. Tell us what failed the test.

4. The umbrella is the one people get wrong most reliably, because the closet feels like the proper place. What is your version of the umbrella, a thing you keep storing somewhere technically correct but too far to actually use?

5. You were never messy; your things simply had nowhere to go. What is one small home, a tray or a hook, that quietly fixed a pile you used to blame yourself for?
