# Chapter 12 · Repurposing Map

How the one chapter fans out into every channel, and which idea leads on each.

## The spine (reused across channels)
- The turn from removing (Sort) to arranging (Straighten), the second S
- The core principle: a place for everything, so everything can find its way back
- The Home Test: putting it back is easier than putting it down anywhere else (one easy motion)
- Why things do not get put away: a missing or bad home, not laziness (design, not willpower)
- The worked pass: the five entryway keepers each get a home
- The reframe: you were never messy
- The first Straighten step on the friction meter (nothing left the house, yet the room got easier to keep)
