# Back Cover Copy: Straighten: A Place for Everything

## Back-cover text

The surface is clear, and the keys are still on it. So is the leash, the umbrella, the bag, and tomorrow's mail. Every one of them earned its place during Sort. Not one of them has a place on this table. "A surface can be clear and still be one busy morning away from a mess."

Chapter 12 of *6S Success: Home Edition* opens Part Four, Straighten, the second of the six S's. Sort was about removing; Straighten is about arranging. Nothing has to leave today. Today, the keepers get homes, one clear spot each, so keeping the surface clear stops depending on you and starts depending on the room. The principle fits in a sentence: "A place for everything, so everything can find its way back."

Things do not get put away because of a bad home or no home, not because of you. "You are not failing the room. The room is failing you." So the chapter gives you one tool, the Home Test: "A home works when putting the thing back is easier than putting it down anywhere else. If returning it takes more than one easy motion, the home is wrong, not you." Then it walks the entryway keepers into homes, keys to a tray or hook, leash to a hook, umbrella to a stand or hook, bag to a hook, tomorrow's mail to an outgoing slot, every home within reach of the door.

Said plainly: "You were never messy. Your things simply had nowhere to go. Give them a home, and putting them away stops taking willpower and starts taking a second." Nothing leaves the house, yet the friction meter takes its first Straighten step, past the Sort-complete milestone toward calm. The step is real, and the goal is still ahead, because Straighten has only begun and four more S's follow. Pick the one thing you reach for most, give it a home within arm's reach, and watch how fast putting it back stops being a decision.
