Last issue closed Sort: the out box left the house and the first S was done. What is left on the console is the short list of keepers, sitting loose where they landed, each without a place of its own. This chapter opens Part Four and gives every keeper a home. The principle fits in a sentence, a place for everything, so everything can find its way back, and the one tool is the Home Test: a home works when putting the thing back is easier than putting it down anywhere else, in one easy motion. Keys, leash, umbrella, bag, and mail each get a home by the door. You were never messy; your things simply had nowhere to go. Nothing leaves, yet the needle takes its first Straighten step.
