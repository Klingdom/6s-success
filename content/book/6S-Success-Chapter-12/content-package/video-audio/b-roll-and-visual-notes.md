- The starting point is the cleared entryway console after Sort. It holds only its keepers now, sitting loose where they landed: a set of keys, the dog's leash, a folding umbrella, a reusable shopping bag, and a small stack of tomorrow's mail. - The bias is drawn, not just stated. A good home is near, open, and reachable in one motion. A bad home is far, behind a door, under a lid, across the room. Show the contrast, do not just say it.

- Keep the whole thing calm and unhurried. No force or violence framing. Nothing is purged or attacked. The tone is anti-shame: you were never messy, the fix is design, not willpower.
