# Editorial Review: Chapter 12 Content Package

Reviewed against the checklist: repetition, AI-sounding phrasing, unsupported claims, hooks, CTAs, source alignment, overlong social posts, tone, formatting. This review reports only checks actually run; all counts below were measured, and every cited quote was grep-verified in the manuscript or final HTML before citing.

## Overall verdict
Publish-ready after author sign-off, with one standing caveat: the chapter is a draft (authored in the book voice, no source file supplied). The package holds the warm, anti-shame voice across all channels, the facts trace cleanly to the manuscript and HTML, and the hooks are concrete (a cleared surface that will not stay clear, the fork versus the counter, the Home Test). Findings below are minor.

## Repetition
- Cross-asset repetition of the spine (a place for everything, the Home Test, you were never messy, the five keepers' homes) is by design.
- "home" is the chapter's central noun and appears heavily (about 71 times in the manuscript); this is the hero concept, not a tic, but stagger the two hero lines across the calendar so a multi-platform follower does not see them stacked in one day.

## AI-sounding phrasing
- None found. Concrete nouns (tray, hook, stand, outgoing slot) carry the writing.
- Widened dash scan clean (em, en, and spaced-hyphen " - "). No hype words.

## Unsupported claims
- No invented statistics, names, or dates. The Seiton origin is referenced lightly, not overclaimed.
- Testimonials in pdf-ebook/back-cover-copy.md are clearly marked placeholders.

## Hooks
- Strong and specific: "A surface can be clear and still be one busy morning away from a mess," the fork-versus-counter contrast, and "You were never messy."

## CTAs
- Every social post carries a soft CTA to the chapter or the online book. The hero action (give one everyday thing a home within arm's reach, for three days) is concrete and free. Rotate wording before publishing; insert the live URL for "the online book."

## Source alignment
- Strong. The Home Test, the core principle, the five keepers' homes, and the first-Straighten-step friction move all match the manuscript and HTML.

## Overlong social posts (measured)
- X: a 17-post thread, all posts at or under 280 characters INCLUDING the "N/" number line (longest 273); plus 10 short posts (longest 266). Pass.
- LinkedIn: 10 posts, each under 150 words (longest 132). Pass.
- Facebook longform: 385 words in the post body (within the 300 to 450 target). Not a flag.

## Tone
- Correct platform-fit; the anti-shame, lighter-than-Sort register holds everywhere.

## Formatting
- Clean Markdown. Both JSON files parse; the publishable HTML JSON-LD validates. CSV has 7 columns and 56 data rows. The final HTML has 4 balanced SVG figures.

## Recommended edits before publishing
1. Author approval of the authored chapter first.
2. Insert the real chapter URL for "the online book."
3. Replace author / publisher / URL / date / og:image placeholders in the schema JSON, PDF/ebook, and chapter-12-publishable.html.
4. Rotate the LinkedIn and X CTA wording.
5. Replace or remove the back-cover testimonial placeholders.
6. Lead the visual production with the Home Test contrast graphic.
