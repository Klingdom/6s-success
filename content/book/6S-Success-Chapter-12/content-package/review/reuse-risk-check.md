# Reuse Risk Check: Chapter 12 Content Package

When one chapter becomes fifty-plus assets, the risk is overexposure: the same line or image hitting the same follower until it feels canned. This check flags where that can happen.

## High-frequency elements
| Element | Appears in | Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| A place for everything, so everything can find its way back | Web, LinkedIn, X, FB, newsletter, quote card, graphics | A follower on several platforms sees the principle repeatedly | Calendar staggers it; vary the phrasing per platform |
| The Home Test (one easy motion) | Hero infographic, carousel, deck, LinkedIn, X | Medium | The chapter's hero device; repetition reinforces. Vary the example. |
| You were never messy | LinkedIn, FB, short video, quote card | Medium | Strongest, kindest hook. Use across formats, not stacked on one day. |
| Move the home, not your willpower | LinkedIn post, X, quote card | Low to Medium | Memorable; rotate placement. |
| The five keepers' homes | Worked-pass graphic, carousel, video, longform | Low | Practical, meant to repeat; vary which keeper you lead with. |
| Soft-CTA sign-off wording | Most LinkedIn and X posts | Medium | Rotate phrasings; most likely thing to feel templated. |

## Same-day collision
The calendar avoids two assets sharing a hook on the same day. If your LinkedIn and Facebook audiences overlap heavily, push one back a day so "you were never messy" does not land twice in one day.

## Cross-posting risk
- The LinkedIn carousel outline and the slides carousel copy cover the same Home Test content. Post only one to LinkedIn (use the copy-ready `slides/linkedin-carousel-copy.md`; keep the outline as planning).
- The LinkedIn newsletter and the email newsletter share structure; do not send both to the same subscriber the same day.

## Duplicate-content / SEO risk
- Web landing intro, ebook description, and LinkedIn article overlap in phrasing. Set the chapter page as the SEO canonical and mark syndicated copies with a canonical link.

## Cross-chapter risk (specific to Chapter 12)
- Chapter 12 opens Part 4 (Straighten); keep it clearly distinct from the Sort chapters (Ch 8 to 11): this is not removing, it is giving homes.
- Chapters 12 to 15 are all Straighten (a place for everything, then by frequency, then visual controls, then the room map). Keep each distinct; in particular, hold Ch 13's "place homes by how often you use them" as a teaser only, so Ch 12 and Ch 13 do not blur.
- The friction-meter "first Straighten step" is specific to this chapter; do not reuse the Sort-complete or held-meter framing in Ch 12 assets.

## Originality and source-fidelity risk
- The chapter is a draft authored in the book voice, not supplied source. All derived assets are faithful to that draft; re-sync if the chapter is revised.
- Quote cards are verbatim (10 cards plus 5 alternates), grep-verified. Nothing borrowed from outside the book.

## Recommendations
1. Rotate CTA wording (highest-value, lowest-effort fix).
2. Pick one carousel version for LinkedIn.
3. Set the chapter page as the SEO canonical.
4. Keep the Part 4 chapters (12 to 15) from blurring, especially Ch 12 versus Ch 13.
5. Re-sync all assets if the authored chapter changes after author review.
