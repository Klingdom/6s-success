# Brand Voice Check: Chapter 12 Content Package

The voice of *6S Success: Home Edition* is warm, plain-spoken, and quietly confident. Chapter 12 opens Part 4 (Straighten) and its specific job is a mood shift: after the hard, sometimes guilt-tinged work of Sort, this chapter is lighter, "closer to carpentry than to soul-searching." The voice must stay encouraging and anti-shame, never preachy.

## Voice fingerprint (from the authored final HTML and manuscript)
- **Warm and personal.** Talks to "you," returns to the entryway drop zone the reader already knows.
- **Anti-shame and reframing.** "You are not failing the room. The room is failing you." The failure to put things away is a design problem, not a character flaw.
- **Plain over clever.** The fork-versus-the-counter contrast teaches the Home Test with no jargon.
- **Concrete.** Keys to a tray, leash to a hook, umbrella to a stand, bag to a hook, mail to an outgoing slot.
- **Honest about scope.** Nothing leaves the house, so the friction-meter move is justified on a new basis (the thought a room costs), not overstated.

## Specific wins
- The anti-shame thread ("You were never messy") is preserved across every channel and is the chapter's most shareable, most kind line.
- "Move the home, not your willpower" carries the whole design-not-discipline argument in six words.
- The Home Test survives intact as the chapter's spine, everywhere.

## Voice risks to watch
- **Contractions on X** (deliberate platform choice).
- **CTA sameness.** Rotate the LinkedIn and X sign-offs.
- **Keep the mood light.** Straighten is the lighter part of the book; do not let any asset slide into the heavier, decision-laden tone of Sort.

## Banned-language audit
- Widened dash scan (em dash, en dash, and spaced-hyphen " - " used as a separator, excluding markdown list bullets): clean across the package; labels use the middot "·".
- No hype words and no force/violence metaphors in any shipped asset. (The word "effortlessly" appears once in a Facebook post, lifted from the manuscript's own line; it is not on the banned list.)

## Verdict
Voice is consistent and intact across all twelve packages, and the anti-shame, design-over-willpower job of Chapter 12 is honored throughout. Approve the voice. The chapter itself still needs author sign-off, since it was authored in the book voice rather than supplied from source.
