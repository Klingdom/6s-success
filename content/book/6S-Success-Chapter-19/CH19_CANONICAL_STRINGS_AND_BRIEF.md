## Hero device (frozen): the 15-minute reset
A short, time-boxed pass through the home, done together on a regular rhythm, that returns every zone to its clean and read baseline, so the whole house stays cared for without anyone ever setting aside an afternoon. Its three levers: it is SHORT (a hard 15-minute stop), SHARED (the whole household at once), and REGULAR (anchored to a daily cue so it becomes a habit, not a decision).
> **The rule (frozen):** A little, often, together.
> **Support line (frozen):** Set the timer, and stop when it rings.

## The drift reframe (frozen, distinct from Ch 16 "clean to see", Ch 17 "a name on every target", Ch 18 "a method problem")
> A home does not get messy in one big event. It drifts, a little at a time, so the thing that keeps it is not a big clean now and then but a small reset, often. Fight a slow daily drift with a rare heroic clean and you will always be behind; meet it with a short daily reset and you are never more than fifteen minutes from baseline.

## The time-box payoff (frozen)
> The timer is the whole device, because a hard stop is what makes people willing to start. An open-ended clean is the dreaded afternoon all over again; a clean that is definitely over in fifteen minutes is one anyone will begin, and a clean you will begin is the only kind that keeps a home.

## The shared-reset payoff (frozen)
> Fifteen minutes is nothing on your own and a great deal shared out. Four people resetting together for a quarter of an hour is a full hour of cleaning that costs each of them only fifteen minutes, and it lands all at once, so the whole home returns to baseline in the time it takes one person to clean a single room.

## One Idea to Keep (frozen)
> A little, often, together. Shrink cleaning to a short shared reset on a regular rhythm, stop when the timer rings, and the home stays clean and read without a single lost afternoon. That steady, self-running baseline is what completes Shine.

## Callouts (frozen wording)
- **Quick Win:** Tonight, set a timer for fifteen minutes and have everyone reset one zone at once, then stop when it rings, even if it is not perfect. Notice how much the whole house changes in a quarter of an hour shared out.
- **Family Challenge:** Pick a daily anchor, after dinner or before bed, and run the fifteen-minute reset at that same moment every day, together. Anchored to something you already do, it stops being a decision and starts being a habit.
- **6S Tip:** Reset, do not deep-clean. The reset returns the home to its baseline; it is not the time to scrub the oven. Keep it short and light and the deep cleans grow rare, because nothing ever gets far enough gone to need one.
- **Common Mistake:** Turning the reset into a big clean, or skipping the timer and going until it is done. An open-ended clean is the dreaded afternoon all over again. The timer is the point: a hard stop is what makes people willing to start.
