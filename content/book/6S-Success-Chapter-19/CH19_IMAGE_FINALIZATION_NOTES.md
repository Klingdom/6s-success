## HELD-CANDIDATE SEARCH FROM OTHER CHAPTERS · result: none used

At the user's request, all 16 held "reset / routine / rhythm" plates from earlier chapters were
evaluated against Chapter 19's specific device (a 15-minute TIMED, SHARED, recurring reset; "a
little, often, together"). Candidates viewed: `ch12-image7`, `ch12-image11`, `ch13-image9/10/14/16/

**Verdict: none is a fit.** Every one is either:
- a **rival numbered "system/method/steps"** competing with the six S's (ch12-7, ch13-14, ch16-9,
  ch16-13, ch16-14, ch18-8, ch18-15),
- a **Standardize** artifact, a printed checklist-as-standard or a daily/weekly SCHEDULE or CALENDAR
  (ch13-9, ch13-16, ch13-19, ch15-15, ch16-15, ch15-10), or

  habit-trackers (ch13-10, ch13-14, ch15-15, ch16-13, ch16-14, ch18-8).

The scale is also consistently wrong: they are 30-second to 5-minute SOLO or single-room resets,
never the 15-minute whole-household pass. This is a useful confirmation that those held plates belong
to **Standardize (Ch 23 to 25)** and **Sustain (Ch 26 to 30)**, not to Chapter 19. The 15-minute
timed shared reset device only exists in Chapter 19's own set.
