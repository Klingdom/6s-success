# Read Next Recommendations: Chapter 19, The 15-Minute Reset

1. Chapter 20, Safety: Protect People First
   The next step, and the turn the whole book has been building toward. Sort cleared the home, Straighten arranged it, and Shine made the whole of it clean, read, and self-keeping on a rhythm; now the fourth S opens Part Six and turns from the state of the room to the people in it. Chapter 20 begins Safety, asking not whether the counter is clear but whether the rug slides, the medicine is within a child's reach, the smoke alarm works, or the thing on the top shelf is waiting to fall, and makes the home not just cared for but safe. Chapter 19 completed Shine; Chapter 20 opens Safety.

2. Chapter 18, Cleaning Methods That Actually Work
   The setup. Before the reset could keep the home clean on fifteen minutes a day, each target needed a method quick enough to do and good enough to work. Chapter 18 gave each line on the Shine list a working method, both effective and easy, because the best method is the one you will actually do, and the targets got cleaned across the home, so the friction meter followed the dashed arrow and took its next Shine step. The reset runs those same methods, briefly, on a rhythm.

3. Run one reset tonight (the chapter action)
   Tonight, set a timer for fifteen minutes and have everyone reset one zone at once, then stop when it rings, even if it is not perfect. Notice how much the whole house changes in a quarter of an hour shared out. It is the smallest possible proof of the whole idea.
