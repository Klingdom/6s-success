## Variant B (the drift reframe)

- og:title: A Home Does Not Get Messy in One Big Event. It Drifts.
- og:description: Fight a slow daily drift with a rare heroic clean and you will always be behind; meet it with a short daily reset and you are never more than fifteen minutes from baseline.
- twitter:title: A Home Does Not Get Messy in One Big Event. It Drifts.
- twitter:description: A one-time clean is a photograph; what you live in is the film, and the film drifts. The 15-minute reset meets it, a little at a time. From Chapter 19 of 6S Success: Home Edition.
- og:image concept: A calm two-week line chart in warm line-and-flat-color style, headed Two Homes, Two Weeks, with slate axes. One line, without a reset, saws violently: it climbs as drift builds, drops sharply on the rare day of a big clean, then climbs again, never settling. The other line, with a short daily reset, stays low and nearly flat near a dashed baseline, a small daily dip keeping it always near clean. Small markers show the daily resets on the flat line and the rare exhausting big cleans on the saw-tooth line. Footer: A little each day keeps the home always near baseline, for less total effort and no dreaded afternoons.
