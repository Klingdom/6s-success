# Newsletter Short Teaser: The 15-Minute Reset

For a roundup or digest. 90 to 140 words.

---

You know why you clean, what to clean, and who cleans it, and a week later the home has quietly drifted back anyway. Not because anyone stopped caring, but because a one-time clean has no way to hold, and "clean the house" is too big a job to ever start. This chapter closes that gap with the 15-minute reset: a short, shared, regular pass that returns every zone to its clean and read baseline, then stops when the timer rings. Its rule is a little, often, together. Short, so it starts; shared, so it is fast and fair; regular, so it is a habit, not a decision. The home keeps itself, and the friction meter reaches the Shine-complete milestone, the third of six.
