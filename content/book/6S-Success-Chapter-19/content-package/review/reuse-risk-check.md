# Reuse Risk Check: Chapter 19 Content Package

When one chapter becomes fifty-plus assets, the risk is overexposure: the same line or image hitting the same follower until it feels canned. This check flags where that can happen.

## High-frequency elements
| Element | Appears in | Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|

| Soft-CTA sign-off wording ("Read it free online", "Read the free chapter in the online book", "the online book") | Most LinkedIn and X posts | Medium | Rotate phrasings; most likely thing to feel templated. |

## Same-day collision
The calendar avoids two assets sharing a hook on the same day. If your LinkedIn and Facebook audiences overlap heavily, push one back a day so "A little, often, together." does not land twice in one day.

## Cross-posting risk
- The LinkedIn carousel outline (`linkedin/linkedin-carousel-outline.md`) and the slides carousel copy (`slides/linkedin-carousel-copy.md`) cover the same reset content. Post only one to LinkedIn (use the copy-ready slides file; keep the outline as planning).
- The LinkedIn newsletter and the email newsletter share structure; do not send both to the same subscriber the same day.

## Duplicate-content / SEO risk
- Web landing intro, ebook description, and LinkedIn article overlap in phrasing. Set the chapter page as the SEO canonical and mark syndicated copies with a canonical link.

## Cross-chapter risk (specific to Chapter 19)
- Chapter 19 is the FOURTH and FINAL Shine chapter and the KEEP-IT chapter. Keep it distinct from Chapters 16 to 18 behind it: Chapter 16 reframed cleaning as inspection (why); Chapter 17 built the list and shared it (what and who); Chapter 18 gave each target a working method (how); Chapter 19 shrinks it to a short shared rhythm (when and how often). Assets must USE the reframe, the list, and the methods in the reset but must NOT re-teach any of them. Grep confirms the reset runs the assigned targets with their working methods, reading as it goes, and does not rebuild or re-argue them.
- Do not let the reset slide FORWARD into later S's. Ch 19 assets must not build the Standardize documented standard-work, written checklists-as-standards, or a formal whole-method schedule (that is Standardize, Ch 23 to 25). This is a CRITICAL fence and it is held: "standardize," "schedule," "audit," and "backsliding" appear in the package only inside named fence declarations (`graphics/diagram-ideas.md`, `slides/visual-storyboard.md`, `workflow/repurposing-map.md`), never as taught content. The reset is a light Shine habit, not a documented standard.
- Do NOT drift into Sustain: the reset keeps Shine, but long-term adherence, backsliding, family problem-solving, audits, and "your next 6S event" are Sustain (Ch 26 to 30), noted lightly at most, not built.
- Do NOT teach Safety: hazards, protection, and the smoke alarm / medicine / sliding rug belong to Chapter 20 and are used here only as the honest handoff ("a home that is clean is not the same as a home that is also safe"), explicitly framed as "not yet touched" and "the fourth S."
- Do NOT re-open Sort or Straighten.
- The friction-meter SHINE COMPLETE milestone is specific to this chapter (a real, earned third milestone at (71,154), calmer than Chapter 18, with Chapter 18's (73,153.5) now the dashed ghost behind it, a slate SHINE COMPLETE marker, NO "SHINE · STEP 2" step label, and NO holds/planning dial marker). Do not reuse Chapter 18's "SHINE · STEP 2" framing or imply the needle merely stepped again; and keep the goal honestly ahead (three of six S's done; Safety, Standardize, and Sustain still follow).

## Originality and source-fidelity risk
- The chapter is a draft authored in the book voice, grounded in the 6S source ("Principles of 6S: Shine Step," "perform initial cleaning and inspection" turned into a repeatable rhythm), not supplied verbatim source. All derived assets are faithful to that draft; re-sync if the chapter is revised.
- Quote cards are verbatim: 10 cards plus 13 alternates (23 quoted pull-lines), each verified byte-identical to a quoted line in `canonical/chapter-quotes.md` (0 mismatches). Nothing borrowed from outside the book; no brand-name cleaning products are named, and the Seiso / Toyota origin is referenced lightly, home-scaled, not overclaimed.

## Recommendations
1. Rotate CTA wording (highest-value, lowest-effort fix; several posts close on "Read it free online").
2. Pick one carousel version for LinkedIn.
3. Set the chapter page as the SEO canonical.
4. Keep Chapter 19 from blurring into its neighbors: distinct from Chapters 16 to 18 behind it (why, what-and-who, how) and distinct from Safety (Ch 20) ahead of it and the later S's (Standardize, Sustain). Chapter 19 is the KEEP-IT: a short shared rhythm that completes Shine.
5. Re-sync all assets if the authored chapter changes after author review.
