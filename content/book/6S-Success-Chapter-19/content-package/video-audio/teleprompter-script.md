# Teleprompter Script: The 15-Minute Reset

**Chapter 19, 6S Success: Home Edition**
**Slug:** the-15-minute-reset
**Length:** about 720 words. Narration only. Short lines for easy reading.

---

[Read at a calm, even pace. Pause at each blank line.]

Last time you gave every target a working method.

So now you have the whole kit.

You clean to see.

A list of every target, with a name on every line.

And a quick method for each one.

Put it together and you can clean the whole home.

But if you stop there, in a week it drifts right back.

Not because any of the work was wrong.

Because a home is not a thing you clean.

It is a thing you keep clean.

And those are completely different problems.

A one-time clean, however good, is a photograph.

What you actually live in is the film, and the film drifts.

The mug on the counter. The coat off the hook. The mail piling up.

Each is nothing.

Together, over a week of nothings, they undo an afternoon.

So the last piece of Shine is not another way to clean.

It is a way to keep clean.

And here is how the mess works.

A home does not get messy in one big event.

It drifts, a little at a time.

Like a beach filling with footprints, all day.

A problem that arrives a little at a time.

Has to be met a little at a time.

And there is a kinder reason too.

Clean the house is enormous, and vague.

So it does not get started. It gets postponed.

It is not that people do not care.

The job as they picture it is too big to begin.

Shrink the job small enough, and the not-starting stops.

So here is the whole device.

You replace keeping the house clean.

With a small, definite, repeatable job: a fifteen-minute reset.

At a set time, everyone spends fifteen minutes, and only fifteen.

Returning their zones to baseline. Reading as they go.

Until the timer rings. Then you stop.

It is not a deep clean. It is a light touch.

Because it happens often, it never has to do much.

Because it never has to do much, it stays short.

Because it stays short, it actually happens.

And that is the rule. Three words, each one load-bearing.

A little, often, together.

A little, so the job is small enough to start.

Often, so the drift never gets ahead.

Together, so it is fast and fair.

Start with the little. It rests on a timer.

The timer is not a detail. It is the device.

The reason a clean gets postponed is not the cleaning.

It is the fear of the open-ended.

Not knowing if it is twenty minutes or all afternoon.

A timer removes that fear.

Fifteen minutes is fifteen minutes.

You can see the end from the beginning.

So set the timer, and stop when it rings.

Stopping mid-task is not a compromise. It is the mechanism.

Now the together.

Fifteen minutes is nothing on your own.

And a great deal shared out.

Four people for a quarter of an hour.

Is a full hour of cleaning, landing all at once.

This is where the names on the list finally pay their way.

Everyone goes to their own zone at the same moment.

Nobody waits. Nobody supervises. Nobody carries it alone.

A reset done together is not just faster. It is fairer.

Now the often.

The trick is not discipline. It is attachment.

Anchor the reset to something you already do.

After dinner. Before bed. When the kids' show ends.

So it stops being a decision.

And becomes simply what happens at that time.

And reset, do not deep-clean.

It returns the home to baseline. It is not the time to scrub the oven.

Keep it short and light, and the deep cleans grow rare.

Fifteen minutes a day, and you get your weekends back.

Now go look at the friction meter.

It reaches its third mark.

Not because of a bigger clean. Because of a rhythm.

The needle arrives at a marked tick: Shine complete.

The third milestone, after Sort complete and Straighten complete.

A home that keeps itself.

Is a home with the friction finally out of the keeping.

And it is honest. Shine complete is the halfway mark.

Three of six S's are done. The goal is still ahead.

The home is clean and kept. It is not yet safe.

So here is your job.

Tonight, set a timer for fifteen minutes.

Have everyone reset one zone at once.

Then stop when it rings, even if it is not perfect.

The home is clean and kept.

Next, we make it safe.
