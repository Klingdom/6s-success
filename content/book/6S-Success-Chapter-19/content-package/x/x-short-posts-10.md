5/
"Set the timer, and stop when it rings."

The timer is not a detail of the reset; it is the device. A hard stop is what makes people willing to start. Fifteen minutes is fifteen minutes, and you can see the end from the beginning.

6/
Fifteen minutes is nothing on your own and a great deal shared out. Four people resetting together for a quarter of an hour is a full hour of cleaning that costs each of them only fifteen minutes, and the whole home returns to baseline all at once.

(251 chars)

7/
"The trick is not discipline; it is attachment."

Anchor the fifteen minutes to something you already do, after dinner or before bed, and it borrows that thing's reliability. Habits attached to daily cues survive busy weeks.

Reset, do not deep-clean. The reset returns the home to its baseline; it is not the time to scrub the oven. Keep it short and light and the deep cleans grow rare, because nothing ever gets far enough gone to need one. Read the free chapter.
