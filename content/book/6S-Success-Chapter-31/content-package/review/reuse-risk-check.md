Chapter 31 was REWRITTEN to be instruction-centric, so its most portable pieces are now the room-as-zones rule, the nine-rule Shine method, and the per-zone procedures with their exact inputs.

## Same-day collision
The calendar avoids two assets sharing a hook on the same day. If your LinkedIn and Facebook audiences overlap heavily, push one back a day so the rule or the Shine-method line does not land twice in one day.

## Cross-posting risk
- The LinkedIn carousel outline (`linkedin/linkedin-carousel-outline.md`) and the slides carousel copy (`slides/linkedin-carousel-copy.md`) cover the same zone-by-zone content. Post only one to LinkedIn (use the copy-ready slides file; keep the outline as planning).
- The LinkedIn newsletter (`linkedin/linkedin-newsletter-version.md`) and the email newsletter share structure; do not send both to the same subscriber the same day.

## Duplicate-content / SEO risk
- Web landing intro, ebook description, and LinkedIn article overlap in phrasing. Set the chapter page as the SEO canonical and mark syndicated copies with a canonical link.

## Cross-chapter risk (specific to Chapter 31)
- Chapter 31 is the FIRST chapter of Part Nine and the OPENING of the applied half, the PROOF chapter for the new instruction-centric room-playbook format. Keep it distinct from the method chapters behind it: it APPLIES the method to a real room and must NOT re-teach Sort, Straighten, Shine, Safety, Standardize, or Sustain (a one-line callback is fine, a re-teach is not). The nine-rule Shine method is applied here as the governing procedure, not taught from scratch. Shine finds hazards and flags them; the safety pass fixes them, so assets must not slide into re-teaching the hazard hunt.
- Do not let this chapter slide FORWARD into the next room. Ch 31 assets stay in the entryway and may hand off to Part 9's next room (Chapter 32, The Kitchen) in a single line, but must NOT teach it. This fence is held: the kitchen is named only as where the loop goes next.
- The recurring-close beat is now the BEFORE-AND-AFTER room signature, NOT the friction meter. The friction meter reached its goal at Chapter 30 and is RETIRED for Part 9; it does not appear in this chapter or any Part 9 chapter. Assets must close each room on its own concrete before-and-after transformation, governed by the frozen before-and-after caption.
- **No brand leaks.** The instruction-centric kit is given as product TYPES only. Assets must never name a brand; the tone/brand scan confirms this held across the package, and the "Method" matches are the ordinary word "method" (the Shine method), not a brand.

## Originality and source-fidelity risk
- The chapter is a draft REWRITTEN in the book voice to be instruction-centric, grounded in the CH31 canonical strings and brief, not supplied verbatim source. All derived assets are faithful to that draft; re-sync if the chapter is revised.
- Quote cards are verbatim: every card in `graphics/quote-card-copy.md` (10 cards) verified byte-identical to a quoted line in `canonical/chapter-quotes.md` (0 mismatches), including the Shine-method line and the nine-rule list. Nothing borrowed from outside the book. The dash scan returned CLEAN (0 em, 0 en, 0 real spaced-hyphen violations), the tone scan returned CLEAN (every match a negation or prohibition-list mention), and the brand-name scan returned CLEAN (product TYPES only, no brands).

## Recommendations
1. Rotate CTA wording (highest-value, lowest-effort fix; several posts close on "Read the free chapter").
2. Pick one carousel version for LinkedIn.
3. Set the chapter page as the SEO canonical.
4. Keep Chapter 31 from blurring into its neighbors: distinct from the method chapters behind it (it applies, does not re-teach) and distinct from Chapter 32 (The Kitchen) ahead of it. Chapter 31 is the instruction-centric PROOF chapter: you do not 6S a whole room, you 6S one zone of it to the nine-rule Shine method, with exact inputs, closing on the before-and-after room signature.
5. Re-sync all assets if the rewritten chapter changes after author review.
