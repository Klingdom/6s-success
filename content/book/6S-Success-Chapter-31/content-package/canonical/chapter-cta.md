## In-chapter actions a reader can take (from the callouts)
- **Quick Win:** Do one zone, start to finish, to the method: gather the inputs into your caddy, clear the surface, then work top to bottom, dry before wet, clean cloth then dry cloth, and finish with the floor. The landing spot takes about twenty minutes and is the entryway's single highest-leverage square foot. You will feel the difference the next time you walk in.
- **6S Tip:** Colour-code your cloths and never wash them with fabric softener. One colour for glass, one for surfaces, one for the floor and the boot tray, so you never carry grit or grime from a dirty surface onto a clean one. Softener coats the fibres and destroys the grab that makes microfiber work, so it wipes like a wet rag instead.
- **Family Challenge:** Everyone claims their own named shoe shelf and their own hook, with the lowest hook set at the shortest person's height, and two hooks left empty for whoever visits. A third pair or a stray coat then has visibly nowhere to go, and nobody has to nag, because the empty hook does the asking.
