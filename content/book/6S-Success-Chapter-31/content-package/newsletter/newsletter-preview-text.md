# Newsletter Preview Text (Preheader): The Entryway

Ten options, each 35 to 90 characters, each paired with a subject line. Char counts noted.

1. Pairs with #1 (Welcome to the part of the book where the teaching stops and the doing begins. The exact, step-by-step way to clean and shine one room.)
   "The teaching stops, the doing begins. Exactly how to clean one room." (67 chars)

2. Pairs with #2 (Shine is the pass most people do worst, because they were never taught a method. Here is the method.)
   "The pass most people do worst, because no one taught them the method." (69 chars)

3. Pairs with #3 (You do not 6S a whole room. You 6S one zone of it, then the next, until the room is done.)
   "Not the whole room. One zone, then the next, until the door is done." (68 chars)

4. Pairs with #4 (The entryway is five small zones, not one big job: the landing spot, the coats, the shoes, the bench, and the door with its mat.)
   "Five small zones, not one big job. Do them one at a time, not in a weekend." (75 chars)

5. Pairs with #5 (The entryway is the room you cross most and notice most, so the smallest room pays back a 6S loop faster than any other.)
   "The smallest room in the house pays back a 6S loop faster than any other." (73 chars)

6. Pairs with #6 (A zone is not clean because it looks clean. It is clean because you cleaned every surface to a method.)
   "A zone is not clean because it looks clean. It is clean by method." (66 chars)

7. Pairs with #7 (Work top to bottom, dry before wet, mist the cloth, clean then dry. The nine rules that clean any room.)
   "Top to bottom, dry before wet, mist the cloth, clean then dry." (61 chars)

8. Pairs with #8 (Coats are counted by weather, not by number. The expensive coat you never wear is not exempt.)
   "Coats are counted by weather, not by number. Even the one you never wear." (73 chars)

9. Pairs with #9 (A mat looks perfectly fine long after it has stopped working, so you test it instead of looking at it.)
   "A mat looks fine long after it stops working, so you test it, not look at it." (77 chars)

10. Pairs with #10 (Give one zone twenty minutes this weekend, cleaned to the method, and feel the difference.)
    "Give one zone twenty minutes this weekend. Feel it when you walk in." (67 chars)
