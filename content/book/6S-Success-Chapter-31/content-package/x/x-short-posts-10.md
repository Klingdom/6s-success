3/
"Shine is cleaning to a method, not wiping until it looks better: work top to bottom and dry before wet, mist the cloth and not the surface, clean with one cloth and dry with a second, and let your eye inspect every surface your hand passes over." Read it free.

4/
"The entryway is five small zones, not one big job: the landing spot where pockets empty, the coats, the shoes, the bench or console, and the door with its mat and the strip of floor inside its swing." One at a time, it takes short sessions. Read it free.

5/
Colour-code your cloths and never wash them with fabric softener. One colour for glass, one for surfaces, one for the floor and the boot tray, so you never carry grit from a dirty surface onto a clean one. Softener kills the grab that makes microfiber work.

(260 chars)

6/
"A mat looks perfectly fine long after it has stopped working, so you test it instead of looking at it. A worn mat is not saving you anything, it is quietly sanding the finish off every floor between the front door and the kitchen." Read the free chapter.

7/
"Coats are counted by weather, not by number. Each person gets one coat per kind of weather your climate genuinely produces, and a second in the same category has to beat the first on a specific, nameable day." The coat you never wear is not exempt. Read it free.

8/
Do one zone this weekend, start to finish: gather the inputs into your caddy, clear the surface, then top to bottom, dry before wet, clean cloth then dry cloth, and finish with the floor. The landing spot takes about twenty minutes. Feel it when you walk in.

(261 chars)

---

9/
The boot tray is the whole point of the shoe zone: its salt and grit is what scratches every floor in the house. Take it outside, hose it, scrub the corners, and dry it fully before it comes back in. Then vacuum both faces of the mat and beat it outside. Read it free.

(271 chars)

10/
"A zone is not clean because it looks clean. It is clean because you gathered the right inputs, took it apart, and cleaned every surface to a method." Do that to one zone this weekend, and let the door work for you.
