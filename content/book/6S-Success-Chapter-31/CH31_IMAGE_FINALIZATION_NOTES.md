## HELD — the two that are one word away (regenerate these first)

### `image04` "Landing Zone · The Entryway Home Base"
A genuinely lovely Zone 1 plate: the tray holding keys, wallet and phone, mail-in, a pen cup so you can act on mail now, and a labelled OUT tray for outgoing mail. It maps cleanly onto the frozen Zone 1 and onto the frozen definition ("keys and phone drop into one tray... and the paper that comes through the door leaves again with a verdict").

Three of those are frozen rules stated almost verbatim. It is the best method-application plate in either room chapter so far.

**Held for the same one-word rename** ("Landing Zone"), plus the contraction in "don't fix yet".

**Both go straight in once the title word is corrected.** They are the top two entries in the regen kit.

## HELD — canon violations (4). Not caption-rescuable.

### `image11` "Friction Meter — Reset Complete" · **the worst plate in the batch**
Three separate violations at once:
1. **The friction meter is retired for Part 9.** The chapter's own brief says so explicitly: the meter reached its goal in Ch 30 and is replaced by the before-and-after room signature. This plate resurrects it.
2. **It replaces the six S's with six invented factors**: Find It · Access It · See It · Keep It Safe · Keep It Clean · Sustain It. Canon is Sort · Straighten · Shine · **Safety** · Standardize · Sustain. **Sort, Straighten and Standardize are all gone.**
3. **Wrong scale.** A 0-to-10 "Chaos → Effortless" bar with an 8.5 before and a 1.5 after. The book's audit scale (Ch 5) is zero to five per S, totalling thirty, and the meter itself has never carried numbers.

Plus a baked **em dash** in the title.

### `image15` "Your Reset Playbook — Get Unstuck and Keep Going"
**Prints "Use the 5S Questions."** This book is **6S**; the sixth S, Safety, is its entire differentiator and the subject of Chapters 20 to 22. Naming the older five-step methodology inside a 6S book is the most damaging single string the generator has produced in this project. Also: "Set a 10-minute timer" and "Use 5–10 minute resets" against the frozen **15-minute reset** (Ch 19); an **em dash** in the title; and the whole plate is motivational filler, which the Project Instructions forbid outright ("Do not write motivational filler or fake inspiration").

### `image14` "Reset Confidence Builder — One Win at a Time"
A rival five-step system (See Clearly · Remove the Extra · Shine Every Day · Keep It Safe · Reset Together) displacing the six S's, with **Straighten and Standardize missing**. Also:
- **An uncredited quotation from *Atomic Habits***: "You don't rise to the level of your goals. You fall to the level of your systems." Reproducing that in a commercial book without attribution is a real exposure, and *Atomic Habits* is one of this book's two named comp titles.
- An **em dash** in the title, and the typo "Notic**c**e the win".

### `image13` "From Reset to Rhythm — Your 30-Day Plan"
The same invented five-step system as `image14`, this time labelled **"THE GOLDEN RULES"**, which presents it as *the* method. Sort, Straighten and Standardize absent. ## Batch-wide pattern

Ten of the fifteen plates are held, and **eight of those ten carry a baked em dash** — the book's one absolute rule. Four separate plates invent a numbered system that displaces the six S's, and three of those four independently converged on the *same* invented five (See Clearly / Remove the Extra / Shine Every Day / Keep It Safe / Reset Together), which suggests the generating thread drifted once and then repeated itself across a run. That is precisely the failure the one-image-per-fresh-chat workflow exists to prevent.
