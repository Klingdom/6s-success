# Image Quality Review · Chapters 1–17 (wired images only)

Date: 2026-07-22
Scope: all 118 images currently wired into the final HTML of Chapters 1–17.
Method: every wired image viewed at full render and judged as a finished, hyper-realistic
book illustration. "Good enough" images are left alone; only defects and major-improvement
opportunities are listed.

**Bottom line:** the set is strong. The standalone photographs are convincingly photoreal
(no malformed hands, faces, or geometry anywhere), and the infographics are clean and legible.
The issues fall into four recurring buckets, none of which is a bad *image* per se: real-brand
logos, baked-in em dashes, fake QR codes, and a handful of garbled-text / mislabel errors.
**None of these can be fixed by editing the HTML — they are painted into the pixels, so each one
is a regenerate-or-retouch task.**

Chapters with NO issues at all: **5, 9** (and 11 is SVG-only). Nearly clean: **10, 12**.

## PART B · The four cross-cutting patterns

**1. Real brand logos / trademarks (a book-wide decision).**
- Clear logos to remove: **Amazon** (ch1-6), **Toyota** wordmark+emblem (ch2-3), **Fjällräven**
  backpack (ch7-5, ch15-1/6/7), **Bounty/Charmin** (ch14-8).
- Real book spines: **KINFOLK / KINFOLK HOME** (ch1-5, ch1-12, ch8-13a, ch12-0a).
- The **same Fjällräven backpack** is the recurring "launch pad" prop, so one regenerated backpack
  asset would clear four images at once.
- Real cleaning-product brands saturate Chapter 3 — a policy call (keep as real-room realism, or
  neutralize).

**2. Baked-in em dashes (violate the book's own no-em-dash rule).** Eight wired plates: ch4-2,
ch6-6, ch7-2, ch7-3, ch15-7, ch16-1, ch16-8, ch17-5. The text scan can't catch these because they
are pixels, not HTML — each needs the plate regenerated with a comma or restructure.

**3. Fake / non-scannable QR codes.** ~15 wired plates carry them: ch3-4, ch3-6, ch4-11, ch8-2,
ch8-3, ch8-4, ch8-6, ch13-1, ch13-2, ch13-7, ch14-7, ch14-8, ch17-5, ch17-9. Every one promises a
downloadable resource. In a printed book a dead QR is a broken promise — strip them, or point them
at real assets, before print.

**4. Garbled text / mislabels (unambiguous errors).** ch3-6/12/13 (Meyer's), ch4-6 ("miilk"),
ch6-2 ("6-022"), ch6-7 ("AFTER6"), ch6-8 (2004), ch8-4 (newspaper), ch10-3a ("becomee" + the
contradictory label), ch13-4 (fork icon for Keys), ch14-4 (jar labels).

## What can and cannot be done from here

- **Cannot be fixed in HTML:** every item above is painted into the image. Fixing means regenerating
  the plate (for text/logo/QR errors) or masking/retouching (for a logo or a QR corner). There is no
  image-generation tool in this environment, so this document is the worklist for that pass.
- **Can be done now if wanted:** for the QR codes specifically, the captions live in the HTML `alt`
  text and figcaptions — those already describe the plate rather than instructing a scan, so nothing
  needs changing there; the QR removal is purely an image task.
