# SEO Title Options: Chapter 29, Daily 6S Times

Five title options. Character counts are in parentheses. The canonical title is listed first.

1. Daily 6S Times (14)
   Angle: canonical chapter title. Use as the H1 and primary on-page heading, with "stop relying on memory," "anchor keeping to the day," "short, anchored, and together," "a rhythm, not a schedule," and "a home that keeps itself" early in the body.

2. Hand the Job to the Day (23)
   Angle: leads with the One Idea to Keep. Names the whole move in a single warm line, that you stop asking your memory to keep the home and hang the upkeep on the day itself, and answers the reader worn out by trying to remember it all.

3. Anchor It to the Day (20)
   Angle: leads with the hero. Says plainly where keeping should hang, on a moment that already happens rather than on your memory, so a keeping-habit becomes a passenger on a trip the day was already taking, and is quotable and snippet-ready.

4. A Rhythm, Not a Schedule (24)
   Angle: names the relief in the reader's own words. Reframes daily keeping from a rigid chore timetable into a couple of small acts bolted to moments that already happen, and answers a real search for how to keep a home without a wall planner of coloured blocks.

5. Stop Asking Your Memory to Keep the Home (40)
   Angle: leads with the honest diagnosis at the heart of the chapter. Concrete and freeing, it says the thing a tired reader most needs to hear, that the trouble was never their character but their method, a keeping-plan with a single point of failure, their memory.
