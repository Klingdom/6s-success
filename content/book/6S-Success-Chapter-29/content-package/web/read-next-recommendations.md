3. Bolt one two-minute keeping-act to the day (the chapter action)
   Pick one thing that already happens in your day without fail, the kettle boiling, the school run leaving, the lights going off at night, and bolt one two-minute keeping-act to it, starting today. You just stopped hanging that bit of keeping on your memory and hung it on the day instead. It costs about two minutes, and it is the smallest possible proof of the whole idea: you will never have to remember it again, because the day will remember it for you, and the reliable old habit drags the small new one along behind it.
