## Same-day collision
The calendar avoids two assets sharing a hook on the same day. If your LinkedIn and Facebook audiences overlap heavily, push one back a day so the rule or the anchor reframe does not land twice in one day.

## Cross-posting risk
- The LinkedIn carousel outline (`linkedin/linkedin-carousel-outline.md`) and the slides carousel copy (`slides/linkedin-carousel-copy.md`) cover the same daily-times content. Post only one to LinkedIn (use the copy-ready slides file; keep the outline as planning).
- The LinkedIn newsletter (`linkedin/linkedin-newsletter-version.md`) and the email newsletter share structure; do not send both to the same subscriber the same day.

## Duplicate-content / SEO risk
- Web landing intro, ebook description, and LinkedIn article overlap in phrasing. Set the chapter page as the SEO canonical and mark syndicated copies with a canonical link.

## Cross-chapter risk (specific to Chapter 29)
- Chapter 29 is the FOURTH chapter of Part Eight and the DAILY-RHYTHM chapter. Keep it distinct from Chapter 28 behind it (which gave the shared way to solve recurring problems), from Chapter 27 (the friendly audit), and from Chapter 26 (the mindset). Chapter 29 uses the mindset, the audit, and the problem-solving but assets must NOT re-teach any of them; they reference the fifteen-minute reset (Chapter 19) in ONE light line as one example of a daily time and must NOT re-teach it; they reference the schedule tool (Chapter 24) lightly and must NOT re-teach it; and they must NOT re-teach Sort, Straighten, Shine, Safety, or Standardize (a one-line callback is fine, a re-teach is not). Keep the daily times distinct from the Chapter 19 reset (the reset is one such daily time, not the whole idea) and from the Chapter 24 schedule tool (a rhythm bolted to the day, not a schedule you consult and remember).
- Do not let this chapter slide FORWARD into the book's close. Ch 29 assets keep it to the daily rhythm and must NOT build the recurring 6S event or the book close (Chapter 30). This fence is held: the later practice is named in the package only as the chapter still ahead, never built.
- The friction-meter beat is specific to this chapter, and it is a small MOVE along the last-gap path, NOT a milestone. The live needle moves a short step from where Chapter 28 left it, at (67.2,154.97), to (67.1,154.98), a little further along the dashed last-gap path toward the GOAL crosshair at (67,155), tagged "SUSTAIN · STEP 3," Chapter 28's position now a faint ghost just behind, Chapter 27's at (67.3,154.96) fainter, and Chapter 26's at (67.4,154.95) fainter still. The move is honestly small in distance and large in meaning, and it comes very close to the goal but does not reach it. Do not overstate this as a milestone and do not understate the move; the goal stays a step ahead, reached only at the very end of the book (Chapter 30), when all six S's are done.

## Originality and source-fidelity risk
- The chapter is a draft authored in the book voice, grounded in the 6S source for Sustain (defining set daily times for keeping, habit anchoring, "a little, often, together"), not supplied verbatim source. All derived assets are faithful to that draft; re-sync if the chapter is revised.
- Quote cards are verbatim: every card in `graphics/quote-card-copy.md` (10 cards) verified byte-identical to a quoted line in `canonical/chapter-quotes.md` (0 mismatches). Nothing borrowed from outside the book. The widened dash scan across the 54 non-review files returned CLEAN. Note that a dedicated hype-word scan, a force / control / discipline scan, and a brand / product-endorsement scan were NOT run programmatically for this package, so no pass is claimed for them here.

## Recommendations
1. Rotate CTA wording (highest-value, lowest-effort fix; several posts close on "Read the free chapter").
2. Pick one carousel version for LinkedIn.
3. Set the chapter page as the SEO canonical.
4. Keep Chapter 29 from blurring into its neighbors: distinct from Chapter 28 (the shared problem-solving), Chapter 27 (the friendly audit), and Chapter 26 (the mindset) behind it, and distinct from the Chapter 30 close ahead of it. Chapter 29 is the DAILY-RHYTHM chapter: keeping fails when it depends on remembering, so hang it on the day; anchor the small upkeep to moments that already happen; keep it short, anchored, and together; and the honest third Sustain step along the last-gap path.
5. Re-sync all assets if the authored chapter changes after author review.
