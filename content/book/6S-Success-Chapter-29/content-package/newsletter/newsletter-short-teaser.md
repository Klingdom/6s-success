# Newsletter Short Teaser: Daily 6S Times

For a roundup or digest. 90 to 140 words.

---

You have the mindset, the friendly audit, and the shared way to solve recurring problems. Together they are most of what keeping takes, but all three still lean on the same fragile thing: someone remembering to do them. And remembering is the least reliable tool in the whole box, working on the calm days a home barely needs keeping and failing on the tired ones it most wants to slide. So you stop asking your memory to keep the home and hand the job to the day: you bolt a couple of short keeping-times to moments that already happen without fail, so the upkeep rides the rhythm of the day itself. Keep them short and anchored, so no one has to remember, and do them together. Nobody has to remember them, because the day remembers them for you.
