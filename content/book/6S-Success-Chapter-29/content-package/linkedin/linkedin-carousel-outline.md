# Chapter 29 LinkedIn Carousel Outline: Stop Trying to Remember, Hang Keeping on the Day

Twelve slides. Slide 1 hooks, last slide carries the soft CTA. Each slide has a headline and one or two lines of body. Warm, light, freeing voice; a rhythm and not a chore schedule, a habit and not willpower; forgetting is normal because memory is unreliable, which is the whole reason the method exists; no force, control, or discipline-as-punishment words, no hype, no brand names.

---

## Slide 1: Hook

**Headline:** Your good intentions about the house keep dying on the tired days, and you blame yourself. You are not the problem. Your method is.

**Body:** Keeping fails when it depends on remembering. Swipe for the light, freeing way a household hangs keeping on the day itself, so it runs whether or not anyone remembers.

---

## Slide 2: The weakness they all share

**Headline:** You now have three real ways to keep a home, and they share one big weakness.

**Body:** The mindset, the friendly audit, and the shared way to solve recurring problems are most of what keeping takes. But every one of them still depends on somebody remembering to do it.

---

## Slide 3: The reframe that lifts the shame

**Headline:** And remembering, it turns out, is the least reliable tool in the whole box.

**Body:** It works on the calm days a home barely needs keeping, and it fails on the tired, overwhelmed days a home most wants to slide. A keeping-plan that runs on memory works only when you least need it.

---

## Slide 4: It is your method, not your character

**Headline:** The reason your good intentions keep failing is not that you are forgetful.

**Body:** It is that you are asking your memory to do a job the day should be doing. That aims the fix at your character, which you cannot change much, instead of your method, which you can change completely.

---

## Slide 5: The fix

**Headline:** Stop hanging keeping on your memory. Start hanging it on the day.

**Body:** Attach the small upkeep to fixed moments that already happen every day without fail, so it rides along on the rhythm of the day, whether or not anyone is thinking about the house.

---

## Slide 6: Anchor it to the day

**Headline:** The easiest new habit is one you never have to start, because it is bolted to an old one that already runs without fail.

**Body:** You do not need to remember to reset the kitchen after dinner if the reset simply is part of after dinner, the way clearing the plates already is.

---

## Slide 7: The mechanism

**Headline:** Bolt a small keeping-act onto a moment that already happens.

**Body:** The kettle boils, the school run leaves, the dishes get cleared, the lights go off. Hang the new habit on one of these, and the reliable old habit drags the small new one along behind it, carried for free.

---

## Slide 8: The definition

**Headline:** What a daily 6S time actually is.

**Body:** A short, shared moment of keeping, anchored to something that already happens in the day, so the small upkeep runs on the day's rhythm rather than on anyone's memory. A couple of them, a few minutes each, are enough.

---

## Slide 9: Short, anchored, together

**Headline:** Three qualities decide whether a daily time survives real life.

**Body:** Keep it short, a few minutes with a hard stop; anchor it to a moment that already happens, so no one has to remember; and do it together, so it is quick and shared rather than one person's chore.

---

## Slide 10: The quiet hum

**Headline:** The daily times are the quiet hum underneath the audit and the problem-solving.

**Body:** The small, constant upkeep that means the home never drifts far enough to need catching or solving in the first place. This is the rhythm that finally makes keeping automatic.

---

## Slide 11: A day with its times

**Headline:** A whole day of keeping, folded in, looks like almost nothing.

**Body:** A two-minute morning tidy anchored to leaving, a short after-dinner reset anchored to clearing the plates, and the rest of the day left alone. Nobody had to remember either one, because the day remembered them.

---

**Headline:** The needle takes its third Sustain step, and it is nearly at the goal now.
