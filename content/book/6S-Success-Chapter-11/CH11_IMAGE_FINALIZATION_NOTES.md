## The core problem: the hero device does not exist anywhere

Chapter 11's frozen hero device is **The Five Doors Out**: every unnecessary thing leaves through
**Donate, Sell, Store, Recycle, or Throw**, with Donate as the deliberately wide default lane and
Sell and Store drawn narrow because they are traps.

Across all 20 plates:

- **The words Sell, Store, Recycle and Throw do not appear once.** Not in any plate, in any form.
- **Donate** appears only as a demoted sublabel underneath "LET GO", or as a plain box label. It
  is never a door, never a lane, never one of five.
- **The frozen worked example appears nowhere.** Not one plate routes a single named item to a
  door. The two umbrellas, the tote bags, the spare key to the sold car, the takeout menus and
  the dead charging cable are all absent.

Instead, six plates (6, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12) build their hero around a **rival three-box device:
KEEP / MAYBE (Decide by 8/15) / LET GO**.

That device is not merely different, it is **actively contradictory**. Chapter 11 opens with the
out box already decided; the chapter's entire job is routing it out of the house. A KEEP/MAYBE
device re-opens the keep-or-discard question that Chapters 9 and 10 already closed, and the
"Decide by 8/15" tags and "Maybe Shelf" are Chapter 10's red tags and holding area reproduced
verbatim, which the frozen brief explicitly fences out.

## Regeneration brief

Discard this set. Generate against the frozen brief, not against generic organizing themes.

**1. The hero device is The Five Doors Out.** Five destinations, named exactly:
**Donate · Sell · Store · Recycle · Throw.** Draw **Donate as the widest lane**, Recycle as the
second fast lane, and **Sell and Store deliberately narrow**, because the chapter teaches that
they are traps.

**2. The routing cascade, in this order:**
- Broken or truly unusable? → **Recycle** if you can, otherwise **Throw**
- Genuinely valuable and worth your time to sell? → **Sell**, with a deadline
- Genuinely needed later, just not now? → **Store**, labelled and dated
- Still useful to someone? → **Donate**. This is where most of the box goes.

**3. The worked example, using these exact items:**

| Item | Door |
|---|---|
| Two extra umbrellas | Donate |
| Surplus tote bags | Donate (worn ones Recycle) |
| Spare key to the sold car | Recycle or Throw |
| Old takeout menus | Recycle |
| Dead charging cable | Recycle, as e-waste |

The whole box goes out through Donate and Recycle. Nothing is sold, nothing is stored. That is
the point of the example.

**4. Do not show:** KEEP / MAYBE / LET GO boxes, red tags, decide-by dates, maybe shelves,
holding areas, retrieval of released items, assigning homes to keepers, habit trackers, daily
routines, or mindset content. Every one of those belongs to another chapter.

**5. If the six S's appear at all:** Sort · Straighten · Shine · **Safety** · Standardize ·
Sustain. Safety fourth, always present. Never "Set in Order". Never "Step Up". Reference image:
`6S-Success-Chapter-3/ch3-image2.png`.

**6. House voice:** no contractions, no em or en dashes.

**7. Four figures are specified in the brief** (opener with the five doors, the routing cascade,
the routing pass with the tally, and the friction meter at the Sort-complete milestone). The
existing SVGs already render all four correctly and can serve as the visual reference for the
photographic versions.
