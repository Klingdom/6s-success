# Back Cover Copy: What to Donate, Sell, Store, Recycle, or Throw Away

## Back-cover text

The out box is full, and every call in it is already made. The umbrellas, the tote bags, the spare key to a car sold two summers ago, the old takeout menus, the dead cable: things you already decided you do not need, sitting together in the corner, waiting to go somewhere. The hard part is behind you. All that is left is to get the box out of the house.

Chapter 11 of *6S Success: Home Edition* is the fourth and final Sort chapter, and it closes Sort. "Deciding a thing is unnecessary and deciding where it should go are two separate acts, and you already finished the first." So this is a routing chapter. Every unnecessary thing leaves through one of five doors, Donate, Sell, Store, Recycle, or Throw, and a quick question picks the door for each item. Two of them finish quickest, Donate and Recycle, and take most of the box; Sell and Store are narrow on purpose, each with a guardrail, because "Storing is not deciding" and "Selling turns clearing into a part-time job."

Donate is the default for anything still usable, and giving it away is not waste: "You are not throwing value away. You are moving it to where it will finally get used, and taking your space back in the bargain." The worn and finished things go to Recycle and Throw without guilt, because the waste, if there ever was any, happened at the store, not at the bin.

Then the box leaves the building, and the friction meter takes its last Sort step to a Sort-complete milestone. The first of the six S's is done, the room is genuinely lighter, and the goal is still ahead, because five S's remain. Grab three things, get them out today, and Sort is finished.
