# What to Donate, Sell, Store, Recycle, or Throw Away

**Part Three, Sort**
Chapter 11 of *6S Success: Home Edition*

Subtitle · The out box is full and the sort is nearly done. Send each thing out through one of five doors, lean hard on the fast ones, and get it out of the house today.

---

## Chapter descriptor

The fourth and final Sort chapter, and the one that closes Sort. The out box holds the things you already decided you do not need; today you only route it. Every unnecessary thing leaves through one of five doors, Donate, Sell, Store, Recycle, or Throw. A quick cascade picks the door, Donate and Recycle take most of the box, and the two slow doors each get a guardrail. Then the box leaves the building and the first S is done.

---

## How to read this chapter

This is the last chapter of Part Three, Sort, and it picks up exactly where the last pass left off, with the out box full and settled in the corner. It is not about deciding what is unnecessary; you made those calls in Chapter 9, and they are done. It is about getting the already-decided box out of the house cleanly, in one trip.

Read it through once for the idea, then tip the out box out and take the items one at a time. Run each down the cascade and stop at the first door that fits: broken or truly unusable, then Recycle it if you can and Throw it if you cannot; genuinely valuable and worth your time to sell, then Sell it with a deadline; genuinely needed later, just not now, then Store it, labeled and dated; still useful to someone, then Donate it, which is where most of the box goes. Lean on the two fast lanes, Donate and Recycle, and keep Sell and Store for the rare item that truly earns them.

Nothing here re-runs the red tags; the tagged maybes stay on their timers in the holding area, and when a date passes they route the same easy way. Nothing here gives the keepers homes either; where your working things live so the space stays easy is the next part, Straighten, and we do not touch it today. Today has one narrow task: send each unnecessary thing through the right door, and carry the box out.

If you only do one thing: grab three things from the out box and get them out of the house today. The usable ones into a donation bag in your car; the broken one to recycling or the bin. Three things gone for good in five minutes.
