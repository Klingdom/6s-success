# Chapter 11 Calls to Action: What to Donate, Sell, Store, Recycle, or Throw Away

This is the fourth and final Sort chapter, and it closes Sort. Its strongest CTA is the smallest possible physical move: get three things out of the house today. The CTAs match the chapter's calm, non-prescriptive, anti-shame tone: donating is not waste, nobody has to sell anything, and throwing a worn-out thing away is not a failure, because the waste, if there ever was any, happened at the store.

## Primary CTA
Get three things out today. "Grab three things from the out box and get them out of the house today. The usable ones into a donation bag in your car; the broken one to recycling or the bin. Three things gone for good in five minutes. Read the chapter free in the online book."

## Secondary CTA
Read the chapter (or the next one). "The out box is full and the sort is nearly done. Chapter 11 sends each thing out through one of five doors, Donate, Sell, Store, Recycle, or Throw, leans hard on the two fast ones, and gets the whole box out of the house in a single trip. Read it free online, then Chapter 12 begins Part Four and gives every keeper a place of its own."

## In-chapter actions a reader can take (from the callouts)
- **Quick Win:** Grab three things from the out box and get them out of the house today. The usable ones into a donation bag in your car; the broken one to recycling or the bin. Three things gone for good in five minutes.
- **Family Challenge:** Everyone picks one thing they have been meaning to sell someday and says honestly whether they will do the work this week. If not, it goes in the donate bag. Nobody has to sell anything; everybody has to be honest about it.
- **6S Tip:** Put the donation bag in your car now, not by the door. A bag by the door gets unpacked; a bag in the car gets dropped off.

## CTA writing notes
- The action is physical and immediate: get things out of the house, not just decided. Lead with "three things out today," which is concrete and can be done in five minutes.
- Keep it non-prescriptive and anti-shame. Donating is not waste, nobody has to sell anything, and worn-out things belong in the bin without guilt.
- "Donate is the default" and the throw reframe are the most memorable, shareable lines. Lead social with them.
- Avoid hype and any force or violence framing. The whole move is routing what is already decided to the door that actually gets it gone.
- Scope note: this routes the out box; it does not arrange the keepers. Giving every keeper a place is Chapter 12, the start of Part Four (Straighten).
