## Sequencing logic

1. Lock `canonical/` first. If the title, slug, the five door names, "Donate is the default," the sell trap or store trap lines, the definition box, or "One Idea to Keep" changes, it ripples everywhere.
2. Build `web/` next, because the chapter URL is the destination every soft CTA points to.
3. Then social and email, which drive traffic to that URL.
4. Video, audio, slides, and graphics are higher-effort and can trail the text assets by a few days.
5. The Five Doors Out and "Donate is the default" are the two highest-value assets. Seed the card early and lead social with the default line.
