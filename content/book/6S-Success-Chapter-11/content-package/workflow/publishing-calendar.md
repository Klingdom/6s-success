# Publishing Calendar: 14-Day Release Sequence

A two-week rollout for Chapter 11, "What to Donate, Sell, Store, Recycle, or Throw Away". This is the fourth and final chapter of Part Three · Sort, and it closes Sort. Chapter 9 made the calls and filled the out box; Chapter 10 built the red tag and the holding area for the honest maybes. This chapter routes the settled out box out through five doors and finishes the first S. The goal is to get readers to route three things from their own out box today: the usable ones into a donation bag in the car, the broken one to recycling or the bin. Publish the home base (the web chapter) first, then drip social and email toward it. Dates are relative (Day 1 = the day the chapter page goes live).

Pacing principle: one anchor asset per day, plus one or two light reshares. The Five Doors Out and "Donate is the default" are your strongest. Lead with them.

## After Day 14

- Recycle The Five Doors Out and the strongest quote cards monthly.
- Keep the chapter page updated with an FAQ block for SEO (see `canonical/chapter-seo.md`). The Out-Box Routing checklist is a strong lead magnet.
- When Chapter 12 publishes, link this chapter from its "previously" section, and link forward from here. This chapter closes Sort, so it hands off a house where what does not belong and what you do not need are both gone, and the next part, Straighten, starts giving the keepers their places.

## Hero CTA reminder

This chapter's best conversion event is routing three real things out of the house. Wherever you can, end with "grab three things from your out box and get them out today. The usable ones into a donation bag in your car, the broken one to recycling or the bin. Three things gone for good in five minutes." A reader who routes three things has finished a corner of Sort.
