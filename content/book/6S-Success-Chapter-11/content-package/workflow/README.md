## The chapter in one screen

- **Hero device · The Five Doors Out.** Every unnecessary thing in the out box leaves through one of five doors: Donate, Sell, Store, Recycle, Throw. A quick question picks the door for each item. Broken or truly unusable goes to Recycle where you can, otherwise Throw. Genuinely valuable and worth your time goes to Sell, with a deadline. Genuinely needed later, just not now, goes to Store, labeled and dated, elsewhere. Still useful to someone goes to Donate, and that is where most of the box goes.
- **Donate is the default.** Most usable things you do not need should go to someone who does, today, not to a someday sale that keeps them in your house for months. Donate and Recycle are the fast lanes and take most of the box.
- **The sell trap.** Selling turns clearing into a part-time job. Sell only what is genuinely worth the work, put a deadline on it, and donate whatever has not sold by the date.
- **The store trap.** Storing is not deciding. Store only what you will truly use later, label it and date it, and never rent space to keep what you do not need.
- **The throw reframe.** Some things are simply worn out, and throwing them away is not the waste. The waste, if there ever was any, happened at the store. Recycle what you can, bin the rest without guilt, and be done.
- **The routing pass.** The entryway out box, item by item: the two extra umbrellas go to Donate, the surplus tote bags go to Donate with the worn ones to Recycle, the takeout menus go to Recycle, the dead charging cable goes to Recycle as e-waste, and the spare key to the sold car goes to Recycle or Throw. Everything went through Donate or Recycle, in one trip, because that is the normal shape of an out box.
- **The needle takes its last Sort step.** The out box leaves the building, so the last of the Sort clutter is gone from the house, not just off the surface. The needle steps from where Chapter 10 held it to a Sort-complete mark on the dial, and Chapter 10's held position becomes the dashed ghost.
