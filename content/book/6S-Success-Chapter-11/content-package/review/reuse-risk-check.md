# Reuse Risk Check: Chapter 11 Content Package

When one chapter becomes fifty-plus assets, the risk is overexposure: the same line, hook, or image hitting the same follower too many times until the message feels canned. This check flags where that can happen and how the calendar manages it. Every element named below was grep-verified in the manuscript, and every asset placement was read in the file named, before being listed.

## Same-day collision check
The calendar is built to avoid two assets sharing the same hook on the same day. If your LinkedIn and Facebook audiences overlap heavily, do not let the "Donate is the default" rule and the "the waste happened at the store" reframe both land the same day, since they both carry the chapter's core anti-guilt beat.

## Platform cross-posting risk
- The LinkedIn carousel outline (`linkedin/linkedin-carousel-outline.md`) and the slides carousel copy (`slides/linkedin-carousel-copy.md`) cover the same twelve-slide five-door content; the copy file states it is "consistent with linkedin-carousel-outline.md" (verified). Post only one to LinkedIn. Use the copy-ready version (`slides/linkedin-carousel-copy.md`) and keep the outline as a planning doc.
- The LinkedIn newsletter version (`linkedin/linkedin-newsletter-version.md`) and the email newsletter version (`newsletter/newsletter-version.md`) share structure and subject. Fine for different lists, but do not send both to the same subscriber the same day.

## Duplicate-content / SEO risk
- The web landing intro, ebook description, and LinkedIn article overlap in phrasing. For SEO, the canonical chapter page should be the only indexed full version. Set syndicated copies (LinkedIn article, Medium, and the like) to point back with a canonical link.

## Cross-chapter risk (specific to Part 3, Sort, Chapters 8 to 11)
- **Keep Chapter 11 "routing the out box" distinct from Chapters 8, 9, and 10.** Chapter 8 sorted by belonging, Chapter 9 sorted by use and filled the out box, and Chapter 10 tagged the honest maybes. Chapter 11 re-decides none of that; it routes. The manuscript draws the line, verbatim: "Deciding a thing is unnecessary and deciding where it should go are two separate acts, and you already finished the first." Do not let a Chapter 11 asset re-open the belonging question, the use test, or the red tags. The manuscript is explicit: "We are not reopening whether anything belongs in the box; that was Chapter 9" and "We are not re-running the red tags" (verified). Its job is disposition, not decision.
- **Keep Chapter 11 distinct from Part 4 (Straighten).** Chapter 11 removes; Chapter 12 arranges. The manuscript holds the fence, verbatim: "We stop removing and we start arranging. That is the whole of the next part." Do not let a Chapter 11 asset give the keepers homes or show anything being put in its place; that is Chapter 12's payoff. The handoff is named, verbatim: "**Next: Chapter 12 · Straighten: A Place for Everything.**"
- **The out box is the shared object across the whole of Sort.** It was filled in Chapter 9, received the expired maybes' pathway in Chapter 10, and is routed and carried out in Chapter 11. When Chapters 9, 10, and 11 assets are live together, make sure a Chapter 11 asset is the only one that shows the box actually leaving; do not let an earlier-chapter asset empty it early, and do not let a Chapter 11 asset re-fill or re-decide it.
- **The red-tagged maybes stay Chapter 10's, with one honest seam.** Chapter 11 notes that when a tagged maybe expires untouched it "will drop into an out box and route exactly the way today's items route" (verified). That is a forward reference, not a re-run of the red tag. Keep Chapter 11 assets from re-teaching the tag or the holding area; they only note that expired maybes rejoin the same five doors later.
- **The friction meter MOVES this chapter, and that is the distinguishing beat.** Chapters 8 and 9 stepped the needle, Chapter 10 held it and drew a dashed forward promise, and Chapter 11 fulfills that promise: the needle steps off its held position onto a marked "Sort complete" tick because the out box physically left the building. Chapter 10's held position is now the faint dashed ghost, and the GOAL crosshair is still ahead because five S's remain (all present in both HTML files, verified). Make sure no Chapter 11 asset shows the needle reaching the goal crosshair or the end of the journey; the milestone closes the first S, not the whole method. Preserve the "first S done, room genuinely lighter, goal still ahead" framing so the move reads as an honest milestone, not a finish line.
- **The entryway example keeps running and now pays off.** The console, the basket, the holding-area shelf, and now the out box leaving are the same entryway carried since Part 2. When Part 2, Part 3, and the coming Part 4 assets are live together, vary which step each post leads with so the same entryway does not feel overused.

## Originality and source-fidelity risk
- The chapter itself is a draft authored in the book voice, not supplied source. All derived assets are faithful to that draft. If the author revises the chapter, refresh the assets so they do not drift from the published version.
- Nothing is borrowed from outside the book. Chapter 11 introduces no new external tool, so it makes no origin attribution and, correctly, no asset presents the five-door routing as either the book's invention or a cited method. (The red tag, the one lightly attributed external idea in this Part, belongs to Chapter 10 and appears here only as a callback.)
- The ten quote cards are verbatim from the manuscript (each string grep-verified as byte-identical; the file marks Cards 1 through 10 as **verbatim** and labels its three alternates **verbatim** as well). That labeling is accurate.

## Recommendations
1. Rotate the six CTA phrasings across posts (highest-value, lowest-effort fix).
2. Pick one carousel version for LinkedIn, not both.
3. Set the chapter page as the SEO canonical and mark syndicated copies.
4. Keep Chapter 11 "routing the out box" clearly separate from Chapters 8, 9, and 10, and hold the keepers' homes as Chapter 12's work, not this chapter's.
5. Preserve the "needle steps to Sort complete, first S done, goal still ahead" framing and the dashed ghost of Chapter 10's position in every asset that shows the gauge.
6. Re-sync all assets if the authored chapter changes after author review.
