# Facebook Group Discussion Post (Home and Organizing Groups)

Written for a home-and-organizing Facebook group. Leads with a genuinely useful nugget (the five doors and the two guardrails), then asks an open question to get people talking. Friendly group tone, soft CTA at the very end, and written so it does NOT read like an ad.

---

Okay, the thing that finally got a full donation bag out of my house in one trip, and then I want to hear what is stalling your last pass.

For years my sorts died at the very end, at the pile of stuff I had decided I did not need but had not actually moved anywhere. It sat in a bag by the stairs for a season, waiting for a plan that never quite arrived.

Here is what changed. I stopped treating it as more deciding and started treating it as routing. Everything already decided leaves through one of five doors: Donate, Sell, Store, Recycle, Throw. You ask one quick question and stop at the first door that fits. Broken, then Recycle or Throw. Genuinely worth selling, then Sell with a deadline. Truly needed later, then Store, labeled and dated. Still useful to someone, then Donate. And Donate is the default, because most usable things you do not need should go to someone who does, today.

The two things that saved me from myself:

Selling turns clearing into a part-time job. So I only sell what is genuinely worth the photographs and the stranger at the door, and I put a sell-by date on it. If it does not sell by then, it goes in the donate bag.

And storing is not deciding. A locker full of things I already decided I do not use is just my old clutter, moved offsite and charged to me monthly.

The last worn-out things went to recycling and the bin without guilt, because the waste, if there ever was any, happened at the store, not at the bin.

So here is what I am genuinely curious about:

What is the door you over-use? Be honest. Are you a someday seller, a just-in-case storer, or do you hoard the broken stuff you feel bad throwing out? And what is one thing you could route out of your house today?

I will go first in the comments with my box of tote bags that somehow multiply. 😄

(If anyone wants this written out properly, it is from Chapter 11 of a book called 6S Success: Home Edition, free to read online. Not selling anything, just the calmest way to finish a sort I have found.)
