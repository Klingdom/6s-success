# Chapter 11 LinkedIn Comment Prompts: What to Donate, Sell, Store, Recycle, or Throw Away

Five prompts to start genuine discussion. Drop one in the comments of a post or article to invite replies.

---

1. Which of the five doors is hardest for you to use: Donate, Sell, Store, Recycle, or Throw? Name it here, no judgment. For a lot of us it is the one that feels most responsible.

2. Be honest about the someday sale. What is one thing you have been meaning to sell for months that could go in the donate bag today instead? What has kept it in the house?

3. Have you ever paid to store something you had already decided you do not use? No shame in it. What would it take to route it out for good?

4. Throwing a worn-out thing away is not the waste; the waste, if there ever was any, happened at the store. What is one broken object you are keeping only because binning it feels wasteful?

5. Picture your own out box tipped out on the floor. Roughly what share of it would go through Donate and Recycle, the two fast lanes, versus Sell and Store? Give us your honest split.
