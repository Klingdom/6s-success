# Chapter 11 LinkedIn Carousel Outline: What to Donate, Sell, Store, Recycle, or Throw Away

Twelve slides. Slide 1 hooks, last slide carries the soft CTA. Each slide has a headline and one or two lines of body.

---

## Slide 1: Hook

**Headline:** The out box is full. Now it just needs to leave.

**Body:** Every thing in it has already been decided. Swipe for the five doors out, and the two that finish the job fastest.

---

## Slide 2: Route, do not decide

**Headline:** You are here to route.

**Body:** Deciding a thing is unnecessary and deciding where it should go are two separate acts. You already finished the first. Today is smaller and calmer than it looks.

---

## Slide 3: The five doors

**Headline:** Five doors out, and only five.

**Body:** Donate · Sell · Store · Recycle · Throw. That is the whole set. A quick question picks the door for each item.

---

## Slide 4: The cascade

**Headline:** Stop at the first door that fits.

**Body:** Broken? Recycle, or Throw. Valuable and worth the work? Sell, with a deadline. Needed later? Store, labeled and dated. Still useful? Donate.

---

## Slide 5: The fast lanes

**Headline:** Two doors take most of the box.

**Body:** Donate and Recycle sit at the top and bottom of the cascade and carry the great majority of anything you route. Sell and Store are narrow on purpose.

---

## Slide 6: Donate is the default

**Headline:** Donate is the default.

**Body:** Most usable things you do not need should go to someone who does, today, not to a someday sale that keeps them in your house for months.

---

## Slide 7: Giving it away is not waste

**Headline:** You are not throwing value away.

**Body:** The thing is not being destroyed. You are moving it to where it will finally get used, and taking your space back in the bargain.

---

## Slide 8: The sell trap

**Headline:** Selling turns clearing into a part-time job.

**Body:** Sell only what is genuinely worth the work, put a deadline on it, and donate whatever has not sold by the date.

---

## Slide 9: The store trap

**Headline:** Storing is not deciding.

**Body:** Store only what you will truly use later, label it and date it, and never rent space to keep what you do not need.

---

## Slide 10: Throw without guilt

**Headline:** Some things are simply worn out.

**Body:** Throwing them away is not the waste. The waste, if there ever was any, happened at the store. Recycle what you can, bin the rest without guilt, and be done.

---

## Slide 11: Sort complete

**Headline:** The needle takes its last Sort step.

**Body:** The out box left the building, so the meter moves to a marked tick: Sort complete. The first S is done. The goal is still ahead, because five S's remain.

---

**Headline:** The best destination is the one that actually empties the box.
