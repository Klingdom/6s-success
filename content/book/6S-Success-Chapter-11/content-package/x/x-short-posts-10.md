# X Short Posts: What to Donate, Sell, Store, Recycle, or Throw Away

Chapter 11 of *6S Success: Home Edition*. Ten standalone posts. Each is at or under 280 characters and makes sense on its own. Soft CTA on a few, not all.

---

1/
The out box is full and every thing in it is already decided. Today it only needs routing, not more hard calls. Chapter 11 of 6S Success: Home Edition sends each thing out through one of five doors and gets the whole box gone in one trip. Free online.

(approx. 248 chars)

---

2/
Five doors out: Donate, Sell, Store, Recycle, Throw. A quick cascade picks the door for each thing. Broken goes to Recycle or Throw. Valuable and worth the work goes to Sell. Needed later goes to Store. Still useful goes to Donate, and most of the box goes there.

(approx. 259 chars)

---

3/
"Donate is the default. Most usable things you do not need should go to someone who does, today, not to a someday sale that keeps them in your house for months."

It is the one exit that finishes the same day. No listing, no buyer, no perfect recipient to wait for.

(approx. 264 chars)

---

4/
Giving a usable thing away is not waste. The thing is not destroyed; it goes to someone who wants it, which is the opposite of waste. You are moving value to where it will finally get used, and getting your space back in the bargain. Both happen now, not eventually.

(approx. 262 chars)

---

5/
"Selling turns clearing into a part-time job. Sell only what is genuinely worth the work, put a deadline on it, and donate whatever has not sold by the date."

The deadline keeps a sale from quietly becoming permanent storage with a price tag on it.

(approx. 247 chars)

---

6/
"Storing is not deciding. Store only what you will truly use later, label it and date it, and never rent space to keep what you do not need."

A rented locker of things you already decided you do not use is just the clutter you cleared, moved offsite and billed monthly.

(approx. 267 chars)

---

7/
"Some things are simply worn out, and throwing them away is not the waste. The waste, if there ever was any, happened at the store. Recycle what you can, bin the rest without guilt, and be done."

The money was spent long ago. Keeping the ruined thing brings none of it back.

(approx. 275 chars)

---

8/
The someday sale and the just-in-case storage both feel responsible. Both are ways of not finishing, because they keep the clutter in your life while you tell yourself you dealt with it. The fast doors are fast because they ask nothing of the future.

(approx. 247 chars)

---

9/
The normal out box splits between two doors. Umbrellas and tote bags to Donate. Menus, a dead cable, a useless old key to Recycle. Nothing worth selling, nothing worth storing, and the whole box out in one trip. That is why Donate and Recycle are the lanes to lean on.

(approx. 266 chars)

---

10/
One move today: put the donation bag in your car now, not by the door. A bag by the door gets unpacked; a bag in the car gets dropped off. Grab three usable things, drop the broken one at recycling or the bin, and Sort is one trip closer to done. Read the chapter free online.

(approx. 274 chars)
