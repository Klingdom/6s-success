Last issue you tagged the honest maybes and set them on their timers. What is left in the corner is the out box, full and settled, every item already decided. This chapter routes it. Every unnecessary thing leaves through one of five doors, Donate, Sell, Store, Recycle, or Throw, and a quick cascade picks each one in a second. Donate is the default, and Recycle is the second fast lane, so those two carry most of the box; Sell and Store are the slow doors, fenced with a deadline and a label. Then the box leaves the house in a single trip, the friction meter steps to Sort complete, and the first S is done.
