# Quote Card Copy: Chapter 11

**"What to Donate, Sell, Store, Recycle, or Throw Away"**
*6S Success: Home Edition · Part Three · Sort*

Ten quote cards, each pulled from `chapter_11_manuscript.md`. Every quoted line below was
grep-verified as present in the manuscript. Cards 1 through 10 are **verbatim** (byte-identical
to the manuscript). The alternates at the end are **verbatim** as well, each a complete
sentence lifted whole. Nothing here is invented, paraphrased, or trimmed.

Each card uses the warm book palette (paper `#F7F2E9`, panel `#FBF7EF`, ink `#2B2622`) and the
Fraunces display face for the quote, Newsreader italic for the attribution. No em dashes, no en
dashes, no floating hyphen separators; labels use the middot.

Suggested attribution for every card: **6S Success: Home Edition · Chapter 11**

### Card 1 (verbatim)
> "You are not here to decide anything today. You are here to route."

- Attribution: 6S Success: Home Edition · Chapter 11
- Design: The anchor card. Large Fraunces on paper, small terracotta `THE CORE INSIGHT` eyebrow. Give it the most room in the set. The line that names the whole chapter's move.

### Card 2 (verbatim)
> "Donate is the default. Most usable things you do not need should go to someone who does, today, not to a someday sale that keeps them in your house for months."

- Attribution: 6S Success: Home Edition · Chapter 11
- Design: The default-door card. Green quote on panel, small green `THE FAST LANE` eyebrow, a small green calm-dot in the corner. The chapter's central rule; give it width.

### Card 3 (verbatim)
> "You are not throwing value away. You are moving it to where it will finally get used, and taking your space back in the bargain."

- Attribution: 6S Success: Home Edition · Chapter 11
- Design: The anti-guilt reframe. Ink Fraunces on paper, generous space, small terracotta `DONATE WITHOUT GUILT` eyebrow. Best on landscape; let it breathe.

### Card 4 (verbatim)
> "Selling turns clearing into a part-time job. Sell only what is genuinely worth the work, put a deadline on it, and donate whatever has not sold by the date."

- Attribution: 6S Success: Home Edition · Chapter 11
- Design: The sell-trap card. Slate quote on panel, small slate `THE SLOW DOOR` eyebrow. Dry and plain; let the sentence land. Longer, give it width.

### Card 5 (verbatim)
> "The deadline is what keeps a sale from quietly becoming permanent storage with a price tag on it."

- Attribution: 6S Success: Home Edition · Chapter 11
- Design: The sell-by card. Ink Fraunces on paper, a faint dated-tag mark behind the text. Eyebrow `A SELL-BY DEADLINE`. Set the closing phrase to breathe.

### Card 6 (verbatim)
> "Storing is not deciding. Store only what you will truly use later, label it and date it, and never rent space to keep what you do not need."

- Attribution: 6S Success: Home Edition · Chapter 11
- Design: The store-trap card. Slate on panel, small slate `THE OTHER SLOW DOOR` eyebrow, a faint labeled-box glyph. Let the first three words carry the weight.

### Card 7 (verbatim)
> "Some things are simply worn out, and throwing them away is not the waste. The waste, if there ever was any, happened at the store."

- Attribution: 6S Success: Home Edition · Chapter 11
- Design: The throw reframe. Ink Fraunces on paper, generous space, small terracotta `NO GUILT AT THE BIN` eyebrow. The sunk-cost callback; best on landscape.

### Card 8 (verbatim)
> "There is no virtue in living among broken objects."

- Attribution: 6S Success: Home Edition · Chapter 11
- Design: The permission card. Short line set large and centered on paper, plenty of air, no accent color. The plainest line in the chapter; let it stand alone.

### Card 9 (verbatim)
> "Deciding a thing is unnecessary and deciding where it should go are two separate acts, and you already finished the first."

- Attribution: 6S Success: Home Edition · Chapter 11
- Design: The two-acts card. Slate quote on panel, calm rhythm. Eyebrow `DECIDED, THEN ROUTED`. Longer, give it width.

### Card 10 (verbatim)
> "The best destination is the one that actually empties the box."

- Attribution: 6S Success: Home Edition · Chapter 11
- Design: The closing card, from One Idea to Keep. Ink on paper, warm and confident, plenty of air. Pair it with a tiny row of five small door glyphs, the widest one green. Eyebrow `ONE IDEA TO KEEP`.
