# Open Graph and Twitter Card Copy: Chapter 11, What to Donate, Sell, Store, Recycle, or Throw Away

Two variants. Use the og:image concept noted under each.

## Variant A (the five doors hook)

- og:title: Five Doors Out, and Most of the Box Goes Through Two
- og:description: Every unnecessary thing leaves through one of five doors, Donate, Sell, Store, Recycle, or Throw. A quick question picks the door, and most of the box goes through Donate.
- twitter:title: Five Doors Out, and Most of the Box Goes Through Two
- twitter:description: You are not here to decide anything today. You are here to route. Donate and Recycle are the fast lanes; Sell and Store are the slow doors, worth it only for the rare item that earns them. From Chapter 11 of 6S Success: Home Edition.
- og:image concept: The five doors, at a glance. The out box on the floor by the front door, a soft stream of items flowing toward five labeled doors set in the wall. Most of the stream pours through a wide green door marked DONATE, and a good share turns into a door marked RECYCLE; the narrow SELL and STORE doors sit almost closed, and THROW takes only what is clearly finished. A donation bag with keys waits by the door. Caption line: Donate takes the usable things, Recycle takes the worn ones, and the box leaves in a single trip.

## Variant B (Donate is the default)

- og:title: Donate Is the Default
- og:description: Most usable things you do not need should go to someone who does, today, not to a someday sale that keeps them in your house for months. Donating is the exit that actually completes.
- twitter:title: Donate Is the Default
- twitter:description: You are not throwing value away. You are moving it to where it will finally get used, and taking your space back in the bargain. From Chapter 11 of 6S Success: Home Edition.
- og:image concept: The default door. A wide, welcoming green door marked DONATE stands open, a donation bag in front of it ready for the car, while two narrow side doors marked SELL and STORE sit nearly closed and labeled "slow." A single line reads "still useful to someone, today." Footer: Choose the door that gets it out of the house.
