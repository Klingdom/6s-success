# Read Next Recommendations: Chapter 11, What to Donate, Sell, Store, Recycle, or Throw Away

1. Chapter 12, Straighten: A Place for Everything
   The next step, and the start of Part Four. Sort is done, and what does not belong and what you do not need are both gone. Only the keepers remain, sitting where they landed. Part Four stops removing and starts arranging, giving every keeper a proper place so the space you cleared stays easy to keep.

2. Chapter 10, Red Tags, Holding Areas, and Sorter's Remorse
   The setup. Before this chapter could run, you gave the honest maybes a red tag and a decide-by date and set them in a holding area, and you quieted the second thoughts. That left the out box, full and settled, as the last loose end. This chapter picks it up and routes it.

3. Get three things out today (the chapter action)
   Grab three things from the out box and get them out of the house today. The usable ones into a donation bag in your car; the broken one to recycling or the bin. Three things gone for good in five minutes.
