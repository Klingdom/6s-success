# Chapter 41 Image Generation Prompts: The Primary Bathroom

Warm, illustrated, flat-color editorial house style (paper #F7F2E9 ground, ink #2B2622 line, terracotta/honey/slate/green accents, Shine green #6E8B5B). No photorealism, no brand names or logos, no text baked into the art beyond the noted labels. Product types only.

## Prompt 1 (opener, two states)
A warm flat-illustration of one primary bathroom shown twice, side by side. Left, labelled "before": a counter crowded with bottles and a heat tool, a shower with a dark seam at the tray, a hazed mirror, a cabinet of expired boxes, cleaners at floor level behind an open door. Right, labelled "after": a clear counter with one tray, filmless glass and mirror, a shower that dries out with no dark seam, a bowl clean under the rim, and chemistry locked up high.

## Prompt 2 (seven zones plan)
A warm overhead floor plan of a primary bathroom, gently shaded, with seven numbered and labelled zones: vanity counter, vanity drawers, under-sink cabinet, medicine cabinet, shower or tub, toilet area, linen storage. Caption strip at the foot: "one room, seven small jobs, spray the wet zones first".

## Prompt 3 (method and kit, two panels)
A two-panel warm infographic. Left "the method": a spray bottle misting a shower wall with a hand walking away, a squeegee on glass beside a bath mat standing on edge and a running fan, an expired tube dropping into a bin, and one bottle per toilet bowl with a crossed-out mixing of two cleaners and a lock high on a cabinet. Right "the kit": labelled type icons for a bathroom grime and soap-film cleaner, a neutral pH cleaner, a streak-free glass cleaner, a floor cleaner, a microfiber set with one toilet-only colour, a detail brush, a shower squeegee, a shower caddy, a lockable medicine box, a leak tray, a moisture absorber, and anti-tip anchors.

## Prompt 4 (shower step ribbon)
A warm step-ribbon titled "how to clean the shower": spray the walls and seams, work the showerhead scale, wipe top down and squeegee, scrub the taps and buff dry, wash the mat and stand it on edge, clear the drain, hang the squeegee. The spray-first and mat-on-edge steps highlighted in the Shine green.

## Prompt 5 (before-and-after signature)
A warm matched pair of the primary bathroom, the counter and mirror and shower, shot from the doorway, before on the left and after on the right, with a small camera icon and the labels "the before, kept" and "the after, kept as the standard".
