# Chapter 41 Diagram Ideas: The Primary Bathroom

House palette (paper #F7F2E9, panel #FBF7EF, ink #2B2622, terracotta #BC4B2A, honey #DDA63A, slate #3C5A6B, green #6E8B5B for Shine). Warm, illustrated, not photoreal. Product types only, no brands or logos. Spark red reserved only for a true hazard (a heat tool cord by a full basin, two cleaners being mixed, crossed out).

## Diagram 1 (opener, figure 1): two states
The primary bathroom before and after, side by side. Before: a counter crowded with bottles and a heat tool left out, a shower with a dark line where the wall meets the tray, a hazed mirror, a medicine cabinet of expired boxes, cleaners at floor level behind an open door. After: a clear counter, filmless glass and mirror, a shower that dries out with no dark seam, a bowl clean under the rim, and the chemistry and medicines locked safely up out of reach.

## Diagram 2 (figure 2): the seven zones plan
A warm overhead plan, seven numbered zones marked and named: vanity counter, vanity drawers, under-sink cabinet, medicine cabinet, shower or tub, toilet area, linen storage. Footer: "one room, seven small jobs, spray the wet zones first".

## Diagram 3 (figure 3): the Shine method and kit
Two panels. Left, the method: spray first (a spray bottle misting a wall, a clock ticking, a hand walking away); dry beats mould (a squeegee on glass, a mat on edge, a fan running); read the date (an expired tube into the bin); chemistry kept safe (one bottle per bowl, a crossed-out mixing of two cleaners, a lock high on a cabinet). Right, the kit: a bathroom grime and soap-film cleaner, a neutral pH cleaner, a streak-free glass cleaner, a floor cleaner, a microfiber set with a flagged toilet-only colour, a detail brush, a shower squeegee, a shower caddy, a lockable medicine box, a leak tray, a moisture absorber, and anti-tip anchors. Footer: "spray first, dry beats mould, read the date".

## Diagram 4 (no HTML twin): the bathroom Shine method
A standalone visual: spray first, dry beats mould, read the date, chemistry kept safe. Names the next chapter, the guest bathroom.

## Diagram 5 (no HTML twin): how to clean the shower
A step ribbon: spray the walls and seams, work the showerhead scale, wipe top down and squeegee, scrub the taps and buff dry, wash the mat and stand it on edge, clear the drain, hang the squeegee. Highlight the spray-first and mat-on-edge steps.

## Diagram 6 (before/after signature) and the photo brief
The Part 9 signature: a matched before-and-after pair of the counter, mirror, and shower, from the doorway. **Best before-and-after photo brief:** stand in the doorway and frame the counter, the mirror, and the shower together in one shot; same spot, angle, and light, before and after; do not tidy before the before (Ch 6); keep the after as the ideal-state standard (Ch 25) the room resets to. The retired friction meter is not drawn.
