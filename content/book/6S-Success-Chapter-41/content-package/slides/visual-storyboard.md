# Chapter 41 Visual Storyboard: The Primary Bathroom

## Design system
House palette: paper #F7F2E9, panel #FBF7EF, ink #2B2622, terracotta #BC4B2A, honey #DDA63A, slate #3C5A6B, green #6E8B5B (Shine). Fraunces display, Newsreader body, Inter labels. Footer strip: SPRAY FIRST, DRY BEATS MOULD, READ THE DATE.

## Recurring motif
The "spray and dwell" cue: a spray bottle misting a shower wall, then a hand walking away, then the same wall wiping clean in one pass. It ties the room's lesson together and reappears on the method slide.

## Two fences
- Never depict two cleaners being mixed, and never an acidic spray on stone; never medicines flushed down the toilet.
- Product types only, no brand names or real logos.

## Beats (twelve)
1. Title over the two-state opener.
2. The first wet room; the enemy is water.
3. Spray first: the mist, then walking away.
4. The seven zones overhead plan.
5. The definition: a bathroom you could hand a guest.
6. The method and kit two-panel (figure 3).
7. Dry beats mould: the squeegee, the mat on edge, the fan.
8. Read the date: an expired tube going into the bin.
9. Colour-code and one-product-per-bowl.
10. Reading the plumbing under the sink.
11. The before-and-after room signature.
12. Close: one idea to keep, and the Chapter 42 hand-off.

## Carrying it across the set
Every asset closes on the before-and-after room signature from the doorway (the clear counter, the filmless glass, the dry shower). The friction meter is retired for Part 9. Register warm, practical, clear about chemistry. Previous Ch 40, next Ch 42 The Guest Bathroom.
