# Chapter 41 Teaching Slide Deck Outline: The Primary Bathroom

Fifteen slides. House palette; Shine green #6E8B5B. Footer strip: SPRAY FIRST, DRY BEATS MOULD, READ THE DATE. Product types only.

1. Title: The Primary Bathroom. Part Nine, Room-by-Room Playbooks. The first wet room.
2. The problem: the enemy is no longer clutter, it is water, which does not wait.
3. What you will learn: the five outcomes.
4. The rule: three problems at once; the method changes for water.
5. Seven zones, not one room: with the overhead plan figure. Start at the counter, spray the wet zones first.
6. The definition: a bathroom you could hand a guest at an hour's notice.
7. The method (its own slide): spray first, dry beats mould, read the date, chemistry kept safe. Figure 3, method and kit.
8. Spray first: spray the wet zones, clean the dry ones while they dwell, squeegee last.
9. Dry beats mould: mould is a water problem; squeegee, drain the mat, run the fan.
10. Read the date: expired is not stock; medicines to a pharmacy take-back.
11. The zones: counter, drawers, under-sink, medicine cabinet, shower, toilet, linen; the key move for each.
12. Colour-code and one-product-per-bowl: the toilet cloth alone; never mix bowl cleaner and bleach.
13. Read the plumbing: keep a drip tray and the trap and valves visible under the sink.
14. The signature: the before-and-after room card. (Friction meter retired.)
15. Close and CTA: one idea to keep. Do one zone this weekend. Next: the guest bathroom.
