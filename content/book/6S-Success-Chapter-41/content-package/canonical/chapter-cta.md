# Chapter 41 CTA and Companion Resources: The Primary Bathroom

## Primary call to action
Do one zone this weekend, the counter or the shower, and give yourself the smallest room you use the most, actually clean and actually safe.

## Companion resources
- **The Bathroom Reset card** (printable and phone wallpaper: the seven zones, the done-looks-like for each, and the spray-first, dry-beats-mould, read-the-date order of work).
- **The Bathroom Kit list** (printable: the cleaners, tools, and storage types the room needs, grouped by job, with the neutral-pH-on-stone, colour-code-the-toilet-cloth, and lock-the-chemistry notes that matter here).
- **The Spray-First Routine card** (the fast weekly pass: spray the wet zones, clean the dry ones while they dwell, squeegee the glass, run the fan).

## Register and fences
- Warm, practical, expert, and clear about chemistry and safety. Product types only, never brand names. No hype, no invented statistics or prices. Never advise mixing cleaners.
- Friction meter retired for Part 9; close on the before-and-after room signature.

## Words to avoid
- No brand names (product types only). Never suggest mixing bowl cleaner with bleach. Never suggest an acidic spray on stone, or flushing medicines down the toilet.
