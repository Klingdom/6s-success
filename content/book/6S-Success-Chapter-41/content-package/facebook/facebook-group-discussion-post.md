# Facebook Group Discussion Post: The Primary Bathroom

For a home or cleaning group. A useful tip, an open question, a soft close.

A bathroom tip that saves you an hour of scrubbing.

Stop scrubbing a dry shower. The wet-room way is to spray the walls, the tray, and the bowl first, then walk away and clean the counter and cabinets while the cleaner works. Chemistry needs time, not muscle, so when you come back the soap film wipes off in one pass instead of ten.

Two more that change everything. First, drying beats scrubbing: mould is a water problem, so squeegee the glass, stand the bath mat on edge to drain, and run the fan, because a surface that dries between uses does not grow mould. Second, read the date on everything, because an expired cream or medicine is a failure on your shelf, not stock, and medicines go to a pharmacy take-back, never the toilet.

And the safety one that matters most: one product per bowl, never mixed, because bowl cleaner and bleach together make a gas.

Do you spray and wait, or scrub and lose the afternoon?
