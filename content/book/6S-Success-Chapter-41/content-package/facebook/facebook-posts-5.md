# Chapter 41 Facebook Posts: The Primary Bathroom

Five short standalone posts.

## 1. Spray first, then walk away
Stop scrubbing a dry shower. Spray the walls, the tray, and the bowl first, then clean the counter while the cleaner works. Chemistry needs time, not muscle, so when you come back the soap film wipes off in one pass instead of ten.

## 2. Drying beats scrubbing
Mould and the pink film are a water problem, not a dirt problem. So the winning move is drying, not cleaning. Squeegee the glass, stand the bath mat on edge to drain, buff the metal dry, and run the fan. A surface that dries between uses does not grow mould.

## 3. Read the date on everything
An expired cream, cosmetic, or medicine is not a supply you are saving, it is a failure already on your shelf. Read the date on every Sort pass and let what has turned go. Medicines go to a pharmacy take-back, never down the toilet.

## 4. One product per bowl, never mixed
The most dangerous mistake in a bathroom is chemistry. Keep one product per bowl and never mix them, because bowl cleaner and bleach together make a gas. And store the bleach and the medicines high, above the height a child reaches by climbing the closed toilet lid.

## 5. Clean under the sink to read the pipes
The under-sink cabinet is the one you clean partly to read the plumbing. Keep a pale drip tray and the trap and valves in plain sight, because a joint that weeps unseen for months turns a cheap washer into a floor that has to come up. Chapter 41. Next: the guest bathroom.
