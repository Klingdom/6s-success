# Chapter 41 Teleprompter Script: The Primary Bathroom

Short lines, one breath each. Warm and practical.

Your bathroom films over by Friday for one reason.
You are fighting water. And water does not wait.
This is the first wet room.
So the method changes.
Spray first. Then walk away.
Spray the tub, the shower walls, and the bowl.
And leave them.
Clean the counter and the cabinets while the cleaner works.
Come back, and it wipes off in one pass instead of ten.
Chemistry needs time. Not scrubbing.
Then the real job. Drying.
Mould is a water problem, not a dirt problem.
A surface that dries out does not grow mould.
Squeegee the glass. Drain the mat on edge.
Buff the metal dry. Run the fan.
Then read the date. On everything.
The cream. The cosmetic. The medicine.
Expired is not stock. It is a failure on your shelf.
Medicines go to a pharmacy take-back. Never the toilet.
And keep the chemistry safe.
One product per bowl. Never mixed.
Locked or high, above what a child can climb to.
Do one zone this weekend. The counter or the shower.
And give yourself a bathroom that stays clean and dry.
