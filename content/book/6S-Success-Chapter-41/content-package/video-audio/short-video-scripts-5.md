# Chapter 41 Short Video Scripts (5): The Primary Bathroom

Each 30 to 90 seconds. Product types only.

## 1. Spray first, then walk away (40s)
HOOK: Stop scrubbing a dry shower.
SCRIPT: The wet-room way is to spray the walls, the tray, and the bowl first, then walk away and clean the counter while the cleaner works. Chemistry needs time, not muscle, so when you come back the soap film wipes off in one pass instead of ten. Squeegee the glass last and leave the fan running.
ON-SCREEN: Spray first, then walk away.
CTA: Chapter 41. Try the spray-first method this weekend.

## 2. Drying beats scrubbing (35s)
HOOK: Mould keeps coming back because you are cleaning it, not drying it.
SCRIPT: Mould and the pink film are a water problem, not a dirt problem. A surface that dries out between uses does not grow mould. So squeegee the glass, stand the bath mat on edge to drain, buff the metal dry, and run the fan.
CTA: Chapter 41, the shower.

## 3. Read the date on everything (30s)
HOOK: Your bathroom shelf is full of failures.
SCRIPT: The separated sunscreen, the crumbling tablet, the mascara from two summers ago. An expired thing is not stock, it is a failure already on your shelf. Read the date on every Sort pass, and take medicines to a pharmacy take-back, never down the toilet.
CTA: Chapter 41, the medicine cabinet.

## 4. One product per bowl, never mixed (30s)
HOOK: Never do this with bathroom cleaners.
SCRIPT: Never mix them. Bowl cleaner and bleach together make a gas. Keep one product per bowl, and store the chemistry and the medicines locked or high, above the height a child reaches by climbing the closed toilet lid.
CTA: Chapter 41. Check your cleaners today.

## 5. Clean under the sink to read the pipes (35s)
HOOK: The most useful thing you will clean is the cabinet under the sink.
SCRIPT: You clean it partly to read the plumbing. Keep a pale drip tray and the trap and valves in plain sight, and run a dry finger around each joint. A joint that weeps unseen for months turns a cheap washer into a floor that has to come up.
CTA: Chapter 41. Next: the guest bathroom.
