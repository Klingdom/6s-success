# Chapter 41 YouTube Script (6 to 8 min): The Primary Bathroom

Alternating HOST and VISUAL. Product types only.

HOST: If your bathroom films over again by Friday no matter how hard you scrub, you are fighting the wrong way. Today we clean the first wet room the way it actually needs it.
VISUAL: Two-state opener, crowded steamy before and clear dry after. A wall misted, a hand walking away.

HOST: This room fights three problems at once: standing moisture, open chemistry, and dates that pass. So three rules run through it: spray first, dry beats mould, and read the date.
VISUAL: The shower sprayed and left; a squeegee on glass; an expired tube into a bin.

HOST: Seven zones. Start at the counter, and spray the wet zones the moment you walk in. Then clean the counter while they dwell: mirror first, the faucet collar and switch plate nobody wipes, and the stone dried off.
VISUAL: Spraying the shower; wiping the mirror; the faucet collar scrubbed.

HOST: Come back to the shower once the chemistry has done the hard part. Wipe top down, squeegee the glass, wash the mat, and stand it on edge to drain. A surface that dries out does not grow mould.
VISUAL: The top-down wipe; the squeegee; the mat on edge.

HOST: Clean the under-sink cabinet partly to read the pipes: a dry finger around each joint, a pale drip tray, the trap and valves kept visible. And the medicine cabinet: shelves out, sticky rings off, and every date read.
VISUAL: A finger around a joint; a drip tray; a date read on a tube.

HOST: The toilet gets its own cloth colour, that job only. Treat the bowl and let it dwell, then work the outside cleanest to dirtiest. And never mix bowl cleaner with bleach.
VISUAL: The toilet-only cloth; the bowl treated; one bottle per bowl.

HOST: Keep the chemistry and medicines locked or high, above the height a child reaches by climbing the closed toilet lid. Do one zone this weekend. Next: the guest bathroom.
VISUAL: A lock on a cabinet; the before-and-after room signature from the doorway. End card.
