# Chapter 41 Landing Page Intro: The Primary Bathroom

The primary bathroom is the smallest room in the house and the one you use the most, and it is where this book crosses from the dry rooms into the wet ones. Everything you have cleaned so far fought dust and clutter, problems that sit still and wait for you. This room fights water, which does not wait.

This chapter breaks the primary bathroom into seven small zones and gives you, for each one, the exact inputs to gather and the numbered steps to clean and shine it: the vanity counter, the vanity drawers, the under-sink cabinet, the medicine cabinet, the shower or tub, the toilet area, and the linen and towel storage. It shows you why the wet-room method starts with spraying and waiting rather than scrubbing, why drying beats mould more surely than any amount of effort, and why you read the date on every product as you go, because in this room an expired thing is a hazard, not a spare.

Product types only, no brands. Start at the counter, the surface you look at twice a day, and spray the wet zones first so the chemistry works while you clean the dry ones.
