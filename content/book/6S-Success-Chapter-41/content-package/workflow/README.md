## The chapter in one line
The primary bathroom is the first wet room, so it fights three problems at once, standing moisture, open chemistry, and dates that pass, and the method changes: spray first and let the chemistry work, treat drying as the real job, and read the date on everything.

## The seven zones
The vanity counter, the vanity drawers, the under-sink cabinet, the medicine cabinet, the shower or tub, the toilet area, and the linen and towel storage. Each carries an Inputs list, numbered Steps, and an inspect-and-flag list.

## The bathroom Shine layer (nine rules from Chapter 31 plus)
- Spray first, dwell, do not scrub: spray the wet zones and clean the dry ones while the cleaner works; squeegee last, fan on.
- Dry beats mould: mould is a water problem, so drying is the real job; a surface that dries between uses does not grow it.
- Read the date, expired is not stock: an expired thing is a failure on your shelf; medicines to a pharmacy take-back.
- Open chemistry kept safe: one product per bowl and never mixed; cleaners and medicines locked or high; keep the plumbing visible.
