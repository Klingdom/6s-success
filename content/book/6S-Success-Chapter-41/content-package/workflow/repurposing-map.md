# Chapter 41 Repurposing Map: The Primary Bathroom

## Spine (the how-to-clean value, in priority order)
1. The wet-room Shine method: spray first, dry beats mould, read the date, chemistry kept safe.
2. The zone-by-zone centerpiece: the exact inputs and numbered steps for the counter, drawers, under-sink, medicine cabinet, shower, toilet, and linen.
3. The spray-and-dwell order and the drying habits that keep a bathroom clean between deep cleans.
4. The calls that matter: the tool that lives out, the expensive shade, the pipe you cannot see, the leftover prescription, the good towels you never use.

## Reuse cautions
- Types, not brands, everywhere. No brand names or invented prices. Never advise mixing cleaners or an acidic spray on stone.
- Never depict two cleaners being mixed, or medicines flushed down the toilet.
- Every asset closes on the before-and-after signature (friction meter retired for Part 9).
- Stay in the primary bathroom: do not reuse nursery (Ch 40) or guest-bathroom (Ch 42) material.
