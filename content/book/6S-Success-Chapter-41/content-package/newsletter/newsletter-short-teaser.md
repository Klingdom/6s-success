**The Primary Bathroom: spray first, dry beats mould, read the date.**

The bathroom is the smallest room you use the most, and it is the first wet room, so the enemy is no longer clutter, it is water. That changes the whole job.

Chapter 41 gives the exact inputs and numbered steps for each of the room's seven zones, plus the three rules that run through all of them: spray the wet surfaces first and let the chemistry work, treat drying as the real job because mould is a water problem, and read the date on everything, because an expired thing here is a hazard, not a spare.

[Read the chapter] and give yourself a bathroom that stays clean and dry.
