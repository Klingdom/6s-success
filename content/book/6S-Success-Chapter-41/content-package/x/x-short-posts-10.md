# X Short Posts: The Primary Bathroom

Chapter 41 of *6S Success: Home Edition*. Ten standalone posts, each at or under 280 characters counting its number line. Plain numbering.

---

1/
The primary bathroom is the first wet room, so the enemy is no longer clutter, it is water. Spray the tub, the walls, and the bowl first, then clean the counter while the cleaner works. Chemistry needs time, not muscle.

(222 chars)

---

2/
Stop scrubbing a dry shower. Spray it and walk away, and let the cleaner break down the soap film while you clean the counter. When you come back it wipes off in one pass instead of ten. Squeegee the glass last, fan on.

(222 chars)

---

3/
Mould keeps coming back because you are cleaning it, not drying it. Mould is a water problem. A surface that dries out between uses does not grow it. Squeegee the glass, drain the mat on edge, buff the metal dry, run the fan.

(228 chars)

---

4/
Every product in a bathroom has a date. An expired cream, cosmetic, or medicine is not stock, it is a failure already on your shelf. Read the date on every Sort pass, and take medicines to a pharmacy take-back, never the toilet.

(231 chars)

---

5/
Never mix bathroom cleaners. Bowl cleaner and bleach together make a gas. Keep one product per bowl, and store the chemistry and medicines locked or high, above the height a child reaches by climbing the closed toilet lid.

(225 chars)

---

6/
The cloth that cleans your toilet should have its own colour and touch nothing else. It is the simplest way to keep the dirtiest patch of the room from contaminating the cleanest.

(182 chars)

---

7/
Clean the cabinet under the sink partly to read the pipes. Keep a pale drip tray and the trap and valves in plain sight, and run a dry finger around each joint. It tells you about a seep before a puddle ever will.

(216 chars)

---

8/
The single most common thing in a medicine cabinet that should not be there is the leftover half-course of antibiotics. A medicine belongs to a condition someone has now. The rest goes to a pharmacy take-back, and it goes first.

(231 chars)

---

9/
Every flush throws a fine mist across the cistern lid. Settle it with one line: nothing that touches your face, your mouth, or your food goes within arm's reach of the flush. The toothbrush lives on the counter, not the tank.

(228 chars)

---

10/
A bathroom is not clean because you scrubbed it hard. It is clean because you sprayed first, dried the surfaces so mould cannot return, and read the date on everything. Chapter 41. Next . Ch 42, The Guest Bathroom.
