# Chapter 41 Ebook Description: The Primary Bathroom

The eleventh room playbook of Part Nine, and the first of the wet rooms. The primary bathroom is the smallest room you use the most, and it fights three problems no other room faces all at once: standing moisture that breeds mould, open chemistry that has to be kept safe, and dates that quietly pass while you sleep.

This chapter gives the exact inputs and the numbered steps for each of the room's seven zones, with three rules running through all of them: spray the wet surfaces first and let the cleaner work while you do the dry ones (chemistry needs time, not scrubbing), treat drying as the real job (mould is a water problem, and a surface that dries between uses does not grow it), and read the date on everything (an expired cream, cosmetic, or medicine is a failure on your shelf, not a spare, and medicines go to a pharmacy take-back). Around all of it, the safety layer keeps cleaners, medicines, water, and electricity apart: one product per bowl and never mixed, chemistry locked or high above a child's climbing reach, and the plumbing kept visible.

You will learn the spray-and-dwell order that cleans a shower in one pass, the drying habits that stop mould, and how to read the plumbing while you clean the under-sink cabinet. The chapter closes on a before-and-after room signature.
