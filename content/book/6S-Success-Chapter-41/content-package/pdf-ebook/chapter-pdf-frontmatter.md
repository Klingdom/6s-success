# The Primary Bathroom

*The primary bathroom is the smallest room you use the most and the first wet room in this book, so cleaning it is not about scrubbing harder, it is about fighting the three problems no other room has all at once, standing moisture, open chemistry, and dates that quietly pass, with a method built for water. This playbook points the whole loop at it one zone at a time, and gives you, for each of the seven zones, the exact inputs to gather and the precise, step-by-step way to clean and shine it, so you spray first, dry properly, and read every date.*

## How to read this chapter

This is a PLAYBOOK, not a method chapter. It applies the six-S loop you already own to one room, decomposed into seven micro-zones, and its centerpiece is the per-zone clean-and-shine instructions with the exact inputs. It recaps but does not re-teach the nine-rule Shine method from Chapter 31; the new material is the wet-room layer (spray first and let the chemistry work; treat drying as the real job; read the date on everything) and the numbered steps for each zone.

Products are named as TYPES, never brands. The exhaustive product list is a separate appendix. Never mix cleaning products; one product per bowl.

The friction meter, which reached its goal at Chapter 30, is retired for Part 9; the chapter closes on a before-and-after room signature.
