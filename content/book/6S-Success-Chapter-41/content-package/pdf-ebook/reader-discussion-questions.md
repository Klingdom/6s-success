# Chapter 41 Reader Discussion Questions: The Primary Bathroom

1. Do you spray the shower and walk away, or scrub it dry? Which keeps it clean longer for you?
2. When did you last read the date on the creams and medicines in your bathroom?
3. Does your toilet cloth have its own colour and touch nothing else?
4. Can you see the trap and shut-off valves under your sink, or are they buried behind stock?
5. What is the oldest thing in your medicine cabinet, and why is it still there?
6. How do you dry your shower, and how fast does the pink film come back?
7. Is anything that touches your face or mouth kept within arm's reach of the flush?
8. Are the bleach and the medicines stored above the height a child reaches by climbing the toilet lid?
9. How many towels does your house actually need, and how many are you storing?
10. Which zone will you do first this weekend, the counter or the shower?
