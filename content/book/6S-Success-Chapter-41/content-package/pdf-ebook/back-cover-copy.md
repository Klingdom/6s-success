# Chapter 41 Back Cover Copy: The Primary Bathroom

## Back-cover text
The primary bathroom is the smallest room you use the most, and it is the first wet room, so scrubbing harder is the wrong move. The enemy is water, and water does not wait.

The Primary Bathroom breaks the room into seven zones and gives you, for each, the exact inputs to gather and the numbered steps to follow. Spray the wet surfaces first and let the chemistry work. Treat drying as the real job, because mould is a water problem. Read the date on everything, because an expired thing here is a hazard, not a spare. And keep the chemistry safe: one product per bowl, locked high, the plumbing visible.

Do one zone this weekend, the counter or the shower, and give yourself a bathroom that stays clean and dry.
