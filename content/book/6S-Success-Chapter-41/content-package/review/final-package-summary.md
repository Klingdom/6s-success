## Source fidelity
The seven zones, their inputs, their step order, and every inspect-and-flag list are folded directly from the Micro Zone Manual's Primary Bathroom content (seven zones, each with shine_detail: surfaces, methods, products_used, inspect_as_you_clean) and the room's 38 mapped product types (introducing the wet-room hero input, the bathroom grime and soap-film cleaner). The reset instructions were carried through faithfully: the top-to-bottom counter with the faucet collar and switch plate, the lift-the-dividers drawers, the read-the-plumbing under-sink cabinet, the shelves-out medicine cabinet, the spray-and-dwell shower with the mat drained on edge, the cleanest-to-dirtiest toilet with a colour-coded cloth, and the press-the-back-wall-for-damp linen shelf. The intro's three-problems framing and the spray-first cleaning note are the source of the chapter's layer, and the calls (the tool that lives out, the expensive shade, the pipe you cannot see, the leftover prescription, what you keep within splash range, the good towels you never use) come straight from the Manual's the_call entries.
