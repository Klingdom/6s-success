# Chapter 41 Editorial Review: The Primary Bathroom

## Verdict
Ready for Phil's review as a draft. The chapter meets the instruction-centric standard: every one of the seven zones carries an Inputs list, numbered Steps, and an inspect-and-flag paragraph, and the wet-room Shine layer is genuinely new, marking the book's crossover from dry rooms to wet ones.

## What works
- **The layer is the right one for the crossover, and it is distinct.** Spray-first, dry-beats-mould, and read-the-date are all specific to a wet room, and none of them repeats any bedroom layer. Framing the chapter as the moment the enemy changes from clutter (which waits) to water (which does not) is a strong, honest pivot that sets up the rest of the wet and utility rooms.
- **The counter-intuitive method is well argued.** The chapter makes the case that spraying and waiting beats scrubbing, and that drying beats cleaning, with concrete mechanics (one pass instead of ten; a surface that dries between uses does not grow mould). This is the most useful single idea in the chapter and it lands.
- **The Manual reset instructions are folded in faithfully.** The read-the-plumbing under-sink pass, the shelves-out medicine cabinet, the spray-and-dwell shower, and the cleanest-to-dirtiest toilet are carried straight from the source shine_detail and elevated into numbered procedures.
- **The safety and chemistry material is handled responsibly.** The one-product-per-bowl, never-mix rule, the measure-from-what-a-child-can-climb height rule, and the pharmacy-take-back for medicines are all correct and clearly stated, and the chapter never advises mixing anything.

## Watch items (minor, non-blocking)
- The chapter is the longest room playbook in the book so far (about 6,727 words), because it carries seven zones plus a heavy calls section; the length cap is lifted for Part 9, so this is acceptable, and the Calls section is the easiest trim if Phil wants tighter.
- Because this is the first of two bathrooms, the guest bathroom (Ch 42) must clearly differentiate on the rarely-used, guest-ready angle rather than re-explaining the wet-room method; the hand-off line sets that up.

## Consistency checks
- Nine Shine rules recapped, not re-taught, with dwell and colour-coding foregrounded: PASS.
- Friction meter retired; before-and-after signature closes: PASS.
- Product types only: PASS.
- Hand-off to Chapter 42 (the guest bathroom) present and light: PASS.

## Recommendation
Approve as draft. No structural edits required.
