# Chapter 41 Brand Voice Check: The Primary Bathroom

## Voice target
Warm, practical, expert, and clear about chemistry without being preachy. The register of a knowledgeable friend who has cleaned a lot of bathrooms and knows that patience beats scrubbing.

## Scan results
- **Product types only, no brands**: PASS. A brand-name scan across the whole package returned no true hits. Everything is a type: bathroom grime and soap-film cleaner, neutral pH multi-surface cleaner, streak-free glass and mirror cleaner, floor-material-compatible cleaner, colour-coded microfiber cloth set, small detail brush set, shower squeegee, shower caddy, lockable medicine box, leak tray, chemical storage bin, moisture absorber or humidity monitor. (The common word "method", as in "the Shine method", is not a brand reference.)
- **No hype, no invented numbers or prices**: PASS. No statistics, no fabricated study claims, no dollar figures. "One pass instead of ten" is a figure of speech, not a measurement.
- **Chemistry stated safely, never advises mixing**: PASS. The chapter states the one-product-per-bowl and never-mix rule plainly, explains why (bowl cleaner and bleach make a gas), and recommends neutral pH on stone rather than an acidic spray. No mixing is ever suggested.
- **Dash and punctuation house style**: PASS. Middot for label separators; no em/en dashes; no prose spaced-hyphen separators.
- **Inclusive, non-gendered**: PASS. "You", "a guest", "a child" throughout; no gendered assumptions.

## Consistency with the series
- The zone-by-zone instruction voice matches Chapters 31 to 40.
- The one-idea-to-keep close and the before-and-after signature match the Part 9 pattern.
- The two fences held everywhere: never mix two cleaners, never flush medicines down the toilet, and never an acidic spray on stone.

## Verdict
On voice. No changes needed.
