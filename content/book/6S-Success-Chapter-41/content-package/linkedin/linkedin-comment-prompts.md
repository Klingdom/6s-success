# Chapter 41 LinkedIn Comment Prompts: The Primary Bathroom

1. Do you spray the shower and walk away, or scrub it dry? Which actually keeps it clean longer?
2. When did you last read the date on the creams and medicines in your bathroom?
3. Does your toilet cloth have its own colour and touch nothing else, or do you use one cloth everywhere?
4. Can you see the trap and shut-off valves under your sink, or are they buried behind stock?
5. What is the oldest thing in your medicine cabinet right now, and why is it still there?
