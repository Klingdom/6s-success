# Spray First, Dry Beats Mould, Read the Date

*A 6S Success: Home Edition newsletter, from Chapter 41, The Primary Bathroom.*

Hi friend,

We cross from the dry rooms into the wet ones this week, starting with the smallest room you use the most.

**It fights three problems no other room faces all at once: standing moisture that breeds mould, open chemistry that has to be kept safe, and dates that quietly pass while you sleep. So the method changes: you spray first and let the cleaner work, you treat drying as the real job, and you read the date on everything.**

The room breaks into seven zones, each with its exact inputs and numbered steps: the vanity counter, the vanity drawers, the under-sink cabinet, the medicine cabinet, the shower or tub, the toilet area, and the linen and towel storage.

## To try this week

Do the shower the wet-room way. Spray the walls, the tray, and the caulk seam and step out to clean the counter while it dwells. Come back, wipe from the top down, squeegee the glass, lift the mat and wash both faces, then stand the mat on edge to drain and leave the door open. Let the chemistry and the drying do the work.
