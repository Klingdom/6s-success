# Chapter 41 LinkedIn Carousel Outline: The Primary Bathroom

Twelve slides. Product types only.

- Slide 1 (hook): The first wet room. The enemy is no longer clutter, it is water.
- Slide 2: Three problems at once: standing moisture, open chemistry, dates that pass.
- Slide 3 (spray first): spray the shower and bowl, then walk away and clean the dry zones.
- Slide 4 (seven zones): counter, drawers, under-sink, medicine cabinet, shower, toilet, linen. Start at the counter.
- Slide 5 (dry beats mould): mould is a water problem. Squeegee, drain the mat, run the fan.
- Slide 6 (read the date): expired is not stock, it is a failure on your shelf.
- Slide 7 (colour-code): the toilet cloth is one colour, that job only, never touches anything else.
- Slide 8 (one product per bowl): never mix bowl cleaner and bleach. Lock the chemistry high.
- Slide 9 (read the plumbing): keep a drip tray and the trap and valves visible under the sink.
- Slide 10 (splash range): nothing that touches your face goes within arm's reach of the flush.
- Slide 11 (standard): a bathroom you could hand a guest at an hour's notice.
- Slide 12 (CTA): Do one zone this weekend. Chapter 41. Next: the guest bathroom.
