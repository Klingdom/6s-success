## Same-day collision
The calendar avoids two assets sharing a hook on the same day. If your LinkedIn and Facebook audiences overlap heavily, push one back a day so the rule or the loop-not-line reframe does not land twice in one day.

## Cross-posting risk
- The LinkedIn carousel outline (`linkedin/linkedin-carousel-outline.md`) and the slides carousel copy (`slides/linkedin-carousel-copy.md`) cover the same next-event content. Post only one to LinkedIn (use the copy-ready slides file; keep the outline as planning).
- The LinkedIn newsletter (`linkedin/linkedin-newsletter-version.md`) and the email newsletter share structure; do not send both to the same subscriber the same day.

## Duplicate-content / SEO risk
- Web landing intro, ebook description, and LinkedIn article overlap in phrasing. Set the chapter page as the SEO canonical and mark syndicated copies with a canonical link.

## Cross-chapter risk (specific to Chapter 30)
- Chapter 30 is the FIFTH and FINAL chapter of Part Eight and the FINALE of the book's main arc, the PERIODIC-RENEWAL chapter. Keep it distinct from Chapter 29 behind it (the daily rhythm), from Chapter 27 (the friendly audit), and from Chapter 26 (the mindset). Chapter 30 USES the audit (Chapter 27) and the daily times (Chapter 29) as two of the three cadences but assets must NOT re-teach either of them; the periodic 6S event must stay distinct from the regular audit (the audit is the small, regular check-in; the event is the occasional, bigger, whole-area renewal) and from the daily times (the tiny, everyday upkeep). Assets make a full-circle callback to choosing the first area (Chapter 4) but must NOT re-teach choosing, and they must NOT re-teach Sort, Straighten, Shine, Safety, or Standardize (a one-line callback is fine, a re-teach is not).
- Do not let this chapter slide FORWARD into the room playbooks. Ch 30 assets close the arc and may tease Part 9 lightly ("any room you like", where to point the loop next) but must NOT teach the room-by-room playbooks. This fence is held: Part 9 is named in the package only as where the loop goes next, never taught.
- The friction-meter beat is the MILESTONE, the first and only time in the book the dial reaches its goal. The live needle rests exactly on the GOAL crosshair at (67,155), tagged "SUSTAIN COMPLETE" with a small "all six S's" note, with Chapter 29's position at (67.1,154.98) now a faint ghost just behind and the dashed last-gap path fully traversed. Read it as the culmination it is, but do not read it as a full stop: the frozen caption keeps completion a beginning (the goal, once reached, becomes the bar you renew and gently raise). All six S's are done.

## Originality and source-fidelity risk
- The chapter is a draft authored in the book voice, grounded in the 6S source for Sustain (schedule the next 6S event, pursue perfection of 6S awareness, communicate 6S success), not supplied verbatim source. All derived assets are faithful to that draft; re-sync if the chapter is revised.
- Quote cards are verbatim: every card in `graphics/quote-card-copy.md` (10 cards) verified byte-identical to a quoted line in `canonical/chapter-quotes.md` (0 mismatches). Nothing borrowed from outside the book. The widened dash scan across the 54 non-review files returned CLEAN, and the tone scan (force / blame / hype) returned CLEAN (every match a negation or prohibition-list item, not usage). Note that a dedicated brand / product-endorsement scan was NOT run programmatically for this package, so no pass is claimed for it here.

## Recommendations
1. Rotate CTA wording (highest-value, lowest-effort fix; several posts close on "Read the free chapter").
2. Pick one carousel version for LinkedIn.
3. Set the chapter page as the SEO canonical.
4. Keep Chapter 30 from blurring into its neighbors: distinct from Chapter 29 (the daily rhythm), Chapter 27 (the friendly audit), and Chapter 26 (the mindset) behind it, and distinct from the Part 9 room playbooks ahead of it. Chapter 30 is the PERIODIC-RENEWAL FINALE: a 6S home is never finished, only renewed, so schedule the next event before this one ends; keeping runs on three cadences; the six S's are yours for any space, for good; and the needle reaches the goal at last (SUSTAIN COMPLETE, all six S's).
5. Re-sync all assets if the authored chapter changes after author review.
