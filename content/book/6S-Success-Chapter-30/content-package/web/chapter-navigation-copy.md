This chapter removes that thought with the simplest possible move: it turns keeping from a line into a loop. It lifts the loop above mere maintenance with pursue perfection, each turn lifts the bar a little because you always notice something this time you were not ready to notice last time, and it hands you your independence, the six S's now a way of seeing you carry into any room, any drawer, any garage, for as long as you live there. The friction meter reaches the goal for the first and only time in the book: all through the book the needle moved toward the goal on the calm green side and never quite reached it, because there was always another S still to come, and there is not, now, this is the last one, so the sliver closes not by clearing some final pile of friction but by making keeping a loop that renews itself, SUSTAIN COMPLETE, all six S's done, six S's complete, the needle is home.
