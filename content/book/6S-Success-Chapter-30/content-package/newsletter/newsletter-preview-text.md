# Newsletter Preview Text (Preheader): Your Next 6S Event

Ten options, each 35 to 90 characters, each paired with a subject line. Char counts noted.

1. Pairs with #1 (A 6S home is never finished, only renewed. Put the next event on the calendar before this one ends, and keeping becomes a loop that comes back around on its own instead of a project that quietly stops.)
   "Never finished, only renewed: put the next event on the calendar." (65 chars)

2. Pairs with #2 (You have been picturing 6S as a line with an end. But a home is never done, because life keeps arriving. 6S is not a line but a loop, and the last step of every turn is simply deciding when the next turn begins.)
   "You have not reached the end of the work, only where it renews itself." (70 chars)

3. Pairs with #3 (Keeping a home runs on three clocks: the daily times, the regular check-in, and the occasional 6S event that renews the whole area from the top.)
   "Keeping runs on three clocks. This is the one that renews the whole area." (73 chars)

4. Pairs with #4 (A 6S event is a scheduled return to an area you have already done, and its most important step is choosing the date of the next one.)
   "A short, whole-area re-run that keeps a good home from becoming an old one." (75 chars)

5. Pairs with #5 (Perfection in a home is not a place you arrive at and stay. It is a direction you keep facing, and each event lifts the bar a little higher.)
   "Perfection is not a place you arrive at. It is a direction you keep facing." (75 chars)

6. Pairs with #6 (The first area you ever chose was the hardest, because all of it was new. Choosing your next one is different, because you already know how.)
   "The first area was the hardest. Now you already know how." (57 chars)

7. Pairs with #7 (A home worth living in is not one you finish once. It is one you know how to renew.)
   "A home worth living in is not one you finish once." (50 chars)

8. Pairs with #8 (The book ends here. The loop does not.)
   "The book ends here. The loop does not." (38 chars)

9. Pairs with #9 (Six S's complete. The needle is home.)
   "Six S's complete. The needle is home." (37 chars)

10. Pairs with #10 (Before you close this book, open your calendar and put one date on it: your next 6S event.)
    "Open your calendar and name one date: your next 6S event." (57 chars)
