# Newsletter Short Teaser: Your Next 6S Event

For a roundup or digest. 90 to 140 words.

---

You have all six S's now, and a home that is calm because of them. So the only danger left is not clutter or a missed hazard, it is one quiet thought that arrives precisely because things are going well: I am finished. The moment you decide you are finished is the moment you stop, and a home that is no longer renewed quietly begins to age. This finale removes that thought by turning keeping from a line that ends into a loop that renews. It teaches the periodic 6S event, a short, occasional, whole-area re-run of the six S's, and the one habit that closes the loop: schedule the next event before this one ends. Six S's complete, and the needle reaches the goal at last.
