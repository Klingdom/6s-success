## CTA writing notes
- The action is a small, provable move that demonstrates the whole chapter, and it is the very last thing the book asks: before you close this book, open your calendar and put one date on it, your next 6S event, on an area you have already done. Lead with it, which is concrete and doable in a minute.
- Keep it warm, proud, and hopeful, the culmination of the whole book: a quiet completion that is also a beginning. Celebrate the reader (they now know how) without smugness, and never imply that they now stop, because the whole point is that the loop keeps turning. The danger is that "finale" reads as an ending; insist that reaching the goal does not stop the dial, it makes the goal a bar the loop renews and gently raises.
- The rule (a 6S home is never finished, only renewed), the loop-not-line reframe, the pursue-perfection reframe, and the full-circle reframe are the most memorable, shareable lines. Lead social with them.
- Avoid corporate framing, hype, and any force, control, or shame language (no "no excuses," "stay disciplined," "make yourself," "hold the line," unlock, leverage, elevate, supercharge, "optimize," "10x"). Do not present the 6S event as a dreaded whole-weekend overhaul; it is a light, regular return. Avoid brand names and named systems.
- Scope note: this chapter owns the periodic 6S EVENT (the occasional scheduled whole-area re-run of the six S's), the one habit that closes the loop (schedule the next event before this one ends, tied to a season or repeating date), the synthesis of Sustain as THREE CADENCES (daily times, regular check-in, periodic event), and the hand-off of independence (the six S's as a way of seeing carried into any space). It USES the audit (Chapter 27) and the daily times (Chapter 29) as two of the three cadences without re-teaching them; it references choosing a first area (Chapter 4) lightly as a full-circle callback, without re-teaching choosing; it teases Part Nine (the room-by-room playbooks) lightly as places to point the loop next, without teaching them; and it does NOT re-teach the six S's. Because keeping has become a self-renewing loop and the home now stays good on its own, the friction meter finally reaches the GOAL for the first and only time in the book: SUSTAIN COMPLETE, all six S's done.
