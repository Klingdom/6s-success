# Chapter 48 Teaching Slide Deck Outline: The Hall Closet

Fourteen slides, about 20 minutes.

1. Title: The Hall Closet. Part Nine, room-by-room playbooks.
2. Every space so far has belonged to somebody.
3. Except this one. The hall closet belongs to nobody.
4. An unowned space accepts anything.
5. So it is not full because you have too much.
6. The second problem: depth. Deeper than your arm.
7. The front eighteen inches, and the rumour behind it.
8. Therefore: you cannot sort it in place.
9. Empty a shelf onto the floor. Keep going to the back wall.
10. Closed and dark means damp: wipe, then wait.
11. The output is journeys, not a pile. Keep a basket beside you.
12. The centerpiece: zone by zone, inputs and numbered steps (walk one zone live).
13. Safety: weight above, chemistry beside, cells below.
14. The signature, and the third photograph this room cannot fake.
