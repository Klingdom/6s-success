# Chapter 48 LinkedIn Carousel Copy: The Hall Closet

**Slide 1**
Every room in your house
belongs to somebody.

**Slide 2**
Except this one.

**Slide 3**
The hall closet belongs to nobody.
And an unowned space accepts anything.

**Slide 4**
It is not full because you have too much.

**Slide 5**
It is full because nothing in it
was ever going to object.

**Slide 6**
And it is deeper than your arm.

**Slide 7**
The front eighteen inches is all
you have used since you moved in.

**Slide 8**
Everything behind that is a rumour.

**Slide 9**
So you cannot sort it in place.
Empty a shelf onto the floor.

**Slide 10**
Most of what comes out
belongs in another room.

**Slide 11**
The proof is one photo:
the back wall, visible.
