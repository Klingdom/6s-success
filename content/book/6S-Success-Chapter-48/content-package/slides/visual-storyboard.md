# Chapter 48 Visual Storyboard: The Hall Closet

Four figures, matching the chapter's SVGs. Warm paper, terracotta, honey, slate, green. Types, not brands.

## Figure 1: the opener, two states
A full-width pair. Left, the closet as it really is: shelves loaded to the depth of an arm with a recognisable front row and an indistinct back half drawn deliberately vaguer, a vacuum wedged in sideways with a broom leaning across it, part-used bottles in no order, unlabelled bins above. Right, the same closet: sheet sets nested in their own pillowcases with counts on the shelf edges, the vacuum inside a taped outline, one labelled caddy per room, a clear paper bin with a min and max card, and a visible back wall.

## Figure 2: the five zones
A warm elevation of a closet interior, five numbered bands stacked and labelled: linen shelf, cleaning equipment, cleaning supplies, paper backstock, seasonal and guest. Footer line: start with the shelf you cannot see the back of, and empty it onto the floor.

## Figure 3: the method and the kit
Two panels. Left, the four rules of the hall closet layer with a gloss under each. Right, the kit as product types, leading with shelf dividers and labels, because this closet is fixed by being made readable.

## Figure 4: the before-and-after signature
A matched pair from the hallway with the camera icon between them, plus an honey-outlined label on the after panel reading "plus a third frame: the back wall". This is the only chapter whose signature calls for three photographs rather than two, and the figure should make that explicit.
