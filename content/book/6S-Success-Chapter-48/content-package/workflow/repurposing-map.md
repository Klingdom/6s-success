## Reuse cautions
- Types, not brands, everywhere. No invented prices.
- **No medical content.** Medication is a lockable-box mention only, never named or filmed.
- Never depict mixing, decanting, or an unlabelled container.
- Never depict floor-level storage in an after state, or anyone on a hallway chair.
- **Do not tidy the hallway mess out of frame.** Emptying a shelf onto the floor is the chapter's central action, and hiding it contradicts the argument.
- **Keep the third photograph in every depiction of the signature.** This is the only chapter whose close is three frames rather than two.
- Stay in the hall closet: do not reuse mudroom (Ch 47) or stair landing (Ch 49) material.
