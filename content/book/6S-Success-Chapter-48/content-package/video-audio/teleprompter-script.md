# Chapter 48 Teleprompter Script: The Hall Closet

*Two minutes. Read at an even pace. Pause at each line break.*

Every space in this book so far has belonged to somebody.

A bedroom belongs to whoever sleeps in it.

A workshop belongs to whoever builds there.

Ownership is quiet, but it is real.

It is what makes a space push back when the wrong thing gets put in it.

The hall closet is the exception. It belongs to nobody.

And an unowned space accepts anything.

So it is not full because you own too much.

It is full because nothing in it was ever going to object.

There is a second problem, and it is physical.

That closet is deeper than your arm.

Which means it hides its own contents.

The front eighteen inches is what you have used since you moved in.

Everything behind that is a rumour.

So you cannot sort this room in place.

Empty a shelf onto the hallway floor. Keep going until you see the back wall.

Your hallway gets worse before it gets better. That is the method working.

Then expect to walk.

Most of what comes out serves another room.

The picture hooks. The spare charger. The birthday candles.

None of it arrived because this was the right home.

It arrived because it had no home.
