# Chapter 48 B-Roll and Visual Notes: The Hall Closet

## Must-get shots
- The closed closet door, then the door opening on a fully loaded shelf. Hold on it.
- An arm reaching in and disappearing to the shoulder, to establish the depth honestly.
- One entire shelf being emptied onto a hallway floor, in one continuous take if possible. This is the chapter's central action.
- The bare shelf board with the back wall visible, held long enough to register.
- The grey dust line along a shelf lip, tight, before and after a wipe.
- A damp cloth on a board, then a hand testing it for dryness, then linen going back. The waiting step needs to be visible.
- A folded sheet set being nested inside its own pillowcase.
- A shelf-edge label being written as "Queen, 3 sets", showing the count rather than the name.
- Bleach and an ammonia product being moved apart onto separate shelves.
- A laundry basket being filled with things that belong in other rooms, then carried away down the hall.
- The third frame being taken: a phone photographing the back wall of the deepest shelf.

## Do not shoot
- Any readable brand name, logo, or label text. Types only.
- Any medication close enough to read, and no medication being handled at all.
- Two cleaners being poured together, or anything being decanted into an unlabelled bottle.
- Anyone standing on a hallway chair to reach a top shelf. If a height shot is needed, use the step stool.
- Anything being stored on the closet floor in the after sequence.

## Tone
Quiet and slightly forensic. This chapter is about seeing rather than doing, so the camera should linger on what was hidden. The mess in the hallway is a feature, not something to tidy out of frame.
