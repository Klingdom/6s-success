# Chapter 48 Short Video Scripts: The Hall Closet

Five scripts, 30 to 60 seconds each.

## 1. Nobody owns it
**Hook:** Every room in your house belongs to somebody. Except one.
**Body:** A bedroom belongs to whoever sleeps in it. A kitchen belongs to whoever cooks. Ownership is what makes a space push back when the wrong thing gets put in it. The hall closet has none.
**Close:** And an unowned space accepts anything.

## 2. Not too much
**Hook:** Your hall closet is not full because you have too much.
**Body:** It is full because nothing in it was ever going to object. Everything in the house that had to go somewhere, and had nowhere obvious to go, ended up behind that door.
**Close:** It never said no.

## 3. The front eighteen inches
**Hook:** You could not list what is on the back of your closet shelves.
**Body:** A closet deeper than your arm hides its own contents. The front eighteen inches is what you have actually used since you moved in. Everything behind that is a rumour.
**Close:** And you would describe it confidently and be wrong.

## 4. You cannot sort it in place
**Hook:** Stop trying to tidy a deep closet while standing in front of it.
**Body:** Sorting in place means judging only the things you can already see, and the things you can already see were never the problem. Empty a shelf onto the hallway floor and keep going until you see the back wall.
**Close:** Your hallway gets worse first. That is the method working.

## 5. The photo you cannot fake
**Hook:** There is one photograph this closet cannot fake.
**Body:** Not the tidy front row, which anyone can produce in ten minutes and which tells you nothing. The back wall of the deepest shelf, visible.
**Close:** The only way to take it is to have actually emptied the shelf.
