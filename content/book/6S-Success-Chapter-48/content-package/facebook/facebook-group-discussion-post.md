# Chapter 48 Facebook Group Discussion Post: The Hall Closet

**Question for the group: without opening the door, could you list what is on the back half of your hall closet shelves?**

Almost nobody can, and that is the point. A closet deeper than your arm hides its own contents well enough that you will describe it confidently and be wrong. The front eighteen inches is what you have actually used since you moved in. Everything behind that is a rumour.

Which is why you cannot sort this room in place. You have to empty a shelf onto the hallway floor and keep going until you can see the back wall.

Two more while we are here:

- **Which room does most of your closet's contents actually serve?** Usually somewhere else entirely. It ended up there because it had no home, not because it belonged.
- **Are bleach and an ammonia glass cleaner on the same shelf in your house?** Not because anyone would mix them, but because one develops a weeping cap and a closed cupboard does the mixing for you.

Tell us what is on your back shelf.
