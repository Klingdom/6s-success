# Chapter 48 Facebook Posts: The Hall Closet

Five standalone posts.

## 1
Every room in your house belongs to somebody. The hall closet belongs to nobody, and an unowned space accepts anything. It is not full because you have too much. It is full because nothing in it was ever going to object.

## 2
Your hall closet is deeper than your arm, which means it hides its own contents. The front eighteen inches is what you have used since you moved in. Everything behind that is a rumour.

## 3
You cannot sort a deep closet in place. Empty a shelf onto the hallway floor and keep going until you can see the back wall, because sorting in place means judging only what you can already see.

## 4
Most of what comes out of a hall closet serves another room. The picture hooks belong with the tools, the spare charger belongs at the desk. It did not arrive because this was the right home. It arrived because it had no home.

## 5
Wipe the shelf boards, then wait. Let them dry completely before any linen goes back, because dry cotton on a damp board is how a whole stack takes on the smell.
