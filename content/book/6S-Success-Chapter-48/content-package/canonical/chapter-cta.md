# Chapter 48 CTA and Companion Resources: The Hall Closet

## Primary call to action
Do one zone this week, and make it the shelf you cannot see the back of, because that is the one the closet has been lying to you about.

## Companion resources
- **The Hall Closet Reset card** (printable: the five zones, the done-looks-like for each, and the empty-it-onto-the-floor rule).
- **The Walk It Back card** (a basket list: the categories that almost always turn out to belong in another room, with the room named).
- **The Shelf Edge Card set** (printable min and max cards, and count labels in the "Queen, 3 sets" format).
- **The Hall Closet Kit list** (printable: shelf order and labels first, then access and safety, then cleaning).

## Register and fences
- Warm, practical, and free of blame. The closet filled because nobody owned it, not because anyone was careless.
- Product types only, never brand names. No invented statistics or prices.
- Never advise mixing cleaners, decanting into unlabelled containers, or storing anything at floor level in this room.
- Medication, if stored here, is named only as belonging in a lockable box; no medical or dosing content of any kind.
- Friction meter retired for Part 9; close on the before-and-after signature plus the third back-wall frame.
- Previous: Chapter 47, The Mudroom. Next: Chapter 49, The Stair Landing.

## Words to avoid
- No brand names. No medical advice. No repair instruction for a vacuum beyond dating the decision and recycling it.
