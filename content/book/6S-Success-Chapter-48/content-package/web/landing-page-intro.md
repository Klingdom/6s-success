# Chapter 48 Landing Page Intro: The Hall Closet

Every space in this book so far has belonged to somebody. A bedroom belongs to whoever sleeps in it. A workshop belongs to whoever builds there. Ownership is quiet, but it is real, and it is what makes a space push back when the wrong thing gets put in it.

The hall closet is the exception. It belongs to nobody. And an unowned space accepts anything, which explains the state of the closet you are about to open better than any amount of talk about clutter.

There is a second problem, and it is physical rather than social. This closet is deeper than your arm, which means it conceals its own contents well enough that you will confidently describe what is in there and be wrong. The front eighteen inches is the part you have used since you moved in. Everything behind that is a rumour.

So the instruction that opens this chapter is blunt: you cannot sort this room in place. Empty a shelf onto the hallway floor and keep going until you can see the back wall.

Five zones, exact inputs, numbered steps. Product types only, no brands. Expect to spend as much of the session walking things back to other rooms as sorting.
