# Chapter 48 LinkedIn Carousel Outline: The Hall Closet

Eleven slides. Product types only.

- Slide 1 (hook): Every room in your house belongs to somebody. Except this one.
- Slide 2: The hall closet belongs to nobody. An unowned space accepts anything.
- Slide 3: It is not full because you have too much.
- Slide 4: It is full because nothing in it was ever going to object.
- Slide 5: And it is deeper than your arm.
- Slide 6: The front eighteen inches is all you have used since you moved in.
- Slide 7: Everything behind that is a rumour.
- Slide 8: So you cannot sort it in place. Empty a shelf onto the floor.
- Slide 9: Keep going until you can see the back wall.
- Slide 10: Most of what comes out belongs in another room. Walk it back.
- Slide 11 (close): The proof is one photo: the back wall, visible.
