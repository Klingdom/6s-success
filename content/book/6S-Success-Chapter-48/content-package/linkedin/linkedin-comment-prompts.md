# Chapter 48 Comment Prompts: The Hall Closet

1. Could you list what is on the back half of your hall closet shelves without opening the door?
2. Which room does most of your hall closet's contents actually serve?
3. What has been in there since you moved in and never been used?
4. Do your cleaning products sit grouped by room, or just wherever they landed?
5. Are bleach and ammonia products on the same shelf in your house right now?
6. Is anything stored on your closet floor, and would you notice a dropped battery there?
7. What does your linen shelf label say: a name, or a count?
8. Which lidded bin have you moved without opening?
