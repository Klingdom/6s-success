## Two firsts and one honest miss

**First: no new product types were required.** The library stands unchanged at 119, and every input the Manual's Hall Closet content names was already catalogued. That is a milestone rather than an omission: the additions made across Chapters 43 to 47 have caught up with the Manual's vocabulary. One mapping fix was made instead, adding nitrile gloves to the Cleaning Supply Zone where the Manual named disposable gloves but the mapping had none. Hall Closet types rise from 31 to 32.

**First: the chapter came in under model.** 5,867 against a target of about 5,900, or 0.6 percent under. It is the first chapter in the run to land below rather than above.

**The miss: the zone-card budget was set for the first time and failed.**

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Frozen strings | ~920 | 941 | 1,006 | 883 | **864** |
| Callouts | ~250 | 357 | 255 | 267 | **267** |

Zone cards were budgeted at ~470 in the brief, per the superprompt rule added after Chapter 47, and came in at 515. Nothing had to be cut because the connective prose absorbed it, but the budget failed.

**The diagnosis is more useful than the budget.** Step count per zone has standardised at exactly ten: 50 steps across 5 zones. The Manual gives this room 34 shine surfaces, so each surface is being expanded into roughly 1.5 steps. The problem is not verbose steps, it is a default step count. **The fix for Chapter 49 is to let step count follow the Manual's surface count rather than rounding to ten.** A zone with 6 surfaces should produce 7 or 8 steps.

## The signature variation is the strongest in Part Nine

This is **the only chapter whose close calls for three photographs rather than two.** The third is the back wall of the deepest shelf, visible.

The argument is airtight and specific to this room. A tidy front row can be produced in ten minutes by anybody, photographs well, and proves nothing, because the front row was never the problem. A visible back wall cannot be faked, because the only way to take that picture is to have genuinely emptied the shelf, judged what came off, and put back less than came down. It is the one self-verifying image in the book, and it is the right answer to a room whose entire problem is concealment.
