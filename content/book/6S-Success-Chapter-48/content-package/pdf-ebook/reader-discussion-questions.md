# Chapter 48 Reader Discussion Questions: The Hall Closet

1. Without opening the door, list what is on the back half of your hall closet shelves. Then open it and check.
2. Who, if anyone, would you say owns that closet in your household?
3. What is in there that has not been touched since you moved in?
4. Which room does most of its contents actually serve?
5. What would fill a laundry basket if you walked back everything that belongs elsewhere?
6. Are bleach and an ammonia product on the same shelf in your house right now?
7. Is anything stored on your closet floor, and would you notice a dropped battery there?
8. Which lidded bin have you moved without ever opening, and what do you think is in it?
