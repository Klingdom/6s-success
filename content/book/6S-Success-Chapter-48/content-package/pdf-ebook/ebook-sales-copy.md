# Chapter 48 Ebook Sales Copy: The Hall Closet

**Your hall closet is not full because you have too much. It is full because nobody owns it.**

Chapter 48 of 6S Success: Home Edition takes the six-S loop into the darkest, deepest storage in the house, and starts by naming the mechanism nobody talks about: every other space in your home belongs to somebody, and ownership is what makes a space push back. This one has none.

Inside:

- The five zones of a hall closet, with the exact inputs and the numbered steps for each, from the grey line along a shelf lip to the bottom of a bin nobody has opened in three years.
- Why the front eighteen inches is all you have really used, and why everything behind it is a rumour.
- The blunt instruction that makes this chapter work: you cannot sort this room in place.
- The cleaning rule with a waiting step built into it, for the least ventilated storage in the house.
- Why most of what comes out belongs in another room, and why this is the only chapter whose output is journeys rather than a pile.
- Five hard calls, including the sheet set too nice to use and the bin you have moved three times without opening.
- The one photograph this closet cannot fake.

Product types, never brands. Five zones, four new rules, and a proof you can pin inside the door.
