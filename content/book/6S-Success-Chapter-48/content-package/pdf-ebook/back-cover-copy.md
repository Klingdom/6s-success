# Chapter 48 Back Cover Copy: The Hall Closet

*Every room in your house belongs to somebody. Except this one.*

The hall closet belongs to nobody, and an unowned space accepts anything. It is not full because you have too much. It is full because nothing in it was ever going to object.

And it is deeper than your arm, which means it hides its own contents. The front eighteen inches is all you have used since you moved in. Everything behind that is a rumour.

Five zones. One blunt instruction. And the single photograph this closet cannot fake.

**6S Success: Home Edition**, Chapter Forty-Eight.
