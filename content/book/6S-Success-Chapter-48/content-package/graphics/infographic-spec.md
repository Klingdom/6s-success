# Chapter 48 Infographic Spec: The Hall Closet

## Figure 2: the five zones
- Canvas 1000 x 440, warm paper fill.
- Title in Fraunces, 22px: "The hall closet, drawn as its five zones".
- Five panels on a 3 plus 2 layout, each 270 x 130, rounded 8, panel fill with a rule-coloured stroke (the same arrangement last used for the five-zone rooms at Chapter 42).
- Each panel carries a green numbered disc and a serif zone name: 1 Linen shelf, 2 Cleaning equipment, 3 Cleaning supplies, 4 Paper backstock, 5 Seasonal and guest.
- Footer in terracotta: start with the shelf you cannot see the back of, and empty it onto the floor.

## Figure 3: the method and the kit
- Canvas 1000 x 480, two panels of 460 x 410.
- Left panel headed "The method": four rules with a gloss under each. Lead lines: "Nobody owns this closet" (terracotta), "Depth lies" (green), "Closed and dark means damp" (slate), "Most of this serves another room" (terracotta).
- Right panel headed "The kit": nine lines of product types, leading with shelf dividers and location labels, because this closet is fixed by being made readable.
- Footer in terracotta: give every shelf a job, empty it to see it, let it dry, and walk the rest back.

## Figure 4: the signature
- Canvas 1000 x 430, matched before and after panels with a slate camera disc between them.
- The after panel carries an honey-outlined label reading "plus a third frame: the back wall", and the caption beneath reads "the after, and the proof".
- **This is the only chapter whose signature calls for three photographs rather than two.** The figure should make that explicit rather than leaving it to the caption.
