# Chapter 48 Image Generation Prompts: The Hall Closet

House style: warm paper background, editorial illustration, serif display type, muted terracotta, honey, slate, and green accents. No logos, no brand names, no readable label text, no QR codes. No em dashes in any rendered text.

1. **Opener, two states.** A hallway storage closet drawn twice side by side. Left: deep shelves crammed to the back, the front row drawn crisply and the back half deliberately indistinct, a vacuum wedged sideways, mismatched bottles, unlabelled bins on top. Right: the same closet with folded linen bundles, labelled shelf edges, a vacuum standing squarely inside a taped floor outline, matching caddies, and a clearly visible back wall.
2. **The five zones.** A warm elevation of a closet interior with five numbered horizontal bands labelled in a serif face.
3. **The front eighteen inches.** A simple plan-view diagram of a deep shelf with the front band shaded and the rear band left pale and empty.
4. **Sorting on the floor.** A person kneeling in a hallway with the entire contents of one shelf laid out around them in rough groups, the empty shelf visible behind.
5. **The walk-back basket.** A laundry basket of assorted household items being carried down a hallway, with open doorways visible either side.
6. **The taped outline.** A vacuum standing inside a floor outline marked in tape, with its attachment bag clipped to the handle.
7. **The third frame.** A close photograph-style illustration of the back wall of a deep, now-empty shelf, plain and unremarkable, which is the point.
