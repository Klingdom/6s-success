# Chapter 48 Diagram Ideas: The Hall Closet

1. **Owned against unowned** (hero): a row of rooms each with a small owner icon, and the hall closet with an empty owner slot. Caption: an unowned space accepts anything.
2. **The front eighteen inches**: a plan view of a shelf with the front band shaded and labelled "used since you moved in", and the deeper band left blank and labelled "a rumour".
3. **Sort in place against sort on the floor**: two panels, one person reaching into a full shelf judging only the front row, one with the whole shelf laid out on a hallway floor.
4. **The walk-back basket**: a basket in the centre with arrows leaving to labelled rooms: tools, desk, kitchen, bathroom. Caption: the output is journeys, not a pile.
5. **Wipe then wait**: a shelf board with a damp sheen, a clock, and then a dry board with linen going back. Caption: dry cotton on a damp board is how a stack takes on the smell.
6. **The weeping cap**: two bottles side by side on a shelf, one with a drip forming, inside a closed door. Caption: the leak does the mixing for you.
7. **The three frames**: the signature drawn as three photographs rather than two, the third being the back wall of the deepest shelf.
