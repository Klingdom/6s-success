# Chapter 48 Newsletter Short Teaser: The Hall Closet

The hall closet is the darkest and deepest storage in the house, and the only one nobody owns. An unowned space accepts anything, which is why everything that had to go somewhere ended up behind that door.

Chapter 48 breaks it into five zones with the exact inputs and numbered steps for each, and adds four rules to the nine rules of Shine: nobody owns it, depth lies so you cannot sort it in place, closed and dark means damp, and most of what is in there serves another room.

Start with the shelf you cannot see the back of.
