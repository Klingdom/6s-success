## HELD — canon and system violations (5). Not caption-rescuable.

### `image02` — "The Seven Kitchen Zones" · **the worst miss in the batch**
The chapter's frozen zone list is: prep counter · cooking zone · sink · **upper cabinets** · **lower cabinets and cookware** · **utensil and utility drawers** · fridge and freezer.

The plate prints a different seven:

| # | Frozen (correct) | Plate (wrong) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Primary prep counter | Primary prep counter ✔ |
| 2 | Cooking zone | Cooking zone ✔ |
| 3 | Sink | Sink zone ✔ |
| 4 | **Upper cabinets** | *Under sink cabinet* (invented; this is part of the sink zone's shine detail, not a zone) |
| 5 | **Lower cabinets and cookware** | *Upper & lower cabinets* (two frozen zones merged into one) |
| 6 | **Utensil and utility drawers** | Refrigerator & freezer |
| 7 | Refrigerator and freezer | *Small appliance & storage* (invented) |

Net effect: **the utensil and utility drawers zone is deleted entirely**, two zones are merged, and two zones are invented. This is the Ch 21 defect exactly — a frozen numbered set replaced by an invented set of the same count. Also carries a **fake QR code** block, a baked **em dash** ("Grease lives here—clean it completely"), and a contraction.

### `image03` — "Kitchen Shine Method" · rival nine-rule system
Prints a nine-step method that is **not** the book's nine. The frozen nine (Ch 31) are: top to bottom · dry before wet · back to front, clean to dirty · mist the cloth not the surface · two cloths, one to clean one to dry · colour-code your cloths · match the cleaner to the surface · give the cleaner a moment to work · clean to inspect and flag, do not fix.

The plate substitutes: soak first · top to bottom · lift don't wipe around · degrease the tacky · two-cloth system · food-safe cloth colours · inspect then finish · **reset every time** · **maintain with a daily rhythm**. The last two are Sustain, imported into Shine (fence violation); four of the frozen rules are dropped. Its cloth-colour key (green/blue/yellow/white) also omits the one colour the brief actually requires: a dedicated colour for the **fridge interior and raw-meat shelf**. Plus **multiple em dashes**, contractions, and a **fake QR code**.

### `image13` — "Decision Time: Keep, Donate, Trash, or Relocate" · **contradicts three chapters at once**
- Prints a **four-door** sort. The book's frozen system (Ch 11) is **five doors: Donate, Sell, Store, Recycle, Throw.** Sell, Store and Recycle are all gone; Keep and Relocate are promoted from Ch 8 Sort outcomes into out-box doors. (Even this chapter's own kit list names a five-container Keep/Relocate/Donate/Recycle/Trash set, so the plate drops Recycle from that too.)
- Prints **"Have I used it in the last 90 days?"** The book has no 90-day rule; Ch 9's test is when you last used it plus would you buy it again, and Ch 10's decide-by windows are a month or a season.
- Prints **"Don't create a maybe pile"**, **"'Maybe' becomes never"**, **"Maybe Later = Pile"**, and a **"ONE TOUCH RULE: decide once, then move it to its next home immediately."** This is the direct opposite of Chapter 10, whose entire teaching is that an honest maybe gets a red tag, a decide-by date, and a holding area, and that forcing a call you do not have is how remorse is manufactured.

A reader who follows this plate is following a different book. Renamed-terminology defect, so never caption-rescuable.

### `image15` — "Dishwasher and Refrigerator" · trademark + garbled text
Good content (it reaches the dishwasher filter and the fridge gaskets), but the Pro Tip panel reproduces a recognisable **ARM & HAMMER baking soda package** with its logo. The brief is product **types only, no brands**, and reproducing a trademarked package in a commercial book is a real exposure. Also carries **garbled overprinted text** in dishwasher step 5 ("...handle. Rem Put everything b ck h rn place"), and omits the condenser coils.

### `image20` — "Maintenance Rhythm" · rival cadence system
Prints **five** cadences (daily / weekly / monthly / seasonal / yearly) plus an invented five-step "Maintenance Flow" (Notice → Act Small → Prevent → Put Away → Check Later). The book's frozen Sustain framework (Ch 30) is **three clocks**: the daily times, the regular check-in, and the 6S event. A room chapter should not be re-teaching Sustain at all, and certainly not with a competing count. Also em dash and contractions.

## Batch-wide observations (feed these back into the prompt kit)

1. **Contractions are in every single plate of the batch** ("DON'T FORGET" appears on 12 of 20). The book runs 129,358 words with only nine contractions. Every plate reads slightly out of voice.
2. **Em dashes appear in 8 of 20** (`02, 03, 05, 14, 17, 18, 19, 20`). This is the book's only absolute rule and the House Style Bible already forbids it.
3. **Fake QR codes in 3 of 20** (`02, 03, 07`) — all three on the highest-value infographics.
4. **Two trademarks** (`15` Arm & Hammer, `18` Magic Eraser) against a types-only brief.
5. **"PRO TIP" is used throughout** where the book's callout is **6S Tip**.
6. **The frozen numbered sets are the failure point, again.** Every canon violation in this batch is a frozen count being replaced: seven zones → a different seven, nine Shine rules → a different nine, five doors → four, three Sustain clocks → five. This is the same root cause recorded for Ch 21, and the fix is the same: generate one image per fresh chat with the frozen list quoted verbatim in the prompt and the anchor image attached.
