3. Clean one kitchen zone to the method this weekend (the chapter action)
   Before you close the book, pick one zone of the kitchen and clean it to the method, start to finish. Gather the inputs into your caddy first, and where the zone soaks, set the soak going before anything else, then work top to bottom, lift rather than wipe around, and degrease the tacky. Start with the cooking zone or the sink, because those two carry the most grease and the most grime and give the biggest win. It is the smallest possible proof of the whole idea, that a kitchen is not clean because the counters are clear, it is clean because you lifted the appliances, soaked the grease, pulled the cloth into the gasket, and cleared the filter, inspecting as you went.
