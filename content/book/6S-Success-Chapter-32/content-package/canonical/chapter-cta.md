## In-chapter actions a reader can take (from the callouts)
- **Quick Win:** Do the cooking zone's soak-first trick. Before anything else, lift the burner grates and caps and the hood filter into hot degreasing water and leave them. While they soften on their own, degrease the splash wall and the hood from the top down. Come back, scrub the loosened grates in a fraction of the time, dry them fully, and the dirtiest corner of the kitchen is done.
- **6S Tip:** Give the degreaser its dwell time instead of scrubbing: spray it, leave it the time on the label, and it lifts the grease for you. Keep a separate cloth colour for the fridge interior and the raw-meat shelf, and never mix bleach with an acidic or ammonia cleaner in the sink, because together they make a gas that will drive you out of the room.
- **Family Challenge:** Give each person a zone to own on reset day, and put the fridge eat-first bin right where the door opens onto it, so the answer to what is for dinner comes from the bin rather than the cupboard, and far less food is thrown away.
