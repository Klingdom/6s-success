Note: This chapter was authored in the established book voice and design (manuscript + final HTML), then packaged. It is a draft for author review. It is the SECOND room playbook of Part Nine (Room-by-Room Playbooks), carrying the instruction-centric template proven in Chapter 31. The kitchen is the busiest, dirtiest, and highest-payback room, so the clean-and-shine instruction matters most here. The CORE of the chapter is the "Zone by Zone: Exactly How to Clean It" section: for each of the seven zones it gives the exact INPUTS to gather, the NUMBERED step-by-step clean-and-shine procedure, and an inspect-and-flag list, all governed by the universal nine-rule Shine method carried from Chapter 31 (work top to bottom, dry or clear before wet, back to front and clean to dirty, mist the cloth not the surface, two cloths one to clean and one to dry, colour-code your cloths, match the cleaner to the surface, give it dwell time, and clean to inspect and flag rather than fix) PLUS the kitchen layer: grease is the whole job and grease hides upward, soak the soakable parts first, give the degreaser its dwell time, switch from everyday cleaner to degreaser the moment a surface is tacky, and hold food-safety cloth discipline. Shine finds the hazards, the safety pass fixes them. The friction meter, retired for Part 9, is replaced by a BEFORE-AND-AFTER room signature: a matched pair of the kitchen's working heart, plus the two photos to take (the before to keep, the after as the standard). The kitchen is worked as seven small zones, not one big job.
