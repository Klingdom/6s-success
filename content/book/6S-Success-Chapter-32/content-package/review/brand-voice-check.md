# Brand Voice Check: Chapter 32 Content Package

The voice of *6S Success: Home Edition* is warm, plain-spoken, and quietly confident. Chapter 32 is the SECOND chapter of Part Nine (Room-by-Room Playbooks), following the instruction-centric template proven at the entryway in Chapter 31. The six-S method is complete (Parts 1 to 8), and the book is now handing the reader detailed, do-it-now instructions for one real room at a time. This chapter is instruction-centric: per Phil's directive, the step-by-step clean-and-shine procedures for each kitchen micro-zone, with their exact inputs, are the CORE of the chapter, which is why it runs longer than a method chapter. The kitchen is the busiest, dirtiest, highest-payback room in the house, so this chapter, more than any other, is about the clean. The register is warm, capable, and lightly celebratory: the room that matters most and the biggest single win in the house, so the feeling must be capability rather than instruction-from-above, the before state must be honoured rather than mocked, and the whole thing must stay free of shame, force, and hype. The chapter governs every zone procedure with the nine-rule Shine method carried from Chapter 31 plus the kitchen layer (grease hides upward, soak first, dwell time, degrease the tacky, lift not wipe around, food-safety cloths) and keeps a clean-to-inspect-and-flag discipline: Shine finds the hazards, the safety pass fixes them. The friction meter, which reached its goal at Chapter 30, is RETIRED for Part 9; each room now closes on its own concrete before-and-after transformation.

## Voice fingerprint (from the manuscript and final HTML)
- **Warm and personal.** Talks to "you," treats the kitchen as the room that matters most and the one you actually want to cook in, and walks the reader through the actual work: set the grates and filter to soak first, degrease the hood top down, lift the toaster and clean under it, pull the cloth into the fridge gasket. "Do one zone this weekend, the sink or the cooking zone, and cook in a room that is finally clean underneath."
- **Concrete and procedural over abstract.** The core is not a concept but a set of instructions with exact inputs, zone by zone (prep counter, cooking zone, sink, upper cabinets, lower cabinets and cookware, drawers, fridge and freezer). Each procedure is built on the nine-rule Shine method plus the grease layer, so the reader is given a repeatable order, not a pep talk.
- **Capable and lightly celebratory, never smug or bossy.** This is framed as the room that matters most and the biggest single win in the house: the method was the hard part and you already have it. The instructions read as a competent friend working alongside you, not a manual barking steps.
- **Honest about the state of the room.** The before is honoured, not mocked; the most daunting room faced as one job becomes a series of finishable pieces faced as seven zones; a sponge is treated as the dirtiest object in the room rather than shamed; the appliance you love but rarely use is counted by real uses. Nothing implies the room must be conquered in one exhausting go.
- **Method, not brand.** The kit and cleaners are given as product TYPES only (neutral pH multi-surface cleaner, food-area degreaser, colour-coded microfiber cloths, a detail brush set). "Colour-code your cloths" and "a dedicated colour for the fridge interior and raw-meat shelf" are techniques, not endorsements; the word "method" throughout means the Shine method, never a brand.

## Voice risks to watch
- **Hero vocabulary is dense.** "zone," "grease," "degrease," "soak," "dwell time," "method," "cloth," "top to bottom," "lift," "hood," "hob," "sink," "fridge" recur because they are the subject of a procedure; expected in an instruction-heavy chapter, not a tic, but vary the surrounding language when excerpting so a single post does not stack "zone" and "grease" and "method".
- **Procedure can read as a manual if stripped of warmth.** Because the core is genuinely step-by-step, short excerpts must keep the capable-friend register (the room that matters most, the biggest single win) and not tip into a cold instruction list.
- **CTA sameness.** Rotate the LinkedIn and X sign-offs; several close on "Read the free chapter" / "Read it free online."
- **Keep the before honoured, never mocked.** The chapter honours the starting-state room; watch that excerpts never shame the reader's current kitchen or imply it must be conquered in one exhausting go.

- **Dash scan:** CLEAN. 0 em dashes, 0 en dashes, and 0 real spaced-hyphen (" - ") violations across the package. - **Brand-name scan:** CLEAN, NO brand-name leaks. The package names product TYPES only, never brands. The "Method" matches are the ordinary word "method" (the Shine method, in headings and prose), not a brand name.
