# Reuse Risk Check: Chapter 32 Content Package

When one chapter becomes fifty-plus assets, the risk is overexposure: the same line or image hitting the same follower until it feels canned. This check flags where that can happen. Chapter 32 is instruction-centric, so its most portable pieces are the seven-zones framing, the grease rule, the nine-rule Shine method plus the kitchen layer, and the per-zone procedures with their exact inputs.

## Same-day collision
The calendar avoids two assets sharing a hook on the same day. If your LinkedIn and Facebook audiences overlap heavily, push one back a day so the grease rule or the seven-zones line does not land twice in one day.

## Cross-posting risk
- The LinkedIn carousel outline and the slides carousel copy cover the same zone-by-zone content. Post only one to LinkedIn (use the copy-ready slides file; keep the outline as planning).
- The LinkedIn newsletter and the email newsletter share structure; do not send both to the same subscriber the same day.

## Duplicate-content / SEO risk
- Web landing intro, ebook description, and LinkedIn article overlap in phrasing. Set the chapter page as the SEO canonical and mark syndicated copies with a canonical link.

## Cross-chapter risk (specific to Chapter 32)
- Chapter 32 is the SECOND chapter of Part Nine, the second of twenty room playbooks, following the instruction-centric format proven at the entryway. Keep it distinct from the method chapters behind it: it APPLIES the method to a real room and must NOT re-teach Sort, Straighten, Shine, Safety, Standardize, or Sustain (a one-line callback is fine, a re-teach is not). The nine-rule Shine method is applied here plus the kitchen grease layer, not taught from scratch. Shine finds hazards and flags them; the safety pass fixes them, so assets must not slide into re-teaching the hazard hunt.
- Keep it distinct from Chapter 31 (The Entryway) behind it: Chapter 32 carries the same nine-rule Shine method but adds the kitchen's own layer (grease, soaking, dwell time, food safety), and its centerpiece is seven zones, not five. Do not blur the two rooms' zone lists or reframes.
- Do not let this chapter slide FORWARD into the next room. Ch 32 assets stay in the kitchen and may hand off to Part 9's next room (Chapter 33, The Pantry) in a single line, but must NOT teach it. This fence is held: the pantry is named only as where the loop goes next.
- The recurring-close beat is the BEFORE-AND-AFTER room signature, NOT the friction meter. The friction meter reached its goal at Chapter 30 and is RETIRED for Part 9; it does not appear in this chapter or any Part 9 chapter. Assets must close each room on its own concrete before-and-after transformation, governed by the frozen before-and-after caption.
- **No brand leaks.** The instruction-centric kit is given as product TYPES only (neutral pH multi-surface cleaner, food-area degreaser, colour-coded microfiber cloths, a detail brush set, a compact vacuum). Assets must never name a brand; the tone/brand scan confirms this held across the package, and the "Method" matches are the ordinary word "method" (the Shine method), not a brand.

## Originality and source-fidelity risk
- The chapter is a draft authored in the book voice to be instruction-centric, grounded in the CH32 canonical strings and brief. All derived assets are faithful to that draft; re-sync if the chapter is revised.
- Quote cards are verbatim: every card in `graphics/quote-card-copy.md` verified byte-identical to a quoted line in `canonical/chapter-quotes.md` (0 mismatches), including the grease rule and the nine-rule list. Nothing borrowed from outside the book. The dash scan returned CLEAN (0 em, 0 en, 0 real spaced-hyphen violations), the tone scan returned CLEAN (every match a negation or prohibition-list mention), and the brand-name scan returned CLEAN (product TYPES only, no brands).

## Recommendations
1. Rotate CTA wording (highest-value, lowest-effort fix; several posts close on "Read the free chapter").
2. Pick one carousel version for LinkedIn.
3. Set the chapter page as the SEO canonical.
4. Keep Chapter 32 from blurring into its neighbors: distinct from the method chapters behind it (it applies, does not re-teach), distinct from Chapter 31 (same method, but the kitchen adds the grease layer and seven zones), and distinct from Chapter 33 (The Pantry) ahead of it. Chapter 32 is the instruction-centric kitchen playbook: you do not clean the kitchen, you clean the cooking zone, to the nine-rule Shine method plus the grease layer, with exact inputs, closing on the before-and-after room signature.
5. Re-sync all assets if the chapter changes after author review.
