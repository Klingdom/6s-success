Twelve slides. Slide 1 hooks, last slide carries the soft CTA. Each slide has a headline and one or two lines of body. Warm, practical, expert voice, the friendly pro who shows you how; foreground the how-to value, the kitchen Shine method, the grease layer, the per-zone inputs and steps, and clean to inspect while the safety pass fixes; no hype, no brand names, product types only.

## Slide 1: Hook

**Headline:** A kitchen is not clean because the counters are clear. Tidy and hygienic are not the same thing.

**Body:** The door is done. Now the room that matters most. Swipe for exactly how to clean and shine the kitchen to a professional standard, grease and all, one small zone at a time.

---

## Slide 2: The room that matters most

**Headline:** If the entryway was the room to start with, the kitchen is the room that matters most.

**Body:** The busiest room in the house, the dirtiest for the work it does, and the one a proper 6S pays back the fastest. That is why this chapter, more than any other, is about the clean.

---

## Slide 3: Seven small zones, not one big job

**Headline:** The kitchen is seven small zones, not one big job.

**Body:** The prep counter, the cooking zone, the sink, the upper cabinets, the lower cabinets and cookware, the utensil and utility drawers, and the fridge and freezer. Do them one at a time and the room comes back to you a zone at a time.

---

## Slide 4: Keep the nine rules, add four moves

**Headline:** The kitchen keeps the nine rules from the door and adds four moves.

**Body:** Soak first, work top to bottom, lift rather than wipe around, and switch to the degreaser the moment a surface is tacky. The one input that changes everything here is the food-area degreaser.

---

## Slide 5: The grease rule

**Headline:** Grease is the whole job in a kitchen, and grease hides upward.

**Body:** Set the soakable parts to soak before you do anything else, work top to bottom so what you loosen falls onto what you have not cleaned yet, and reach for the degreaser the moment your everyday cleaner only smears a tacky film around.

---

## Slide 6: The most powerful tool is time

**Headline:** The most powerful tool in a kitchen clean is not a scrubbing arm, it is time.

**Body:** Set the burner grates and the hood filter to soak in hot degreasing water before you touch anything else, and let every degreaser sit for its dwell time. The chemistry lifts baked grease while you work elsewhere.

---

## Slide 7: The cooking zone, step by step

**Headline:** The dirtiest corner of the kitchen, started before you touch it.

**Body:** Soak the grates, caps, and hood filter first. Degrease the hood top down, then the splash wall. Clean the hob and wells. Come back and scrub the loosened grates in a fraction of the time.

---

## Slide 8: The sink resets the whole room

**Headline:** The sink is the point the whole kitchen resets from, so it has to be genuinely clean, not just rinsed.

**Body:** The sponge is the dirtiest object in the room and the dishwasher filter is why your glasses come out gritty. In this one zone, the things you never think to clean are the ones that matter most.

---

## Slide 9: Lift, do not wipe around

**Headline:** A wipe around the clutter is not a clean.

**Body:** The crumbs and grease you never see live under the toaster's feet, on top of the cabinets, and in the fold of the fridge gasket. Lifting the appliance, emptying the shelf, and pulling the cloth into the fold is the difference between tidy and hygienic.

---

## Slide 10: Food-safety discipline

**Headline:** Give the degreaser its dwell time, and keep your cloths in their lanes.

**Body:** A separate cloth colour for the fridge interior and the raw-meat shelf. And never mix bleach with an acidic or ammonia cleaner in the sink, because together they make a gas that will drive you out of the room.

---

## Slide 11: Clean to inspect, the safety pass fixes

**Headline:** Your hand passes over every surface, so your eye should too.

**Body:** The grease-soaked hood filter, the rocking grate, the slow leak under the sink, the slack gasket: note it and keep cleaning. Shine finds hazards, the safety pass fixes them.
