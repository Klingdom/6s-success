# Newsletter Short Teaser: The Kitchen

For a roundup or digest. 90 to 140 words.

---

If the entryway was the room to start with, the kitchen is the room that matters most: the busiest, the dirtiest, and the one 6S pays back the fastest. This edition, more than any other, is about the clean. It carries the nine-rule Shine method from the door and adds the kitchen's one big opponent, grease, which hides upward: soak the soakable parts first, work top to bottom, lift rather than wipe around, and switch to the degreaser the moment a surface is tacky. Seven small zones, not one big job, each with the exact inputs to gather and the precise, step-by-step way to clean it. A kitchen is not clean because the counters are clear.
