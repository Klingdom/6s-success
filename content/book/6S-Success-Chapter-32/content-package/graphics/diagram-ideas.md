## 5. How to Degrease the Cooking Zone (the step ribbon, no direct HTML twin)

**Purpose:** Render the exact order of work for the dirtiest corner of the kitchen, the cooking zone, so the
reader sees the soak-first sequence as one calm left-to-right ribbon and understands that the soak is set going
before anything else so it works while the top-down degrease happens. This diagram has no direct HTML figure
twin; it makes the Quick Win concrete, the soak-first trick that gets the biggest, greasiest win for the least
scrubbing.

**What it shows:** A gentle left-to-right ribbon of the moves the cooking zone runs in order, each a small step
card with a tiny type icon and a green-numbered node. `SOAK THE GRATES AND FILTER`, lift the burner grates and
caps and the hood's metal grease filter into hot degreasing water and leave them, so the soak softens the baked
grease on its own while you do everything else. `HOOD, TOP DOWN`, spray the underside and face of the hood with
degreaser, give it the label's dwell time, wipe it down, and finish with a clean damp pass. `SPLASH WALL`, spray
it, let it sit, and wipe from the top down so the loosened grease runs toward the hob and not onto clean wall,
giving baked spatter a second pass. `SCRUB THE SOFTENED GRATES`, come back to the soaked grates and caps, scrub
the softened rings with a detail brush in a fraction of the time, and rinse. `DRY AND REFIT`, dry the grates and
caps fully before they go back so they do not rust onto the hob, and rinse, dry, and refit the hood filter. A
faint note under the ribbon reminds that the soak, set first, is what makes the rest quick.

**Rough layout:**
- A calm panel `#FBF7EF`. Top, sans caps letter-spaced soft: `HOW TO DEGREASE THE COOKING ZONE`, a Fraunces line
  beneath in the label green: "soak first, then top down, then scrub what the soak has already loosened".
- Five step cards left to right, each a green-numbered node with a Fraunces label and a Newsreader italic gloss:
  - `1 · soak the grates and filter` · into hot degreasing water, before anything else
  - `2 · hood, top down` · spray, dwell, wipe, clean damp pass
  - `3 · splash wall` · top to bottom, so grease runs toward the hob
  - `4 · scrub the softened grates` · a detail brush, a fraction of the time
  - `5 · dry and refit` · dry fully so nothing rusts onto the hob
- The soak step (`1`) drawn a notch larger and greener with a small clock, because setting it first is the whole
  trick; a faint dashed line loops from step 1 to step 4 to show the soak working in the background while steps 2
  and 3 happen.
- A foot line in Fraunces slate: `soak first, hood and wall top down, then scrub the softened grates dry`.
- Caption (from the chapter Quick Win): "Do the cooking zone's soak-first trick. Before anything else, lift the
  burner grates and caps and the hood filter into hot degreasing water and leave them. While they soften on
  their own, degrease the splash wall and the hood from the top down. Come back, scrub the loosened grates in a

**Note for the illustrator:** this is the soak-first order distilled, so keep it a calm ribbon, five moves left
