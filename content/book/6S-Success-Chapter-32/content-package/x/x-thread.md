"If the entryway was the room to start with, the kitchen is the room that matters most." It is worked hardest and gets dirtiest, so this is the chapter, more than any other, that is about the clean.

3/
"The kitchen is seven small zones, not one big job: the prep counter, the cooking zone, the sink, the upper cabinets, the lower cabinets and cookware, the utensil and utility drawers, and the fridge and freezer." Run them one at a time, not as a lost weekend.

4/
You already know the nine rules of Shine from the door, and every one still holds. "The kitchen simply adds the hardest opponent in the house, grease, and grease does not behave like ordinary dirt." So it adds four moves on top of the nine.

(243 chars)

---

5/
"The kitchen keeps the nine rules from the door and adds four moves: soak first, work top to bottom, lift rather than wipe around, and switch to the degreaser the moment a surface is tacky. The one input that changes everything here is the food-area degreaser."

(264 chars)

---

6/
Here is the principle the whole room turns on. "Grease is the whole job in a kitchen, and grease hides upward." So you clean it in a set order, not wherever your eye lands, and you let chemistry do the lifting.

(213 chars)

---

7/
"Set the soakable parts to soak before you do anything else, work top to bottom so what you loosen falls onto what you have not cleaned yet, and reach for the degreaser the moment your everyday cleaner only smears a tacky film around."

(238 chars)

---

8/
"The most powerful tool in a kitchen clean is not a scrubbing arm, it is time. Set the burner grates and the hood filter to soak in hot degreasing water before you touch anything else, and let every degreaser sit for its dwell time."

(236 chars)

---

9/
Food-safety cloth discipline, one rule above all. "Keep a separate cloth colour for the fridge interior and the raw-meat shelf, and never mix bleach with an acidic or ammonia cleaner in the sink, because together they make a gas that will drive you out of the room."

(269 chars)

---

10/
The cooking zone, worked. Lift the grates, caps, and hood filter into hot degreasing water and leave them. Degrease the splash wall and hood top down while they soften. Come back, scrub the loosened grates in a fraction of the time, and the dirtiest corner is done.

(269 chars)

---

11/
"The sink is the point the whole kitchen resets from, which means it has to be genuinely clean, not just rinsed." The sponge is the dirtiest object in the room, and the dishwasher filter is why glasses come out gritty: twist it out and rinse the mesh.

(255 chars)

---

12/
"The crumbs and grease you never see are the ones living under the toaster's feet, on top of the cabinets, and in the fold of the fridge gasket." A wipe around the clutter is not a clean. Lift the appliance, empty the shelf, and pull the cloth into the fold.

(262 chars)

---

13/
In the fridge, clean what a quick wipe misses: run the cloth around the door gasket, pulling into each fold where mold gathers, then vacuum the dust off the condenser coils behind the unit so the motor stops running hot. Set the eat-first bin where the door opens.

(268 chars)

---

14/
"A kitchen is not clean because the counters are clear." It is clean because you soaked the grease, pulled the cloth into the gasket, and cleared the filter, zone by zone. Do one this weekend, the sink or the cooking zone.
