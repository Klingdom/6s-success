2/
"Grease is the whole job in a kitchen, and grease hides upward." So set the grates and hood filter to soak first, work top to bottom so what you loosen falls onto what is still dirty, and switch to degreaser the moment your cleaner smears. Read it free.

(256 chars)

3/
"The kitchen is seven small zones, not one big job: the prep counter, the cooking zone, the sink, the upper cabinets, the lower cabinets and cookware, the utensil and utility drawers, and the fridge and freezer." One at a time, the room comes back. Read it free.

(265 chars)

---

4/
The cooking zone trick: before anything else, lift the burner grates, caps, and hood filter into hot degreasing water and leave them. Degrease the splash wall and hood from the top down while they soften. Come back and the loosened grates scrub in a fraction of the time.

(274 chars)

5/
"A finished kitchen is one you could start cooking in with your eyes shut": a clear run of prep counter, your oil and turner within one reach of a clean hob, and an empty dry sink the room resets from. Read the free chapter.

(227 chars)

6/
"The sink is the point the whole kitchen resets from, which means it has to be genuinely clean, not just rinsed." The sponge is the dirtiest object in the room, and the dishwasher filter is why glasses come out gritty: twist it out and rinse the mesh. Read it free.

(268 chars)

---

7/
"The crumbs and grease you never see are the ones living under the toaster's feet, on top of the cabinets, and in the fold of the fridge gasket." A wipe around the clutter is not a clean. Lift the appliance, empty the shelf, pull the cloth into the fold.

(257 chars)

---

8/
In the fridge, clean the parts a quick wipe always misses: pull the cloth all the way around the door gasket into each fold, vacuum the dust mat off the condenser coils so the motor stops running hot, and set the eat-first bin right where the door opens onto it.

(265 chars)

---

9/
Food safety in one zone: keep a separate cloth colour for the fridge interior and the raw-meat shelf, and "never mix bleach with an acidic or ammonia cleaner in the sink, because together they make a gas that will drive you out of the room."

(244 chars)

---

10/
"A kitchen is not clean because the counters are clear." It is clean because you soaked the grease, pulled the cloth into the gasket, and cleared the filter, zone by zone. Do one this weekend, the sink or the cooking zone.
