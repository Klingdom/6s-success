# Chapter 46 Facebook Posts: The Workshop

Five standalone posts.

## 1
This is the room 6S came from. Every other room in this book fails into inconvenience. The workshop fails into injury, which is the reason the whole discipline exists.

## 2
The PPE station is the smallest zone in a workshop and it is the one you do first, because everything else in there involves a blade, a solvent, or a cloud of fine dust. It is not the reward at the end of the clean. It is the permission to begin.

## 3
Vacuum a workshop, never sweep it. A broom does not remove the finest sawdust, it launches it back into the air, where it settles onto wet finish and into your lungs. And the fines are the fraction that actually burns.

## 4
A workshop's mess is not about storage. It is three verdicts you keep postponing: offcuts, half-finished projects, and paint. You will sort the fasteners twice before you touch the offcut pile once.

## 5
A blade guard is a spring-loaded part living in sawdust, and sawdust is what makes a spring sticky. It degrades slowly, it degrades silently, and the first time you notice is the time it does not come back.
