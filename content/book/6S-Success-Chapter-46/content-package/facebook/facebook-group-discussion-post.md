# Chapter 46 Facebook Group Discussion Post: The Workshop

**Question for the group: which zone of your workshop have you been avoiding?**

Be honest, because the answer is the whole diagnosis. A workshop's mess is never really about storage. It is three verdicts you keep postponing: on offcuts, on half-finished projects, and on paint.

And most of us know exactly which one we dodge. You will sort the fasteners twice before you touch the offcut pile once, because tipping out a jar of screws feels like progress and putting good wood in the fire bucket feels like waste.

Two more while we are here:

- **Do you sweep your shop or vacuum it?** A broom launches the finest sawdust back into the air, where it settles onto wet finish and into your lungs, and the fines are the part that actually burns.
- **When did you last work every blade guard with your thumb?** It is a spring-loaded part living in sawdust. It degrades slowly and silently, and the first time you notice is the time it does not come back.

Tell us which zone you are doing next.
