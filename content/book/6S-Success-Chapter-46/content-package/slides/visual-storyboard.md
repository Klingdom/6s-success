# Chapter 46 Visual Storyboard: The Workshop

Four figures, matching the chapter's SVGs. Warm paper, terracotta, honey, slate, green. Types, not brands.

## Figure 1: the opener, two states
A full-width pair. Left, the room as it runs: safety glasses on a far wall behind a stack of timber, a half-assembled project holding the best surface, a tin of mixed hardware, an offcut bin overflowing onto the floor, part tins standing open. Right, the same room: glasses and muffs on labelled hooks at the door, a bare bench with the vise and the lamp, every outline on the tool wall filled, the offcut bin at its written size floor, and the finishes behind a metal door.

## Figure 2: the six zones
A warm overhead plan, six numbered cells in two rows of three: PPE station, main workbench, power tools, fasteners, material rack, finishing. Footer line: the smallest zone leads, because it is the permission to begin. The PPE cell should read as first, not sixth.

## Figure 3: the method and the kit
Two panels. Left, the four rules of the workshop layer with a short gloss under each. Right, the kit as product types, with protection on the first two lines rather than cleaning, mirroring the order of the room itself.

## Figure 4: the before-and-after signature
A matched pair from the door you come in through, with the camera icon between them. The after panel carries a small honey-outlined label reading "start cutting inside a minute", because in this room the standard is about readiness rather than appearance.
