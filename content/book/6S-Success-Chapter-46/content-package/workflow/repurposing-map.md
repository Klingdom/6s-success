## Reuse cautions
- Types, not brands, everywhere. No invented prices.
- **Never depict sweeping**, and never show a tool running.
- **Never depict or describe a guard held, taped, or wedged open**, except the thumb test on an unplugged tool.
- No machine setup, tuning, or repair content of any kind.
- Disposal is hazardous waste or metal recycling; coated and treated offcuts never go on a fire.
- Nobody appears in the shop without eye protection, including in the background.
- Every asset closes on the before-and-after signature.
- Stay in the workshop: do not reuse garage (Ch 45) or mudroom (Ch 47) material.
