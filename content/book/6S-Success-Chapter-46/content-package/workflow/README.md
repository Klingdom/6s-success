## Fences for this chapter
- Product **types** only, never brand names.
- **No machine setup, tuning, blade-changing, or repair instruction.** The chapter cleans, looks, and flags, and takes a tool out of service rather than fixing it.
- **Never advise modifying, taping, wedging, or bypassing a guard**, for any reason, in any asset.
- Disposal is household hazardous waste or metal recycling. Never a drain, never a bin, and coated or treated offcuts never go on a fire.
- **Never depict sweeping.** It contradicts the chapter's single strongest instruction.
- No tool is shown running in any b-roll; this is a cleaning and inspection film.
- Friction meter retired for Part 9; every asset closes on the before-and-after signature.
