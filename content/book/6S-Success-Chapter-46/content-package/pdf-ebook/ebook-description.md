# Chapter 46 Ebook Description: The Workshop

The sixteenth room playbook of Part Nine, and a homecoming.

This is the room 6S came from. Everything in this book, the traced outlines and the labels and the photograph of the ideal state, was invented somewhere like this, by people for whom a missing tool was a stopped line and a sticky guard was a lost hand. It is also the one chapter where the stakes are genuinely different: every other room in the book fails into inconvenience, and this one fails into injury.

The room breaks into six zones, each with its exact inputs and numbered steps: the safety and PPE station, the main workbench, the power tool storage, the fastener and hardware zone, the material rack, and the finishing and chemical zone. The smallest zone leads, which happens nowhere else in the book, because everything here involves a blade, a solvent, or a cloud of fine dust.

On top of the nine rules of Shine it adds four of its own, including one that overrides habit: you vacuum rather than sweep, because a broom launches the finest sawdust into the air where it settles onto wet finish and into lungs, and the fines are the fraction that burns.

And it names the thing that actually keeps a workshop from improving. The mess is not about storage. It is three verdicts nobody wants to make, on offcuts, on half-finished projects, and on paint, and the zones you keep skipping are the zones holding the room back.
