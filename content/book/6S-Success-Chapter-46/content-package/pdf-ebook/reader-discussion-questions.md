# Chapter 46 Reader Discussion Questions: The Workshop

1. Which of the three verdicts, offcuts, half-finished projects, or paint, do you avoid most, and what does that avoidance cost you?
2. If you had to name the next physical action on your stalled project and a date this month, could you do both in one breath?
3. Where does your PPE actually live, and is it on the path you walk in on?
4. Do you sweep or vacuum, and had you thought about where the fines go?
5. When did you last work every blade guard with your thumb?
6. What is your visitor rule, and has anyone other than you ever heard it?
7. Which tool in your shop has not done work in twenty-four months?
8. How many of the paint colours you are storing are still on a surface in this house?
