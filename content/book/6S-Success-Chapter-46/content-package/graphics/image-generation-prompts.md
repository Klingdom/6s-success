# Chapter 46 Image Generation Prompts: The Workshop

House style: warm paper background, editorial illustration, serif display type, muted terracotta, honey, slate, and green accents. No logos, no brand names, no readable label text, no QR codes. No em dashes in any rendered text.

1. **Opener, two states.** A home workshop drawn twice side by side. Left: a bench buried under a half-assembled project, tools scattered, an overflowing offcut bin, part tins open on a shelf, safety glasses barely visible on a far wall. Right: the same shop with glasses and muffs on labelled hooks at the door, a bare bench holding only a vise and a task lamp, tools on painted outlines, and a closed metal cabinet.
2. **The six zones.** A warm overhead floor plan of a workshop with six numbered areas, the PPE station drawn at the door and clearly marked as first.
3. **Broom against vacuum.** Two panels of the same floor: a broom with a visible cloud of fine dust rising, and a vacuum nozzle with clear air. Diagrammatic and calm.
4. **The PPE board.** Hooks by a door with labelled silhouettes behind safety glasses and ear defenders, one hook marked for visitors, one silhouette empty.
5. **The guard check.** A close crop of a thumb pushing a retracting saw guard open, with the tool unplugged and clearly at rest on a bench.
6. **The offcut bin.** A single bin with a size floor written on the front in marker and a fill line, a hand placing one piece in and removing a shorter one.
7. **The signature pair.** A matched before-and-after of the workshop from the door you come in through, same framing, same light.
