# Chapter 46 Infographic Spec: The Workshop

## Figure 2: the six zones
- Canvas 1000 x 440, warm paper fill (back to two rows from the seven-zone Garage canvas of 560).
- Title in Fraunces, 22px: "The workshop, drawn as its six zones".
- Six panels, two rows of three, each 270 x 130, rounded 8, panel fill with a rule-coloured stroke.
- Each panel carries a green numbered disc and a serif zone name: 1 PPE station, 2 Main workbench, 3 Power tools, 4 Fasteners, 5 Material rack, 6 Finishing.
- Footer in terracotta: the smallest zone leads, because it is the permission to begin.

## Figure 3: the method and the kit
- Canvas 1000 x 480, two panels of 460 x 410.
- Left panel headed "The method": four rules with a gloss under each. Lead lines: "Here the stakes are physical" (terracotta), "The smallest zone leads" (green), "Vacuum, never sweep" (slate), "Three postponed verdicts" (terracotta).
- Right panel headed "The kit": nine lines of product types, with protection on the first two lines rather than cleaning, mirroring the order of the room.
- Footer in terracotta: protection first, smallest zone first, vacuum not broom, and take the three verdicts.

## Figure 4: the signature
- Canvas 1000 x 430, matched before and after panels with a slate camera disc between them.
- The after panel carries an honey-outlined label reading "start cutting inside a minute", and the caption beneath reads "the after, hung where you stand".
