# Chapter 46 Diagram Ideas: The Workshop

1. **Inconvenience against injury** (hero): two columns of failure modes. Left, other rooms: a duplicate jar, a second tumble, a musty towel. Right, the workshop: a sticky guard, a blade in a drawer, a rag in a bin. Caption: same discipline, different stakes.
2. **The order of work**: the six zones drawn as a sequence with the smallest cell first and visibly smaller than the rest. Caption: the permission to begin.
3. **What a broom does**: two panels of the same floor, one with a broom and dust rising in a visible cloud, one with a vacuum nozzle and the air clear. Caption: a broom does not remove the fines, it launches them.
4. **The three verdicts**: three doors labelled offcuts, projects, and paint, with a well-worn path leading past all three to a fourth door labelled fasteners. Caption: the zones you keep skipping.
5. **The offcut bin, settled by limits**: a bin with a size floor marked on the front and a fill line, and one short piece leaving as a new one arrives.
6. **The guard test**: a close detail of a thumb pushing a retracting blade guard, with the return arrow emphasised. Caption: if it hesitates at all, the tool is out of service.
7. **The PPE board**: hooks at a door with labelled silhouettes, one hook marked for visitors, and one silhouette empty.
