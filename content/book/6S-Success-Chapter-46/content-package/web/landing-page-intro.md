# Chapter 46 Landing Page Intro: The Workshop

This is the room 6S came from.

Everything in this book, the traced outlines and the labels and the photograph of the ideal state, was invented somewhere like this, by people for whom a missing tool was a stopped line and a sticky guard was a lost hand. So this chapter is a homecoming, and it is also the one chapter where the stakes are genuinely different. Every other room in this book fails into inconvenience. This one fails into injury.

Which is why the order of work here is unlike anywhere else. You start at the smallest zone in the room, the PPE station, because everything else involves a blade, a solvent, or a cloud of fine dust, and you want your glasses findable before you spend a Saturday handling any of it.

And there is one more thing to name before you begin. The workshop's mess is not about storage. It is three verdicts you have been postponing: on offcuts, on half-finished projects, and on paint. You already know which ones you avoid, because you will sort the fasteners twice before you touch the offcut pile once.

Six zones, exact inputs, numbered steps. Product types only, no brands.
