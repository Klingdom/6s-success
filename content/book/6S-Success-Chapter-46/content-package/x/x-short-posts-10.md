Every other room in this book fails into inconvenience. The workshop fails into injury. That is not a safety costume over a tidiness problem, it is the reason the discipline exists.

In a workshop you do the smallest zone first. The PPE station is not the reward at the end of the clean. It is the permission to begin.

(138 chars)

4/
Vacuum a shop, never sweep it. A broom does not remove the finest sawdust, it launches it, into the air, onto wet finish, and into your lungs.

(145 chars)

5/

(137 chars)
