# Chapter 46 CTA and Companion Resources: The Workshop

## Primary call to action
Do one zone this week, and make it the PPE station, because it is the smallest zone in the room and it is the one that gives you permission to do the other five.

## Companion resources
- **The Workshop Reset card** (printable: the six zones in the order they are actually done, the done-looks-like for each, and the PPE-first rule).
- **The Workshop Kit list** (printable: protection first, then cleaning, then storage and control, grouped by job).
- **The Three Verdicts card** (offcuts, half-finished projects, and paint, each with the test that settles it, including the size floor and bin cap).
- **The Visitor Rule card** (to hang on the wall: one labelled set per user, one spare set for visitors, and the near side of the doorway for anyone who will not wear it).

## Register and fences
- Warm, practical, expert, and serious about injury without dramatising it. Product types only, never brand names.
- Disposal is always household hazardous waste or metal recycling, never a drain, a bin, or a fire. Coated and treated stock never goes on the fire.
- No machine setup, tuning, blade-changing, or repair instructions. The chapter cleans, looks, and flags, and takes tools out of service rather than fixing them.
- Friction meter retired for Part 9; close on the before-and-after room signature.
- Previous: Chapter 45, The Garage. Next: Chapter 47, The Mudroom.

## Words to avoid
- No brand names. No cutting, setup, or machining technique. No advice to modify, bypass, or remove a guard for any reason.
