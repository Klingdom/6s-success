# Chapter 46 Comment Prompts: The Workshop

1. Which zone of your workshop have you been avoiding, and be honest about why.
2. How long has the half-finished project been holding your best surface?
3. Do you sweep your shop or vacuum it?
4. When did you last work every blade guard with your thumb?
5. Where do your oil finish rags go at the end of a session?
6. Do you have a visitor rule, or do you handle it case by case?
7. What is the oldest tin of paint in your workshop, and is that colour still on a wall in this house?
8. Is there a tool in there that has not cut anything in two years?
