# Chapter 46 Teleprompter Script: The Workshop

*Two minutes. Read at an even pace. Pause at each line break.*

This is the room 6S came from.

The outlines, the labels, the photograph of the ideal state.

None of it was invented for pantries. It was invented on shop floors.

Where a missing tool was a stopped line, and a sticky guard was a lost hand.

So this chapter is a homecoming.

And it is the one chapter where the stakes are different.

Every other room in this book fails into inconvenience.

This one fails into injury.

Which is why you start at the smallest zone in the room.

The PPE station. A board of hooks by the door.

Everything else in here involves a blade, a solvent, or a cloud of fine dust.

The PPE station is not the reward at the end of the clean.

It is the permission to begin.

Then one rule that overrides habit. Vacuum. Never sweep.

A broom does not remove the finest sawdust. It launches it.

Into the air, onto wet finish, and into your lungs.

And the fines are the fraction that actually burns.

Now the part that decides whether the room changes.

A workshop's mess is not about storage.

It is three verdicts you keep postponing. Offcuts. Projects. Paint.

You will sort the fasteners twice before you touch the offcut pile once.

The zones you keep skipping are the zones holding the room back.

Do one zone this week. Make it the PPE station.
