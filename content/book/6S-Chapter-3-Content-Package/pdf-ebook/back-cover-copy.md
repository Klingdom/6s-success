## Back-Cover Blurb

Six steps. One small cabinet. The whole method, start to finish.

In Chapter 3 of 6S Success: Home Edition, you stop reading about organizing and watch it actually happen. The method is 6S: Sort, Straighten, Shine, Safety, Standardize, Sustain. The space is the cabinet under a kitchen sink, the kind that went feral one reasonable item at a time.

Walk it once and you see the part most advice misses. The power is not in the six words. It is in the handoffs. Sort makes room. Shine, done slowly, finds a leak the clutter was hiding. Crouch to a child's height and the drain cleaner is suddenly at grabbing level. Each step makes the next one possible, which is why 6S is an order, not a menu, and why organizing that skips steps slides back.

Then it is your turn. One small space. Fifteen minutes. One full lap.
