## You have a cabinet that went feral.

You know the one. Under the kitchen sink, probably. It did not become a disaster on purpose. It happened one reasonable item at a time, each thing arriving for a good reason and never leaving. Now you reach in for a clean sponge and your hand finds a damp bag of rags. A bottle tips. Something rolls into the dark at the back. It is not even that dirty or that full. The problem is that nothing in there has a job, a home, or a reason.

And you have probably tried to fix it before. You organized it, felt good for a week, and watched it drift right back. That is the part most organizing advice never explains: why it never lasts.

## Watch the whole method work on one space.

6S Success: Home Edition teaches a six-step method that holds: Sort, Straighten, Shine, Safety, Standardize, Sustain. Chapter 3 is where you see it run, end to end, on that exact cabinet. Not a list of tips. One slow, complete walk, from the first decision to the last habit.

You will see why the order matters. Sort empties the cabinet so Straighten has room to work. A slow wipe during Shine finds a hidden leak the clutter had hidden for months, because cleaning done with attention is really inspection. Crouch to a toddler's height and the drain cleaner is suddenly at grabbing level, right where you can fix it. Each step hands the next one something it could not have done alone. That is why organizing that skips steps slides back, and this does not.
