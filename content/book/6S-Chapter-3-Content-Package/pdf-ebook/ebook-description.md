# Ebook Store Description

*6S Success: Home Edition. Store / retailer description, foregrounding Chapter 3.*

---

Most home organizing advice gives you tips. 6S Success: Home Edition gives you a method, and Chapter 3 is where you watch it work.

The method is called 6S: Sort, Straighten, Shine, Safety, Standardize, Sustain. It comes from the workplace, where it keeps shared spaces orderly and safe, and this book brings it home. The first two chapters explain what 6S is and where it came from. Chapter 3 stops explaining and starts doing. It picks one small, recognizable disaster, the cabinet under a kitchen sink, the kind that went feral one reasonable item at a time, and walks all six steps through it in order, from the first decision to the last habit.

Along the way you see the real point of the method. It is not the six words, which are easy to memorize. It is the handoffs between them. Sort hands Straighten an empty space. A slow wipe during Shine finds a hidden leak the clutter had concealed for months, because cleaning done slowly is really inspection. Crouching to a toddler's height reveals the drain cleaner sitting at grabbing level. Each step makes the next one possible, which is why 6S is an order, not a menu.

Then the chapter hands the work to you. Pick one small space, set a fifteen-minute timer, and take one full lap. Not perfect. Complete.

The room-by-room playbooks later in the book are the same loop, not new methods. Learn the lap once, and the rest follows.
