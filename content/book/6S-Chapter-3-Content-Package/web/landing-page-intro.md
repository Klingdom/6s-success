The cabinet under your kitchen sink did not become a disaster on purpose. It filled up one reasonable item at a time, and nothing ever left. Half-used spray bottles, a crusted sponge, three brands of trash bags, a bucket with a crack, and somewhere in the back, a slow drip that has been quietly warping the wood for months. It is not filthy and it is not overflowing. It just went feral, running itself badly while you were busy living your life.

You already know what 6S is. This chapter is where you watch it work. We take all six steps in order, Sort, Straighten, Shine, Safety, Standardize, and Sustain, and walk them through that one cabinet from the first decision to the last habit. You will see how each step hands the next one something it could not do alone, including the moment a slow wipe finds the leak the clutter was hiding. Cleaning, it turns out, is inspection.

Then it is your turn. One small space, fifteen minutes, all six steps. Read the chapter free in the online book and take your first lap.
