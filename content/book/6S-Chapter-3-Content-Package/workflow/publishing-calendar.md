# Publishing Calendar: 14-Day Release Sequence

A two-week rollout for Chapter 3, "The Six Steps That Transform Any Space". This is the chapter where the method gets demonstrated, so the goal is to show the loop working on a real space and get readers to take one fifteen-minute lap. Publish the home base (the web chapter) first, then drip social and email toward it. Dates are relative (Day 1 = the day the chapter page goes live).

Pacing principle: one anchor asset per day, plus one or two light reshares. The hidden-leak moment and the 6S Loop graphic are your strongest hooks. Lead with them.

## Hero CTA reminder

This chapter's best conversion event is the fifteen-minute lap. Wherever you can, end with "set a fifteen-minute timer on one small space and walk all six steps once." A reader who does that has practiced the method.
