# Chapter 3 Calls to Action: The Six Steps That Transform Any Space

This is the chapter where reading turns into doing. Its strongest CTA is built in: take one fifteen-minute lap on a small space. The CTAs below match the chapter's encouraging, low-pressure tone.

## Primary CTA
Take your first lap. "Pick one small space, a drawer, a shelf, or the cabinet under your own sink. Set a fifteen-minute timer and walk all six steps once: Sort, Straighten, Shine, Safety, Standardize, Sustain. Do not aim for perfect. Aim for one full lap. Read the chapter free in the online book."

## Secondary CTA
Read the chapter (or the next one). "Watch the whole 6S method walk through one cabinet, leak and all, in Chapter 3. Read it free in the online book, then Chapter 4 helps you choose a target worth winning."

## In-chapter actions a reader can take (from the callouts)
- **Quick Win:** Open one low cabinet, crouch to a child's height, and move anything that could burn, poison, or cut up high. A real Safety step in under a minute.
- **Quick Win:** Set a fifteen-minute timer and point it at one small space. Aim for one full lap, not perfect.
- **Family Challenge:** Everyone walks one small space through all six steps this week, then reports the single thing Shine revealed. Best discovery wins.

## Lead-magnet / resource CTAs (planned companion assets, from the signature)
- **The 6S Loop card:** a printable and phone wallpaper of the six-node loop with one-line meanings and handoff verbs. A companion to Chapter 2's Six Words card.
- **One-Space Lap Sheet:** the "one small space, six steps" checklist as a single tear-off page, with a spot to note what Shine revealed. The reader's first workbook page.

## CTA writing notes
- The fifteen-minute lap is the hero CTA. It is concrete, time-boxed, and forgiving (one full lap beats a perfect Sort).
- Keep the tone encouraging. The chapter rewards starting, not perfection.
- Lead with the leak-found-while-cleaning hook for curiosity. It is the chapter's most memorable, shareable moment.
- Avoid hype and big promises. Small space, small move. Keep claims honest.
