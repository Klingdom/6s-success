# LinkedIn Carousel Copy
## The Six Steps That Transform Any Space
### Chapter 3, 6S Success: Home Edition

A 10-slide carousel. On-slide copy is written out, ready to set. Short and punchy. Slide 1 hooks, slide 10 is a soft CTA. Palette and motif notes per slide match the book: warm paper, terracotta and honey accents, slate for Safety and data, green calm-dot and red friction-spark vocabulary.

---

### Slide 1 (hook)
**The cabinet under your sink went feral.**

Not on purpose. One reasonable item at a time, and none of them ever left.

Six steps fix it. Here is the whole method, on one cabinet.

[VISUAL] Honest photo of a cluttered under-sink cabinet, doors open. One red friction-spark glinting at the back corner. Title in Fraunces, lower third.

---

### Slide 2
**The problem is not the amount of stuff.**

It is that nothing in there has a job, a home, or a reason.

The cabinet stopped being storage. It became a place where things land.

[VISUAL] Line-art cabinet, before. Red friction-sparks on the snags: the leak, the low chemical, the tangle of rags.

---

### Slide 3
**6S is a loop, not a menu.**

Most people pick their favorite three and skip the rest. It never works.

The six steps are a chain. Each one sets up the next.

[VISUAL] The 6S Loop. Six nodes in a ring, handoff arrows clockwise: Sort, Straighten, Shine, Safety (slate), Standardize, Sustain. Sustain loops back to Sort. Center: "Walk it once."

---

### Slide 4
**Sort. Straighten.**

Empty the cabinet onto a towel. Three piles only: keep, relocate, release. You are not organizing yet, you are deciding.

Then give what stays a home by how often you use it. Daily things to the front.

[VISUAL] Two-panel: items sorted on a towel, then the cabinet with a caddy and frequency zones. Green calm-dots appearing on the kept items.

---

### Slide 5
**Shine is not about clean. It is about inspection.**

Wipe the empty cabinet slowly. As the cloth reaches the back corner, the wood is dark and soft.

A slow leak the clutter had hidden for months.

[VISUAL] Close-up of cloth revealing a water ring and warped board. One red friction-spark on the stain.

---

### Slide 6
**A plumber would have charged you to find this.**

Your sponge found it for free.

Clean slowly enough to notice. The leak, the loose hinge, the rust ring. That is Shine doing its real job.

[VISUAL] Macro of the warped board, friction-spark pulsing. Honey pin note: "Repair before it rots through."

---

### Slide 7
**Now crouch down. Safety.**

You are at a two-year-old's eye level. The drain cleaner is right there, at grabbing height, behind a door that does not lock.

Move it up high. Add a dollar latch.

[VISUAL] The Safety swap. Left: bottle low, red spark, small reaching hand. Right: bottle up high, green calm-dot, child latch on the door. Slate accent on the word Safety.

---

### Slide 8
**Standardize, then Sustain.**

Tape a photo of the finished cabinet inside the door. Now the right way is a picture anyone can match, not a memory.

Then a ten-second reset. That is all it takes to keep.

[VISUAL] Cabinet door with a small photo label taped inside. A ten-second reset arrow looping over the caddy. Green calm-dots throughout.

---

### Slide 9
**Same cabinet. One full lap.**

The sparks did not get cleaned away. They got handed, step to step, until there was nothing left to spark about.

That is the whole point: the handoffs.

[VISUAL] Before/after split. Left: cabinet covered in red friction-sparks. Right: sorted, caddy, leak dried, chemical up high, photo on the door, all green calm-dots.

---

### Slide 10 (soft CTA)
**Your turn. Fifteen minutes.**

One drawer, one shelf, or the cabinet under your own sink. Walk all six steps once. Do not aim for perfect. Aim for one full lap.
