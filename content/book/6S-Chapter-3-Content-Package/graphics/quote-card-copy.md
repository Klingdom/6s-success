# Quote Card Copy: Chapter 3

**The Six Steps That Transform Any Space**
**6S Success: Home Edition**

Ten quote cards. Every quote is pulled verbatim from `chapter-quotes.md` (which lifts directly from the manuscript and final HTML). None contain em dashes. Each is short enough to set large on a card without crowding.

Shared attribution line for all cards: **6S Success: Home Edition, Chapter 3**

Palette tokens referenced below: paper `#F7F2E9`, panel `#FBF7EF`, ink `#2B2622`, terracotta `#BC4B2A`, honey `#DDA63A`, slate `#3C5A6B`, green `#6E8B5B`, spark `#CB4B36`. Quote text in Fraunces, attribution in a small sans, any supporting line in Newsreader italic.

---

### Card 1
**Quote:** "The cabinet stopped being storage and became a place where things land."
**Attribution:** 6S Success: Home Edition, Chapter 3
**Design note:** Warm paper background, ink quote in Fraunces. Tiny before-cabinet line motif in the lower corner with one faint red spark. This is the chapter's opening diagnosis, so keep it spare.

---

### Card 2
**Quote:** "The problem is not the amount of stuff. The problem is that nothing in there has a job, a home, or a reason."
**Attribution:** 6S Success: Home Edition, Chapter 3
**Design note:** Panel background, terracotta rule under the quote. Set "a job, a home, or a reason" on its own line for rhythm. Motif: three empty home-outlines in green, waiting to be filled.

---

### Card 3
**Quote:** "The steps are not six separate chores that happen to start with the same letter. They are a chain. Each one sets up the next."
**Attribution:** 6S Success: Home Edition, Chapter 3
**Design note:** The 6S Loop motif. Six small terracotta nodes in a faint ring behind the text, Safety node in slate. Ink quote on paper. Good as the lead card in a carousel.

---

### Card 4
**Quote:** "Each step makes the next one easier. The work compounds. By the time you reach the end, the space almost finishes itself."
**Attribution:** 6S Success: Home Edition, Chapter 3
**Design note:** Clockwise terracotta arrow arc across the top, suggesting the loop. Honey accent on "compounds." Warm paper, ink type.

---

### Card 5
**Quote:** "You are not organizing yet. You are deciding. Keep, relocate, or release."
**Attribution:** 6S Success: Home Edition, Chapter 3
**Design note:** Sort card. Three small tag chips along the bottom labeled KEEP (green), RELOCATE (slate), RELEASE (spark). Panel background. The three-pile motif from the Sort and Straighten panel.

---

### Card 6
**Quote:** "Tidy means everything looks lined up. Easy means the thing you need most is the thing you reach first."
**Attribution:** 6S Success: Home Edition, Chapter 3
**Design note:** Straighten card. Small caddy line-icon at front, daily bottles in terracotta and honey. Set the two sentences as a contrast, "Tidy" muted in soft, "Easy" in ink. Warm paper.

---

### Card 7
**Quote:** "Shine is not really about clean. Shine is about inspection."
**Attribution:** 6S Success: Home Edition, Chapter 3
**Design note:** Shine card. Cloth-and-ring motif from the leak close-up, one red spark on a faint water ring. Slate type for the second sentence to signal the shift to inspection. Panel background.

---

### Card 8
**Quote:** "A plumber would have charged you to discover this. Your sponge found it for free."
**Attribution:** 6S Success: Home Edition, Chapter 3
**Design note:** The memorable Shine beat. Warm paper, ink Fraunces, a single green calm-dot replacing the spark to show the leak now found and handled. Close-up sponge line motif in the corner.

---

### Card 9
**Quote:** "Safety rides on the back of the first three steps. That is why it sits in the middle of the loop and not tacked on at the end."
**Attribution:** 6S Success: Home Edition, Chapter 3
**Design note:** Safety card, the only slate-forward card. Slate background panel, paper-colored type, honey accent. Behind the text, a faint loop with the middle Safety node lit in slate. Carries the chapter's key structural idea.

---

### Card 10
**Quote:** "Sustain hands you back to Sort, a little wiser, and the loop goes around again."
**Attribution:** 6S Success: Home Edition, Chapter 3
**Design note:** Closing card. A full green loop arrow curving from Sustain back to Sort, the return arrow drawn slightly heavier. Warm paper, ink type, green calm-dot at the join. Natural carousel ender.
