Chapter 3 stops explaining 6S and finally walks all six steps through that one small space, in order: Sort, Straighten, Shine, Safety, Standardize, Sustain. The real point is the handoffs, the way each step hands the next one something it could not have done alone. You will watch a slow wipe find a leak the clutter had hidden for months, and a crouch reveal the drain cleaner sitting at a toddler's eye level. Then you get your first lap: fifteen minutes, one small space, one full loop. Not perfect, just complete.
