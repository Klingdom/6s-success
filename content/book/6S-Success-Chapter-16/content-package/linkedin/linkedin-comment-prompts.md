# Chapter 16 LinkedIn Comment Prompts: Cleaning as Inspection

Five prompts to start genuine discussion. Drop one in the comments of a post or article to invite replies. Anti-shame voice: a home that went unread is a normal home, not a dirty one, and not cleaning enough is not a character flaw.

---

1. Don't just clean it. Read it. Think of one surface you wiped this week on autopilot. If you had looked while you cleaned it, what might it have been trying to tell you? A damp end, a loose fitting, a worn line? No judgment here; most of us only ever do the first job of the cloth.

2. Here is the honest one. What is the small thing in your home that was cheap to fix once and became expensive because nobody looked in time? A slow leak, a rusting base, a screw that finally let go. A home that went unread is a normal home, not a failing. Naming it is how the reading starts.

3. A surface talks in marks. Pick one everyday spot and tell us what it is quietly saying: a water mark, a fresh scratch, crumbs gathering in one corner, a hook that gives when you touch it. What did you notice once you were actually looking?

4. The umbrella stand was rusting in standing water and the key hook had worked loose, both invisible under the daily blur until someone cleaned and looked. What have you stopped seeing in a room you walk past a dozen times a day, the way your eye slides over your own face in the mirror?

5. Shine is not about a spotless house. It is about keeping a space clear enough that it can tell you what it needs. Have you ever cleaned something properly and had it hand you a problem while it was still small and cheap? What did the cleaning catch?
