# Newsletter Short Teaser: Don't Just Clean It. Read It.

For a roundup or digest. 90 to 140 words.

---

The room is cleared and arranged now, but no one has really looked at it, and a room that has never been inspected is full of small secrets. This chapter opens Shine, the third S, by changing what cleaning is for. The rule is the whole method in five words: don't just clean it, read it. Every surface you wipe is a surface you get to read, because a wipe does two jobs at once, it cleans the surface and it shows you the surface. A first pass at the launch pad proves it, catching an umbrella-stand base rusting in standing water and a loose key hook while both are still cheap. The friction meter takes its first Shine step, off the Straighten-complete mark toward calm.
