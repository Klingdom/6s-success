## Search intent
Reframing cleaning as a way of noticing, not decluttering or arranging. Readers arriving here have already cleared their space (Sort) and arranged it into homes and zones (Straighten), but the surfaces have gone unread and the daily blur hides small problems. They want a reason to clean that pays off beyond appearance, and a way to catch wear early while it is still cheap. The chapter answers with a rule and a device: don't just clean it, read it, because a wipe does two jobs at once, and cleaning as inspection means keeping a space clean enough to see clearly so problems show themselves while they are still small. It teaches a short vocabulary of tells (a water mark, a scratch, gathering crumbs, a loose fitting), does one worked first pass on the launch pad that catches a rusting umbrella-stand base and a loose key hook, and moves the friction meter its first Shine step.
