# Brand Voice Check: Chapter 16 Content Package

The voice of *6S Success: Home Edition* is warm, plain-spoken, and quietly confident. Chapter 16 is the FIRST chapter of Part Five and OPENS Shine, the third of the six S's. Its job is a reframe of a chore people dread or feel judged by, so the register must be permission and relief, not exhortation: not "clean harder," but "clean so you can see." The voice must stay anti-shame (a home that went unread is a normal home, not a dirty one), keep "spotless" firmly as a rejected non-goal, and be honest that this is the first Shine step, not the finish line.

## Voice fingerprint (from the authored final HTML and manuscript)
- **Warm and personal.** Talks to "you," returns to the launch pad by the door, and pictures an ordinary morning wiping the hall shelf and reading the damp patch that turns out to be a leak.
- **Concrete over abstract.** The concept is taught through the umbrella-stand base sitting in a shallow ring of rainwater and starting to rust, the key hook that gives a little when your hand closes on it, and the "two jobs of a wipe."
- **Anti-shame.** "This is not a failing of attention or effort. It is simply what happens to any space you live in long enough." Not seeing condition is habituation, not carelessness.
- **The reframe (distinct from earlier chapters).** "You were taught to clean so the place would look good for other people. Shine asks you to clean so the place makes sense to you." A handing-back of a chore, not a reproach.
- **Honest about scope and the meter.** The needle earns a real move because the room's *condition* changed, but the goal is kept honestly ahead: "The needle moved for a true reason today. It also has a good way left to travel, and both of those things are true at once."

## Specific wins
- "Don't just clean it. Read it." is the most portable line in the chapter and travels intact across channels (present in 36 package files).
- "Spotless is not the target and never was. Seen is the target." delivers the anti-perfectionism turn in nine words and lands as a shareable reveal.
- "The dirt you are removing is also the blindfold you are removing" gives the hero device a clean, quotable form.

## Voice risks to watch
- **Hero vocabulary is dense.** "clean," "surface," "see," "read," "inspect," "notice" recur because they are the subject; expected, not a tic, but vary the surrounding language when excerpting, and watch "honest," "small," "care/cared," and "wipe" as the payoff words most likely to stack.
- **CTA sameness.** Rotate the LinkedIn and X sign-offs; several close on "Free online" / "Read the free chapter."
- **Keep the pride quiet.** The chapter opens a whole new S, so a note of momentum is right; do not let any asset tip into a "cleaning will change your life" register. The frozen framing keeps the goal honestly ahead (Shine has only begun; Safety, Standardize, and Sustain still follow).

## Banned-language audit (scans actually run, LC_ALL=C, across manuscript, final HTML, publishable HTML, and all 52 content-package files)
- **Widened dash scan** (em dash, en dash, and spaced-hyphen " - " used as a separator, excluding markdown list bullets): **zero** in every category. Dash HTML entities (&mdash;, &ndash;, numeric): **zero**. Labels use the middot "·" (present in 30 files).
- **Hype-word scan** (unlock, leverage, elevate, supercharge, seamless, game-changing, "almost magical", "fast-paced", "sparkling", "dive in", "in conclusion"): **zero** in all files.
- **Force / violence scan:** "war on dirt" and "pull the trigger" **zero**. "attack," "blitz," and "purge" appear ONLY as **negated production/scope guidance** in three editor-note files: `canonical/chapter-cta.md` (line 32), `video-audio/b-roll-and-visual-notes.md` (lines 16 and 130), and `workflow/repurposing-map.md` (line 34), all of the form "nothing is attacked, blitzed, or purged." None appears in any reader-facing asset as a metaphor in use. Not a voice violation; recorded for full honesty.
- **"Spotless" check:** **117** occurrences across the package, and every one is a NEGATED non-goal or an editor note enforcing that rule ("not about a spotless house," "not here to make this room spotless," "The goal is not a showroom. Spotless is not the target and never was," "a race to spotless," "Seen is the target, not spotless," "not a spotless house, but a seen one"). No aspirational use found. This is intentional per the brief; **not a flag**.

## Verdict
Voice is consistent and intact across all eleven channels, and the permission-and-relief, anti-shame, honest-first-step job of Chapter 16 is honored throughout. Approve the voice. The chapter itself still needs author sign-off, since it was authored in the book voice (grounded in the 6S source, "Principles of 6S: Shine Step") rather than supplied from a source file.
