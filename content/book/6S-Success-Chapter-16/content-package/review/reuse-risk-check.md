# Reuse Risk Check: Chapter 16 Content Package

When one chapter becomes fifty-plus assets, the risk is overexposure: the same line or image hitting the same follower until it feels canned. This check flags where that can happen.

## High-frequency elements
| Element | Appears in | Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Don't just clean it. Read it. | Web, LinkedIn, X, FB, newsletter, quote card, graphics, slides (36 files) | A follower on several platforms sees the rule repeatedly | Calendar staggers it; vary the phrasing around it per platform |
| Every surface you wipe is a surface you get to read | Canonical, LinkedIn, X, quote card, graphics (37 files) | Medium | The rule's companion; repetition reinforces. Vary the surrounding setup. |
| The Shine definition ("clean enough to see clearly...") | Canonical, LinkedIn, X, quote card, graphics (25 files) | Medium | The woven definition; pair with a different worked tell each time. |
| Spotless is not the target. Seen is the target. | LinkedIn, FB, X, quote card | Medium to High | Strongest, most shareable reframe. Use across formats, not stacked on one day. |
| The not-spotless pull-quote | LinkedIn, X, FB, quote card | Medium | Vary which half you lead with (the rejected showroom vs. the cared-for home). |
| Soft-CTA sign-off wording ("Free online", "the online book", "Read the free chapter") | Most LinkedIn and X posts | Medium | Rotate phrasings; most likely thing to feel templated. |

## Same-day collision
The calendar avoids two assets sharing a hook on the same day. If your LinkedIn and Facebook audiences overlap heavily, push one back a day so "Spotless is not the target. Seen is the target." does not land twice in one day.

## Cross-posting risk
- The LinkedIn carousel outline (`linkedin/linkedin-carousel-outline.md`) and the slides carousel copy (`slides/linkedin-carousel-copy.md`) cover the same cleaning-as-inspection content. Post only one to LinkedIn (use the copy-ready slides file; keep the outline as planning).
- The LinkedIn newsletter and the email newsletter share structure; do not send both to the same subscriber the same day.

## Duplicate-content / SEO risk
- Web landing intro, ebook description, and LinkedIn article overlap in phrasing. Set the chapter page as the SEO canonical and mark syndicated copies with a canonical link.

## Cross-chapter risk (specific to Chapter 16)
- Chapter 16 OPENS Shine after four chapters of Straighten. Keep it distinct from the Straighten set: nothing is re-sorted, re-placed, re-labelled, or re-mapped here. The launch pad's layout is exactly where Straighten left it; only its *condition* changes. Assets must not drift back into arranging.
- Do not let cleaning-as-inspection slide FORWARD into the neighboring Shine chapters. Ch 16 assets must not build the list of what to clean or divide the labor (that is Chapter 17), teach cleaning methods, products, or tools (Chapter 18), or build a repeatable routine or schedule (Chapter 19). The first pass here is a one-time initial cleaning-and-inspection, not a habit yet. Grep confirms the reader-facing copy holds this fence: "routine/schedule/checklist" appear only as a CSS class name (`.checklist`), named fences, or forward teasers.
- The friction-meter FIRST SHINE STEP is specific to this chapter (off the Straighten-complete milestone toward calm). Do not reuse the earlier per-step Straighten framing or the Straighten-complete *milestone* framing in Ch 16 assets, and keep the goal honestly ahead (Shine has only begun; Safety, Standardize, and Sustain still follow).

## Originality and source-fidelity risk
- The chapter is a draft authored in the book voice, grounded in the 6S source ("Principles of 6S: Shine Step"), not supplied verbatim source. All derived assets are faithful to that draft; re-sync if the chapter is revised.
- Quote cards are verbatim: 10 cards plus 13 alternates (23 quoted lines), each verified byte-identical to a quoted line in `canonical/chapter-quotes.md`. Nothing borrowed from outside the book; Total Productive Maintenance and the Toyota / factory-floor origin are referenced lightly, home-scaled, not overclaimed.

## Recommendations
1. Rotate CTA wording (highest-value, lowest-effort fix; several posts close on "Free online").
2. Pick one carousel version for LinkedIn.
3. Set the chapter page as the SEO canonical.
4. Keep Chapter 16 from blurring into its neighbors: distinct from Straighten (nothing moves house) behind it, and distinct from Chapters 17 to 19 (targets, methods, routine) ahead of it. Ch 16 is a reframe plus one first pass.
5. Re-sync all assets if the authored chapter changes after author review.
