# Read Next Recommendations: Chapter 16, Shine: Cleaning as Inspection

1. Chapter 17, Shine Targets and Assignments
   The next step, and the one that turns the reframe into a plan. You cleaned one zone to prove the idea; now Shine gets a plan. Chapter 17 maps what to clean across the whole home, the surfaces, the equipment, the stored things, and the spots you use most, and sorts out who does what, so the reading you did on the launch pad happens everywhere, without falling on one person. Chapter 16 reframed cleaning as inspection and did one first pass; Chapter 17 continues Part Five, Shine.

2. Chapter 15, The Room Map
   The setup. Before you could clean and read a zone, the room had to be cleared and arranged. Chapter 15 took the fourth and final Straighten step, gathering the well-placed, self-explaining homes into a handful of zones by activity and drawing the whole arrangement into one shared map, so the running entryway example was revealed as one zone all along, the launch pad. What it left behind was a room that was cleared and fully arranged but not yet cared for, its surfaces still gathering dust and its condition never once looked at. This chapter opens the third S and starts to read what the arrangement has been hiding.

3. Do your first inspection (the chapter action)
   Pick one surface you use every day and clean it properly, once. As you do, look at what the cleaning uncovers, a mark, a wobble, a worn spot you had stopped seeing. You just did your first inspection.

4. The Inspection Wipe card (free resource)
   A printable and phone wallpaper carrying the rule, don't just clean it, read it, plus the handful of tells to watch for, a water mark, a scratch, a loose fitting, and a spreading stain.

5. The First Shine Pass sheet (free resource)
   A printable to pick a surface, clean it, and log what it told you, so your first cleaning-and-inspection pass leaves a record you can compare against next time.
