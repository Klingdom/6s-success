## You are here blurb
You are at Chapter 16, the first chapter of Part Five, Shine, and the opening step of the third S. Sort emptied the room and Straighten arranged it into well-placed, self-explaining homes gathered into zones and drawn into one shared map, yet every step so far was about where things go, not the condition of anything, so the surfaces have gone unread and small problems sit in plain sight under the daily blur. This chapter changes what cleaning is for: not a spotless house but a seen one, cleaning done so you can see, so the act of cleaning doubles as the act of inspecting and small problems show themselves early, while they are still small. The hero rule is five words, don't just clean it, read it, because every surface you wipe is a surface you get to read and a wipe does two jobs at once, it cleans the surface and makes it legible. A short vocabulary of tells makes reading concrete: a water mark means a slow leak, a scratch means something rubbing, gathering crumbs mean a gap, a loose fitting means wear. One first cleaning-and-inspection pass on the launch pad proves it, catching the umbrella-stand base rusting in a ring of rainwater and the key hook worked loose in the wall, both cheap to fix today and expensive to ignore, with nothing sorted or rearranged. This chapter does not re-open Sort or re-teach Straighten (Chapters 12 to 15), does not build the list of what to clean or divide the labor (Chapter 17), does not teach cleaning methods, products, or tools (Chapter 18), and does not build the repeatable reset (Chapter 19). The room's condition changed, so the friction meter takes its first Shine step, off the Straighten-complete milestone toward calm, and the goal is still ahead, because Shine has only begun and Safety, Standardize, and Sustain still follow.
