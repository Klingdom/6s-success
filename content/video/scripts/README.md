# Shooting scripts

Production scripts for **The 6S Micro Zone Reset**, the headless video series that takes one micro
zone from its current state to its finished standard in six passes: Sort, Straighten, Shine, Safety,
Standardize, Sustain. Safety is the fourth pass, every time.

Everything in this directory is written to be read aloud on set by one person whose hands are busy.
Short sentences. Verbatim voiceover. No presenter on camera at any point.

## The format reference

**`content/video/pilot/Entryway Pilot - Shooting Scripts.html` is the format.** It carries the five
Entryway episodes and it is the thing every later room is written to match: fixed opener, six
segments in canonical order, a close that shows the done state and names the trigger, and a Short cut
lifted out of each segment.

Do not invent a new structure for a new room. If a room genuinely needs a structural change, change
the pilot first and say why, so all 114 episodes stay one series.

Three more documents constrain every script here:

- `content/video/6S Micro Zone Reset - YouTube Production Plan.html` sets the series spec, the
  per-segment beats, and the rule that six Shorts come out of every episode, one per S, each written
  to survive being lifted out on its own.
- `content/video/6S_SHORT_FORMAT_DISTRIBUTION_PLAN.md` sets the framing rules. Shoot 4K 16:9 and
  compose inside a centred 9:16 safe box, keep on screen text inside that box, and capture a
  deliberate vertical take for each of the six segment openers. Every shot table in this directory
  carries a 9:16 column for that reason.
- `content/manual/source/content.json` is the source of truth for what each zone is for, what done
  looks like, what each pass actually does, what the hazards are, and what standard and trigger get
  left behind. Scripts draw the room facts from there rather than inventing them.

`content/video/6S-Micro-Zone-Reset-tracker.csv` holds the episode codes, titles, runtimes and
status. Zone names in the scripts follow the tracker, which matches the site and the book.

## House rules for anything written here

- No em dashes and no en dashes anywhere. A gate checks this.
- **Straighten**, never "Set in Order". Safety is the fourth S, never the last.
- Product types, never brand names. A non scratch scrubbing sponge, not a brand of sponge.
- No invented statistics, no claims about results, no fabricated testing.
- Nothing that needs a licensed trade gets taught. Name the fault, then name the trade.
- Spoken language. A person is reading this aloud with their hands in a drawer.

Suggested next rooms, in this order and for these reasons:

1. **Pantry.** It reuses the Kitchen's dating and eat first thinking, so the writing is fast and the
   two rooms cross link naturally.
2. **Primary Bathroom.** Seven episodes, high search interest, and hazards that are real without
   being specialist.
3. **Laundry Room.** Small zone count, strong visible before and after, and the chemical handling
   rules are already established by the Kitchen sink zone.

Leave the Garage and the Workshop until the format is proven on camera. Those shoots are the longest
and the least forgiving, and their hazard load is the heaviest in the series.
