VIDEO TITLE
Doormat Reset: How to Tell When a Mat Has Stopped Working

DESCRIPTION
The mat just inside your door can look completely fine and have quietly stopped working. Once the fibres flatten and the grit rides on top of the pile, every shoe lifts it back off and carries it down the hall.

This is the last zone in the entryway, the door, the mat, and the strip of floor right inside it. It is the smallest patch in the house and it quietly does the most work, because everything that reaches the rest of your floors crosses it first. In this reset you clear the floor to one rule, set up the two mats that actually do the job, clean the one part of the mat everyone skips, deal with the trip waiting at the threshold, and give the mat a home anyone can see.
The test for a mat is simpler than the label on it. Walk across the inside mat at your normal pace and watch your feet. On most mats the second foot lands past the end, onto bare floor, and that is exactly how the grit gets past. Length is what matters here, not the pattern. A coarse mat outside scrapes, an absorbent mat inside dries, and both feet have to land on the inside one or it is too short to catch anything.
Safety is the fourth of the six passes, and it is never the one you skip. It deals with the curled corner you catch when your arms are full and your eyes are down, the mat that slides on hard floor even lying flat, and the loose door hinge that swings harder than you expect and catches a finger against the frame. Then you tape a thin outline on the floor, so the mat's home is something anyone can read at a glance instead of a rule that lives only in your head.
Finished looks like this. Two mats lying dead flat inside their taped outlines, not a curled corner on either one, nothing stored on the floor inside the swing of the door, and a door that latches on the first push. The standard that holds all of it is the simplest one in the house. Nothing is stored on this floor, ever. And the trigger is the last key of the night, when whoever comes home last straightens the mat with a foot before they turn it.
