VIDEO TITLE
Entryway Landing Zone Reset: Keys, Mail, and the Paper With No Verdict

DESCRIPTION
The keys were never the problem on this surface. It is the paper that never got a verdict, the same few sheets sliding from one end to the other for weeks.

This is the landing surface just inside your front door, the spot where your pockets empty out at the end of the day. In this reset we tip the whole thing onto the floor, keys, tray, and every stacked sheet, so you can see the real volume for the first time. Then we run all six passes on this one surface: Sort, Straighten, Shine, Safety the fourth S, Standardize, and Sustain.
The heart of it is the paper. Every sheet gets one of three answers, and only three: act, file, or recycle. Nothing comes back onto the surface undecided. The sheets that need you go upright into a folder with the date written in the corner, and that date becomes the clock that decides for you later. You will also see where the tray actually belongs, set by where your hand opens on the way in, and why the charger has to sit at the dry end, away from anything that drips off a wet sleeve.
Safety here is real and physical. Water and electricity meet under the hooks, one outlet quietly gets overloaded, and a bag on the floor is the thing you trip over in the dark with your arms full. We deal with all three while the surface is clear.
Done looks like this: the tray, the folder standing beside it, and nothing else. A small photo of that finished state taped inside the cupboard door so it teaches itself, and a habit tied to a moment you already have, the pause when you take your coat off. If your entryway keeps collecting paper it never resolves, this is the one surface to start with.
