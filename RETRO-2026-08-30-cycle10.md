# Retrospective, tenth cycle ending 2026-08-30

Three commits, back on product work. The largest single performance change this
site has had, and it was sitting in plain sight the whole time.

---

## What went well

**The room pages finally went through the pipeline everything else uses.** All
41 photographs shipped as a bare img with no srcset, no webp, no width and no
height, at 1402x1122 and 1536x1024. A phone at 390 CSS pixels was downloading
roughly three and a half times the pixels it can display, and every image
shifted the text under it as it arrived. 10.4 MB across twenty pages became
0.8 MB on a phone. That is a 92 percent reduction on the heaviest real content
on the site, for about ten minutes of work.

**Dimensions are read from the images rather than assumed.** Width and height
are now facts recorded from the files themselves, which is the difference
between reserving the right space and reserving space that happens to be right
today.

**The ordering trap did not happen twice.** The variant pass is chained ahead
of the page write, because the markup names files the same run produces. The
social preview images hit exactly this two cycles ago by generating afterwards,
and remembering that cost nothing this time.

---

## What did not go well

**I put a 404 inside a live srcset.** The first version named three widths for
every image, but one source is portrait at 1122 wide, so its 1280 variant was
never produced. A browser recovers from a missing srcset candidate silently and
picks another, which is exactly why nobody would ever report it and why it
could have sat there for months. Found by resolving every referenced file
rather than by looking at a page.

**That is the second time this session I have written a reference to a file
that does not exist.** The first was the social preview images. Both times the
generator constructed a path by pattern and never asked the filesystem whether
the result was real. The pattern is now clear enough to name: constructing a
URL is not the same as having an asset, and only one of those two things can be
checked.

**I still have not touched the things a first time visitor meets.** The Quest
ships the returning user's screen and collapses into the first run screen once
JavaScript arrives. There is no skip link on any of 189 pages. A cart drawer
that can never be filled sits in the tab order of every page with no Escape
handler. All three were named in an audit two days ago, all three affect every
visitor, and I have spent the cycles since on images and gates.

---

## What to change next cycle

**Do the three visitor-facing defects, and do them first.** They are the oldest
open findings I own and the only ones that touch every single person who
arrives. Images and gates have had four cycles; the first-run experience has
had none.

**Every generated URL gets resolved before it ships.** Twice this session a
constructed path pointed at nothing. A one line existence check at the point of
construction would have caught both, and it belongs in the generators rather
than in a gate that runs later.
