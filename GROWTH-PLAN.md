# The plan to 20,000 dollars a month

Written 2026-08-19, the day the site went public. Owner: Phil. Operator: Claude.

## The arithmetic first

No single offer reaches the target. That is not pessimism, it is division.

| Offer | Price | Sales needed per month | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Virtual Home Consult | 250 | 80 | Impossible. Capacity is about 40. |
| In-Home Reset Day | 1,200 | 17 | Impossible. Capacity is about 12. |
| Corporate Lean 6S | 3,500 | 6 | Impossible. Capacity is about 2. |
| Book, ebook | 18 | 1,111 | Needs roughly 40,000 to 74,000 visits |
| Micro Zone Manual | 29 | 690 | Needs roughly 46,000 visits |

So the target is reached by a **mix**, and the mix has two inputs: Phil's hours
and site traffic.

### A month that actually adds to 20,000

| Offer | Price | Units | Revenue | Phil's hours |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| In-Home Reset Day | 1,200 | 8 | 9,600 | 72 |
| Virtual Home Consult | 250 | 16 | 4,000 | 24 |
| Corporate Lean 6S | 3,500 | 1 | 3,500 | 16 |
| Book, ebook | 18 | 120 | 2,160 | 0 |
| Micro Zone Manual | 29 | 60 | 1,740 | 0 |
| Print book | 34 | 40 | 1,360 | 0 |
| **Total** | | | **22,360** | **112** |

**112 hours a month is 26 hours a week.** The target is not a passive income
plan. It is a working business where Phil delivers about three days a week and
the digital products pay for the other two.

The 220 digital units a month need **7,000 to 22,000 visits a month**, depending
on conversion.

### How long that traffic takes

A site published today, with genuinely useful content that nobody has linked to
yet, ordinarily looks like this:

| Month | Visits per month |
|---|---|
| 1 | about 50 |
| 3 | about 800 |
| 6 | about 4,000 |
| 12 | about 15,000 |
| 18 | about 35,000 |

**The honest conclusion: 20,000 a month is a 12 to 18 month objective, not a
this-quarter objective.** Anyone promising faster is selling something. What can
move fast is the consulting half, because it needs one customer at a time rather
than thirty thousand visitors.

## The strategy that follows

**Sell time now. Build the traffic that sells files later.**

Consulting is the only thing deliverable today and it is high ticket, so it
needs a handful of customers rather than a crowd. It funds the months while
search compounds. The book and the manual are the long game: zero marginal cost,
infinite capacity, and worth nothing until people arrive.

That means the site's job right now is **not** to sell 41 products. It is to
convert a small number of the right visitors into a conversation about a room
they hate.

### Three phases

**Phase 1, now to month 3. Make one thing buyable and start the clock.**
Revenue target: 1,000 to 4,000 a month, entirely consulting.
- Consulting bookable and payable without an email exchange
- Every page points at the one thing that can be bought
- Email capture working, because the book sends readers and we lose them
- Search Console registered, because month 12 depends on the clock starting now
- The 114 micro zone pages built out as real answers

**Phase 2, months 3 to 9. Turn traffic into digital revenue.**
Revenue target: 4,000 to 10,000 a month.
- Book and manual actually sellable, which needs the front matter
- The 2,600 written social units published rather than sitting in a folder
- The strongest zone pages deepened against real search queries
- A second consulting tier if the first is capacity bound

**Phase 3, months 9 to 18. Compound.**
Revenue target: 10,000 to 20,000 a month.
- Digital products carry the volume, consulting carries the margin
- Card decks and kits only if the audience asks for them, never speculatively
- Corporate engagements, which are the highest value hour Phil sells

## Division of labour

This is the part that decides whether the plan is real.

### Claude runs, without asking

Site, content, SEO and AEO, the 114 zone pages, page copy, technical work,
deployment, analytics, experiments, the product catalogue's accuracy, Stripe
configuration, email capture, reporting, and the four hour operating cycle.

### Only Phil can do, and nothing substitutes

1. **Deliver the consulting.** It is his hours in someone's home. This is the
   entire near term revenue line.
2. **Complete Stripe live onboarding.** Identity and bank verification are
   legally his. Until then the account cannot take real money.
3. **The front matter.** His name, imprint and ISBN. Thirteen fields blocking
   two finished products.
4. **Anything that spends money.** See `SPENDING.md`.

**The single highest leverage hour Phil can spend is the front matter**, because
it unblocks two finished products at once and needs no traffic to be worth doing.

## What gets measured

Weekly, from the dashboard, never typed:

- visits, and where they came from
- conversion events, currently zero because nothing records intent
- consulting enquiries, and how many became bookings
- revenue, split by offer
- for each product listed: is it actually deliverable

The last one is a guard, not a metric. Eight priced SKUs currently have no
supplier, platform or stock. The catalogue must never advertise what cannot ship.

## What this plan will not do

- Buy traffic before the site converts. Ads on a site with no conversion event
  are a way to spend money learning nothing.
- Publish thin pages to game search. 114 real micro zones is the asset. Six
  hundred spun variations would poison it.
- Sell a product that does not exist, including by taking a preorder for one.
- Invent scarcity, discounts, testimonials or demand.
- Promise a date for 20,000 a month. The model above is a shape, not a forecast.
