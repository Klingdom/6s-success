# Retrospective, cycle 32 (2026-08-31)

## What this cycle did

Implemented the new growth super prompt rather than filing it, and shipped the
one customer-facing item in it that could be shipped honestly today.

**Risk 5 is fixed.** The footer offered a box labelled "Your email" and a button
reading "Join", with no statement of what pressing it gets you. It now carries
the offer the prompt specifies word for word: "Get your first five Quest cards.
One small zone each day, with a clear finish line."

**The important decision was how to deliver it.** Shipping that as an email
promise would have been wrong. Listmonk cannot store an address: its root URL is
still localhost:9000, so every double opt in link is unclickable, and its from
address belongs to another brand. Promising five cards by email would be a
promise that fails on arrival, which is worse than a vague button.

But the five cards do not need email. They are in the Quest, free, right now,
with no account. So the offer is delivered by a link rather than promised by a
form. Phil's words, a mechanism that works today.

## What went well

**I did not build the thing that would have been most impressive.** 6S Plus is
$11,250 of the $21,500 target and the largest gap. Phil has specified it
precisely: $5.99 monthly, $39.99 annually, $29.99 founding. It would have been
easy to stand up a subscription page and call the biggest line "started". But
of its eleven promised features, cross device sync, households, zone ownership,
audit scheduling and the AI allowance all need accounts and a server that do not
exist. Selling that today is selling vapour, which section 8 rules out. The
honest position is recorded instead.

**The ownership gate earned itself.** Changing site.css re-fingerprints it, and
site/sw.js precaches by exact URL including that hash, so the committed service
worker was pointing at a stylesheet the pages no longer request. Offline that is
a real outage, not a cosmetic drift: the fetch handler matches the exact URL, a
stale hash never matches, and the asset falls through to the network, which is
the one thing that does not work in the garage the feature exists for. I ran the
fingerprinter and forgot the builder. The gate I unblocked two days ago caught
it within a minute.

**Verified rather than assumed, again.** Every offer link resolves from both
page depths. The footer still measures clean at 360px and 390px, which mattered
because that exact column had an unshrinkable width floor a week ago.

## What did not go well

**186 identical copies of one footer.** No generator holds the markup; they copy
it from a page. So a one line copy change is a 186 file commit, and the only
thing standing between that and silent divergence is a gate. It worked, but the
underlying shape is fragile and I did not fix it.

**The biggest revenue line moved by nothing.** Recording honestly that Plus
cannot be built yet is the right call and is still not progress against
$11,250. The recurring half of the target needs an accounts layer, and that is a
build, not a cycle.

## What to change next cycle

1. **Give the footer one source.** 186 copies of a block that six generators
   copy from a page is the duplicate drift I have been removing everywhere else,
   sitting in the most visited markup on the site.

2. **Scope the accounts layer rather than admiring the gap.** Plus needs
   identity, household and sync. Naming the smallest version of that which makes
   one Plus feature real would turn a wall into a first step.

3. **Keep testing the prompt's claims before acting.** Two of its seven risks
   were not defects in the form stated, and I would have "fixed" both.
